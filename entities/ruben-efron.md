# Ruben Efron

**Senate Armed Services Committee Consultant** assigned to [[entities/richard-russell|Senator Russell's]] 1955 USSR/Czechoslovakia mission. ^[extracted]

## Significance to the wiki

Efron is the **third witness** of the [[references/sighting-trans-caucasus-1955-10-04|4 Oct 1955 Trans-Caucasus sighting]]. He saw only a brief glimpse of disc 1 (he reached the train window after Russell and [[entities/e-u-hathaway|Hathaway]] due to crowding) and a full view of disc 2.

His evidentiary contribution is **note-keeping**: Efron kept **detailed contemporaneous notes** during the trip, which he referred to during the 13 Oct 1955 USAIRA Prague debrief for **times and places**. The chronology section of [[references/iaf-ir-193-55-russell-1955|IR 193-55]] — the precise *"after we left ATJATY, we were there at 1900 and we saw the aircraft at 1910. The next place we saw was ADZHLJABUL"* citation, the disc-light-position **sketch produced "with aid of Mr. Efron"** (page 4), and the disc-position-on-windows reconstruction — depend on Efron's notes. ^[extracted]

[[entities/thomas-s-ryan|Lt Col Ryan]] (USAIRA Prague) characterized Efron's role at the debrief: *"Hathaway did most of the explaining and answering with Mr. Efron supplementing and adding support... Mr. Efron had kept detailed notes which he referred to for times and places."* ^[extracted]

## What is preserved about Efron

Beyond the sighting role, the bundle preserves only one substantive other detail attributed to Efron — the **biographical sketch of `EGOROV`** the trans-polar Russian pilot encountered between Tiflis and Sochi: *"The history of EGOROV, according to Mr Efron, is that Egorov was one of the original pilots who made the trans-polar Russian flight to Washington State in 1935 or 36."* ^[extracted]

The bundle does **not** preserve Efron's:

- full pre-1955 résumé;
- Committee role specialty (foreign affairs vs. intelligence vs. armed forces oversight);
- US-citizenship / immigration background;
- the **actual notes** he kept on the trip — only USAIRA's transcribed quotation of relevant fragments.

The 1955 Russell mission's **Committee Consultant** position is not normally a public-record role; Efron does not appear to have been a publicly-named Senate staffer in the way Hathaway (a uniformed Army officer on detail) would have been. ^[inferred]
