# Lonnie Zamora

Officer, **Socorro Police Department**, Socorro, New Mexico. ~5 years on the force as of 24 April 1964. ^[extracted] Resident at **606 Reservoir Street, Socorro, NM**; office phone **835-0941**; home phone **835-1134**; on the **2:00 PM – 10:00 PM shift** at the time of the sighting. ^[extracted] On the afternoon of **24 April 1964**, Zamora became the **single-witness anchor** for one of the most-investigated landing-class UAP encounters in the Project BLUE BOOK era — see [[references/sighting-socorro-nm-1964-04-24]] for the case page and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]] for the FBI Albuquerque report.

## What Zamora reported

In summary (full sequence on the [[references/sighting-socorro-nm-1964-04-24|sighting page]]): ^[extracted]

- ~5:45 PM — broke off a speeding-suspect chase (a "new black Chevrolet" he believed might be *"Floyd Reynolds' boy, Vivian, about 17"*) on **Old Rodeo Street** south of Socorro after hearing a roar and seeing a *"bluish and sort of orange too"* funnel-shaped flame to the SW;
- drove up a steep gravel hill (3 attempts), then sighted an **aluminum-white object** ~150–200 yards south of the road, off-road, **resting on two slanted "legs"** ~3.5 ft above ground;
- saw **two figures in white coveralls** very close to the object — *"appeared normal in shape — but possibly they were small adults or large kids"* — for ~2 seconds; one figure *"seemed to turn and look straight at my car and seemed startled — seemed to quickly jump somewhat"*;
- radioed Sheriff radio operator [[entities/nep-lopez|Nep Lopez]] *"Socorro 2 to Socorro, possible 10-44 (accident)"*;
- heard **2–3 loud "thumps"** *"like someone possibly hammering or shutting a door or doors hard"*;
- heard the **roar resume** + saw the object **rise vertically** with a *"light blue and at bottom … sort of orange color"* flame; the object was **smooth, no windows or doors**, with **red insignia ~2.5 ft × ~2 ft** in the middle (Zamora's hand-drawn pictograph of both shape and insignia is **redacted in the released-tranche FBI copy** ^[ambiguous]);
- ran north, putting his car between him and the object; bumped his leg on the rear fender, glasses fell off; ducked at edge of the hill;
- watched object rise to ~20–25 ft over ~6 sec → transition to **horizontal flight at 10–15 ft AGL** SW-bound, *"very fast"*;
- heard a terminal **sharp tone whine from high to low**, ~1 sec → **complete silence**;
- saw the object clear the **dynamite shack** by ~3 ft, depart over Box Canyon / Six Mile Canyon Mountain;
- radioed Lopez to look for it — *"It looks like a balloon"*;
- drew the **red insignia from memory** before Sgt Chavez arrived;
- found by Sgt M. S. Chavez (NMSP) *"sweating … white, very pale"*; brush burning at the site.

## Witness-character note (from FBI SA Byrnes)

[[entities/d-arthur-byrnes-jr|SA D. Arthur Byrnes Jr.]] (FBI Albuquerque) had known Zamora *"intimately for approximately five years"* and entered an explicit on-record character note in the 8 May 1964 Albuquerque report:

> *"Officer Zamora, known intimately for approximately five years, is well regarded as a sober, industrious, and conscientious officer and not given to fantasy. Officer Zamora was noted to be perfectly sober and somewhat agitated over his experience."* ^[extracted]

Zamora's own self-disclosure: *"Feeling in good health. Last drink — two or three beers — was over a month ago. Noted no odors. Noted no sounds other than described."* ^[extracted] These are **structurally analogous to SA Rathbun's 1957 character note for [[entities/wladyslaw-krasuski|Krasuski]]** (*"no indication of irrational or otherwise abnormal behavior"*) — the FBI's standing investigative habit of an explicit witness-credibility note is consistent across the corpus's 1957 and 1964 cases. ^[inferred]

## Position in the witness corpus

Zamora is the corpus's **first local-LE-as-witness witness** with **federal-LE-as-recording-authority**. ^[inferred] The wiki's [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern|federal-LE-as-witness pattern]] now has a structural sister case: same trained-observer institutional class, but at the **local PD level** rather than federal LE; the recording authority (FBI) is one tier above the witness. The 5-year personal acquaintance between Byrnes and Zamora pre-dates the sighting — Byrnes's character note is **not** a stranger-evaluator note, it is a colleague-of-five-years note, which is a structurally stronger credibility signal than a one-shot interview-impression note. ^[inferred]

Zamora's morphology vocabulary (legs, smooth-no-windows, insignia, white coveralls, transition between vertical and horizontal flight) is **conservative** in the close-encounter genre: ^[inferred] he explicitly hedges his observation of the two figures (*"normal in shape — but possibly they were small adults or large kids"*) and provides time anchoring at sub-second granularity for the close-range sequence. He does **not** characterize the figures as "occupants" or "beings" — only as "two persons" or "two people in white coveralls".
