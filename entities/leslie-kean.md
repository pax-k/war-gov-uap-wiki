# Leslie Kean

San Francisco Bay-area investigative journalist, radio producer, and human-rights advocate who, in 2000–2001, became the **primary English-language vehicle for translating the 1999 [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]] into US and Irish broadsheet readership**. Three appendix-bundled articles in the archived COMETA bundle are by her:

| 3 May 2001 | The Providence Journal | Feature, NARCAP / Haines-centered (with COMETA framing) | *covered inline below* |

Page 93 of the COMETA archival bundle carries her one-page CV — included as appendix material in the same NARA RG 255 file, indicating that the journalist's professional bona fides were considered part of the bundle's authority signal. ^[inferred]

## Professional positioning at time of writing (1998–2001)

Per her one-page CV (page 93 of the [[references/cometa-report-1999|archival bundle]]):

- **Associate / Senior Producer and co-host** of *Flashpoints*, a daily drive-time investigative news magazine on **KPFA** public radio (San Francisco Bay Area), 1998–2001.
- **Director, The Burma Project USA**, 1991 onward — human rights and media advocacy work.
- Bylines across major US, international, and op-ed venues — The Boston Globe, The Baltimore Sun, The Nation, The Progressive, The Sacramento Bee, The Minneapolis Star Tribune, The Providence Journal, The Philadelphia Inquirer, The St. Louis Post Dispatch, The San Francisco Examiner, The Bangkok Post, the International Herald Tribune, the Sydney Morning Herald, the Kyoto Journal, Internazionale (Italy), VSD (France), and the Irish Independent.
- Syndication via **Knight-Ridder, Scripps-Howard, New York Times Wire Service, Pacific News Service**, and the National Newspaper Publishers Association.
- Co-author with Alan Clements of *Burma's Revolution of the Spirit: The Struggle for Democratic Freedom and Dignity* (Aperture, 1994).
- **Fund for Investigative Journalism** grantee 1996, 1997, 1998; **Project Censored Honorable Mention** 1998; **The Nation Institute** grantee 1997; 1996 *Nation* cover-story selected as the magazine's submission for the George Polk Award.

She was, at the time of the COMETA pieces, a credentialed investigative reporter coming to the UAP beat from a Burma-and-human-rights base, not from the pre-existing US ufology scene. ^[inferred] This is structurally important: COMETA's argument that *"sober credentials matter"* is mirrored by the credentials of the journalist who broke it in English.

## The May–June 2000 syndicated COMETA story

The Boston Sunday Globe article on **21 May 2000** ([[references/boston-globe-ufo-2000-05-21]]) was substantially re-run by the Irish Independent on **17 Jun 2000** under the byline "Leslie Kean" with a slightly tightened title (*"UFOs once the preserve of the purely paranoid…"*). Headline, photograph credits (Bernard Thomasi / VSD photo archives), and quoted sources (Edgar Mitchell, Willard Miller, Lord Hill-Norton) are common across both. ^[extracted] This is a syndicated rather than independently reported pair.

The article's structural moves:

1. **Anchor on Area 51 imagery curiosity** (April 2000 commercial-satellite release of Area 51) as a hook for the COMETA story.
2. **Translate COMETA's institutional credentials** for US readers — names Norlain (4-star, former French Tactical Air Force commander, military counselor to the prime minister), Letty (Air Force general, fighter pilot), Lebeau (former CNES head — "the French equivalent of NASA"), and Velasco (SEPRA head). Three-star Admiral Marc Merlo, national police chief Denis Blanchet, and "scientists and weapons engineers" are bundled as additional contributors.
3. **Foreground the ETH conclusion verbatim**: *"the extraterrestrial hypothesis"* is *"the best scientific hypothesis"* and *"strong presumptions exist in its favor."*
4. **Cross-cite US sources** — Apollo 14 astronaut **Edgar Mitchell** ("at a confidence level above 90 percent, that there is reality to all of this"); UK five-star Admiral **Lord Hill-Norton** calling for US congressional hearings; **US Naval Reserve Commander Willard H. Miller** with 30 years in Navy / Joint Interagency operations and previously-undisclosed Pentagon briefings on military UFO policy.
5. **Inventory the US regulatory regime that COMETA criticizes** — AFR 200-2, JANAP 146, the Espionage Act exposure.
6. **The Rockefeller-Clinton briefing** (1995) — philanthropist **Laurance Rockefeller** provided UFO briefing materials to President Clinton, Hillary Clinton, and Presidential science advisor Jack Gibbons at Rockefeller's Wyoming ranch. Clinton tasked **Webster Hubbell** (Associate Attorney General at DOJ) to investigate, per Hubbell's memoir *Friends in High Places* — Hubbell was unable to obtain information. ^[extracted-from-kean] This is a separate event from the 1997 [[references/pocantico-colloquium-1997|Pocantico colloquium]] but the same patron, two years apart. ^[inferred]
7. **Missouri ballot initiative** (March 2000) urging Congress to convene UFO hearings — certified by the secretary of state in March.
8. **March 1997 Arizona triangular-craft wave** ("the Phoenix Lights") — referenced via Sen. John McCain's 2000 statement that it has "never been fully explained."
9. **5 January 2000 St. Claire County, Illinois** — four police officers at separate locations witness a "huge, brightly lighted, triangular craft flying and hovering at 1000 feet"; Scott AFB and FAA report no knowledge. Defense Department search returned no acknowledging records, US District Court Judge Stephen M. McNamee (Phoenix) ruling 30 Mar 2000 that *"a reasonable search was conducted"* even though no information was found.
10. **The FEMA Fire Officer's Guide to Disaster Control, 2nd ed.** — used by FEMA and taught at the seven US universities offering fire-science degrees — warns of *"UFO hazards"* including electrical fields that cause blackouts, force fields, and physiological effects: *"Do not stand under a UFO that is hovering at low altitudes. Do not touch or attempt to touch a UFO that has landed."* ^[extracted-from-kean] First reference in the wiki corpus to a US federal agency authoring an operational UFO-encounter protocol — distinct from intake / reporting regulations.
11. **Closing quote** from **John E. Mack** (Harvard psychiatry professor, Pulitzer Prize-winning author) at a UFO conference at the New York Hall of Science (May 2000): *"No culture from the beginning of time, no culture from anywhere on the planet, has ever evaded the idea of intelligent life other than ourselves… That's arrogance."*

## The May 2001 Providence Journal piece

A **DIFFERENT article**, focused on **[[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA]]** as a frame but anchored to **NARCAP** (National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena, founded **November 2000**) and **Richard F. Haines's** October 2000 study *"Aviation Safety in America — A Previously Neglected Factor"*.

Key new corpus-relevant points:

- **Richard F. Haines** — formerly NASA's **chief of the Space Human Factors Office** at NASA-Ames Research Center; Raytheon contract scientist; chief scientist for NARCAP. ^[extracted-from-kean]
- Haines's study compiles **over 3,400 aviation-related UAP cases**, including **100+ pilot/crew reports** that *"appear to have compromised aviation safety"*, with **56 near-misses**.
- Named pilot encounters:
  - **TWA Capt. Phil Schultz** (1981, over Lake Michigan) — *"large, round, silver metal object"* with dark portholes equally spaced around the circumference, *"descended into the atmosphere from above"*; high-speed evasive turn.
  - **Japan Airlines 747 Capt. Kenju Terauchi** (1986, over Alaska) — *"two space ships stopped in front of our face, shooting off lights… inside cockpit cabined brightly and I felt warm in the face"*. Grounded by JAL for speaking out despite FAA stability determination.
  - **Swissair Boeing 747** (1997, over Long Island) — near-miss with glowing white cylindrical object; pilot Philip Bober (FAA Civil Aviation Security Office memorandum).
  - **United Airlines Capt. Neil Daniels** — DC-10 forced into a left turn by magnetic interference from a UAP. ("The energies out there are absolutely profound.")
- **John Callahan**, former division chief of the Accidents and Investigations Branch of the FAA, scheduled to disclose **FAA documentation and subsequent CIA suppression of the Terauchi encounter** at a **National Press Club briefing** (Wednesday following 3 May 2001). Callahan would be joined by *"more than 20 other government and military witnesses, and dozens more on videotape"*. ^[extracted-from-kean] The handwritten Renaissance Hotel note from [[entities/carol-rosin|Carol Rosin]] referencing *"P 62 — John Callahan's testimony"* almost certainly refers to Callahan's testimony in [[entities/marie-galbraith-briefing-document-1995|Marie Galbraith's *Briefing Document — The Best Available Evidence* (1995)]] which has the matching pagination. ^[inferred — bundle composition] The May 2001 Press Club briefing was the institutional follow-on to that 1995 document's witness-disclosure thread.
- **Jim McClenahan** — recently retired FAA air-traffic-control specialist, NARCAP technical adviser.
- **Brian E. Smith** — current head of the Aviation Safety Program at NASA-Ames Research Center; supports Haines's study.
- **General Ricardo Bermúdez Sanhueza** — president of Chile's **CEFAA** (Committee for the Study of Anomalous Aerial Phenomena), formed in 1997 following publicly acknowledged Chilean-airport UFO observations. CEFAA and the French group both made "overtures to the US government for cooperation… with no response."
- **Barnaul Siberia airport closure** (27 Jan 2001) is cited in the article opening — referenced via Agence France Presse via the [[references/sighting-russia-airport-2001-01-27|Interfax-sourced 27 January 2001 closure]]. ^[extracted]

## Why this entity matters to the wiki

- **First journalist in the corpus** treated as a substantive entity rather than incidental. Almost every prior wiki witness/journalist appearance was as a tertiary attribution; Kean's three-article COMETA campaign is the corpus's clearest case of a single journalist driving the US-language public reception of a UAP-policy artifact.
- **Cross-cutting hub**: her articles attest the **Mitchell**, **Hill-Norton**, **Miller**, **Rockefeller-Clinton 1995**, **Haines/NARCAP**, **Callahan**, **CEFAA / Bermúdez**, **Phoenix Lights 1997**, and **FEMA Fire Officer's Guide** threads — all in two pieces. Most of these are not yet anchored to dedicated pages, so the Kean entity functions as the corpus's initial **placeholder hub** for those threads. ^[inferred]
- **Bridges COMETA-the-French-artifact to the US disclosure movement** of the early 2000s — without Kean's syndication, COMETA had limited US-press reach. The COMETA file's NARA RG 255 routing (via Rosin to Goldin) is the **insider channel**; the Boston Globe syndication is the **public channel**. Both go through the same source documents.
