# 1st Lt Russell J. Carey Jr.

**1st Lieutenant, United States Air Force**, **Base Intelligence Officer**, **331st AFBU (Air Force Base Unit), Reserve Training, Stout Field, Indianapolis IN**, in late July 1948. Carey signs as **"Russell J. Carey, Jr. / 1st Lt., USAF / Intel Officer"** on the morphology sketches and location diagrams attached to two Project SIGN incident files. ^[extracted]

Carey is the **only AMC field-intel officer** in the [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-101-172|101–172 bundle]] who personally signs hand-drawn morphology illustrations of witness-reported objects. ^[inferred]

## Why Carey matters for the wiki

### 1. Field-intel artifact production at the AFBU level

The Carey sketches are the **earliest preserved hand-signed officer-drawn morphology illustrations** from AMC's field-intel cadre in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] They evidence the production of **visual-record material at the local AFBU level**, not just at AMC HQ T-2 in Dayton. The artifacts confirm a methodology of **same-day callout + on-site witness validation + officer-drawn sketch under signature** as an intake-pipeline step.

### 2. Two-window sight-line verification

Carey's investigation of [[references/sighting-indianapolis-1948-07|Inc 152 Swigert]] documents a **two-window sight-line cross-check** as a witness-validation method — verifying that both Mr Swigert (bathroom window W-facing) and Mrs Swigert (kitchen window S-facing) had unobstructed views consistent with an east-bound trajectory at the altitude reported. ^[extracted]

This is the wiki's earliest preserved instance of **a Project SIGN field-intel officer making a same-day callout to a civilian residence to verify sight-lines as part of intake validation**. ^[inferred]

### 3. Connection to the broader Carey family

The case files spell the surname twice — once as **"Carey"** and once as **"Carye"** (in the typewritten name above the signature on the Inc 152 sketch). The signature itself reads **"Carey"** consistently. The "Carye" appears to be a typist's transcription error. ^[ambiguous]

Carey's middle initial is consistently "J." and the suffix "Jr." indicates a junior. The senior Russell J. Carey is not identified in the source. ^[ambiguous]
