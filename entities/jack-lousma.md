# Jack R. Lousma

**Jack Robert Lousma** (b. 1936) — **US Marine Corps naval aviator** and **NASA Astronaut Group 5** ("the Original Nineteen", 1966 — same group as **[[entities/paul-weitz|Paul Weitz]]**), Pilot of **[[entities/skylab|Skylab 3]]** (28 Jul – 25 Sep 1973). ^[inferred]

In the wiki's UAP corpus, Lousma enters as the **initial spotter** of the **[[references/sighting-skylab-3-red-object-1973-09|SL-3 multi-witness "red object" sighting]]** (*"Jack first noticed this rather large red star out the wardroom window"* — [[entities/owen-garriott|Garriott]]). ^[extracted] Lousma also provides the corpus's most direct phrasing of the **morphology-unresolved characterization**: *"it never did take the shape of an object but it was always brighter than any other star or planet in the night sky."* ^[extracted]

## SL-3 d7 testimony

Lousma's contributions across §7 and §20.0:

> **LOUSMA**: *"You might point out that it never did take the shape of an object but it was always brighter than any other star or planet in the night sky. It was much brighter."* ^[extracted]

> **LOUSMA**: *"In doing T002, I had on other occasions, at least once or twice, seen other satellites although they appeared as star points of light."* ^[extracted]

> **LOUSMA** *(opening §20.0)*: *"Let's talk about visual sightings. Any comments on countdown. You saw your way to the booster or you didn't see that. Visual sighting no problem there."* ^[extracted]

> **LOUSMA** *(§20.0)*: *"I saw a couple of satellites that appeared like a satellite would on the Earth. I saw one that was not like one you would see on Earth, so why don't you mention it."* ^[extracted]

> **LOUSMA** *(§20.0)*: *"Powered Flight: I watched the booster protector cover go off and lots of flashes and debris and everything in every separation, but that's all normal. During orbit: -"* ^[extracted]

The substantive content for the corpus:

1. **Initial spotter / acquisition phenomenology** — Lousma first noticed the red object from the wardroom window. ^[extracted] His initial description is *"a large red star"* — i.e., **acquired as a stellar-class point source, not as a structured body** — consistent with the body being beyond the eye's resolution limit even at the closest range estimate. ^[inferred]
2. **Morphology-unresolved characterization** — *"it never did take the shape of an object"* is the corpus's **cleanest single-line statement that the morphology is unresolved**. ^[extracted] This is the central evidence for placing the SL-3 red object in **sub-class F (morphology-unresolved)** rather than **sub-class E (morphology-resolved)** — see [[concepts/orbital-uap-sighting]].
3. **Brightness comparator** — *"always brighter than any other star or planet in the night sky. It was much brighter"* — corroborates [[entities/owen-garriott|Garriott]]'s *"much brighter than Jupiter or any of the other planets"*. ^[extracted] Independent witness corroboration of the brightness anomaly.
4. **T002-context comparator** — Lousma's reference to **T002** (a Skylab navigation experiment using a hand-held sextant) ^[inferred] establishes that he had **separately observed at least one or two other satellites that appeared as star points of light** under similar viewing conditions. ^[extracted] This is **direct empirical confirmation** that Lousma had a baseline phenomenology for **identified** co-orbital satellites and that the SL-3 red object **diverged from that baseline** in brightness and color. ^[inferred]
5. **Direct anomaly framing** — *"I saw one that was not like one you would see on Earth"* — Lousma is the corpus witness who **most directly distinguishes the red object from his prior satellite observations**. ^[extracted] The framing is **astronaut-spontaneous + structurally explicit anomaly identification** — different in tone from the [[entities/owen-garriott|Garriott]]-style scientific-curiosity framing.

## Witness pedigree

Lousma is the corpus's **first USMC astronaut witness** — distinct from the prior corpus's USN naval aviators ([[entities/jim-lovell|Lovell]], [[entities/pete-conrad|Conrad]], [[entities/dick-gordon|Gordon]], [[entities/alan-bean|Bean]], [[entities/gene-cernan|Cernan]], [[entities/ronald-evans|Evans]]) and USAF officers ([[entities/buzz-aldrin|Aldrin]], [[entities/michael-collins|Collins]], [[entities/frank-borman|Borman]]). His **Marine Corps aviation training** is structurally relevant to the SL-3 testimony's terse anomaly-framing language (*"not like one you would see on Earth"*) — his framing is more directly assertive than Garriott's scientist-class request-for-explanation style. ^[inferred] The two SL-3 witnesses' complementary framings strengthen the observation record: Lousma's anomaly framing + Garriott's quantitative analysis = both ends of the witness-pedigree spectrum on the same observation. ^[inferred]
