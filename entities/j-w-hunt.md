# J. W. Hunt / A. Hunt

**This is an open-question stub.** A name appears on the cover-routing slip of the 1963 [[references/hunter-1963-space-alien-race-memo|NASC "Space Alien Race" memo]] that is not resolvable from the OCR alone. ^[ambiguous]

## What the source shows

- **Page 1** of the memo bundle is a routing slip listing **`From - J. W. Hunt`** with a **`(signed) A. Hunt July 22, 1963`** signature line.
- **Page 6** of the substantive memo is signed **`Maxwell W. Hunter, II`** (a known [[entities/national-aeronautics-and-space-council|NASC]] Professional Staff member — see [[entities/maxwell-w-hunter]]).

## Candidate interpretations

Three candidates are open and not resolvable from the source: ^[ambiguous]

1. **OCR misread of "M. W. Hunter"** — the substantive author's name distorted by Mistral OCR through a faded cover slip. Plausible mechanically (`M.W.HUNTER` → `J.W.HUNT` is one or two character substitutions), and consistent with the 22 July 1963 date being four days after the memo's 18 July 1963 effective date — a plausible cover-slip stamp date for an internal NASC review. ^[inferred]
2. **A separate routing-slip endorsement** — a different NASC or NASC-recipient staffer named "Hunt" who initialed the cover slip on receipt. The corpus does not surface this person elsewhere. ^[inferred]
3. **A different staffer entirely** — clerical, secretarial, or routing staff at NASC or at the [[entities/robert-f-packard|OISA]] / State recipient end. No corroboration in the corpus. ^[inferred]

The stub exists because the OCR ambiguity is on the cover slip — not on the substantive memo body, whose authorship is not in question (Hunter II signs page 6).

## Why this matters

The OCR ambiguity is preserved here rather than silently resolved because:
- "Hunter II" is the wiki's only NASC staffer entity to date; reading "J. W. Hunt" as a separate person would imply a second NASC node not visible elsewhere in the corpus.
- A future re-OCR pass against a higher-resolution scan, or against another copy of the same NASC `SP 16` document, would resolve the question.
