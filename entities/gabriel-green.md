# Gabriel Green

A **1960s American contactee figure**, founder/president of [[entities/amalgamated-flying-saucer-clubs-of-america|AFSCA]] (Amalgamated Flying Saucer Clubs of America, Inc.), and editor (with Helen Green as Asst. Ed.) of AFSCA's quarterly magazine *Flying Saucers International* (formerly *UFO International*). ^[extracted]

Green operates AFSCA out of his home at **2004 N. Hoover St., Los Angeles, CA 90027** *"to cut down on the overhead"*. ^[extracted] In a 12 Apr 1964 / 25 Mar 1966 Houston-paper interview reproduced in AFSCA's July 1966 issue, Green describes himself as *"a serious young man"* (per the *NY Times Service* convention coverage) and the *"head saucerer"*. ^[extracted]

Green enters the wiki corpus through **[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 serial 449]]** — the October 1966 LA SAC → Director memo that filed AFSCA's July 1966 magazine, of which Green is editor and the named president. He is also a quoted figure in two reproduced press clippings inside the same magazine: ^[extracted]

- The **WSJ** "New 'Fireball' Raises an Old Question" piece (Carlson, 13 Dec 1965), which quotes Green explaining that *"space people give contactless information about life on other planets and solutions to insurmountable problems on our planet"*, that *"one suggestion allegedly made to Mr. Green in 1965 by a visit from Alpha Centauri: Run for President as a write-in candidate"*. ^[extracted]
- The **Harold Scarlett "Who's in All Those Saucers?" interview** (Houston, ~12 Apr 1964 / re-run 25 Mar 1966), in which Green tells the journalist he met *"a Martian … wore a short-sleeve, shimmery, metallic-looking shirt which he said would stop bullets"*, plus *"a four-foot man from the Alpha Centauri star system"* and other contactee figures. ^[extracted]

## Stated claims

The Green-attributed material in the artifact is **wholly Green's testimony as a contactee** ^[extracted]; the FBI itself takes no position on any of it. ^[inferred] Notable Green claims, paraphrased from the artifact's interviews ^[extracted]:

- He has met *"five or six"* members of extraterrestrial spacecraft crews around Los Angeles over the years. ^[extracted]
- He has *"sighted 85 to 100 spacecraft"* around Los Angeles. ^[extracted]
- AFSCA has clubs *"in 50 major U.S. cities"*. ^[extracted]
- The Martian he met explained that *"the Martian canals were really canals for distributing the short water supply into their polar regions"* and that the two Martian moons are *"artificial satellites just like the Russian astronomers have supposed"*. ^[extracted]
- Green ran briefly as a **1960 / 1965 write-in presidential candidate on a "flying saucer" / "Space People" ticket** ^[extracted] (the WSJ reports a 1965 visit from Alpha Centauri suggesting the candidacy; the *NY Times Service* convention coverage describes a separate "presidential candidate campaigning on the flying saucer ticket" ^[extracted] — chronology and party-of-record not anchored from this single artifact ^[ambiguous]).
- Green attributes JFK's assassination to *"the Military Industrial octopus"* via the Korendor channelled message reproduced as the magazine's lead article (which is **Renaud-via-Retan-channelled**, not Green-authored, but Green publishes it). ^[extracted]

These are **the contactee genre's canonical claims** and are presented in the artifact without external evidence or corroboration. ^[inferred]
