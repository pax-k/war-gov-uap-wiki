# Borderland Sciences Research Associates (BSRA)

A **civilian saucer-research organization** founded ~1945 in **San Diego, California** by **[[entities/n-meade-layne|N. Meade Layne]]** (publisher of *Round Robin* journal). ^[inferred] The earliest **contactee-adjacent** organization documented in the FBI corpus. ^[inferred] Captured in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 7]] serials 311-312 (pages 22, 32-33) as 5 Aug 1952 + 18 Aug 1952 SAC San Diego JFS → Director memos. ^[extracted]

## Officers

Per the 5 Aug 1952 SAC San Diego memo: ^[extracted]

| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| **Director** | Mead Layne (also published as **N. Meade Layne**, *Round Robin* founder; cf. [[entities/n-meade-layne]]) |
| **Director (co-)** | Max Freedom Long (Huna religion / Hawaii esoterics) |

Mailing address (per IFSB *Space Review* Jan 1953 directory): **3524 Adams Avenue, San Diego 16, California**. ^[extracted]

## What the FBI documented

### The 5 Aug 1952 SAC San Diego memo (serial 311 area)

**Francis Ohm** (operator of Businessmens Assurance Company in San Diego, **office in same building as the FBI San Diego field office**) calls SAC San Diego on 1 Aug 1952 *"urgently"* requesting a stenographer attend a 1:00 PM BSRA meeting that afternoon. ^[extracted]

Ohm provides the SAC with literature and reports the following BSRA claims: ^[extracted]

> Through the means utilized by his organization (which the enclosed literature will reflect borders on the occult), they were able to prognosticate well in advance the recent earthquake which occurred in Tehachapi, California. He advised that they have also been able to obtain the following information:
>
> 1. That a very severe tidal wave will hit the Pacific Ocean sometime in the not too remote future and that most of the Japanese Islands will be washed away and disappear.
>
> 2. That all fishes with scales in Japanese waters will soon leave these waters for other destinations, in view of the oncoming tidal wave.
>
> 3. That this tidal wave will wash away the Hawaiian Islands.
>
> 4. That the West Coast of the United States will likewise be affected by this tidal wave.
>
> 5. That they prognosticated the appearance of a new island in the Pacific which was published in the newspapers within the past week.
>
> 6. That the flying saucers are not fantasies; that they are factual and actual; that his associates in the captioned organization have been in conversation with the men operating the flying saucers, and that they have asked that high officials of the U. S. Government be informed that they are here on our planet on a peaceful mission and that they are not on a warlike mission. However, if the U. S. Government continues to send planes after these flying saucers and if these planes fire on the flying saucers, they have disintegrators which they will utilize and which will disintegrate these planes completely in no time flat.

The SAC declines to attend or send a stenographer. ^[extracted] Ohm offers to advise of important developments; SAC files the literature. ^[extracted]

### The 18 Aug 1952 SAC San Diego memo (serial 312)

A follow-up memo enclosing additional BSRA literature: ^[extracted]

> 1. Mimeographed letter consisting of four pages addressed to His Excellency, The President of the United States.
> 2. Mimeographed announcement consisting of three pages entitled "For Information of the Public — and the Sake of the Record — A Synopsis of Important Data".

I.e. by mid-Aug 1952, BSRA is **drafting open letters to the US President** as an institutional posture. ^[inferred]

## Position of FBI San Diego SAC

The SAC explicitly declines investigation: ^[extracted]

> I should like to point out that OHM appears to be a perfectly sane, sound individual; he operates a very large insurance business in San Diego, and is convinced in his own mind of the efficacy of this organization.
>
> No investigation is being conducted concerning this matter and I do not contemplate attending any meetings of this organization.

I.e. the FBI's posture toward BSRA is **observe-only, no investigation, no engagement** — consistent with the post-[[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|BB-57]] passive-intake-only protocol. ^[inferred]

## Continuity from 1947

[[references/round-robin-flying-roll-1947|Layne's 9-point memo]] of 1947 (filed in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-1|62-HQ-83894 section 1]] serials 1-area) is the **earliest BSRA-affiliated wiki document** ^[inferred] — the 1952 reappearance in section 7 demonstrates **organizational continuity across 5 years**. ^[inferred]

The 1947 9-point memo posited saucers as **"etheric" / inter-dimensional craft** ^[extracted] — and the 1952 BSRA Ohm narrative extends this with **"in conversation with the men operating the flying saucers"** ^[extracted]. The conversational-contact narrative is **structurally novel** in section 7 vs Layne's 1947 framing. ^[inferred] The shift between 1947 (etheric craft) and 1952 (conversation with operators) tracks the **broader contactee-narrative crystallization** documented in [[concepts/contactee-movement-1952-1954]]. ^[inferred]

## What BSRA pioneers in the contactee narrative

The 1952 BSRA narrative establishes several **structurally novel** features that recur in subsequent contactee literature ^[inferred]:

| Feature | First in BSRA 1952 |
|---|---|
| **Direct conversation with saucer operators** | YES (Ohm channel via "associates") |
| **Peaceful-mission framing** | YES ("they are here on our planet on a peaceful mission") |
| **Conditional retaliation threat** | YES ("disintegrators … will disintegrate these planes completely") |
| **Communication-with-government request** | YES ("high officials of the U. S. Government be informed") |
| **Apocalyptic prophecy ecology** | YES (tidal waves, Japanese Islands, Hawaii, fish migration) |
| **Earthquake prediction** | YES (Tehachapi CA prediction credited as already verified) |
| **Pacific-island appearance prediction** | YES (claimed verified) |

The pattern is **structurally aligned with mid-20th-century New Age + Theosophist frames** ^[inferred] — Layne's *Round Robin* journal is editorially **Theosophical-adjacent**. ^[inferred] BSRA + IFSB + Bethurum + Adamski form a **multi-organizational network** in which contactee narratives propagate through magazines, lectures, and interpersonal correspondence ^[inferred] — see [[concepts/contactee-movement-1952-1954]].

## Cross-reference to IFSB

The IFSB's January 1953 *Space Review* (serial 326) advertises BSRA: ^[extracted]

> BORDERLAND SCIENCES RESEARCH ASSOCIATES located at 3524 Adams Ave., San Diego 16, California, would like to have IFSB members join their society.

I.e. **BSRA + IFSB are linked organizations** by Jan 1953 ^[extracted] — the contactee-network is **deliberately cross-promotional**. ^[inferred]
