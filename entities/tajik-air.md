# Tajik Air

The Tajikistan flag carrier whose **Boeing 747SP** was the platform for the 27 Jan 1994 UAP encounter reported in [[references/dos-cable-kazakhstan-1994-01|DUSHANBE 00259]]. The cable establishes three things about Tajik Air in early 1994 that are otherwise unusual for a Central-Asian-successor carrier of that era:

1. The airline was operating a **Boeing 747SP** (a Western wide-body) — distinct from the Tu-154 / Yak-42 fleet typical of post-Soviet Central Asian carriers.
2. The chief pilot — **Capt Ed Rhodes** — and at least **two of his fellow line pilots** were **American citizens** (`AMCIT`).
3. All three American pilots had previous careers flying passenger aircraft for **PanAm** before joining Tajik Air. ^[inferred] (PanAm shut down in December 1991, ~25 months before the cable; many of its pilots took foreign-airline contracts in 1992–94. ^[inferred])

The cable is the only artifact about Tajik Air in the corpus. Everything beyond the cable's explicit statements (fleet composition, American-pilot recruitment, route structure, ownership) is **outside the corpus** and should be treated as `^[inferred]` or `^[open]` when written down here.

## Role in the 1994 cable

- The 747SP was at **41,000 ft** at the time of the initial encounter, position **45°N 55°E** (over Kazakhstan, north of the Caspian / Aral basin).
- The aircraft was making **>500 knots** during the contrail follow-on observation 45 minutes later.
- The flight deck supplied **three** corroborating witnesses, all American, all ex-PanAm.

## Cross-references

- Comparable in role to [[references/sighting-papua-new-guinea-1985-01-24|Air Niugini]] in the 1985 PNG cable — both are foreign commercial carriers whose cockpit observations reach a US embassy. The difference: Air Niugini's pilot was a *foreign* witness whose report was *relayed via PNG NIO*; Tajik Air's flight deck is *American* witnesses approaching the US embassy *directly*.
- Comparable in role to **Northwest Airlines** in the 30 Jul 1949 [[references/sighting-portland-or-1949-07|Portland OR sighting]] (NWA Capt Thrush, M-202) — but again the 1949 case is intra-US.
