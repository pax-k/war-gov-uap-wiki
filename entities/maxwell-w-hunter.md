# Maxwell W. Hunter, II

Listed in the signature block of the [[references/hunter-1963-space-alien-race-memo|18 July 1963 NASC memo]] as **"Maxwell W. Hunter, II — Member, Professional Staff."** That places him on the staff of the [[entities/national-aeronautics-and-space-council|National Aeronautics and Space Council]] (Executive Office of the President) in mid-1963.

## Extracted from the source

- Authored the memo **"Thoughts on the Space Alien Race Question"** (file marker SP 16, 18 Jul 1963, OFFICIAL USE ONLY) addressed to [[entities/robert-f-packard|Robert F. Packard]] of State/OISA.
- Self-positions on the alien-contact question as **neither a "flying saucer advocate" nor a strict scientific-impossibility partisan** — he finds both frames "disturbing."
- Demonstrates working command of mid-1963 astronautics — quotes specific escape and braking velocities (Mars 16,500 fps, lunar braking under 10,000 fps), payload-fraction rough numbers for Mars→Moon trajectories, and casual fluency in chemical-rocket vs. nuclear-rocket trade space.
- References then-current observations (crater **Alphonsus** outgassing, lunar IR hot spots, **Lunik III** lunar farside imagery, **Mariner II** Venus data, the radio-SETI **Ozma** project) and the "Canali" tradition.
- Distinguishes three policy-relevant classes of contactable alien race: chemical-rocket, nuclear-class, and Einstein-violating. See [[concepts/three-class-alien-taxonomy]].

## Inferred / contextual

- The Maxwell W. Hunter II of mid-1963 NASC staff is plausibly the same Maxwell W. Hunter II later associated with **Lockheed missiles/SDI-era space-launch advocacy** ("Thrust Into Space," 1966), but this identification is not made in the source itself and should not be asserted from this memo alone. ^[inferred]
- His tone in this memo is **half-policy, half-thought-experiment** — comfortable with explicit speculation flagged as such, while clearly directing the conclusion at a State Department reader. ^[inferred]

## Role in the corpus

Hunter is the wiki's first cataloged **policy-side** UAP-adjacent author — a counterweight to the operational/intelligence figures (e.g. [[entities/c-p-cabell|C. P. Cabell]]) who dominate the 1948 [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] material. The two figures inhabit different institutional tracks; see [[synthesis/early-uap-policy-vs-operational-track]].
