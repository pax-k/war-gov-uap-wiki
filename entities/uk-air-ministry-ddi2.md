# UK Air Ministry — DDI2 / A.D.I.(K)

Two interlinked WWII Air Ministry intelligence branches that handled the British-side response to the [[entities/shaef-air-staff|SHAEF]] foo-fighter / "[[concepts/balls-of-fire-red|Balls of Fire — Red]]" routing:

## DDI2 — Deputy Directorate of Intelligence (Foreign)

The Air Ministry's branch responsible for foreign air-intelligence assessment and enemy-aircraft identification. ^[inferred]

In the [[references/foo-fighters-shaef-1944-1945|SHAEF file]], DDI2 issued the **13 March 1945 reply** to A/Cmdr [[entities/c-m-grierson|Grierson]]'s 11 February 1945 routing memo. The reply, signed **Group Captain E. D. M. Hopkins**, carried the file references **111/45/DDI2** and **6c A 37153**, under the subject **"BALLS OF FIRE - RED"**. ^[extracted]

The substantive reply (in full):

> *"The papers dealing with the above subject which you enclosed with your memo SHAEF Air/TS.37153/A2 dated 11th February, have been carefully examined and discussed with the various other Departments concerned.*
>
> *Bomber Command crews have for some time been reporting similar phenomena. A few of the alleged aircraft may have been Me. 262 and for the rest, flak rockets are suggested as the most likely explanation.*
>
> *The whole affair is still something of a mystery and the evidence is very sketchy and varied so that no definite and satisfactory explanation can yet be given."* ^[extracted]

DDI2 thus confirmed two important things on the institutional record: (a) **RAF Bomber Command** had been independently reporting similar phenomena, and (b) the Air Ministry could not explain them. ^[extracted]

## A.D.I.(K) — Air Intelligence Directorate (K)

The Air Ministry branch responsible for **interrogation of captured Luftwaffe personnel and exploitation of captured German air materiel**. ^[inferred]

A.D.I.(K)'s 17 February 1945 reply to Grierson, signed **W/Cdr S. D. Felkin**, was a single paragraph offering the **[[concepts/flak-bombe-hypothesis|Flak Bombe candidate explanation]]**: ^[extracted]

> *"this probably refers to 'Flak Bombe' mentioned in A.D.I.(K) Report No. 562/1944, paragraphs 128 and 129."*

A.D.I.(K) Report 562/1944 is **not in the file**. ^[extracted] If a declassified copy exists, the wiki should ingest it.

## Relationship between the two branches

DDI2 (foreign air intelligence) and A.D.I.(K) (POW interrogation) were sister directorates within the Air Ministry's intelligence apparatus. The 1945 reply chain — A.D.I.(K) reaches into its POW-interrogation backlog for a candidate weapon system; DDI2 reaches into the body of cross-source reports for a pattern-level conclusion — captures the **two distinct evidential channels** Air Ministry intelligence brought to the foo-fighter problem. ^[inferred]

DDI2 did not pick up A.D.I.(K)'s Flak Bombe identification in its later reply, suggesting either disagreement or that the Flak Bombe covered only a sub-class of cases (most plausibly the cylindrical-object class — see [[references/sighting-cylindrical-object-vicinity-f5710-1945-03-01]]). ^[inferred]

## Routing context

Other Air Ministry traffic in the file (4–5 March 1945) shows DDI2 also coordinating with USSTAF on the **bomb torpedoes recovered from the Trippelwerke at Molsheim** (sent to Hornchurch) — illustrating that the same DDI2 staff handling the foo-fighter file were simultaneously running enemy-armaments collection. ^[extracted]

## Place in the larger UAP picture

DDI2 is the **first national-level intelligence agency** in the wiki to formally state, in writing, that the unexplained-aerial-phenomena reports it had collected could not be explained — a posture that prefigures the formal AARO admission of unresolved cases nearly 80 years later. ^[inferred]
