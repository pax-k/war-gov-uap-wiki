# Nep Lopez

**Sheriff radio operator** at the **Socorro, New Mexico** State Police / Sheriff's office on the late afternoon of **24 April 1964**. ^[extracted] Lopez is the **first non-witness party** in the communications chain of the [[references/sighting-socorro-nm-1964-04-24|Socorro NM landing-class UAP case]] — receiving [[entities/lonnie-zamora|Officer Lonnie Zamora]]'s radio call at ~5:45 PM and being **physically co-located** with FBI [[entities/d-arthur-byrnes-jr|SA D. Arthur Byrnes Jr.]] *"thirty feet down the hall"* at the time of the call (per Byrnes's 8 May 1964 Albuquerque-letterhead investigative narrative — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]]). ^[extracted]

## What the wiki has on him

The wiki's primary-source record of Lopez consists of two communications exchanges with Zamora during the active sighting, reproduced verbatim in Zamora's witness statement and Byrnes's report: ^[extracted]

- **~5:48 PM** — Zamora's initial call: *"Socorro 2 to Socorro, possible 10-44 (accident); I'll be 10-6 (busy) out of the car checking the car down in the arroyo."*
- **shortly after** — Zamora's follow-up after the object's departure: *"It looks like a balloon."*

Lopez relayed both to **Sgt M. S. Chavez (NMSP)**, who arrived on scene and found Zamora *"sweating … white, very pale"*; brush burning at the site. ^[extracted]

## Significance for the corpus

- **First UAP-event radio operator in the wiki.** Lopez is a **non-witness procedural intermediary** in the Socorro communications chain — the wiki's first such role at this granularity. ^[inferred]
- **Concurrent placement with FBI agent.** The fact that FBI SA Byrnes was *"thirty feet down the hall"* at the State Police office at the moment of Zamora's radio call to Lopez is the structural fact that makes the Socorro case the corpus's **first FBI same-day on-site agent presence** — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]] and [[entities/d-arthur-byrnes-jr]] for the consequences for the corpus's FBI-instrument table.

The two roles are likely co-located in a small-town shared radio room. ^[inferred]
