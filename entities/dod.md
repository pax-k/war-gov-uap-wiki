# Department of Defense (DoD)

The **United States Department of Defense** — executive-branch military umbrella established **18 September 1947** by the National Security Act of 1947 (originally as the National Military Establishment; renamed DoD in 1949) ^[inferred — general historical background; not stated in the wiki's sources]. Headquartered at the Pentagon, Arlington VA.

This page is a **stub hub**. The wiki's primary-source coverage of DoD is concentrated in service-level pages ([[entities/usaf]], Navy, etc.) and in DoD-level offices that appear in specific source documents — see those for substantive content. This page exists primarily so the high-volume incoming wikilinks resolve.

## Role in the wiki's UAP corpus

DoD surfaces across the corpus through several institutional layers and time periods:

- **DOD-OPI / Office of Public Information (1952 era)** — [[entities/albert-chop|Albert Chop]] served as the DoD-OPI press-relations gatekeeper for AF UAP information during the 1952 Washington saucer-flap window — see [[references/usaf-flying-saucers-press-fact-sheet-1952]]. ^[extracted]
- **DOPSR — Office of Prepublication and Security Review (2020s era)** — the **DoD Office of Prepublication and Security Review** is the clearance authority cited in [[references/dow-uap-pr20-prepublication-clearance-2026-03]]. ^[inferred]
- **AARO — All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (2022– )** — [[entities/aaro]] is a DoD-affiliated office responsible for UAP reporting/assessment across all operating domains. ^[inferred]
- **Service-level activity** — DoD is the parent over [[entities/usaf]] (1947– ), the **US Navy**, the **US Army**, etc. The wiki's institutional-actor coverage is concentrated at the service level rather than DoD-umbrella level. (Navy and Army entity pages not yet created.)

## Significance for the corpus

- DoD is the **policy-umbrella** under which every service-level UAP project the wiki tracks (Project SIGN → Project GRUDGE → Project BLUE BOOK → contemporary AARO) has operated. ^[inferred]
- The **DOD-OPI press-relations layer** (Chop / 1952) and the **DOPSR prepublication layer** (2020s) bracket the wiki's coverage of DoD's public-facing UAP information-control function. ^[inferred]
- The **DoD-affiliated AARO office** is the contemporary node where current UAP-incident assessment and post-hoc instrumented measurement is centralized. ^[inferred]
