# AFBIR-CO

The **AAF Pentagon-level analytical office** identified on the 30 Jul 1947 18-sighting analytical document at [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|62-HQ-83894 serial 130]] OCR p. 73 ^[extracted]:

> CONFIDENTIAL / 62-83894-130 / FLYING DISCS / 30 July 1947
> For purposes of analysis by **AFBIR-CO**, eighteen reported sightings of "Flying Discs" were selected for breakdown into detailed particulars.

The same identifier appears on the **Browning sworn statement** in the same packet ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|serial 130]] OCR p. 111) ^[extracted]:

> Received **AFBIR-CO** 16 July 47

confirming AFBIR-CO is the **organizational receiver** of AAF disc-witness sworn statements arriving at HQ. ^[inferred]

## Acronym decomposition

The most economical decomposition: ^[inferred]

- **AF** — AAF (Army Air Forces)
- **BIR** — Brigade Intelligence Requirements (the AC/AS-2 BIR Branch)
- **CO** — Collection (Branch suffix at AAF AC/AS-2 BIR)

Full reading: **AAF AC/AS-2 BIR Collection Branch**, AAF HQ Pentagon, 1947. ^[inferred] The **Collection Branch suffix is consistent with NRL Mellen's 7 Aug 1947 memo TO the "Collection Branch, AAF, Washington 25, D.C."** ^[extracted] in the same serial — confirming the addressee designation.

## Function

AFBIR-CO is the AAF HQ-level **statistical-analytical office** for the 1947 flying-disc collection regime. ^[inferred] Its observed functions: ^[extracted]

1. **Receive sworn AAF-officer statements** on disc sightings from across the AAF intelligence chain. (Browning B-25 statement received 16 Jul 1947 same-day as deposed.)
2. **Receive Air Inspector / Office reports** on disc sightings.
3. **Direct interview requests via NRL Army Liaison Office** — Mellen's 18 Jul 1947 + 28 Jul 1947 + 7 Aug 1947 memos to AC/AS-2 Collection Branch are explicitly performed *"at the request of Lt. Col. G. D. Garrett, AAF A-2"* ^[extracted] — i.e. AFBIR-CO operates **directly under the [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett A-2 line]]**.
4. **Compile and analyze** received reports against a 21-field breakdown schema (date, hour, location, observer occupation, ground-or-air, number, altitude, direction, speed, distance, time-in-sight, deviation, color, size, shape, sound, trail, weather, manner-of-disappearance, remarks). See [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30]].
5. **Produce analytical priors** that feed downstream into the AAF intelligence regime — particularly the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|Garrett-Reynolds-Schulgen cooperation regime]] and ultimately Schulgen's 5 Sep 1947 formal denial letter to Hoover.

## Position in AAF intelligence chain

The relationship in mid-1947: ^[inferred]

```
AAF AC/AS-2 (Counter Intelligence Branch + Collection Branch)
├── BIR (Brigade Intelligence Requirements)
│   └── Collection Branch (AFBIR-CO) ← 30 Jul 1947 18-sighting analysis lives here
│       ├── receives: AAF-officer sworn statements (Browning, Burniston, Kayko et al.)
│       ├── receives: NRL ALO interview reports (Zohn, Rockwood, Cole)
│       ├── receives: 4AF / 14th AF / 15th AF / TAC / ADC MOICs
│       └── tasks: NRL ALO Mellen interviews via Garrett A-2 line
└── Counter Intelligence Branch ← 25 Jul 1947 OFFICE OF THE AIR INSPECTOR memo addressee
```

This places AFBIR-CO **structurally upstream** of [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN's]] 1948 stand-up, as the **pre-USAF (AAF-era) analytical apparatus** for the disc-collection function. ^[inferred] When AAF transitioned to USAF in **September 1947**, the **AC/AS-2 Collection Branch** function passed to **HQ-USAF Directorate of Intelligence** under Cabell — eventually appearing as the **15 Feb 1949 Cabell AIRMEM #4 distribution apparatus** at [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|serial 164]]. ^[inferred] AFBIR-CO is the **direct pre-Cabell ancestor** of the Cabell channel.

## Co-location with Bureau Bulletin No. 42

AFBIR-CO's 30 Jul 1947 analytical document is dated the **same day** as [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]] (also 30 Jul 1947, FBI HQ → all SACs). ^[extracted] This is **not coincidental**: ^[inferred] the two instruments are **synchronous parts of the AAF + FBI cooperation regime** that [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett]] and [[entities/s-w-reynolds|Reynolds]] negotiated through July 1947. AFBIR-CO collects + analyzes upstream sightings; BB42 organizes downstream FBI field-office investigation. The 30 Jul 1947 same-day issuance is consistent with **deliberate co-publication** of the regime's AAF and FBI components. ^[inferred]

## Sunset

AFBIR-CO's analytical function appears to have been **superseded by Project SIGN's 22 Jan 1948 stand-up** at AMC Wright Field. ^[inferred] The wiki corpus does not preserve any subsequent AFBIR-CO analytical instrument after 30 Jul 1947 — the office's institutional successor is **AMC T-2 → SIGN → GRUDGE → BLUE BOOK** (Wright-Patterson AFB), and at HQ-USAF, the **Cabell A-2 line** taking over inter-agency disc-collection in 1948-49. ^[inferred]
