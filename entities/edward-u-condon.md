# Edward U. Condon

**Edward Uhler Condon** (1902–1974) — American physicist; former **Director of the National Bureau of Standards** (1945–1951); by 1966 at the **University of Colorado Physics Department**. **Director** of the **University of Colorado UFO Study** (Nov 1966 – late 1968), the project popularly known as the [[concepts/condon-committee|Condon Committee]] whose final report (the *"Condon Report"*) is the proximate institutional warrant for the 17 December 1969 [[concepts/project-blue-book-termination-1969|termination of Project Blue Book]]. ^[inferred — general historical background; the wiki's sources do not state his birth/death dates or pre-Colorado biography directly]

## Role in the wiki's UAP corpus

- **Director of the Condon Committee (1966–1968)** — the **University of Colorado UFO study**, formally titled *"Scientific Study of Unidentified Flying Objects"*, conducted under USAF contract **F44620-67-C-0035** at ~$300K. ^[extracted]
- **Condon Report final delivery (Jan 1969)** — 3 volumes, 1,465 pages, 68 plates; institutional warrant for the AF's termination of its UFO program 11 months later. ^[extracted]
- **The peer-review precondition** — the AF commissioned the study explicitly to provide an external, peer-reviewable basis for closing or continuing its UFO program. ^[inferred] Condon's directorship was the institutional credentialing layer that made the AF's closure defensible.

## Significance for the corpus

- Condon is the **named individual** the AF chose to authorize closure of a 22-year (1947–1969) institutional UAP-investigation program. ^[inferred]
- The Condon Committee is the **bridge** between the 1958–1962 FBI policy stasis and the 17 Dec 1969 Blue Book termination ^[inferred] — Condon is the named director at the apex of that bridge.
- The Condon Report's three conclusions (no national-security indication / no scientific-anomaly indication / no extraterrestrial indication) are preserved verbatim in the wiki via the [[references/fbi-blue-book-termination-fact-sheet-1977|1977 UFO Fact Sheet]] forwarded by Lt Col McClanahan. ^[extracted]
