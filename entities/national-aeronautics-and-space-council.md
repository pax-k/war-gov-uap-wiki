# National Aeronautics and Space Council (NASC)

The originating office of the [[references/hunter-1963-space-alien-race-memo|1963 "Space Alien Race Question" memo]]. The memo's letterhead reads "**EXECUTIVE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT / NATIONAL AERONAUTICS and SPACE COUNCIL / WASHINGTON**" with file marker **SP 1-NASC**, indicating an internal NASC document number. The "OSA FILE COPY" annotation suggests the file copy was retained by the Office of the Space Affairs / Office of Spacecraft Affairs ^[ambiguous — exact OSA expansion not extractable from the OCR alone].

## Extracted from the source

- NASC operated as an Executive Office of the President advisory body in 1963.
- It maintained a **Professional Staff** that drafted policy papers — Hunter's signature line "Member, Professional Staff" makes this explicit.
- The memo refers to discussions during deliberations on **BNSP Task I** ("Basic National Security Policy" ^[inferred from era usage]), placing the NASC staff inside national-security policy coordination, not just civilian space planning.
- Routing on this single memo includes initials **RR / EMK / E05 / CED / AMM** in the file-stamp block — likely reviewers/distributees, not extractable as full names from the OCR.

## Inferred / contextual

- NASC was created by the National Aeronautics and Space Act (1958) to advise the President on aeronautical and space matters and was chaired by the Vice President. It operated through 1973 and was reconstituted later. The 1963 incarnation discussed here was the original Eisenhower/Kennedy-era body. ^[inferred — common knowledge, not in the source]
- That a NASC staff member would forward a "what if we discover an alien intelligence?" thought-paper to **State/OISA** (rather than DoD or CIA) suggests the question was being handled in 1963 as a **diplomatic / international-scientific-affairs** matter, not a counter-intelligence one. ^[inferred]
