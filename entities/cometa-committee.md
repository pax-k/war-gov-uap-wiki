# COMETA (Comité d'Etudes Approfondies)

A **private French committee** governed by the Law of 1 July 1901, formed under the auspices of the **IHEDN Auditors Association** to update an earlier 1976 IHEDN preliminary report on UFOs. The acronym expands to *Comité d'Etudes Approfondies* — Committee for In-Depth Studies. Chaired by **[[entities/denis-letty|Air Force General Denis Letty]]** (2nd Section), AA promotion 35.

The committee originated in March 1995, when Letty visited then-IHEDN director **General Bernard Norlain** to propose a follow-on study. Twenty years had passed since the 1976 IHEDN report by General Blanchard's committee that recommended the creation of [[entities/gepan-sepra|GEPAN]].

## Composition

COMETA's committee membership, as listed in the 1999 [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]] (front matter):

- **General Denis Letty** — Air Force, 2nd Section, AA (35) — chair
- **Michel Algrin** — State Doctor of Political Science, attorney-at-law, AA (35)
- **Pierre Bescond** — Weapons Engineer General, 2nd Section, AA (48)
- **Denis Blancher** — Chief of Police, Police Nationale, Ministry of the Interior
- **Jean Dunglas** — Doctor of Engineering (Ret.), AR (48)
- **Bruno Le Moine** — Air Force General, 2nd Section, AA (41)
- **Françoise Lépine** — Fondation pour les Etudes de Défense, AA (33)
- **Christian Marchal** — Chief Mining Engineer, Research Director at ONERA
- **Marc Merlo** — Admiral, 2nd Section, AA (35)
- **Alain Orszag** — Doctor of Physical Sciences, Weapons Engineer General, 2nd Section
- **Joseph Domange** — Air Force General, AA delegate general

**Contributors** (not formally listed members but whose work appears throughout the report):

- **Jean-Jacques Vélasco** — Head of [[entities/gepan-sepra|SEPRA]] at CNES.
- **François Louange** — Chief Executive Officer of Fleximage; photo-analysis methodology author.
- **Jean-Charles Duboc, Jean-Pierre Fartek, René Giraud** — civilian and military pilot witnesses.
- **Edmond Campagnac** — former technical director of Air France at Antananarivo (1954 sighting witness).
- **Michel Perrier** — Squadron Commander, Gendarmerie Nationale.
- **M. Soun** — Direction Générale de l'Aviation Civile (DGAC).
- **Air France Air Operations Command Center** — investigation of [[references/sighting-air-france-3532-1994|AF 3532]] ^[inferred-as-target] (24 Jan 1994).
- **General Bernard Norlain** — former IHEDN director; foreword.
- **[[entities/andre-lebeau|André Lebeau]]** — former CNES chairman; foreword.

The membership pattern is notable: **almost all members had or had had important responsibilities in defense, industry, teaching, research or central administrations** — but none was acting in their official government capacity. This is the structural distinction between COMETA and [[entities/national-aeronautics-and-space-council|NASC]]: NASC was an Executive Office of the President body; COMETA was a private association of senior figures with credentials *from* government bodies.

## Activity

- **March 1995** — Letty proposes the committee to Norlain.
- **February 1995** — Letty had organized an Ecole de l'Air alumni conference where former pilots related UFO encounters; SEPRA presented results; an astronomer presented the extraterrestrial hypothesis.
- **~1995–1999** — committee meets and drafts; "spared no effort for close to three years" per Letty.
- **July 1999** — [[references/cometa-report-1999|the COMETA Report]] is published as a special issue of VSD magazine.
- **2001** — [[entities/carol-rosin|Carol Rosin]] hand-delivers a copy to NASA Administrator Daniel Goldin (probable). ^[inferred]
