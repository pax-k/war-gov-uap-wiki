# George F. Schulgen

Brigadier General **George F. Schulgen**, US Army Air Forces — **Chief of the Requirements Intelligence Branch** of AAF Intelligence in 1947, captured in the FBI corpus as the **architect of the FBI's UAP-engagement role**. ^[extracted]

## What he did (per the FBI corpus)

Section 1 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|file 62-HQ-83894]] documents Schulgen's institutional moves through three FBI memoranda: ^[extracted]

| Date | Document | What Schulgen does |
|---|---|---|
| **9 Jul 1947** | E. G. Fitch → D. M. Ladd memo (serial 33) | Schulgen meets [[entities/s-w-reynolds\|SA S. W. Reynolds]] at the Pentagon and **formally requests FBI cooperation** in the disc investigations. ^[extracted] |
| **24 Jul 1947** | E. G. Fitch → Ladd follow-up memo | Schulgen accepts Hoover's full-disc-access precondition, **issues field instructions** for AAF cooperation with FBI, and commits to making AAF scientific findings available to the Bureau as a courtesy. ^[extracted] |
| **30 Jul 1947** | [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947\|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]] | Hoover distributes the cooperation regime to all FBI field offices. ^[extracted] |
| **5 Sep 1947** | **Schulgen → FBI Director formal letter** ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2\|section 2]] OCR p. 123, serial 84; declassified NND 90986) | Schulgen formally certifies in writing to Hoover: *"a complete survey of research activities discloses that the Army Air Forces has no project with the characteristics similar to those which have been associated with the Flying Discs."* ^[extracted] The letter is a **direct response** to [[entities/g-d-garrett\|Lt Col Garrett's]] private admission to Reynolds that flying discs are likely a classified USG project — Schulgen is forced to formally close that hypothesis after Reynolds escalates via Forney + Generals Chamberlin/Todd. ^[extracted] |

## What Schulgen tells the FBI

His framing of the disc problem to Reynolds, as relayed in the Fitch memo: ^[extracted]

> The Air Corps has taken the attitude that every effort must be undertaken in order to run down and ascertain whether or not the flying disks are a fact and, if so, to learn about them.… [The] research is being conducted with the thought that the flying objects might be a celestial phenomenon and with view that they might be a foreign body mechanically devised and controlled.

> The possibility exists that the first reported sightings of the so-called flying disks were fallacious and prompted by individuals seeking personal publicity, or were reported for political reasons. He stated that if this was so, subsequent sightings might be the result of a mass hysteria. He pointed out that the thought exists that the first reported sightings might have been by individuals of Communist sympathies with the view to causing hysteria and fear of a secret Russian weapon.

These three framing layers — **(1) celestial phenomenon, (2) foreign mechanical device, (3) Communist-sympathizer-induced mass hysteria** — define the AAF's 1947 working hypothesis space and propagate into [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42's]] field-office instructions verbatim. ^[inferred]

## What Schulgen guarantees

Schulgen explicitly assures Reynolds that: ^[extracted]

- **No War Department or Navy Department research projects** are tied to flying discs;
- The AAF will provide **complete cooperation** to the FBI;
- **Disc-recovery access** will be granted to FBI field agents (per Hoover's precondition);
- AAF **scientific findings** will be shared back to the Bureau through Liaison.

## Position in corpus

Schulgen is **structurally upstream** of every FBI saucer-investigation in the 1947-49 window. ^[inferred] His Requirements Intelligence Branch is also institutionally upstream of [[entities/c-p-cabell|C. P. Cabell's]] later AFOIR-CO directorate (Cabell becomes Director of Intelligence in 1948 and supersedes Schulgen's Bureau-coordination role with [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|AIRMEM #4]] in Feb 1949). ^[inferred]

Schulgen is **best known to the broader UAP-research community** as the addressee of [[entities/c-p-cabell|Lt Gen Nathan F. Twining]]'s famous **23 Sep 1947 "Schulgen memo"** on flying-disc characteristics — which lies outside the FBI corpus per se but is the upstream-AAF policy document that downstream-cites the same framing space (celestial / foreign-mechanical / hoax). ^[inferred]
