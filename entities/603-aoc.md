# 603rd Air and Space Operations Center (603 AOC)

The **603rd Air and Space Operations Center** — the USAF / **USEUCOM** theater Operations Center at **Ramstein AB Germany** ^[inferred — open-source standard attribution; the wiki's dow-uap d14 OCR names the unit but not the garrison.]. The AOC is the centralized command-and-control node for air operations across the **USEUCOM** and (historically) **USAFRICOM** AORs, including the European theater, Mediterranean, and (depending on tasking lane) North Africa.

This page is a **stub hub**. 603 AOC's significance in the dow-uap corpus rests on two structural attestations: it **executes** the d14 EUCOM Misrep (first non-USCENTCOM Misrep), and it **hosts the POC home** of the d16 OIR-Syria Misrep (second cross-OC mission with **first inverse-direction pairing** in dow-uap corpus).

## Role in the dow-uap corpus

### d14 — 603 AOC as executing OC (29-30 May 2022)

[[references/dow-uap-d14-mission-iraq-2022-05-29|d14]] (29-30 May 2022) is the dow-uap corpus's first non-USCENTCOM Misrep — **COCOM = USEUCOM** and **executing Operations Center = 603 AOC / 603rd**. Its mission profile:

- **Forward-deployed Sigonella AB Italy** (LICZ ICAO) — first dow-uap Misrep from a EUCOM forward operating location. ^[extracted]
- **[[entities/432-aew|432 AEW]] POC Wing** + **50 ATKS POC Unit** + **POC home OC = 609 CAOC** (CENTCOM) → **executing OC = 603 AOC** (EUCOM) — **first cross-OC mission** in dow-uap corpus. ^[extracted]
- **Op HUMMER SICKLE** tasking — Russian-Mediterranean-Squadron watch over Tartus naval base, Latakia, Bassel Al Assad airfield, Beirut military facilities. ^[extracted]
- **Operational Area 41 Eastern Mediterranean** (41EMD) — first EMD attestation in dow-uap. ^[inferred]
- **First foreign-state fighter close-approach in dow-uap corpus** — Russian Federation Air Force SU-30 at 5NM / FL190 underneath the MQ-9 at FL243; ESM = No, Countermeasures = NONE; non-visual acquisition. ^[extracted]

### d16 — 603 AOC as POC home OC (30-31 Jul 2022)

[[references/dow-uap-d16-mission-syria-2022-07-30|d16]] (30-31 Jul 2022) inverts the cross-OC mission direction:

- **POC home OC = 603 AOC** (EUCOM) — first 603 AOC POC home attestation; first inverse-direction cross-OC mission in dow-uap corpus. ^[extracted]
- **Executing OC = 609 / 609 CAOC** (CENTCOM) — d16 is operationally USCENTCOM + Operation INHERENT RESOLVE + Syria sub-theater + Marine TF CHOSIN-supported. ^[extracted]
- **[[entities/432-aew|432 AEW]] POC Wing** + **89 ATKS POC Unit** + 603 AOC POC home — plausibly anchors **forward-deployed Sigonella-or-similar EUCOM-homed 432 AEW squadron pool** parallel to d14's 50 ATKS Sigonella deployment ^[inferred].
- **Launched from Muwaffaq Salti Airbase (OJMS) Jordan** — first dow-uap Misrep where 603 AOC POC routes a mission via Jordan-based launch into Syria interior ^[inferred].
- **OP SPECTRE DAGGER ISO TF CHOSIN** — counter-ISIS Marine ground-task-force support in UTM 37S FU + 37S EV + 37S FV (Dayr Az Zar eastern Syria). ^[extracted]

**The 603 AOC pairing at d14 (executing) + d16 (POC home) anchors the bidirectional cross-OC mission class at N=2 in dow-uap corpus** — distinct directions, same OC. ^[inferred]

## Significance for the corpus

- **OIR cluster restored via sub-theater split.** The d14-anchored "OIR May-2022 cluster contradicted at N=3 — d14 is EUCOM" closure stands narrowly, but d16 (30-31 Jul 2022) restores OIR at N=3 via the **OIR-Syria sub-theater split** ^closed-by-dow-uap-d16 (UTM 37S + TF CHOSIN + OP SPECTRE DAGGER + Dayr Az Zar, distinct from but sister to OIR-Iraq d10+d12). The 603 AOC POC home OC at d16 routes an EUCOM-rooted squadron into CENTCOM OIR-Syria — institutionally distinct from d14's EUCOM Russian-Mediterranean-Squadron watch. ^[inferred]
- **Bidirectional cross-OC mission class at N=2 — first inverse-direction pairing.** d14 + d16 anchor the cross-OC class with **opposite directions** (d14 CENTCOM→EUCOM + d16 EUCOM→CENTCOM); 603 AOC pivots between **executing OC** (d14) and **POC home OC** (d16). ^[inferred]
- **432 AEW cross-COCOM organic ISR capacity.** The d14 attestation establishes 432 AEW as a wing that flies under at least three operational lanes within 2022 alone: NAVCENT (legacy), OIR/USCENTCOM, and now USEUCOM. The d10 → d14 corpus span (6-7 May 2022 → 29-30 May 2022; ~22 days) firms the cross-COCOM reading at byte-for-byte unit-level. ^[inferred]
- **`ACC = ANG-gaining-command` reading falsified.** d12 introduced `MAJCOM = ACC` and the candidate reading was "ACC routes ANG units." d14 carries **`MAJCOM = ACC` + active 432 AEW** (NOT ANG), falsifying the ANG-specific reading: ACC is the **owning MAJCOM** for both active-component and ANG wings under specific tasking. ^[inferred; closed-by-dow-uap-d14]
- **`iraq` filename contradicted.** d14's filename is `iraq-may-2022` but body coordinates decode to Eastern Mediterranean — third dow-uap filename-curator-mismatch token joining `arabian-gulf` (d4/d5/d7) and `djibouti` (d8). ^[extracted; closed-by-dow-uap-d14]
