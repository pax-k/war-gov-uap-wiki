# 603rd Air and Space Operations Center (603 AOC)

The **603rd Air and Space Operations Center** — the USAF / **USEUCOM** theater Operations Center at **Ramstein AB Germany** ^[inferred — open-source standard attribution; the wiki's dow-uap d14 OCR names the unit but not the garrison.]. The AOC is the centralized command-and-control node for air operations across the **USEUCOM** and (historically) **USAFRICOM** AORs, including the European theater, Mediterranean, and (depending on tasking lane) North Africa.

This page is a **stub hub**. 603 AOC's significance in the dow-uap corpus rests on a **single decisive contradiction**: it anchors the d14 Misrep that breaks the "OIR May-2022 cluster N≥3" framing built up from d10 + d12.

## Role in the dow-uap corpus

[[references/dow-uap-d14-mission-iraq-2022-05-29|d14]] (29-30 May 2022) is the first and only dow-uap Misrep with **COCOM = USEUCOM** and **Operations Center = 603 AOC**. Its mission profile:

- **Forward-deployed Sigonella AB Italy** (LICZ ICAO) — first dow-uap Misrep from a EUCOM forward operating location. ^[extracted]
- **[[entities/432-aew|432 AEW]] POC Wing** + **50 ATKS POC Unit** + **603 AOC Operations Center** — same POC Wing as the d10 OIR mission but routed through a different theater AOC. ^[extracted]
- **Op HUMMER SICKLE** tasking — Russian-Mediterranean-Squadron watch over Tartus naval base, Latakia, Bassel Al Assad airfield, Beirut military facilities. ^[extracted]
- **Operational Area 41 Eastern Mediterranean** (41EMD) — first EMD attestation in dow-uap. ^[inferred]
- **First foreign-state fighter close-approach in dow-uap corpus** — Russian Federation Air Force SU-30 at 5NM / FL190 underneath the MQ-9 at FL243; ESM = No, Countermeasures = NONE; non-visual acquisition. ^[extracted]

## Significance for the corpus

- **OIR cluster contradiction.** The d10-anchored "OIR May-2022 cluster at N=2 (d10 + d12); validate at N≥3 (d14 / d16 queued)" open thread is **CONTRADICTED** ^closed-by-dow-uap-d14 — d14 is NOT OIR, but a distinct EUCOM tasking. OIR cluster remains capped at **N=2** (d10 + d12). ^[extracted]
- **432 AEW cross-COCOM organic ISR capacity.** The d14 attestation establishes 432 AEW as a wing that flies under at least three operational lanes within 2022 alone: NAVCENT (legacy), OIR/USCENTCOM, and now USEUCOM. The d10 → d14 corpus span (6-7 May 2022 → 29-30 May 2022; ~22 days) firms the cross-COCOM reading at byte-for-byte unit-level. ^[inferred]
- **`ACC = ANG-gaining-command` reading falsified.** d12 introduced `MAJCOM = ACC` and the candidate reading was "ACC routes ANG units." d14 carries **`MAJCOM = ACC` + active 432 AEW** (NOT ANG), falsifying the ANG-specific reading: ACC is the **owning MAJCOM** for both active-component and ANG wings under specific tasking. ^[inferred; closed-by-dow-uap-d14]
- **`iraq` filename contradicted.** d14's filename is `iraq-may-2022` but body coordinates decode to Eastern Mediterranean — third dow-uap filename-curator-mismatch token joining `arabian-gulf` (d4/d5/d7) and `djibouti` (d8). ^[extracted; closed-by-dow-uap-d14]
