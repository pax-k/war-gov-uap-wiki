# Ryan Graves

**Ryan Graves** is a former **US Navy F/A-18F Super Hornet pilot** ^[inferred] and a prominent **post-2017-disclosure-era UAP witness** who has testified publicly before both the **US Congress** (prior to 12 Sep 2023; specific hearing date not in corpus ^[ambiguous]) and the **Mexican Congress** (12 Sep 2023, per [[references/dos-cable-mexico-2023-09|MEXICO 2544 cable's ¶11]]).

## Role in MEXICO 2544 ¶11

The cable identifies Graves explicitly as a *"former U.S. Navy pilot... who previously testified before the U.S. Congress."* ^[extracted] He testified at the **Mexican Congress's 12 Sep 2023 hearing** on UAP language in the **Aerial Space Protection Law**, alongside [[entities/jaime-maussan|Jaime Maussan]] and other unnamed experts. ^[extracted]

The cable preserves Graves' **on-the-record post-hearing dissent** from Maussan's alleged-corpse display:

> *"After the hearing, Graves lamented the display took away from his and other pilots' experiences with UAP and expressed disappointment with Maussan's 'unsubstantiated stunt.'"* ^[extracted]

The phrase *"unsubstantiated stunt"* is the cable's only quoted-utterance from Graves and the only direct first-person utterance from any UAP witness preserved in the cable. ^[extracted]

This is the corpus's **first instance of a named US-Navy-pilot UAP witness publicly criticizing another UAP witness's evidence display in the same legislative venue**. ^[inferred] Graves' role in the cable is structurally **stance-asymmetric** with Maussan: Maussan presents alleged physical evidence; Graves rejects the evidence as a "stunt" while affirming the broader experiential UAP-witness category from his own pilot pedigree. ^[inferred]

## Why this entity matters for the corpus

- **First named US-Navy-pilot UAP witness** in the corpus. Prior corpus military-aviation witnesses were USAAF (1944–45 415th NFS foo-fighters), USAF (1948–49 Project SIGN reports), and Navy enlisted (1949 Kodiak — USN Lt Cdr Shepard). Graves is the first **named, post-disclosure-era, F/A-18F-pedigreed** US-Navy witness in the corpus. ^[inferred]
- **First witness with prior US Congressional testimony pedigree** in the corpus. Russell (1955 Trans-Caucasus) was a US Senator but did not testify to Congress about his sighting; Graves is the first witness whose **public-record UAP testimony before the US Congress** predates his appearance in a corpus document. ^[inferred]
- **First explicit cross-witness skepticism** in a single corpus artifact. The 1949 Mountain Home ID case (witness Harry Clark only) contained no cross-witness skepticism. The 2023 Sept FBI FD-302 pair shows two witnesses who largely agree (with morphology divergences). MEXICO 2544 ¶11 is the first artifact to preserve two named witnesses publicly disagreeing on the same day at the same venue. ^[inferred]

## Public-record context (limited corpus knowledge)

The cable does **not** detail Graves' prior testimony. From external common knowledge ^[inferred]:

- Graves served as an F/A-18F pilot with the US Navy and is widely associated with the **VFA-11 "Red Rippers"** squadron's 2014–15 UAP encounters off the US East Coast involving Advanced Targeting Forward-Looking Infrared (ATFLIR) sensor recordings. ^[inferred]
- Graves testified before the **US House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs** on **26 July 2023** — likely the "previously testified before the U.S. Congress" referent. ^[inferred]
- Graves is associated with **Americans for Safe Aerospace (ASA)**, an advocacy organization for aviation safety and UAP transparency. ^[inferred]

These contextual claims are **unsubstantiated by the corpus** and should be re-anchored to a primary source if Graves re-surfaces in a future ingest.

## Stance on UAP evidentiary standards (inferable from the cable)

The cable's preserved Graves utterance — *"unsubstantiated stunt"* — combined with the paraphrase *"Graves lamented the display took away from his and other pilots' experiences with UAP"* — places Graves in the **prepared-skepticism** rhetorical posture (see [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame]]):

- Graves **affirms** the experiential UAP-witness category — he and other pilots have *"experiences with UAP"* worth taking seriously.
- Graves **rejects** the alleged-physical-evidence category — Maussan's corpses are an "unsubstantiated stunt."
- The two stances are **simultaneously held** — affirming experiential evidence while rejecting unverified-physical-evidence is the characteristic move of the prepared-skepticism middle-ground rhetoric. ^[inferred]

This places Graves as the **first post-2017-era US-pilot witness** in the corpus who fits the same rhetorical posture that Hunter (1963 NASC), the COMETA committee (1999), and AARO (modern) all inhabit — though Graves arrives at it from witness experience rather than from policy-staff or institutional analysis. ^[inferred]
