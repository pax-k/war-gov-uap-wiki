# National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena (NICAP)

A **private civilian research group** organized in **January 1957** to "tie together a number of UFO clubs being formed throughout the world" and to **collect, investigate, evaluate, and publicize flying-saucer reports**. ^[extracted] NICAP is the institutional center of the **post-1957 civilian-research-organization era** that succeeds [[entities/borderland-sciences-research-associates|BSRA]] (1945) and [[entities/international-flying-saucer-bureau|IFSB]] (1952) in the wiki corpus, and runs in parallel with [[entities/oklahoma-ufo-research-association|OUFORA]] (mid-1960s) on the contactee-vs-research axis. ^[inferred]

## Wiki primary-source mention

NICAP enters the wiki corpus through **[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-sub-a|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 sub-file A]] page 17** — a **15 January 1957 AP wire by Vern Haugland** announcing NICAP's organizational meeting. ^[extracted] The clipping was filed by FBI HQ Central Research Section under sub-file A's press-clippings appendix and routed via 0-19 form (Rev. 11-22-55) to the standard HQ executive distribution: Tolson, Nichols, Boardman, Belmont, Mason, Mohr, Parsons, Rosen, Tamm, Nease, Winterrowd, Tele. Room, Holloman, Gandy. ^[extracted]

## Director of investigations: [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Donald Keyhoe]]

By **late 1953-early 1954** (i.e. before NICAP's January 1957 founding), Keyhoe is already named in the press as *"director of the National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena"*. ^[extracted] Sub-A page 10 (1957-period AP coverage) names Keyhoe as NICAP's director when noting his ETI hypothesis. ^[extracted] **The chronology is ambiguous**: the Haugland AP wire (sub-A page 17) describes a 15 Jan 1957 *organizational* meeting, while contemporaneous wire copy already references NICAP as if it has been operating. ^[ambiguous] Possible reading: NICAP was operationally active under Keyhoe in 1953-54 and only formally-incorporated / publicly-launched with Fahrney as board chair in January 1957. ^[inferred]

## Cross-corpus position

NICAP is structurally novel in the wiki corpus as the **first explicitly-credentialed civilian research organization** (Fahrney = ex-RAdm, ex-Navy-guided-missiles head; Keyhoe = retired Major, USMC, Naval Academy 1919). ^[extracted] By contrast:

- [[entities/borderland-sciences-research-associates|BSRA]] (Layne, San Diego, 1945) — etheric / inter-dimensional posture, no ex-military leadership in the wiki record
- [[entities/international-flying-saucer-bureau|IFSB]] (Bender, Bridgeport CT, 1952) — civilian hobbyist, ages 23 founder, Civil Defense / Ground Observation Corps cross-recruitment
- [[entities/amalgamated-flying-saucer-clubs-of-america|AFSCA]] (Green, LA, mid-1960s) — contactee-movement umbrella org, Martian + Alpha-Centaurian narratives

NICAP's credentialed posture made it **the bridge between the contactee movement and the post-1966 Condon-Report-era credibility framework**. ^[inferred]
