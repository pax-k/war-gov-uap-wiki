# AMEMBASSY Moscow

The **United States Embassy in Moscow**, Russia — originating diplomatic post of the [[references/dos-cable-russia-georgia-2001-10|30 October 2001 cable MOSCOW 13169]], "UFOS Over Georgia: Strange Encounters of an MFA Kind."

## Why this page exists

The embassy is the wiki's **fourth** archived diplomatic post that produced a State Department cable surfaced in a UAP-keyword FOIA release — after [[entities/amembassy-port-moresby|AMEMBASSY Port Moresby]] (1985 PNG), [[entities/amembassy-dushanbe|AMEMBASSY Dushanbe]] (1994 Kazakhstan), and [[entities/amembassy-ashgabat|AMEMBASSY Ashgabat]] (2004 Turkmenistan). **Like Ashgabat but unlike Port Moresby and Dushanbe**, the cable that anchors this page is **not a UAP-sighting cable**: it is a Russia-Georgia bilateral-relations cable about Russian airspace-violation denials, in which the word "UFO" appears as a **rhetorical / sarcastic device** rather than as a description of UAP phenomena. See [[references/dos-cable-russia-georgia-2001-10]] for the boundary-case framing and [[concepts/figurative-ufo-rhetoric]] for the typology.

Moscow therefore sits **outside** the [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting|diplomatic-channel UAP reporting]] umbrella, alongside Ashgabat — the umbrella's two substantive instances are Port Moresby (sub-pattern A — foreign-liaison-inquiry) and Dushanbe (sub-pattern B — citizen-witness-relay). The page exists primarily to anchor that distinction and to track AMEMBASSY Moscow's full FOIA-released `059uap*` traffic as the rest of the series is ingested.

## What the cable shows the embassy doing

In the [[references/dos-cable-russia-georgia-2001-10|30 Oct 2001 cable]], the embassy:

1. **Convenes a meeting** between [[entities/alexander-vershbow|Ambassador Vershbow]] and Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Mamedov on 30 October 2001 to raise reports (per Reftel B = TBILISI 3087) of Russian airspace violations and Kodori Gorge bombings on 28–29 Oct.
2. **Records the Russian denial**: Mamedov, citing the Russian Ministry of Defense, categorically denies any Russian aircraft involvement.
3. **Records a parallel demarche** to Russian MFA Georgia desk chief Tereoken, who echoes Mamedov's denial — and adds the **"might as well have been about UFOs"** trope and the corollary that Moscow lacks the technical capability to determine whether foreign aircraft were in the region. ^[extracted]
4. **Pushes back on the no-time / no-observers argument** on the [[entities/russia-mfa|Russian MFA]]'s Gudauta-base withdrawal — noting that two more loaded trains awaiting Abkhaz permission contradict the no-time claim.
5. **Forwards Tereoken's read** of Speaker Zvania's 25–28 Oct Moscow visit (positive tone, no practical outcome) and the rumored Ivanov / Tbilisi visit.
6. **Closes with an explicit dismissive comment** on the UFO posit (¶8): *"TO POSIT THAT THEY COULD BE UFOS WOULD BE HUMOROUS IF IT WERE NOT FOR THE SERIOUSNESS OF THE VIOLATIONS."* — i.e., the embassy explicitly rejects the UFO posit as denial rhetoric and reads the Russian behavior as "a traditional Russian penchant to avoid an awkward admission with a bold lie." ^[extracted]
7. **Routes the cable** action-line `IO-00` (Bureau of International Organization Affairs) — the multilateral / UN / OSCE angle — at `PRIORITY` precedence, with `AMEMBASSY TBILISI`, `USMISSION USUN`, the Moscow Political Collective, AMEMBASSY BAKU and YEREVAN, USOSCE, SECDEF, and the Joint Staff on info. ^[extracted]

The cable signature reads `VERSHBOW` — **[[entities/alexander-vershbow|Alexander R. Vershbow]]**, US Ambassador to Russia 2001–2005. Classification authority is **[[entities/george-krol|POLMINCOUNS George Krol]]** under reason `1.5 (B/D)` of E.O. 12958. ^[extracted]

## Cross-references

- Of the four UAP-keyword DOS cables in the corpus, AMEMBASSY Moscow is the **only post** that uses an explicitly **adversarial / state-relations** frame. The other three posts forward narratives or NGO assessments without taking a substantive position; Moscow takes a substantive position **against** the Russian denial and reads the UFO trope as denial rhetoric. ^[extracted]
- This is also the **only one of the four cables** to ride at `PRIORITY` precedence — Port Moresby's `IMMEDIATE` reflects 1985 USCINCPAC asset-query urgency; the 1994 and 2004 cables ride at `Routine`. ^[extracted]
- The cable's broad info-line distribution (TBILISI, USUN, BAKU, YEREVAN, USOSCE, SECDEF, JS, plus the Moscow Political Collective) is **standard for high-stakes Russia-Georgia bilateral cables in the 2001 pre-summit posture** ^[inferred] — not specific to UAP content.
- AMEMBASSY Moscow is **the corpus's first US-State-Department post inside a strategic adversary's capital**, distinct from the small-post / post-conflict / non-allied contexts of Port Moresby, Dushanbe, and Ashgabat.
