# Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI)

The **Office of Naval Intelligence** — the intelligence service of the **United States Navy**, headed by the **Director of Naval Intelligence (DNI)**. Established **23 March 1882** as the oldest member of the US Intelligence Community ^[inferred — general historical background; the wiki's sources do not state ONI's founding date directly].

This page is a **stub hub**. ONI surfaces across the wiki's FBI UAP corpus through (1) regional **DIO confidential intelligence reports** routed to FBI HQ, and (2) ONI/DNI as a **named recipient** in HQ inter-agency distribution lists.

## Role in the wiki's UAP corpus

ONI appears in the corpus through several distinct institutional layers:

- **Regional DIO reports cross-filed in 62-HQ-83894** — e.g. the **23 Jun 1949 DIO-11ND Confidential Intelligence Report Serial 23-09 (San Diego ONI)** on LtJG Joseph C. Shell's 27 May 1949 SNJ-pilot Hart Mountain Oregon sighting — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5]] serial 197 + [[references/sighting-shell-oregon-1949-05-27]]. ^[extracted]
- **Inter-agency distribution recipient** — when FBI HQ redistributes UAP material to the broader IC (e.g. the 1957 Krasuski case after NSC routing), the **Director of Naval Intelligence** is one of 6 standard recipients alongside State (Office of Security), CIA (Deputy Director Plans), Army G-2, OSI/Air Force, and Immigration and Naturalization Service. ^[extracted]
- **The Frances Swan / Wilbur Smith case (1954)** — retired Adm Herbert B. Knowles writes to **DNI** as one of his routing principals on the psychic-channeling UAP case; Hoover's 9 Aug 1954 letter to OSI/AF also cc's DNI. ^[extracted] See [[references/sighting-frances-swan-1954-07]] and [[concepts/psychic-channeling-uap]].
- **Archival cross-filing channel** — Box 7 of **RG 38 (CNO/Naval Records)** holds the [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|Project SIGN incident-summary bundle 173–233]] as an ONI archival cross-file — even though that bundle is institutionally a USAF/AMC/Project SIGN product, it lives in RG 38 (ONI archival space). ^[inferred]

## Significance for the corpus

- ONI is the **Navy-side institutional counterpart** to FBI HQ in the IC inter-agency UAP distribution pattern of the 1947–1969 era. ^[inferred]
- The **regional DIO field-channel** (e.g. DIO-11ND San Diego) is a wiki-corpus-documented operational layer of ONI distinct from the HQ/DNI distribution-recipient layer.
- ONI's archival reach extends across services — Box 7 of RG 38 holding USAF/Project SIGN material illustrates the cross-service archival cross-filing pattern. ^[inferred]
