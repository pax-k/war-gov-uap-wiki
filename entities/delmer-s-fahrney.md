# Delmer S. Fahrney

Retired US Navy **Rear Admiral**; once head of the **Navy's guided-missiles program**. ^[extracted] In **January 1957** Fahrney chaired the **organizational meeting** of [[entities/nicap|NICAP]] (National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena) and publicly committed the new committee to *"collect and investigate flying-saucer reports, evaluate them and make public its findings"*. ^[extracted]

## Wiki primary-source anchor

Fahrney enters the wiki corpus through **[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-sub-a|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 sub-file A]] page 17** — Vern Haugland's 15 Jan 1957 AP wire "There Are Saucers, Expert Says". ^[extracted]

## Public claims (15 Jan 1957 news conference)

| Claim | Provenance |
|---|---|
| *"Reliable reports indicate that there are objects coming into our atmosphere at very high speeds."* | extracted (Fahrney quoted directly) |
| *"No agency in this country or Russia is able to duplicate at this time the speeds and accelerations which radar and observers indicate these flying objects are able to achieve."* | extracted |
| He has **not personally seen** a flying saucer; bases his claim on conversations with *"scientists and engineers who reported seeing strange flying objects"* | extracted |
| NICAP was set up *"largely to tie together a number of UFO clubs being formed throughout the world"* | extracted |

## Why this matters in the FBI corpus

Fahrney is the **first ex-military-flag-officer in the wiki corpus to publicly endorse the UAP-exceeds-earth-aerodynamics claim** — antedates the [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell]] / [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]] / [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|BB-57]]-era institutional figures by ~10 years (their position was operational, not advocacy). ^[inferred]

The credentialing matters because Fahrney's prior role — head of the **Navy guided-missiles program** — directly contradicts the standard 1947-1957 AAF/AF posture that *"none of the three military departments nor any other agency in the government is conducting experiments … with flying objects which could be a basis for the reported phenomena"* ([[references/usaf-flying-saucers-press-fact-sheet-1952|USAF/DOD-OPI fact sheet]]). ^[inferred] Fahrney was on the inside of the only US service that ran a major guided-missiles R&D program during the 1947-1957 window — he is **structurally the most credentialed witness in the wiki corpus to the claim that UAP are not US-experimental aircraft**. ^[inferred]
