# André Lebeau

**Former chairman of the Centre National d'Etudes Spatiales (CNES)** — the French national space agency. Author of the foreword to the 1999 [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]] (titled in translation *"Stripping the UFO phenomenon of its irrational layer"*).

The CNES connection is institutionally significant: CNES is the parent agency of [[entities/gepan-sepra|GEPAN/SEPRA]], the wiki's first dedicated civilian-government UAP research program. Lebeau's foreword therefore carries the weight of the program's parent-agency leadership at one remove (he was *former* chair at the time of writing, but the institutional lineage is direct).

## Foreword: methodological argument

Lebeau's foreword is a careful methodological essay rather than an endorsement of the extraterrestrial hypothesis. Its thread:

1. **Scientific facts and reproducibility** — meteorites are introduced as the historical analogue: long dismissed as folklore until 1803 (Biot at Laigle), then admitted to science when collective and indisputable observation made them impossible to ignore.
2. **The UFO problem's structural difficulty** — UFOs are *rare* and *discrete*; not amenable to reproducible experiment, only to sustained observation.
3. **Background-noise contamination** — modern aerospace and atmospheric activity generates a background of inadvertent phenomena; UFO data lives mixed with this noise.
4. **The extraterrestrial-hypothesis question is not purely scientific** — it has sociological, media, religious dimensions that contaminate research.
5. **The COMETA contribution** — the report "contributes toward stripping the phenomenon of UFOs of its irrational layer," whether or not the authors believe in extraterrestrial visitors.
6. **A scientist's belief is irrelevant to results** — only the rigor of inquiry matters. ^[extracted]

This is a textbook case of the [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame|"prepared skepticism"]] posture the wiki tracks across decades — and it is signed by a former national-space-agency chairman, which is a different institutional weight than Hunter's NASC-staff signature on the 1963 memo. ^[inferred]

## NASA-meteorite reference

Lebeau wryly notes: *"One century later NASA, no doubt hastily, elevated these stones to proof of the existence of primitive life on Mars."* — a reference to the 1996 announcement of putative Martian-meteorite (ALH84001) microfossils. The report's foreword author is therefore engaging directly with NASA's own scientific-claim-handling track record. ^[inferred]
