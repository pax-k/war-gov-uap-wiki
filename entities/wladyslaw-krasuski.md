# Wladyslaw Krasuski (Walter Krasuski)

A Polish-born Displaced Person, Detroit brewery worker, and the witness whose 1957 letter to the White House became the entry point for [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|FBI Detroit file 100-26505]].

## Biographical timeline (from the FBI Blank Memo, 7 Nov 1957)

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **(b)(6) — redacted** | Born in Poland (specific date and place redacted under FOIA(b)(6)) |
| **May 1942** | Brought from Poland to **Gut Alt Golssen**, Germany (~30 mi east of Berlin) as a **Prisoner of War**. Held there until "a few weeks after the end of World War II." |
| **1944, month unrecalled** | Observed the Gut Alt Golssen UAP-adjacent event ([[references/sighting-gut-alt-golssen-1944]]) |
| **Summer 1945** | Released from Gut Alt Golssen; entered Displaced Persons camps (Kork, Strasburg, Offenburg, Mülheim, Freiburg) |
| **1945–1949** | Attended **radio-technician school at Freiburg, Germany**; ~1 year employed at a textile mill in **Laurachbaden, Germany** |
| **2 May 1951** | Arrived in the United States at New York via the **S.S. General Stewart**, as a Displaced Person |
| | Destined to **Reverend Father Petro Walkowiak**, Hamtramck, Michigan |
| | Alien Registration Number — **(b)(6) redacted** |
| **May 1951 →** | Employed at the **Gobel Brewery, Detroit** |

## Records-check results (FBI Detroit, 1957)

- **FBI Detroit Indices** — no prior record on Krasuski or his wife.
- **BUFILES** — Director's URGENT teletype expressly states "BUFFLES REFLECT NO INFORMATION RE KRASUSKI."
- **Mich. Merchants Credit Association** (Manager William Recknagel, advised IC Paul Desmond on 7 Nov 1957) — only routine credit inquiries on "Walter Krasuski" during 6/56, 3/57, and 6/57; confirmed local address and Gobel-Brewery employment.
- **FD-125 records request** drafted by FBI Detroit (criminal-records check, "any record" on Krasuski / aka Walter) — outcome not preserved in this OCR'd extract.

The records-check pattern is structurally identical to the FBI's modern witness-validation workflow ([[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023]] uses the same "search BUFILES + local indices + creditor records" template). ^[inferred]

## Witness profile

- **Multilingual**: Polish (native), German (5 years' occupation in Germany 1942–47), English (acquired post-1951 in Detroit).
- **Trained in radio technology**: post-war Freiburg radio-technician schooling. This gives Krasuski a **better-than-baseline familiarity with electric / generator / oscillator phenomena** — relevant context for his "high-pitched whine similar to that produced by a large electric generator" descriptor in the 1944 account.
- **No prior police, security, or psychiatric record** on file in 1957 Detroit.
- **Family-grounded** (married 1952, four children), employed continuously since 1951 at the same brewery, settled in an old Polish neighborhood — none of the volatile-witness markers SA Rathbun would have flagged.
- **Self-aware route choice**: Krasuski chose to write to **Cutler** rather than Eisenhower directly after seeing Cutler's photo and presidential-office identification in a local paper. This is a reasoned routing decision, not a generic "letter to the President."
