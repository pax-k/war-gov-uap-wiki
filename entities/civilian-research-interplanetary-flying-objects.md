# Civilian Research, Interplanetary Flying Objects (CRIFO)

A **Cincinnati-based civilian saucer-research organization** founded ~1954 by [[entities/leonard-h-stringfield|Leonard H. Stringfield]] (Advertising Manager, DuBois Company, Inc.). Director's address **7017 Britton Avenue, Cincinnati 27, Ohio** ^[extracted]; mailing address **P.O. Box 1855, Cincinnati, Ohio** ^[extracted]; later masthead **"C.R.I.F.O. Orbit"**. ^[extracted]

CRIFO publishes a **multi-lithographed monthly Newsletter** (designated *"Vol. I No. 7"* on 1 Oct 1954, indicating publication began ~Apr 1954) at **$2.00/year subscription**, with a **claimed worldwide circulation of about 4,000 copies** by Oct 1954. ^[extracted] By Mar 1957 the publication is referenced under the title *"C.R.I.F.O. Orbit"*. ^[extracted]

FBI Cincinnati's standing posture is **passive accumulation**: clip the Newsletter, file the inbound letters, share with **OSI Wright-Patterson**, take no investigative action against CRIFO or Stringfield. ^[inferred]

## CRIFO's institutional posture

Stringfield's organizational posture, as reflected in the Oct 1954 Newsletter content (filed in section 8 as enclosure to serial 349): ^[extracted]

- **Public ETI-attribution**: Stringfield maintains *"saucers are interplanetary"* — see [[concepts/eti-attribution-statements]] for the cross-corpus pattern.
- **AF-Intelligence-access claim**: Stringfield reports a private 21 Sep 1954 interview with **Lt Col John O'Mara, Deputy Commander, Air Force Intelligence, Wright Field**. ^[extracted]
- **AF-censorship narrative**: Stringfield asserts *"the U. S. Air Force maintains a strict censorship of news"* on saucer matters and links the firing of Frank Edwards (former AFL radio commentator) to AF interference. ^[extracted]
- **Self-published 1955 article**: section 8 enclosure to serial 349 is a Stringfield article entitled *"SAUCERS HARASS EARTH; FEAR OF WORLD PANIC FORCES INTERNATIONAL CENSORSHIP"* — explicitly framing the saucer phenomenon as a **suppressed-information** topic. ^[extracted] Cites Logan UT crater ("violent explosion and gaping crater following mysterious 'streak of light' in sky", 7 May 1954, with Lincoln La Paz [[entities/lincoln-la-paz|]] of the Institute of Meteoritics among the investigating geologists), the Cincinnati paint-discoloration incident (11 Sep 1954), and the *"Chilean Naval Mission in Washington"* censorship correspondence as datapoints.

## Position in the corpus

- **First Cincinnati-area civilian saucer-research org** in the FBI corpus ^[inferred];
- **First civilian saucer-research org documented to maintain direct AF-Intelligence access** at the field-officer level (Lt Col O'Mara) ^[inferred];
- **First civilian-saucer-org publication** of which **substantive content is preserved verbatim** in the FBI corpus (the Stringfield "Saucers Harass Earth" article, distinct from earlier organizational artifacts like the 1953 IFSB *Space Review*) ^[inferred];
- **First civilian-saucer-org principal pursued by an external technical-controversy figure** seeking testimonial use of UFO data (Eva Reich, Mar 1957) ^[inferred];
- **Adjacent to but distinct from the [[concepts/contactee-movement-1952-1954|contactee-movement]] founding period** — CRIFO is a **research-and-publishing** organization without a personal-encounter contactee narrative, unlike IFSB / BSRA / Bethurum. ^[inferred]

## Membership / network

Per FBI Cincinnati informant Jack Gunderman (3914 Teleford Avenue, Cincinnati 20, Ohio; layout artist at DuBois Company): ^[extracted]

- Stringfield is **director** and is **assisted by his wife**.
- DuBois Company copywriter **Herbert E. Clark** (4974 Strathmore Drive, Cincinnati) is a close friend of Stringfield and active in CRIFO.
- Stringfield regularly communicates with **Frank Edwards** (former AFL radio commentator) on saucer matters from his DuBois office.
- By Apr 1957 Stringfield's correspondence network includes **Eva Reich, M.D.** (daughter of Wilhelm Reich), who treats Stringfield as a potential testimonial witness on UFO data.
