# Aerial Phenomena Research Organization (APRO)

A **civilian saucer-research organization** documented in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-8|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 8]] **serial 369 (7 Sep 1956)** as publishing **"The A.P.R.O. Bulletin"** from **1712 Van Court, Alamogordo, New Mexico**. ^[extracted] APRO was founded by Coral and Jim Lorenzen ~1952 (initially in Wisconsin, relocated to Alamogordo by 1956) ^[inferred] and is one of the longest-running civilian UAP-research organizations of the 1950s-1980s era.

This is the **first FBI-corpus reference to APRO** ^[inferred] — the wiki had no prior appearance of the Lorenzen organization.

## FBI-corpus engagement (Aug-Sep 1956)

Per FBI St. Louis serial 369 (7 Sep 1956) and the resulting Hoover → OSI/AF letter (20 Sep 1956): ^[extracted]

- **31 Aug 1956 — Harlan N. Wold** (electrician, Department 155, McDonnell Aircraft Corporation, St. Louis County, Badge No. 55185, residence 1829a North Market Street, St. Louis MO) walks into the FBI St. Louis office.
- Wold reports he and a group of fellow workers at McDonnell Aircraft have **subscribed to the APRO Bulletin** after seeing an advertisement in a magazine.
- The Bulletin is forwarded to FBI HQ via standard channels.
- Hoover writes OSI/AF on **20 Sep 1956** with the Bulletin enclosed; OSI is informed but no investigation is initiated.

## Why this case is structurally diagnostic

The APRO appearance is structurally informative for the FBI's **mid-1950s civilian-saucer-org posture** ^[inferred]:

- **Defense-contractor employee subscription as the trigger**: Wold is a McDonnell Aircraft electrician, and his subscription to a saucer-publication enters the FBI institutional pipeline because **defense-employer-adjacent saucer interest is itself a flag**. The trigger is *not* the content of the APRO Bulletin but the subscriber's defense-employment context.
- **Standard passive-accumulation disposition**: FBI files the Bulletin, forwards to OSI, takes no investigation against APRO or against Wold.
- **No prior FBI-corpus reference to APRO**: APRO had been operating for ~4 years by 1956, but did not generate FBI HQ filings until a defense-contractor subscriber tip surfaced. This is consistent with the file's **passive-accumulation function** — civilian saucer orgs enter the file when a third-party tipster (citizen, FBI informant) brings them to the Bureau, not because the Bureau actively monitors them. ^[inferred]
