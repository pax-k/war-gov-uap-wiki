# Guy Hottel

**Special Agent in Charge of the Washington Field Office (WFO) of the FBI** in 1950 ^[extracted], signatory of the **22 March 1950 memo to the Director** that has become — in popular UFOlogy — the most-cited declassified FBI document on flying saucers. ^[inferred] Filed as serial 209 in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 5]]. ^[extracted]

## Position in the wiki corpus

Hottel appears in the wiki primary-source record **only via this single memo** (serial 209, [[references/fbi-hottel-memo-1950-03-22|22 March 1950]]). ^[extracted] The memo's institutional positioning:

- **Hottel signs as the cover author** of the memo (header: *"FROM: GUY HOTTEL, SAC, WASHINGTON"*) ^[extracted]
- **The actual drafter was SA R. H. Kurtzman** (initials *RHK:VIM*) ^[extracted]
- **The actual content source was Karl Howe (MPD Sex Squad)** ^[extracted]
- **Howe's source was an unnamed "investigator for the Air Forces"** ^[extracted]

Hottel's role is **administrative cover for an SA-drafted forwarding memo** — i.e. routine WFO practice for sending memo content from a WFO agent (Kurtzman) up to the Director through the SAC's sign-off. ^[inferred] The memo's content is not Hottel's investigative product; it is a fourth-hand intake.

## Why Hottel's name carries the memo's reputation

The 22 March 1950 memo became a **flagship document** in UFOlogy because: ^[inferred]

1. It is **a real FBI document** (declassified, indexed, and dated) describing crashed saucers + humanoid bodies + radar-interference recovery — i.e. it appears to be FBI institutional content endorsing the [[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950|Aztec myth-seed]];
2. **Hottel's signature** as SAC of the FBI Washington Field Office gives the memo a high-credibility institutional veneer;
3. The memo's brevity (one page) and clarity make it easy to circulate in popular UFOlogy as a primary source.

The structural reading correction: ^[inferred] Hottel as SAC-WFO is **not the originator of the claim** — he is the cover-signatory routing a fourth-hand intake to HQ. SA Kurtzman's closing line **"No further evaluation was attempted by SA KURTZMAN concerning the above"** ^[extracted] is the memo's actual institutional position: **the FBI did not investigate it, did not corroborate it, and did not act on it**. The memo is filed in the topic-anchored 62-HQ-83894 accumulator, not in any investigative case file.
