# SHAEF Air Staff

The Air Staff of **Supreme Headquarters, Allied Expeditionary Force**, the unified Anglo-American command headed by Eisenhower that ran combined Allied operations in North-West Europe from 1943 until SHAEF's dissolution in July 1945. ^[inferred]

In the [[references/foo-fighters-shaef-1944-1945|1944–45 file]], SHAEF Air Staff is the **routing hub** that bridges:

- **Below**: the US tactical air chain — XII Tactical Air Command (Adv), First Tactical Air Force (Prov), IX TAC ([[entities/415th-night-fighter-squadron|415th NFS]], 67 TAC/R, 107 PR Squadron). ^[extracted]
- **Above / laterally**: USSTAF, the UK [[entities/uk-air-ministry-ddi2|Air Ministry (DDI2)]] and A.D.I.(K), and the SHAEF Scientific Investigation Division. ^[extracted]

## File reference architecture

The bundle uses the SHAEF subject file number **37153**, with the more specific reference **SHAEF Air/TS.37153/A-2** for the foo-fighter / night-phenomena correspondence and **SHAEF/AIR/S.37103/A-2** for the cylindrical-object photo-request thread. ^[extracted]

The trailing **A-2** denotes the **Assistant Chief of Staff for Intelligence** office within SHAEF Air Staff — the same Anglo-American intelligence-staff numbering system used in US continental commands. The signing officer for both threads is A/Cmdr [[entities/c-m-grierson|C. M. Grierson]]. ^[extracted]

## Distribution patterns

The mailing-form distribution slips on signal-traffic pages of the file show a standardised SHAEF Air Staff distribution:

```
ACTION  | INFO
A-2 ☑   | A-2
A-3     | A-3
A-4     | A-4
S-11    | S-11
A-O / SMO ☑
```

A-2 (Intelligence) is the action office for both threads; SMO (Senior Medical Officer) is sometimes also marked, suggesting at least informational concern about aircrew effects. ^[inferred]

## Internal divisions referenced

- **A-2 RECCE** (Lt Col T. G. Ames) handled the cylindrical-object photo request. ^[extracted]
- **Scientific Investigation Division** ("Mr. Robertson") was asked to consider the foo-fighter problem. ^[extracted] Likely **H. P. Robertson**, the same physicist who later chaired the [[projects/uap/uap|1953 Robertson Panel]] for the CIA. ^[inferred]
- **Air Division (Main)** — A.D.I.(K)'s reply (SDF/GJ) was addressed to "A/Cdr. Grierson, S.H.A.E.F. Main, (Air Div.)". ^[extracted]

## Place in the larger UAP picture

SHAEF Air Staff is the **first multinational** intelligence node to formally route foo-fighter / unexplained-aerial-phenomena reports. The institutional mechanism — collect, route up, refer to technical-intelligence and to a designated science adviser, accept an inconclusive answer, file the case — is the same mechanism the postwar [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] would adopt and that modern AARO ([[entities/aaro]]) inherits today. ^[inferred] See [[synthesis/foo-fighters-to-flying-discs]].
