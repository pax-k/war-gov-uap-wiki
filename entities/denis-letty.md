# Denis Letty

**Air Force General, 2nd Section** — French *Armée de l'Air* general retired into the *2e section des officiers généraux* (the reserve list of general officers). **Auditeur of IHEDN promotion 35** (AA 35).

Letty initiated and chaired **[[entities/cometa-committee|COMETA]]** — the private committee that produced the 1999 [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]] *UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For?*

## Origin of COMETA

- **February 1995** — Letty organized, within the framework of the **Ecole de l'Air Alumni Association**, a conference on unidentified aerospace phenomena. Former pilots related encounters; the head of UAP study at CNES (i.e., [[entities/gepan-sepra|GEPAN/SEPRA]]) presented results; an unnamed astronomer presented "a scientifically acceptable version of the extraterrestrial hypothesis."
- **March 1995** — Letty visited then-IHEDN director **General Bernard Norlain** to propose a new study committee. Norlain referred him to the IHEDN Auditors Association, which gave its support.
- **~1995–1999** — Letty led the committee through "close to three years" of work.
- **July 1999** — COMETA Report published as a special VSD issue.

## Role in the report

- Authored the second introductory essay of the report ("Consider all of the hypotheses").
- Acknowledged Norlain and André Lebeau as co-enablers.
- Convened the membership and external contributors.
- Took the chair of the COMETA association into which the committee transformed.

Letty's framing in the introduction — *"The accumulation of well-documented sightings made by credible witnesses forces us to consider from now on all of the hypotheses regarding the origin of unidentified flying objects, or UFOs, and the extraterrestrial hypothesis, in particular"* — is the report's normative posture compressed into one sentence. He explicitly inhabits the [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame|scientific-vs-saucer-advocate frame]] from a senior-officer position.
