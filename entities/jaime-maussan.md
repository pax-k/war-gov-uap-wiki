# Jaime Maussan

**Jaime Maussan** (full name **Jaime Maussan Flota**) ^[inferred] is a Mexican journalist and **longstanding public figure in the Mexican UFO / UAP discourse community**. ^[inferred] He appears in the wiki by way of the [[references/dos-cable-mexico-2023-09|MEXICO 2544 cable's ¶11]] — Mission Mexico's report that the Mexican Congress heard testimony on UAP on 12 September 2023, at which Maussan presented **two alleged alien corpses** alongside videos of Mexican-pilot UAP encounters.

## Role in MEXICO 2544 ¶11

The cable identifies Maussan as one of the experts who testified at the **Mexican Congress's 12 September 2023 hearing** on UAP language in the **Aerial Space Protection Law**. ^[extracted] Maussan presented:

- **Two alleged alien corpses** physically displayed in the Congressional chamber (Reuters-credited photo `img-1.jpeg` on p.5 of the cable, captioned *"Mexican journalist Jaime Maussan presented the alleged remains of non-human beings to Congress"*) ^[extracted]
- **Videos of Mexican pilots' encounters with fast-moving flying objects during flight** ^[extracted]

The cable closes the bullet with the editorial observation: *"Scientists have discredited previous alleged alien corpses Maussan presented as evidence of alien life."* ^[extracted] The phrasing — "previous alleged alien corpses" (plural) — implies that Maussan has presented multiple such artifacts in earlier public episodes that have been subsequently rejected by the scientific community. ^[inferred]

## Cross-witness disagreement on the day of the hearing

The cable preserves a **direct contemporaneous public criticism** of Maussan's display by his fellow witness [[entities/ryan-graves|Ryan Graves]]. Graves *"lamented the display took away from his and other pilots' experiences with UAP and expressed disappointment with Maussan's 'unsubstantiated stunt.'"* ^[extracted]

This is the corpus's **first instance of explicit on-the-record disagreement between two witnesses at the same UAP-related legislative hearing**. ^[inferred] The juxtaposition of Maussan's alleged-corpse-display and Graves' US-Navy-pilot-pedigreed experiential testimony is the cable's most informative structural feature for ¶11's UAP-substantive content. ^[inferred]

## Why this entity matters for the corpus

- **First named non-US-government UAP public figure** in the corpus. The wiki's prior named UAP witnesses are all military, intelligence, or law-enforcement personnel (foo-fighter aircrew, USAF officers, FBI special agents, NASA astronauts, federal LE USPERs, intel-community officials, ambassadors). ^[inferred]
- **First Spanish-language-media-anchored figure** in the corpus. ^[inferred]
- **First witness whose corpus-credibility is** *negatively bracketed* **by the relaying institution**. The 1994 [[references/dos-cable-kazakhstan-1994-01|DUSHANBE 00259]] cable bracketed Capt Ed Rhodes' ETI-attribution claim with a flat "WE HAVE NO OPINION"; MEXICO 2544 brackets Maussan's display with the much-stronger *"scientists have discredited previous alleged alien corpses."* ^[extracted] The two cables are 29 years apart and demonstrate a rhetorical-bracketing gradient — neutral-disclaim (1994) vs. relayed-discrediting (2023). ^[inferred]

## Background context (limited corpus knowledge)

The cable does **not** detail Maussan's prior history. The wiki notes the following from external common knowledge ^[inferred]:

- Maussan is associated with the **"Nazca tridactyl mummies"** episode (a series of small humanoid skeletal artifacts presented from ~2017 onward, alleged to be of non-human origin), which independent academic / journalistic analyses have variously identified as composite human-and-animal remains. ^[inferred]
- Maussan's media presence in Mexico predates the modern post-2017 US disclosure era by decades — he is a longstanding TV figure on UFO topics. ^[inferred]
- Maussan's prior public episodes are the most likely referent of the cable's *"previous alleged alien corpses"* plural construction. ^[inferred]

These contextual claims are **unsubstantiated by the corpus** and should be re-anchored to a primary source if Maussan re-surfaces in a future ingest.
