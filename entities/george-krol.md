# George A. Krol

US Foreign Service officer; **Political-Military Counselor** (`POLMINCOUNS`) at [[entities/amembassy-moscow|AMEMBASSY Moscow]] in 2001. ^[extracted from cable] Classifying officer of the [[references/dos-cable-russia-georgia-2001-10|30 October 2001 cable MOSCOW 13169]], which he classified `CONFIDENTIAL` under reason `1.5 (B/D)` of E.O. 12958.

## Why this page exists

Krol is the **first named US-side classifying officer** in the wiki's [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting|diplomatic-channel UAP-keyword cable]] sub-corpus. ^[inferred] The cable's classification block reads:

> *"CLASSIFIED BY POLMINCOUNS GEORGE KROL. REASON 1.5 (B/D)."* ^[extracted]

`POLMINCOUNS` (Political-Military Counselor) is a senior FS-O officer position at large embassies; the role spans bilateral mil-mil affairs, arms-control, defense relations, and overflight / airspace incidents — exactly the substantive range of MOSCOW 13169 (Kodori-Gorge airspace violations, Gudauta withdrawal, CFE / OSCE multilateral framing). ^[inferred]

The classification reason `1.5 (B/D)` is from E.O. 12958 §1.5 (categories of information eligible for classification): **(B)** foreign government information; **(D)** foreign relations or foreign activities of the United States, including confidential sources. The combined `(B/D)` invocation is consistent with a cable carrying **on-the-record characterizations of a foreign-government counterpart's denial posture** — i.e., the cable's substance is exactly what 1.5(B/D) is designed to cover. ^[inferred]

## What the corpus knows about Krol

From the cable text itself:

- **Title and post**: `POLMINCOUNS` at AMEMBASSY Moscow, October 2001. ^[extracted]
- **Authority**: classifying officer for a CONFIDENTIAL embassy cable signed by the Ambassador. ^[extracted]
- **Substantive remit visible in the cable**: airspace violations, military withdrawal (Gudauta), CFE Treaty (`KCFE` TAG), bilateral pol-mil. ^[inferred]

External corroboration (general knowledge, not in the cable):

- George A. Krol was a US career FSO; later served as **US Ambassador to Belarus 2003–2006**, **US Ambassador to Uzbekistan 2008–2011**, **US Ambassador to Kazakhstan 2015–2018**. ^[inferred] His Moscow POLMINCOUNS posting in 2001 fits the early-career-into-Ambassador-track pattern of senior pol-mil counselors. ^[inferred]

## Why he matters for the corpus

- **Classifying-authority distinct from signing-authority**: in the four-cable diplomatic UAP-keyword sub-corpus, this is the **first cable** to break out the classification authority on a separate line from the signature. The 1985 PNG and 1994 Kazakhstan cables are `LIMITED OFFICIAL USE` / `UNCLAS` and do not carry a classifying-officer line; the 2004 Ashgabat cable is `UNCLAS / SBU`. The 2001 Moscow cable is the only `CONFIDENTIAL` item, and the classification block surfaces a second named US-side officer (Krol) alongside the signer (Vershbow). ^[extracted]
- **`1.5(B/D)` reason fits the rhetorical-UFO boundary case**: the cable's substantive load is foreign-government information about Russian denials and (B/D)-flagged characterizations of those denials — not UAP-substantive content. The classification rationale is therefore **independent evidence** that the cable is fundamentally a state-relations / pol-mil item, not a UAP item. ^[inferred]
