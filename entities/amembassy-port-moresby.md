# AMEMBASSY Port Moresby

The **United States Embassy in Port Moresby**, Papua New Guinea — the originating diplomatic post of the [[references/dos-cable-papua-new-guinea-1985-01|28 January 1985 cable PORT M 00199]] reporting the PNG NIO inquiry into [[references/sighting-papua-new-guinea-1985-01-24|24 January 1985 high-altitude overflights]].

## Why this page exists

The embassy is the wiki's **first archived diplomatic-channel intake point** for a UAP-adjacent foreign-government inquiry — see [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting]]. The 1985 cable is the artifact that establishes that pattern in the corpus. As more State Department UAP releases come in, this page can hold the cumulative AMEMBASSY-level institutional behavior for the post.

## What the cable shows the embassy doing

In the [[references/dos-cable-papua-new-guinea-1985-01|28 Jan 1985 cable]], the embassy:

1. **Receives** an *informal* inquiry from the [[entities/png-national-intelligence-organization|PNG National Intelligence Organization (NIO)]] on 28 Jan 1985.
2. **Aggregates** four distinct PNG witness streams (NIO's Wewak officer + Air Niugini commercial pilot + two contrail waves) into a single cable.
3. **Queries internal records** for any US air-asset activity in PNG airspace on 24 Jan 1985.
4. **Performs a `TELCON` with the 43rd Strategic Wing** at Andersen AFB Guam — the operationally relevant US air unit in the region (B-52 capable). ^[inferred]
5. **Issues a negative-confirmation back to NIO**: no B-52, no US aircraft in PNG airspace 24 Jan.
6. **Asks USCINCPAC and the 43rd SW for confirmation** and any further information.
7. **Distributes for information** to AMEMBASSY Canberra, AMEMBASSY Jakarta, and SECSTATE WASHDC.

The cable signature line reads **`GARDNER`** — the surname of the ranking officer at post who released the cable; the standing US Ambassador to PNG in early 1985 is **likely Paul F. Gardner** (ambassador July 1984 – August 1986). ^[inferred] The cable could also have been released by a Chargé under that surname, but Ambassador-level signature is the more common pattern for `O`-precedence cables touching political-level matters.

## Captioning structure

The cable is captioned for action by:

- `STATE FOR EAP/PIA AND PM/RSA` — i.e., the State Department's **Bureau of East Asian and Pacific Affairs / Office of Pacific Island Affairs** (EAP/PIA) and **Bureau of Political-Military Affairs / Office of Regional Security Affairs** (PM/RSA). ^[inferred]
- `USCINCPAC FOR J3 AND POLAD` — the J3 Operations directorate and the Political Adviser at [[entities/uscincpac|USCINCPAC]].
- `43 SW FOR DONO` — the 43rd Strategic Wing's `DONO` line. ^[inferred] (DONO is plausibly the Director of Operations Night Operations or a unit-internal notification line; not confirmed from the cable text.)

The action / info clearance line includes `INR-10` (State Department Bureau of Intelligence and Research) and `CIAE-00` (CIA's State Department liaison) — confirming that the cable was machine-cleared into the intelligence-community read stream as well as the EAP regional stream.

## Cross-references

- The 1985 PNG case is the **first non-Anglophone host-government** UAP-adjacent matter in the corpus. The reports surface to the **PNG Prime Minister** through the East Sepik Provincial Premier ^[inferred] before a US-side disposition is reached.
- The embassy's behavior — query records, TELCON the relevant unified-command unit, formally answer "no US asset" — is the same pattern that AARO inherits ~40 years later for non-attribution carve-outs. ^[inferred] See [[entities/aaro]].
