# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894

A large, multi-year **FBI Headquarters file** under Class **62** (the FBI's catch-all *"administrative miscellaneous"* classification). ^[inferred] Indexed and serialized at FBI HQ in Washington — distinct from the field-office 100-class case files in the wiki's existing FBI corpus (e.g. Detroit `100-26505`, Detroit `100-18221`). ^[inferred] Declassification of the file's content is *"derived from the FBI Automatic Declassification Guide, issued 24 May 2007"*. ^[extracted]

This page is the **parent-file hub** for a planned **17-serial wiki ingest series**. Two serials are now ingested: **serial 220 (Apr 1950)** and **serial 403 (Sep 2022 release tranche of a 1956 artifact)**. The remaining ~15 serials (164, 438, 449, plus sections 1–10 and sub_a) will be ingested in subsequent passes.

## What 62-HQ-83894 appears to be — refined again with N=3 (HEADLINE: prior reading partially refuted)

From **serial 403 alone**, the most economical reading was that 62-HQ-83894 functions as a **content-monitoring / topic-clearinghouse file** at FBI HQ — i.e. HQ clips and serializes publicly-available materials about a topic the Bureau is tracking. ^[inferred] **Serial 220 broadened this** to a **multi-channel content-accumulation-only file** holding clipped publications + unsolicited inbound civilian mail (with FBI in-house translation), but still **no FBI-authored investigative content**.

**Serial 438 is a counter-example that partially refutes the content-accumulation-only framing.** ^[inferred]

The 1964 serial 438 is **FBI-authored field-investigation product** — a 10-page Albuquerque-letterhead investigative narrative on the Lonnie Zamora Socorro NM 24 Apr 1964 landing case, distributed in 4 carbon copies, including same-day on-site physical-trace observations by SA Byrnes, character assessment of Zamora, full witness-statement transcript, and hand-drawn site-trace diagrams. It is **not** clipped material, it is **not** inbound mail, it is the Bureau's own evidentiary work product. See [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]] and [[references/sighting-socorro-nm-1964-04-24]].

**Refined reading:** 62-HQ-83894 is a **UAP-topic file at FBI HQ** that holds **whatever UAP-topic paper crosses HQ's desk regardless of whether the paper is FBI-authored or externally-sourced**. ^[inferred] The file's *agency posture* is **not** "monitor without investigating" — it is **"accumulate UAP-topic paper, including the Bureau's own investigative product on high-profile cases"**. ^[inferred] The intake-channel inventory is now: ^[inferred]

| Channel | Serial | Year | Content type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Clipped public publication** | 403 | 1956 | Publisher's book jacket of [[references/barker-1956-they-knew-too-much\|Barker's *They Knew Too Much About Flying Saucers*]] |
| **(b) Unsolicited inbound civilian correspondence** (foreign-language, FBI in-house translated) | 220 | Apr 1950 (intake) | Spanish-language saucer letter + enclosed Mexican press clipping + sender's diagrams |
| **(c) FBI field-office investigative report on a high-profile UAP case routed up to HQ** | **438 (NEW)** | **May 1964** | **10-page Albuquerque field-office report on the Socorro NM landing case (physical-trace observations + witness interview + diagrams), distributed in 4 carbon copies** |

The **intake-channel count grows from 2 to 3**. The file's **date span widens to ≥ 14 years (1950 → 1964)** ^[inferred]; the upper bound is still unknown. Plausible additional channels the remaining 14 queued serials may surface:

- HQ-side clipping of magazine / journal / newspaper articles (extension of channel (a));
- Citizen letters routed via the White House (analogue of the [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|1957 Krasuski Detroit case]], but at HQ);
- US-citizen unsolicited mail (a domestic counterpart to channel (b)); ^[inferred]
- Inter-agency forwards (USAF, CIA, DOS) of UAP material that would naturally accumulate at HQ; ^[inferred]
- Additional FBI field-office investigative reports on subsequent UAP cases (an extension of the new channel (c)); ^[inferred]
- Director-level outgoing memoranda or institutional-policy material on UAP-topic posture. ^[inferred]

## Position in the FBI corpus

This file is the **earliest FBI-originated UAP-adjacent record location in the wiki**. ^[inferred] Serial 220's **April 1950 intake stamp** moved the FBI-corpus floor back to April 1950, and serial 438's **8 May 1964 Albuquerque report** now extends the file's footprint forward to mid-1964 — bridging the gap between the 1957 Detroit Krasuski sit-down interview and the 2023 parallel FD-302s. ^[inferred] The wiki's FBI corpus now spans **two field-office attributions** (Detroit + Albuquerque) plus modern redacted-field-office FD-302s, all routed up to HQ. See [[entities/fbi]] for the full cross-era table.

With N=3 ingested serials, **the file's "content-accumulation" function is now bimodal**: ^[inferred]

- **Externally-sourced accumulation** (serials 220 + 403): clipped publications + inbound civilian mail. Agency posture: "collect, don't investigate".
- **Internally-authored accumulation** (serial 438): FBI field-office investigative product on a high-profile UAP case routed up to HQ. Agency posture: "the Bureau's own evidentiary work routes to this file too".

The wiki's prior FBI artifacts in field-office files (Detroit 100-26505, 100-18221) all document **agent-driven action** — interviews, citizen-call routing, civilian-org redirects, evidentiary intake. The 62-HQ-83894 file is now confirmed to hold **both** externally-sourced topic material **and** the Bureau's own investigative narratives — i.e. the HQ Class-62 file functions as a **topic-anchored cross-channel accumulator** rather than a single-instrument case file. ^[inferred] This is structurally distinct from the field-office case-file model (one file per substantive case, single instrument).
