# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894

A large, multi-year **FBI Headquarters file** under Class **62** (the FBI's catch-all *"administrative miscellaneous"* classification). ^[inferred] Indexed and serialized at FBI HQ in Washington — distinct from the field-office 100-class case files in the wiki's existing FBI corpus (e.g. Detroit `100-26505`, Detroit `100-18221`). ^[inferred] Note however that **at least one serial (449, this batch) is dual-classified** — simultaneously held under an LA-side Class-100 organizational counterintelligence file (`100-24442-40`) and the HQ-side Class-62 admin-misc filing — so the HQ-vs-100-class distinction is **not absolute** for individual serials in this file. ^[inferred] Declassification of the file's content is *"derived from the FBI Automatic Declassification Guide, issued 24 May 2007"*. ^[extracted]

This page is the **parent-file hub** for a planned **17-serial wiki ingest series**. **Four serials are now ingested**: serial 220 (Apr 1950), serial 403 (1956 artifact, 2022 DIPA release), serial 438 (8 May 1964 Albuquerque report on Socorro NM), and **serial 449 (3 Oct 1966 LA SAC AFSCA-magazine memo, this batch)**. The remaining 13 serials (164 plus sections 1–10 and sub_a) will be ingested in subsequent passes.

## What 62-HQ-83894 appears to be — refined again with N=4 (HEADLINE: channel inventory grows to 4)

From **serial 403 alone**, the most economical reading was that 62-HQ-83894 functions as a **content-monitoring / topic-clearinghouse file** at FBI HQ — i.e. HQ clips and serializes publicly-available materials about a topic the Bureau is tracking. ^[inferred] **Serial 220 broadened this** to a **multi-channel content-accumulation-only file** holding clipped publications + unsolicited inbound civilian mail (with FBI in-house translation), but still **no FBI-authored investigative content**. **Serial 438 partially refuted the content-accumulation-only framing** by introducing FBI-authored field-investigation product. **Serial 449 (this batch) introduces a fourth structural channel**: citizen-political-tip → field-office indexing → HQ filing, with no investigation conducted. ^[inferred]

The 1964 serial 438 is **FBI-authored field-investigation product** — a 10-page Albuquerque-letterhead investigative narrative on the Lonnie Zamora Socorro NM 24 Apr 1964 landing case, distributed in 4 carbon copies, including same-day on-site physical-trace observations by SA Byrnes, character assessment of Zamora, full witness-statement transcript, and hand-drawn site-trace diagrams. It is **not** clipped material, it is **not** inbound mail, it is the Bureau's own evidentiary work product. See [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]] and [[references/sighting-socorro-nm-1964-04-24]].

The 1966 **serial 449 (this batch)** is the entire AFSCA July 1966 magazine clipped and filed, plus a single-page **3 Oct 1966 LA SAC → Director memo** filed jointly under LA Class-100 file `100-24442-40` and HQ Class-62 file `62-HQ-83894-449`. ^[extracted] The intake trigger was **not UAP-interest**: an IRS-employee citizen (Jarvis H. Cooper, Philadelphia) wrote alleging the magazine *"expounded the CP line"*. LA explicitly states *"no investigation has ever been conducted on captioned organization"* and *"no further action is contemplated"*. The UAP material is **incidental to the FBI's filing interest**. See [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449]] and [[entities/amalgamated-flying-saucer-clubs-of-america]].

**Refined reading:** 62-HQ-83894 is a **UAP-topic file at FBI HQ** that holds **whatever UAP-topic paper crosses HQ's desk** — including paper that arrives because the **non-UAP institutional content** of a UAP-topic publication triggered indexing elsewhere in the Bureau. ^[inferred] The file's *agency posture* is **bimodal at N=4**: ^[inferred]

- **Passive accumulation** (serials 220 + 403 + 449, three of four): no investigation, content filed, agency labour limited to translation [220] / clipping [403] / indexing-and-routing [449].
- **Active investigation** (serial 438, one of four): substantive on-scene field-investigative product, distributed in 4 carbons to HQ.

The **intake-channel count grows from 3 to 4**. The file's **date span widens to ≥ 16 years (Apr 1950 → Oct 1966)** ^[inferred]; the upper bound is still unknown. Plausible additional channels the remaining 13 queued serials may surface:

- US-citizen unsolicited mail (a domestic counterpart to channel (b)); ^[inferred]

## Position in the FBI corpus

This file is the **earliest FBI-originated UAP-adjacent record location in the wiki**. ^[inferred] Serial 220's **April 1950 intake stamp** moved the FBI-corpus floor back to April 1950, serial 438's **8 May 1964 Albuquerque report** extended the file's footprint forward to mid-1964, and **serial 449's 3 Oct 1966 LA SAC memo (this batch)** now extends the file's footprint to **late 1966** — co-incident with (but structurally unrelated to) the Oct 1966 Detroit OUFORA SAC redirect filed in `100-26505 serial 6`. ^[inferred] The wiki's FBI corpus now spans **three field-office attributions** (Detroit + Albuquerque + LA) plus modern redacted-field-office FD-302s, all routed up to HQ. See [[entities/fbi]] for the full cross-era table.

With N=4 ingested serials, **the file's content-accumulation function is now bimodal-with-three-passive-channels-and-one-active**: ^[inferred]

- **Externally-sourced passive accumulation** (serials 220 + 403): clipped publications + inbound civilian mail. Agency posture: "collect, don't investigate".
- **Citizen-political-tip passive accumulation** (serial 449, NEW): UAP-publication arrives because **non-UAP institutional content** triggered counterintelligence indexing; HQ files the magazine, no investigation conducted. Agency posture: "index in the right place, no further action".
- **Internally-authored active accumulation** (serial 438): FBI field-office investigative product on a high-profile UAP case routed up to HQ. Agency posture: "the Bureau's own evidentiary work routes to this file too".

The wiki's prior FBI artifacts in field-office files (Detroit 100-26505, 100-18221) all document **agent-driven action** — interviews, citizen-call routing, civilian-org redirects, evidentiary intake. The 62-HQ-83894 file is now confirmed to hold **externally-sourced topic material**, **Bureau-authored investigative narratives**, AND **counterintelligence-indexed UAP-publications** — i.e. the HQ Class-62 file functions as a **topic-anchored cross-channel accumulator** rather than a single-instrument case file. ^[inferred] This is structurally distinct from the field-office case-file model (one file per substantive case, single instrument).
