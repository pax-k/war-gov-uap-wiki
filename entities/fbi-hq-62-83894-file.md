# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894

A large, multi-year **FBI Headquarters file** under Class **62** (the FBI's catch-all *"administrative miscellaneous"* classification). ^[inferred] Indexed and serialized at FBI HQ in Washington — distinct from the field-office 100-class case files in the wiki's existing FBI corpus (e.g. Detroit `100-26505`, Detroit `100-18221`). ^[inferred] Note however that **at least one serial (449) is dual-classified** — simultaneously held under an LA-side Class-100 organizational counterintelligence file (`100-24442-40`) and the HQ-side Class-62 admin-misc filing — so the HQ-vs-100-class distinction is **not absolute** for individual serials in this file. ^[inferred] Declassification of the file's content is *"derived from the FBI Automatic Declassification Guide, issued 24 May 2007"*. ^[extracted]

This page is the **parent-file hub** for a planned **17-serial wiki ingest series**. **Nine artifacts are now ingested**: **section 1 (serials 1-52, 6 Jul - 12 Sep 1947)**, **section 2 (serials 53-100, ~6 Aug - 17 Sep 1947)**, **section 5 (serials 186-245, 15 Jul 1949 - 8 Sep 1950 — this batch)**, serial 130 (12 Sep 1947 ADC Mitchel Field AFBIR-CO compilation), serial 164 (15 Feb 1949 Cabell AIRMEM #4, 17 carbon copies), serial 220 (Apr 1950), serial 403 (1956 artifact, 2022 DIPA release), serial 438 (8 May 1964 Albuquerque report on Socorro NM), and serial 449 (3 Oct 1966 LA SAC AFSCA-magazine memo). The remaining 8 sections / serials (sections 3-4, 6-10, sub_a) will be ingested in subsequent passes.

## HEADLINE — section 5 captures BOTH the FBI (Oct 1947) AND AF (Dec 1949) saucer-investigation terminations + a NEW intake channel (i)

**Section 5 (this batch)** is the **single most institutionally consequential ingested artifact** for the post-cooperation-regime period. ^[inferred] Serial 212 of the section is **[[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|D. M. Ladd's 28 March 1950 Boggs-Hearn memo to Hoover]]**: ^[extracted]

> The Air Force discontinued their intelligence project to determine what flying saucers are the latter part of last year. They publicly announced to the press in December, 1949, that the project had been discontinued. ... You will recall that the investigation of flying saucers was discontinued by the Bureau in October, 1947, in order that the Air Force could take over such investigations. (62-83894-141-160)

The memo confirms **both the Air Force (December 1949 GRUDGE termination) and the FBI (October 1947 internal termination)** had formally exited active saucer investigation. ^[extracted] The Bureau's October 1947 closure ([[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]] terminated by Bureau Bulletin No. 57, 1 Oct 1947 — referenced in section 5 serial 206 ^[extracted]) means **the regime that [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-1|section 1]] spent 52 serials negotiating and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]] spent 47 serials operationalizing was shut down internally within ~6 weeks of full deployment**. ^[inferred] See [[concepts/project-grudge]] (NEW) and [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28]] (NEW).

Section 5 introduces a **structurally novel intake channel** previously unrepresented in the file: ^[inferred] **(i) Press-amplified myth-seed intake via multi-channel laundering** — the **[[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950|Aztec/Newton/Koehler/Scully crashed-saucer myth-seed]]** enters FBI HQ via **four independent intake channels** (Wyandotte Echo OSI TWX serial 203, Denver SAC URGENT serial 206, [[references/fbi-hottel-memo-1950-03-22|Hottel WFO memo serial 209]], New Orleans SAC report serial 213) within ~2.5 months (Jan-Mar 1950). ^[inferred] The structural distinctive: each channel is uncoordinated, but all converge on the same myth-seed package (NM/Mojave + 3-ft humanoids + metallic suits + radar-interference recovery). ^[inferred] The 4-channel filing — without investigation — is the **clearest test case for the post-1947 passive-accumulation function** of 62-HQ-83894. ^[inferred]

Section 5 also captures: ^[inferred]

- **The first radar-tracked critical-facility UAP event in the FBI corpus** — the [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-adcock-1950-03|Oak Ridge / Adcock 1-8 Mar 1950]] 9-day multi-agency dry-run (serials 215-219) — broadest inter-agency UAP coordination through 1950.
- **The first 1949 Navy-originated UAP report in the FBI corpus** — [[references/sighting-shell-oregon-1949-05-27|LtJG Joseph C. Shell Hart Mountain Oregon 27 May 1949]] (serial 197), 5,000+ flight-hour pilot witness, DIO-11ND ONI Confidential Intelligence Report Serial 23-09.
- **The first AF-radar+B-29-chase UAP event in the FBI corpus** — the [[references/sighting-phoenix-b29-chase-1950-06-29|Phoenix B-29 Chase 29 Jun 1950]] (serial 234), 509th Bomb Group Roswell B-29 chasing object Phoenix→Blythe.
- **A bound press article retained as paper-volume content** — the [[references/usnews-flying-saucers-real-1950-04-07|7 Apr 1950 U.S. News & World Report 4-page "Flying Saucers - The Real Story" article]] advancing the Navy-Zimmerman-XF5U-1-experimental hypothesis (filed between serials 218 and 220).
- **6+ confirmed-hoax events** (Elmira NY cardboard saucer, Borden UNM trick photo, Halfery NO Wiesbaden press fabrication, Hixenbaugh Louisville Times match-flip film, Alice TX welded-wings prank, etc.) — strongly reinforcing [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default]].

The intake-channel inventory grows from 8 to 9 with channel (i). ^[inferred]

## Earlier HEADLINE — serial 130 captures the AAF's earliest formal analytical instrument (AFBIR-CO 30 Jul 1947) + a NEW intake channel (h)

**Serial 130 (this batch)** introduces a structural intake channel previously unrepresented in N=7 ingested artifacts: ^[inferred] **(h) Inter-agency analytical compilation product retained at HQ as a discrete serial** — an AAF-compiled multi-document statistical packet (the **30 Jul 1947 AFBIR-CO 18-sighting analysis** plus its 16-document AAF / NRL / Newfoundland source-package), assembled at **HQ ADC Mitchel Field on 12 Sep 1947**, retained as one discrete FBI HQ serial rather than as individual serial-by-serial accumulation. ^[inferred] See [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130]] and [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30]].

The serial-130 packet is **paradigm-defining for the corpus's pre-Twining analytical apparatus**: ^[inferred] AFBIR-CO's 30 Jul 1947 21-field statistical breakdown of 18 selected disc sightings is the **earliest formal AAF analytical UAP instrument** in the wiki, predating the 23 Sep 1947 Twining "AMC Opinion" memo by ~8 weeks and **issued the same day as [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]]** (30 Jul 1947 FBI HQ → all SACs). ^[inferred] AFBIR-CO + BB42 are **synchronous co-published instruments** of the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]] founding. ^[inferred] Serial 130 also preserves the **earliest formal optical-skepticism framework** in the corpus (the 12 Jul 1947 anonymous "[[references/nrl-flying-saucer-mystery-solved-1947-07-12|Flying Saucer Mystery Solved]]" reflection-theory memo), the **earliest commercial-airline-pilot UAP encounter** ([[references/sighting-pan-am-powell-white-1947-08-04|Pan Am Powell-White 4 Aug 1947]]), and the **earliest non-continental-US multi-witness UAP cluster** ([[references/sighting-newfoundland-cluster-1947-07|Newfoundland 9-23 Jul 1947]]). The serial also **partially duplicates** content already preserved in section 2 serial 95 (Arnold autobiographical statement + Smith UA + Johnson Idaho — confirms multi-channel AAF distribution of this work product). ^[inferred]

## Earlier HEADLINE — section 2 captures the AAF-side internal collapse of the prosaic-explanation framework

Section 2 (this batch) is the **operational continuation** of section 1, covering serials 53-100 across ~6 Aug - 17 Sep 1947 — i.e. the ~6-week window in which the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|FBI-AAF cooperation regime]] runs at full throttle, and in which **AAF Intelligence's own internal credibility on the secret-project hypothesis breaks**. ^[inferred] The structurally definitive event: ^[extracted]

- ~22 Sep 1947, Lt Col [[entities/g-d-garrett|G. D. Garrett]] of AAF Intelligence privately tells SA [[entities/s-w-reynolds|Reynolds]] that flying discs are likely a **classified US Government experiment** (and that scientist "Mr. Carroll" agrees) — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2]] serial 86. Reynolds escalates → Forney → Generals Chamberlin + Todd cross-check → all deny → Schulgen agrees a memo will go from McDonald (A2) to LeMay (R&D). Result: **5 Sep 1947 [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]] formal letter to Hoover** (serial 84): "*a complete survey of research activities discloses that the Army Air Forces has no project with characteristics similar to those which have been associated with the Flying Discs.*" ^[extracted]

This is the **closure of the AAF-side prosaic-explanation hypothesis as a working frame** — at the same moment Twining's Wright Field staff are drafting the famous 23 Sep 1947 AMC memo ("real and not visionary or fictitious"). ^[inferred] Section 2 also captures **the first formal FBI rejection of joint AAF investigation under BB42** — Hoover's 30 Aug 1947 Fugate-block teletype on the Rhodes Phoenix interview (serial 68) — and **the first explicit field-office citation of "Bureau Bulletin No. 42, paragraph B"** in operational reporting (Banister Butte SAC on the Hedstrom Twin Falls 19 Aug 1947 mass-formation, serial 66).

## Earlier HEADLINE — section 1 reframes the file as opened-from-day-1, not retroactively-topical

Section 1 introduces the file's **founding tranche** (serials 1-52, 6 Jul - 12 Sep 1947) and **moves the file's date-floor back ~19 months** from 15 Feb 1949 (serial 164) to **6 July 1947** — i.e. **the first day of the FBI's saucer engagement**. ^[extracted] Critically, the section reveals that 62-HQ-83894 was **opened in direct response** to: ^[inferred]

- the 24 Jun 1947 [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Kenneth Arnold]] sighting and the resulting national press wave;
- a **9 Jul 1947 in-person request** from [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Brig. Gen. George F. Schulgen]] (AAF Requirements Intelligence Branch) to [[entities/s-w-reynolds|SA S. W. Reynolds]] (FBI Liaison Section) for the FBI to investigate disc-witness sincerity nationwide;
- Hoover's marginal-note precondition (*"I would do it but before agreeing to it we must insist upon full access to discs recovered"*) — the founding constraint of the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|FBI-AAF cooperation regime]];
- formal codification as [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42, Series 1947]] on 30 Jul 1947.

The framing-shift: the file's earlier prior reading ("*topic-anchored cross-channel accumulator that grew topical retroactively*") is **partially correct but its starting point is not incidental** — 62-HQ-83894 was **operational from day 1** of the FBI's UAP engagement, opened as the documentary infrastructure for the FBI-AAF cooperation regime. ^[inferred] The retroactive-topicalization framing remains valid for serials 164+ (1949-1966, where the file accumulates externally-sourced material in passive-accumulation mode) but **incorrect for the 1947 founding period** (where the file is the active operational record of a deliberate Bureau program). ^[inferred]

## What 62-HQ-83894 appears to be — refined with N=8 (HEADLINE: channel inventory grows to 8; serial 130 adds the analytical-compilation channel)

From **serial 403 alone**, the most economical reading was that 62-HQ-83894 functions as a **content-monitoring / topic-clearinghouse file** at FBI HQ — i.e. HQ clips and serializes publicly-available materials about a topic the Bureau is tracking. ^[inferred] **Serial 220 broadened this** to a **multi-channel content-accumulation-only file** holding clipped publications + unsolicited inbound civilian mail (with FBI in-house translation), but still **no FBI-authored investigative content**. **Serial 438 partially refuted the content-accumulation-only framing** by introducing FBI-authored field-investigation product. **Serial 449 introduced a fourth structural channel**: citizen-political-tip → field-office indexing → HQ filing, with no investigation conducted. ^[inferred] **Serial 164 (this batch) introduces a fifth structural channel**: inter-agency distribution of USAF intelligence directives to the FBI Director, retained at HQ in the topic-anchored Class-62 file. ^[inferred] Critically, serial 164's date (**15 February 1949**) **moves the file's date-floor back ~14 months from Apr 1950 to 15 Feb 1949** — the corresponding artifact is now also the **earliest-dated record in the entire FBI corpus**. ^[inferred]

The 1964 serial 438 is **FBI-authored field-investigation product** — a 10-page Albuquerque-letterhead investigative narrative on the Lonnie Zamora Socorro NM 24 Apr 1964 landing case, distributed in 4 carbon copies, including same-day on-site physical-trace observations by SA Byrnes, character assessment of Zamora, full witness-statement transcript, and hand-drawn site-trace diagrams. It is **not** clipped material, it is **not** inbound mail, it is the Bureau's own evidentiary work product. See [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]] and [[references/sighting-socorro-nm-1964-04-24]].

The 1966 serial 449 is the entire AFSCA July 1966 magazine clipped and filed, plus a single-page **3 Oct 1966 LA SAC → Director memo** filed jointly under LA Class-100 file `100-24442-40` and HQ Class-62 file `62-HQ-83894-449`. ^[extracted] The intake trigger was **not UAP-interest**: an IRS-employee citizen (Jarvis H. Cooper, Philadelphia) wrote alleging the magazine *"expounded the CP line"*. LA explicitly states *"no investigation has ever been conducted on captioned organization"* and *"no further action is contemplated"*. The UAP material is **incidental to the FBI's filing interest**. See [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449]] and [[entities/amalgamated-flying-saucer-clubs-of-america]].

The 1949 **serial 164 (this batch)** is **17 OCR-identical carbon copies of an inbound USAF intelligence directive** — [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell]]'s **AIRMEM #4 "Unconventional Aircraft" of 15 Feb 1949** — distributed by HQ-USAF Directorate of Intelligence to the FBI Director under the directive's own distribution list. ^[extracted] The directive **supersedes residual Army instruments** (DA Coll Memo #7 of 21 Jan 1948 and CSGID 425.1 of 25 Mar 1948) — i.e. the formal Army → USAF doctrinal handover for disc-reporting. ^[extracted] Most striking: AIRMEM #4 Part II names *"FBI records"* as an explicit witness-credibility source, **institutionalizing a reverse-pull from the Air Force onto the Bureau**. ^[extracted] See [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164]] and [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol]].

**Refined reading:** 62-HQ-83894 is a **UAP-topic file at FBI HQ** that holds **whatever UAP-topic paper crosses HQ's desk** — including externally-sourced clipped material, unsolicited civilian mail, FBI-authored field-investigation product, counterintelligence-indexed civilian publications, AND inter-agency intelligence directives that name the FBI Director as a distribution recipient. ^[inferred] The file's *agency posture* is **trimodal at N=5**: ^[inferred]

- **Passive accumulation** (serials 164 + 220 + 403 + 449, four of five): no investigation, content filed, agency labour limited to filing-and-retention [164] / translation [220] / clipping [403] / indexing-and-routing [449].
- **Active investigation** (serial 438, one of five): substantive on-scene field-investigative product, distributed in 4 carbons to HQ.
- **Doctrinal-foundation accumulation** (serial 164, NEW first axis): inbound directive that institutionalizes the Bureau's role as a witness-credibility source for the Air Force — *the file holds the intake doctrine that other agencies use*.
