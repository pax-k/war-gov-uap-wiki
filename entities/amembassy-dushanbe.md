# AMEMBASSY Dushanbe

The **United States Embassy in Dushanbe**, Tajikistan — the originating diplomatic post of the [[references/dos-cable-kazakhstan-1994-01|31 January 1994 cable DUSHANBE 00259]] reporting [[references/sighting-kazakhstan-1994-01-27|three American Tajik Air pilots' 27 January 1994 UAP encounter]] over Kazakhstan.

## Why this page exists

The embassy is the wiki's **second archived diplomatic-channel intake point** for a UAP-adjacent matter — after [[entities/amembassy-port-moresby|AMEMBASSY Port Moresby]] in the 1985 PNG case. Dushanbe represents a **different sub-pattern** of [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting|diplomatic-channel intake]]: where Port Moresby received an inquiry from a *foreign* intelligence service, Dushanbe receives a narrative directly from *American citizen* aircrew flying for a foreign carrier and forwards it on the science-bureau ladder rather than the pol-mil-and-combatant-command ladder.

## What the cable shows the embassy doing

In the [[references/dos-cable-kazakhstan-1994-01|31 Jan 1994 cable]], the embassy:

1. **Receives** an unsolicited witness narrative on **29 January 1994** from [[entities/tajik-air|Tajik Air]]'s chief pilot, AMCIT Ed Rhodes, and his two American pilot colleagues, recounting their 27 Jan 1994 encounter at FL410 over Kazakhstan.
2. **Probes the witness account** with a candidate conventional explanation — "the object might have been a meteor entering and skipping off the Earth's atmosphere" — which the witnesses reject on the basis of their PanAm-era familiarity with meteors.
3. **Cables the report up** to `SECSTATE WASHDC`, action `OES-09` ([[entities/state-department|Bureau of Oceans and International Environmental and Scientific Affairs]] Science office). ^[inferred]
4. **Distributes for information** to AMEMBASSY MOSCOW, TASHKENT, ASHGABAT, ALMATY, BEIJING, BISHKEK, plus CIA WASHDC and DIA WASHDC.
5. **Issues a flat no-posture closer**: "WE HAVE NO OPINION AND REPORT THE ABOVE FOR WHAT IT MAY BE WORTH."
6. **Does not perform any US-asset disambiguation** (no `TELCON` to a combatant command, no negative-confirmation paragraph, no pol-mil action addressee).
7. **Provides a desk-officer routing handle** at the Department: **Lowry Taylor, Tajikistan Desk** — the named referent for any photo follow-on.

The cable signature line reads **`ESCUDERO`** — the surname of the ranking officer at post who released the cable; the standing US Ambassador to Tajikistan in early 1994 is **likely Stanley T. Escudero** (ambassador 1992–1995). ^[inferred]

## Captioning structure (vs. Port Moresby)

| Feature | AMEMBASSY Port Moresby (1985) | AMEMBASSY Dushanbe (1994) |
|---|---|---|
| Action addressee | `USCINCPAC HONOLULU IMMEDIATE` (combatant command) | `SECSTATE WASHDC` (State only) |
| Internal action line | `PM-11` (Pol-Mil) | `OES-09` (Science) |
| Captioned for | `STATE FOR EAP/PIA AND PM/RSA`; `USCINCPAC FOR J3 AND POLAD`; `43 SW FOR DONO` | `DEPARTMENT FOR OES/S` |
| `TAGS` | `MARR`, `PP` | `TSAP, EAIR, KZ, TI, TAJIK AIR, (RHODES, ED)` |
| `UFO` tag | absent | absent |
| US-side disposition | "no US aircraft in PNG airspace" | "we have no opinion" |
| Initiator class | foreign-government intelligence service | US-citizen aircrew |
| Sub-pattern | foreign-liaison-inquiry | citizen-witness-relay |

This pair — Port Moresby and Dushanbe — is the wiki's first concrete evidence that **the diplomatic-channel UAP intake pattern admits at least two distinct routing variants**, which the [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting]] concept page now treats as sub-patterns. ^[inferred]

## Cross-references

- **First post-Soviet, post-Cold-War-era US-embassy UAP-intake post** in the corpus.
- **First commercial-airline-cockpit American-citizen witness** in the corpus to make their report to a US embassy directly.
- The embassy's behavior — receive, probe with a conventional candidate, forward without taking a position — is **structurally similar** to AMEMBASSY Port Moresby's behavior in PORT M 00199, except the US-asset-attribution step is missing because there's no US-asset hypothesis on the table. ^[inferred]
