# 432d Air Expeditionary Wing (432 AEW)

The **432d Air Expeditionary Wing** — the USAF expeditionary wing that supports **forward-deployed MQ-9 Reaper operations**, headquartered at **Creech AFB Nevada ^[inferred]** with deployed elements at multiple AORs. ^[inferred — open-source general background; the wiki's dow-uap sources name the Wing repeatedly but do not state its garrison location.]

This page is a **stub hub**. 432 AEW is the **most heavily-cited operational USAF unit in the dow-uap mission-report corpus**, anchoring the **2020 NAVCENT MQ-9 cluster sextuplet** and the **2022 cross-COCOM ISR lane** spanning USCENTCOM (OIR) and USEUCOM.

## Role in the dow-uap corpus

432 AEW surfaces as the **POC Wing** (Point of Contact — the originating Wing) of every full USMTF Misrep in the dow-uap corpus where unit attribution is preserved. Across N=9 full Misreps:

- **2020 NAVCENT cluster (6 of 9, 8 Aug → 2 Nov 2020)** — paired with [[entities/482-atks|482ATKS]] as POC Unit, [[entities/609-caoc|609 CAOC]] as Operations Center, and (for d64) **379 AEW** as APPROVER Wing. See [[references/dow-uap-d60-mission-persian-gulf-2020-08-08|d60]], [[references/dow-uap-d61-mission-persian-gulf-2020-08-27|d61]], [[references/dow-uap-d62-mission-strait-of-hormuz-2020-09-16|d62]], [[references/dow-uap-d63-mission-strait-of-hormuz-2020-10-02|d63]], [[references/dow-uap-d64-mission-iran-2020-11-02|d64]], [[references/dow-uap-d65-mission-persian-gulf-2020-07-16|d65]]. ^[extracted]
- **2022 USCENTCOM Operation INHERENT RESOLVE (d10, 6-7 May 2022)** — same 432 AEW POC Wing, same 609 CAOC Operations Center, **redacted POC Unit (`1.4a`)** rather than 482ATKS; first cross-operation/cross-theater attestation of 432 AEW outside NAVCENT. See [[references/dow-uap-d10-mission-middle-east-2022-05-06|d10]]. ^[extracted]
- **2022 USEUCOM Eastern Mediterranean (d14, 29-30 May 2022)** — 432 AEW POC Wing with **50 ATKS** as POC Unit and **603 AOC** (Ramstein-based) as Operations Center, **forward-deployed Sigonella AB Italy** for Russian-Mediterranean-Squadron watch (Op HUMMER SICKLE). See [[references/dow-uap-d14-mission-iraq-2022-05-29|d14]]. ^[extracted]

## Significance for the corpus

- **Cross-COCOM organic MQ-9 ISR capacity** ^[inferred] — 432 AEW is the dow-uap corpus's first attested USAF wing that flies **three distinct operational lanes within 2022 alone**: NAVCENT-support (2020 legacy), OIR (d10), and EUCOM (d14). The Wing operates from at least three forward operating locations during the dow-uap window: Al Udeid AB Qatar / Al Asad AB Iraq for CENTCOM tasking, and **Sigonella AB Italy** for EUCOM tasking. ^[inferred]
- **379 AEW APPROVER lane** — at d10 + d64, the APPROVER block carries `379 AEW` (Al-Udeid AB Qatar ^[inferred]), suggesting the APPROVER role tracks 432 AEW POC missions specifically when routed through the 609 CAOC's USCENTCOM tasking lane. Falsified at d12 (163 AW POC) where APPROVER flips to `Other`. The refined reading: **379 AEW tracks 432 AEW POC + 609 CAOC + OIR/NAVCENT tasking**, not OIR-class universally or wing-class universally. ^[inferred] See [[references/dow-uap-d65-mission-persian-gulf-2020-07-16|d65]]'s falsification narrative and [[references/dow-uap-d10-mission-middle-east-2022-05-06|d10]] + [[references/dow-uap-d14-mission-iraq-2022-05-29|d14]]'s refined recovery.
- **Linear-time Misrep ID counter** — the dow-uap full Misrep ID counter (d60 4592219 → d10 7473483 → d12 7528881 → d14 7561279) advances at ~4,500–5,200 IDs/day across **22 months and two COCOMs**, indicating the counter is **pooled at Joint-Staff-tranche / Harrison-MDR-coordinator level**, NOT 609 AOC-rooted, NOT 432-AEW-rooted. ^[inferred]
