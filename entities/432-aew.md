# 432d Air Expeditionary Wing (432 AEW)

The **432d Air Expeditionary Wing** — the USAF expeditionary wing that supports **forward-deployed MQ-9 Reaper operations**, headquartered at **Creech AFB Nevada ^[inferred]** with deployed elements at multiple AORs. ^[inferred — open-source general background; the wiki's dow-uap sources name the Wing repeatedly but do not state its garrison location.]

This page is a **stub hub**. 432 AEW is the **most heavily-cited operational USAF unit in the dow-uap mission-report corpus**, anchoring the **2020 NAVCENT MQ-9 cluster sextuplet** and the **2022 cross-COCOM ISR lane** spanning USCENTCOM (OIR) and USEUCOM.

## Role in the dow-uap corpus

432 AEW surfaces as the **POC Wing** (Point of Contact — the originating Wing) of every full USMTF Misrep in the dow-uap corpus where unit attribution is preserved. Across N=10 full Misreps the Wing operates **four distinct operational lanes within 2022 alone** + the 2020 NAVCENT-cluster legacy:

- **2020 NAVCENT cluster (6 of 10, 8 Aug → 2 Nov 2020)** — paired with [[entities/482-atks|482ATKS]] as POC Unit, [[entities/609-caoc|609 CAOC]] as Operations Center, and (for d64) **379 AEW** as APPROVER Wing. See [[references/dow-uap-d60-mission-persian-gulf-2020-08-08|d60]], [[references/dow-uap-d61-mission-persian-gulf-2020-08-27|d61]], [[references/dow-uap-d62-mission-strait-of-hormuz-2020-09-16|d62]], [[references/dow-uap-d63-mission-strait-of-hormuz-2020-10-02|d63]], [[references/dow-uap-d64-mission-iran-2020-11-02|d64]], [[references/dow-uap-d65-mission-persian-gulf-2020-07-16|d65]]. ^[extracted]
- **2022 USCENTCOM Operation INHERENT RESOLVE — Iraq sub-theater (d10, 6-7 May 2022)** — same 432 AEW POC Wing, same 609 CAOC Operations Center, **redacted POC Unit (`1.4a`)** rather than 482ATKS; first cross-operation/cross-theater attestation of 432 AEW outside NAVCENT. ISO MAG (Marine Aircraft Group ^[inferred]) in UTM 38S MC Iraq/Syria Levant. See [[references/dow-uap-d10-mission-middle-east-2022-05-06|d10]]. ^[extracted]

## Significance for the corpus

- **Cross-COCOM + cross-sub-theater organic MQ-9 ISR capacity** ^[inferred] — 432 AEW is the dow-uap corpus's first attested USAF wing that flies **four distinct operational lanes within 2022 alone**: NAVCENT-support (2020 legacy), OIR-Iraq (d10), EUCOM RUS-MED (d14), and **OIR-Syria (d16, 30-31 Jul 2022)**. The Wing operates from at least **four forward operating locations** during the dow-uap window: Al Udeid AB Qatar / Al Asad AB Iraq for CENTCOM NAVCENT + OIR-Iraq tasking, **Sigonella AB Italy** for EUCOM tasking, and **Muwaffaq Salti AB (OJMS) Jordan** for OIR-Syria tasking (d16). ^[inferred]
- **Bidirectional cross-OC mission class** ^[inferred] — d14 + d16 anchor the **cross-OC mission class at N=2 with first inverse-direction pairing**. d14 = POC 609 CAOC (CENTCOM-rooted) → executing 603 AOC (EUCOM); d16 = POC 603 AOC (EUCOM-rooted) → executing 609 (CENTCOM). The 432 AEW maintains squadrons that route between CENTCOM and EUCOM tasking via the cross-OC mechanism. The 89 ATKS / 603 AOC POC home OC pairing at d16 plausibly anchors a **forward-deployed Sigonella-or-similar EUCOM-homed 432 AEW squadron pool** parallel to d14's 50 ATKS. ^[inferred]
- **379 AEW APPROVER lane** — at d10 + d64, the APPROVER block carries `379 AEW` (Al-Udeid AB Qatar ^[inferred]), suggesting the APPROVER role tracks 432 AEW POC missions specifically when routed through the 609 CAOC's USCENTCOM tasking lane. Falsified at d12 (163 AW POC = `Other`), d14 (50 ATKS POC = `Other`), and **d16 (89 ATKS POC = `Other`)** ^closed-by-dow-uap-d16. At N=5 corpus-testable points 379 AEW stays at **2-of-5 = 40%** — refined reading: **379 AEW lane is OIR-Iraq + 2020-NAVCENT-cluster specific within 432 AEW POC subset**, NOT all 432 AEW POC missions universally. d16's OIR-Syria sub-theater is outside the 379 AEW APPROVER lane. ^[inferred]
- **Linear-time Misrep ID counter** — the dow-uap full Misrep ID counter (d60 4592219 → d10 7473483 → d12 7528881 → d14 7561279 → d16 7816710) advances at ~3,600–5,200 IDs/day across **24 months and two COCOMs and two OIR sub-theaters**, indicating the counter is **pooled at Joint-Staff-tranche / Harrison-MDR-coordinator level**, NOT 609 AOC-rooted, NOT 432-AEW-rooted, NOT POC-home-OC-rooted. ^[inferred] N=5 corroboration points decisively confirm the pooling lane.
