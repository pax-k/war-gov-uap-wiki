# Army G-2

**Army G-2** — the **Assistant Chief of Staff, G-2 (Intelligence)** of the US Army (and within the wiki's 1947–1957 corpus, of the US Army at large including pre-1947 AAF intelligence). G-2 is a standard staff-designation (G-1 personnel, G-2 intelligence, G-3 operations, G-4 logistics) inherited from the WWI/WWII General Staff structure ^[inferred — general historical background; the wiki's sources do not state the G-staff convention's history directly].

This page is a **stub hub**. Army G-2 surfaces across the wiki's FBI UAP corpus as (1) the **doctrinal origin** of the disc-reporting protocol, (2) a **standard recipient** in HQ inter-agency UAP distribution, and (3) a **regional intelligence-coordination layer** in alert/briefing chains.

## Role in the wiki's UAP corpus

- **The CSGID 425.1 disc-reporting protocol (25 Mar 1948)** — the **10-element witness report template** the wiki tracks as [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol]] was originally inherited from Army G-2 (Chief of Staff Intelligence Division) via CSGID 425.1, then formally consolidated under USAF in AIRMEM #4 (15 Feb 1949). ^[inferred] G-2 is the **doctrinal origin** of the post-1947 institutional witness-intake template that propagated across services and into FBI bureau-bulletin practice. ^[inferred]
- **The 19 May 1950 D-3 briefing (Mr. Stiles)** — the Army G-2/G-3 channel forwards a paradigm-defining institutional analytical statement to the FBI file via *"Rec'd from G-3 5-22-50 C.W. J."* — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5]]. ^[extracted]
- **Inter-agency distribution recipient** — when FBI HQ redistributes UAP material to the broader IC (e.g. the 1957 Krasuski case after NSC routing), the **Chief Security Division, Army G-2** is one of 6 standard recipients alongside State (Office of Security), CIA (Deputy Director Plans), Director of Naval Intelligence, OSI/Air Force, and Immigration and Naturalization Service. ^[extracted]
- **The Frances Swan case (1954) routing** — Hoover's 9 Aug 1954 letter to OSI/AF cc DNI **+ cc Army G-2** illustrates the standard tri-service IC distribution in the psychic-channeling UAP case. ^[extracted] See [[references/sighting-frances-swan-1954-07]].
- **Richmond high-alert (8 Dec 1950)** — concurrent **Army Intelligence Richmond high-alert** with the Oak Ridge NEPA radar+visual sequence — see [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file]] / [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-nepa-1950-12-1951-01]]. ^[extracted]

## Significance for the corpus

- Army G-2 is the **upstream doctrinal source** of the [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|disc-reporting protocol]] that USAF inherits in 1949 — the Army → USAF doctrinal handover for disc-reporting (Mar 1948 → Feb 1949) is the corpus's **first inter-service transfer of UAP responsibility**. ^[inferred]
- The **G-2/G-3 briefing channel** documented in section 5 (Stiles 19 May 1950) is the wiki's earliest institutional-analytical UAP-staff-briefing artifact. ^[inferred]
- Army G-2 routing is **bidirectional**: G-2 originates material into the FBI file (CSGID 425.1, D-3 briefing) and also **receives** FBI-side outbound distributions on UAP cases (Krasuski 1957, Swan 1954). ^[inferred]
