# Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)

The FBI is the principal federal investigative agency of the US Department of Justice. The wiki's UAP corpus now contains **FBI-originated UAP-adjacent records spanning 65 years**, from a **1958 Detroit Office Memorandum** routing a citizen sighting to the Air Force, to **two parallel 2023 FD-302 evidentiary interviews** of co-incident witnesses to a contractor-convoy sighting. Across both eras, the FBI appears as an **intake authority** — but with different *instruments*, *response postures*, and *recording conventions* in each.

## Cross-era pattern: FBI as routing vs. FBI as evidentiary recorder

The 1958 and 2023 artifacts together establish that the FBI's UAP-adjacent role has changed in **instrument** and **substantive engagement** between the two eras: ^[inferred]

- **1958, Detroit, [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] / early-BLUE-BOOK era** — when a citizen calls the FBI about a UFO at 0408 hrs, the agency's response is to **forward to USAF**. The recording instrument is a *routing memo*, not an interview form. Compatible with a division of labor in which the **Air Force** (Selfridge / Project BLUE BOOK lineage) was the substantive UAP-investigation authority and the FBI functioned as a **fallback intake channel**. The witness in the 1958 case explicitly tried Selfridge **first** and only called the FBI when the AF channel failed.
- **2023, post-AARO era** — the FBI **takes statements directly** (one per witness) on the agency's standard *judicially-aimed* evidentiary form (FD-302), preserving morphology / behavioural detail that the 1958 office memo would have discarded. There is no analogue of the 1958 "advise proper air force authorities" disposition; the recording happens **in-FBI**.

The shift from **routing instrument** (SF-64) to **evidentiary instrument** (FD-302) tracks the broader institutional shift away from a single-agency UAP authority (USAF / Project BLUE BOOK closed in 1969) toward **multi-agency intake** in the [[entities/aaro|AARO]] era. ^[inferred] It is also consistent with the underlying Sept 2023 case being a **substantive investigation** at the FBI (the FD-302 footer explicitly disclaims any FBI conclusion, but the form itself is reserved for case work) rather than an incidental phone-in. ^[inferred]

## Role in the corpus

- **Intake authority** spanning the late-1950s citizen-call / phone-routing era through the modern multi-agency / multi-witness-FD-302 era.
- **1958**: routing instrument (Office Memorandum) producing **one item per citizen contact**, filed under file `100-18221` in Detroit (item 844 — implying ≥843 prior serials in the same case file). Cross-references file `100-26505`.
- **2023**: judicially-aimed evidentiary form (FD-302) producing **one item per witness** for a single multi-witness incident, with parallel-FD-302 recording preserving cross-witness divergence (cigar-vs-linear shape, bronze-vs-gray color, "saw it" vs "tried-and-failed" on the second-vehicle observation) for downstream analysis. ^[inferred]
- **Standard FBI control disclaimer** in the 2023 footer ("neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI… not to be distributed outside your agency"); the 1958 memo carries no equivalent disclaimer because it was not an investigative product.

## Position in the institutional layering

The wiki distinguishes five institutional layers across its UAP corpus (see [[projects/uap/uap]]). The FBI now sits **structurally adjacent to but distinct from** the other federal LE / intake authorities already in the corpus:

|---|---|---|---|

| **FBI (2023)** | Federal LE intake — multiple parallel FD-302 interviews of co-incident witnesses | **FD-302** narrative form | [[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023]], [[references/fbi-fd302-serial-3-driver-redacted-2023]] |

| [[entities/air-materiel-command]] | 1948 Project SIGN intake hub | USAF correspondence | [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948]] |

Distinctively, the FBI's modern intake instrument is a **judicially-aimed evidentiary form** — FD-302s are recorded with the structural assumption that they may later be relied on in court. ^[inferred] That is operationally distinct from the AARO briefing-deck format and from the DOS cable format — and from the **FBI's own 1958 office-memorandum** form, which is a *routing instrument* in the same agency. The serial-3 / serial-4 pair also shows that the FBI's 2023 intake methodology produces **one FD-302 per witness**, not a single rolled-up multi-witness narrative — a structural choice that preserves cross-witness divergence (cigar-vs-linear shape, "saw it" vs "tried to see and failed" on the second-vehicle observation) for downstream analysis. ^[inferred]

## Witness classes seen across eras

- **1958 (Detroit)** — Single civilian witness ([[references/sighting-detroit-1958-04-17|David Weaver]]), age 23, son of a Detroit policeman, prior Civil Air Patrol experience but self-described as not familiar with aircraft identification. ^[extracted]
- **2023 (FD-302 pair)** —
  - **Contractor drone pilot** (serial-4, FaceTime) — trained-operator class with negative-space familiarity for drones, helicopters, and small aircraft. Sleep-disturbance aftermath. ^[extracted]
  - **Lead-vehicle driver / range or test-site authority** (serial-3, in-person, supervisor present) — long-tenured site authority with airspace-restriction power. Reported social-cost-of-reporting (co-workers ridiculed her). ^[extracted]

This extends the trained-observer-witness pattern catalogued in [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]] to span **untrained civilian witnesses (1958)** through **contracted technical operators *and* on-site test/range supervisors (2023)** — a 65-year arc within a single agency's records. ^[inferred]
