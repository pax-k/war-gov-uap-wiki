# Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)

The FBI is the principal federal investigative agency of the US Department of Justice. In the wiki's UAP corpus it appears as the **intake authority** for **at least two** redacted FD-302 witness interviews from a single September 2023 UAP incident — both drafted **2 October 2023** ([[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023|serial-4]] / [[references/fbi-fd302-serial-3-driver-redacted-2023|serial-3]]) — recording a contractor drone pilot's and the lead-vehicle driver's testimony. The consolidated multi-witness sighting narrative is at [[references/sighting-fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-2023-09]].

## Role in the corpus

- **Intake authority** for the Sept 2023 UAP-adjacent incident, working through **two parallel FD-302 interviews** of two witnesses from the same lead vehicle, plus a contested third (second-vehicle) account.
- Recording instrument: **FD-302** (Rev. 5-8-10) — the FBI's standard narrative-interview evidentiary form. Both interviews conducted by Special Agents on the same drafting date (2 Oct 2023); modalities differed — **FaceTime video** (serial-4, drone pilot) and **in-person** at the witness's office (serial-3, driver) with the witness's supervisor as observer.
- The interviews were filed on redacted FBI **file numbers**, with the standard FBI control disclaimer in the footer ("neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI… not to be distributed outside your agency").
- The release tranche is a **numbered serial** (sources/serial-N_redacted.json); the corpus now holds **serial-3 and serial-4**, implying **at least two more documents** (serial-1, serial-2) likely exist. ^[inferred]

## Position in the institutional layering

The wiki currently distinguishes five institutional layers across its UAP corpus (see [[projects/uap/uap]]). The FBI sits **structurally adjacent to but distinct from** the other federal LE / intake authorities already in the corpus:

Distinctively, the FBI's intake instrument is a **judicially-aimed evidentiary form** — FD-302s are recorded with the structural assumption that they may later be relied on in court. ^[inferred] That is operationally distinct from the AARO briefing-deck format and from the DOS cable format. The serial-3 / serial-4 pair also shows that the FBI's intake methodology produces **one FD-302 per witness**, not a single rolled-up multi-witness narrative — a structural choice that preserves cross-witness divergence (cigar-vs-linear shape, "saw it" vs "tried to see and failed" on the second-vehicle observation) for downstream analysis. ^[inferred]

## Witness class

The Sept 2023 FD-302s establish two witness classes within a single incident:

- **Contractor drone pilot** (serial-4, FaceTime) — trained-operator class with negative-space familiarity for drones, helicopters, and small aircraft. Sleep-disturbance aftermath. ^[extracted]
- **Lead-vehicle driver / range or test-site authority** (serial-3, in-person, supervisor present) — long-tenured site authority with airspace-restriction power. Reported social-cost-of-reporting (co-workers ridiculed her). ^[extracted]

This extends the trained-observer-witness pattern catalogued in [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]] beyond federal LE special agents and intel officials to **contracted technical operators *and* on-site test/range supervisors**. ^[inferred]
