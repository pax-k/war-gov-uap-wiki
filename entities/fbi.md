# Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)

The FBI is the principal federal investigative agency of the US Department of Justice. In the wiki's UAP corpus it appears so far in **one role** — as the **intake authority** for a redacted FD-302 witness interview about a September 2023 UAP sighting by a contractor drone pilot ([[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023]] / [[references/sighting-fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-2023-09]]).

## Role in the corpus

- **Intake authority** for a redacted Sept 2023 UAP witness interview.
- Recording instrument: **FD-302** (Rev. 5-8-10) — the FBI's standard narrative-interview evidentiary form. The interview was conducted by a Special Agent (and one observer) via FaceTime.
- The interview was filed on a redacted FBI **file number**, with the standard FBI control disclaimer in the footer ("neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI… not to be distributed outside your agency").

Distinctively, the FBI's intake instrument is a **judicially-aimed evidentiary form** — FD-302s are recorded with the structural assumption that they may later be relied on in court. ^[inferred] That is operationally distinct from the AARO briefing-deck format and from the DOS cable format.

## Witness class

The Sept 2023 FD-302 witness fits the **contractor / drone pilot** class — a trained observer in a deconflicted-airspace operational context (LiDAR field test). This extends the trained-observer-witness pattern catalogued in [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]] beyond federal LE special agents and intel officials to **contracted technical operators**. ^[inferred]
