# Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)

The FBI is the principal federal investigative agency of the US Department of Justice. The wiki's UAP corpus now contains **FBI-originated UAP-adjacent records spanning 73 years**, from an **April 1950 HQ filing** of an unsolicited Spanish-language Mexican civilian saucer-letter (FBI-translated in-house and filed under HQ admin file 62-HQ-83894 as serial 220), through a **1956 HQ filing of a clipped publisher's book jacket** (62-HQ-83894 serial 403), through a **November 1957 Detroit Director-driven sit-down interview** of a Polish DP citizen who wrote to the White House, **April 1958 Detroit phone-in routing memo**, **October 1966 civilian-research-org redirect to USAF**, to **two parallel 2023 FD-302 evidentiary interviews** of co-incident witnesses to a contractor-convoy sighting. Across all eras, the FBI appears as an **intake authority** — but with different *instruments*, *response postures*, and *recording conventions* in each.

## Cross-era pattern: FBI postures across 73 years

The corpus's FBI artifacts establish that the FBI's UAP-adjacent role has shifted in **instrument**, **field-office attribution**, and **substantive engagement** across 73 years. The **1957 Detroit Krasuski sit-down interview**, the **1964 Albuquerque on-scene Socorro investigation**, and the **2023 parallel FD-302s** are now the corpus's **three primary FBI-authored field-investigation anchors** — three field offices (Detroit, Albuquerque, redacted), three different decades, three different recording instruments (Blank Memo + AIRTEL; Albuquerque-letterhead narrative; FD-302), but **all three** are substantive evidentiary work with explicit on-record witness-character notes. ^[inferred] Strikingly, the **1957 vs 1958 contrast is within the same Detroit field office, ~5 months apart**, and is driven entirely by the **entry path**: ^[inferred]

- **Nov 1957, Detroit — WH-routed letter** ([[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|file 100-26505]]). When a citizen letter arrives **via [[entities/robert-cutler|Cutler]] (Eisenhower's Special Assistant)** and is forwarded to the Bureau, the **Director** sends an URGENT teletype, the field office conducts a **sit-down interview** the next day, and an AIRTEL goes back to the Director with a 3-page narrative Blank Memo enclosed in 5 copies. **The FBI substantively investigates.** No USAF referral on the artifact.
- **Apr 1958, Detroit — citizen phone-in** ([[references/fbi-detroit-100-18221-serial-844-1958|file 100-18221, serial 844]]). When a citizen calls the FBI about a UFO at 0408 hrs, the agency's response is to **forward to USAF**. The recording instrument is a *routing memo* (Standard Form 64), not an interview form. Compatible with a division of labor in which the **Air Force** (Selfridge / Project BLUE BOOK lineage) was the substantive UAP-investigation authority and the FBI functioned as a **fallback intake channel**. The witness in the 1958 case explicitly tried Selfridge **first** and only called the FBI when the AF channel failed.
- **24 Apr → 8 May 1964, Albuquerque — same-day on-site agent presence** ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438|62-HQ-83894 serial 438]]). When [[entities/lonnie-zamora|Officer Zamora]]'s ~5:45 PM radio call comes in to the Socorro NM State Police office's adjacent Sheriff radio room with [[entities/d-arthur-byrnes-jr|SA Byrnes]] physically present **thirty feet down the hall** on unrelated business, the FBI is **on-scene within ~15 minutes** — without a routing pathway, without a citizen letter, without a Director-level entry path. The standing instrument is an **Albuquerque-letterhead investigative narrative** (analogous to the 1957 Blank Memo in form but distinct in field office) plus a **10-page Zamora witness statement** plus **hand-drawn ground-trace diagrams**. The witness-character note is colleague-of-five-years-quality (Byrnes had known Zamora for ~5 years prior to the sighting), structurally **stronger** than Rathbun's 1957 stranger-evaluator note. ^[inferred] The 1964 disposition is **substantive investigation routed to HQ** — not redirect-to-USAF — and the report is filed at HQ Class-62 admin file 62-HQ-83894 alongside externally-sourced UAP-topic material.
- **Oct 1966, Detroit — civilian UFO-org inquiry** ([[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|same file 100-26505, serial 6]]). When the [[entities/oklahoma-ufo-research-association|OUFORA]] director writes asking for early-1966 sighting information, SAC Stoddard's reply is to redirect to **USAF Wright-Patterson AFB**. The 1958 disposition is now extended to **organization-level inquiries**.
- **2023, post-AARO era** — the FBI **takes statements directly** (one per witness) on the agency's standard *judicially-aimed* evidentiary form (FD-302), preserving morphology / behavioural detail that the 1958 office memo would have discarded. There is no analogue of the 1958 "advise proper air force authorities" disposition; the recording happens **in-FBI**.

The 1957 case demonstrates that **the FBI was *capable* of substantive UAP-adjacent investigation in the late-Project-SIGN / early-BLUE-BOOK era** — it just required a Director-level entry path (here, a WH-routed letter). The **1964 Socorro case anchors a different model**: substantive FBI investigation can also happen via **same-physical-location-and-time accident** when a federal LE agent is in the right place at the right time. ^[inferred] The 1958 and 1966 cases demonstrate that, *absent* either of those triggers, the standing Detroit disposition was redirect-to-USAF. The shift to **FD-302 evidentiary instrument** in 2023 tracks the broader institutional shift away from a single-agency UAP authority (USAF / Project BLUE BOOK closed in 1969) toward **multi-agency intake** in the [[entities/aaro|AARO]] era. ^[inferred] It is also consistent with the underlying Sept 2023 case being a **substantive investigation** at the FBI (the FD-302 footer explicitly disclaims any FBI conclusion, but the form itself is reserved for case work) rather than an incidental phone-in. ^[inferred]

## Role in the corpus

- **Intake authority** spanning the late-1950s WH-routed-letter / citizen-call / phone-routing era through the 1960s civilian-research-org redirect era through the modern multi-agency / multi-witness-FD-302 era.
- **1957**: investigative instrument (Blank Memo + AIRTEL) producing **a 3-page witness-interview narrative** plus a 5-copy distribution to the Bureau, filed under `100-26505` in Detroit (the file referenced by the 1958 Weaver "see also" footer).
- **1958**: routing instrument (Office Memorandum) producing **one item per citizen contact**, filed under file `100-18221` in Detroit (item 844 — implying ≥843 prior serials in the same case file). Cross-references file `100-26505` (the [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|Krasuski case]]).
- **1966**: outgoing redirect-letter producing **one item per civilian-research-org inquiry**, filed under `100-26505` (`100-26505-6`).
- **2023**: judicially-aimed evidentiary form (FD-302) producing **one item per witness** for a single multi-witness incident, with parallel-FD-302 recording preserving cross-witness divergence (cigar-vs-linear shape, bronze-vs-gray color, "saw it" vs "tried-and-failed" on the second-vehicle observation) for downstream analysis. ^[inferred]
- **Standard FBI control disclaimer** in the 2023 footer ("neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI… not to be distributed outside your agency"); the 1957 Blank Memo carries an analogous "loaned to you by the FBI, neither it nor its contents are to be distributed outside your agency" footer (page 11 of the Krasuski file). The 1958 routing memo carries no equivalent disclaimer because it was not an investigative product.

Distinctively, the FBI's modern intake instrument is a **judicially-aimed evidentiary form** — FD-302s are recorded with the structural assumption that they may later be relied on in court. ^[inferred] That is operationally distinct from the AARO briefing-deck format and from the DOS cable format — and from the **FBI's own 1958 office-memorandum** form, which is a *routing instrument* in the same agency. The serial-3 / serial-4 pair also shows that the FBI's 2023 intake methodology produces **one FD-302 per witness**, not a single rolled-up multi-witness narrative — a structural choice that preserves cross-witness divergence (cigar-vs-linear shape, "saw it" vs "tried to see and failed" on the second-vehicle observation) for downstream analysis. ^[inferred]

## Witness classes seen across eras

- **1957 (Detroit)** — Single immigrant DP witness ([[entities/wladyslaw-krasuski|Wladyslaw Krasuski]]), Polish, ex-WWII POW at Gut Alt Golssen, employed at Gobel Brewery Detroit since 1951, post-WWII radio-technician training in Freiburg. Reporting **delayed-13-years recall** triggered by November 1957 Levelland TX news on Polish radio. SA Rathbun's character note: "no indication of irrational or otherwise abnormal behavior." ^[extracted]
- **1958 (Detroit)** — Single civilian witness ([[references/sighting-detroit-1958-04-17|David Weaver]]), age 23, son of a Detroit policeman, prior Civil Air Patrol experience but self-described as not familiar with aircraft identification. ^[extracted]
- **2023 (FD-302 pair)** —
  - **Contractor drone pilot** (serial-4, FaceTime) — trained-operator class with negative-space familiarity for drones, helicopters, and small aircraft. Sleep-disturbance aftermath. ^[extracted]
  - **Lead-vehicle driver / range or test-site authority** (serial-3, in-person, supervisor present) — long-tenured site authority with airspace-restriction power. Reported social-cost-of-reporting (co-workers ridiculed her). ^[extracted]

This extends the trained-observer-witness pattern catalogued in [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]] to span **immigrant DP wartime-recall witness (1957)**, **untrained civilian witnesses (1958)**, **contracted technical operators *and* on-site test/range supervisors (2023)** — a **66-year arc** within a single agency's records. ^[inferred]
