# Wilbur B. Smith

A **Canadian Government physicist** with the Department of Transport, identified in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-8|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 8]] serial 346 (9 Aug 1954) as **"Wilber B. Smith, a Physicist of the Canadian Government"** present at [[entities/herbert-b-knowles|Adm Herbert B. Knowles]]'s South Berwick ME residence on **24-26 July 1954** in an **unofficial capacity**, alongside his family ^[extracted]. Smith is widely understood in UAP scholarship to be the principal of Canada's **Project Magnet** (1950-54), a Department of Transport / Defence Research Board UAP-and-geomagnetism research program with at points an explicitly ETI-friendly working hypothesis. ^[inferred]

## Role in the Swan case (Jul-Aug 1954)

- Present at Knowles's residence 24-26 Jul 1954 simultaneously with BuAer Security Officer John Hutson during the FBI/Navy intake of [[entities/frances-swan|Frances Swan]]'s [[concepts/psychic-channeling-uap|psychic-channeling]] claim. ^[extracted]
- Both Hutson and Smith *"were looking for proof and they wanted to know whether they could contact the man from 'outer space.'"* ^[extracted]
- Smith stated he would attempt **radio contact on Sunday 1 Aug 1954** using a **high frequency**; Swan would notify the senders so they could bring a ship within **100 miles of Ottawa, Canada** to make the contact unambiguous. ^[extracted]
- Outcome of the 1 Aug 1954 radio test is not preserved in serial 346. ^[ambiguous]

The presence of a sitting Canadian Government physicist at the same residence as a US Navy security officer during a US-Federal-Government UAP intake interview is **structurally novel** in the FBI corpus ^[inferred]:

- Confirms an **ad-hoc US-Canadian unofficial cooperation thread** on psychic-channeling-style UAP cases.
- Smith's framing of an actual radio-contact test (with Swan as relay) operationalizes the channeling claim into an experimentally falsifiable form.
- The meeting is informal — no Canadian DRB / DOT formal liaison is recorded — which suggests **personal-network operations** at the institutional fringes, not a state-to-state collaboration.

## Project Magnet context

Project Magnet (Canada DOT 1950-54) is referenced **by name in section 8 serial 358** ^[extracted] — Charles A. Yost (St. Clair Shores MI civilian electric-fields researcher) writes to a contact in Alaska that his electric-fields work is *"not a 'copy' of what Keyhoe mentioned in his recent book relating to the Canadian Project."* ^[extracted] This makes serial 358 the **first by-name FBI-corpus reference to Canadian Project Magnet** ^[inferred] and confirms that Project Magnet was sufficiently public knowledge by Nov 1953 ([[references/barker-1956-they-knew-too-much|Keyhoe]] published the reference in *Flying Saucers from Outer Space*, 1953) for a US civilian researcher to distinguish his work from it.

## Position in the corpus

- **First Canadian Government technical principal** in the FBI HQ UAP corpus ^[inferred];
- **First foreign-government physicist** recorded as unofficially-present at a US-Federal-Government UAP intake interview ^[inferred];
- **First proposed radio-contact-test in the FBI corpus** (1 Aug 1954, high frequency, 100 miles of Ottawa) ^[inferred];
- **Indirectly referenced as "the Canadian Project"** in the same section 8 (serial 358, Yost letter), suggesting Project Magnet was institutionally legible across the section. ^[inferred]
