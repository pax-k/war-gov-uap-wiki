# Oklahoma UFO Research Association (OUFORA)

## Documented activity in the corpus

On **2 October 1966**, Liesch wrote to the **FBI Office, 913 Federal Building, Detroit, Michigan**, asking for "any information that you could send us on Unidentified Flying Objects, specifically those sightings in your area in early spring of this year."

On **11 October 1966**, **Paul H. Stoddard** (SAC, FBI Detroit, P.O. Box 2118, Detroit 48231) replied that "this office does not have the information you have requested. It is possible that the United States Air Force, **Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Dayton, Ohio**, may be the proper agency to contact."

The exchange is preserved in [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|FBI Detroit file 100-26505]] at serial `100-26505-6`.

## Position in the corpus

- **First civilian UFO research organization** in the wiki to formally approach the FBI for sighting information.
