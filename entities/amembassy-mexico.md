# AMEMBASSY Mexico

The **US Embassy in Mexico City** — the diplomatic mission of the United States to the **United Mexican States** and the originating post of `MEXICO 2544` (the **23 MEXICO 2544** weekly political blotter cable, 16 Sep 2023, [[references/dos-cable-mexico-2023-09|covered separately]]).

## Why this page exists

AMEMBASSY Mexico is the wiki's **fifth archived diplomatic post that produced a State Department cable surfaced in a UAP-keyword FOIA release** — after [[entities/amembassy-port-moresby|AMEMBASSY Port Moresby]] (1985 PNG, sub-pattern A), [[entities/amembassy-dushanbe|AMEMBASSY Dushanbe]] (1994 Kazakhstan, sub-pattern B), [[entities/amembassy-moscow|AMEMBASSY Moscow]] (2001 Russia-Georgia, Type-2 figurative boundary), and [[entities/amembassy-ashgabat|AMEMBASSY Ashgabat]] (2004 Turkmenistan, Type-1 referential boundary).

It is also the **first Western-Hemisphere** originating post in the corpus's diplomatic UAP-keyword sub-corpus, the **first post-2017-disclosure-era** post, and the post that **anchors sub-pattern C** of [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting|the diplomatic-channel UAP reporting]] umbrella — *host-country legislative-event reportage*. ^[inferred]

## Institutional position

- **Chief of Mission**: Ambassador (cable signature `SALAZAR`; almost certainly **Ken Salazar**, US Ambassador to Mexico May 2021 – ~2024). ^[inferred]
- **Released-by officer on the cable**: `MEXICO:Moreno, Sergio A` — Foreign Service officer in Mission Mexico's executive office. ^[extracted]
- **Approval block on the cable**: `EXEC:Johnson, Mark` — likely DCM (Deputy Chief of Mission) or other senior executive officer. ^[inferred]
- **Drafting/clearing block**: spans POL, EXEC, ECON, PD, INL/PROG, CONS/AG sections — five drafters and six clearers, characteristic of a **multi-section weekly product** rather than a single-officer cable. ^[inferred]

The embassy is a **large mission** in DOS terms — Mexico is one of the most consequential US bilateral relationships, the embassy maintains numerous specialised sections (POL, EXEC, ECON, PD, INL/PROG, CONS/AG visible in the cable; numerous others not visible here), and the post's information distribution defaults to NSC, OVP, DNI, CIA, DIA, NORTHCOM, DHS, DOJ, SOUTHCOM, all WHA posts, and all US consulates in Mexico. ^[inferred]

## What this entity contributes to the corpus

**Sub-pattern C anchor** for the diplomatic-channel umbrella. AMEMBASSY Mexico is the originating post of the only cable in the corpus where the embassy's role is **passive observation and forwarding** of a host-country political event — neither a foreign-service inquiry (sub-pattern A, PNG) nor a US-citizen-witness account (sub-pattern B, Kazakhstan). The cable's UAP content (¶11) is **embedded in a periodic non-UAP-primary product** — the embassy aggregates host-country political activity weekly and relays it to State HQ; UAP content surfaces only when a host-country political event happens to involve UAP. ^[inferred]

The embassy is also the corpus's **first Western-Hemisphere** UAP-keyword originating post, **first post-2017** UAP-keyword post, and the **first** post whose UAP cable is signed by a person other than the cable's primary drafter. ^[inferred]
