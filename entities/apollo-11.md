# Apollo 11

NASA's **Apollo 11** — the **first crewed lunar landing**, launched **16 July 1969** and recovered **24 July 1969**. Crew: **[[entities/neil-armstrong|Neil A. Armstrong]]** (Commander), **[[entities/buzz-aldrin|Edwin E. "Buzz" Aldrin Jr.]]** (Lunar Module Pilot), and **[[entities/michael-collins|Michael Collins]]** (Command Module Pilot). ^[inferred] Spacecraft callsigns: **CSM "Columbia"** and **LM "Eagle"**. ^[inferred]

In the wiki's UAP corpus, Apollo 11 enters as the **mission frame** for the **[[references/nasa-uap-d4-apollo-11-technical-crew-debriefing-1969|d4 Technical Crew Debriefing]]** — chronologically the **earliest Apollo-mission artifact** in the corpus, predating [[entities/apollo-12|Apollo 12]]'s d1 voice loop by 4 months and [[entities/apollo-17|Apollo 17]]'s d5/d6 debriefings by 3.5 years. The d4 artifact contains the corpus's **first sub-class E (strong-positive) on-orbit observation** — the famous **Day-3 "second look" cylinder / open-suitcase translunar object** observed by all three crewmen ([[references/sighting-apollo-11-cylinder-1969-07]]) — and the corpus's **first 1969-vintage [[concepts/cosmic-ray-light-flashes|cosmic-ray retinal flash]]** characterization (Aldrin primary, Armstrong corroborating).

## Mission record (UAP-relevant only)

| Date / phase | Observation | Witness(es) | Wiki anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **~18 Jul 1969 UTC, ~Day 3 translunar coast (PTC)** | **Cylinder / two-rings / open-suitcase morphology** unidentified object observed through cabin window → monocular → sextant; **S-IVB explicitly ruled out** by Mission Control range (6,000 mi away); urine dump ruled out; jettison debris chronologically incoherent; **deliberately inconclusive** crew conclusion | [[entities/neil-armstrong\|Armstrong]] + [[entities/buzz-aldrin\|Aldrin]] + [[entities/michael-collins\|Collins]] (three-witness simultaneous) | [[references/sighting-apollo-11-cylinder-1969-07]] |
| **Days 2–8** (in-cabin, dark-adapted) | **In-cabin "double flashes" / "penetration"** observation; ~1 flash/min; entry-impact two-point structure; partial sun-direction correlation; static-electricity rule-out | [[entities/buzz-aldrin\|Aldrin]] (primary, "second night" onset); [[entities/neil-armstrong\|Armstrong]] corroborating on last night with *"50 significant observations"* in 1 hr | [[concepts/cosmic-ray-light-flashes]] / [[references/nasa-uap-d4-apollo-11-technical-crew-debriefing-1969]] |
| **Lunar orbit, post-LM-ascent, approaching CDH** | Bright light source initially called *"a possible laser"* to Mission Control; **post-flight self-reattributed** to **specular reflection of the Sun off a smooth body of water (a lake)** | [[entities/buzz-aldrin\|Aldrin]] | [[references/nasa-uap-d4-apollo-11-technical-crew-debriefing-1969]] §21.5 |
| **Transearth coast** | Mars *"coming from a region where it couldn't come from"* — astronaut-self-attributed as **optical illusion** (Armstrong: *"immediately adjacent to the horizon"*) | [[entities/buzz-aldrin\|Aldrin]] | [[references/nasa-uap-d4-apollo-11-technical-crew-debriefing-1969]] §21.4 |

The d4 artifact is **strong-positive** (sub-class E in the d-cluster typology) — Apollo 11's Day-3 observation is the corpus's **single most-resistant-to-conventional-explanation astronaut-class observation**, owing to (a) three-witness simultaneity, (b) instrument-mediated morphology resolution, and (c) Mission Control range elimination of the S-IVB hypothesis. The Aldrin in-cabin flashes are **not** UAP-class but are corpus-relevant as the **earliest first-principles characterization of the [[concepts/cosmic-ray-light-flashes|cosmic-ray retinal flash phenomenon]]** that NASA later instrumented through ALFMED on [[entities/apollo-17|Apollo 17]]. ^[inferred]

## Mission profile (overview)

- **Launch vehicle**: Saturn V (AS-506). ^[inferred]
- **Launch**: 16 July 1969, 13:32 UTC, Kennedy Space Center LC-39A. ^[inferred]
- **TLI burn**: ~16 Jul 1969 16:22 UTC. ^[inferred]
- **S-IVB jettison + slingshot to solar orbit**: post-CSM/LM extraction; deflection burn placed S-IVB on lunar-flyby + solar-orbit trajectory. ^[inferred]
- **Lunar orbit insertion**: 19 Jul 1969 17:21 UTC. ^[inferred]
- **Lunar landing**: **20 Jul 1969 20:17 UTC** at Mare Tranquillitatis (Tranquility Base). ^[inferred]
- **First lunar EVA**: 20–21 Jul 1969 (Armstrong + Aldrin); EVA duration ~2.5 hr; Collins remained in lunar orbit aboard CSM *Columbia*. ^[inferred]
- **LM ascent and rendezvous**: 21 Jul 1969. ^[inferred]
- **LM "Eagle" jettison**: ~21 Jul 1969, post-rendezvous. ^[inferred]
- **Trans-Earth Injection (TEI)**: 22 Jul 1969. ^[inferred]
- **Recovery**: 24 Jul 1969 by **USS Hornet (CV-12)** in the Pacific. ^[inferred]
- **Total mission duration**: ~8 days 3 hours. ^[inferred]
- **Distinguishing role in Apollo program**: **first crewed lunar landing**; first humans on the surface of another celestial body; satisfied [[entities/jfk|JFK]]'s 1961 "before this decade is out" goal. ^[inferred]

The biographical / mission detail above is **not in the d4 OCR excerpt** and is filled in from general program knowledge — flagged `^[inferred]` accordingly. The d4 excerpt's confirmable mission-profile facts are: PTC active during Day-3 translunar coast; high-gain antenna anomaly during the same period; LM jettison releases Mylar / docking-ring debris (Day 8); §21.0 Visual Sightings as a structured Vol II chapter heading.

## Institutional context

Apollo 11 was operated under the **[[entities/nasa|NASA]] / Manned Spacecraft Center (MSC) Houston** organizational chart. The d4 Technical Crew Debriefing was prepared by the **Mission Operations Branch, Flight Crew Support Division, MSC** — **distinct from** the **Training Office, Crew Training and Simulation Division** that prepared the [[references/nasa-apollo-17-technical-crew-debriefing-1973|d6 MSC-07631 Apollo 17 Technical Crew Debriefing]] 3.5 years later. ^[inferred] This is a **sub-office distinction** within MSC that the wiki's [[entities/nasa]] page should track — the Apollo 11 era used a different MSC sub-office for the technical debriefing than the late-Apollo era did.

The mission **straddles the active-but-terminal phase of [[concepts/project-sign|Project BLUE BOOK]]** (closed 17 December 1969, ~5 months after Apollo 11 splashdown). Whether BLUE BOOK opened a case file on the Day-3 cylinder observation is **open**. ^[open] The 24 Jul 1969 splashdown to 17 Dec 1969 BLUE BOOK closure window is exactly **5 months** — long enough for a routine BLUE BOOK case-file to be opened, processed, and closed if the technical debriefing's classification permitted disclosure to the USAF investigative system. The d4 excerpt does not preserve any BLUE BOOK or AFOSI cross-routing artifact.

The Apollo 11 d4 artifact is the corpus's **first sub-class E entry** and **chronologically earliest Apollo-program artifact**. ^[inferred] It also contains the corpus's **first cosmic-ray-flash testimony** — predating Schmitt's d6 ALFMED testimony by 3.5 years and demonstrating that the phenomenon was **astronaut-class characterizable on first principles** before NASA instrumented the follow-up.

- Locate the **Apollo 11 Mission Report (MSC-00171, Nov 1969)** for any treatment of the Day-3 cylinder observation outside the technical debriefing channel. ^[open]
- Cross-reference whether **Project BLUE BOOK** (closed 17 Dec 1969) or **AFOSI** opened any file on the Day-3 cylinder observation; the 24 Jul – 17 Dec 1969 window is exactly 5 months. ^[open]
- Cross-reference the **Apollo 11 onboard 70 mm Hasselblad and 16 mm DAC film** for any frames bearing on the Day-3 cylinder observation. ^[open] Aldrin's d4 observation about the lunar-orbit "laser/lake" — *"In the film, it didn't appear as though this was going to show up at all. The Earth was too bright"* — is suggestive that the Day-3 observation may also be missing from the film record. ^[inferred]
- Cross-reference the **NORAD / US Space Surveillance Network catalog** for any tracked objects near Apollo 11 on Day 3 (~18 Jul 1969 UTC). ^[open]
- Identify the **specific lake** Aldrin's "laser/lake" reattribution targeted on Earth, given the Day 7 / Day 8 transearth-coast Earth-disk geometry. ^[open]
- Resolve **why Vol II of the d4 debriefing was actively declassified 6/1/72** under E.O. 11652 (~3 years post-mission) while Vol I appears to have been declassified through a separate, undated path (RF 74365 footer authority). ^[open]
- Cross-reference the **[[entities/apollo-11-pao|Apollo 11 PAO press releases (24–31 Jul 1969)]]** for any public mention of the Day-3 observation prior to the technical debriefing's classification. ^[open]
- Cross-reference **Aldrin's autobiographies** (*Return to Earth* 1973, *Magnificent Desolation* 2009, *No Dream Is Too High* 2016) for any subsequent treatment of the Day-3 observation outside the official debriefing record. ^[open]
