# Richard F. "Dick" Gordon Jr.

**Richard F. "Dick" Gordon Jr.** (1929–2017) — **US Navy naval aviator** and **NASA Astronaut Group 3** ("the Fourteen", 1963 — same group as **[[entities/gene-cernan|Gene Cernan]]**), Command Module Pilot of **[[entities/apollo-12|Apollo 12]]** (14–24 Nov 1969), the **second crewed lunar landing**. ^[inferred] In the wiki's UAP corpus, Gordon enters as a **named speaker** on the **[[references/nasa-uap-d1-apollo-12-transcript-1969|d1 Apollo 12 air-to-ground voice loop excerpt]]** at MET ~5d 19h 20m onward, on the **rendezvous greetings** with CAPCOM and the **post-rendezvous sextant search** for the LM that anchors **[[entities/pete-conrad|Conrad]]**'s tracking-light burnout exchange.

## Apollo 12 d1 transcript lines

The d1 OCR excerpt preserves Gordon as **CMP** on the Tape 90/3 + Tape 93/8 segments:

> **CMP** *(Gordon)*: *"Hello, Houston; Yankee Clipper."* ^[extracted]
>
> **CC**: *"Yankee Clipper, Houston. Loud and clear."* ^[extracted]
>
> **CMP** *(Gordon)*: *"Well, hello there, stranger. How are you?"* ^[extracted]

(Tape 90/3, MET ~5d 19h 20m — first contact between Gordon in CSM and CC after a quiet stretch; Gordon is in lunar orbit aboard *Yankee Clipper* awaiting LM ascent and rendezvous.) ^[inferred]

> **CMP** *(Gordon)*: *"But I don't have you in the sextant. That's okay. Your blinking light's just not blinking, that's all."* ^[extracted]
>
> *(later — after Conrad reports the tracking light burned out)*
>
> **CMP** *(Gordon)*: *"You're — they're — You're flying through the air backwards, then, Pete, because I don't see it."* ^[extracted]

(Tape 93/8, MET ~6d 00h 21m — post-rendezvous configuration; Gordon is searching for *Intrepid* through the CSM sextant and reports a non-flashing light, prompting Conrad's tracking-light burnout assessment.) ^[extracted]

The lines are **not** UAP reports; they are **operational rendezvous chatter**. Gordon's contribution to the d1 corpus entry is to **establish the post-rendezvous configuration** (CSM-LM in close proximity, CSM searching for LM through the sextant, LM tracking-light filament burned out) that frames Conrad's empirical co-orbital-debris confirmation. ^[inferred]

## Witness pedigree

Gordon is in the same **astronaut-class** witness category as the other Apollo CMPs in the corpus (**[[entities/jim-lovell|Lovell]]** — GT-7 Pilot rather than CMP, but same Group 2 cohort; **[[entities/ronald-evans|Evans]]** — [[entities/apollo-17|Apollo 17]] CMP). Distinguishing feature: Gordon's d1 transmissions are **operational** (rendezvous mechanics) rather than observational (the AOT particle-flash phenomenology that Bean reports). ^[inferred] His on-record contribution to the d1 artifact is the corpus's **first direct astronaut-to-astronaut exchange** between separate spacecraft (LM-CSM rendezvous) — vs. the GT-7 1965 entry, in which Borman and Lovell are aboard the same spacecraft. ^[inferred]
