# Amalgamated Flying Saucer Clubs of America (AFSCA)

A **mid-1960s American civilian saucer / contactee organization** headquartered at **2004 N. Hoover St., Los Angeles, CA 90027** (phone 662-4404). ^[extracted] Self-described in the 1966 issue as a *"Non-profit Organization … Dedicated to The Physical, Spiritual, and Economic Emancipation of Man"*. ^[extracted]

AFSCA enters the wiki corpus through **FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894 serial 449** ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449]]) — the **3 October 1966 LA SAC → Director memo** captioned *"FLYING SAUCERS INTERNATIONAL, OFFICIAL JOURNAL OF THE AMALGAMATED FLYING SAUCER CLUBS OF AMERICA, INC. / IS - MISCELLANEOUS"*, with the entire **July 1966 Issue No. 24** of AFSCA's magazine clipped and filed as the substantive content of the serial. ^[extracted]

**Section 10 (serial 448, 6 Sep 1966)** adds a **parallel intake channel** for the same Issue No. 24 ^[extracted] — Mrs. Loretta G. Dow of Goffstown NH (NICAP member, ~63 years old, NH Insurance Co reinsurance manager) writes Hoover on 31 Aug 1966 enclosing the **AFSCA "3rd National Flying Saucer Convention" flyer** (Reno NV, 8-10 Jul 1966) + the **July 1966 Issue No. 24** of *Flying Saucers International* — same content as serial 449, but received **27 days earlier** via citizen-letter routing rather than via Philadelphia → LA SAC re-routing. ^[inferred] Mrs. Dow's letter expresses concern that AFSCA *"isn't just what it appears on the surface … with my limited idea of how Communists operate it strikes me it is backed by them"* — the same CP-line concern that the Philadelphia/LA chain processed at serial 449. ^[extracted] Hoover's 6 Sep 1966 reply is the standard *"this Bureau is strictly an investigative agency … neither makes evaluations nor draws conclusions"* form-letter. ^[extracted] The **dual-channel filing** (448 + 449) of identical magazine content within ~4 weeks is structurally distinctive — most FBI corpus contactee-magazine filings are single-channel. ^[inferred]

## What AFSCA was, structurally

AFSCA is a **contactee-movement organization**, distinct from the wiki corpus's other civilian-research entities ^[inferred]:

- It is **not** a research-survey organization in the [[entities/oklahoma-ufo-research-association|OUFORA]] / NICAP mode (no field-investigation methodology, no incident-summary forms, no analyst-evaluator structure).
- It is **not** a publishing-research operation in the [[entities/gray-barker|Gray Barker]] / *Saucerian* mode (Barker focused on collecting researcher-witnessed events and documenting silencing patterns; AFSCA centers on contactee testimony and channelled extraterrestrial messaging).
- It is **a "saucer-movement" / contactee umbrella**: it organizes conventions and meetings around speakers who claim direct contact with extraterrestrial intelligences — typically by face-to-face meeting on Earth, by radio (Bob Renaud's "Korendor" claim), by telepathic appointment, or by being taken aboard spacecraft. ^[inferred]

The 21-person speaker roster for AFSCA's 3rd National Convention is the **first single-artifact named-contactee roster** in the wiki: ^[extracted]

> Wayne S. Aho, Carl A. Anderson, Orfeo Angelucci, Michael "X" Barton, Wesley Bateman, Marianne Francis, Daniel W. Fry, Calvin C. Girvin, Gabriel Green, George King (Aetherius Society), Johannes "Hanno" Mayberry, Howard Menger, Laura Mundo, Mel Noel, Sidney Padrick, Chief Frank B. Standing Horse, Frank E. Stranges, Mollie Thompson, Hope Troxell, George W. Van Tassel, Hal Wilcox.

These are widely-recognized 1950s–60s American contactee figures ^[inferred]; the corpus does **not** anchor entity pages for any of them at this single-artifact ingest.

## What the FBI did, and didn't do, with AFSCA

The 3 October 1966 LA SAC memo's findings on AFSCA are explicit ^[extracted]:

> *"The indices of the Los Angeles Division contain no information identifiable with RENAUD, and no investigation has ever been conducted on captioned organization. … No further action is contemplated in this matter by the Los Angeles Office, UACB."*

Three observations: ^[inferred]

1. **AFSCA had a pre-existing LA-side file**. The memo carries the LA file number `100-24442-40` — i.e. AFSCA was already indexed under the LA Class-100 (counterintelligence / domestic-security) prefix at serial 40 by Oct 1966. The October 1966 Cooper-tip serial is the 40th item in `100-24442`. What's in serials 1–39 is unknown; the LA-side file at **40 prior serials** implies AFSCA had been on the LA radar for some period of time before October 1966. ^[inferred]
2. **Despite the indexing depth, no LA investigation had ever been conducted.** This is the same disposition pattern the corpus has seen on the FBI's other 1960s civilian-saucer-organization items: pass through, redirect, but do not investigate. ^[inferred] The Cooper "CP-line" tip did not change the LA disposition.
3. **The HQ-side filing (62-HQ-83894 serial 449) is content-accumulation**, consistent with the parent file's broader **(channel d) citizen-political-tip → field-office indexing → HQ filing** pattern. ^[inferred] The Bureau is on this evidence **not investigating AFSCA** but **collecting AFSCA-derived material at HQ** when it arrives via citizen-tip routing.
