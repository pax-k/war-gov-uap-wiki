# AARO

The **All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office** — a U.S. Department of Defense-affiliated office responsible for receiving, reviewing, and assessing reports of unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) across all operating domains. ^[inferred] (the slide deck never expands the acronym; identification follows from public knowledge of the AARO program)

This page is currently a stub anchoring AARO mentions across the wiki. It will be expanded as more AARO-handled material is ingested.

## Role observed in source

In the [[references/western-us-event-2026|Western U.S. Event]] slide deck, AARO appears as the post-hoc assessment authority that:

- **Re-measured the range** of the [[references/sighting-fiery-orb-western-us|Fiery Orb]] — witness 500–600 m → AARO ~1050 m.
- **Re-estimated object size** — witness "small helicopter cockpit" → AARO 12–18 m diameter.
- **Reclassified the [[references/sighting-dark-kite-western-us|Dark Kite]] shape** in later discussions as **triangular**.

This implies AARO has the capability to conduct **physical site survey / photogrammetry / line-of-sight measurement** after the fact. ^[inferred]
