# Jacques Vallée

**Jacques F. Vallée** (b. 1939) — French-born astronomer, mathematician, and computer scientist whose first FBI-corpus appearance is in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-10|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 10]] **serial 462 enclosure (Aug 1967)** — the *UFO CONTACT igap journal Vol 2 No 5 (Jun 1967)* reproducing ICUFON's UN-Outer-Space-Conference invitation roster, which names Vallée in **tier B (Scientific Researchers)** as *"mathematician and astronomer of Northwestern University, Mars Map Project. USA"*. ^[extracted] (Note: the OCR omits the acute accent on the surname; the entity title preserves it.)

## Wiki primary-source mentions

### Section 10 serial 462 enclosure (ICUFON Press Release No. 6, 2 Feb 1967)

The ICUFON Enclosure No. 1 roster — VonKeviczky's proposed delegate list for the U.N. Peaceful Uses of Outer Space Conference (Sep 1967, Vienna) — places Vallée in **Section B (Scientific Researchers)** alongside [[entities/j-allen-hynek|Prof. Dr. Allen J. Hynek]] (US, astronomer + AF UFO consultant since ~1947) and [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Donald E. Keyhoe]] (US, retired Marine major, NICAP founder). ^[extracted]

The roster characterization: ^[extracted]

> *"Jacques Vallee — mathematician and astronomer of Northwestern University, Mars Map Project. USA."*

The **Mars Map Project** reference places Vallée at Northwestern University's astronomy department during 1962-1967 — i.e. the period when he was working with Hynek at Northwestern after his 1962 emigration from France via the University of Chicago. ^[inferred — historical background] The roster categorization as **U.S.** (rather than France) reflects Vallée's working location at the time, not his origin. ^[inferred]

## Structural significance in the FBI corpus

Vallée is structurally significant in section 10 as ^[inferred]:

- **First academic European-emigré UAP-researcher** named in the FBI corpus. ^[inferred] Distinct from the **Aimé Michel** (French, listed in ICUFON tier C) entry — Vallée is U.S.-resident at Northwestern, while Michel is France-resident.
- **The Hynek-Vallée institutional bond** is visible via the roster's tier-B co-listing ^[extracted] — the 1967 ICUFON proposal treats Hynek and Vallée as a **paired academic-UAP-research unit at Northwestern**. ^[inferred] Vallée's later 1969 book *Passport to Magonia* and his 1979 *Messengers of Deception* would crystallize an alternative-to-ETH framework, but the 1967 roster catches him pre-publication, still framed as Hynek's mathematical-analysis collaborator. ^[inferred — historical background]
- **No FBI-corpus retention of post-1967 Vallée material**: section 10 (which closes at 1977) does not retain any later Vallée correspondence, books, or articles. ^[extracted] The Vallée material in the wiki UAP corpus terminates at the ICUFON-roster mention. ^[inferred]

## What Vallée did in this period

Per historical record (referenced obliquely by section 10 content) ^[inferred — historical background]:

- **Mars Map Project** — early-1960s computer-aided Mars-cartography work at the University of Chicago / Northwestern; provided Vallée's institutional anchor for Hynek-collaboration. ^[inferred — historical background]
- **Computer-aided UFO-case-catalog work** — Vallée applied statistical and database methods to UFO sighting catalogs (the GEPAN-era cataloging methodology was anticipated by Vallée's mid-1960s Northwestern work). ^[inferred — historical background] Not directly visible in the FBI corpus.
- **Anatomy of a Phenomenon (1965)** — Vallée's first book, published 2 years before the ICUFON roster lists him. ^[inferred — historical background] Not present in the FBI corpus.
- **Passport to Magonia (1969)** — the folklore-parallels framework. ^[inferred — historical background] Not present.

## Why Vallée matters in the corpus

1. **First Northwestern-University UAP-researcher named**: Vallée's section-10 mention establishes Northwestern as a non-government UAP-research center in the FBI corpus alongside the Univ of Colorado ([[concepts/condon-committee]]) and CUFOS (which Hynek would found in 1973). ^[inferred]

2. **Tier-B academic-civilian-research positioning**: the ICUFON roster treats Vallée and Hynek as academic-tier (B) figures parallel to [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Keyhoe]] (civilian-research/NICAP), without distinguishing AF-consultant status. ^[inferred] By 1967 the AF-consultant / academic-researcher / civilian-research distinction is collapsing — the ICUFON taxonomy is the wiki's earliest documentation of this collapse.

3. **Anchor for later non-FBI corpus material**: Vallée's later books and papers (1969+) form a substantial UAP-research literature that the FBI corpus does not retain. The Section 10 mention is the **single FBI-corpus reference** to Vallée — a marker for cross-referencing future ingests of his work. ^[inferred]
