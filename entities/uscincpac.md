# USCINCPAC

**U.S. Commander-in-Chief, Pacific** — the unified-combatant-command headquarters at Camp H.M. Smith, Honolulu, Hawaii, with operational authority over US military forces in the Pacific theater. The title is the predecessor of today's **USPACOM** (US Pacific Command, since renamed **USINDOPACOM**). ^[inferred]

## Why this page exists

USCINCPAC is the **action addressee** of the [[references/dos-cable-papua-new-guinea-1985-01|28 Jan 1985 DOS cable PORT M 00199]] — the first artifact in the corpus where a UAP-adjacent inquiry from a foreign government is routed *through* a US embassy *to* a unified-combatant-command for technical confirmation. The cable establishes USCINCPAC as a UAP-corpus institutional node; this page anchors the thread for future ingest.

## What the cable shows

- The cable is captioned `O 280653Z JAN 85 ... TO USCINCPAC HONOLULU HI IMMEDIATE` — `O` (Operational Immediate) precedence, indicating that AMEMBASSY Port Moresby treated the inquiry as time-sensitive enough to warrant `IMMEDIATE` precedence to a 4-star command.
- Captioning specifies `USCINCPAC FOR J3 AND POLAD` — i.e., the J3 Operations directorate (responsible for current operational picture across the theater) and the Political Adviser (`POLAD`, a senior State Department officer assigned to the unified command). ^[inferred]
- The 43rd Strategic Wing at Andersen AFB, Guam — operationally the most relevant unit for "what US aircraft were where on 24 Jan 1985 in the western Pacific" — is on the cable as `INFO ... 43SW ANDERSON AFB GU IMMEDIATE`, and is also queried directly by AMEMBASSY Port Moresby via `TELCON`. ^[inferred]
- The USCINCPAC reply to PORT M 00199 is **not in the corpus**. ^[open]

## Institutional position (background)

USCINCPAC sits at the top of the US military operational hierarchy in the Pacific. The Pacific theater includes most of Asia-Pacific, including Papua New Guinea. Routing a foreign-government inquiry to USCINCPAC J3 is the *correct* embassy-to-DoD path for "is this US military aviation activity?" questions. ^[inferred]

In a UAP context, USCINCPAC's role here is precisely that of an **operational disambiguator**: confirm or deny that the reported overflights match scheduled US flight activity in the Pacific theater. The cable does not advance any UAP-specific role for USCINCPAC.

## Why this matters for the corpus

- **First unified-combatant-command node** in the wiki's UAP corpus. Prior corpus institutional nodes are USAF/[[entities/air-materiel-command|AMC]] at [[entities/wright-patterson-afb|Wright-Patterson]] (1948), [[entities/military-air-transport-service|MATS]] at Andrews (1949), [[entities/shaef-air-staff|SHAEF]] (1944–45 wartime joint command), [[entities/national-aeronautics-and-space-council|NASC]] (1963 EOP staff), and [[entities/aaro|AARO]] (modern). USCINCPAC fills the unified-combatant-command gap.
- **Diplomatic-channel UAP intake**: this cable shows the routing pattern *foreign intelligence service → US embassy → unified-combatant-command (with INFO to State Dept HQ)* — see [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting]] for the full pattern.
