# D. Arthur Byrnes Jr.

**Special Agent, Federal Bureau of Investigation, Albuquerque (NM) field office** as of 24 April 1964. ^[extracted] Author of the **8 May 1964 Albuquerque report** on the Lonnie Zamora Socorro NM landing case, filed at FBI HQ as **62-HQ-83894 serial 438** — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]].

## Role in the corpus

Byrnes is the corpus's **first FBI Special Agent attributed to the Albuquerque field office** ^[inferred] (prior FBI-corpus field-office work is concentrated in [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|Detroit 1957–66]] and one redacted 2023 field office). He is also the **author of the corpus's first FBI-authored field-investigation product on a high-profile UAP case at HQ Class-62** — a structurally novel intake channel for the [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894 parent file]] that until now had only held clipped publications + inbound civilian mail.

## What Byrnes did

- **Was on unrelated business** at the **State Police office in Socorro NM** on the late afternoon of 24 April 1964 — geographically and temporally co-located with [[entities/lonnie-zamora|Officer Lonnie Zamora]]'s ~5:45 PM radio call to Sheriff radio operator [[entities/nep-lopez|Nep Lopez]] *"thirty feet down the hall"*. ^[extracted] Byrnes finished his work at ~6:00 PM and **proceeded to the site** — same-day on-site presence within ~15 minutes of Zamora's radio call. ^[extracted]
- Found Zamora, **Sgt M. S. Chavez** (NMSP), **Officer Ted Jordan** (NMSP), and **Undersheriff Jim Luckie** (Socorro County) already assembled at the site. ^[extracted]
- **Personally observed the physical traces** — four rectangular indentations (~16 × 6 in, ~2 in deep, with angled-in geometry), three burned grass clumps inside the four-depression quadrilateral, one additional burned area outside, three smooth ~4-inch circular marks. ^[extracted] These observations are the corpus's **strongest FBI-authored on-site physical-trace record**. ^[inferred]
- **Collected a 10-page Zamora witness statement** the night of 24 April 1964 and the early morning of 25 April 1964 — verbatim, time-anchored, with explicit epistemic hedging preserved. ^[extracted]
- **Authored the 10-page Albuquerque report** + 2-page hand-drawn ground-trace diagrams, distributed in **4 carbon copies**, routed to FBI HQ as 62-HQ-83894 serial 438. ^[inferred]
- **Entered an explicit witness-character note** for Zamora based on a **5-year personal acquaintance**:

> *"Officer Zamora, known intimately for approximately five years, is well regarded as a sober, industrious, and conscientious officer and not given to fantasy."* ^[extracted]
