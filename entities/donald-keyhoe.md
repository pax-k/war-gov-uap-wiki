# Donald Keyhoe

Retired US Marine Corps major (Naval Academy 1919) and aviation journalist whose work in **January-September 1950** became the **most-influential mainstream-press popularization of the ETI hypothesis** for flying saucers. ^[inferred] In the wiki's primary-source corpus, Keyhoe is named in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 5]] in **two distinct contexts** — once via the [[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950|Aztec myth-seed]] channel δ (serial 213 New Orleans SAC, where [[entities/george-koehler|Koehler]] claims Keyhoe visited him) ^[extracted], and once via direct civilian-correspondence channel (serial 239, Glenn Lee Adams Louisville KY postcard query about Keyhoe's book) ^[extracted].

## Wiki primary-source mentions

### Section 5 serial 213 (31 Mar 1950) — New Orleans SAC

> KOEHLER has been telling of this story off and on for the three month period prior to January, 1950, and is said to have notified ARMSTRONG of it weeks prior to the publication of a flying disc article published in the **True Magazine**, and one by FRANK SCULLY published in the Variety Magazine in January, 1950. **KOEHLER claimed to have been visited by DONALD KEHOE, author of the article in the True Magazine.** ^[extracted]

The OCR rendering "KEHOE" is a recurring corpus variant of "KEYHOE" — same person. ^[ambiguous] The structural significance: ^[inferred] [[entities/george-koehler|Koehler]] presents Keyhoe as a contact who **visited him personally** during the late-1949 / early-1950 myth-seed-seeding period — i.e. Keyhoe was in **direct communication with the [[entities/silas-newton|Newton]]/Koehler promoter network** before publishing his January 1950 *True* article.

### Section 5 serial 239 (3-11 Jul 1950) — Adams postcard + Hoover reply

Glenn Lee Adams (Louisville KY) postcard to Hoover ~3 Jul 1950: ^[extracted]

> I would like to know just how much try to do in Donald Keyhoe book "The Flying Saucers Are Real," published this year by the Fawcett Publications, Inc. Keyhoe quotes a vast amount of information from the Air Force, and the F.B.I.

Hoover form-letter reply (11 Jul 1950): ^[extracted]

> I am sure you will find on a close examination of the book you mentioned that it contains no quotations from the FBI but merely mentions this agency.

The drafter's NOTE (DDC:mjp:mad): ^[extracted]

> Correspondent refers to a book "The Flying Saucers are Real" by Donald Keyhoe, which has been read and will be reviewed by the Crime Records Section in the near future. The book mentions briefly that the FBI was investigating flying saucers and that **Bureau Agents witnessed saucers at Las Vegas, New Mexico, 12-8-48**. It is not malicious in the references to that FBI, Keyhoe wrote the article on saucers for Brus Magazine.

This is the **earliest FBI corpus characterization of Keyhoe's book**: ^[inferred]

- Title: *The Flying Saucers Are Real* (Fawcett Publications, 1950)
- Crime Records Section formal review pending
- Book content: Keyhoe asserts FBI was investigating saucers + Bureau agents witnessed saucers at **Las Vegas NM 8 Dec 1948**
- FBI assessment: *"not malicious in the references"*
- Magazine pre-history: Keyhoe wrote for "Brus Magazine" (likely *True* — drafter's typographical variant) ^[ambiguous]

The "Las Vegas NM 8 Dec 1948" reference is structurally significant: 8 Dec 1948 was during the **[[concepts/green-fireballs-new-mexico|Dec 1948 NM green-fireballs cluster]]** — i.e. Keyhoe (writing in 1950) appears to have **inside knowledge of the Project SIGN incident reporting** that places FBI agents as witnesses, even though no parallel record exists in [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|the 1949 USAF Flying Discs file]] confirming the FBI-as-witness claim. ^[inferred]

## What Keyhoe did with his sources

Keyhoe's January 1950 *True* article *"The Flying Saucers Are Real"* and its September 1950 expanded book of the same title were the **mainstream-press counterpart** to [[entities/frank-scully|Scully]]'s *Variety* article + *Behind the Flying Saucers*. ^[inferred] The structural difference: Scully canonized the **crashed-saucer-with-bodies** narrative; Keyhoe pushed the **interplanetary-craft-under-observation** ETI narrative + the **government-cover-up** narrative. ^[inferred] Both writers drew on the [[entities/silas-newton|Newton]]-[[entities/george-koehler|Koehler]] Denver promoter network for material in late 1949 / early 1950, but Keyhoe's corpus diverged from Scully's: Keyhoe's later books (*Flying Saucers from Outer Space* 1953, *The Flying Saucer Conspiracy* 1955, *Flying Saucers: Top Secret* 1960) **distance** from the Aztec / crashed-saucer material as it became publicly discredited 1952-53. ^[inferred]

## Why Keyhoe matters in the corpus

1. **Direct Newton-Koehler-network contact**: serial 213 places Keyhoe in direct contact with Koehler during the myth-seeding period — i.e. Keyhoe is **part of the press amplification of the Aztec myth-seed**, not independent of it. ^[inferred]

2. **Earliest FBI institutional read of UFOlogy literature**: the Crime Records Section review of *The Flying Saucers Are Real* (planned per serial 239 NOTE) is the FBI's **earliest formal book review** of UFOlogical literature in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred]

3. **The "FBI agents witnessed saucers Las Vegas NM" claim**: Keyhoe's book asserts a 8 Dec 1948 FBI-agent witness event during the green-fireballs cluster. The wiki has **no primary-source corroboration** for this claim — the FBI's section-5 self-assessment is *"not malicious in the references"* but does not confirm or deny the FBI-witness claim. ^[inferred]

4. **Synchronous with Boggs-Hearn**: Keyhoe's *True* article (Jan 1950) is the **referenced "magazine articles" target** of the [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|28 March 1950 Boggs-Hearn memo]] explanation of post-1949 saucer reporting. ^[inferred] AF Intelligence (Boggs/Hearn) in March 1950 is **explicitly framing Keyhoe's press cycle** as the cause of the Mar 1950 saucer-reporting wave.

### Sub-A continuation (this batch): NICAP director-of-investigations role

[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-sub-a|Sub-file A]] continues Keyhoe's wiki primary-source presence into the **1953-1959 window**: ^[extracted]

- **Sub-A pages 35-36** — Richard Reilly *Times-Herald* 3-part series "Saucers Held Space Ships by Ex-Marine" (28 Dec 1953): names Keyhoe as the principal proponent of the ETI hypothesis post-Scully, references his 1953 book *Flying Saucers from Outer Space*, walks through the Albert Chop letter on the dust jacket and the Utah-film analysis dispute. ^[extracted] Keyhoe is also explicitly framed as **claiming AF cover-up** in this period.
- **Sub-A pages 10 + 17** (1957 NICAP coverage) — Keyhoe is named as **director of [[entities/nicap|NICAP]]** during the 15 Jan 1957 Fahrney organizational announcement. ^[extracted] The Reilly series (Dec 1953) already calls Keyhoe NICAP's director — chronology suggests Keyhoe was operating NICAP informally before [[entities/delmer-s-fahrney|Fahrney]]'s January 1957 formal launch as board chair, or that the press treats "NICAP" loosely between 1953 and 1957. ^[ambiguous]

This trajectory **transitions Keyhoe from press-amplifier of the Aztec / Newton-Koehler myth-seed** (1950) to **institutional director of a credentialed civilian-research organization** (NICAP, 1957-onward). ^[inferred] Keyhoe is the **single individual in the wiki corpus who bridges the contactee / myth-seed era with the NICAP-credentialed-research era**. ^[inferred]

### Section 10 ICUFON-roster continuation (this batch): named in 1967 UN-proposal delegate list

[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-10|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 10]] **serial 462 enclosure (Aug 1967)** — the *UFO CONTACT igap journal Vol 2 No 5 (Jun 1967)* reproducing the **Intercontinental UFO Observer and Analytic Network (ICUFON) Press Release No. 6** — places Keyhoe in **Section B (Scientific Researchers)** of VonKeviczky's proposed delegate list for the U.N. Peaceful Uses of Outer Space Conference (Sep 1967, Vienna). ^[extracted]

The roster characterization: ^[extracted]

> *"Donald E. Keyhoe — US major ret., pioneer UFO researcher, founder of NICAP. USA."*

This is the **third FBI-corpus characterization of Keyhoe as NICAP founder** ^[inferred] — corroborating the sub-A pages 10 + 17 + 35-36 entries (1953-1959 window) and refining the wiki's institutional placement: by 1967, ICUFON's international civilian-research roster treats Keyhoe's NICAP-founder status as canonical. ^[inferred]

**Structural significance**: ^[inferred]

- **Tier-B co-listing with [[entities/j-allen-hynek|Hynek]] and [[entities/jacques-vallee|Vallée]]**: by 1967 the ICUFON taxonomy places Keyhoe (civilian-research / NICAP) **on the same tier** as Hynek (AF-consultant astronomer) and Vallée (Northwestern academic) — collapsing the AF-consultant / academic / civilian-research distinction. ^[inferred] This is structurally distinct from Keyhoe's earlier (1950) framing in the corpus, where he was strictly a press-amplifier of the Aztec myth-seed independent of academic or AF-consultant channels.
- **Roster co-listing with [[entities/aerial-phenomena-research-organization|APRO]] founder Coral Lorenzen** (tier C, NORTH AMERICA AND ARCTIC): by 1967 NICAP and APRO are treated as the **two principal U.S. civilian-research orgs** by the international ICUFON network. ^[inferred]
- **The 1967 ICUFON roster's framing as *"pioneer UFO researcher, founder of NICAP"*** completes Keyhoe's wiki-corpus trajectory: from 1950 press-amplifier (channel δ via Koehler) → 1953-1959 NICAP director (sub-A) → 1967 international-civilian-research delegate (ICUFON). ^[inferred]
