# AMEMBASSY Ashgabat

The **United States Embassy in Ashgabat**, Turkmenistan — originating diplomatic post of the [[references/dos-cable-turkmenistan-2004-11|12 November 2004 cable ASHGABAT 1028]] on the [[entities/union-of-ufologists-turkmenabat|Union of UFOlogists of Turkmenabat]].

## Why this page exists

The embassy is the wiki's **third** archived diplomatic post that produced a State-Department cable surfaced in a UAP-keyword FOIA release — after [[entities/amembassy-port-moresby|AMEMBASSY Port Moresby]] (1985 PNG) and [[entities/amembassy-dushanbe|AMEMBASSY Dushanbe]] (1994 Kazakhstan). **Unlike its two sister posts**, the cable that anchors this page is **not a UAP-sighting cable**: it is a civil-society / NGO grant-assessment cable about a Turkmen organisation whose name happens to be the "Union of UFOlogists." See [[references/dos-cable-turkmenistan-2004-11]] for the boundary-case framing.

Ashgabat therefore sits **outside** the [[concepts/diplomatic-channel-uap-reporting|diplomatic-channel UAP reporting]] umbrella that Port Moresby and Dushanbe instantiate, even though all three posts produced cables in the same UAP-keyword release universe. The page exists primarily to anchor that distinction and to track Ashgabat's full FOIA-released traffic as the rest of the `059uap*` series is ingested.

## What the cable shows the embassy doing

In the [[references/dos-cable-turkmenistan-2004-11|12 Nov 2004 cable]], the embassy:

1. **Convenes a meeting** on 5 November 2004 between the embassy DCM, USAID Director, and the Board of the [[entities/union-of-ufologists-turkmenabat|Union of UFOlogists]] (UOU) of Turkmenabat.
2. **Reports favourably** on the UOU as a USG implementing partner under the State-Department-funded Counterpart International (CHAP humanitarian assistance) program.
3. **Forwards three pending USAID grant proposals** for HQ awareness: $8,532 (NGO-registration assistance), $15,000 (capacity building, already through local grants committee), $30,000 (printing equipment for an "independent newsletter").
4. **Notes (without endorsing or pursuing)** UOU President [[entities/union-of-ufologists-turkmenabat|Ovezberdy Muradov]]'s claim that *"the Turkmen military and government authorities had consulted him about mysterious occurrences in Turkmen airspace, but he said there had been no confirmed sightings of UFOs in Turkmenistan."* ^[ambiguous] The embassy does not perform any US-asset query, witness probe, or follow-on action on the airspace claim.
5. **Distributes for information** to a broad CIS / OSCE / EU / DoD / IC routing list (including CIA, DIA, NSC, USCENTCOM, SECDEF, Joint Staff) — but the captioned action is `DEPT FOR EUR/CACEN (RUBIN)`, i.e., the Bureau of European and Eurasian Affairs / Office of Caucasus and Central Asian Affairs. The routing is **regional civil-society**, not UAP-substantive.
6. **Closes with a comment**: *"Crazy? Like a fox; and worthy of USG attention and support."* — i.e., a positive-flavour grant-recipient endorsement, not a UAP determination.

The cable signature reads `JACOBSON` — likely **Tracey Ann Jacobson**, US Ambassador to Turkmenistan 2003–2006. ^[ambiguous]

## Cross-references

- The DOS subject-tag schema's **3-of-3 absence of `UFO`** is now confirmed across substantive UAP cables (1985, 1994) and a non-substantive UAP-keyword cable (2004). The schema appears to route by substantive category regardless of whether "UFO" is in the cable's subject-line text. ^[inferred]
- Ashgabat is the **first 2000s-era originating post** in the corpus's diplomatic-channel sub-corpus, but contributes a non-substantive item — the timeline gap between 1994 and 2023 is closed only procedurally.
- The cable's broad DoD / IC info routing (CIA, DIA, NSC, USCENTCOM, SECDEF, Joint Staff) is **standard for regional reporting cables in 2004 post-9/11 routing posture** ^[inferred] — not specific to UAP content. The reader should not infer DoD UAP-handler engagement from the routing alone.
