# G. D. Garrett

**Lieutenant Colonel G. D. Garrett**, Intelligence Division of the Army Air Forces, Washington — captured in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894 section 2]] as the **single most institutionally consequential AAF voice in the BB42 cooperation regime**: the officer whose **personal admission of belief that flying discs ARE a classified US Government project** forced the Bureau-AAF channel into a formal closure on the prosaic-explanation hypothesis. ^[inferred]

## What Garrett did

Section 2 of file [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894]] documents Garrett's institutional moves through three FBI memoranda spanning ~3 weeks of Sep 1947: ^[extracted]

| Date | Document | What Garrett does |
|---|---|---|
| ~22 Aug 1947 | Fitch → Ladd memo (section 2 OCR p. 95, serial 74) | Initial cross-check on the **Hedstrom Twin Falls 19 Aug 1947 mass-formation observation** (serial 66) — Garrett tells Reynolds the AAF runs no R&D in the Twin Falls area; commits to checking with the Army area for confirmation. ^[extracted] |
| ~29 Aug 1947 | Same | Garrett follow-up: "the Army area" confirms no experiments at Twin Falls. ^[extracted] |
| **~22 Sep 1947** | **Fitch → Ladd memo (section 2 OCR p. 128, serial 86) — the GARRETT ADMISSION** | Garrett **personally tells Reynolds** that flying discs **are likely a classified US Government experiment**, names scientist **"Mr. Carroll"** of Air Forces Intelligence as agreeing; cites Sweden ghost-rocket "high brass concern" vs US-disc "high brass unconcern" as his reasoning; agrees a memo will go from Maj Gen McDonald to Gen LeMay R&D requesting confirmation. ^[extracted] |
| ~22 Sep 1947 | Same memo p. 2 | Reynolds escalates to **Col L. R. ## The verbatim Garrett admission

The full text of Garrett's reasoning, recorded by Fitch in the 22 Sep 1947 memo to Ladd: ^[extracted]

> Special Agent S. W. Reynolds of the Liaison Section, while discussing the above captioned phenomena with Lieutenant Colonel Garrett of the Air Forces Intelligence, expressed the possibility that flying discs were, in fact, a very highly classified experiment of the Army or Navy. **Mr. Reynolds was very much surprised when Colonel Garrett not only agreed that this was a possibility, but confidentially stated it was his personal opinion that such was a probability.** Colonel Garrett indicated confidentially that **a Mr. Carroll, who is a scientist attached to the Air Forces Intelligence, was of the same opinion.**

> Colonel Garrett stated that he based his assumption on the following: He pointed out that when flying objects were reported seen over Sweden, the "high brass" of the War Department exerted tremendous pressure on the Air Forces Intelligence to conduct research and collect information in an effort to identify these sightings. Colonel Garrett stated that, in contrast to this, **we have reported sightings of unknown objects over the United States, and the "high brass" appeared to be totally unconcerned. He indicated this led him to believe that they knew enough about these objects to express no concern.** Colonel Garrett pointed out further that the objects in question have been seen by many individuals who are what he terms "trained observers," such as airplane pilots. He indicated also that several of the individuals are reliable members of the community. He stated it is his conclusion that these individuals saw something. He stated the above has led him to come to the conclusion that there were objects seen which somebody in the Government knows all about.

The institutional consequence Reynolds and Garrett both identified: ^[extracted]

> Mr. Reynolds pointed out to Colonel Garrett that **if it is a fact experimentations are being conducted by the United States Government, then it does not appear reasonable to request the FBI to spend money and precious time conducting inquiries with respect to this matter.** Colonel Garrett stated that he agreed with Mr. Reynolds in this regard and indicated that **it would be extremely embarrassing to the Air Forces Intelligence if it later is learned that these flying discs are, in fact, an experiment of the United States Government.**

## Why Garrett matters

Garrett's admission is **the FBI corpus's earliest record of internal AAF doubt about the prosaic-explanation framework that BB42 propagated to all field offices** — and by the same Air Forces Intelligence apparatus that authored the framework. ^[inferred] The structural sequence is paradigm-defining:

1. **30 Jul 1947** — [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]] codifies the AAF's "wire-released-disc" cover-story across all FBI field offices. ^[extracted]
2. **Mid-Aug 1947** — operational cases (Hedstrom Twin Falls serial 66 + Rhodes Phoenix serial 80) start producing pilot-witness and physical-trace data that **don't fit** the prosaic frame. ^[inferred]
3. **~22 Sep 1947** — Garrett (one of the AAF's own framework-authors) **privately concedes the framework is false** but in the opposite direction his framework predicted: not "discs are misidentifications" but "**discs ARE a classified USG project**". ^[extracted]
4. **5 Sep 1947** — Schulgen's formal denial letter forecloses Garrett's hypothesis, leaving the AAF in the position that it **publicly denies a USG-project explanation while privately having no working alternative**. ^[inferred] This is **structurally upstream of [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]]** — created late 1947 / early 1948 precisely because no internal AAF working hypothesis remained viable. ^[inferred]
5. **Late 1947** — Twining's 23 Sep 1947 AMC memo formally concludes the discs are "**real and not visionary or fictitious**" — i.e. AAF Materiel Command takes the position that pulled Schulgen's denial into a **"real-but-unidentified"** working frame. ^[inferred]

Garrett thus sits at the **inflection point** between BB42's prosaic regime and Project SIGN's investigative regime. His admission is the **personal-credibility data point that forced AAF Intelligence to recognize the regime had failed** — both before AND after the formal denial. ^[inferred]

## Identity ambiguity

The FBI corpus consistently refers to Garrett as **"Lieutenant Colonel George D. Garrett"** or **"Lt Col G. D. Garrett"** of the Intelligence Division of the Air Forces. ^[extracted] This identification overlaps with **Lt Col George D. Garrett** of AAF Intelligence Collection Branch, who is in the broader UAP-research-community literature recognized as **a key co-author (with [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]]) of the famous 9 Aug 1947 secret-saucer-characteristics estimate** that preceded Twining's 23 Sep 1947 memo. ^[ambiguous] Whether the FBI's "Lt Col Garrett" is the same person as the broader literature's George D. Garrett is **not directly verified by the FBI corpus** but is highly likely given the matching service / position / role. ^[inferred]

## Position in corpus

Garrett's role per the FBI corpus is **strictly Sep 1947**. ^[extracted] The corpus does not document Garrett further:

- He is **not** the AAF Director of Intelligence (that is Maj Gen McDonald in late 1947, [[entities/c-p-cabell|C. P. Cabell]] from Nov 1948).
- He is **not** a Schulgen successor — the Schulgen-Reynolds channel persists into late 1947 / early 1948.
- His admission is **not formally retracted** in the FBI corpus — it is **silently superseded** by Schulgen's 5 Sep 1947 letter, leaving Garrett's personal opinion as **the AAF's last on-record privately-held position before the secret-project hypothesis becomes formally untenable**. ^[inferred]
