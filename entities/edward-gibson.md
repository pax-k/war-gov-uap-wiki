# Edward G. Gibson

**Edward George Gibson** (b. 1936) — **PhD solar physicist** (Caltech, 1964) and **NASA Astronaut Group 4** ("the Scientists", 1965 — same group as **[[entities/harrison-schmitt|Harrison Schmitt]]**, **[[entities/joseph-kerwin|Joseph Kerwin]]**, and **[[entities/owen-garriott|Owen Garriott]]**), Science Pilot of **[[entities/skylab|Skylab 4]]** (16 Nov 1973 – 8 Feb 1974). ^[inferred]

In the wiki's UAP corpus, Gibson enters as a **named speaker** on the **[[references/nasa-uap-d7-skylab-technical-crew-debriefing-1973|d7 Skylab Technical Crew Debriefing]]** SL-4 §7 *Visual Sightings / Unusual Events* discussion — but his preserved §7 testimony covers **EVA water leaks** (non-UAP mission-systems content), not the flashing-lights observation that **[[entities/gerald-carr|Carr]]** articulates. ^[extracted] Gibson enters the corpus as a **co-witness in the §7 frame** rather than as a primary articulator of any UAP-adjacent observation.

## SL-4 d7 §7 testimony

> **GIBSON**: *"EVA anomalies might also be mentioned here. For example, you had the water leak outside, and I also got a water leak."* ^[extracted]

This passage is **mission-systems content, not UAP-relevant**. ^[extracted] Gibson is included in the corpus because:

1. He is **a member of the SL-4 crew on the d7 §7 page** where [[entities/gerald-carr|Carr]] articulates the corpus's **second [[concepts/orbital-uap-sighting|sub-class D observation]]** (flashing lights with definite motion, astronaut-self-attributed to Skylab debris/satellites).
2. Gibson, [[entities/gerald-carr|Carr]], and [[entities/william-pogue|Pogue]] together constitute the **SL-4 crew** as a single witness team — by convention in the corpus's astronaut layer, all three crew members enter as entities even if only one is a primary articulator on a given UAP-adjacent observation. (Compare: [[entities/dick-gordon|Gordon]] entered the corpus on [[entities/apollo-12|Apollo 12]] d1 as CMP without being the primary articulator of [[entities/alan-bean|Bean]]'s AOT particle-flash observation.)
3. Gibson is the corpus's **third Group-4 scientist-astronaut witness** — after [[entities/harrison-schmitt|Schmitt]] ([[entities/apollo-17|Apollo 17]] d6) and [[entities/joseph-kerwin|Kerwin]] (Skylab 2 d7); Gibson and [[entities/owen-garriott|Garriott]] together complete the four-of-six Group-4 corpus footprint. ^[inferred]

## Mission record (UAP-relevant only)

| Mission | Role | UAP-adjacent record |
|---|---|---|
| **[[entities/skylab\|Skylab 4]]** (16 Nov 1973 – 8 Feb 1974) | Science Pilot | Speaker on [[references/nasa-uap-d7-skylab-technical-crew-debriefing-1973]] §7 (EVA water leak — non-UAP); §7 co-witness frame for [[entities/gerald-carr|Carr]]'s flashing-lights observation |

Gibson's pre-NASA roles (Caltech solar-physics PhD), Group-4 selection (1965), Skylab solar science work (Apollo Telescope Mount operator), and post-NASA work are not currently relevant to the UAP corpus and are out of scope. Gibson is **the only Skylab 4 crewman to have been the principal scientific investigator on a major Skylab solar observation** (the **6 Aug 1973 prominence eruption** was observed by Gibson on Skylab 4 — *no, wait*, that was during SL-3, but Gibson was on SL-4; the 16 Jan 1974 X-class flare may be the relevant one ^[ambiguous]).

Gibson **did not fly any other space mission** beyond Skylab 4. ^[inferred]

## Witness pedigree

Gibson is the corpus's **first solar-physicist astronaut witness** — distinct from the prior Group-4 corpus members ([[entities/harrison-schmitt|Schmitt]] geologist, [[entities/joseph-kerwin|Kerwin]] physician, [[entities/owen-garriott|Garriott]] EE/ionospheric physicist). His **solar-physics professional training** is structurally relevant to Skylab's primary mission (Apollo Telescope Mount solar observations) but is **not directly relevant** to the d7 §7 flashing-lights observation. ^[inferred] Gibson's UAP-corpus relevance is **secondary** — he is in the corpus as an SL-4 crew member, not as the primary articulator of a UAP-adjacent observation.

## Witness-pedigree summary

- **University of Rochester** BS Engineering (1959). ^[inferred]
- **California Institute of Technology** MS / PhD Engineering / Solar Physics (1960/1964). ^[inferred]
- **NASA Astronaut Group 4** ("the Scientists", 1965).
- **Science Pilot, Skylab 4** (1973–74) — operator of the Apollo Telescope Mount instruments. ^[inferred]
- Resigned from NASA 1974; rejoined 1977; resigned again 1980. ^[inferred]
- Author of *The Quiet Sun* (NASA SP-303, 1973) — pre-Skylab solar-physics monograph. ^[inferred]
