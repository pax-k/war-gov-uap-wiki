# Franciszek Grabowski

A **named-only co-witness** to the 1944 Gut Alt Golssen observation. Stub entity — preserved here so the named co-witness link survives in the wiki graph for any future ingest that finds traces of him.

## What is recorded

From [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|FBI Detroit file 100-26505]] / [[references/sighting-gut-alt-golssen-1944]]:

- **Name**: Franciszek Grabowski.
- **Age in 1944**: approximately 50 years old (so born ~1894, ^[inferred]).
- **Role**: member of the 16–18-man work crew of Russian, French, and Polish POWs cutting "hay" in the swamp area north of Gut Alt Golssen, Germany, when Krasuski observed the 1944 ascending circular vehicle.
- **Address**: not known to Krasuski.
- **Postwar fate**: Krasuski had had **no contact since 1945** at the time of his 7 Nov 1957 FBI interview, and **presumed Grabowski had returned to Poland**.

## Why this entity is preserved

The 1944 observation is **single-witness on the artifact level** in the FBI record. Grabowski is the **only other crew member Krasuski could recall by name** when interviewed 13 years later. Preserving this stub:

- Anchors any future trace of Grabowski (e.g., Polish-government wartime-deportation registries, post-1945 Polish DP records) that could provide secondary corroboration of the 1944 observation.
- Marks the **failure point of the FBI's 1957 corroboration attempt** — SA Rathbun's record-of-interview did not pursue Grabowski (Cold-War-era Poland would have made any FBI follow-through procedurally hard). ^[inferred]
- Keeps the witness graph honest: the [[references/sighting-gut-alt-golssen-1944]] page would otherwise read as a strict single-witness account when in fact a named (if uncontactable) co-witness exists.
