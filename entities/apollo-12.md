# Apollo 12

NASA's **Apollo 12** — the **second crewed lunar landing**, launched **14 November 1969** and recovered **24 November 1969**. Crew: **[[entities/pete-conrad|Charles "Pete" Conrad Jr.]]** (Commander), **[[entities/dick-gordon|Richard F. "Dick" Gordon Jr.]]** (Command Module Pilot), and **[[entities/alan-bean|Alan L. Bean]]** (Lunar Module Pilot). ^[inferred] Spacecraft callsigns: **CSM "Yankee Clipper"** and **LM "Intrepid"**. ^[extracted]

In the wiki's UAP corpus, Apollo 12 enters as the **mission frame** for the **[[references/nasa-uap-d1-apollo-12-transcript-1969|d1 air-to-ground voice loop excerpt]]** — the **first NASA d-cluster artifact** ingested in the corpus and the corpus's **first Apollo-mission voice-loop record** (in contrast with the Apollo 17 d6/d5 entries, which are post-mission **debriefings**, not real-time voice loops). ^[inferred]

## Mission record (UAP-relevant only)

| Date / phase | Observation | Witness | Wiki anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~MET 5d 19h 27m (~ 20 Nov 1969 ~11:50 UTC) — landed LM on Ocean of Storms | *"Particles of light, flashes of light"* visible through the AOT in the dark quadrant; Bean self-attributes to water-boiler effluent or lunar outgassing | [[entities/alan-bean\|Bean]] | [[references/nasa-uap-d1-apollo-12-transcript-1969]] |
| ~MET 5d 20h 09m — landed LM | AGS DEDA "all 8's flash" pulsing at one-fifth brilliance — known EMI anomaly characterized at Grumman Bethpage during pre-flight testing | [[entities/alan-bean\|Bean]] (witness) + CC + Fredo (ground confirmation) | [[references/nasa-uap-d1-apollo-12-transcript-1969]] |
| ~MET 6d 00h 21m — post-rendezvous | Tracking light burned out; *"little bits and pieces floating along with us"* visible both with tracking-light flash (first nightside pass) and after burnout — empirical confirmation of co-orbital-debris baseline | [[entities/pete-conrad\|Conrad]] | [[references/nasa-uap-d1-apollo-12-transcript-1969]] |

The artifact is **partial-signal** (sub-class D in the d-cluster typology) — Bean's AOT observation is a real spontaneous astronaut on-orbit ephemeral-light report, but Bean himself offers a conventional explanation in the same transmission. Conrad's tracking-light exchange is **not** a UAP observation but is the corpus's first **empirical confirmation** of the canonical "your own debris field" prior described on [[concepts/orbital-uap-sighting]]. ^[inferred]

## Mission profile (overview)

- **Launch vehicle**: Saturn V (AS-507). ^[inferred]
- **Launch**: 14 November 1969, 16:22 UTC, Kennedy Space Center LC-39A. ^[inferred] (Notable for surviving two lightning strikes during ascent through a thunderstorm; SCE-to-AUX recovery.) ^[inferred]
- **Landing site**: **Ocean of Storms** — near Surveyor 3 (touched down ~180 m from the unmanned Surveyor 3 lander, Apr 1967). ^[inferred]
- **Lunar surface duration**: ~31.5 hours; two EVAs by Conrad + Bean. ^[inferred]
- **CMP solo activity**: Gordon remained in lunar orbit aboard CSM *Yankee Clipper* and conducted multispectral and geological observations of candidate future landing sites. ^[inferred]
- **Recovery**: 24 November 1969, **USS Hornet (CV-12)** in the Pacific. ^[inferred]
- **Total mission duration**: ~10 days 4 hours. ^[inferred]
- **Distinguishing role in Apollo program**: first **precision** lunar landing (within walking distance of Surveyor 3 — the crew retrieved instrument samples for return to Earth analysis); first deployment of the full ALSEP (Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package). ^[inferred]

The biographical / mission detail above is **not in the d1 OCR excerpt** and is filled in from general program knowledge — flagged `^[inferred]` accordingly. The d1 excerpt's confirmable mission-profile facts are: spacecraft callsigns ("Yankee Clipper", "Intrepid"); LM lift-off time **142:03:47** MET; mission phase (rendezvous-prep through post-rendezvous configuration); CMP-LM voice-active state; CAPCOM on duty as "Jerry". ^[extracted]

## Institutional context

Apollo 12 was operated under the **[[entities/nasa|NASA]] / Manned Spacecraft Center (MSC) Houston** organizational chart — the same office that originated the **[[references/nasa-pao-t-00763-r1b-gemini-7-1965|1965 GT-7 PAO release tape]]** four years earlier and the **[[references/nasa-apollo-17-technical-crew-debriefing-1973|1973 Apollo 17 MSC-07631 technical debriefing]]** four years later. ^[inferred] The d1 OCR excerpt's PAO-narration commentary is **not preserved in the bundle** (vs. the GT-7 tape's explicit *"this is Gemini Control"* sign-offs), so whether the d1 segment was pushed through the MSC PAO release-by-default channel cannot be confirmed from the artifact alone. ^[inferred]

The Apollo 12 mission **straddles the closure of [[concepts/project-sign|Project BLUE BOOK]]** (closed 17 December 1969, ~3 weeks after Apollo 12 splashdown). Whether BLUE BOOK opened a case file on Bean's AOT particle-flash observation is **open**; if not, the d1 artifact would be the corpus's **last NASA-originated UAP-adjacent record from the BLUE BOOK era** — and the GT-7 1965 / Apollo 12 1969 / Apollo 17 1973 sequence brackets the **collapse of BLUE BOOK as an institutional consumer of NASA-origin UAP-adjacent records**. ^[inferred]
