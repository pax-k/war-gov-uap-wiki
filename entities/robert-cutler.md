# Robert Cutler

**Special Assistant to President Dwight D. Eisenhower for National Security Affairs** during the Eisenhower administration (in office during the period relevant to this file: November 1957). ^[inferred]

In the wiki's UAP corpus, Cutler appears in a **single but structurally important** role: as the **Eisenhower-administration figure to whom citizen [[entities/wladyslaw-krasuski|Wladyslaw Krasuski]] addressed a 4 November 1957 UFO-adjacent letter** that was subsequently forwarded to the FBI Director and triggered an URGENT teletype to SAC Detroit. ^[extracted from [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957]]]

## Why this routing matters

Krasuski's letter explicitly explains his choice of recipient: he saw **Cutler's photo and identification with the President's Office in a local paper** and wrote to him rather than directly to Eisenhower. The choice is recorded in the FBI Detroit AIRTEL of 7 November 1957 (`100-26505-3`): "KRASUSKI advised that his communication was directed to Mr. ROBERT CUTLER rather than to President EISENHOWER after seeing Mr. CUTLER's picture and identification with the President's Office in a local paper."

The routing chain that resulted:

1. **4 Nov 1957** — Krasuski → Cutler (letter).
2. **(unknown date)** — Cutler's office → FBI Director / J. Edgar Hoover (forward; specific routing slip not in the file).
3. **6 Nov 1957, 5:40 PM** — FBI Director URGENT teletype → SAC Detroit, instructing immediate interview.
4. **7 Nov 1957** — SA Cassius Rathbun interviews Krasuski in person.
5. **7 Nov 1957** — SAC Detroit AIRTEL → FBI Director, with 5 enclosure copies of the Blank Memo.

The structural significance: a citizen letter to the President's national security special assistant **compelled the FBI Director** to mobilize a field-office interview within ~48 hours on a UAP-adjacent matter. That is qualitatively different from the [[references/fbi-detroit-100-18221-serial-844-1958|1958 Weaver]] disposition (~5 months later, same field office), in which a phone-in produced **only a routing memo to USAF**, with no Director-level traffic.

The two cases delineate **two distinct FBI UAP-adjacent intake patterns** active in Detroit ~5 months apart: ^[inferred]

| Pattern | Entry path | Director-level involvement | FBI substantive engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WH-routed citizen letter (1957 Krasuski)** | citizen → Cutler (WH) → FBI HQ → Director URGENT teletype → SAC | **Yes** | **Sit-down interview**, records check, AIRTEL-with-enclosure |
| **Citizen phone-in (1958 Weaver)** | citizen → FBI Detroit phone | No — local SA → SAC only | **None** — disposition "Advise proper air force authorities" |
