# Ronald E. Evans Jr.

**Ronald E. "Ron" Evans Jr.** (1933–1990) — **US Navy naval aviator** and **NASA Astronaut Group 5** ("The Original Nineteen", 1966), Command Module Pilot of **[[entities/apollo-17|Apollo 17]]** (7–19 Dec 1972). ^[inferred] As CMP, Evans remained in lunar orbit aboard CSM *America* during the lunar-surface phase and conducted the SIM-bay scientific instrument observations. ^[inferred]

In the wiki's UAP corpus, Evans enters as a **named speaker** on the **[[references/nasa-apollo-17-technical-crew-debriefing-1973|MSC-07631 Apollo 17 Technical Crew Debriefing]]** page-24-4 excerpt — speaking twice, both observations resolving to **conventional, well-understood orbital-spaceflight phenomena**, neither a UAP report. ^[inferred]

## Apollo 17 debriefing lines (page 24-4)

### 1. Reentry-plasma "tunnel with bright spot"

> **EVANS** (CONT'D): *"...after the brightness of the fireball decreased, I could look back up through the rendezvous window and see what to me was kind of like a tunnel with a bright spot in the middle of the tunnel. Way down the tunnel, way back behind, I could see the fireball."* ^[extracted]

Evans describes the **trailing plasma sheath / wake** visible from inside the Command Module during atmospheric reentry, looking rearward through the rendezvous window after peak plasma brightness decreased. The "fireball" referent is the **reentry plasma**, not a bolide. ^[inferred] The observation is a **documented Apollo CM-window phenomenon** from this vantage point. ^[inferred]

### 2. Window-fog clarifying remark

> **EVANS**: *"Well, it was kind of foggy on the windows."* ^[extracted]

In the immediate context of [[entities/gene-cernan|Cernan]]'s "tin can" line about the recovery aircraft carrier — this is Evans's own clarification that the misidentification was driven by post-splashdown window condensation, not by anything unusual about the recovery vessel. ^[extracted]

## Witness class

Evans's two debriefing lines are noteworthy precisely because they are **both negative-resolved**: the "fireball / tunnel" line names a phenomenon that sounds anomalous but resolves to known reentry plasma, and the "kind of foggy on the windows" line **explicitly walks back** the carrier-superstructure misidentification. ^[inferred] This is the **first instance in the corpus of an astronaut-class witness self-correcting an apparently anomalous observation in the same debriefing exchange**. ^[inferred] Compare with [[entities/frank-borman|Borman]]'s GT-7 line — Borman's "we have an actual sighting" remark is **not** walked back on the [[references/nasa-pao-t-00763-r1b-gemini-7-1965|released PAO transcript]]; the bogey-vs-booster ambiguity persists. The Evans / Borman contrast may be useful as a reference baseline for orbital-class self-correction patterns once more astronaut testimony enters the corpus. ^[inferred]
