# Leonard H. Stringfield

A **Cincinnati advertising executive** (Advertising Manager, **DuBois Company, Inc.**, 1120 West Front Street, Cincinnati 3, Ohio) and the founder/director of **[[entities/civilian-research-interplanetary-flying-objects|Civilian Research, Interplanetary Flying Objects (CRIFO)]]**, a saucer-research-and-publishing organization based at his home address (**7017 Britton Avenue, Cincinnati 27, Ohio**). ^[extracted] CRIFO publishes a multi-lithographed monthly *"Newsletter"* (later *"C.R.I.F.O. Orbit"*) at $2/year subscription with claimed worldwide circulation of ~4,000 copies by Oct 1954. ^[extracted]

Stringfield first appears in the FBI HQ corpus in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-8|62-HQ-83894 section 8]] **serial 349 (2 Nov 1954)** ^[extracted] and recurs across at least four section-8 serials (349, 350, 376, 377), spanning Nov 1954 → Apr 1957. He is the **first FBI-corpus civilian saucer-researcher with documented direct Air Force Intelligence access** ^[inferred] — Stringfield reports a private interview with **Lt Col John O'Mara, Deputy Commander, AF Intelligence, Wright Field (Wright-Patterson AFB)** on **21 Sep 1954**, recorded in the Oct 1954 CRIFO Newsletter. ^[extracted]

## Stringfield's institutional posture

Per FBI Cincinnati's case officer reporting (serial 349, drafted from informant Jack Gunderman of DuBois Company): ^[extracted]

- **Open posture toward AF Intelligence**: Stringfield met with Lt Col O'Mara in person; reported the conversation in his newsletter; treated it as continuing access.
- **ETI-attribution-public stance**: Stringfield told Gunderman *"The Air Force can't do anything to me. I'm claiming saucers are interplanetary."* ^[extracted] — a deliberate strategic posture treating ETI-attribution as a legal-political shield against AF interference.
- **Censorship-narrative articulation**: Stringfield maintained in his Newsletter and personally that *"the U. S. Air Force maintains a strict censorship of news"* on the saucer topic ^[extracted].
- **Cross-influence with Frank Edwards**: Stringfield regularly communicates with [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Frank Edwards]] (former AFL radio commentator), who *"recently warned Stringfield to be very careful or the Air Force would stop Stringfield's publishing activities."* ^[extracted] Stringfield maintains the AF was responsible for Edwards being fired from his AFL radio program for using it to publicize his saucer interest. ^[extracted]
- **Ex-USAF Intelligence officer self-claim**: Eva Reich's Mar 1957 letter to Stringfield references his *"official capacity as former USAF Intelligence officer"* citing Edward J. Ruppelt's *The UFO Story* as source. ^[extracted]

## FBI Cincinnati posture toward Stringfield (1954-57)

Standard **passive-accumulation channel (a)** + **clipped/forwarded inbound civilian correspondence channel (b)** ^[inferred]: ^[extracted]

- Cincinnati files the CRIFO Newsletter and clipping inbounds.
- No investigation conducted on Stringfield personally.
- Information shared with **OSI Wright-Patterson** *"of the substance of the information in this letter and that no further action is being taken."* ^[extracted]
- Cincinnati indices reflect only **one prior reference to Stringfield** (Dec 1951 Jack Gunderman walk-in unrelated to saucers) ^[extracted] — i.e., the saucer activity is the operative trigger.

## The Reich connection (Mar-Apr 1957, serials 376-377)

A structurally striking 1957 thread: ^[extracted]

- **Eva Reich, M.D.** (daughter of Wilhelm Reich — the orgone-energy theorist; address Route 1, P.O. Box 721, Alexandria, VA, telephone South 5-7081), writes Stringfield a 19 Mar 1957 letter referencing *"a series of facts and events designated 'OROP Desert Ea'"* (an unspecified Reich-orgone-related expedition).
- Reich asks Stringfield whether he would testify under oath about UFO sighting reports collected via CRIFO Orbit, and whether he would reveal what he knows in his *"official capacity as former USAF Intelligence officer."*
- Stringfield **does not know about OROP Desert Ea or "the orgone energy motor"** and brings the letter to FBI Cincinnati on 25 Mar 1957.
- FBI Cincinnati and Norfolk conduct cross-checks on Eva Reich; the file notes Wilhelm Reich's 1956 contempt-of-court conviction following the Food and Drug Administration injunction barring interstate shipment of his orgone-energy devices.
- Reich's letter mentions the FBI *"has so far declined to investigate as the subject is 'not within their jurisdiction.'"* ^[extracted]

The Reich-Stringfield exchange is the **first FBI-corpus contact between the saucer-research and orgone-energy communities** ^[inferred] — though Stringfield treats it as out-of-scope and Reich treats Stringfield as a potential testimonial witness for an unrelated court case.
