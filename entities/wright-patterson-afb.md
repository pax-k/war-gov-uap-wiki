# Wright-Patterson Air Force Base

USAF base in Dayton, Ohio. In the 1948 [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] correspondence, every routing slip directs sighting reports to:

> Commanding General, Air Materiel Command, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Dayton, Ohio
> ATTN: MCI (later TSDIN)

Wright-Patterson is therefore the **physical address of the early US government UFO collection effort**. ^[extracted]

## Tenants relevant to the wiki

- [[entities/air-materiel-command]] — formally designated as the Flying Disc collection agency.
- T-2 (technical intelligence) — held custody of the [[entities/horten-brothers|Horten Brothers]] T-2 report (F-SU-1110-HD) and the [[references/loedding-flying-disc|Loedding Flying Disc]] drawing under patent control.

## Cultural / historical weight

Wright-Patterson's later association in popular UFO discourse (e.g. "Hangar 18" rumors, alleged crash-debris storage) traces back to its very real 1948 status as the only authorized destination for US flying-disc reports — an institutional fact that grew into folklore. ^[inferred]
