# E. U. Hathaway

US Army Lieutenant Colonel; **staff officer assigned to the [[entities/richard-russell|Senate Armed Services Committee]]** in October 1955. ^[extracted]

The source records his name as both **`Lt. Col. E. U. Hathaway`** (page 2 cable text) and **`Lt. Col. E. W. Hathaway`** (cover sheet, evaluation block) — likely an OCR or typist's variant of the same person. ^[ambiguous]

## Significance to the wiki

Hathaway is the **second-witness** of disc 1 and full-witness of disc 2 in the [[references/sighting-trans-caucasus-1955-10-04|4 Oct 1955 Trans-Caucasus sighting]]. He was the **primary debriefer** at the 13 Oct 1955 0900–1200 USAIRA Prague meeting, providing most of the explanation and answers while [[entities/ruben-efron|Mr. Efron]] supplemented from his contemporaneous notes.

[[references/iaf-ir-193-55-russell-1955|USAIRA Prague IR 193-55]]'s author [[entities/thomas-s-ryan|Lt Col Ryan]] characterizes Hathaway directly: *"Lt Col. Hathaway proved to be an excellent observer."* ^[extracted]

Hathaway's structured-observer competence is supported across the bundle by the **non-UAP collateral observations** he produced over the 9-day trip:

- **Dnepropetrovsk Airfield, 9 Oct 55** — counted **42 stubby jet fighters**, distinguished them from **MIG-15 / Fagot** by fuselage stubbiness, described a **multi-jet bomber** with detailed morphology (canvas-covered wing-buried engines, swayback fuselage top, severely swept wings to mid-fuselage, low tailplane, silver bulbous tailcone bulge aft of rudder line), and produced a profile sketch (page 8). ^[extracted]
- **Moscow-Minsk highway, 42 km marker** — sketched a **two-radar Soviet site**; sketches were the basis for Ryan's tentative ID as **one Woodgage / Gage and one square-pole Freya**. ^[extracted]
- **Tiflis (~5 Oct 55)** — sighted a **banana-shaped helicopter** with two main rotors at each end and "many more windows" than the analogous US type he had seen. ^[extracted]
- **Outside Sochi** — observed a **T-34 tank** with a muzzle brake on the gun barrel ("looked very different from any he had seen during a combat tour in Korea") on a flatcar. ^[extracted]
- **COP (Chop) USSR-Czech border, 12 Oct 55** — observed and explained the **rail-gauge switching operation** (Russian broad gauge → Czech standard gauge) in detail, with Ryan rendering the operation as a multi-step sketch (IR 193-55 page 9). ^[extracted]
- **Czech rail leg COP → Prague** — noted **prefabricated Czech rail-electrification construction**, a **long fenced/guarded area** at dusk, large RR marshaling yards with manned guard towers, and aluminum RR cars believed to be the local plant's product. ^[extracted]

His **Korean combat tour** is a single-line detail in the bundle ("any he had seen during a combat tour in Korea"); no specific unit is given. ^[extracted]

## Witness-quality argument

The collateral observations matter as **prior on the sighting**. Hathaway's observations across the trip are dense, structured, and aircraft / armor / radar-correct at a level consistent with a competent G-2-style ground observer. ^[inferred] The sighting is therefore **not** the only structured observation in the bundle to assess his credibility against — it is the headline of a 9-day observer-trip in which his other observations check out morphologically.

That argument is what underwrites Ryan's **B-2 evaluation** ("usually reliable" / "probably true") and Ryan's USAIRA comment that the sighting "lends credence to many 'saucer' reports." ^[inferred]
