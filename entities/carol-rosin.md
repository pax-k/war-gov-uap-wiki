# Carol Rosin

US space-policy advocate. Self-described in the 30 April 2001 cover letter to NASA as:

- **Founder of the Institute for Security and Cooperation in Outer Space (ISCOS)** — founded 1983, "closed down" prior to 2001.
- **Spokesperson for Wernher von Braun** during the last years of his life. ^[extracted-as-her-claim]
- Resident at **498 Manzanita Ct., Ventura, CA 93001** (April 2001 letter address).
- Married for fifteen years to **Jon Cypher** (actor — *Hill Street Blues*, *Major Dad*, Broadway productions). ^[extracted]

The wiki's first entry on Rosin is solely as the **routing courier** of the [[references/cometa-report-1999|1999 COMETA Report]] from France/private channels into NASA archives.

## The April–May 2001 NASA delivery

The cover letter is dated **30 April 2001** and addressed to **"Dan"** at NASA — almost certainly **Daniel Goldin**, NASA Administrator from April 1992 until November 2001. ^[inferred] Key points:

- Rosin and Cypher had a meeting scheduled at "your office" on the **Friday following 30 April 2001** at **3:30 pm**.
- The visit was to deliver a *"package that will give you the idea"* — explicitly described as costing *"neither you nor NASA a dime."*
- Rosin frames her current mission as completing a commitment von Braun made her give him: *"to find the path to open the doors to space… with his full and unlimited vision."*
- They were lodged at the **Renaissance Hotel, 999 9th NW, 202-898-9000**.

A handwritten note (on Renaissance Hotel notepad) accompanying the package reads: *"DAN - AS PROMISED, HERE IS THE FRENCH COMETA REPORT… IT IS A PRIVATE - NOT GOVERNMENT - REPORT BUT THE QUALITY AND SOBER NATURE OF THE PARTICIPANTS STILL, I THINK, RECOMMENDS IT TO YOU."*

The note also references **"P 62 — JOHN CALLAHAN'S TESTIMONY"** and **"P. 105 — SENIOR AIR TRAFFIC CONTROLLER AT MEX CITY INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT - ENRIQUE KOLBECK"** — page references that do **not** match COMETA's pagination, suggesting **at least one additional document** was delivered alongside COMETA, probably **Marie Galbraith's *Briefing Document — The Best Available Evidence*** (1995, cited in COMETA Chapter 9.1) which has matching pagination. ^[ambiguous — bundle composition].

A second handwritten note signed *"Jon Lepsher"* (probably **Jon Cypher**, OCR misread) closes with *"I ALWAYS THINK OF HAMLET'S 'THERE ARE MORE THINGS TWIXY [sic — 'twixt] HEAVEN AND HELL THAN ARE DREAMED OF IN YOUR PHILOSOPHY, HORATIO.'"* ^[extracted]

## Why the routing matters

The COMETA Report ended up in **NARA RG 255 (Records of NASA)** — i.e., it was filed in NASA's recordkeeping. The chain is:

```
COMETA Committee (private, France)
    → published as VSD magazine special issue (1999)
    → physical copy to Carol Rosin (private, US, undocumented sub-route)
    → hand-delivered to Daniel Goldin at NASA HQ (May 2001)
    → archived in NARA RG 255
```

This is the corpus's first **non-governmental → governmental routing path** for a UAP policy document, and it parallels the [[entities/oklahoma-ufo-research-association|OUFORA → FBI Detroit]] inquiry (1966) on a different decade and different agency. ^[inferred]
