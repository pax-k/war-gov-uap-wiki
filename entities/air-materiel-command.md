# Air Materiel Command (AMC)

USAF command headquartered at [[entities/wright-patterson-afb|Wright-Patterson AFB]], Dayton, OH. In the [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948|1948 Cabell directive]], AMC was formally designated:

> "the Air Force agency to collect, collate, evaluate, and distribute to interested government agencies and contractors all information concerning sightings and phenomena in the atmosphere which can be construed to be of concern to the national security."

This made AMC the operational hub of [[concepts/project-sign]]. ^[extracted]

## Authority granted

- All Air Force installations within and outside the Zone of Interior and Alaska were instructed to report sightings **directly** to AMC.
- AMC was authorized to **contact installations directly** when developing additional information on flying discs (i.e., bypassing normal command channels for follow-up).
- The Department of the Army was *requested* to similarly route reports through AMC. ^[extracted]

The "T-2" prefix on AMC reports refers to its intelligence (technical-intelligence) division. ^[inferred]

## Key personnel mentioned

- **Col H. M. McCoy** — Deputy Commanding General, Intelligence (T-2). Retained the T-2 report "German Flying Wings Designed by Horten Brothers" (F-SU-1110-HD).
- **Col Robert Taylor (III)** — appears as coordinating officer on multiple disc-related routing slips into AMC.

## AMC's stance during 1948

AMC pushed for a much more aggressive interception posture: continuous fighter alert at every USAF installation, gun cameras, dedicated armament. The Director of Plans and Operations rejected this in March 1948 (see [[concepts/project-sign]]). AMC continued its collection/collation role but never got the active interception arm it had asked for. ^[extracted]

## Successor

AMC was reorganized into Air Force Logistics Command in 1961, and the technical intelligence functions migrated to what eventually became the Foreign Technology Division and later AFRL. ^[inferred] *(Background context — not in the 1948 file.)*
