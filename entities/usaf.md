# United States Air Force (USAF)

The **United States Air Force** — independent service of the **Department of Defense**, established **18 September 1947** under the **National Security Act of 1947**, succeeding the Army Air Forces (AAF) as the executive-branch home of US military aviation. ^[inferred] *(Founding date and predecessor framing are general historical background — the source bundle does not state them.)*

This page is a **stub hub**. The wiki's primary-source coverage of USAF is concentrated in narrower entity, concept, and reference pages — see those for the substantive content. This page exists primarily so the high-volume incoming wikilinks resolve.

## What the wiki has on it

USAF is the **dominant institutional actor** in the wiki's 1947-1965 UAP corpus. The institution surfaces across the corpus through several distinct functional layers:

- **Headquarters intelligence directorate** — HQ USAF, Pentagon. Directorate of Intelligence under Maj Gen [[entities/c-p-cabell|C. P. Cabell]] (~1948-1953) signed [[concepts/usaira-reporting-channel|USAIRA reporting]] doctrine and AIRMEM #4 "Unconventional Aircraft" (15 Feb 1949) — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164]]. This was the addressee node for most TS-track material flowing in from theater commands.
- **Formal projects** — [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] (Jan 1948 – Feb 1949) → [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]] (Feb 1949 – Dec 1949, with public termination announcement) → **Project BLUE BOOK** (1952 – 1969). All ran out of [[entities/wright-patterson-afb|Wright-Patterson AFB]] under [[entities/air-materiel-command|Air Materiel Command]] / later ATIC.
- **Theater commands** — [[entities/usafe|USAFE]] (Europe), Far East Air Force, Alaskan Air Command, Caribbean Air Command. USAFE in particular ran a parallel TS-track liaison pipeline with foreign-allied AISs (e.g. [[entities/swedish-air-intelligence-service|Swedish AIS]]) — see [[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11]].
- **Field installations and OSI** — Air Force Office of Special Investigations (OSI) at base level (e.g. **Williams AFB** ^[inferred]) ran ground-level radar acquisition and interception coordination for active sightings — see [[references/sighting-phoenix-b29-chase-1950-06-29]] and [[references/sighting-kirtland-mass-1949-01-30]].
- **Press relations and public posture** — Office of Public Information (later OIS) at Pentagon level; **[[entities/albert-chop|Albert Chop]] (DOD-OPI)** was the press-relations gatekeeper as of 1952 (see [[references/usaf-flying-saucers-press-fact-sheet-1952]]). The public posture by 1949-1952 was **discontinuation + non-existence** at HQ level even while project work continued at Wright-Patterson.
- **AFOIR-CO field-installation reporting** — [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948|the 1948 USAF flying-disc compendium]] and [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|its 1948-50 continuation]] document a CONUS-plus-Alaska-plus-overseas-posts reporting network at CONFIDENTIAL/SECRET level, distinct from the USAFE TS track. ^[inferred]
- **Reporting doctrine** — [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|the disc-reporting protocol]] inherited 25 Mar 1948 from Army G-2 (CSGID 425.1) and formally consolidated under USAF in AIRMEM #4 (Feb 1949). [[references/adc-stratemeyer-flying-disc-1948-02-04|Stratemeyer (ADC) Feb 1948]] is the corpus's earliest USAF major-command directive on the subject.

## Significance for the corpus

- **The Army → USAF doctrinal handover for disc-reporting** (Mar 1948 → Feb 1949) is the corpus's first inter-service transfer of UAP responsibility — see [[entities/c-p-cabell]] and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164]] for the structural detail.
- The wiki's **press-relations gatekeeper layer** (Chop / OIS) is in tension with the **continuing project work** (SIGN → GRUDGE → BLUE BOOK at Wright-Patterson) — that public-private split is a recurring corpus-wide pattern. ^[inferred]
- USAF's **TS-track parallel pipeline** through USAFE is the wiki's clearest demonstration that the AFOIR-CO field-installation track at HQ USAF was not the only USAF UAP-information channel — see [[entities/usafe]].
