# Thomas S. Ryan

USAF Lieutenant Colonel; **US Air Attaché Prague** ([[entities/usaira-prague|USAIRA Czechoslovakia]]) in October 1955. ^[extracted]

The bundle's signature lines render the name as **`Thomas S. Ryan, Lt. Col. Air Attache`** (cover sheet) and **`T.S. RYAN`** (closing of cable C-103) and **`T.S. Ryan / Lt Col USAF`** (close of supplement page 7). ^[extracted]

## Significance to the wiki

Ryan is the **drafter of [[references/iaf-ir-193-55-russell-1955|IR 193-55]]** — the wiki's **first 1955-era** UAP-adjacent artifact and **first USAIRA-channel** record of any kind. He also signed the prior **TOP SECRET cable C-103** (13 Oct 1955) to HQ USAF DINTA whose verbatim text is preserved on page 2 of the IR.

Period of service in Prague: at minimum **17 months by Oct 1955** — Ryan tells the witnesses *"I don't disbelieve anything after having been in Czechoslovakia for 17 months"* (page 3). ^[extracted] This places his arrival at USAIRA Prague at roughly **May 1954**. ^[inferred]

Ryan conducted the 0900–1200 13 Oct 1955 debrief in Col. Thomas Dooley's (US Army Attaché) office, jointly with Dooley. Both took notes and provided sketch paper.

## Institutional posture

Ryan's USAIRA comments embedded in IR 193-55 articulate the **air-attaché institutional posture** toward UAP-adjacent witness reports in 1955:

- **Cover-sheet endorsement**: *"The significance of this report re the USAF project 'Unidentified Flying Objects' is remarkable and lends credence to many 'saucer' reports."* ^[extracted]
- **Cable-text recommendation**: *"RECOMMEND COMPLETE DEBRIEFING OF RUSSELL GROUP UPON RETURN AND SUGGEST COMMENDATION FROM D/I FOR EFFORTS."* ^[extracted]
- **Closing comment**: *"Due to the time available for debriefing and the physical limitations of our office in Prague, no more can be reported from these excellent sources."* ^[extracted]
- **Mid-debrief reassurance to witnesses**: *"I stated that I don't disbelieve anything after having been in Czechoslovakia for 17 months."* ^[extracted]
- **Egorov skepticism**: *"It is difficult for me to believe that this experienced pilot in polar navigation would be retired and doing nothing but travelling around the Trans Caucasus region with a young engineer."* ^[extracted]

Across all five comments, Ryan adopts an **endorsing, source-weighted, intelligence-officer posture** — not the recording-without-endorsing posture of the AFOIR-CO field-installation track (cf. [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948]] and [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949]]). The contrast is informative: see [[concepts/usaira-reporting-channel]]. ^[inferred]

Ryan's posture is consistent with a **trained HUMINT collector** evaluating sources whose reliability he can independently assess, in a hostile-territory operating context where he operates near the [[entities/usafe|USAFE]]-adjacent foreign-liaison interface that produced [[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11|USAFE TT 1524]] seven years earlier. ^[inferred]

## Operational tradecraft visible in the bundle

- Refused to discuss the witnesses' substantive material at the **US Residency** the night of arrival (12 Oct 1955) "because of the absence of security precautions." Moved the debrief to the Army-Attaché's office the next morning. ^[extracted]
- Took **structured notes alongside Col. Dooley** during the debrief, with both providing sketch paper. ^[extracted]
- **Compared the witness's Soviet-jet-fighter description against a scale model of a Fagot** kept in the office (production-grade reference equipment). ^[extracted] *("Sort of rules out Frescos of which I could use a model as I don't have any.")*
- Cited a specific Standing Requirement intelligence document — **SRI No. TIC 6244** — for the candidate Soviet jet-fighter composite. ^[extracted]
- Cabled cable C-103 at **TOP SECRET / OPERATIONAL IMMEDIATE** within hours of the debrief; followed up with the formal AF Form 112/112a IR 193-55 the next day. ^[extracted]

The bundle provides a window into 1955 USAIRA tradecraft: **classified-debrief → TS cable → SECRET formal IR**, with reference equipment, SRI catalog citations, and structured note-taking inside an allied-attaché's office. ^[inferred]
