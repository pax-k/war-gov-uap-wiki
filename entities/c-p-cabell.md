# C. P. Cabell

USAF general officer. Across the corpus he appears under three distinct titles, marking two separate promotions through 1948–1949:

- **Brigadier General, U.S.A.F. — Chief, Air Intel. Req. Div., Directorate of Intelligence** (early Feb 1948 directives — AFOIR-CO-5; 6 Feb 1948 letter)
- **Major General, U.S.A.F. — Chief, Air Intel. Req. Div., Directorate of Intelligence** (later 1948 — AFGIR-CO-5 series)
- **Major General, U.S.A.F. — Director of Intelligence, Office of Deputy Chief of Staff, Operations** (signed AIRMEM #4 of 15 Feb 1949 — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164]])

The 15 Feb 1949 AIRMEM #4 anchors a clean **promotion-timestamp**: by that date Cabell has moved up from *Chief of the Air Intel. Req. Div.* (a divisional role) to **Director of Intelligence** of the *Office of Deputy Chief of Staff, Operations* (the directorate's head). ^[extracted]

## Significance to Project SIGN

Cabell's 27 February 1948 disposition form (AFOIR-CO-5) is the foundational charter for [[concepts/project-sign]]:

- Established the four-verb mandate: **collect, collate, evaluate, distribute**.
- Designated [[entities/air-materiel-command|Air Materiel Command]] as the central agency.
- Authorized AMC to contact installations directly during follow-up.
- Codified the [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element observational template]].

Without this directive, the bottom-up flow of sighting reports up to [[entities/wright-patterson-afb|Wright-Patterson]] would not have been formalized. ^[inferred]

## Significance to the November 1948 USAFE TS dispatch

By November 1948 Cabell was the **named substantive addressee** of [[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11|USAFE TT 1524]] (TS Control # 2-5317, 4 Nov 1948). Item 14 of that dispatch — the [[entities/swedish-air-intelligence-service|Swedish Air Intelligence Service]]'s *"outside the earth"* assessment of flying-saucer phenomena, plus the [[references/sighting-swedish-lake-crash-1948|Swedish naval lake-salvage operation]] and the [[references/sighting-neubiberg-1948-10|Neubiberg ~30-min hover]] — closes with an explicit policy ask: *"What are your reactions?"*

This places Cabell in **two distinct routing roles** within calendar year 1948:

- **Directive role** (Feb 1948, AFOIR-CO-5): Cabell *signs* the foundational disc-reporting directive that establishes the AFOIR-CO field-installation reporting channel.
- **Consultative role** (Nov 1948, TT 1524 Item 14): Cabell is *consulted* by USAFE on a foreign-allied-state institutional ETI-adjacent attribution arriving via the parallel TS sub-track — a dispatch his D/I directorate would not have generated. ^[inferred]

Whether Cabell **replied** to USAFE TT 1524 is **not in the bundle** the wiki has — the dispatch but not the response is preserved. ^[open]

The 8 Nov 1948 AFOIR-CO disposition slip on TT 1524 redistributes USAFE 14 to **AMC**, **C17**, and the **Armament Intelligence Branch (DCS/M)** — i.e., the substantive content moves into [[entities/air-materiel-command|Air Materiel Command]]'s hands while Cabell himself is being asked the policy question. ^[inferred]

See [[concepts/eti-attribution-statements]] for the cross-era pattern that USAFE 14 anchors.

## Significance to the 15 Feb 1949 AIRMEM #4 (Cabell as Director of Intelligence)

By **15 February 1949** Cabell signs **AIRMEM #4 "Unconventional Aircraft"** as **Major General, USAF, Director of Intelligence, Office of Deputy Chief of Staff, Operations** — see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164]]. The directive **supersedes Department of the Army Collection Memorandum Number 7 (21 Jan 1948) and letter CSGID 425.1 (25 Mar 1948)** *"both subject as above, which have been transferred to Air Force agencies for action"* — i.e. the formal **Army → USAF doctrinal handover** for the disc-reporting requirement, executed under Cabell's signature ~18 months after the [[entities/usaf|USAF]] became an independent service. ^[extracted]

AIRMEM #4 also institutionalizes two pieces of the 1948 reporting machinery:

- The **MCIAXO-3 attention line** is codified at the Directorate-of-Intelligence level (it had been introduced as a TWX modifier on 26 Oct 1948).
- **"FBI records"** is named explicitly as a witness-credibility source in Part II — a reverse-pull from USAF onto the Bureau, structurally parallel to (but distinct from) the November 1948 USAFE TT 1524 ask. ^[extracted]

Part II of AIRMEM #4 expands the [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element 1948 sighting template]] into a **~50-field 1949 schema** (20 object-fields + 10 observer-fields + radar fields + 10 General fields). ^[extracted] This is the **substantive doctrinal evolution** of the protocol Cabell himself authored 12 months earlier.

The **17 OCR-identical carbon copies of AIRMEM #4** filed at FBI HQ as serial 164 are visible institutional consequence of the directive's distribution list, which **explicitly names "Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation"** as one of eight inter-agency addressees alongside Director of Central Intelligence, State, GSUSA, ONI, and USCG. ^[extracted]

## Later career (background)

Cabell went on to become Director of the Joint Staff under the JCS and Deputy Director of Central Intelligence (1953–1962). His 1948 UFO directive is the earliest of the major intelligence-policy documents he is associated with. ^[inferred] *(Not stated in the source file — general historical background.)*
