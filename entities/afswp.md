# Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP)

The **Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (AFSWP)** — a **tri-service joint command** (Army / Navy / Air Force) established **29 January 1947** to assume military responsibility for the nuclear-weapons stockpile, custodial-base operations, and special-weapons logistics from the Manhattan Project's wartime arrangements. AFSWP operated from **1947 until 1959**, when it was reorganized into the **Defense Atomic Support Agency (DASA)** ^[inferred — general historical background; the wiki's sources do not state AFSWP's founding date or 1959 reorganization directly].

This page is a **stub hub**. AFSWP surfaces in the wiki via its **National Atomic Stockpile Sites** (specifically **Killeen Base / Camp Hood**), which are the focus of recurring **1949 UAP sightings** in the Army Texas G-2 channel.

## Role in the wiki's UAP corpus

- **Killeen Base / Camp Hood National Atomic Stockpile Site (1949)** — AFSWP operated Killeen Base (later renamed) as one of the original **National Atomic Stockpile Sites**. The **Camp Hood / Killeen 6–7 Mar 1949 events** are the wiki's first preserved Camp Hood-area UAP datapoints. ^[inferred] See [[references/fbi-san-antonio-vital-installations-1949]].
- **Vital-installations framing** — the AFSWP custodial-base layer is part of the broader **"vital installations"** framing applied to UAP-near-nuclear sightings in the 1949 timeframe. ^[inferred]
- **Doctrinal context** — by 1949 the AFSWP custodial-base operational layer is one of the wiki's earliest **nuclear-adjacent UAP-encounter** geographies. ^[inferred]

## Significance for the corpus

- AFSWP is the **institutional operator** of the 1949 Killeen Base UAP-sighting site — i.e. the UAP-near-nuclear-stockpile co-occurrence pattern the wiki tracks at the 1949 horizon traces back to AFSWP's custodial-base infrastructure. ^[inferred]
- AFSWP's existence and dissolution (1947–1959) brackets the wiki's earliest **nuclear-stockpile-adjacent UAP sighting cluster** (1949–1950 Camp Hood, plus contemporaneous Oak Ridge cluster which is technically AEC custodial rather than AFSWP). ^[inferred]
