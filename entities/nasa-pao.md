# NASA Public Affairs Office (PAO)

NASA's **Public Affairs Office** — the press / commentary / release function of [[entities/nasa|NASA]] that operates as a **release-by-default channel** for mission data. In the wiki's UAP corpus, PAO is the **originating intake-and-release node** for the **[[references/nasa-pao-t-00763-r1b-gemini-7-1965|GT-7 PAO Tape T-00763(R1b)]]** record, and so is the **first release-by-default channel** in the corpus.

The artifact's PAO commentator self-identifies as **"P.A.O."** and as **"Gemini Control"** (page 0). At the time of GT-7 (Dec 1965), Gemini Control was the public-affairs voice loop running in parallel with the operational Mission Control voice loop at the **Manned Spacecraft Center, Houston**. ^[inferred]

## What the PAO channel does (release-by-default)

| Step | Action | Source evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mission CAPCOM-to-spacecraft voice loop is recorded on master tape at MCC Houston | *"the master tape here in the Control Center"* (page 0) |
| 2 | PAO selects relevant excerpts; dubs them off the master tape | *"we have dubbed the tape off"* (page 0) |
| 3 | PAO reads brief framing commentary: situating the excerpt in the mission timeline (MET stamp), naming the witnesses, naming the objects | *"...at 4 hours 24 minutes into the flight, this is Gemini Control"* (page 0) |
| 4 | PAO plays the excerpt for the press in real or near-real time | *"we are now prepared to play it for you"* (page 0) |
| 5 | Tape and transcript become public record (NARA RG 255 deposit) | T-00763(R1b) is in NARA RG 255 ^[inferred] |

The **same news cycle** disclosure timing is what makes this channel structurally distinct from every prior UAP-adjacent intake in the corpus. ^[inferred]

## What the PAO channel does **not** do

- **Investigate.** PAO has no investigative authority over what the witnesses observed. It frames and releases.
- **Classify.** PAO operates under the 1958 Space Act dissemination presumption; classification of a PAO-handled tape would normally come from a *parallel* DoD or NASA review process, not from PAO itself. ^[inferred]
- **Cross-check against other intake channels.** There is no source-side evidence that PAO consulted USAF Project BLUE BOOK, AFOIR, USAIRA, FBI, or DOS before releasing the GT-7 bogey tape. PAO's posture is "this is what the tape says — make of it what you will." ^[inferred]

## Three-class object framing — PAO's contribution

PAO's **explicit three-class framing** of the GT-7 voice loop — *"the third and unidentified object of course was a bogey ... there were several references to the bogey"* — is the **PAO commentator's interpretation**, layered over the underlying voice loop. ^[extracted]

The underlying Borman + Lovell exchange does **not** unambiguously establish three distinct objects (Borman never explicitly differentiates the bogey from the booster; Houston offers booster-or-natural-sighting as the dichotomy; Borman doesn't choose between them). PAO's commentary makes a stronger claim than the voice loop alone supports — though PAO may have had access to crew discussion or post-event traffic outside the released voice loop. ^[ambiguous]

This is structurally important: **PAO's framing has primary-source weight equal to the witnesses' own** in the public record, even though PAO is a non-witness, non-investigative third party at MCC Houston. ^[inferred] Downstream interpretation should distinguish *what Borman said* from *what PAO said about what Borman said*.

## Open questions

- Identify the **specific MSC PAO commentator** voice on T-00763(R1b). The 1965-era MSC PAO included **Paul Haney** as a senior officer ("the voice of Apollo" in popular memory); whether Haney specifically narrated this tape is unclear. ^[open]
- Locate any **PAO-internal release decision memo** for T-00763(R1b). The R1b suffix implies a **revision** — what was R1a? Did NASA edit the tape? ^[open]
- Cross-check whether **MA-6 PAO release** (Glenn's "fireflies", Feb 1962) and **GT-4 PAO release** (McDivitt's "cylinder", Jun 1965) used the same release-by-default treatment. ^[open]
- Determine whether **Project BLUE BOOK** received the GT-7 PAO tape via NASA / USAF liaison and opened a case file. ^[open]
- Determine whether the modern **NASA PAO** retains a release-by-default posture for in-mission UAP-adjacent observations. ^[open]
