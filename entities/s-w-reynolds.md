# S. W. Reynolds

**Special Agent S. W. Reynolds**, FBI Liaison Section — the operational **courier** between FBI HQ and the Army Air Forces' intelligence apparatus on flying-disc matters in July-August 1947. ^[extracted]

## Role per the FBI corpus

Reynolds is the FBI agent that section 1 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|file 62-HQ-83894]] sends through every door at the AAF: ^[extracted]

| Date | Counterpart | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| **9 Jul 1947** | [[entities/george-f-schulgen\|Brig Gen Schulgen]], AAF Requirements Intelligence Branch | Initial cooperation request; Schulgen frames the disc problem (celestial / foreign / Communist-induced hysteria); requests FBI investigative cooperation. ^[extracted] |
| Same day | Col L. R. Forney, Military Intelligence Division | Forney concurs that, since the discs are not Army or Navy origin, *"the matter is of interest to the FBI"*. ^[extracted] |
| **24 Jul 1947** | Schulgen | Schulgen accepts Hoover's full-disc-access precondition; commits to AAF cooperation regime; Reynolds confirms FBI will route information through standard liaison channels. ^[extracted] |
| **6 Aug 1947** | Lt Col C. P. Martin, AAF Intelligence | Re Maury Island / Davidson-Brown plane crash — Martin states the dead CIC agent was on a "top secret mission", possibly to/from interview with Kenneth Arnold. ^[extracted] |

## The "Mr. Reynolds" channel

Across section 1, AAF-side counterparts (Schulgen, Forney, Martin) consistently address their relays to *"Mr. Reynolds"* — i.e. Reynolds **personally** is the institutional face the AAF deals with on the FBI side. ^[inferred] The corresponding FBI memos (Fitch → Ladd, often) frame Reynolds as *"Special Agent S. W. Reynolds of the Liaison Section"* relaying outbound FBI positions and inbound AAF guarantees. ^[extracted]

## Initials and signature

The FBI processing-trail signature *"SWR:AJB"* and *"SWR:mr"* appears on the Fitch → Ladd memos as the typist-identifier. ^[extracted] **AJB** is the typist; **mr** appears on the 6 Aug 1947 Maury Island memo. ^[ambiguous]

## Position in corpus

Reynolds is the **operational counterpart** to [[entities/d-m-ladd|D. M. Ladd's]] **gatekeeping** role. Ladd decides; Reynolds executes. ^[inferred] The Reynolds → Schulgen → AAF Intelligence axis is the **single most-trafficked channel** in section 1 — every operational memo about the cooperation regime cites Reynolds by name as its conduit. ^[extracted]

After this 1947 founding period, Reynolds' role in subsequent FBI UAP material is **not yet documented in the wiki corpus**. The post-1948 inter-agency channel migrates to whatever Reynolds-successor handles [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell's]] [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|AIRMEM #4]] propagation in 1949. ^[inferred]
