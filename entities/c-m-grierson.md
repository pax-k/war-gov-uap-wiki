# C. M. Grierson

**Air Commodore C. M. Grierson, Assistant Chief of Staff A-2 (Intelligence), [[entities/shaef-air-staff|SHAEF Air Staff]]**. Acting "for the Deputy Supreme Commander", Grierson signs the routing memoranda that constitute the spine of the [[references/foo-fighters-shaef-1944-1945|SHAEF Air/TS.37153/A-2 file]]. ^[extracted]

The memoranda Grierson signed (or that name him as addressee):

| Date | Reference | Direction | Subject |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 Feb 1945 | SHAEF AIR/TS.37153/A-2 | → USSTAF (attn Col Bradley) | Forwards the 415th NFS bundle for technical-intelligence consideration |
| 11 Feb 1945 | 37153 | → First Tac AF (Prov.) | Confirms SHAEF has no information explaining the night phenomena, refers matter to Air Ministry |
| 14 Feb 1945 | SHAEF AIR/S.37153/A.2 | → A.D.I.(K) | Encloses CX report (the Felkin Flak Bombe reply responds to this) |
| 17 Feb 1945 | (incoming) SDF/GJ 2365 4350 | ← A.D.I.(K) (Felkin) | A.D.I.(K) offers the [[concepts/flak-bombe-hypothesis|Flak Bombe]] candidate explanation |
| 13 Mar 1945 | (incoming) 111/45/DDI2 | ← Air Ministry DDI2 (Hopkins) | DDI2 reply: Me 262 / flak rockets / "still something of a mystery" |
| 14 Mar 1945 | SHAEF/AIR/S.37103/A-2 | → 1st US TAC AF | A-2 RECCE (Lt Col T. G. Ames) closes out cylindrical-object photo request |
| 18 Mar 1945 | SHAEF/A/TS.37153/A.2 | → First Tac AF (Prov.) | Closes the foo-fighter loop with the DDI2 reply |

One signature appears as **"J. H. Grierson"** but in context (same name block, "C.M. GRIERSON / Air Commodore / A.C. of S., A-2") it is the same person — likely a transcription/OCR variant of "C. H." or simply a misread cursive. ^[ambiguous]

## What Grierson's memoranda establish

- That **SHAEF treated the [[entities/415th-night-fighter-squadron|415th NFS]] reports as serious enough to escalate to USSTAF technical intelligence, the [[entities/uk-air-ministry-ddi2|Air Ministry]], and the SHAEF Scientific Investigation Division**. ^[extracted]
- That [[entities/shaef-air-staff|SHAEF Air Staff]] held the institutional position: *"there must be something more than mere imagination behind the matter, and in view of the fact that pilots and crews are becoming slightly worried by them, it is considered that everything possible should be done to get to the root of the matter."* ^[extracted]
- That **the closure of the file was procedural, not substantive**: the 18 March 1945 letter says only "no further, or more definite, information can be given." ^[extracted]

Grierson is therefore the WWII institutional figure most directly analogous to the role [[entities/c-p-cabell|Maj Gen C. P. Cabell]] would play in 1948 USAF Project SIGN: the senior-rank intelligence officer who **kept the unexplained-aerial-phenomena reporting loop alive even when no satisfactory technical explanation could be produced**. ^[inferred]
