# US Air Forces in Europe (USAFE)

US Air Forces in Europe — the European-theater **major command** of the post-war USAF, headquartered (1948) at Wiesbaden Air Base, West Germany. ^[inferred] *(Headquarters and chain-of-command details are general historical background — the source bundle does not state them.)*

## What the wiki has on it

The wiki's only primary-source touchpoint for USAFE is **[[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11|USAFE TT 1524, 4 Nov 1948]]**, the wiki's first archived TS-track Project SIGN-era artifact. From that source:

- USAFE sent **TS Control # 2-5317** (TT 1524) to the **USAF Directorate of Intelligence (HQ USAF, 4B-054 Pentagon)** on 4 Nov 1948, addressed substantively to **[[entities/c-p-cabell|Maj Gen C. P. Cabell]]**. ^[extracted]
- The dispatch contained five items — **USAFE 2** (cover note for the European Command special intelligence organization's complete report set), **USAFE 10** ([[references/sighting-holland-coast-1948-09-05|Holland-coast unidentified jet, 5 Sep 1948]]), **USAFE 14** (the [[entities/swedish-air-intelligence-service|Swedish AIS]] visit + [[references/sighting-neubiberg-1948-10|Neubiberg ~30-min hover]] + [[references/sighting-swedish-lake-crash-1948|Swedish lake-crash]] account), **USAFE 16** (Soviet radar-tube acquisition), and **USFA-1** (Soviet TU-2 / Kholomia "Redut" radar debrief). ^[extracted]
- USAFE officers conducted a **late-October 1948 liaison visit to Swedish Air Intelligence**, returning with the institutional Swedish "outside the earth" assessment of the flying-saucer phenomenon. ^[extracted]
- The USAFE 2 cover note explicitly framed the dispatch as a **funding-and-allocation argument**: *"We are of the opinion that inspection of these reports will lend weight to arguments for allocating Air Force funds to this organization."* ^[extracted]

## Significance

- **First USAFE-originated UAP-adjacent record** in the wiki. The 1948 [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948|AFOIR-CO file]] and 1948–50 [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|continuation file]] are dominated by CONUS field installations + Alaska + a handful of overseas posts — but USAFE itself did not appear there as an originating command. ^[inferred]
- **First foreign-liaison TS-track UAP routing in the wiki.** USAFE in late 1948 was running its own parallel intelligence-collection-and-dissemination pipeline at TOP SECRET, distinct from the AFOIR-CO CONFIDENTIAL/SECRET track at HQ USAF.
- **The European-theater liaison hub** for foreign-allied-state intelligence-service inputs into US flying-saucer / UAP intelligence in 1948. The Swedish AIS visit captured in TT 1524 is the wiki's only documented case of this hub function so far, but the structural claim (USAFE → foreign AIS → Cabell) is a routing channel that the AFOIR-CO field-installation track lacked. ^[inferred]
- **Operational-tempo claim**: USAFE's framing of "the recurring reports on flying saucers... they periodically continue to crop up" implies USAFE held an internal running file of European-theater observations as of November 1948 — separate from any reports forwarded into the AFOIR-CO channel. ^[inferred] No primary-source materials from this internal USAFE file are in the corpus.
