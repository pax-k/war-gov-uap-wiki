# Miguel Ángel García Macías

Mexican civilian correspondent who, on **19 March 1950**, mailed an **unsolicited Spanish-language letter** plus drawings from **No. 324 Avenida Pino Suárez, Veracruz, Veracruz** to the *"President of the Commission of Scientific Investigation of the United States of North America, New York"*, offering his theory that **flying saucers** are *"stratospheric aerostats"* powered by *"atomic force"*. ^[extracted] The letter was received in the United States and routed into the FBI's HQ administrative file **62-HQ-83894** as **serial 220**, with an FBI-prepared English translation by **Mrs. Sophia Saliba** (likely a New York field office translator ^[inferred]) filed alongside the Spanish original. ^[extracted] The FBI New York stamp on the artifact reads **APR 7 1950**. ^[extracted]

## Self-described identity

García Macías signs his letter (in both languages) as:

> *"Pianista Compositor Descubridor de Inventor Ideógrafo"* / *"Pianist Composer Discoverer and Ideographic Inventor"* — *"FOR UNIVERSAL PROGRESS"*. ^[extracted]

He gives no institutional affiliation. The page header on the Spanish original lists his address (Av. Pino Suárez Núm. 324, Veracruz, Ver.) as both residence and apparent professional address.

## What the letter claims

García Macías's letter is structured as **two concatenated arguments**:

1. **A list of his prior "Ideographic Inventions"** that he says were *"stolen from him and patented in your Nation"* or *"given to another person whose identity I do not know"*. ^[extracted] The catalogue includes:
   - *"Gote-Graduns"* (graduated dropper), allegedly stolen and patented in the US;
   - *"Automatic Shovels"* for dump-trucks, sent to the Mexican Secretariat of National Economy and allegedly given to another patentee after eight months;
   - *"Rooms for Measuring Optics"*, unregistered, in his possession;
   - *"The Gradual Centimetric Music of the Future"* — a system of *"PIANOMETRICS, PHONOMETRICS, CENTIMETRICS, and DISCOPHONOMETRICS"*, plans deposited in the Veracruz Town Library;
   - *"Ferrotactometer and Ferrocemactometer"* — anti-collision devices for railways and roads;
   - *"Exquinocis"* — a system for graduating time and distance for clocks, also deposited in the Town Library;
   - Stability and lighting designs for transatlantic ships and combustible-cargo wharves;
   - A study of *"Multiples and Sub-Multiples of Mechanized Gears"*, correlated with his music theory.
2. **The "Stratospheric Aerostats" thesis** — his proposal that flying saucers are man-made apparatuses combining the *"Global-Stability of the balloon"* with the *"Semi-Global Stability of the parachute"* into a *"Super-Stability"* body of *"Conic-Global or Global-Conic form"*, propelled by *"stratospheric skyrockets ... using Nuclear-Atomic force"*, with velocity *"greater than that of Light or Sound in accordance with the quantity of Atomic Material used in their fabrication"*. ^[extracted] He posits that the US already possesses these apparatuses *"making use of ATOMIC force"*. ^[extracted]

He encloses hand-drawn diagrams labelled *"ESTABILIDAD GLOBAL-CÓNICA Y SEMI-GLOBAL"*, *"ASPAS Y HÉLICES"* (Wings and Propellers), and *"HÉLICE / CAPTU-AEREU-FUERZA"* (Propeller / Air-Seizing Force) showing a multi-level oxygen chamber and air-current channels. ^[extracted]

## What he encloses about the Robles Jr. case

García Macías attaches a clipping from a 1950 Mexican newspaper — *"Las Primeras Fotografías de un «Plato Volador», Logradas en Durango a Nueve mil Pies de Altura"* — reporting **[[entities/german-horacio-robles-jr|Germán Horacio Robles Jr.]]**'s photographs of a flying saucer over Durango at ~8,000–9,000 ft altitude. ^[extracted] The clipping's masthead carries the OCR-mangled date *"JUEVES 18 DE MARZO DE 1880"* — almost certainly **Thursday 18 March 1950** ^[ambiguous] (the day before García Macías's cover letter, consistent with Aleman-era Mexico and the saucer-photo timing). The clipping is reproduced in [[references/sighting-durango-mexico-1950-03]].

## Posture: saucer-advocate pole, eccentric register

García Macías occupies the **saucer-advocate pole** of the [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame|scientific-vs-saucer-advocate frame]] — but in an idiosyncratic, **crank-adjacent** register: he doesn't claim to have *seen* a saucer, doesn't relay other witnesses systematically, and doesn't argue from observed phenomena. Instead he proposes a **physical-mechanism unifier** ("super-stability + atomic propulsion") and pre-emptively interprets the US as already possessing the technology. ^[inferred] His many self-stolen-invention complaints, the personal-grievance frame around US patent practice, and the highly idiosyncratic vocabulary (*"Pianometrics"*, *"Discophonometrics"*, *"Exquinocis"*, *"Ferrocemactometer"*) place his letter in the **unsolicited inventor-correspondence** genre familiar to mid-20th-century US federal agencies. ^[inferred] The wiki notes this faithfully — the letter is not sanitized into a serious physics submission.

The institutionally-relevant datum is **not** the merit of his theory, but the fact that the FBI **received, translated (in-house), and filed** unsolicited foreign-language saucer-correspondence at HQ across the credibility spectrum. ^[inferred] García Macías is the wiki's **first instance** of the *unsolicited civilian saucer-correspondent* class arriving via mail at FBI HQ — a structurally distinct intake channel from the WH-routed letter pattern (1957 Krasuski, see [[entities/wladyslaw-krasuski]]) in which a citizen letter reaches the FBI via the Special Assistant to the President. ^[inferred]

## OCR / source-record peculiarities

- The English translator is identified twice as **"Mrs. Sophia Saliba"**. ^[extracted] No federal-agency masthead, no FBI typographic signature, no agent badge. Plausibly an FBI New York field-office contract translator. ^[inferred]
- The envelope OCR shows the routing line **"U.S. Court House, Foley, Tex."** in addition to the New York destination — peculiar; possibly an OCR error or an interim re-routing point. ^[ambiguous]
- The cover slip reads **Class 0052** in the table cell while the page-header / serial-number block confirms **62 (= 0062)**. The **header** is authoritative; the cell appears to be an OCR digit error. ^[ambiguous]
