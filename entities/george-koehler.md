# George T. Koehler

Denver-based promoter, associated with **radio station KMYR Denver** (per [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 5]] **serial 206**) ^[extracted] and resident at **315 Franklin Street, Denver, Colorado** (per **serial 213**) ^[extracted]. Named in two distinct FBI section-5 intake channels (β and δ) as the **primary circulation hub** for the [[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950|1949-50 Aztec/Scully crashed-saucer myth-seed]]. ^[inferred] Almost certainly the same person as the unnamed **"Coulter from Denver Ford agency"** in the Wyandotte Echo / OSI 13th District TWX (channel α, serial 203) ^[inferred] — though OCR / proper-noun differences leave that identification provisional. ^[ambiguous]

## Wiki primary-source mentions

### Channel β — Denver SAC FBI Kramer URGENT (9-10 Mar 1950, serial 206)

> **TWO SOURCES ADVISED TODAY THAT UNIDENTIFIED INDIVIDUAL HAS GIVEN AT LEAST ONE AND POSSIBLY MORE LECTURES BEFORE CLASSES AT DENVER UNIVERSITY YESTERDAY OR TODAY IN WHICH HE DISCUSSED FLYING SAUCERS WHICH HE ALLEGEDLY PERSONALLY OBSERVED.** ... THIS LECTURE REFUSED TO REVEAL IDENTITY, BUT IS KNOWN TO **GEORGE KOEHLER, WHO IS CONNECTED WITH RADIO STATION KMYR, DENVER. KOEHLER REPORTED TO HAVE MADE SOME PREVIOUS CLAIMS HIMSELF WITH RESPECT TO OBSERVING FLYING SAUCERS.** ... OSI, DENVER, HAS NO ADDITIONAL INFORMATION EXCEPT THAT GEORGE KOEHLER IN JANUARY THIS YEAR REPORTED TO HAVE MADE SIMILAR CLAIMS AND UPON INVESTIGATION WAS UNABLE TO PRODUCE ANY VERIFIABLE INFORMATION. **OSI CONSIDERS KOEHLER AS PROBABLE MENTAL CASE.** ^[extracted]

Hoover's reply: *"NO FURTHER ACTION DESIRED."* ^[extracted]

### Channel δ — New Orleans SAC → Director (31 Mar 1950, serial 213)

> KOEHLER is alleged to have told ARMSTRONG in January, 1950, that he (KOEHLER), knows a prominent Denver oilman named SILAS NEWTON, also known as a "Mysterious Mr. X", and an official of the Newton Oil Co. ... NEWTON is claiming that he leased land in the Mojave Desert in California and that on this land a flying disc had been found intact, with eighteen three-foot tall human-like occupants, all dead on it but not burned. ... KOEHLER is said to have exhibited a radio set to ARMSTRONG purported to be a souvenir of the space disc. ... KOEHLER had been telling the tale so prolifically in Denver that he claimed to have had **telephone calls from Washington, D. C. and from the Federal Bureau of Investigation in which he was requested to keep the information to himself** and that, thereafter, he became mysterious about the entire matter. ^[extracted]

Note: The "telephone calls from FBI" claim is **internally inconsistent with the FBI's own files** — neither the Denver SAC URGENT (serial 206) nor any other section-5 serial documents an FBI call requesting Koehler's silence. ^[inferred] The most-economical reading: **Koehler is fabricating an FBI-suppression frame** to enhance the credibility of the recovery claim — a structural element common to crashed-saucer narratives. ^[inferred]

## Why Koehler is the circulation hub

Koehler appears in 3 of 4 FBI intake channels (β, δ, and almost certainly α via "Coulter") but is **never himself the originating source** — he always cites [[entities/silas-newton|Newton]] as the recovery-claim source ^[inferred]. The structural pattern: ^[inferred]

| Channel | Koehler's role |
|---|---|
| α (Wyandotte Echo) | "Coulter from Denver Ford agency" — almost certainly Koehler under different name; sources Newton-network ^[inferred] |
| β (Denver University lectures) | "Lecturer is known to George Koehler"; OSI Denver previously interviewed Koehler in Jan 1950 — **"unable to produce any verifiable information"** ^[extracted] |
| γ (Hottel) | Not named — but content alignment strongly suggests indirect Koehler-network sourcing ^[inferred] |
| δ (New Orleans) | Direct named circulator — story flows Koehler → Armstrong → Tool brother → New Orleans SAC ^[extracted] |

The **OSI Denver assessment** (channel β reply): *"OSI CONSIDERS KOEHLER AS PROBABLE MENTAL CASE"* ^[extracted] is the **earliest documented institutional skepticism** of Koehler in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] The assessment predates the December 1949 GRUDGE termination ([[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|Boggs-Hearn memo]]) by ~3 months and the Newton-Koehler fraud-scheme unwinding (1952-53) by ~2-3 years.

## Koehler's pre-Aztec history per OSI

Per the Denver SAC URGENT reply (serial 206): ^[extracted]

> GEORGE KOEHLER IN JANUARY THIS YEAR REPORTED TO HAVE MADE SIMILAR CLAIMS AND UPON INVESTIGATION WAS UNABLE TO PRODUCE ANY VERIFIABLE INFORMATION.

I.e. Koehler's saucer claims were **already in OSI-Denver investigative orbit** in January 1950 — i.e. synchronous with the Wyandotte Echo Coulter article (6 Jan 1950, channel α) and the [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Keyhoe]] *True* / [[entities/frank-scully|Scully]] *Variety* magazine articles (January 1950). ^[inferred] The "no verifiable information" finding is structurally consistent with the post-1952 unwinding: **the Aztec myth-seed has no underlying recovery; it is press-amplified fraud**. ^[inferred]
