## Key Takeaways

- **NEW — Same conceptual layer ≠ same posture across decades**. The Hunter 1963 NASC memo and the 1999 COMETA Report both occupy the policy-staff layer. They share the same conditional-policy framing and the same prepared-skepticism rhetorical posture. But the 1999 paper, with 36 years of accumulated case data ([[entities/gepan-sepra|GEPAN/SEPRA]] from 1977; ~22 years of investigations) plus outside-government authorship that permits commitments US internal staff cannot make, *commits* to ETH and prescribes six concrete state actions where Hunter only described the conditional and conceded its futility. Layer-position alone does not predict posture; empirical foundation thickness and authorship constraints do. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — The "best scientific hypothesis" claim is institutionally bracketed, not asserted as fact**. COMETA's foreword (Lebeau) and main argument (Letty / committee) carefully distinguish between *plausibility* (the claim is "the best scientific hypothesis") and *truth* (which requires irrefutable proof not yet in hand). This is a stronger commitment than Hunter's 1963 conditional, but a weaker commitment than typical advocacy literature. The narrow gap between the two is what makes COMETA structurally interesting. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — NASA's institutional posture on in-mission UAP-adjacent observations is release-by-default, not classify-by-default.** The 1965 GT-7 PAO tape was dubbed off the master tape at Mission Control Houston and played for the press the same news cycle. This is the wiki's first record where the originating-agency intake channel disclosed rather than classified. Whether NASA's release-by-default posture survived through later eras (Apollo, Shuttle, ISS, modern) is open. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Astronaut-class witnesses do not, on their own, resolve UAP-adjacent claims.** The GT-7 bogey is **single-witness** (Borman) within a multi-crew exchange — Lovell describes only the booster. Test-pilot pedigree raises the trained-observer floor but does not eliminate the booster-vs-bogey ambiguity inherent in initial visual acquisition of a known orbital object at unknown range. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — The USAIRA HUMINT channel is a parallel, BLUE-BOOK-bypassing path for USAF UAP collection**. The 1955 Russell Trans-Caucasus sighting entered USAF intelligence as IR 193-55 routed to USAF / USAFE / USAIRA Moscow — not to MCIAXO / Wright-Patterson. If routing patterns generalize, an unknown but possibly meaningful fraction of 1950s–60s USAF HUMINT UAP records sit outside the BLUE BOOK file the public uses to characterize the era. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Senatorial-class witness pedigree did not move the underlying institutional posture**. Russell — chair of the committee with constitutional oversight authority over the entire USAF — did not personally attend the Prague debrief, did not publicly disclose the sighting in his subsequent career, and did not (visibly in the corpus) trigger any policy-level USAF response. The system absorbed a top-tier witness without altering its handling pattern. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Observer-suppression behavior is an independent signal class** distinct from witness morphology data. When the third-party operator at the witness's vantage suppresses further observation contemporaneous with a sighting, the suppression itself is evidence about *what the third party knew was there* — separate from the witness's own description. The 1955 Soviet-trainmen reaction is the corpus's first clean instance. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — FBI substantive UAP-adjacent investigation existed in 1957**: the November 1957 Krasuski case shows that the FBI was *capable* of running a real witness interview on a UFO-adjacent matter in the late-Project-SIGN / early-BLUE-BOOK era when the entry path was elevated (here, via Cutler at the Eisenhower White House). The 1958-style "redirect to USAF" disposition was the field-office *default* posture absent such an entry path. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — 1944 Gut Alt Golssen as a second-order WWII witness datum**: a Polish POW's 1944 ground-level account of a 75–100 yd ascending circular vehicle near Berlin, recorded by FBI 13 years later, fits *neither* the foo-fighter / balls-of-fire regime nor the disc / flying-wing regime, and is not naturally accommodated by 1944–45 SHAEF or 1948 USAF candidate-explanation menus. Treat as supplementary witness recall, not as confirmation of any specific German-program hypothesis. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Earliest UAP-adjacent operational record in the corpus is still the WWII SHAEF file** (Dec 1944 – Mar 1945) on the *intelligence-apparatus* timeline; the 1944 Gut Alt Golssen observation predates SHAEF foo-fighter reports on the *witness* timeline but is recorded only in 1957.
- **NEW — First diplomatic-channel UAP-adjacent artifact**: the 1985 PNG cable shows a US embassy's procedural carve-out — query records, TELCON the relevant unit, formally answer "no US asset," forward the unanswered question to USCINCPAC. Same operational pattern AARO inherits ~40 years later for non-attribution. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — First foreign-government intelligence-service node** ([[entities/png-national-intelligence-organization|PNG NIO]]) and **first commercial-aircraft-radar cue** (Air Niugini pilot off Angoram).
- **NEW — Allied UAP-reporting institutional template is at least as old as WWII**, not a postwar phenomenon. Same 5-step loop (witness → unit S-2 → tactical A-2 → senior A-2 → tech-intel + science adviser → file closes without resolution) runs in 1944–45 SHAEF, 1948 USAF SIGN, 2026 AARO. ^[inferred]
- **First policy-staff-layer artifact in the corpus**: the 1963 NASC memo is the wiki's first archived EOP-staff-level treatment of the alien-contact question — addressed to State/OISA, not DoD, framing contact as a diplomatic problem. Its core recommendation, in any contact case: "the immediate burying of all Terrestrial hatchets."
