## Key Takeaways

- **Project SIGN's operational hub** in 1948 was Air Materiel Command at Wright-Patterson AFB; all USAF installations were ordered to route disc reports there directly via Maj Gen Cabell's 27 Feb 1948 directive (AFOIR-CO-5).
- **The 10-element 1948 sighting template** is the prototype for every later UAP report schema, including the structured intake AARO inherits today. ^[inferred]
- **Multi-sensor cross-channel corroboration is the strongest evidence class so far in the wiki**: the 2025 helicopter encounter has FLIR (LP/OP) + NVG (pilots) + naked eye (intel official) all aligning on the same time-stamped events — including a "super-hot" hovering orb that broke into two objects, a ~10 ft close approach, and at least six sequential flare-up/flare-down formations in 30 minutes, plus apparent target switching to a flight of military aircraft.
- **Federal LE multi-team multi-vantage corroboration** (2026 Event 1) significantly raises the credibility floor of a sighting compared to single-witness cases — but trained observers still systematically underestimate range/size, as AARO's Fiery Orb correction shows.
- **Partial transparency + beam-blocking + selective-witness visibility** stack in the 2026 "Transparent Kite" sighting into one of the more anomalous reports in the cluster.
