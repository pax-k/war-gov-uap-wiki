## Key Takeaways

- **NEW — The Project SIGN intake apparatus had a previously-undocumented Sep–Dec 1948 mass-event period**. The 173–233 bundle reveals that Project SIGN's first quarter as a routinized intake stream (Sep 1948 – Jan 1949) processed **58 incidents** in four months — a rate of ~14 incidents/month. This rate is roughly an order of magnitude higher than the wiki's prior 1948 picture (~4 sightings preserved across the Feb–Jun 1948 startup file). The intake was therefore at high operational tempo during precisely the months when the [[concepts/horten-flying-wing|Horten thesis]] and [[entities/swedish-air-intelligence-service|Swedish AIS "outside the earth" assessment]] were being formed. The wiki's prior under-coverage of this period was a **sampling artifact** of which NARA boxes had been ingested, not a real gap in SIGN activity. ^[inferred]

- **NEW — Engagement-class behavior is NOT a modern artifact of the drone era**. The 18 Nov 1948 Andrews AFB pursuit (Inc 207) is a textbook engagement case ~77 years before the modern multi-sensor 2025 helicopter-orb encounter. The behavioral signature — close approach, sustained co-location, target-following with vertical-axis evasion, sensor-stimulus response (brightening to landing-light illumination) — is **structurally identical** to the modern engagement-class corpus. This collapses one of the cleaner "modern phenomenon" framings of UAP behavior. ^[inferred]

- **NEW — The Dec 1948 New Mexico green fireball wave is the corpus's first multi-event single-region UAP cluster** with real-time professional-academic analysis. La Paz's analytical conclusions (not Geminid; horizontal trajectory breaks the meteor identification; correlated with but not reducible to standard meteor-shower scheduling) are reasoning artifacts that survive the institutional pipeline and are preserved as part of an Incident Summary Sheet's second page. The capability of the SIGN apparatus to incorporate civilian academic expertise inside a classified evaluation chain is the corpus's earliest such precedent. ^[inferred]

- **NEW — Two-source presence of the same UAP event in independent files is now a confirmed phenomenon**. The Fairfield-Suisun 3 Dec 1948 event appears in both [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|the 1949 file (EEI form)]] and [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|the 173–233 bundle (Inc 215 summary)]]. Same event, two intake pipelines, two records of different granularity. This means the corpus's 1948 sighting count is **not** simply additive across files — there will be overlap to deduplicate. The Tom Rush Jackson MS 1 Jan 1949 event (Inc 233) and the Edmonton/Alberta 17 Nov 1948 event (Inc 203) are similarly cross-listed. ^[inferred]

- **NEW — Diplomatic-channel umbrella covers payload-driven UAP-keyword DOS cables; figurative-UFO-rhetoric typology covers keyword-match-only cables — the two universes are complementary, not exhaustive**. After the `059uap*` series complete ingest, the wiki's diplomatic-channel umbrella has three substantive sub-patterns (A foreign-liaison-inquiry, B citizen-witness-relay, **C host-country legislative-event reportage**) and the figurative-UFO-rhetoric typology has two boundary anchors (Type-1 referential, Type-2 figurative). The **2023 Mexico cable demonstrates** that future cables can surface in either framework: **substantive payload → umbrella sub-pattern**; **keyword-only match → typology Type**. Future ingests could populate sub-pattern D (third-country diplomatic correspondence forwarded) or Type 3 (citation / quoted-source reference) — both forecasts remain open. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Release-tranche selection effect is real**. With the `059uap*` series complete (3 cables: 1 substantive sub-pattern C + 2 boundary), the differential vs. `CSP-2025-00040` (2 cables: 2 substantive sub-patterns A + B) is sharp. The boundary-case fraction tracks **release tranche**, not subject-matter prevalence. The mechanism (tranche-selector quirk, era effect, geographic effect, FOIA-process variation) remains open, but the differential itself is now well-supported by two release-tranche samples with sharply different substantive yields. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — `TSPA` is the de-facto positive subject-routing tag for aviation/airspace content in the DOS schema**. 3-of-5 cables (1994, 2004, 2023). The `UFO`-tag-absence (5-of-5) is a negative observation about what's missing; the `TSPA` 3-of-5 presence pattern is a positive observation about what's present in cables whose substantive subject involves space, civil aviation, or airspace. The two observations are independent — `TSPA` is not UAP-specific (the 2004 cable carries it because of civil society / aid framing; the 1994 and 2023 cables carry it because of aviation framing). ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Embassy stance toward UAP content is content-source-class-dependent, not era-dependent**. The 5-cable gradient (1985 → 2023) is not a chronological softening or hardening; each cable's stance is determined by who/what the cable is *about* (foreign service, US citizen, foreign MFA, foreign NGO, host-country legislature) and **what question the embassy is asking HQ** (asset-disambiguation, narrative-relay, demarche-on-violations, NGO-grant-assessment, periodic-political-reporting). The 2023 stance — **forward-and-relay-quoted-skepticism via selective inclusion of skeptical voices** — is the post-2017-era / Western-Hemisphere / periodic-political-reporting realization of this content-source-driven posture. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Two structurally distinct boundary-case types now anchor a typology**. The 2001 MOSCOW 13169 + 2004 ASHGABAT 1028 ingest pair establishes [[concepts/figurative-ufo-rhetoric]] as a typology of how "UFO" can appear in a DOS cable without UAP narrative payload: **referential (Type 1)** — the cable's subject entity has "UFO" in its proper name (NGO name); **figurative (Type 2)** — the cable deploys "UFO" as a rhetorical / sarcastic device in inter-state denial discourse. The two mechanisms are not collapsible: Type-1 has a *real-world organisational fact* driving the keyword match; Type-2 has a *content claim about denial / implausibility* driving it. Naming the typology lets downstream synthesis cite a single referent rather than re-explaining the boundary mechanism per cable. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — FOIA-release scope ≠ substantive UAP content** (extended). With four DOS UAP-keyword cables ingested (1985, 1994 substantive; 2001, 2004 boundary), the FOIA release process selects on **keyword match in subject-line text** — and the keyword can come from a referenced entity name (Type 1) or from a figurative trope in cable content (Type 2). Substantive UAP cables are a *subset* of the released corpus, not coextensive with it. The corpus's `059uap*` series partial ingest (00011, 00012 done) is **2-of-2 boundary cases**; the prior `CSP-2025-00040` tranche was **2-of-2 substantive**. The boundary-case fraction tracks **release-tranche**, not subject-matter prevalence. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Embassy-stance gradient runs neutral → rejecting → endorsing across the four-cable diplomatic UAP-keyword corpus**: 1985 PNG forward-and-disambiguate ("not us") → 1994 Kazakhstan forward-and-disclaim ("we have no opinion") → 2001 Moscow forward-and-dismiss ("would be humorous if not for the seriousness") → 2004 Ashgabat forward-and-endorse ("worthy of USG attention and support" — about the NGO, not airspace). The 2001 cable inverts the forwarded-without-endorsement default of the substantive cables and is the corpus's first explicit US-embassy-principal dismissal of a UAP mention. ^[inferred]
- **NEW — Foreign-government rhetorical use of "UFO" as a denial register** is corpus-novel. The 2001 MOSCOW 13169 cable preserves a Russian foreign-ministry official (Tereoken) deploying "UFOs" as a sarcastic-denial figure in a meeting with the US Ambassador. - **NEW — Same conceptual layer ≠ same posture across decades**. The Hunter 1963 NASC memo and the 1999 COMETA Report both occupy the policy-staff layer.
