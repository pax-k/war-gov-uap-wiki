# Early UAP Policy Track vs. Operational Track

The wiki's UAP corpus spans 1948–2026 and divides cleanly into three institutional layers, each producing a different kind of artifact:

## The three layers

| Layer | Primary document type | Wiki examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational / intel collection** | sighting reports, intake protocols, witness statements, interrogation memos, technical analyses | [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948]] (1948 USAF); [[references/western-us-event-2026]] (2026 AARO-handled federal LE cluster); [[references/usper-statement-2025]] (2025 redacted intel-official statement) |
| **Policy-staff (gov & private senior-credentialed)** | conditional-policy thought-papers, "what should we do if…" memoranda; case-investigation-grounded national-policy reports | [[references/hunter-1963-space-alien-race-memo]] (1963, US, EOP, internal); [[references/cometa-report-1999]] (1999, France, private committee, public — see [[synthesis/policy-papers-uap-1963-vs-1999]]) |
| **Modern multi-track agency** | sensor data, multi-witness corroboration, agency reports, briefing decks | overlaps with the operational layer in 2025–2026; [[entities/aaro|AARO]] is the institutional anchor |

## What each layer optimizes for

- **Operational** optimizes for *establishing the fact pattern of an individual sighting* — who, what, where, when, with what sensors, in what condition. The 1948 [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element reporting protocol]] is a clean exemplar.
- **Policy-staff** optimizes for *handling the conditional* — assuming contact, what is national posture? The 1963 [[entities/maxwell-w-hunter|Hunter]] memo is a textbook conditional case: it never asks "did this sighting happen?" only "if a contact event were confirmed, what then?" The 1999 [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]] occupies the same layer with a notably different posture — it grounds the conditional in extensive case investigation, asserts the extraterrestrial hypothesis is "the best scientific hypothesis," and proposes six concrete state-action recommendations. The two papers are compared in [[synthesis/policy-papers-uap-1963-vs-1999]].
- **Modern multi-track** is structurally a *return to the operational layer* with better sensors and (sometimes) cross-channel corroboration; the policy-staff layer is largely absent from the wiki's modern corpus.

## Why the policy track matters separately

A wiki that catalogs only the operational track ends up with a high-resolution map of *individual sightings* and a near-zero map of *what states would do if any of them were confirmed*. The two policy-staff artifacts in the corpus take that second question seriously:

**[[references/hunter-1963-space-alien-race-memo|Hunter 1963]] (US, EOP):**

- The [[concepts/three-class-alien-taxonomy|chemical / nuclear / Einstein-violating taxonomy]] for triaging contact by demonstrated physics command — orthogonal to every operational classification scheme in the wiki. ^[inferred]
- The diplomatic recommendation that contact triggers "the immediate burying of all Terrestrial hatchets" — a policy posture not derivable from any operational document.
- The honest concession that no realistic preparation is possible because institutional incentives don't reward it ("our policy will be determined in the traditional manner of grand panic").

**[[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA 1999]] (France, private committee, public):**

- The [[concepts/uap-state-knowledge-classes|four-class state-knowledge taxonomy]] (a/b/c/d) — orthogonal to Hunter's alien-side taxonomy; classifies *states* by their relationship to the phenomenon.
- The [[concepts/four-phase-contact-strategy|four-phase contact-strategy thought experiment]] — an inverse-first-person model of how an advanced civilization would approach Earth (observation / sampling / influence / direct contact).
- The [[concepts/mhd-propulsion-uap|MHD propulsion]] physical model — a known-physics framework that explains a substantial fraction of the otherwise-paradoxical aerodynamic features.
- Six concrete state-action recommendations, including diplomatic démarches to the United States to compel disclosure.
- A grounded-in-casework empirical posture that Hunter's pure-thought-paper lacks (Lakenheath, RB-47, Tehran, Trans-en-Provence, AF 3532, etc.).

## Posture parallels across layers

Despite the different optimizing targets, the three layers share a common posture across decades — what the wiki calls [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame|prepared skepticism]]:

- **1948 operational:** USAF collects disc reports, pursues conventional [[concepts/horten-flying-wing|Horten]] and [[references/loedding-flying-disc|domestic disc]] explanations, declines to assert the phenomenon is real or unreal.
- **1963 policy:** Hunter brackets both saucer advocates and strict scientific dismissers, writes the conditional policy paper.
- **1999 policy:** COMETA's foreword (Lebeau) inhabits the same posture — *"stripping the UFO phenomenon of its irrational layer"* — but the committee proper, after reviewing 50+ years of cases, ends up *committing* to the extraterrestrial hypothesis as "the best scientific hypothesis." The same posture-in-the-middle leads to a different output 36 years later because the empirical foundation has thickened. ^[inferred]
- **2026 modern:** AARO-handled briefing presents multi-witness federal LE testimony, applies corrections, declines either-direction conclusions; see [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]].

The structural similarity across 78 years is itself notable. ^[inferred]

## What the wiki is missing on the policy-staff layer

This layer now has **two** documents in the corpus (Hunter 1963 + COMETA 1999). Likely candidates to fill the gap further if/when sources land:

- 1953 CIA-convened **Robertson Panel** report (operational/policy hybrid). ^[inferred-as-target]
- 1968 **Condon Report** (University of Colorado / USAF) — operational with explicit policy framing. ^[inferred-as-target]
- 2022–2024 **AARO Historical Record Reports** — modern policy-layer self-assessments. ^[inferred-as-target]
- Any congressional testimony that crosses the operational→policy boundary. ^[inferred-as-target]
- Non-US, non-French national policy papers (UK MOD Sec(AS)2a internal memos referenced in COMETA Ch. 9.2; Russian Academy of Sciences materials referenced in COMETA Ch. 9.3). ^[inferred-as-target]
