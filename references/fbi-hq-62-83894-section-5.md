# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Section 5 (Serials 186-245)

The **fifth section** of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894]] — 209 OCR pages of declassified documents indexed serials **186 through 245** ^[extracted], covering ~15 July 1949 through ~8 September 1950 ^[inferred] (date stamps on individual filings span 11 Jul 1949 → 8 Sep 1950, with retroactive copy stamps from 1964, 1972, 1977, 1985, 2011, etc. on receipt-slip and declassification-stamp pages). Section 5 sits **chronologically wedged** between (a) [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]]'s September 1947 endpoint and (b) [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-403|serial 403]]'s 1956 / [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438|serial 438]]'s 1964 / [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449|serial 449]]'s 1966 endpoints — i.e. the section captures the **post-cooperation-regime period** when both the FBI (October 1947) and the Air Force (December 1949) had formally terminated active saucer investigation. ^[inferred]

## Structural function — section 5 is the post-regime tranche

Section 5 sits chronologically **after** the FBI's **October 1947 internal exit** from saucer investigation (referenced inside this section as "62-83894-141-160" — i.e. the unrescued-by-this-batch serial range in queued sections 3-4) ^[extracted] and **just after** the Air Force's **December 1949 public termination** of [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]]. ^[extracted] **Three structurally novel events** in this section reframe the file's intake function:

### 1. The Boggs-Hearn discontinuation memo — both agencies confirmed exited (28 Mar 1950)

The **single most institutionally consequential document** in section 5: ^[inferred] [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|D. M. Ladd → The Director, 28 March 1950]] — a one-page response to Hoover's hand-routing-slip query *"Just what are the facts re 'flying saucers'? A short memo as to whether it is true or just what Air Force etc. think of them."* ^[extracted] [[entities/s-w-reynolds|SA S. W. Reynolds]] obtains the answer the same day from **Major Boggs** and **Lt Col J. V. Hearn** of Air Force Intelligence: ^[extracted]

> The Air Force discontinued their intelligence project to determine what flying saucers are the latter part of last year. They publicly announced to the press in December, 1949, that the project had been discontinued. They advised that the press release had been concurred in by the Army and Navy. The reason for the discontinuance, according to Major Boggs and Lieutenant Colonel Hearn, was that after two years of investigation over three-fourths of the incidents regarding flying saucers proved to be misidentifications of a wide variety of conventional items such as lighted weather balloons and other air-borne objects.

The memo includes an **internal back-reference** to the FBI's own October 1947 termination: ^[extracted]

> You will recall that the investigation of flying saucers was discontinued by the Bureau in October, 1947, in order that the Air Force could take over such investigations. (62-83894-141-160)

The institutional consequence: as of late March 1950, **both the FBI and the Air Force have formally exited active saucer investigation**, with the AAF's December 1949 [[concepts/project-grudge|GRUDGE]] termination press release positioned as concurred-in by Army and Navy. The cooperation regime negotiated in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-1|section 1]] (BB42, 30 Jul 1947) and operationalized in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]] is **dead by ~3 months after the [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett admission]]** — see [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947]]. ^[inferred] The 62-83894-141-160 back-reference is the **single biggest open thread** in the wiki's FBI corpus — the Bureau's October 1947 internal decision-making is in queued sections 3-4. ^[inferred]

### 2. The Aztec/Scully myth-seed enters FBI HQ via three independent channels (Mar 1950)

Section 5 captures the **first FBI-corpus documentation** of the [[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950|Aztec/Scully crashed-saucer myth]] — the persistent UFOlogical claim that a flying disc with humanoid bodies was recovered in the southwestern US. ^[inferred] The myth enters the file via **three independent intake channels** within ~3 weeks of each other in early 1950, all traceable to a single Denver promoter network ([[entities/george-koehler|George T. Koehler]] / [[entities/silas-newton|Silas Newton]]) and one Variety-magazine writer ([[entities/frank-scully|Frank Scully]]):

| Channel | Date | Serial | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DC PD → FBI HQ** | 22 Mar 1950 | **209** | **[[references/fbi-hottel-memo-1950-03-22\|Guy Hottel "three saucers" memo]]** — Washington SAC reports info from MPD Sex Squad investigator Karl Howe, secondhand from "an Air Forces investigator", that **3 saucers, 50-ft diameter, with 3 three-foot bodies in metallic suits each** were recovered in New Mexico ^[extracted] |
| **Denver SAC → FBI HQ** | 9 Mar 1950 | **206** | URGENT teletype on **Denver University lectures** by an unnamed individual claiming personal observation of saucers and 3-foot dead occupants in NM, **known to George Koehler** of radio station KMYR Denver; Hoover replies *"NO FURTHER ACTION DESIRED"* ^[extracted] |
| **New Orleans SAC → FBI HQ** | 31 Mar 1950 | **213** | SA H. Warren Tool Jr.'s brother (Wayne Welch advertising agency Denver) reports **Koehler / Newton / 18 three-foot bodies / Mojave Desert intact disc** story; references the **True magazine** [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Donald Keyhoe]] article (Jan 1950) and the **Variety magazine [[entities/frank-scully|Frank Scully]] article** (Jan 1950) ^[extracted] |

The most economical reading: a **single myth-seed laundered through 3 independent FBI intake paths** rather than 3 independent witness reports. ^[inferred] The Hottel memo's most-charitable reading is **not** "FBI documents crashed saucers" but **"Aztec myth-seed launders through DC PD into FBI HQ"** — see [[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950]]. ^[inferred] All three serials predate the September 1950 publication of [[entities/frank-scully|Scully]]'s book *Behind the Flying Saucers* (which canonized the Aztec story); the section thus preserves the **pre-publication myth-seed phase**. ^[inferred]

### 3. The Oak Ridge Adcock radar event — first critical-facility radar case (Mar 1950)

Section 5 captures the **first radar-tracked critical-facility UAP event** in the FBI corpus: ^[inferred] the **Stuart Adcock / Oak Ridge TN** sequence of 1-8 March 1950 ([[references/sighting-oak-ridge-adcock-1950-03|sighting page]], serials 215-219). Adcock, owner of radio station WROL Knoxville and an amateur radar enthusiast operating a surplus APN-7 set, reports detecting an unidentified object **circling at 100,000 ft over Oak Ridge** ^[extracted] — a critical AEC atomic facility. The 9-day institutional response involves AEC, FBI, CIC (3rd Army), OSI (USAF), Naval Reserve, NEPA-Oak Ridge Engineering, and a CIA radar technician dispatched from Washington. ^[extracted] The FBI position throughout: SA Belmont's instruction to **observer-only status, no investigation** — while the Air Force engineering team (Col Gasser / Capt Cross NEPA) ultimately concludes Adcock's equipment is **"haphazard at best"** with **"some degree of inebriation"** affecting reliability. ^[extracted] No object is verified by the Naval Reserve radar.

The event's **institutional novelty**: it is the **first critical-facility coordination drill** the FBI participates in for a UAP report — and the post-mortem (in serial 218 closing) raises the **procedural-confusion** question: *"despite the fact that all agencies seemed to be operating in the manner prescribed by agreement … there nevertheless seemed to be an impressive lack of any agency actually taking the responsibility for the situation … While it is now felt that this entire matter was in a manner of speaking 'dry run', it nevertheless warrants some consideration from a procedural standpoint."* ^[extracted] This is a **dry-run failure mode** documented at HQ for AEC-facility air-defense coordination — analogous to but distinct from the [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|FSR 200-4 1949 Flight Service routing]]. ^[inferred]

## Themes

### "62-83894-141-160" — the unrescued back-reference

The Boggs-Hearn memo's parenthetical "(62-83894-141-160)" is the **single most important open thread** in the wiki's FBI corpus. ^[inferred] Serials 141-160 are between section 2's endpoint (100) and section 5's start (186) — i.e. they are in queued **section 3 or section 4**. They contain the Bureau's **internal October 1947 decision** to discontinue saucer investigation and hand off to the Air Force. ^[extracted] The decision is structurally consequential: it formally terminates the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|FBI-AAF cooperation regime]] **only ~3 months after** the [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett admission]] (~22 Sep 1947, [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]] serial 86) — i.e. the regime that section 1 spent 52 serials negotiating and section 2 spent 47 serials operationalizing, **the FBI shut down within ~6 weeks of full deployment**. ^[inferred] **OPEN: ingest sections 3-4 next to recover this content.**

### "Bureau Bulletin Number Five Seven dated October One, Nineteen Fortyseven"

The Denver SAC URGENT teletype reply (serial 206, 10 Mar 1950) references **Bureau Bulletin Number Five Seven (BB57) of 1 October 1947**. ^[extracted] This is the **first known reference in the wiki corpus** to BB57 — chronologically positioned to be the formal codification of the Bureau's October 1947 termination of saucer investigation. ^[inferred] BB57 is the **counterpart instrument** to [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|BB42]] (which opened the cooperation regime 30 Jul 1947, ~9 weeks earlier); BB57 closed it. ^[inferred] **OPEN: locate BB57 in queued sections 3-4.**

### Press-fraud documentation as a structural intake channel

Section 5 includes **at least 4 substantively documented press / photo fabrications** — Hixenbaugh Louisville Times film (serial 240), Borden UNM photo (serial 214), Halfery New Orleans German press (serial 229), Alice TX welded-wings (serial 236-238) — plus **prima facie suspect material** (Pervier Russian-saucer-base map, Ward Russian-mechanism drawings, Holmberg Danse-Macabre crystal-glass-wings letter). ^[inferred] The Bureau's **non-investigative posture** doesn't suppress these — it **catalogs them**, often referring source-debunking to local-paper colleagues (Steinau / Davis on Hixenbaugh; Chief McEnheimer on Alice TX) or local agents (Wyly Albuquerque on Borden) ^[extracted]. The intake function is: **HQ files everything, regardless of evidentiary value, on the topic-anchored 62-HQ-83894 file**. ^[inferred] This refines [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default]].

### Civilian mail's Russian-attack-vector hypothesis

Three independent civilian letters in section 5 — Pervier Tulsa (serial 208, Feb 1950), Hatten Circleville OH (serial 201, Oct 1949), Ward Yuba City CA (serial 226, Apr 1950) — frame **flying saucers as Russian weapons** with elaborate proposed counter-tactics (Hatten: drop anti-communist newspapers via **fin-stabilized cardboard discs**; Pervier: locate the eastern-Siberia base; Ward: report Ubalsky's mechanical drawings to Army). ^[extracted] This **3-of-12 framing prevalence** confirms the **early-Cold-War "Russian-saucer" hypothesis** as a stable civilian-channel attractor — distinct from the [[concepts/civilian-saucer-hypothesis-1947|1947 civilian hypotheses]] (foreign-state-weapons, etheric/inter-dimensional, censorship-request). ^[inferred] The Cold-War hardening between 1947 and 1949-50 (Berlin airlift, Soviet bomb, Korea pre-war buildup) drives the new framing-class. ^[inferred]

### The Army G-3 D-3 briefing (serial 230) — the analytical posture in 1950

The 19 May 1950 D-3 briefing (Mr. Stiles) ([[entities/army-g-2|Army G-2]]/G-3 channel forwarding to the file via "Rec'd from G-3 5-22-50 C.W. J.") is a **paradigm-defining institutional analytical statement**: ^[inferred]

> The continued reporting of aerial phenomena must then be attributed to a mass hysteria caused by the present tenseness in the international situation; the public belief in the ability of science to accomplish miracles; and to statements in the press by "name" individuals hinting at the existence of some new weapon. ^[extracted]

The briefing explicitly **rules out** all of (1) space-ships from other planets ("technical achievement required ... several orders of magnitude beyond ... earth today and probably would have resulted in some firm contact prior to this, either through deliberate landings or unscheduled crashes (Even these have been reported in the press - complete with descriptions of men only 18 inches tall! Such reports are sheer fabrication.)") ^[extracted]; (2) Soviet missiles ("absolutely no evidence ... and the use of atomic energy for the propulsion of any aerial vehicle is still at least several years in the future") ^[extracted]; (3) US new weapons ("US is not experimenting with any new weapon that could reasonably be mistaken by identified as a flying disc or saucer") ^[extracted]. **Only mass hysteria + balloon misidentification remains** — the D-3 framing **predates Project BLUE BOOK (which formally adopts the same posture in 1952)** by ~2 years. ^[inferred]
