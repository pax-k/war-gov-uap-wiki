# U.S. News & World Report — *Flying Saucers: The Real Story* (7 April 1950)

A **4-page magazine article** from *U.S. News & World Report* Volume XXVIII No. 14, 7 April 1950, Washington DC ^[extracted], titled *"FLYING SAUCERS - THE REAL STORY: U.S. BUILT FIRST ONE IN 1942"* with subtitle *"Jet-Propelled Disks Can Outfly Other Planes"* ^[extracted]. Filed inside [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 5]] as **paginated content** between serial 218 (Hoover-AEC letter, 14 Mar 1950) and serial 220 (NY SAC García Macías forward, 11 Apr 1950) ^[inferred] — i.e. a clipped magazine retained as paper-volume content within the section. FBI cover annotations: *"62-1384 / File in Flying Disc File / file 5 EHM / 52 APR 20 1950"* ^[extracted].

## What the article argues

The article advances a single coherent hypothesis: ^[extracted]

> Flying saucers, seen by hundreds of competent observers over most parts of U.S., are accepted as real. Evidence is that they are **aircraft of a revolutionary type, a combination of helicopter and fast jet plane**. They conform to well-known principles of aerodynamics. **An early model of these saucers was built by U.S. engineers in 1942**, achieved more than 100 successful test flights. **That project then was taken over by the Navy in wartime**. Much more advanced models now are being built.

The article's specific claims: ^[extracted]

1. **Origin**: First US flying-saucer aircraft built by US Government engineers of NACA (National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics) in 1942, designed by **Charles H. Zimmerman**.
2. **Original design**: Elliptical, twin-piston-engine, twin-propeller — *"maximum speed between 400 and 500 miles an hour"*; near-vertical takeoff; 35 mph minimum landing speed.
3. **Wartime transfer**: The Navy took over the project in wartime.
4. **Current state**: Saucers ~105 feet diameter, jet-nozzle-rim propulsion, 3-layer construction (centre layer slightly larger), built of metal alloy with dull whitish color; observed-and-measured by a White Sands Proving Ground scientific team in April 1949 at speeds well over Mach 1.
5. **AF investigation called off December 1949**: *"Official inquiry by the Air Force, in the face of overwhelming evidence that the saucers are real, was called off last December. This indicates clearly that top Air Force officials know where the saucers originate and are not concerned about them, as they would be if these aircraft were from Russia or Mars."* ^[extracted]
6. **Best service-attribution**: The article concludes the saucers are likely **US Navy** rather than US Air Force, citing the post-1942 Chance-Vought project transfer + Navy guided-missile budget + carrier-aviation tactical fit.

The article references [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]]'s December 1949 termination ^[extracted] (without naming it) and reads it as **evidence the AF knows the saucers are friendly Navy hardware** — i.e. the article's framing inverts the [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|Boggs-Hearn memo]]'s "no investigation because they're misidentifications" reading: U.S. News & World Report instead reads the AF discontinuation as **"no investigation because we know it's our own"**. ^[extracted]
