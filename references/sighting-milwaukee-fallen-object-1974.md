# Milwaukee Fallen Object — NMCC Inquiry (FBI Command Center, 22 Aug 1974)

A **22 August 1974 12:07am phone call** from **Major Horn** of the **National Military Command Center** (NMCC, Pentagon) to the **FBI Command Center**, inquiring about a UFO-related fallen object recovered by **Milwaukee Police Department** at **~5:55pm 21 August 1974**. ^[extracted] The call is documented in a same-day FBI memorandum from **T. J. Smith** to **W. R. Wannall**, dated 22 Aug 1974, captioned *"MILITARY INQUIRY REGARDING UNIDENTIFIED OBJECT THAT FELL FROM SKY AT MILWAUKEE, WISCONSIN 8/21/74; INFORMATION CONCERNING"*. ^[extracted] HQ filed the memo at **62-HQ-83894 serial 482**. ^[extracted]

This is the **second physical-object recovery case in the FBI corpus** (after [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-8|section 8]]'s Houston Blevins case) ^[inferred] and the **first instance of NMCC-to-FBI direct routing** of a UAP-adjacent inquiry through the FBI Command Center channel. ^[inferred]

## The chain of inquiry

The 12:07am call sequence ^[extracted]:

1. **Security Patrol Clerk (SPC) Charles F. Halty** of the FBI Intelligence Division receives the Major Horn call.
2. **Halty has no info available** at the Intelligence Division.
3. **Night Duty Supervisor Harry E. Morris Jr.** calls **SPC Robert Koller** at the **FBI Milwaukee Office**.
4. **Koller advises**: an unidentified object had been recovered by Milwaukee police at ~5:55pm 21 Aug 1974, *"but that it was still in possession of police."*
5. **Captain Wills** of **Milwaukee Police Department** had called the Milwaukee FBI Office to report the recovery.
6. **Morris relays the info back to Major Horn** at NMCC, suggesting Horn contact Captain Wills directly.
7. **HQ files the memo "for information"**.

## The object's described characteristics

> *"Very little was known about the object which was described as about 13 x 8 x 5 inches, metallic in substance and color, jagged on one side and had an 'internal heat source.'"* ^[extracted]

Five features ^[extracted]:

- **Dimensions**: 13×8×5 inches (~33×20×13 cm) — pocket-/briefcase-scale, not a vehicle.
- **Material**: metallic.
- **Color**: metallic (i.e., unpainted metal sheen).
- **Geometry**: jagged on one side — i.e., asymmetric, suggesting fragment-from-larger-object rather than designed-shape.
- **Thermal**: *"internal heat source"* — i.e., the object was warm/hot when recovered, hours after the alleged fall.

The **"internal heat source"** descriptor is structurally significant ^[inferred] — for a passively-cooling fallen object recovered hours after impact, residual warmth implies one of:

- **Recent atmospheric reentry** (hours of cooling consistent with a man-made satellite or rocket fragment);
- **Internal radioactive heating** (RTG/thermoelectric source — but Milwaukee Police would likely have hazmat-flagged a known RTG);
- **Internal chemical reaction** (the *"internal heat source"* phrase as recorded by Milwaukee Police could mean the police observed continued heat generation, not just residual warmth);
- **Press/observer-reported attribution** without direct measurement.

The OCR doesn't disambiguate. ^[ambiguous]

## Local context — UPI coverage

> *"This matter received United Press International Coverage on morning of 8/22/74."* ^[extracted]

The UPI wire is **not retained** in the file — only the FBI's awareness of it. ^[extracted] The press-coverage status places the case in the **public-known-incident** category, not a classified-channels-only event. ^[inferred]

## Why NMCC routed the call to FBI

The Pentagon's **National Military Command Center** is the **24-hour DoD operations center** ^[inferred — institutional knowledge]. NMCC's role on UFO-adjacent fallen objects post-Blue-Book closure (Dec 1969) is structurally novel ^[inferred] — by 1974 there is no active AF UFO investigation function, but NMCC retains continuity-of-operations responsibilities for **debris-fall events of potential national-security interest** (e.g., reentering Soviet satellites, classified US space assets). ^[inferred]

The fact that Major Horn calls the FBI Command Center suggests:

- **NMCC was first-tracking the event** because it appeared (per UPI coverage) to be a fall from sky.
- **NMCC asked FBI** because Milwaukee Police had handed the object to *"local FBI"* (per Major Horn's framing).
- **The actual chain was different** — Captain Wills had only **called** the Milwaukee FBI to report, and the object was still with police, not with FBI. ^[extracted]

The handoff between local-LE → field-FBI → HQ-FBI → NMCC → DoD-AF-channel is the **post-Blue-Book interagency-coordination problem** that [[concepts/post-bb-uap-coordination-question-1977|the Carter White House asked about 3 years later]]. ^[inferred] In 1974, no formal coordination function existed; the FBI's response was to **relay info to NMCC and file for record**. ^[inferred]

## Position in the corpus

The Milwaukee Fallen Object case sits at the intersection of three threads ^[inferred]:

- **Physical-trace cases** — second after Houston Blevins ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-8]] serial 380, Oct 1957) in the FBI corpus. The 17-year gap reflects the 1958-1974 paucity of physical-trace cases routed to FBI HQ. ^[inferred]
- **Post-Blue-Book interagency channels** — earliest FBI-corpus instance of NMCC routing UFO debris-recovery to FBI. ^[inferred]
- **Institutional-coordination gap** — pre-figures the [[concepts/post-bb-uap-coordination-question-1977|June 1977 Carter White House coordination question]] by 3 years.

## Open questions

- **What was the object?** ^[open] Section 10 has no follow-up. Possible candidates include: meteorite, aircraft debris, balloon-related fragment, satellite-reentry fragment. The *"internal heat source"* descriptor weakly favors the recent-reentry hypothesis. ^[inferred]
- **Did NMCC follow up with Captain Wills as Morris suggested?** ^[open]
- **Was the object analyzed by Milwaukee Police labs, the FBI Lab, or any DoD lab?** ^[open]
- **Was the UPI wire substantively different from the FBI memo's description?** ^[open] The FBI memo says *"very little was known"* — the wire may have had additional detail.
- **What was the trajectory or witness account of the fall?** ^[open] Section 10 has no description of the fall itself — only the recovery.
