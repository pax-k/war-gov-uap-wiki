# Sighting — Delaware + Ashley Ohio Multi-Witness Cluster (8 Apr 1948)

A **daylight 2-town 7-witness simultaneity cluster** on **8 April 1948 between 1400 and 1430** in central Ohio — same white cylindrical-then-spherical object reported independently from **Ashley OH** (3 witnesses, 1400) and **Delaware OH** (4 witnesses, ~17 miles SW, 1430), all describing southwest-bound motion. Filed as **Project SIGN Incidents 112, 112a, 112b, 112c, 112d, 112e, 112f** in [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-101-172|the 101–172 incident summary bundle]] under cover **RESTRICTED**, alerted to both **Delaware airport** and **Perkins Observatory** during observation. ^[extracted]

## What makes this cluster paradigm-class

### Two-town simultaneity across ~17 miles

The Ashley reports run 1400–1430; the Delaware reports center on 1430. **Object motion is SW at slow drift speed in every account** — consistent with a single object traversing the corridor from Ashley toward Delaware in the 30-minute window. ^[inferred] The Delaware sub-cluster places it overhead at 1430; the Ashley sub-cluster had it overhead 17 mi NE at 1400 — a ground-track speed of ~34 mph, matching every witness's "slow" / "drifting" speed report. ^[inferred]

### Cross-confessional witness depth

The 7-witness pool spans the social spectrum:

- **2 ordained Methodist pastors** (Rev James B. Stephenson, Rev John H. Bergen) — the highest credentialing of any civilian witness pool in the 101–172 bundle.
- **2 pastors' wives** (Mrs Stephenson, Mrs Bergen) — co-located observers with their husbands but independent intake-form-recorded reports.
- **1 newspaper reporter** (Mrs Lelah Stephens of Ashley) — trained observer of an institutional class.
- **2 commercial / bank-staff civilians** (Winifred Paines = bank Asst Cashier; Bertha Slack = housekeeper).
- **1 housewife** (Mrs Kirkpatrick) — with a follow-up second-hand citation from son Robert Cochran (Merchant Marine, civilian maritime service).

The pastor-pair clustering is a structurally distinctive *clergy-witness* sub-class ^[inferred] — credentialed-civilian witnesses whose institutional reputations gate the credibility of their account. The two pastors' simultaneous reports at Delaware OH should be read alongside the Ashley reporter's report — **the cluster is institutionally pre-credentialed across journalism + religion + finance + housewife testimony**. ^[inferred]

### Real-time institutional follow-up

Two observatory / airport contact requests were triggered **during the sighting window**:

1. **Delaware Airport** — alerted by Rev James B. Stephenson during the event; airport saw nothing in 30 min. ^[extracted]
2. **Perkins Observatory** (Ohio Wesleyan University, ~1 mi N of Delaware) — alerted during Mrs Bergen's sighting; unable to locate the object. ^[extracted]

The negative-result follow-up by two independent professional observation posts is **evidentially load-bearing** — it does not corroborate but it also does not refute, and it documents that the witnesses treated the event as serious enough to invoke institutional verification within minutes. ^[inferred]

### Morphology drift across observers

The same object is reported by different witnesses as:

- **Oblong / cylindrical / vertical streak** (Paines/Slack, Stephens, Mrs Stephenson, Rev Stephenson)
- **Spherical with protrusion / "blob with streaks"** (Mrs Bergen, Rev Bergen, Mrs Kirkpatrick)

The transition correlates loosely with viewing angle and time — Ashley reports earlier and from a more distant aspect; Delaware reports from closer / overhead. **A vertically-oriented object viewed end-on becomes round; viewed broadside becomes elongated.** ^[inferred] Rev Bergen's "5 ft width minimum" + Mrs Kirkpatrick's "not large enough for a person to be inside" bracket apparent size at meters-scale, not aircraft-scale. ^[extracted]

The "vapor streaks above" / "streamers of vapor attached" feature — recorded independently by Rev Stephenson + Mrs Bergen + Rev Bergen + Mrs Kirkpatrick — is the cluster's most distinctive morphology marker. The form **retained its shape and maintained steady movement on a straight course against the wind** (Rev Stephenson). ^[extracted] Movement-against-wind rules out simple cloud or balloon drift.
