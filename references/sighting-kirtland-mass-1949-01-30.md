# Sighting — Kirtland AFB Multi-State Mass Event (30 January 1949)

A **mass UAP event** observed at **2255Z 30 January 1949** across at least **five New Mexico installations and adjacent Texas locations** — El Paso, Albuquerque, Alamogordo, Roswell, Socorro — with an estimated **30 directly observed witnesses and ~100 total sightings**, all consistent with a single object at different points in trajectory. ^[extracted] Reported by **CO Kirtland AFB** to **USAF Director of Special Investigations (Office of the Inspector General)** in OSI dispatch number **OSI-1-9C, 31 January 1949**, classified CONFIDENTIAL. ^[extracted] The cable explicitly states: *"AEC, AFSWP, 4th Army, local commanders perturbed by implications of phenomena."* ^[extracted]

## Verbatim cable text (page 105)

> Reference previous reports, subject; unknown, aerial phenomena, file number 24-8. Sighting of identical object reported at 2255Z 30 Jan 49 by aprx 30 people. Estimate at least 100 total sightings. AEC, AFSWP, 4th Army, local commanders perturbed by implications of phenomena. Sighting reported from El Paso, Alburquerque, Alamogordo, Roswell, Socorro, and other locations. All appear to be same object at different points in trajectory. Unless instructed to contrary this office will make all out investigation with view to location of impact point if any. Request reply.

## Geographic profile

The five named locations form an **~400 km north-south corridor** along the Rio Grande / Sandia / Roswell triangle of southern and central New Mexico — encompassing:

- **Los Alamos National Lab** (the AEC primary nuclear-weapons research facility, ~80 km NW of Albuquerque)
- **Sandia Base** (the AFSWP nuclear-weapons assembly + custody site, Albuquerque)
- **Holloman AFB / White Sands** (Alamogordo)
- **Walker AFB / Roswell Army Air Field** (Roswell — site of the [[references/sighting-roswell-1947-07-08|July 1947 Roswell-Dallas teletype]])
- **Socorro NM** (later site of the [[references/sighting-socorro-nm-1964-04-24|April 1964 Lonnie Zamora landing]])

The **trajectory implied by simultaneous observation** at El Paso (south) through Socorro (central) within Roswell (east-central) is **structurally consistent** with a **southwest-to-northeast or south-to-north fast crossing** of the southern New Mexico airspace, **overflying the AEC nuclear-weapons triangle**. ^[inferred] The event is the **wiki's first primary-source mass-witness UAP overflight of the Los Alamos / Sandia / Holloman corridor**. ^[inferred]

## "AEC, AFSWP, 4th Army, local commanders perturbed"

The OSI cable's institutional framing is **without precedent in the wiki corpus** for the early 1949 period: ^[inferred]

- **AEC** = Atomic Energy Commission (civilian custody of nuclear weapons + research)
- **AFSWP** = Armed Forces Special Weapons Project (joint AF/Army/Navy nuclear-weapons employment-planning + technical training)
- **4th Army** = Army area command with protective responsibility for Los Alamos, Sandia, Camp Hood

Three nuclear-mission-critical institutions report being "perturbed by implications" of the same UAP event. ^[extracted] This is the **structural anchor** for the **AEC nuclear-installation UAP-threat-vector framing** that propagates through:

- The 16 Feb 1949 Los Alamos AEC/AFSWP/4th Army green-fireball conference (see [[references/fbi-san-antonio-vital-installations-1949]])
- [[concepts/green-fireballs-new-mexico|the Dec 1948 - Mar 1949 NM green-fireball wave]]'s institutional response
- The 1950 [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-adcock-1950-03|Oak Ridge / Adcock 9-day dry-run]] (radar-tracked critical-facility UAP event 14 months later)

## Relation to the green-fireball wave

The Kirtland mass-event happens **8 weeks after** [[concepts/green-fireballs-new-mexico|the 5-13 Dec 1948 NM green-fireball wave]]'s peak, **17 days before** the 16 Feb 1949 Los Alamos AEC conference, and **~6 weeks before** the [[references/fbi-san-antonio-vital-installations-1949|22 Mar 1949 SAC San Antonio memo]] documenting the conference. ^[extracted] The OSI cable does **not** describe the object's color, shape, or trajectory profile — preventing a definitive green-fireball classification. ^[extracted] But the event's:

- **timing** (within the green-fireball wave window),
- **geography** (within the Los Alamos / Las Vegas NM / West Texas triangle the [[references/fbi-san-antonio-vital-installations-1949|22 Mar 1949 memo]] confines the wave to),
- **single-object-multi-witness** profile (similar to [[concepts/green-fireballs-new-mexico|Inc 223]] of 5 Dec 1948 with 11 witness sets across 2 hours), and
- **AEC/AFSWP institutional response** (matching the 16 Feb 1949 conference)

…all suggest the Kirtland 30 Jan 1949 event was **incorporated into the green-fireball portfolio** at the AMC T-2 level, even if the cable itself doesn't use the "green fireball" descriptor. ^[inferred]

## What's missing from the cable

The OSI cable is **brief by design** — it requests permission to investigate, not a detailed report. Critical missing data:

- **Color** of the object (no green / orange / silver / metallic descriptor)
- **Apparent altitude** or angular size
- **Duration** (single-pass instant-arc vs sustained over-flight)
- **Trail** or visible exhaust
- **Sound**
- **Estimated speed**

These would have been captured in any subsequent OSI-1 follow-up report. ^[inferred] The wiki does not yet contain the follow-up. The 22 Mar 1949 SAC San Antonio memo (page 119-120) refers to *"the so-called 'Green fire ball phenomena' which began about December 5, 1948"* without specifically discussing the 30 Jan 1949 mass-event — suggesting the event was either subsumed under the green-fireball portfolio or filed independently. ^[inferred]

## Why this matters

1. **First mass-witness multi-state UAP event in the post-1947 corpus**: the FBI corpus contains nothing comparable in scale before this date.
2. **First AEC + AFSWP framing**: the cable's *"AEC, AFSWP, 4th Army, local commanders perturbed by implications of phenomena"* is the **earliest documented nuclear-mission-critical institutional UAP concern** in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred]
3. **Trigger for the 16 Feb 1949 Los Alamos conference**: the cable's institutional alarm plausibly catalyzes the conference 17 days later, which assigns the green-fireball portfolio to AMC T-2 (i.e. [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]]). ^[inferred]
4. **First "investigation with view to location of impact point" framing**: CO Kirtland's request to investigate physical-impact locations is **structurally distinct** from the [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|hoax-default]] dispatching of disc cases; it treats the phenomena as **physically impactful** by default. ^[inferred]
5. **Structural anchor for the AEC-installation UAP-threat-vector**: the cable establishes a **primary-source institutional framing** (1949 cable, declassified) for the AEC + nuclear-installations UAP-concern pattern that later threads through Oak Ridge (1950) → AEC files → INTRUDER program (Twinkle).
