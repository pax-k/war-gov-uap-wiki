# Sighting — Socorro, NM (24 April 1964) — Lonnie Zamora landing

A **landing-class single-witness close-encounter** with **multi-witness on-scene physical-trace corroboration**, occurring at approximately **5:45 PM, 24 April 1964**, in an isolated arroyo area approximately **1 mile southwest of Socorro, New Mexico**. ^[extracted] The principal witness is **[[entities/lonnie-zamora|Officer Lonnie Zamora]]**, ~5-year veteran of the Socorro Police Department; corroborating witnesses to the **physical traces** (not the object itself) are **[[entities/d-arthur-byrnes-jr|FBI SA D. Arthur Byrnes Jr.]]** (Albuquerque), **Sergeant M. S. Chavez** (NMSP), **Officer Ted Jordan** (NMSP), and **Undersheriff Jim Luckie** (Socorro County). ^[extracted]

The wiki-canonical primary source for this case is the FBI Albuquerque report by SA Byrnes filed 8 May 1964 as [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 serial 438]] — see that page for filing-metadata and document-internal structure.

| ~5:45 PM | Zamora hears a **roar** + sees a **bluish-orange funnel-shaped flame** ~½–1 mi to the SW; thinks a dynamite shack has exploded; abandons the chase | Zamora ^[extracted] |
| ~5:45–5:50 PM | Zamora drives up a steep ~60-ft gravel hill (3 attempts) toward the flame; roar audible; *"Sound distinctly from high to low until it disappeared"* | Zamora ^[extracted] |

| ~5:49 PM | Object **clears the dynamite shack by ~3 ft** (shack ~8 ft tall), travels SW *"very fast"*, *"seemed to just clear the Box Canyon or Six Mile Canyon Mountain"*, disappears over the mountain | Zamora ^[extracted] |

| ~6:00 PM | **SA Byrnes arrives** at the site (he was at the State Police office in Socorro on unrelated business when Zamora's radio call came in to radio operator Nep Lopez ~30 ft down the hall ^[extracted]). Finds Zamora, Chavez, Jordan, and Luckie assembled at the site. | Byrnes ^[extracted] |

| 8 May 1964 | Byrnes files the **Albuquerque FBI report** (cover sheet + 10-page narrative + 2-page hand-drawn ground-trace diagrams), distributed in **4 carbon copies**, routed to FBI HQ as **62-HQ-83894 serial 438** | Byrnes ^[extracted] |

## Object morphology

Synthesized from Zamora's narrative across all three close-range observation phases (resting / rising / departing): ^[extracted]

| Feature | Witness datum |
|---|---|
| **Color** | *"like aluminum — whitish against the mesa background, but not chrome"* |
| **Surface** | *"smooth — no windows or doors"* |
| **Shape (resting)** | *"car turned upside down"* / *"car appeared turned up like standing on radiator or on trunk"* (Zamora's first-glance attempt to make sense of the object as a car); pictograph in the original report is **FBI-redacted** in the released tranche ^[ambiguous] |
| **Footprint geometry** (resting) | Two slanted **"legs"** to the ground; object body **~3.5 ft above ground** |
| **Insignia** | **Red lettering** *"of some type"*; ~**2.5 ft high × ~2 ft wide**; in the middle of the object; pictograph **FBI-redacted** in the released tranche ^[ambiguous] |
| **Flame (rising)** | *"under the object"*; *"light blue and at bottom was sort of orange color"*; *"possibly a four feet area"*; *"No smoke, except dust"* |
| **Sound (rising)** | Roar — low-frequency-rising-to-high; *"not like a jet — knows what jets sound like"* |
| **Sound (departure)** | Roar abrupt silence → *"sharp tone whine from high tone to low tone"*, ~1 sec → **silence** |
| **Departure mode** | Vertical rise ~20–25 ft → 90° transition to **level horizontal flight at 10–15 ft AGL**, SW heading; *"no flame whatsoever … no smoke or noise"* in cruise |
| **Two figures** at close range (resting) | *"two people in white coveralls very close to the object"*; *"appeared normal in shape — but possibly they were small adults or large kids"*; *"I don't recall noting any particular shape or possibly any hats or headgear"*; only visible for ~2 seconds; **not seen again after Zamora got out of his car** |

## Physical traces — Byrnes's same-day on-site observations

The **physical-trace evidentiary chain** is independent of Zamora's verbal account — Byrnes observed the traces directly, on-scene, the same evening, with NMSP and SCSO corroboration. ^[extracted]

| Trace | Geometry | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Four indentations** | ~16 × 6 inches rectangular, ~2 inches deep, "object going into the earth at an angle from a center line", "pushed some earth to the far side" | Quadrilateral arrangement consistent with **landing-leg footprints**; the angle-of-entry asymmetry is consistent with **slanted leg geometry** seen by Zamora at the object ^[inferred] |
| **Three burned grass clumps** | Inside the four-depression quadrilateral | Other clumps "appeared not to be disturbed" — i.e. **localized scorching**, not area burn |
| **One additional burned area** | Outside the four-depression quadrilateral | — |
| **Three smooth circular marks** | ~4 inches diameter, ~⅛ inch deep, "as if a jar lid had gently been pushed into the sand" | Distinct from the four landing-leg indentations; geometry suggests a **second class of contact point** (possibly probe / sample / sensor extension ^[inferred]) |

Hand-drawn diagrams (artifact pages 37, 39) preserve measurements: footprint #1–#4 separated by **9'4" / 6'4" / 10'8"** with **90° orientations**; burns labeled **Buen #1–#4**; *"apparent direction of burn or heat"* arrow noted. ^[extracted] (The **scale of the trace pattern (~10–15 ft)** is consistent with Zamora's estimate of the object's vertical takeoff height of ~20–25 ft.) ^[inferred]

## Witness-character and credibility flags

Byrnes's **explicit on-record character note** for Zamora, from a 5-year personal acquaintance:

> *"Officer Zamora, known intimately for approximately five years, is well regarded as a sober, industrious, and conscientious officer and not given to fantasy. Officer Zamora was noted to be perfectly sober and somewhat agitated over his experience."* ^[extracted]

Zamora's own self-disclosure: *"Feeling in good health. Last drink — two or three beers — was over a month ago. Noted no odors. Noted no sounds other than described."* ^[extracted] Sgt Chavez observed Zamora as *"sweating … white, very pale"* on arrival at the site. ^[extracted] These observations are structurally analogous to **SA Rathbun's character note for [[entities/wladyslaw-krasuski|Krasuski]] in Nov 1957** (*"no indication of irrational or otherwise abnormal behavior"*) — i.e. **the FBI's standing investigative habit of an explicit witness-credibility note** is consistent across the 1957 and 1964 cases. ^[inferred]

The **multi-witness ground-corroboration** is the strongest credibility flag: at least **four trained-observer law-enforcement witnesses** (FBI SA + 2 NMSP officers + 1 county undersheriff) saw the physical traces within ~15–30 minutes of the event. ^[extracted] Local PD officer (Zamora) is the **single-witness anchor for the object itself** but is **embedded in a multi-witness LE corroboration cohort for the traces**. The Lopez radio operator stays at the Sheriff's office throughout; he is a **paper-trail witness** to Zamora's contemporaneous radio reporting (the *"It looks like a balloon"* exchange). ^[inferred]

## Position in the corpus

### Landing-class events

This is the corpus's **second landing-class event with multi-witness ground-corroboration**, after the 1948 [[references/sighting-hamel-mn-1948-08|Hamel MN]] event (Project SIGN Inc 162). ^[inferred]

| Event | Date | Trace | Corroboration | Object morphology |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[references/sighting-hamel-mn-1948-08\|Hamel MN]] | 11 Aug 1948 | 1 × 2-ft ground depression with leveled rocks | 1 SIGN field agent (corroborates trace) + 1 postmaster (second-line) | 2-ft dull-gray metallic round object, "whistled like a steam whistle" |
| **Socorro NM (this page)** | **24 Apr 1964** | **4 rectangular depressions (~16×6 in, ~2 in deep) + 3 burned grass clumps + 3 circular indents** | **FBI SA + 2 NMSP officers + 1 county undersheriff** (corroborate traces); local PD officer is single-witness for the object | **Aluminum-white smooth object, ~3.5 ft above ground on 2 slanted "legs", red insignia ~2.5 × 2 ft, two figures in white coveralls** |

Socorro is **structurally stronger** on three axes: ^[inferred]

1. **Trace richness**: 4 + 3 + 3 distinct trace classes with measured geometry, vs. 1 trace at Hamel.
2. **Corroboration class**: 4 trained-LE-observers vs. 1 SIGN field agent + 1 postmaster.
3. **Object-morphology specificity**: explicit landing-leg geometry + insignia + occupant figures + acoustic profile + flame color + departure-mode transition, vs. Hamel's "round metallic + whistling".

The **acoustic transition signature** (low-rising-to-high roar → terminal high-low whine → silence) is **unique in the wiki corpus** — closest analogues are the [[references/sighting-japan-glow-1949-01|Japan glow]] thunder-roar (delayed, not landing-coupled) and the [[references/sighting-andrews-1948-11|Andrews engagement]] sustained pursuit (no terminal-whine signature). ^[inferred]

### Engagement-class lineage

Socorro is **explicitly NOT** an engagement-class event in the [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement|engagement-class typology]]. ^[inferred] All three 1948 engagement-class events ([[references/sighting-chiles-whitted-1948-07|Chiles–Whitted]], [[references/sighting-gorman-fargo-1948-10|Gorman]], [[references/sighting-andrews-1948-11|Andrews]]) involve **aircraft-vs-UAP airborne engagement**. Socorro is a **ground-vehicle-vs-grounded-UAP** scenario, with no aircraft involvement. ^[inferred] This distinguishes it as a **landing-class** case proper, separate from the engagement-class taxonomy.

### Federal LE involvement

Socorro is the corpus's **first 1960s FBI-involved UAP case** and the **first FBI-authored field-investigation product** at HQ Class-62. ^[inferred] See [[entities/fbi]] for the cross-era table and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]] for the artifact-level analysis. Distinct from the [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern|federal-LE-as-witness pattern]]: at Socorro, **federal LE is the recording authority, not the witness**; the witness is **local LE** (Socorro PD). The trained-observer-witness argument therefore extends to **local LE under federal-LE recording**, a structural variant of the federal-LE-as-witness pattern. ^[inferred]
