# Sighting — Pan Am Powell-White / Boston Approach (4 Aug 1947)

A two-witness commercial-airline UAP encounter on **4 August 1947 at 1600 EDT**, on the Pan American Airways Inc. **Constellation flight from Gander Newfoundland → La Guardia Field New York**, at a position **between the Everett (Mass) Fan Marker and the Bedford Radio Beacon** (Everett 3 mi NW of Boston, Bedford 15 mi NW). ^[extracted] The aircraft was at **8,000 ft, airspeed 265 mph, course 244° magnetic**, with **6-8/10ths cloud coverage tops at 10,000 ft, wind 270/25 at 8,000 ft**. ^[extracted]

Documented in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|62-HQ-83894 serial 130]] via two HQ Air Defense Command, Mitchel Field NY interview reports: **12 September 1947 (Powell)** and **15 September 1947 (White)** — the first two documents in the serial-130 packet. ^[extracted] **First commercial-airline-pilot UAP encounter in the wiki corpus.** ^[inferred]

## What Powell reported

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date / time | 4 Aug 1947 ~1600 EDT |
| Aircraft | Pan American Airways Constellation |
| Route | Gander Newfoundland → La Guardia Field NY |
| Position | between Everett MA Fan Marker and Bedford Radio Beacon (~10 mi NW of Boston) |
| Altitude | 8,000 ft |
| Airspeed | 265 mph |
| Course | 244° magnetic |
| Weather | Visibility good; cloud coverage 6-8/10ths, tops at 10,000 ft; wind at 8,000 ft 270/25 mph |
| Witness role | First pilot (took over aircraft at Gander 1230 EDT) |
| Witness experience | **4,000+ command-pilot hours**; Maxwell Field AL Aviation Cadet Class 41-C graduate |
| Initial sighting | White (in copilot seat) called Powell's attention to "**a bright orange object**" on the right side of the plane, slightly below aircraft level |
| Powell's view | Powell could not initially see the object from the left seat (no visibility right-and-down); he "**unbuckled his seat belt and got over to the right side**" to confirm |

The HQ ADC Mitchel Field interview is dated 12 September 1947 — i.e. **39 days after the sighting**, and ~1 week after the **5 Sep 1947 [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]] formal denial letter to Hoover** ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2 serial 84]]). ^[inferred]

## What White reported (independent)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date / time | 4 Aug 1947 ~1600 EDT |
| Position | ~10 mi NW of Boston |
| Witness role | Navigator (sitting in copilot seat looking right for other aircraft) |
| Initial distance | "about 3 miles away, and at least 1,000 feet below the level of the Constellation" |
| Time observed before alerting Powell | "almost 30 seconds" |
| Color | "**Deep gold in color, and that its surface reflected light**" |
| Size | "about **15 feet in length, and from 2-3 feet in depth**" |
| Shape | "**Elliptical in shape and blunted at the ends**" |
| Speed | ~175 mph |
| Direction | Easterly, ~110° magnetic |
| Final view | Lost sight when the Constellation banked left to give Powell visibility |

White's interview is **3 days later than Powell's** (15 Sep vs 12 Sep), confirming **independent witness solicitation by ADC** rather than joint interview. ^[inferred]

## Why this case matters

### First commercial-airline-pilot UAP encounter in the corpus

The wiki corpus does not contain a prior commercial-airline-pilot sighting before 4 Aug 1947 (Powell-White). ^[inferred] The next commercial-airline encounter is **9 Jul 1947 [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Capt E. J. Smith United Airlines DC-3]]** Boise → Seattle — but Smith's case appears in serial 130 as **AFBIR-CO Report #8**, predating Powell-White by ~26 days. ^[extracted] Powell-White is the **second** commercial-airline-pilot encounter chronologically — but the **first Pan American Airways pilot encounter** and the **first commercial-airline encounter on the Atlantic / North-American-East-Coast corridor**. ^[inferred]

### Powell's character profile

The agent's notes on Powell are unusually detailed: ^[extracted]

> Mr. A. O. Powell is a graduate of the Aviation Cadet Flying Training Program, having graduated from Maxwell Field, Alabama, with the Class of 41-C. Since graduation, Mr. Powell has flown for Pan American Airways and, at this date, has over 4,000 command pilot hours to his credit. **Mr. Powell appears to be a calm, intelligent individual, not given to flights of fancy, or easily swayed by what he has previously read in the newspapers as regards reports of this type. Mr. Powell has a fear of publicity and seemed hesitant to even tell his story lest he become the object of ridicule.** Mr. Powell was questioned as to the possibility that what he sighted might have been a tow target, a pilot balloon, or a radiosonic device used for meteorological purposes. Mr. Powell stated that he has seen numerous pilot balloons, radiosonic devices and tow targets, while on flights; the object observed on this flight definitely was not one of them.

The character profile is structurally identical to the agent-notes profile of Arnold preserved at [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|serial 95]]: ^[inferred]

| Field | Arnold (Brown 4AF) | Powell (HQ ADC) |
|---|---|---|
| Family + provider framing | "well thought of in the community … very much the family man" | "calm, intelligent individual" |
| Anti-fancy framing | implicit | "not given to flights of fancy" |
| Anti-press-suggestibility framing | implicit | "not easily swayed by what he has previously read in the newspapers" |
| Publicity aversion framing | "outspoken and somewhat bitter" (Arnold post-press) | "fear of publicity and seemed hesitant to even tell his story lest he become the object of ridicule" (Powell pre-press) |
| Conventional-explanation challenge | aeronautical-chart cross-checking | balloon / radiosonde / tow-target challenge |

The structural parallel suggests AAF intelligence officers were **applying a standard credibility-elicitation script** to commercial-airline-pilot witnesses across the 1947 wave. ^[inferred] The script's elements — *family man*, *not given to fancy*, *not press-suggestible*, *fearful of ridicule*, *able to rule out conventional explanations* — are the **proto-version of the [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|Cabell 10-element directive's]] witness-credibility section** issued 18 months later (Feb 1949). ^[inferred]

### Conventional explanations White ruled out

White's 30-second observation period and his explicit **distinction from any meteorological balloon** is significant: ^[extracted]

- **Pilot balloon / radiosonic device**: Both Powell and White ruled this out (Powell explicitly told the interviewer; White's elliptical 15×2-3 ft profile is incompatible with a 1947-era pibal which would be roughly spherical).
- **Tow target**: Powell explicitly ruled out (had seen "numerous tow targets" on flights).
- **Other aircraft**: Powell's 4,000+ hours and White's navigator role both required identification of all visible aircraft; neither identified the object as conventional aircraft.

The **deep-gold surface-reflective color + elliptical-blunted-ends shape + 15-ft length** signature is **morphologically distinct** from the **silvery-disc / circular-saucer** signature dominant in [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30|the AFBIR-CO 18-witness inventory]]. ^[inferred] **None of the AFBIR-CO 18 reports describes a deep-gold elliptical object** — Powell-White's morphology is **outside the analytical priors** AFBIR-CO synthesized by 30 Jul 1947, which is consistent with the AFBIR-CO breakdown's **selection cutoff at 12 Jul 1947**. ^[inferred]

## Open questions

- **Is the Powell-White case the same incident as any other 4 Aug 1947 New England commercial-aviation report?** The corpus does not yet contain an independent corroboration of an Aug-4 Boston-area sighting. ^[open]
- **What is Powell's relationship to** the **Sorensen / Aldrich / Rankin Pan American MOICs** at [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2 serial 53]]? Pan Am's Aug 1947 institutional response to the disc wave evidently routed multiple pilot reports through 4AF Hamilton Field — but the section 2 serial 53 corpus has not yet been resolved against Powell. ^[open]
- **Why was Powell's interview delayed 39 days after the sighting?** Standard AAF practice in serial 130's other cases shows interviews within 7-10 days of sighting; Powell's 39-day delay may indicate the case was not initially routed up the AAF intelligence chain. ^[inferred]
- **Were Powell's and White's recollections cross-corroborative on the bank-left-then-lost-sight maneuver?** Powell's report (interview 12 Sep) records that he sighted the object after unbuckling and crossing to the right; White's report (interview 15 Sep) records the bank-left-then-lost-sight maneuver after he alerted Powell. The maneuver-and-loss may indicate Powell briefly saw the object, then it was lost during the bank — but the OCR record is partially truncated and this should be re-read in full. ^[ambiguous]
