# The Round Robin / The Flying Roll — Meade Layne 9-point Memorandum (8 Jul 1947)

A **mimeographed 9-point "memorandum of importance"** issued by [[entities/n-meade-layne|N. Meade Layne]] from his San Diego address (3615 Alexia Place) on **8 July 1947** under the publication banners **"The Round Robin / The Flying Roll"**. ^[extracted] Filed in the FBI corpus as a **dropping into the civilian-mass-correspondence channel** of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|file 62-HQ-83894]] section 1 (no specific serial number in the OCR — the document appears at p. 160). ^[extracted] It is the corpus's **first explicit eti-attribution document** and the structurally earliest civilian-paranormal-hypothesis text in the wiki. ^[inferred]

## What the memo says

Layne addresses the document *"to certain scientists of distinction, to important aeronautical and military authorities, to a number of public officials, and to a few publications"* and prefaces it: ^[extracted]

> The writer has little expectation that anything of import will be accomplished by this gesture. The mere fact that the data herein were obtained by so-called supernormal means is probably sufficient to insure its disregard by nearly all the persons addressed; nevertheless it seems a public duty to make it available. (The present writer has several university degrees and was formerly a university department head).

The 9-point manifesto, verbatim: ^[extracted]

1. Part of the disks carry crews, others are under remote control.
2. Their mission is peaceful. The visitors contemplate settling on this planet.
3. These visitors are human-like but much larger in size.
4. They are NOT excarnate earth people, but come from their own world.
5. They do NOT come from any "planet" as we use the word, but from an etheric planet which interpenetrates with our own and is not perceptible to us.
6. The bodies of the visitors, and the craft also, automatically "materialize" on entering the vibratory rate of our dense matter. (Cp. "apports.")
7. The disks possess a type of radiant energy, or a ray, which will easily disintegrate any attacking ship. They reenter the etheric at will, and so simply disappear from our vision, without trace.
8. The region from which they come is NOT the "astral plane", but corresponds to the **Lokas or Talas**. Students of esoteric matters will understand these terms.
9. They probably cannot be reached by radio, but probably can be by radar, if a signal system can be devised for that apparatus.

The closing addendum describes the craft physically: ^[extracted]

> The Lokas are oval shape, fluted lengthwise of the oval with a heat-resisting metal or alloy not yet known to us; the front edge contains the controls; the middle portion is a laboratory; the rear contains armament. The [armament] consists essentially of a powerful radio energy apparatus, perhaps a ray apparatus.

## Why this matters

This is **the corpus's earliest eti-attribution document**. ^[inferred] Specifically: ^[inferred]

- It **predates the language of "extraterrestrial"** in mainstream discourse by ~3 years (the term emerges in 1950 with Frank Scully's *Behind the Flying Saucers*).
- It **introduces the inter-dimensional / "etheric" framing** that will recur in saucer literature (Tibetan-Buddhist Loka cosmology mapped onto the saucer phenomenon).
- It **predicts disintegrating-ray armament** and **radar (not radio) detectability** — both claims later widely repeated in saucer-paranormal literature without attribution to Layne.
- It **anticipates the language of materialization/dematerialization** later associated with the abduction phenomenon (1960s+).

Layne is the founder of **Borderland Sciences Research Associates** (1945, San Diego), one of the earliest organized civilian-paranormal-research orgs to engage flying saucers. ^[inferred] The Round Robin / The Flying Roll were his society's mimeographed bulletins. ^[inferred]

## How the FBI processed it

The FBI's processing trail is **invisible** in section 1 — the document is filed but no acknowledgment letter, routing memo, or follow-up appears. ^[extracted] This is consistent with the **passive-accumulation** posture that 62-HQ-83894 takes toward civilian-mass-correspondence: ^[inferred] inbound material is filed-and-retained, no investigation conducted, **no acknowledgment if the writer's letterhead suggests fringe-paranormal status** ^[inferred].

This stands in contrast to the **acknowledgment-and-forward-to-War-Department** treatment given to civilian letters whose authors held conventional credentials (Reibold-Omaha → War Dept; Hoersch-Jersey-City → War Dept; Sroka-Waukesha → War Dept) — see [[concepts/civilian-saucer-hypothesis-1947]]. ^[inferred]

## Position in corpus

The Layne memo is the earliest example in the corpus of a **structural pattern** that recurs throughout the file: civilian writers project frameworks (orbital germ-bombs, inter-dimensional travel, Russian leakage, German experimental aircraft) onto an unexplained phenomenon, with the FBI passively retaining the diversity of frames without endorsing any. ^[inferred] See [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame]] for the broader pattern that Layne occupies the **saucer-paranormal pole** of.
