# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Section 4 (Serials ~113-185)

A **214-OCR-page** declassified tranche of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]] (size 274 KB, SHA-256 `d4978e19…`), the **tenth ingested artifact** in the file's 17-section ingest series. ^[inferred] Section 4 sits **between section 5 (serials 186-245, Jul 1949 - Sep 1950)** and the unrescued sections 1-3 / 6-10 / sub_a; the page-1 serial header on page 174 confirms `62-83894-177` and other internal serial markers (130-185 range) place this section's effective span at **serials ~113-185**, **October 1947 through approximately mid-1949**. ^[extracted] Internal serial citations include 140 (Stratemeyer), 144, 146-160, 167 (Atkins-De Jarnett), 170 (Mrs Gardner), 177 (closing serial bleed), with OCR-drift outliers (`1`, `1169`, `1418`) treated as page-furniture or typo artifacts. ^[ambiguous]

## HEADLINE — Section 4 closes the BB-57 open thread

Section 4 contains **the first verbatim citations of [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 57, Series 1947, dated October 1, 1947]]** in the wiki corpus. ^[extracted] Four documents in the section cite BB-57 by name and date, three directly transcribing its operational paraphrase:

1. **SAC San Francisco (Kimball) → Director, 12 Feb 1948** (page 34, serial 140) — flags conflict between Stratemeyer ADC instructions (4 Feb 1948) and *"Bureau Bulletin No. 57, Series 1947, dated October 1, 1947, in which it is indicated that all future reports connected with flying discs should be referred to the Air Force and no investigative action taken by Bureau agents"*. ^[extracted]
2. **Director → SAC San Francisco, 20 Feb 1948** (page 38) — *"Bureau Bulletin No. 57 dated October 1, 1947, still applies with respect to flying discs. The Bureau is conducting no investigations concerning flying discs."* ^[extracted]
3. **Fletcher → Ladd memo, 14 Mar 1949** (page 117, serial 160) — *"You will recall that by Bureau Bulletin #57, Series 1947, dated October 1, 1947, the field was advised that effective as of that date the Bureau had discontinued its investigative activities in connection with flying discs. The field was advised that all future reports concerning this matter received in the field should be referred to the Air Forces."* ^[extracted]
4. **[[references/fbi-sac-letter-38-1949|SAC Letter No. 38, Series 1949, 25 Mar 1949]]** (page 124) — *"Your attention is directed to Bureau Bulletin #57, Series 1947, dated October 1, 1947, relating to the discontinuance of investigation by this Bureau in matters concerning flying discs."* ^[extracted]

These four citations **promote BB-57 from `^[inferred]` to `^[extracted]`** in the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|FBI-AAF cooperation regime]] termination row, **closing an open thread on [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|the parent file page]]**. ^[inferred] The bulletin itself is **not transcribed in section 4** — only cited from later documents — so the wiki's BB-57 content remains reconstructed-from-references rather than primary-source. ^[inferred]

## HEADLINE — The 4-beat post-BB-57 narrative arc

Section 4 captures four institutional events that **complicate the prior wiki framing** of "FBI saucer investigation silently terminated October 1947": ^[inferred]

| Beat | Date | Event | Section-4 anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Termination** | 1 Oct 1947 | **BB-57 issued.** All future disc reports referred to Air Force; no investigative action by FBI agents. | cited verbatim in pages 34, 38, 117, 124 |
| **2. USAF resistance** | 4 Feb 1948 | **[[references/adc-stratemeyer-flying-disc-1948-02-04\|ADC HQ Mitchel Field, by command of Lt Gen Stratemeyer]]** issues *"Investigation and Reporting of 'Flying Disc' Incidents"* directing 1st/2nd/4th/10th/11th/14th AF Commanding Generals to **coordinate disc investigations with the FBI Office concerned**. ^[extracted] SAC Kimball flags conflict to HQ. Hoover (via Keay/Reynolds memo, 1 Mar 1948) reaffirms BB-57 still applies; Lt Col Martin of AF Intelligence agrees Mitchel Field was *"not aware that the Bureau was no longer conducting investigations regarding flying discs"* and commits to *"straighten out the Air Defense Command as to the present policy"*. ^[extracted] | pages 34-40 |
| **3. Operational selectivity** | 27-29 Dec 1948 | **[[references/sighting-noack-helendale-1948-12\|Lonnie Edward Noack Houston "flying disc" case]]**. Inspector [[entities/h-b-fletcher\|H. B. Fletcher]] dispatches Houston SAC + LA SAC to **secure photographs/film, locate the disc, send Agents to find it and guard it until properly handled**. ^[extracted] LA SAC Hood resolves within hours: object is a **tow target/kite** made by Olson and Rice Manufacturing Co., LA, abandoned after failed flight tests. Identified by manufacturer Henry T. Rice. **All work immediately discontinued.** ^[extracted] The case shows BB-57 was **operationally selectively non-applied** — a 14-month-post-BB-57 active FBI dispatch, with Fletcher explicitly instructing *"FBI representatives should assume an active interest in the investigation throughout, cooperating, of course, with O.S.I. representatives"*. ^[extracted] | pages 81, 88-98, 122 |
| **4. Partial reactivation** | 25 Mar 1949 | **[[references/fbi-sac-letter-38-1949\|SAC Letter No. 38, Series 1949]]** — Hoover-signed all-SAC directive: *"As set forth in Bureau Bulletin #57, referred to above, no investigation should be conducted by your office relative to flying discs, however, the attached memorandum should be referred to in securing data from persons who desire to voluntarily furnish information to your office relating to flying discs."* ^[extracted] Attachment: [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164\|Cabell AIRMEM #4 "Unconventional Aircraft" 15 Feb 1949]]. Rationale: a *"reliable and confidential source"* (identified in serial-160 Fletcher → Ladd memo as **[[entities/c-d-gasser\|Col C. D. Gasser]], AMC Engineering Field Officer, Oak Ridge Tennessee**) advised flying discs are man-made missiles, USSR experimentation ≥4 years, northerly approach direction. ^[extracted] | pages 117, 124 |

The arc is: **termination → USAF resistance to it → operational selective non-application → formal partial reactivation**. The cooperation regime is not "silently superseded" but **operationally nonlinear** — terminated, contested, breached, then partially reactivated within ~17 months. ^[inferred]

## HEADLINE — Project GRUDGE by-name first appearance

Section 4 contains what appears to be the **earliest by-name "Project Grudge" reference in the wiki corpus**: a **22 March 1949 San Antonio SAC → Director memo** (Bureau File 65-58300, *Protection of Vital Installations*) reads: ^[extracted]

> G-2, 4th Army, has now advised that the above matter is now termed "Unconventional Aircraft" and investigations concerning such matters have been given the name "Project Grudge".

This **predates** the prior wiki anchor (28 Mar 1950 Boggs-Hearn memo, [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]] page line 21) by **~12 months**. ^[inferred] It is also the wiki's first datapoint **synchronizing** "Unconventional Aircraft" (the AIRMEM #4 / SAC Letter No. 38 nomenclature) with "Project Grudge" (the AF program label). ^[inferred] The memo also confirms G-2 / ONI / OSI / FBI **Weekly Intelligence Conferences in the Fourth Army Area** as the cross-agency channel through which UAP information is exchanged — see [[references/fbi-san-antonio-vital-installations-1949]]. ^[extracted]

## HEADLINE — The Kirtland 30 Jan 1949 mass green-fireball event

A 1 February 1949 OSI dispatch from **CO Kirtland AFB to USAF Director of Special Investigations** (page 105) reports: ^[extracted]

> Sighting of identical object reported at 2255Z 30 Jan 49 by aprx 30 people. Estimate at least 100 total sightings. AEC, AFSWP, 4th Army, local commanders perturbed by implications of phenomena. Sighting reported from El Paso, Alburquerque, Alamogordo, Roswell, Socorro, and other locations. All appear to be same object at different points in trajectory.

See [[references/sighting-kirtland-mass-1949-01-30]]. This is the wiki's **first 30+-witness multi-state mass-event of UAP** in the post-1947 corpus, ^[inferred] and the **first explicit AEC + AFSWP (Armed Forces Special Weapons Project) cross-agency framing** of UAP as a critical-installation security concern. ^[extracted] Section 4 also captures the [[concepts/green-fireballs-new-mexico|Dec 1948 NM green-fireball wave]] continuation: the **22 Mar 1949 San Antonio SAC memo** (Bureau File 65-58300) reports a **16 Feb 1949 conference at Los Alamos** to consider the *"Green fire ball phenomena"* — citing **[[entities/lincoln-la-paz|Dr Lincoln La Paz]]** of UNM as conferee. ^[extracted] The investigation is *"now the primary responsibility of the U.S. Air Force, Air Materiel Command, T-2"*. ^[extracted]

## HEADLINE — Bureau File 65-58300 cross-class linkage

Section 4 documents that **UAP material is being filed cross-class**: the [[references/fbi-san-antonio-vital-installations-1949|San Antonio SAC's UAP-content memo (31 Jan 1949 + 22 Mar 1949)]] is filed under **Bureau File 65-58300, *"Protection of Vital Installations"***, **NOT** 62-HQ-83894. ^[extracted] This is the wiki's **first evidence of cross-class FBI filing of UAP material**. ^[inferred] The corpus's other cross-class case is [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449|serial 449]] (1966) which dual-filed UAP material under HQ 62 + LA 100. **Section 4 establishes the cross-class pattern earlier (Jan 1949)**. ^[inferred] 65-58300 is **not** a UAP file per se — it is a Vital-Installations counterintelligence file in which UAP material appears because of Los Alamos / Sandia / Camp Hood / White Sands threat-vector framing. ^[inferred]

## What section 4 confirms

1. **BB-57 was a real, dated, formally-numbered bulletin** — not a folkloric construct. ^[inferred] The bulletin number, date, and operational paraphrase appear identically across **four** independent documents spanning 12 Feb 1948 to 25 Mar 1949. ^[inferred]
2. **The Stratemeyer ADC re-enlistment attempt is a discrete documented event** — not just a rumored bureaucratic conflict. ^[extracted] The 4 Feb 1948 letter, the 12 Feb 1948 SAC SF flag, the 20 Feb 1948 Hoover reaffirmation, and the 1 Mar 1948 Reynolds-Martin reconciliation are all documented in section 4. ^[extracted]
3. **The FBI did selectively investigate at least one case post-BB-57** — the Dec 1948 Noack Houston case had Inspector Fletcher actively dispatching agents (with explicit orders to *"guard the disc"*) before LA SAC Hood resolved it as a tow kite. ^[extracted] This complicates the wiki's prior framing of BB-57 as a hard cutoff.
4. **SAC Letter No. 38 is the operational hinge** between the 1947-Dec1949 GRUDGE-period FBI passive-monitoring posture and the post-1949 reactive-tip-routing pattern visible in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|section 5]]'s 1949-1950 caseload. ^[inferred]
5. **The Project GRUDGE name appears institutionally by 22 March 1949** — three days before SAC Letter No. 38 — confirming that "Project Grudge" was the SIGN-successor program identifier already in use at G-2 4th Army by late March 1949. ^[extracted]
6. **AEC + AFSWP + 4th Army had institutional UAP concern by 30 Jan 1949** — pre-dating the 16 Feb 1949 Los Alamos green-fireball conference. ^[extracted] The Kirtland mass-event is the **first primary-source AEC framing** of UAP in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred]
7. **Walter Winchell was an active informal HQ-tip channel** — three independent Winchell relays appear in section 4 (Atkins/De Jarnett 10 Apr 1949 page 140; Mrs Gardner late Apr 1949 page 150; Robert Ripley/Japanese-saucer 26 May 1949 page 159). See [[entities/walter-winchell]]. ^[inferred]
