# COMETA Report — UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For? (1999)

A **private French report** issued in July 1999 by **[[entities/cometa-committee|COMETA]]** (*Comité d'Etudes Approfondies* — Committee for In-Depth Studies), chaired by retired Air Force General **[[entities/denis-letty|Denis Letty]]** and incubated under the **Institut des Hautes Etudes de Défense Nationale (IHEDN)** Auditors Association. The report was published as a special issue of the magazine **VSD** in July 1999 (G.S. Presse Communication, Levallois-Perret, ISSN 1278-916 X, copyright deposit July 1999) and titled *Les OVNI et la défense : à quoi doit-on se préparer ?* — *UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For?* The report is **not a government document**. The cover letter that accompanied this archival copy explicitly notes: *"I MISSPOKE - IT IS A PRIVATE - NOT GOVERNMENT - REPORT."*

The report is structured in three parts:

1. **Facts and Testimonies** (Chapters 1–5) — pilot encounters, radar/visual cases, ground sightings, close encounters, and counterexamples of explained phenomena.
2. **The Extent of Our Knowledge** (Chapters 6–9) — the [[entities/gepan-sepra|GEPAN/SEPRA]] research program in France, hypotheses and partial models (with a [[concepts/mhd-propulsion-uap|magnetohydrodynamic propulsion]] thought-experiment), and an organizational survey of UFO research in the US, UK, and Russia.
3. **UFOs and Defense** (Chapters 10–14) — strategic, aeronautical, scientific, political/religious, and media implications, plus a four-phase visitor-strategy thought experiment ([[concepts/four-phase-contact-strategy]]).

A 7-appendix section follows: radar detection in France; astronomers' sightings; life in the universe; colonization of space; Roswell and disinformation; a long-history chronology; and reflections on US politics.

## Provenance and routing

The archival copy ingested into the wiki is bundled with a **two-page cover letter dated 30 April 2001** from **[[entities/carol-rosin|Carol Rosin]]** (founder of the Institute for Security and Cooperation in Outer Space; former spokesperson for **Wernher von Braun** in his last years ^[inferred — Rosin asserts]) and her husband **Jon Cypher** (actor) addressed to **"Dan"** at NASA — almost certainly **Daniel Goldin**, NASA Administrator from April 1992 until November 2001. ^[inferred] The handwritten note on a Renaissance Hotel notepad references *"P 62 — John Callahan's testimony"* and *"P. 105 — Senior Air Traffic Controller at MEX City — Enrique Kolbeck"* — page references that do **not** match the COMETA Report itself, suggesting the in-person delivery on Friday following the letter included **at least one additional document**, probably **Marie Galbraith's *Briefing Document — The Best Available Evidence*** (1995), which COMETA's Chapter 9.1 cites and which has the matching pagination. ^[ambiguous — bundle composition].

The fact that the COMETA Report ended up in **NARA RG 255 (Records of NASA)** is a routing provenance worth noting: a private French report, hand-delivered by a private US advocate to NASA, archived in NASA records. The chain is **non-governmental → non-governmental → civilian US space agency**, distinct from every other policy-track artifact in the corpus.

## Part 1 — Facts and Testimonies (selected cases)

### French pilot testimonies

- **Mirage IV pilot Hervé Giraud (7 March 1977, Dijon area)** — twice-repeated tail-chase by an "assailant" glow at 9,600 m / Mach 0.9; no radar contact at Contrexéville; no sonic boom; controller suspected oxygen hallucination. Two separate maneuvers, each disengaged by hard banks (3–4 g, then 6.5 g).
- **Colonel Claude Bosc (3 March 1976, T-33, Tours)** — head-on green-trailed luminous object (1–2 m), grazed right wing at near-collision; <5-second event; two trailing pilots saw it from a distance.
- **Air France AF 3532 (28 January 1994, Coulommiers)** — Captain **Jean-Charles Duboc** + Copilot Valérie Chauffour + chief steward see a brown-bell-then-lens-shaped craft at ~10,500 m, ~50 km off; Cinq-Mars-la-Pile **CDC radar track for 50 seconds** correlating to the visual; CODA confirms no flight plan, rules out weather balloon. The wiki's first **commercial-airliner radar/visual UAP correlation in the corpus** ^[inferred].

### Five major foreign aeronautical cases

- **Lakenheath / Bentwaters (UK, 13–14 Aug 1956)** — multi-vantage radar/visual; RAF Venom intercept tail-chased; Condon-Commission "unidentified."
- **RB-47 (US, 17 Jul 1957)** — multi-frequency radar + multi-officer visual + ground-radar (Utah / Dallas) cross-correlation over Mississippi → Texas → Oklahoma; CIRVIS report filed.
- **Tehran (18–19 Sep 1976)** — Imperial Iranian Air Force Phantom F-4 intercepts, instruments and weapons-fire-control disabled at 45 km closure; DIA assessment graded a "classic case."
- **Pereslavl-Zalesski (USSR, 21 Mar 1990)** — Aviation General **Igor Maltsev**'s public *Rabochaya Tribuna* article on >100 visual sightings + radar.
- **San Carlos de Bariloche (Argentina, 31 Jul 1995)** — Aerolineas AR 674 Boeing 727 + ground witnesses + control-tower correlation; **town-wide power outage co-temporal with object proximity**.

### Ground sightings and close encounters

- **Antananarivo (Madagascar, 16 Aug 1954)** — multi-hundred-witness "metal rugby ball" + detached green lens; shop lights extinguished as it overflew.
- **Trans-en-Provence (France, 8 Jan 1981)** — landed object left a circular ground depression; INRA chlorophyll-and-amino-acid analysis (Prof. Michel Bounias) consistent with a powerful pulsed microwave field.
- **"Amaranth" / Nancy (France, 21 Oct 1982)** — 20-minute hover; amaranth bush leaf-tip drying consistent with >200 kV/m electric field.
- **Valensole (France, 1 Jul 1965)** — Maurice Masse paralyzed; "Dauphine"-shaped craft on six legs; long-lasting plant effects.
- **Cussac (France, 1967)** — children + cow-herd witnesses; sphere with four small black beings; sulfurous odor; gendarme corroboration.
- **Kapustin Yar missile base (USSR, 28–29 Jul 1989)** — KGB-declassified file; >7 military depositions of saucer-class objects.

The committee includes two **counterexamples** (a polystyrene show-prop float on a highway; a corona-arcing 10kV power line in Dombes) explicitly to demonstrate explained-cases methodology.

## Part 2 — Hypotheses and partial models

- **GEPAN/SEPRA classification** of cases A–D, where **Category D (UAP D)** = "phenomenon that cannot be identified despite the abundance and quality of the data" — 4–5% of all sightings.
- **Statistical claim**: ≥500 well-documented aeronautical UAP D sightings between 1942 and 1995 worldwide; ~21% are radar/visual.
- **Partial physical models**: [[concepts/mhd-propulsion-uap|magnetohydrodynamic propulsion]] for atmospheric flight; particle-beam / antimatter / Dyson-stellar-slingshot for interstellar; high-power microwave emission for engine-shutoff and locomotive-paralysis effects.
- **Citation of Twining (Sep 1947)**: General Twining's "flying disks" memo concluding "the phenomenon reported is something real, and not visionary or fictitious… [the maneuvers] lead one to assume that they are piloted or operated by remote control" — embedded as the touchstone for the report's institutional-conclusion arc.

## Part 3 — Defense recommendations

Chapter 10's strategic-planning framework introduces a **four-class state-knowledge taxonomy** ([[concepts/uap-state-knowledge-classes]]) — orthogonal to [[concepts/three-class-alien-taxonomy|Hunter's three-class alien taxonomy]] — and Chapter 13's **four-phase visitor-strategy thought experiment** ([[concepts/four-phase-contact-strategy]]).

The committee's six **Conclusions and Recommendations** to the French state:

1. Inform French state agencies (military and civilian schools, defense research bodies, defense communications) about the phenomenon and disinformation processes.
2. Boost SEPRA's investigation, analysis, data, and foreign-relations resources.
3. Make UFO detection an objective for civilian and military space surveillance systems.
4. Create a **unit at the highest State level** to collaborate with SEPRA — formulating prospective hypotheses, promoting research, and coordinating sectorial international cooperation.
5. Initiate **diplomatic démarches to the United States**, with EU support, "to urge the superpower to collaborate" and exert "useful pressure to clarify this crucial issue."
6. Reflect, at the level of public authorities, on measures to take in the event of a spectacular and indisputable manifestation.

The committee also frames the **MacArthur 1962 West Point address** ("ultimate conflict between a united human race and the sinister force of some other planetary galaxy") and **Reagan's 1987 UN General Assembly speech** ("how quickly our differences worldwide would vanish if we were facing an alien threat from outside this world") as evidence of senior-leader awareness of the framing.

## Appendix 5 — The Roswell Affair and disinformation

A dedicated 8-page treatment, separating "reducing disinformation" ([[concepts/robertson-panel-runup-1952|Robertson Panel]] 1952; Klass-style trivialization of Lakenheath, RB-47, Tehran) from "amplifying disinformation" (Bennewicz / Lear / Cooper trajectory; the questionable "alien autopsy" video). The committee endorses the *extraterrestrial-vessel* hypothesis as plausible, citing General du Bose's 1991 affidavit and Lt Col Marcel's 1978 statements. ^[extracted]

## Part II — Appendix-bundled content (COMETA pages 47–93)

The back half of the report (Chapters 9–14 + the seven appendices + glossary + bibliography + bundled US-press receptions) covers material structurally distinct from the case-investigation body. This section anchors it for the wiki.

### Chapter 9 — Foreign research organizations

COMETA Chapter 9 surveys non-French UAP-research organizations as a comparative backdrop:

- **§9.1 United States** — The 1953 Robertson Panel's *"strip the UFO phenomenon of its aura of mystery"* mandate is identified as the institutional pivot for US post-1953 secrecy. COMETA describes the 1997 [[references/pocantico-colloquium-1997|Pocantico Colloquium]] funded by Laurance Rockefeller at the Pocantico estate near West Point (Rockefeller Brothers Fund property), moderated by Stanford astrophysicist Peter Sturrock, focused on physical evidence. *"French participation was quite noteworthy; it consisted of the head of SEPRA and two members of the scientific council. A summary document expressed the desire that many countries have a UFO research organization comparable to that of France."*
- **§9.1 — Colonel Corso's theory**. COMETA gives a balanced inline review of **Colonel Philip J. Corso's** 1997 *The Day After Roswell* (Pocket Books) — Corso served as the military member of the NSC staff (1953–57, under Eisenhower); Strom Thurmond wrote the original foreword, then requested its removal in reprints (request granted), claiming he had not been told the book was about UFOs. COMETA's posture: *"the entire contents of the book cannot be easily dismissed when one considers the remarkable career of its author and Senator Thurmond's tribute to him."* The committee notes that the US Air Force's 1997 second Roswell report (*The Roswell Report, Case Closed*) — the second since 1994 — was published in apparent response to Corso. ^[extracted]
- **§9.2 United Kingdom** — UK MOD's UFO study unit, **Sec(AS)2a** (Department 2a of the Secretariat (Air Staff) division), created 1964. **Nick Pope** was head 1991–1994; published *Open Skies, Closed Minds* (Pocket Books, 1997) in a *"very lively style"*. **Ralph Noyes** (Pope predecessor 1969–72, ended career as MOD Undersecretary of State for Defence 1977) and **Lord Hill-Norton** (Admiral of the Fleet, Chief of Defence Staff 1971–73) are both cited as suspecting that a *secret* MOD UFO-research department exists beyond Sec(AS)2a's "alone" desk. Lord Hill-Norton was among *"some thirty lords"* active in a House of Lords group studying UFOs in the 1980s. ^[extracted]
- **§9.3 Russia / USSR** — Academy of Sciences UFO studies since at least 1979 under **Vladimir Migouline** and his assistant **Platov** (1992 book *UFOs and Modern Science*). In **1992**, Migouline and Platov **proposed a scientific and technical cooperation program to SEPRA, but CNES management did not follow up**. ^[extracted] The Siberian section of the Academy of Sciences "does not rule out the extraterrestrial hypotheses, and even favor it." During Glasnost, the **KGB declassified 124 pages** of UFO-sighting documents in 1991 (Cases of Sightings of Abnormal Events over USSR Territory, 1982–1990, 17 regions); in 1994, **Colonel Boris Sokolov** sold ABC News a collection of military investigations from 1978–1988.

### Chapter 10 — Strategic Planning (the report's policy core)

COMETA's policy proper is in Chapter 10:

- **§10.1**: characterization of *"these extraterrestrials"* as *"highly endowed intellectually and… technologically advanced over us."* Apparent contradiction noted between visitor interest in us and visitor furtiveness: *"Rather than observe us, it seems that they want to show themselves to us and to gradually acclimate us to the idea of their existence."* ^[extracted]
- **§10.2**: what intentions / strategies can be deduced? Survey of behavioral evidence — overflights of nuclear missile bases, intimidation maneuvers (Luxeuil, Tehran), witness paralysis + engine shutoff + lights going out (San Carlos de Bariloche). COMETA speculates: *"Wouldn't it be logical to think that these extraterrestrial civilizations have established stations, even colonies, in the asteroid belt and why not relay stations on the moon? Our forays and projects studied in the United States for modifying the orbits of asteroids using H bombs in order to bring them closer to the earth's orbit for mining purposes could be disturbing them."* ^[extracted]
- **§10.3**: the [[concepts/uap-state-knowledge-classes|four-class state-knowledge taxonomy]] (a/b/c/d). Already in wiki.
- **§10.4**: have contacts been made? COMETA gives a one-sentence judgment on US policy: *"The simplest response would be that the United States wants to maintain at any cost military technological superiority over rival countries and, perhaps, a preferential contact."* Cites General Goldwater's claim of UFO files classified above Top Secret. Identifies **AFR 200-2** (USAF Air Force Regulation 200-2) and **JANAP 146** (Joint Army Navy Air Force Publication 146) as the regulatory apparatus underwriting US non-disclosure since 1953. JANAP 146 applies to military personnel, commercial airline pilots, and merchant marine captains; unauthorized disclosure punishable by 10 years' prison and $10,000 fine. ^[extracted]
- **§10.5–10.6**: structural recommendations (already summarized in the Recommendations section above).

### Chapters 11–14 — Implications by domain

- **Ch. 11 (Aeronautical)** lays out the five aeronautical-case implication classes (simple sighting / radar track / EM interference / shadowing / aggressive conduct) and details the personnel who should be sensitized: flight crews, ATC controllers, meteorologists, CNES engineers, aeronautics engineers. Civilian-controller training already happens at ENAC; the committee recommends extending it to all flight-crew training schools.
- **Ch. 12 (Scientific and Technical)** recommends three actions: (12.1) step up data collection by GEPAN/SEPRA; (12.2) techno-watch on MHD propulsion, particle beams, microwaves; (12.3) global-context research on extrasolar planets, exobiology, evolution of civilizations.
- **Ch. 13 (Political and Religious)** is the [[concepts/four-phase-contact-strategy|four-phase contact strategy]] thought experiment (already in wiki).
- **Ch. 14 (Media)** identifies four government fears of media curiosity (panic, mistrust, ridicule, manipulation), inventories tabloid / major-newspaper / television-and-movie attitudes, and recommends a *strengthened SEPRA* dedicate effort to *"training of journalists"* and a *"documentary site on the Internet."* ^[extracted]

### Appendices 1–7 — supporting material

- **App. 1 (Radar Detection in France)** — primary vs. secondary radar distinction; **STRIDA** (Système de Traitement des Informations de Défense Aérienne) network coverage of a 4,500-km square. Only military primary radars can detect non-transponder UAPs.
- **App. 2 (Astronomers' Sightings)** by **Jean-Claude Ribes** — [[entities/j-allen-hynek|Hynek]]'s 1950s survey of ~40 astronomers (~10% had sightings, including Lincoln La Paz [Director, Institute of Meteoritics, University of New Mexico] and Clyde Tombaugh [discoverer of Pluto, d. 1997]). Peter Sturrock's 1970s survey of 2,611 American Astronomical Association members (~50% response rate, 60 sightings). Marseilles astronomers **Georges Courtès** and **Maurice Viton** cited for a French sighting. ^[extracted]
- **App. 3 (Life in the Universe)** — exobiology / SETI status as of 1999; the canceled-then-private-funded NASA SETI program; French radiotelescope at **Nançay** scheduled to participate.
- **App. 4 (Colonization of Space)** — the **moon-base / Biosphere 2 / O'Neill-cylinder thought experiment** that grounds Chapter 13's four-phase model. Russian closed-ecosystem experiments dating to 1961; **Biosphere 2** (1.3 ha greenhouse, Arizona, four men + four women 1991–1993 then a six-month follow-on, University of Columbia post-1994 takeover) treated as the prototype future-moon-base ecosystem. American physicist **O'Neill's cylindrical space-city design** (30 km × 6 km, rotating for artificial gravity, supporting millions). **MHD propulsion** offered as the *"interesting prospect"* for stealthy atmospheric movement in another star system's planet. ^[extracted] This is COMETA's most explicit grounding of its Chapter 13 thought experiment in late-20th-century space-systems science — the "ship-worlds" and "bionic robot" framing in §13 is the human-mirror of human plans documented here.
- **App. 5 (Roswell and disinformation)** — already in wiki coverage above.
- **App. 6 (Long History — Chronology)** — pre-1947 UFO chronology:
  - **1880–1900** — "airships" wave (US 1896–97; UK turn of century; Spain, Germany, Sweden, Russia spurs).
  - **1946** — Sweden "ghost rockets" (~1,000 detected Feb–Dec 1946); never officially attributed.
  - **WWII** — [[concepts/foo-fighter-phenomenon|foo fighters]] ("Kraut fireballs"); reported as Allied secret weapon by German pilots (Berlin board of inquiry reportedly created); reported as German secret weapon by Allied authorities. ^[extracted] Files held under military secrecy until at least 1949.
  - Earlier centuries — Goethe's 1768 trip Frankfort-to-Leipzig sighting; Nuremberg April 1561 and Basel August 1566 "sky battles"; Detchani Yugoslavian monastery (1327–1335) frescoes of angels in vessels; Agobard Bishop of Lyons under Charlemagne saving three men and one woman from the stake who claimed to have descended from an airship; Anaxagoras's celestial-lights testimony; Daimachos's globe-of-fire during the 78th Olympiad.
- **App. 7 (Reflections on Various Aspects)** — the report's most candid section. *"A large number of Americans are convinced of the physical reality of UFOs, of their extraterrestrial origin, and of the fact that the U.S. government is systematically covering up the truth with lies and disinformation."* §7.2.1 introduces the term *"anthropocentric humanism"* for the scientist-side resistance pattern; §7.2.2 documents the political-side. Quotes:
  - **General MacArthur, West Point address 1962**: *"You now face a new world… of ultimate conflict between a united human race and the sinister force of some other planetary galaxy."*
  - **President Reagan, 42nd UN General Assembly address, 21 Sep 1987**: *"how quickly our differences worldwide would vanish if we were facing an alien threat from outside this world."*
  - **Mikhail Gorbachev** (1985 summit) — Reagan had told him in private that if Earth had to confront an extraterrestrial invasion the US and USSR would join forces. Reported by Gorbachev later. ^[extracted]
  - **President Jimmy Carter** and **Senator Barry M. Goldwater** named as rare political exceptions who took the question seriously.
