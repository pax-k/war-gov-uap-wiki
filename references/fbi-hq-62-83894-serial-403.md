# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Serial 403 (Gray Barker book-jacket clipping)

A 3-page Mistral-OCR'd declassified artifact from the **FBI Central Records Center / FBI HQ**. Filed at **Class 0062 / Case 83894 / Sub —/ Vol — / Serial 403 (ONLY)** with secondary marker **62-HQ-83894-E483** and DIPA reference **#1142292**. The cover slip is stamped *DO NOT DESTROY* and notes that **declassification authority is derived from the FBI Automatic Declassification Guide, issued 24 May 2007**. ^[extracted] OCR-visible date stamp on the routing slip parses as **9/18/22 6771 RRP00A/1WL** — likely a Records Center processing tag from 2022 (or an OCR error). ^[ambiguous]

This is the **first artifact of a planned 17-file ingest series** drawn from FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894. ^[inferred] See [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file]] for the parent-file hub.

## What's in the artifact

The 3 OCR'd pages decompose into two distinct documentary layers:

1. **Page 1 — FBI Records Center routing/cover slip.** Carries the Class–Case–Sub–Vol–Serial filing block (`0062 83894 — — 403 ONLY`), the secondary marker `62-HQ-83894-E483`, the DIPA control number `1142292`, the *DO NOT DESTROY* stamp, and the FBI 2007 Automatic Declassification Guide note. ^[extracted]
2. **Pages 2–3 — clipped publisher's book-jacket copy** for the **first edition of Gray Barker's "They Knew Too Much About Flying Saucers"**, published **1956** by **University Books, Inc., 404 Fourth Avenue, New York 16, NY**, jacket designed by **Gropper Associates, Inc.**, list price **$3.50**. The clipping includes: ^[extracted]
   - Front jacket flap promoting Barker's thesis that *"the leading figures among flying saucer researchers, who have challenged the government denial that saucers come from outer space, have been silenced"* by *"three men in dark suits"* of unknown affiliation;
   - Endorsement attribution to **H. G. Rhawn**, publisher and owner of the *Clarksburg [WV] Daily News* (Barker's home town paper), under a "carefully disclaiming any credence in flying saucers" framing;
   - Back jacket flap with the **Buffington Studios** photographic credit for Barker's portrait + a biographical sketch describing him as a six-footer in his thirties, formerly head of an English department in a Maryland school system, an audio-visual education consultant, theatre manager, and motion picture projection technician, currently operating a film booking and buying agency in the Clarksburg News Building and publishing **The Saucerian** flying-saucer periodical. ^[extracted]

The artifact contains **no FBI-authored investigative content** — no FD-302, no AIRTEL, no SAC routing memo, no narrative interview, no analytical memo. ^[extracted] Page 1 is the Records Center filing instrument; pages 2–3 are publisher-authored promotional copy that has been clipped, kept, and serialized as the actual content of the serial. ^[inferred]

## What 62-HQ-83894 appears to be

**The HQ Class 62 prefix is the FBI's catch-all "administrative miscellaneous" classification** — used historically for material that doesn't fit a substantive case-type prefix (counterintelligence 65, security 100, civil rights 44, etc.). ^[inferred] The presence of a **clipped publisher's book-jacket as the sole content of a serial** in an HQ-level Class 62 file is more consistent with a **content-monitoring / topic-clearinghouse function** than with a substantive investigation: ^[inferred]

- An **investigative file on Gray Barker as a person** (e.g. an HQ Class 100 or Class 105 internal-security file) would contain agent reports, name-trace results, address checks, and interview narratives — none of which are present here.
- A **substantive UFO-investigation file** would carry incident-summary forms, witness statements, or correspondence with USAF / Project BLUE BOOK liaison. None present here.
- A **content-monitoring file** — i.e. one in which the FBI clips publicly-available materials (book jackets, magazine articles, newspaper clippings) about a topic the Bureau is tracking but not actively investigating — fits the present serial's morphology directly. ^[inferred]

This is a **categorical inference from one serial**, however. ^[ambiguous] The 16 remaining serials in the planned ingest (220, 438, 449, 164, plus sections 1–10 + sub_a, ranging in size from 5 KB to 408 KB) will resolve whether 62-HQ-83894 is a clearinghouse on UAP-research-as-public-discourse, a topic file on civilian saucer researchers as a class, a surveillance file on Barker specifically, or something else. The serial-number gap (this is **403**, with much-lower-numbered serials like 164 and 220 still queued) implies the file is large and long-running — likely with serials originating across many years. ^[inferred]

## What the jacket copy itself asserts

For provenance: the substantive "content" of the serial is jacket-copy authored by **University Books, Inc.** to sell Barker's book. It is not an FBI claim; it is an unsigned-marketing-copy claim that the FBI has clipped and filed. ^[inferred] The jacket's assertions, attributable to Barker as the book's author or to University Books as its promoter:

- Saucer researchers who *"challenged the government denial that saucers come from outer space"* have been *"silenced … one by one"* (jacket-copy claim, attributable to Barker via the book) ^[ambiguous]
- The silencing was performed by *"three men in dark suits"* of unknown affiliation — *"government agents … men from outer space, or … men muscled into a situation"* (jacket-copy claim; this is the **earliest documented appearance** of the *"men in dark suits"* / **MIB** trope to enter the FBI's HQ paper in this corpus, but it is being entered via a publisher's jacket flap, not via an FBI-authored claim). ^[inferred]
- Barker's interest dates to **1952**, when *"one of the most astonishing [saucers] allegedly landed near his home in West Virginia"* — this is the **Flatwoods, WV "Braxton County Monster" event of 12 Sep 1952**. ^[inferred] Not in the wiki; deferred to a future ingest.
- Barker has *"close contact with the leading men in the field"* and finds them *"suddenly silenced, one by one"* (jacket-copy claim). ^[ambiguous]

The wiki should not promote any of these jacket-copy claims to extracted-status outside this artifact page. ^[inferred] The MIB trope, in particular, is **publisher-promoted folklore the FBI clipped, not an FBI conclusion**. Whether the FBI ever engages substantively with the MIB folklore is an open question to be resolved by later serials in the 62-HQ-83894 ingest. ^[inferred]
