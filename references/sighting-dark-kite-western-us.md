# Sighting: Dark Kite (Western US, Pre-dawn)

Event 3 of the [[references/western-us-event-2026|Western U.S. Event]] cluster.

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Western U.S., **restricted zone** with a road |
| Time of day | Pre-dawn |
| Witnesses | USPER5, USPER6 (federal LE special agents) |
| Lights | Two: one red, one white |
| Light height (initial) | ~2–3 ft above ground |
| Lateral speed | 15–20 mph |
| Standoff after stop | ~100 m off the side of the road |
| AARO-described shape | Triangular |

## Description — vehicle phase

USPER5 and USPER6 initially thought they were watching a **car** traveling along a road inside a **restricted zone**. The "car" had two lights (one red, one white) about 2 to 3 feet off the ground.

## Description — anomalous phase

As the agents pursued and closed to within a few hundred feet, the object:

1. Suddenly **moved off the road over the desert** without changing its orientation with respect to the observers — i.e., it appeared to **move laterally** at an estimated 15–20 mph.
2. Did not change height during the lateral move.
3. Moved with "**zero resistance**" — same descriptor used for the [[references/sighting-fiery-orb-western-us|Fiery Orb]] hover.
4. Stopped about 100 m off the side of the road and **turned off its lights**.

After the lights extinguished, the agents' descriptions diverged on shape:

- **USPER6**: "thin line" from the back.
- **USPER5**: "ill defined, dark kite shape that had some rounded width to the sides."

Slide footnote: *In later discussions with AARO, the object is described as **triangular**.* ^[ambiguous] — three coexisting witness/AARO descriptors (thin line, dark kite with rounded sides, triangular) for the same object.

## Description — NVG observation

USPER6 used **night vision goggles (NVGs)** to view the object once it had stopped. They:

- Saw the object "**for a split second in the NVGs before the light turned off**."
- After lights off, observed a "**very thin line**" remaining.
- Estimated the object at **~4 ft wide**, positioned **horizontally to the ground**.
- Then watched it move at 15–20 mph and "**move up in height…, but [remain] a flat line.**"
- Lost sight after a few seconds.

(Image 1 in the slide is described as a recreation of USPER6's NVG drawing.)

## Why it matters

- **Vehicle-mimicking lighting at car altitude** with non-vehicular kinematics (lateral departure off a road over desert without re-orienting, hovering, climbing) is a notable signature.
- "**Zero resistance**" hovering recurs across [[references/sighting-fiery-orb-western-us|Event 2]] and this event — a possibly-load-bearing phrase that warrants comparison with later UAP reporting vocabulary. ^[inferred]
- The thin-line / kite / triangle ambiguity speaks to the difficulty of inferring 3D shape from low-light, partial-aspect observations of a possibly thin-aspect-ratio object. ^[inferred]
- See [[references/sighting-transparent-kite-western-us]] for the **near-immediate (~30 min) follow-on sighting** that appears to involve the same or an identical-class object.
