# SAC Letter No. 83, Series 1952

A **field-office bulletin** issued by FBI HQ on **29 August 1952** to all Special Agents in Charge (SACs), reaffirming the **post-cooperation-regime saucer-intake protocol** established by [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 57]] (1 Oct 1947) and [[references/fbi-sac-letter-38-1949|SAC Letter No. 38]] (25 Mar 1949). ^[extracted] Captured verbatim in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 7]] page 42 (serial 313). ^[extracted]

## Verbatim text

The OCR-recovered text reads: ^[extracted]

> **SAC Letter #83**
> 8-29-52
>
> (C) FLYING DISCS — Reference is made to Bureau Bulletin #57, Paragraph D, dated October 1, 1947, and SAC Letter #38, dated March 25, 1949, in the captioned matter.
>
> It is noted that some Bureau field offices are not furnishing to OSI locally complaints regarding flying discs, pursuant to existing Bureau instructions.
>
> Upon receipt of information by your office relating to the sighting of a flying disc, you should endeavor to determine from the complainant details of the type referred to in the Air Force memorandum, which was furnished to your office as an enclosure to SAC Letter #38. The information should then be promptly furnished to OSI locally by your office. As you are aware, the investigation of so-called flying discs is the responsibility of the Department of the Air Force.
>
> ALL INFORMATION CONTAINED HEREIN IS UNCLASSIFIED
> DATE 6/20/83 BY Sp-8BH/Dg

The original is filed at **`66-04-1649`** ^[extracted] (FBI's Class-66 administrative-correspondence file holding all SAC Letters as a series — distinct from the topic-anchored 62-83894 where the cross-reference is filed). ^[inferred]

## What triggered the issuance

The 22 Aug 1952 **Branigan → Belmont memo** (serial 313 page 38) records the precipitating analysis: ^[extracted]

> **PURPOSE:** To recommend existing instructions to the field re flying discs be called to the attention of the field.
>
> **BACKGROUND:** A review of communications received from the field indicates they are not observing existing instructions contained in Bureau Bulletin #57, paragraph D, dated 10/1/47, and SAC Letter #38, dated 3/25/49, to refer details of complaints regarding flying saucers to OSI locally.
>
> **ACTION:** It is recommended the attached SAC Letter be issued.

I.e. **field-office non-compliance with the 5-year-old BB-57 + 3-year-old SAC Letter #38** is the proximate trigger. ^[extracted] The 1952 saucer wave's **inbound-volume scale-up** had exposed the gap between the 1947-49 protocol and field-office practice in 1952. ^[inferred]

## Companion bulletin: Branigan → Belmont, 18 Aug 1952

11 days earlier, Branigan had issued a **structurally parallel internal-supervisory memo** ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|section 7]] serial 314 page 40) on **after-hours OSI hand-off**: ^[extracted]

> Captain William Deegan, OSI, 4th Air Force Base, Bowling Field, has requested that any information concerning the sighting of flying discs (saucers, etc.) be telephonically furnished immediately to his office, day or night, by dialling Code 1261, and asking for Extension 509. Captain Deegan advised the Air Force is greatly concerned about the captioned matter, and would appreciate the Bureau's cooperation in immediately advising of details received concerning such complaints.
>
> **ACTION:** It is recommended a copy of this memorandum be filed in the night and week-end Supervisor's book in your office for their information.

I.e. **internal-FBI supervisory routing** is updated in parallel with the SAC-letter formal field-office bulletin. ^[inferred] The 1952 wave is treated by FBI as a **24/7 referral-volume event**, not just a daytime-mail-room event. ^[inferred]

## Verification — first explicit field-office citation in the corpus

[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|Section 7]] serial 318 (3 Oct 1952 SAC Newark → Director memo on Riley/Stock Passaic NJ) provides the **first explicit field-office citation of SAC Letter #83 in operational reporting** ^[extracted]:

> In accordance with SAC letter #83, Series 1952 dated 8/29/52, the results of the credit and criminal checks and the neighborhood investigations were forwarded to the local OSI Office, New York City and no further investigations have been made by this office.

I.e. by ~5 weeks after issuance, the bulletin is being explicitly cited in field-office case-disposition language. ^[inferred]

## Significance

The SAC Letter #83 issuance is the **third reaffirmation** of the post-cooperation-regime intake protocol within 5 years ^[inferred]:

| Year | Instrument | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **1947 (1 Oct)** | [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947\|Bureau Bulletin No. 57]] | Terminate active investigation; retain passive intake under paragraph D |
| **1949 (25 Mar)** | [[references/fbi-sac-letter-38-1949\|SAC Letter No. 38]] | Formalize EEI questionnaire intake under [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164\|Cabell AIRMEM #4]] schema |
| **1952 (29 Aug)** | **SAC Letter No. 83 (this page)** | **Reaffirm both predecessors under 1952-wave volume** |

The triple-instrument structure confirms the post-cooperation-regime intake protocol is a **stable institutional framework** for the 1947-54 period — and SAC Letter #83 demonstrates that **periodic reaffirmation is required to maintain field-office compliance**. ^[inferred] The bulletin's existence also confirms the FBI did **not** rescind BB-57 in 1949 (when SAC Letter #38 was issued) — both instruments coexist as cumulative-not-superseding ones. ^[inferred]

The bulletin is a **direct datapoint for the wiki's [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894 channel (j)]] policy-bulletin self-referential intake**: the bulletin governs the FBI's saucer intake, but also gets filed within the topic file (62-HQ-83894) it governs. ^[inferred] **N=2 confirmation** of channel (j) (BB-57 + SAC Letter #38 + SAC Letter #83 all cited within the file). ^[inferred]
