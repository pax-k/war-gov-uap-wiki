# Sighting — Roswell NM (8 July 1947)

The 8 July 1947 recovery of an alleged "flying disc" near Roswell NM by the **8th Air Force** — captured in the FBI corpus as a **single URGENT teletype** filed at FBI HQ as serial 18 of file [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894]]. ^[extracted] This wiki page covers what the FBI's contemporaneous evidentiary trail says about the event — **not** the broader Roswell mythology that crystallized decades later.

## What the FBI teletype says (verbatim provenance)

The teletype is **FBI Dallas → Director and SAC Cincinnati URGENT**, dated 8-7-47 (the OCR on the section 1 source shows date stamp `JUL 29 1947` indicating the receipt date in DC). ^[extracted] Its full text: ^[extracted]

> FLYING DISC, INFORMATION CONCERNING. MAJOR CURTAN, HEADQUARTERS EIGHTH AIR FORCE, TELEPHONICALLY ADVISED THIS OFFICE THAT AN OBJECT PURPORTING TO BE A FLYING DISC WAS RECOVERED NEAR ROSWELL, NEW MEXICO, THIS DATE. THE DISC IS HEXAGONAL IN SHAPE AND WAS SUSPENDED FROM A BALLOON BY CABLE, WHICH BALLOON WAS APPROXIMATELY TWENTY FEET IN DIAMETER. MAJOR CURTAN FURTHER ADVISED THAT THE OBJECT FOUND RESEMBLES A HIGH ALTITUDE WEATHER BALLOON WITH A RADAR REFLECTOR, BUT THAT TELEPHONIC CONVERSATION BETWEEN THEIR OFFICE AND WRIGHT FIELD HAD NOT BORNE OUT THIS BELIEF. DISC AND BALLOON BEING TRANSPORTED TO WRIGHT FIELD BY SPECIAL PLANE FOR EXAMINATION INFORMATION PROVIDED THIS OFFICE BECAUSE OF NATIONAL INTEREST IN CASE AND FACT THAT NATIONAL BROADCASTING COMPANY, ASSOCIATED PRESS, AND OTHERS ATTEMPTING TO BREAK STORY OF LOCATION OF DISC TODAY. MAJOR CURTAN ADVISED WOULD REQUEST WRIGHT FIELD TO ADVISE CINCINNATI OFFICE RESULTS OF EXAMINATION. NO FURTHER INVESTIGATION BEING CONDUCTED.
> — WYLY (FBI Dallas)

Source: section 1 OCR p. 69, recorded `162-83894-18`. ^[extracted]

## What the teletype establishes

- **Recovery date**: 8 July 1947 (the same day as the Roswell Army Air Field's famous "RAAF Captures Flying Disc on Ranch in Roswell Region" press release). ^[extracted]
- **Source**: Major Curtan, **HQ 8th Air Force** (Fort Worth), telephonic. ^[extracted]
- **Object description**: hexagonal disc + ~20-ft-diameter balloon + cable. ^[extracted]
- **8th AAF disposition at time of teletype**: object resembles a **weather balloon with radar reflector** — but Wright Field's telephonic feedback to 8th AAF *"had not borne out this belief"*. ^[extracted]
- **Disposition**: in transit to **Wright Field** by special plane for examination. ^[extracted]
- **FBI involvement**: notified for press-management reasons (NBC + AP attempting to break the story). **No FBI investigation conducted.** ^[extracted]
- **Wright Field follow-up commitment**: 8th AAF's Curtan undertakes to have Wright Field report results to **FBI Cincinnati** SAC. ^[extracted] No follow-up appears later in section 1. ^[inferred]

## What the teletype does NOT establish

- The hexagonal disc's **dimensions, weight, or material composition**. ^[extracted]
- The **identity of the recovery personnel** or the precise location of recovery (the teletype names only "near Roswell"). ^[extracted]
- The Wright Field examination's **conclusion**. ^[extracted]
- Any presence of bodies, biological material, or recovered occupants. ^[extracted] **The contemporaneous FBI teletype is silent on these points.**

## Position in the corpus

This is the **only Roswell-related artifact in the FBI corpus** as ingested. ^[inferred] It carries **substantial evidentiary weight** because: ^[inferred]

- It is **contemporaneous** (8 Jul 1947, hours after the recovery);
- It is **inter-agency** (8th AAF telephonic to FBI Dallas, then teletype to HQ);
- It is **filed within the FBI's master saucer-engagement file** [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894]] at the **first month** of the file's existence;
- It captures a **disagreement between 8th AAF and Wright Field** on the weather-balloon framing — i.e. *the prosaic-explanation framing was already contested within the AAF on day 1*. ^[extracted]

The teletype is not by itself proof of an extraordinary recovery; it is, however, **proof that the weather-balloon framing was not unanimous within the AAF on the day of recovery**. ^[inferred]
