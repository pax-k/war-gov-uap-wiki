# Sighting — Grand Blanc, MI (21 February 1960) — Joseph Perry telescope photograph

A **single-frame 35mm slide** of *"a saucer-like object silhouetted against the moon"* taken by **Joseph F. Perry** of **3079 Edwards Street, Grand Blanc, Michigan**, at approximately **1:00 AM, 21 February 1960**. ^[extracted] The case is documented at FBI HQ across **at least seven serials of [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-9|62-HQ-83894 section 9]]** (410, 411, 412, 413, 414, 415, 416, with a 1966 inquiry citing the case at 445). ^[extracted]

This is the section's **most-extended single-event filing thread** by serial count and time span (~6 years from initial intake to last citation), illustrating how a **photographic UFO case** gets handled differently from a witness-only saucer report — the photographic medium creates **physical-evidence custody** and **press-handling** problems that letter-class cases do not generate. ^[inferred]

## Witness profile

**Joseph F. Perry** — described in the FBI filings as: ^[extracted]

- *"a professional photographer the past thirty years"* (per his own self-description to Flint Resident Agency, 4 Mar 1960)
- *"proprietor of a restaurant [pizzeria] in Grand Blanc"*
- amateur lunar photographer with a **homemade telescope** as his observing instrument
- in 1960 ~50+ years of age based on photographer career length ^[inferred]

## Why the case lingered six years

Three structural reasons: ^[inferred]

1. **The photograph creates evidence custody.** Perry surrendered a physical 35mm slide. He wanted it back. The FBI no longer had it (passed to OSI Selfridge); OSI's analysis-or-return policy was opaque to Perry. The photographic medium creates a **custodial obligation** that no purely-testimonial case generates. ^[inferred]
2. **The press attached itself early.** Before the FBI even had time to clarify its non-investigative role, the Detroit Times had already published *"FBI is interested in the picture"*. ^[extracted] Hoover's reputation-protection instinct then required active pushback through correspondence, not passive non-engagement. ^[inferred]
3. **Perry escalated to the President.** A letter to Eisenhower (or, per the OCR, to *"the President"* dated 21 Mar 1959 [sic — almost certainly 1960]) was forwarded by DOJ to FBI for response. ^[extracted] White House routing of a UFO inquiry obligates a higher-grade FBI reply than a citizen's direct inquiry would. ^[inferred]

## Filing-channel observation

The Perry case is filed in section 9 across **multiple FBI channel types**:

- **Detroit field office** (intake, witness interview)
- **Flint Resident Agency** (initial press contact)
- **HQ Director's office** (Hoover personal correspondence)
- **NICAP-channel evidence** (referenced but not commissioned)
- **OSI Selfridge AFB** (technical custody)

This **multi-channel disposition** is distinctive — most section-9 cases are single-channel field-to-HQ-to-OSI; the Perry case **fans out** because of media coverage and witness persistence. ^[inferred]

## What we never learn

- **What's actually in the photograph.** ^[ambiguous] No FBI filing in section 9 describes the visual content of the slide beyond *"a saucer-like object silhouetted against the moon"*. ^[extracted] The OSI analysis is not summarized into the FBI file.
- **Whether OSI returned the slide.** ^[open] Section 9 ends with the slide in OSI custody; the 1966 Snigier inquiry presumes Perry never got it back, but no definitive disposition is recorded.
- **Whether the object was a known camera artifact.** ^[ambiguous] Reflections, dust, lens flares, and double exposures are common in amateur lunar photography, but no analysis result is in section 9.

## Provenance / Marker note

The case shows the **Hoover-personal-correspondence channel** in good operational form: Perry receives a personal Hoover letter on 21 Apr 1960 (serial 416) — the same letter form Hoover uses for citizen UFO inquiries (Maney 394, Mastroberte 428, etc.) but with **case-specific operational detail** rather than the generic policy disclaimer. ^[inferred] This is unusual within section 9.
