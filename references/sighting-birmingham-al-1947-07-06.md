# Sighting — Birmingham AL Photographed Discs (6 Jul 1947)

A **mass-sighting + photographic evidence + AAF-witness statement** event on the **night of Sunday, 6 July 1947** over Birmingham AL, documented by **Headquarters Birmingham Air Reserve Training Detachment, Birmingham Army Air Field** under **Major Jack C. White** (Air Corps Commanding) on **8 July 1947**. ^[extracted] Routed via **14th Air Force, Orlando FL** (1st Indorsement, **24 July 1947**, signed W. Murphy) → **Commanding General, Air Defense Command, Mitchel Field NY**. ^[extracted] Filed at FBI HQ as part of [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|62-HQ-83894 serial 130]] OCR pp. 4-13.

## Routing

The Birmingham AAF report stands out among 1947 disc reports for **bypassing 4AF Hamilton Field** — instead routing **through 14th AF Orlando** (the 14th's intelligence chain). ^[inferred] The 14th AF cover letter explicitly states: ^[extracted]

> 2. This Headquarters has made no investigation of "Flying Disc" reports because **this is an isolated case**.

The 14th AF's "isolated case" framing is **structurally significant** — by 24 Jul 1947, the AAF intelligence regime was already segmenting reports into **case-by-case** vs **cluster** classifications, with the 14th AF declining to investigate further on grounds of isolation. ^[inferred] This contrasts with **4AF's saturated investigation regime** ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2 serials 53, 95, 97, 98]]) where MOICs accumulated daily.

## What was reported

### The Birmingham AAF cover letter (Maj Jack C. White, 8 Jul 1947)
^[extracted]

> 1. Inclosed herewith is a photograph of the "publicized flying disc," which was reportedly witnessed over Birmingham on the night of Sunday, 6 July 1947.
>
> 2. Attention is invited to the two light spots on the print and the light trail following the two discs. The arrow at the top of the photograph indicates the direction of flight. **This has been examined by professional photographers in Birmingham and their general opinion is that the two spots shown on the light trail are no flaws in the negative, but instead, an actual photograph of the mysterious disc.**
>
> 3. The undersigned officer did not personally witness the flight of any disc, however, in view of the numerous reports received from the citizens of Birmingham, **it is the general opinion in Birmingham that "something was in the air."** Attached hereto as inclosure number two is a statement made by the only military personnel of this organization who personally witnessed the flight of the mysterious disc.

The professional-photographer assessment of the negative (no-flaw, real-object interpretation) is **structurally significant** — it is the **only 1947-corpus instance where independent professional-photographer review is performed before AAF transmission**. ^[inferred]

### Sgt Ira L. Livingston eyewitness statement (the only military witness)
^[extracted]

> I, Staff Sergeant **Ira L. Livingston**, RA 14 153 972, Air Corps, have approximately 250 hours flying time as pilot and Armorer Gunner have the following statement to make concerning the appearance of "Flying Discs" in the vicinity of Birmingham, Alabama.
>
> At **2045 hours, 6 July 1947**, while I was eating supper at my residence at 1354 Meadow Lane, Green Acres, Birmingham, Alabama, my next door neighbor, Mr. **Herman M. Sookwell**, called for me to come to the front door that there were some "Flying Discs" outside. Immediately I went out in the front yard to observe the objects. The objects appeared to the **West of Birmingham traveling in a South Eastern direction**. They appeared to be approximately **2000 feet above the horizon at a 45 degree angle** from where I was standing at an unestimated distance away. **The objects appeared to be approximately two (2) feet in diameter, round in shape, producing a dim glow of light and traveling at an estimated speed of five (5) to six (6) hundred miles per hour.** **The objects or object appeared to be traveling in a definite arc rather than straight and as soon as one was out of sight another would appear behind it, but not always in** [continues]

Livingston's report combines: ^[extracted]

- **AAF-officer credibility**: Air Corps Sgt with ~250 hrs flying time as pilot + armorer-gunner.
- **Civilian witness corroboration**: neighbor Sookwell first observed and alerted Livingston.
- **Quantitative observation data**: angular position (2000 ft above horizon at 45°), apparent diameter (~2 ft), speed estimate (500-600 mph), arc rather than straight path.
- **Sequencing**: "as soon as one was out of sight another would appear behind it" — suggests **multiple discs in sequential formation**.

The "**dim glow of light**" and **6 July 1947** date make this case structurally similar to the **Browning B-25 sighting same day** ([[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30|AFBIR-CO Report #9]]) west of Kansas City, but Birmingham is **~600 mi SE of Browning's track** — i.e. distinct events. ^[inferred]

### Newspaper enclosures

The package includes clippings from **The Birmingham News** + **The Age-Herald**: ^[extracted]

- **"Mysterious Flying Saucers Reported In Birmingham Skies"** — front-page article.
- **"RADIO-GUIDED, SAYS LAD"** — 10-yr-old **Michael Eisman**: *"I'd like to give my opinion about what the flying saucers really are."*
- **Charles F. Bradley, Birmingham weatherman**: "I do not believe the spectacle was a weather phenomena. I have instructed my staff to be on the alert for any information they m[ay receive]."
- **Fairfield Highlands citizen**: discs came over residence at ~1,500 mph, "would go in opposite directions, stop, and bump into e[ach other]."

The **bump-into-each-other behavior** report is **morphologically distinct** from the dominant 1947 signature (**no-deviation straight-flight, no-sound** per [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30|AFBIR-CO]]). ^[inferred] AFBIR-CO's analytical sample **does not include any Birmingham datapoints** — Birmingham AAF's report did not reach AFBIR-CO before the 30 Jul 1947 cutoff, possibly because the 14th AF declined to investigate. ^[inferred]

## Why this case matters

### First photographed-and-professional-evaluated 1947 disc photograph in the corpus

The Birmingham AAF photograph (showing two light spots + light trail with direction-arrow annotation) was **examined by professional photographers in Birmingham** before transmission, with their conclusion that the spots are **not negative-flaws but actual photographic evidence of the discs**. ^[extracted] This is the **earliest professionally-evaluated UAP photographic evidence** in the corpus. ^[inferred] The corpus does not yet have access to the actual photograph image (it is referenced as Inclosure 1 in the 8 Jul 1947 transmittal letter but is not part of the OCR'd serial 130 packet text).

For comparison, [[references/sighting-rhodes-phoenix-1947-07-07|Rhodes Phoenix]] (7 Jul 1947) — the famous photographic case — is **dated one day later** than Birmingham. The two cases run **in parallel** through the 4AF and 14th AF intelligence chains. ^[inferred]

### Mass-witness + civilian-press structural template

The Birmingham case is the **earliest 1947-corpus case combining four institutional intake channels**: ^[inferred]

1. **AAF-Reserve military witness** (Sgt Livingston).
2. **Civilian-neighbor witness** (Sookwell first, then alerted Livingston).
3. **Press wave** (The Birmingham News + Age-Herald).
4. **Independent third-party evaluator** (professional photographers + Birmingham weatherman Bradley).

This 4-channel template **anticipates** the [[concepts/civilian-saucer-hypothesis-1947|civilian-saucer-hypothesis taxonomy]] documented in section 1, but at higher fidelity — Birmingham preserves all 4 channels in **a single AAF transmittal package** rather than fragmenting them across separate FBI HQ intake events.

### "Isolated case" framing as institutional-resistance signal

The 14th AF's 24 Jul 1947 framing — *"This Headquarters has made no investigation of 'Flying Disc' reports because this is an isolated case"* — is **structurally consistent with the [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|hoax-default]] disposition** but inverts its mechanism: ^[inferred] instead of *investigating-to-confirm-hoax*, the 14th AF is *not-investigating-because-isolated*. **The "isolated case" framing institutionally legitimates non-investigation** — a pattern that would later harden into the 1948-49 Twining-era public-debunking posture. ^[inferred]
