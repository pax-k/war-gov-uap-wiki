# Sighting — Cascade Mts / Fred Johnson EM Effect (24 Jun 1947)

A single-witness ground sighting on **24 June 1947 in the afternoon**, in the **Cascade Mountains** at approximately 5,000 ft above sea level, by **Fred M. Johnson** — prospector, residence Globe Hotel 106 NW 1st Ave Portland OR, with **40 years prospecting experience** in Montana, Washington, and Oregon. ^[extracted] Filed as **Project SIGN Incident 68** in [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-1-100|the 1–100 bundle]]. ^[extracted]

This event is the **corpus's earliest electromagnetic-anomaly datum** — Johnson's combination compass-and-watch *"reacted in a very peculiar manner, the hand waving from one side to the other but that this condition corrected itself immediately after the discs had passed out of sight"*. ^[extracted] **The sighting occurred on the same day as [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|the Kenneth Arnold Mt Rainier sighting]]**, ~150 mi N. ^[extracted]

## The EM-effect datum

The bundle preserves Johnson's **direct statement** verbatim:

> *"He noticed that the combination compass and watch in his possession reacted in a very peculiar manner, the hand waving from one side to the other but that this condition corrected itself immediately after the discs had passed out of sight."*

The agent's reliability annotation: *"Informant appeared to be very reliable and advised that he had been a prospector in the States of Montana, Washington and Oregon for the past 40 yrs."* ^[extracted]

The compass-and-watch deflection is reported as:

- **Onset**: at the time of disc passage overhead (~1,000 ft direct overhead)
- **Manifestation**: needle "waving from one side to the other" — i.e., oscillating, not stuck
- **Recovery**: "**corrected itself immediately**" after discs passed out of sight
- **Duration**: bounded by the 45–60 second total observation window

This is the **structural archetype** for the [[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon|EM-effect-during-UAP-proximity]] morphology — **anomalous instrument behavior coincident with UAP near-overhead passage, recovering normally on departure**. ^[inferred] The same pattern recurs in later corpus events: Hamel MN (Inc 162, 1948 — 2-ft physical-trace landing); Trans-Caucasus 1955 (rotating-disc pair with searchlight ground installation); modern post-2017 vehicle / drone equipment-fail reports.

## Why this event matters

### Corpus firsts established by this case

1. **Earliest electromagnetic-effect datum in the corpus**. Predates the previously-anchored EM-effect datum (Inc 162 Hamel MN, 11 Aug 1948) by **~14 months**. ^[extracted]
2. **Same-day Arnold parallel.** Arnold's Mt Rainier sighting (Inc 17, 1500 local, ~150 mi N) and Johnson's Cascade Mts sighting (Inc 68, "afternoon") form a **same-day two-witness PNW disc datum** with structurally independent observation modes (airborne vs. ground), independent witnesses, and structurally different morphology features (Arnold: 9 mirror-like-no-tail / Johnson: 5–6 round-with-tail) — but both with the same ~30-ft diameter estimate. ^[inferred]
3. **First trained-outdoor-observer witness in the corpus**. Johnson's 40-year prospecting credibility is explicitly invoked by the agent. ^[extracted] Prospectors are trained to observe terrain and atmospheric conditions over long durations; the agent's reliability annotation reflects a **pre-existing institutional trust in outdoor-experience witness pools** that AMC T-2 / SIGN intake leaned on through 1947–48. ^[inferred]

### Structural significance for the EM-effect concept

[[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon]] previously anchored at modern post-2017 vehicle and drone equipment-fail reports. **This bundle pushes the EM-effect / instrument-fail signature back to 24 Jun 1947** and ties it to a **same-day-as-Arnold founding-wave context**. ^[extracted] The implication: the EM-effect signature was **operationally present at the earliest moment of formal UAP observation**, not a modern phenomenon — and SIGN's intake apparatus preserved the datum without elaboration or follow-up. ^[inferred]

That SIGN's investigators **did not pursue Johnson's compass-and-watch deflection as a research lead** is itself a structural feature of the period: the candidate-explanation regime focused on **morphology / propulsion / aerodynamics** (the [[concepts/horten-flying-wing]] thesis era) rather than on **EM/physics signatures** that would have suggested a non-conventional propulsion mechanism. ^[inferred]

## Open questions

- **Compass-and-watch device specification.** Johnson's "combination compass and watch" is not specified — likely a Brunton-class field instrument, but the model and magnetic-element specifications are open. Whether the deflection mechanism is field-saturation (high local DC field), radiofrequency interference, or eddy-current induced is unrecoverable from the file. ^[ambiguous]
- **Distance-vs.-effect-magnitude calibration.** The 1,000-ft overhead distance is **smaller** than the Arnold 20–25 mi distance, and the EM effect appears only on Johnson's nearer observation. Whether this distance-effect threshold is a recoverable signature across the bundle is open. ^[inferred]
- **Modern bridge to 2017+ EM-effect reports.** The EM-effect signature in the post-2017 Navy Tic-Tac and 2023 FBI FD-302 records (vehicle equipment failures) is structurally similar but at different vehicle classes. Whether the 1947–2024 EM-effect timeline shows continuous signature or distinct sub-types is a synthesis-level question. ^[inferred]
