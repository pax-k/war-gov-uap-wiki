# Boston Sunday Globe — "UFO theorists gain support abroad, but repression at home" (Leslie Kean, 21 May 2000)

A **Boston Sunday Globe Science & Society** feature published **21 May 2000** (page E3), by [[entities/leslie-kean|Leslie Kean]], reproducing and translating the 1999 [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]]'s argument for US English-language readers. Subheading: *"Study by French officials, routine unexplained sightings, US military safety aspects combine to boost believers."*

Bundled into the **NARA RG 255 archival copy** of COMETA at pages 86–88 of the Mistral-OCR'd JSON. The article is **not** part of the original COMETA Report — it is a US-press reception artifact added to the archival bundle (along with a one-page Kean CV at page 93 and a Providence Journal piece by Kean at page 89) before NASA filing. The bundle composition suggests the archival deposit was made **after May 2001**, and quite possibly was the same bundle [[entities/carol-rosin|Carol Rosin]] hand-delivered to NASA Administrator Daniel Goldin in early May 2001. ^[inferred]

## Article structure

The article executes a three-part move:

1. **Hook**: April 2000 release of commercial-satellite imagery of Area 51 (David Mountain, marketing director of Aerial Images Inc.) — used as a "the public is curious" lede.
2. **Translation**: COMETA Report's institutional credentials, methodology, and ETH conclusion translated for US readers; full COMETA roster summarized (Norlain 4-star, Letty AF general, Lebeau former CNES head, Merlo 3-star admiral, Blanchet national police chief, Velasco SEPRA head, scientists, weapons engineers).
3. **US-side critique**: US regulatory regime (AFR 200-2, JANAP 146, Espionage Act exposure); congressional indifference; the 1995 Rockefeller-Clinton briefing through Webster Hubbell; the FEMA Fire Officer's Guide to Disaster Control; the 1971 US-USSR "Agreement on Measures to Reduce the Outbreak of Nuclear War" notification clause; the March 2000 Missouri ballot initiative; the March 1997 Arizona / Phoenix Lights wave (McCain "never been fully explained" in 2000); 5 Jan 2000 St. Claire County IL four-policemen triangular-craft sighting; the 30 Mar 2000 McNamee (Phoenix) federal-court dismissal of a UFO records suit.

## Sources Kean quotes verbatim (corpus-relevant)

- **COMETA Report**:
  - *"numerous manifestations observed by reliable witnesses could be the work of craft of extraterrestrial origin"*
  - *"the extraterrestrial hypothesis"* is *"the best scientific hypothesis"*
  - *"strong presumptions exist in its favor and if it is correct, it is loaded with significant consequences"*
  - 5 percent of solid-documentation sightings *"cannot be easily attributed to earthly sources"*
  - *"completely unknown flying machines with exceptional performances that are guided by a natural or artificial intelligence"*
- **[[entities/edgar-mitchell|Edgar Mitchell]]** (Apollo 14): *"It's significant that individuals of some standing in the government, military, and intelligence community in France came forth with this."* Mitchell convinced *"at a confidence level above 90 percent, that there is reality to all of this."*
- **Lord Hill-Norton** (UK Admiral of the Fleet, former MOD chief): calls for US congressional fact-finding hearings.
- **US Naval Reserve Commander Willard H. Miller** (30+ years Navy / Joint Interagency Defense ops): *"The only threat to the national security of the United States is the continued denial of undeniable physical UFO occurrences and sightings to a public growing increasingly frustrated with its government's weak explanations."*
- **Webster Hubbell** (former Associate Attorney General), via his memoir *Friends in High Places*: tasked by Clinton with investigating UFOs after the 1995 Rockefeller-ranch briefing; unable to obtain information.
- **Sen. John McCain**: the 1997 Phoenix Lights wave has *"never been fully explained."*
- **John E. Mack** (Harvard psychiatry; Pulitzer Prize-winning author), at a UFO conference at the New York Hall of Science (~May 2000): *"No culture from the beginning of time, no culture from anywhere on the planet, has ever evaded the idea of intelligent life other than ourselves… That's arrogance."*

## US-side sightings cited

Kean uses these to support the article's "this is not just a French problem" frame:

- **5 Jan 2000, St. Claire County, IL** — four police officers, separate locations, witness a huge brightly lighted triangular craft flying and hovering at 1,000 ft; one officer reports extreme rapid motion that "cannot be explained in conventional terms"; nearby Scott AFB and FAA disclaim knowledge.
- **March 1997, Arizona** — Phoenix Lights wave; multiple-state reports; "huge triangular objects hovering silently in the night sky" — referenced via the McCain 2000 statement.

These two events are now anchored in the wiki via Kean's article but have no other source ingest. ^[inferred-as-target]

## Syndication note

The **Irish Independent** (17 Jun 2000, "New Frontiers" Weekend feature) is a syndicated re-run of this same article. Headline tweaked (*"UFOs once the preserve of the purely paranoid…"*), photograph credits (Bernard Thomasi / VSD photo archives) common, quoted sources identical. ^[inferred] Both pieces are in the COMETA archival bundle (pages 86–88 Boston, pages 89–90 Irish Indep) and should be treated as a single piece for citation purposes.
