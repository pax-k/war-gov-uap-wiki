# Pocantico Colloquium (Rockefeller-Sturrock UFO Physical-Evidence Workshop, 1997)

A 1997 **invitation-only scientific colloquium** funded by **Laurance Rockefeller** at the **Pocantico estate near West Point** (on Rockefeller Brothers Fund property in Tarrytown, NY), moderated by **astrophysicist Peter Sturrock** (Stanford), focused on **physical evidence concerning UFOs**. Described inline in [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]] Chapter 9.1.

The colloquium is the corpus's first **non-government science-funded UAP research workshop**, structurally distinct from:

- Government research programs ([[entities/gepan-sepra|GEPAN/SEPRA]], [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]]).
- Government-convened scientific panels ([[concepts/robertson-panel-runup-1952|Robertson Panel]]).
- Civilian saucer-research organizations ([[entities/aerial-phenomena-research-organization|APRO]], [[entities/borderland-sciences-research-associates|BSRA]]).

## What COMETA reports

Per COMETA §9.1 (page 47 of the OCR), verbatim:

> *"the [Pocantico] colloquium organized in October 1997 by Laurance Rockefeller at Pocantico, near West Point, on the property of the Rockefeller Bros. Fund. Moderated by astrophysicist Peter Sturrock, this colloquium focused on physical evidence concerning UFOs.*
>
> *Specialists on radar, the biological effects of microwaves, photography, etc., who often were not very familiar with the UFO problem, formed a scientific council there that judged the papers presented by the UFO researchers. French participation was quite noteworthy; it consisted of the head of SEPRA and two members of the scientific council. A summary document expressed the desire that many countries have a UFO research organization comparable to that of France."*

Key structural elements:

- **Convener**: Laurance Rockefeller (philanthropist).
- **Moderator**: Peter Sturrock (astrophysicist, Stanford).
- **Venue**: Pocantico estate, Tarrytown NY, Rockefeller Brothers Fund property.
- **Date**: October 1997 (per COMETA Ch. 9.1).
- **Format**: *"Scientific council"* — physical-domain specialists (radar, microwave-bioeffects, photography) who *"often were not very familiar with the UFO problem"* judging papers presented by UFO researchers.
- **French participation**: **Head of SEPRA** ([[entities/gepan-sepra|Jean-Jacques Vélasco]], per COMETA roster) + **two members of the scientific council**.
- **Output**: a summary document urging international adoption of France-style civilian UAP-research bodies.

## Lineage with the 1995 Rockefeller-Clinton briefing

The same patron, **Laurance Rockefeller**, briefed President Bill Clinton, Hillary Clinton, and Presidential science advisor **Jack Gibbons** at Rockefeller's **Wyoming ranch** in **1995**, providing UFO briefing materials. Clinton tasked **Associate Attorney General Webster Hubbell** at DOJ to investigate. The lineage is documented in [[entities/leslie-kean|Leslie Kean]]'s [[references/boston-globe-ufo-2000-05-21|May 2000 Boston Sunday Globe piece]], citing Hubbell's memoir *Friends in High Places* — Hubbell reports being unable to obtain UFO information despite his commander-in-chief's request.

The structural pattern is **Rockefeller-funded UAP advocacy across two-and-a-half years**:

| Year | Event | Audience | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1995 | Rockefeller-Clinton ranch briefing | POTUS, FLOTUS, science advisor | Hubbell DOJ inquiry — *"no information"* response |
| 1997 | Pocantico colloquium | Sturrock-moderated scientific council; UFO researchers | Summary doc urging France-model civilian agencies internationally |

The 1997 colloquium is the **scientific-council** instantiation; the 1995 briefing was the **executive-branch** instantiation. Both come from the same philanthropic patron. ^[inferred]

## Why this artifact matters

- **First non-government science workshop on UAP physical evidence in the wiki corpus.** Distinct from [[concepts/robertson-panel-runup-1952|Robertson Panel]] (CIA-convened, 1952) and the [[concepts/condon-committee|Condon Committee]] (Air Force-funded, 1966–68): Pocantico was privately funded by a single philanthropist.
- **SEPRA's first attested international scientific-collaboration appearance.** COMETA Chapter 9.1 surfaces this — the head of SEPRA + two French scientific-council members participated. Position-wise, Pocantico is one of the earliest events in the wiki where a non-French body explicitly endorsed the France-model civilian UAP-research approach. ^[inferred]
- **The Rockefeller-Pocantico track is structurally orthogonal to the Carol Rosin → NASA track.** Both are private US channels routing French UAP science into US policy receptiveness. The Pocantico track is **science-to-science** (Sturrock + scientific-council members); the [[entities/carol-rosin|Rosin]] track is **advocate-to-administrator** (Rosin → Goldin). ^[inferred]
- **Possibly catalytic for COMETA**: COMETA's drafting period was 1995–1999, overlapping the 1997 Pocantico event. SEPRA participated in both. Whether Pocantico's discussion fed into COMETA Chapter 9.1's writing is not documented in this source but is the natural structural read. ^[inferred-as-target]
