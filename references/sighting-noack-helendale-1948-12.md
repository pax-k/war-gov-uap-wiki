# Sighting — Noack Helendale CA Tow-Kite Object (December 1948)

A **purported flying-disc recovery** in the **Mojave Desert near Helendale CA** (~20 mi from Lone Pine, ~150 mi NE of Pasadena), reported to the FBI Houston field office on **27 December 1948** by attorney Fuller Blackwell on behalf of his friend **Lonnie Edward Noack**, a Houston machinist with Humble Oil and Refining Company. ^[extracted] Resolved on **28 December 1948** as an **Olson and Rice Manufacturing Co. (Los Angeles) tow-target/kite prototype** — abandoned by the manufacturer after failed flight tests. ^[extracted] The case is significant for **operational selectivity in the post-BB-57 FBI saucer policy**: Inspector [[entities/h-b-fletcher|H. B. Fletcher]] **actively dispatched** Houston SAC + LA SAC to recover and *guard* the disc despite BB-57 having been in force for 14 months. ^[extracted]

## What this case complicates

The Noack case is the **only documented active-FBI dispatch on a possible disc recovery in the post-BB-57 wiki corpus**. ^[inferred] [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|BB-57]] (1 Oct 1947, ~14 months prior) explicitly directed: *"all future reports connected with flying discs should be referred to the Air Force and no investigative action taken by Bureau agents"*. ^[extracted]

Inspector Fletcher's actions on 28 Dec 1948 are **directly inconsistent** with this directive: ^[inferred]

1. **He instructed LA SAC Hood to "send out sufficient Agents to find the disc and guard it until it could be properly handled by the appropriate Government authority"** — i.e. an active FBI search-and-protect operation. ^[extracted]
2. **He instructed Houston SAC**: *"FBI representatives should assume an active interest in the investigation throughout, cooperating, of course, with O.S.I. representatives"* — i.e. active investigative engagement. ^[extracted]
3. **He coordinated with FBI Lab** for examination of the recovered cylinder. ^[extracted]

The case thus shows that **BB-57 was operationally selectively non-applied** when **(a) physical recovery was credibly imminent**, **(b) film evidence was available**, and **(c) AF/OSI was already on-scene**. ^[inferred] The selectivity logic is plausibly: *"BB-57 prohibits routine investigation but does not prohibit physical-evidence recovery operations that AF cannot perform alone in time"*. ^[inferred] This is **operational nonlinearity** in the cooperation-regime termination, not policy revision.

## What "active investigation" looked like in 5.5 hours

Between 9 AM and 5 PM EST on 28 Dec 1948, the FBI:

- **Issued an ASMS teletype demand** (Houston → HQ → LA) for full case summary and signed witness statement
- **Dispatched Houston SA Graham W. Kitchel** to take Noack's signed statement
- **Coordinated with OSI Ellington Field** (Mrs Rose C. Rotsel transcribing for OSI; SA Robert B. Fleming joint)
- **Dispatched LA SAC Hood** to locate Person + Smiths, secure film, and guard the recovery site
- **Notified Olson and Rice Manufacturing Co.** through SA Edward Olsen — produced the manufacturer's confession of the abandoned-prototype identity
- **Coordinated FBI Lab** (separate routing) for cylinder analysis

This is **the speed of a cooperation-regime operation**. ^[inferred] In ~8 hours, the FBI had dispatched, secured, identified, and resolved a possible disc recovery — at a time when [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|BB-57]] supposedly held the Bureau out of disc cases entirely. ^[inferred]

## Resolution — the tow-target/kite identification

Olson and Rice Manufacturing Company of Los Angeles produced a **tow target prototype** (manufacturer's term per Henry T. Rice; OCR: *"model tow target or kite"* on Houston file synopsis page 122) — disc-center + wings configuration, abandoned after failed flight tests. ^[extracted] The aluminum cylinder Noack recovered was a wing-tip attachment (rocket housing or fuel tank conjecture per Noack page 90; conventional conclusion: tow-attachment hardware).

The Olson and Rice tow-target case **functions as a hoax-default discovery for the FBI**: the path from "possible flying disc" to "abandoned manufactured tow-target" took 5.5 hours. The case strengthens [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|the saucer-as-hoax-default operating presumption]]. ^[inferred]

The Olson and Rice product is **likely the same toy-engineering pattern** as the *"FLYIN-SAUCER (A Gyroscopic Airfoil)"* product whose flight-instructions are filed in section 4 pages 175-176 — both products appear to be **late-1940s Los Angeles disc-shaped toy/target manufacturing experiments** captured in the FBI's hoax-default monitoring. ^[inferred] This is the corpus's first explicit primary-source documentation of **commercial disc-shaped toys in the post-BB-57 FBI corpus**. ^[inferred]

## Why this matters

The Noack case is the **operational counterevidence** to a strict reading of BB-57. ^[inferred] It establishes that:

1. **The FBI Liaison and field offices retained capacity to actively investigate flying-disc cases** ~14 months after BB-57.
2. **Inspector H. B. Fletcher had institutional authority** to dispatch agents and coordinate with AF on a disc case, **without re-clearing BB-57** at HQ Director level.
3. **The "sufficient Agents to ... guard the disc"** language is one of only two documented FBI guard-the-disc instructions in the wiki corpus (the other being Hoover's 1947 marginal note: *"we must insist upon full access to discs recovered"* — see [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]]).
4. **The Olson and Rice → tow-target identification** is the **first physically-traceable disc-prank identification** in the post-1947 FBI corpus.
5. **Fletcher** is the same FBI Inspector who **15 weeks later drafts** the [[references/fbi-sac-letter-38-1949|SAC Letter No. 38]] proposing partial reactivation of FBI saucer engagement — see [[entities/h-b-fletcher]]. ^[inferred] The Noack case is plausibly **part of the institutional context** that motivated SAC Letter 38: Fletcher had already operationally treated saucer-recovery as a Bureau function, and now formalized that posture for all SACs.
