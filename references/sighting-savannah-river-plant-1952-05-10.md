# Sighting — Savannah River Plant AEC, 10 May 1952

A 30-minute multi-cluster sighting of **at least 8 disc-shaped objects** over the **400 area of the Savannah River Plant** (Aiken SC, near Ellenton), by **four E. I. du Pont Company employees** at the AEC critical facility on **10 May 1952** between approximately **10:45 PM and 11:15 PM EST**. ^[extracted] Captured in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-6|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 6]] at serial ~284 (pages 139-148). ^[extracted] **The first SRP UAP event in the FBI corpus** ^[inferred] — **3 months earlier than** the canonical [[references/sighting-savannah-river-plant-1952-08-08|8 August 1952 SRP event]] anchored in section 7.

## The teletype

The 12 May 1952 7:58 PM FBI Savannah → Director URGENT teletype, signed **SCHLENKER**, transcribed (with OCR truncations) ^[extracted]:

> SAVANNAH RIVER PLANT, ATOMIC ENERGY COMMISSION, FLYING DISC. AT APPROXIMATELY TEN FORTYFIVE PM, MAY TEN LAST FOUR EMPLOYEES OF DUPONT CO., EMPLOYED ON SAVANNAH RIVER PLANT NEAR ELLENTON, S. C., SAW FOUR DISC SHAPED OBJECTS APPROACHING THE FOUR HUNDRED AREA FROM THE SOUTH, DISAPPEARING IN NORTHERNLY DIRECTION.
>
> AT APPROXIMATELY ELEVEN FIVE PM, ABOVE MENTIONED EMPLOYEES SAW TWO SIMILAR OBJECTS APPROACH FROM SOUTH AND DISAPPEAR IN NORTHERNLY DIRECTION.
>
> AT APPROXIMATELY ELEVEN TEN PM ONE SIMILAR OBJECT APPROACHED FROM THE NORTHEAST AND DISAPPEARED IN SOUTHWESTERNLY DIRECTION.
>
> ONE MORE OBJECT SIGHTED ABOUT ELEVEN FIFTEEN PM TRAVELLING FROM SOUTH TO NORTH.
>
> EMPLOYEES DESCRIBED OBJECTS AS BEING ABOUT FIFTEEN INCHES [in apparent diameter at maximum visibility], BUT SEEMED TO HOLD GENERAL COURSE. ALSO STATED DUE TO SPEED AND ALTITUDE THEY WERE ONLY VISIBLE FOR FEW SECONDS. SAVANNAH OFFICE IS NOT ACTIVELY CONDUCTING INVESTIGATION IN THIS MATTER AND IS FURNISHING THIS INFO TO BUREAU FOR WHATEVER ACTION THEY DEEM ADVISABLE.

## Event sequence

| Time | Objects | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **22:45 EST** | **4 disc-shaped objects** | S → N |
| **23:05 EST** | **2 similar objects** | S → N |
| **23:10 EST** | **1 similar object** | NE → SW |
| **23:15 EST** | **1 similar object** | S → N |

**Total: 8 disc-shaped objects over ~30 minutes**, with **3 of 4 clusters following the same S → N corridor over the 400 area**. ^[extracted] The single NE → SW transit at 23:10 is structurally anomalous within the otherwise-S-to-N pattern. ^[inferred]

## Routing and follow-up

Per Hoover's 15 May 1952 follow-up letter to OSI ^[extracted]: *"The above data, which was previously furnished to your office through this Bureau's liaison representative, is being furnished for your information and any action you desire to take in this matter. No investigation is being conducted by this Bureau."*

The letter copies ^[extracted]:

- **Assistant Chief of Staff, G-2, Department of the Army, Attn: Chief, Intelligence Division**
- **Director of Naval Intelligence**
- **Captain John A. Waters, Director of Security, Atomic Energy Commission** (Room 805B, 333 Third Street NW, Washington DC)

The routing is **classic BB-57 / SAC Letter #38 passive-intake**: FBI accumulates the report, transmits to OSI + Army G-2 + DNI + AEC Security, **no FBI investigation**. ^[extracted] Internal annotations: *"5-14-52 62 advised DS; 5/14/52 John White AEC Advised US; 5/14/52 Col Free OSI advised US"* + *"N.W. Philcox also advised AEC + OSI stated they are conducting no inquiry."* ^[extracted]

**Notably**: by mid-May 1952, **OSI is conducting no inquiry** on an AEC critical-facility multi-witness disc-cluster event ^[extracted]. The institutional posture is **passive accumulation by all agencies** — the **structural pre-1952-wave hands-off frame** ^[inferred].

| **Direction** | **3 of 4 clusters S→N** | **NE direction** |
| **Apparent size** | ~15 inches | (unspecified) |
| **Time** | 10:45 PM - 11:15 PM EST | ~9:30 PM EST |
| **FBI investigation** | None | None |
| **Follow-on routing** | OSI + Army G-2 + DNI + AEC Security | (per section 7 anchor) |
| **Section / serial** | Section 6, serial ~284 | Section 7, serial 302 |

The two SRP events ^[inferred]:

- **Both occur at the 400 area** of SRP.
- **Both are multi-witness E. I. du Pont employees**.
- **Both are passive-intake-only** for FBI.
- **The May 1952 event is structurally richer** (8 objects, 4 clusters, 30-min duration vs. 1 object, single observation, ~few seconds).
- **The August 1952 event is the canonical anchor** in UFO history while the May 1952 event has been largely overlooked. ^[inferred]

The **3-month gap between the two SRP events** is a meaningful institutional datapoint ^[inferred] — the May 1952 event did **not** trigger a heightened intelligence posture at SRP; SRP-internal physical-security responses appear absent from both records. The **AEC critical-facility-anomaly threshold for active investigation** has shifted significantly since the 1950-51 [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-adcock-1950-03|Oak Ridge / Adcock]] dry-run ^[inferred] — by mid-1952, AEC critical-facility UAP events are **routinely filed and not investigated**, in marked contrast to the 9-day Oak Ridge multi-agency response of March 1950.

## Witness pool

The four du Pont employees are **not individually named** in the section-6 record ^[ambiguous] — the teletype refers to them only as *"four employees of DuPont Co., employed on Savannah River Plant near Ellenton, S. C."* The 8 August 1952 anchor (section 7) similarly does not individually name its two-witness pool. ^[ambiguous] The institutional choice to file by **employer-aggregate witness count** rather than individual identification is a recurring pattern in **AEC-contractor-staffed UAP reports**. ^[inferred]

## Institutional significance

The 10 May 1952 SRP event is structurally distinctive in the FBI corpus ^[inferred]:

1. **First SRP UAP event in the FBI corpus** — predating the canonical August 1952 anchor by 3 months.
2. **First multi-cluster multi-direction UAP event at an AEC critical facility** in the FBI corpus.
3. **First documented "all agencies are conducting no inquiry" institutional posture** for an AEC critical-facility UAP event — establishing the **late-pre-1952-wave routine-filing baseline**.
4. **Confirms the [[concepts/project-twinkle|Project Twinkle]] / Belmont memo institutional framework persists into mid-1952**: SRP is an AEC critical facility analogous to the **"sensitive installations in New Mexico"** the Twinkle network was instituted to monitor — yet **no analogous program exists at SRP**. ^[inferred] The Twinkle observational architecture remained **NM-specific** through the 1952-wave window. ^[inferred]
5. **Predates [[references/fbi-sac-letter-83-1952|SAC Letter #83]] by ~3.5 months** — the May 1952 SRP event arrives **before** Branigan's mid-Aug 1952 finding that field offices were not observing BB-57 paragraph D. ^[inferred] The 10 May 1952 SAC Savannah handling appears **fully compliant** with BB-57: report to FBI HQ, transmit to OSI, no investigation. The institutional friction that triggers SAC Letter #83 is in the **other** direction — field offices were investigating without HQ knowledge, **not** failing to report. ^[inferred]
