# Cochran-Schneider Carter White House UFO Coordination Inquiry (FBI HQ, 14-15 Jun 1977)

The **14 June 1977 telephone inquiry** from **Stanley Schneider** (Assistant to the Director, **Office of Science and Technology Policy**, EOP) to **Jay Cochran Jr.** (FBI Assistant Director, Technical Services Division), and its same-day FBI internal memorandum + 15 Jun follow-up letter. ^[extracted] Filed at **62-HQ-83894 serial 486** ^[extracted] — the **last filing in the OCR'd section 10**, and pending the missing section 6, the **last filing in FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894 itself**. ^[ambiguous]

This is the FBI corpus's primary source for the [[concepts/post-bb-uap-coordination-question-1977|Carter-era post-Blue-Book UAP coordination question]]. ^[inferred]

## The 14 June 1977 phone call (per Cochran's memo to McDermott)

The same-day Cochran-to-McDermott memo (Optional Form 10, July 1972 edition) records ^[extracted]:

> *"Stanley Schneider of the Office of Science and Technology, White House, telephonically contacted me today to inquire as to the Bureau's procedure for handling information received from the public or otherwise, relating to sightings of UFOs. He advised that Jody Powell of the President's staff has raised the question as to whether or not there is any coordination within the Executive Branch relating to information concerning this subject. He stated that the U.S. Air Force had closed their investigation of these matters several years ago and currently does nothing but refer such information to appropriate local authorities."*

> *"I advised him that as far as the FBI is concerned there appears to be no conceivable jurisdiction for us to conduct any inquiries upon receipt of information relating to a UFO sighting and, in the absence of some investigative jurisdiction based upon the information furnished, that information would be referred to the Department of the Air Force without any action being taken by the Bureau."*

> *"He thanked me for the information and stated that if any further contact was necessary he would call back."*

Cochran's memo is a **single-page record-only filing** ^[extracted]. Action: *"None. For information."* ^[extracted]

## Distribution

The Cochran-McDermott memo carries an unusually broad cc list ^[extracted]:

- **1 - Mr. Adams** (Associate Director or AD-equivalent) ^[inferred]
- **1 - Each Assistant Director** (i.e., copies to all Asst Directors)
- **1 - Mr. Boynton**

Plus the standard signature block listing all 17 named Asst-Director slots ^[extracted]:

> *"Adm. Serv. / Crim. Inv. / Fin. & Pers. / Ident. / Intell. / Laboratory / Legal Coun. / Plan. & Insp. / Rec. Mgmt. / Spec. Inv. / Tech. Servs. / Training / Public Affs. Off. / Telephone Rm. / Director's Sec'y."*

The total distribution is **16 cc copies** ^[extracted]. This is the **broadest distribution of any single UAP-related memo in the section-10 file** ^[inferred] — wider than annual self-review memos, wider than the 1969 Hennessey-summary Liaison memo, wider than the 1973 Ozanne impostor LHM. ^[inferred]

The breadth signals **institutional-record-protection** ^[inferred] — Cochran is ensuring that every Asst Director's office has the record of his answer to a White House question, presumably so that no later challenge could claim the FBI failed to inform other Asst Directors of the inquiry. ^[inferred]

## The 15 June 1977 follow-up letter (Cochran → Schneider)

The next day, Cochran sends Schneider a sealed enclosure ^[extracted]:

> *"Mr. Stanley Schneider*
> *Assistant to the Director*
> *Office of Science and Technology Policy*
> *Executive Office of the President*
> *Washington, D. C. 20500*

> *Dear Mr. Schneider:*
> *Pursuant to our conversation today, there is enclosed a copy of the FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin for the month of February, 1975. Beginning on page 16 there is an article covering the handling of UFO sightings which may be of interest to you in connection with your current inquiry."*

> *"If there is any way we may be of additional service to you, please do not hesitate to let me know.*

> *Sincerely yours,*
> *Jay Cochran, Jr.*
> *Assistant Director FBI*
> *Technical Services Division"* ^[extracted]

The reference to **the February 1975 FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin UFO article** is the only known FBI-corpus reference to **internal UAP guidance for state and local law enforcement** ^[inferred]. The article itself is not in section 10 — only the Cochran transmittal is.

The FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin (FBI LEB) is the FBI's monthly publication distributed to state and local police agencies ^[inferred — institutional knowledge], with content typically including investigative techniques, legal updates, and case studies. A Feb 1975 UFO article would be the **earliest documented FBI guidance to civilian LE on UFO sighting handling** ^[inferred].

## Routing — the political context

The chain of routing places the call at a structurally significant level ^[inferred]:

- **Jody Powell** (Press Secretary to President Carter) raises the question.
- **Stanley Schneider** (OSTP Asst Director) makes the call to FBI.
- **Jay Cochran Jr.** (FBI Asst Director, Tech Services) takes the call.
- **Mr. McDermott** (Cochran's superior or peer-equivalent) receives the FBI internal memo.
- **All Asst Directors + Mr. Adams + Mr. Boynton** receive cc.
- **The FBI Director (Clarence M. Kelley until end of Jul 1977; then William H. Webster)** — does not appear on the cc list. ^[inferred]

The **Director-level absence** is significant ^[inferred] — by 1977 the post-Hoover Bureau had distributed UAP correspondence handling away from the Director. ^[inferred] The question is handled at the Asst-Director level; the answer is recorded for the Asst-Director-level institutional memory; the Director is not asked for and does not provide a substantive position.

## Why this matters

The Cochran-Schneider exchange is the FBI corpus's:

- **First documented OSTP-to-FBI direct contact on UAP coordination.** ^[inferred]
- **First documented Press-Secretary-level Executive-Branch UAP question.** ^[inferred]
- **Strongest 1977 statement of the FBI's "no jurisdiction, no mechanism" disclaimer** — the *"no conceivable jurisdiction"* formulation is more emphatic than Hoover's earlier *"not within our investigative jurisdiction"* form-letter pattern. ^[inferred]
- **First explicit Executive-Branch acknowledgment that the AF UFO program has been closed for years.** ^[inferred] The 1969 Blue Book termination is institutionally **acknowledged** by Schneider on behalf of the White House — a landmark separate from the Bureau's 1977 own-acknowledgment via the [[references/fbi-blue-book-termination-fact-sheet-1977|McClanahan-Moore exchange]] (serials 483-484, Jan 1977).
- **The file's effective last filing.** ^[ambiguous] Pending section 6, the file accumulates no further material after this 14 Jun 1977 memo.

- **Did Schneider call back?** ^[open] No record in section 10.
- **What was in the Feb 1975 FBI LEB UFO article?** ^[open] The article itself is not retained.
- **Is there a parallel OSTP-side memo on this inquiry?** ^[open] Outside the FBI corpus.
- **Was OSTP coordinating its inquiry with any other agency (DoD, CIA, NASA)?** ^[open] Cochran's memo doesn't say.
