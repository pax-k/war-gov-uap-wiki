# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Serial 449 (LA SAC → Director memo + AFSCA *Flying Saucers International* magazine, Oct 1966)

An **18-OCR-page Mistral-OCR'd declassified artifact** from the **FBI Central Records Center / FBI HQ**. Page 0 carries the Records Center cover line *"G2-HQ-33394 Vol 1 Serial 449- ONLY"* (OCR digit-confusion of the canonical `62-HQ-83894`; the cover-slip filing block is authoritative ^[inferred]) with the FBI 2007 Automatic Declassification Guide note. ^[extracted] The serial-449 cross-stamp `82-83894-449` is visible at the foot of page 1 (also OCR digit-drift of `62-83894-449`). ^[inferred]

This is the **fourth artifact ingested** in the planned 17-serial 62-HQ-83894 wiki series — and the **second structurally novel intake channel** the file has surfaced (after [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438|serial 438]]'s FBI-authored field-investigation channel). See [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file]] for the parent-file hub.

## What's in the artifact

The 18 OCR'd pages decompose into **three documentary layers**:

1. **Page 0 — FBI Records Center routing/cover slip.** Carries the Class–Case–Sub–Vol–Serial filing block (`62-HQ-83894 Vol 1 Serial 449 ONLY` ^[inferred] from the OCR-degraded `G2-HQ-33394`), processing tag *"8/11/1274157 RRP003INFZ"*, and the FBI 2007 Automatic Declassification Guide note. ^[extracted]
2. **Page 1 — single-page LA SAC → Director memo dated 3 Oct 1966** captioned *"FLYING SAUCERS INTERNATIONAL, OFFICIAL JOURNAL OF THE AMALGAMATED FLYING SAUCER CLUBS OF AMERICA, INC. / IS - MISCELLANEOUS"*, filed as **LA serial `100-24442-40`** with distribution `2 - Bureau (RM) (CNC-1) / 1 - Los Angeles`, signed `JST:gcw (3)`, with HQ stamp `82-83894-449` (OCR digit-drift of `62-83894-449`). ^[extracted]
3. **Pages 2–17 — the entire enclosed magazine.** Issue No. 24, July 1966, of [[entities/amalgamated-flying-saucer-clubs-of-america|*Flying Saucers International*]] (formerly *UFO International*), the *"Official Journal of the Amalgamated Flying Saucer Clubs of America, Inc."* (AFSCA), 50¢ cover price, *"Special AFSCA Third National Flying Saucer Convention Issue"*, edited by [[entities/gabriel-green|Gabriel Green]] (Helen Green Asst. Ed.) at AFSCA HQ 2004 N. Hoover St., Los Angeles 90027. ^[extracted] The OCR captures the full magazine including the **Master Kalen-Li Retan / Korendor / Bob Renaud** AFSCA convention message (received *"by special directional short-wave radio"* on 4 May 1966 ^[extracted]), the AFSCA 3rd National Convention Reno NV registration form, the speaker roster (21 named contactees + saucer-movement figures), Mollie Thompson's "Cockeyed Ballad" and "Space Age Songs" lyrics, and a substantial set of clipped news articles on 1966 saucer sightings (Kettle Point ON, Wanaque NJ, Wichita KS, Mt. Wilson CA, Pasadena CA, Ringwood NJ, Hillsdale MI, the WSJ "New Fireball" story, the Pretoria South Africa case, and others). ^[extracted]

The artifact contains **no FBI-authored substantive content** beyond the single-page LA SAC routing memo. ^[extracted] The bulk of the artifact is **the externally-published AFSCA magazine**, clipped and filed in toto. The LA SAC's only operational gesture is **filing + indexing** under the LA Class-100 organization-counterintelligence file `100-24442` plus routing one carbon to HQ where it was serialized as `62-HQ-83894-449`. ^[extracted]

## What the LA SAC memo contains

The 3 Oct 1966 memo (single page, signed `JST:gcw`) reports the following ^[extracted]:

- The **Philadelphia Division** by letter dated **22 Sep 1966** furnished LA with Issue No. 24 (July 1966) of *Flying Saucers International*.
- Philadelphia received the magazine from **Mr. Jarvis H. Cooper**, IRS, 401 North Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA, on **19 Sep 1966**.
- Cooper said he subscribed *"because his son had exhibited an interest in flying saucers and outer space"* and complained that **pages 2 and 3 contained an article which he believed expounded the Communist Party (CP) line**.
- LA notes that **AFSCA's International Headquarters** is at 2004 N. Hoover St., Los Angeles, CA 90027.
- The CP-line article is the *"Special AFSCA Convention Message"* allegedly authored by *"Master KALEN-LI RETAN, Head of the planet KORENDOR"*, received on **5/4/66** *"by special directional short-wave radio"* by **BOB RENAUD**.
- LA's findings: **"The indices of the Los Angeles Division contain no information identifiable with RENAUD, and no investigation has ever been conducted on captioned organization."**
- LA's disposition: **"No further action is contemplated in this matter by the Los Angeles Office, UACB."** (`UACB` = "unless advised to the contrary by the Bureau" ^[inferred].)
- Distribution: 2 copies to the **Bureau** (with `CNC-1` annotation — likely "carbon-copy / non-confidential, 1" or a routing code ^[ambiguous]) and 1 to LA. The clerk's initials `JST:gcw (3)` indicate a 3-copy total prepared by clerk `gcw` for SA `JST` (full names not released). ^[extracted]

## What's in the magazine itself

The 16-page July 1966 issue is a **near-complete period snapshot of the mid-1960s American "saucer movement" / contactee subculture**. ^[inferred] The CP-line allegation by Cooper is mechanically located on **pages 2–3** of the magazine — the *"Bright World in Hands of Saucer Movement"* convention message attributed to a non-human author (Kalen-Li Retan of Korendor), allegedly received by short-wave radio by *"young electronic-technician"* Bob Renaud. The message's substantive content includes ^[extracted]:

- Anti-Vietnam-War framing: *"Your government is a military puppet, a mere parrot of the monstrosity that is the Military-Industrial complex. … Your President is, unfortunately, a pawn of their needs and demands."*
- Anti-news-media framing: *"Who controls all your news media? The government and the advertisers."*
- A Kennedy-assassination explanation tying it to the "Military Industrial octopus".
- An economic-system advocacy: *"Universal Economics … the non-money economic system"* as a precondition for civilizational survival.
- An interventionist-extraterrestrial frame: *"We have interfered in such places as the United States, China, Russia, and on a large scale in Vietnam. … we have infiltrated your planet."*
- Korendian humanoid morphology details (3–4 ft natural height, teleportation-based height-adjustment for terrestrial appearance, tan skin, a galactic *Galingua* / *Solex Mal* language family).

This is **not Communist Party doctrine** — it is **contactee-channeled extraterrestrial-utopian rhetoric** with anti-imperialist and anti-Vietnam-War framing common in mid-1960s peace-movement-adjacent literature. ^[inferred] Cooper's CP-line attribution is **a layperson's misread** of countercultural pacifism as Marxism-Leninism. ^[inferred] The FBI's institutional response confirms this read: LA **opens no investigation** and **takes no further action**.

The remainder of the issue covers ^[extracted]:
- AFSCA's 3rd National Convention (Reno NV, 8–10 Jul 1966, Centennial Coliseum, $2/day) with a **21-person speaker roster** including [[entities/gabriel-green|Gabriel Green]], Wayne S. Aho, Carl A. Anderson, Orfeo Angelucci, Daniel W. Fry, George King (Aetherius Society), Howard Menger, Sidney Padrick, Frank E. Stranges, George Van Tassel, and others.
- A book-promotion list (Van Tassel *Council of Seven Lights*, Bryant Reeve *Cosmic Viewpoint*, Albert K. Bender *Flying Saucers and the Three Men*, Frank Edwards *Flying Saucers Serious Business*, Donald E. Keyhoe *Top Secret*, Raymond Bernard *The Hollow Earth*, etc.).
- **The Wall Street Journal's** 13 Dec 1965 piece *"New 'Fireball' Raises an Old Question: Do Flying Saucers Exist?"* (Carlson) reproduced in full — quoting Donald H. Menzel (Harvard), J. Allen Hynek (Northwestern, USAF consultant), Frank B. Salisbury (Colorado State), Charles A. Maney (Defiance College). ^[extracted]
- A clipped Houston-paper interview with Gabriel Green about meeting *"a Martian"* and *"a four-foot man from the Alpha Centauri star system"*. ^[extracted]
- Multiple 1966 news clippings on UFO sightings (heavily OCR-degraded — see Open threads).
- Mollie Thompson's *"Cockeyed Ballad"* and *"Space Age Songs"* lyrics — period contactee-movement songcraft. ^[extracted]

## What 62-HQ-83894 appears to be — refined again, with N=4

Serials 220 (1950) + 403 (1956) jointly framed 62-HQ-83894 as a **content-accumulation-only file**. Serial 438 (1964) **partially refuted** that reading by introducing **(c) FBI-authored field-investigative product**. Serial 449 introduces **a fourth structural channel**, distinct from all three: ^[inferred]

| Channel | Serial | Year | Content type | Agency posture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Clipped public publication** | 403 | 1956 | Publisher's book jacket of Barker's *They Knew Too Much…* | Filed only |
| **(b) Unsolicited inbound civilian saucer-correspondence** (foreign-language, FBI in-house translated) | 220 | Apr 1950 | García Macías Spanish-language letter + Mexican press clipping + diagrams | Filed + translated |
| **(c) FBI-authored field investigation routed up to HQ** | 438 | May 1964 | 10-page Albuquerque report by SA Byrnes on Socorro NM Zamora landing | Investigated substantively + filed |
| **(d) NEW — Citizen political-tip → field-office indexing → HQ filing** | **449 (this serial)** | **Oct 1966** | **LA SAC routing memo + entire AFSCA July 1966 magazine** | **Indexed only — explicitly "no investigation conducted on captioned organization"** |

Channel (d) is structurally distinct from (a) and (b) in **two ways**: ^[inferred]

1. **The inbound trigger is not UAP-interest, it is political-tip-interest.** Cooper, an IRS employee, did not write because he found a saucer story noteworthy — he wrote because he believed the article *"expounded the CP line"*. The UAP material is **incidental to the FBI's filing interest**.
2. **The artifact is dual-classified.** It carries an **LA Class-100 file number** (`100-24442-40` — Class 100 is the FBI's domestic-security / counterintelligence prefix on which OUFORA's redirect was also filed via Detroit's `100-26505`) **alongside** its HQ Class-62 (admin misc) serialization (`62-HQ-83894-449`). ^[inferred] This is the **first dual-file-classification artifact in the corpus** — the same 18-page packet sits in both an LA-side counterintelligence-indexing file *and* the HQ-side UAP-topic accumulation file. ^[inferred]

The agency posture across N=4 is now **bimodal**: ^[inferred]
- **Passive accumulation** (220 + 403 + 449): no investigation, content filed, agency labour limited to translation [220] / clipping [403] / indexing-and-routing [449].
- **Active investigation** (438): substantive on-scene field-investigative product, distributed in 4 carbons to HQ.

The file's date span widens from ≥ 14 years to **≥ 16 years (Apr 1950 → Oct 1966)**. ^[inferred] (The terminal upper bound is still unknown; later serials may extend further. Serials 164 + sections 1–10 + sub_a remain queued.)

The **dual-file-classification** datapoint also re-frames a question the parent-hub flagged: 62-HQ-83894 is **not** an "isolated topic file" — at least one of its serials is **explicitly indexed under another FBI file** for institutional reasons orthogonal to the UAP topic. ^[inferred] The 17-serial wiki ingest series may surface additional dual-classified artifacts.

## Position in the FBI corpus

This artifact is the **second 1966 FBI item in the corpus** (after the Detroit OUFORA SAC redirect letter ^[extracted] in [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|file 100-26505 serial 6]]). Both 1966 items have the **same agency disposition**: no FBI investigation. But the entry-paths are categorically distinct: ^[inferred]

- **Detroit Oct 1966 OUFORA**: a civilian-research org wrote to the FBI **asking about UAP sightings** → SAC redirected the org to USAF Wright-Patterson. Civilian-research-org → FBI inquiry, USAF-redirect disposition.
- **LA Oct 1966 AFSCA**: a private citizen wrote to the FBI's Philadelphia division **alleging political subversion in a UAP magazine** → Philadelphia forwarded to LA → LA indexed under counterintelligence and forwarded to HQ. Citizen → FBI complaint, no-investigation-counterintelligence-index disposition.

The cross-era table now expands to **8 distinct recording instruments across 73 years**: ^[inferred]

The **LA field-office attribution** is **structurally novel** for the FBI corpus. ^[inferred] Prior FBI field-office work was Detroit (Krasuski, Weaver, OUFORA-redirect), Albuquerque (Byrnes Socorro), and modern-redacted (2023 FD-302s) — LA has not previously appeared. The corpus's FBI field-office spread is now **3 confirmed offices** (Detroit + Albuquerque + LA) plus modern-redacted.

The **two October 1966 same-month artifacts** (this LA SAC memo + the Detroit OUFORA SAC redirect on serial 6 of `100-26505`) form a **coincident-timing pair**. ^[inferred] The shared month is unlikely to be causal — these are different field offices, different correspondents, different dispositions, separated by ~2 weeks (no coordination signal in either artifact). ^[inferred] The pair does, however, document that **two independent civilian-research-adjacent items entered FBI channels in the same month** without triggering investigation in either field office. The 1966 disposition pattern is **redirect-or-index, not investigate** — consistent with the 1958 Weaver routing-to-USAF posture continuing through the 1960s outside the Director-driven (1957 Krasuski) and same-physical-location-and-time-accident (1964 Socorro) entry paths.
