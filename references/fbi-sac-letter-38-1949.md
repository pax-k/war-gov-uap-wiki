# SAC Letter No. 38, Series 1949 (25 March 1949)

A **Hoover-signed all-SAC directive** that **partially reactivates** the FBI's saucer information-gathering function ~17 months after [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 57]] (1 Oct 1947) terminated active FBI investigation. ^[extracted] The letter is the **operational hinge** between the cooperation-regime closure (Oct 1947 - Mar 1949) and the post-1949 reactive-tip-routing pattern visible in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|section 5]]'s 1949-1950 caseload. ^[inferred]

## Verbatim core text (page 124, paraphrased where OCR-ambiguous)

> Your attention is directed to Bureau Bulletin #57, Series 1947, dated October 1, 1947, relating to the discontinuance of investigation by this Bureau in matters concerning flying discs.
>
> For your confidential information, a reliable and confidential source has advised the Bureau that flying discs are believed to be man-made missiles rather than natural phenomenon. It has also been determined that for approximately the past four years the USSR has been engaged in experimentation on an unknown type of flying disc.
>
> The Department of the Air Force has furnished to the Bureau the attached memorandum classified "restricted" dated February 15, 1949, entitled "Unconventional Aircraft." This memorandum is being furnished to you in order that all agents assigned to your office can be informed of the type of information desired by the Air Force in this matter.
>
> As set forth in Bureau Bulletin #57, referred to above, no investigation should be conducted by your office relative to flying discs, however, the attached memorandum should be referred to in securing data from persons who desire to voluntarily furnish information to your office relating to flying discs.

The closing clause is the directive's **operational substance**: ^[inferred]

- **Reaffirm** that no active investigation will be conducted (preserves BB-57)
- **Reactivate** information-gathering at the field-office reception desk
- **Channel** the gathered data via the Cabell AIRMEM #4 EEI schema attached to the letter
- **Forward** the data to the Air Force as the operational customer

This is **passive reception with active intelligence-gathering schema** — distinct from BB-57's blanket disengagement and from BB-42's joint-investigation arrangement. ^[inferred]

## The "reliable and confidential source"

The SAC Letter does not name the source. The Fletcher → Ladd memo of 14 Mar 1949 ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-4|page 117]]) — the document drafted by H. B. Fletcher to obtain Executive Conference approval for the SAC Letter — names: ^[extracted]

> Colonel C. D. Gasser, Resident Engineer, Air Materiel Command, Nuclear Energy for the Propulsion of Aircraft Research, Oak Ridge, Tennessee, has recently and confidentially advised the Bureau that flying discs are believed by the Air Force to be man-made missiles rather than some natural phenomenon and that as much as four years ago it was learned that some type of flying discs were being experimented upon by the Russians. It was further determined from Colonel Gasser that most all of the flying discs seen by persons in the United States approached this country from a northerly direction and returned in the same direction, indicating the strong possibility that they are coming from Russia.

See [[entities/c-d-gasser]] for the source's full institutional context. The "reliable and confidential source" in the SAC Letter is **[[entities/c-d-gasser|Col C. D. Gasser]]**. ^[extracted]

The substantive Gasser information passed to the field through SAC Letter No. 38 is therefore: ^[extracted]

1. **Air Force assessment**: discs are man-made missiles, not natural phenomena.
2. **Origin attribution**: USSR experimentation, ≥4 years duration.
3. **Trajectory pattern**: northerly approach, returns in same direction (suggesting trans-polar route from Russia).

The "Russian missile" hypothesis is **the institutional posture transmitted to all FBI field offices in March 1949** — see [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default]] for how this comports with the prior Bureau cover-story framing. The hypothesis is **structurally distinct** from the [[concepts/civilian-saucer-hypothesis-1947|civilian "Russian saucer" hypothesis]] documented in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-1|section 1]] mass-correspondence: the Mar 1949 institutional version originates from AMC Engineering Field Office. ^[inferred]

## How the directive operated in practice

Section 4 contains **multiple post-25-Mar-1949 examples** of the new pattern: ^[extracted]

| Date | Document | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 22 Apr 1949 | SAC Little Rock → Director memo (page 132) | Wade H. Harrison Fort Smith AR sighting; civilian witness narrative; **forwarded to HQ for AF retention**, no FBI investigation |
| 2 May 1949 | Hoover → AF DSI memo (page 138) | Forwards Atkins/De Jarnett Griffith Park 1 Apr 1949 sighting tip; *"no investigation is being conducted relative thereto by this Bureau"* — characteristic SAC Letter 38 pattern ^[extracted] |
| 10 May 1949 | Hoover → AF DSI memo (page 150) | Forwards Mrs Frank Gardner letter dated 29 Apr 1949 to AF; standard form |

The pattern is uniform: civilian or third-party tip arrives at HQ → Hoover or Liaison Section forwards it to AF DSI Pentagon "for any action you may deem advisable" → no FBI investigation. ^[extracted] This is the operational footprint of SAC Letter No. 38 visible in the file: routing-only.

## Cabell AIRMEM #4 attached

The "memorandum dated February 15, 1949 captioned 'Unconventional Aircraft'" attached to SAC Letter No. 38 is **[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|Cabell AIRMEM #4]]** — the same directive separately ingested as serial 164 (17 OCR-identical carbons distributed to FBI Director by HQ-USAF Directorate of Intelligence). The attachment to SAC Letter No. 38 is **the FBI's internal redistribution channel** for that AF directive. ^[inferred] All FBI field offices received Cabell AIRMEM #4 via this attachment to SAC Letter No. 38, ~6 weeks after Cabell's original 15 Feb 1949 issuance. ^[inferred]

The arc shows BB-57's "termination" was **not** absolute — the doctrinal posture between Oct 1947 and Mar 1949 is **best described as active disengagement**, between Mar 1949 and Dec 1949 as **passive intelligence-feeding**, and between Dec 1949 and Mar 1950 as **dormant routing-only**. ^[inferred]

## Why this matters

SAC Letter No. 38 is the **proximate documentary anchor** for: ^[inferred]

1. **The "Russian missile" hypothesis being formally transmitted to all FBI field offices** as the AF's institutional posture — antedating the [[references/fbi-hottel-memo-1950-03-22|Hottel memo (Mar 1950)]] by ~12 months.
2. **The post-BB-57 institutional ambiguity** of FBI saucer policy — the Bureau is simultaneously *not investigating* and *gathering data via the field*. This nonlinearity is what drives the "termination → resistance → selectivity → partial reactivation" 4-beat narrative across section 4.
3. **The dependency between FBI HQ Class-62 file 62-HQ-83894 and AF directive AIRMEM #4** — the AF's reporting schema gets propagated to the FBI's UAP-document-accumulation machinery via this letter.
4. **The continuity between BB-57 and the 1950 Boggs-Hearn confirmation** — by Dec 1949, the SAC Letter 38 channel is the only active FBI-side instrument for saucer matters, and the [[concepts/project-grudge|AF-side termination]] of Dec 1949 leaves it without operational customer.
