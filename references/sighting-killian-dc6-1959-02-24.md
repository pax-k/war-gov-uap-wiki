# Sighting — Killian AAL DC-6 Newark→Detroit (24 Feb 1959)

On the **non-stop AAL DC-6 flight from Newark NJ to Detroit MI on the night of 24 Feb 1959**, AAL Capt **Peter Killian** of Syosset NY (a 15-year-veteran passenger-plane pilot), co-pilot **John Dee** of Nyack NY, the cabin crew, and **35 passengers** sighted **three mysterious objects that "looked like shining saucers"** appearing to accompany the plane for **45 minutes**. ^[extracted]

| Pursuit duration | 45 minutes | extracted |
| Disappearance | Lost in haze when plane began descent for landing at Detroit Metropolitan Airport, while plane was over Cleveland OH | extracted |
| Killian's pilot statement | *"I have never seen anything like it before."* | extracted |

## Wiki primary-source position

This is the **newest sighting** in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-sub-a|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 sub-file A]] page 2 — an **AP wire dated 25 Feb 1959**, filed at FBI HQ on 0-19 form (Rev. 1-28-59) and routed to the standard 1959-period executive distribution: Tolson, Belmont, DeLoach, McGuire, Mohr, Parsons, Rosen, Tamm, Trotter, W.C. Sullivan, Tele Room, Holloman, Gandy. ^[extracted]

It is a **mid-late-1950s commercial-aviation mass-witness UAP event** with a uniquely large witness population (35 passengers + crew) for the wiki corpus. ^[inferred]

## Cross-corpus position

Killian's case sits between:

- **Earlier** [[references/sighting-pan-am-powell-white-1947-08-04|Pan Am Powell-White 4 Aug 1947]] (the wiki's earliest commercial-airline-pilot UAP encounter, single-pilot witness) and the **Pan Am Adams** Norfolk VA 8-saucer event (sub-A page 58, 1952).
- **Concurrent** with [[entities/nicap|NICAP]]'s 1957-59 publicity campaigns under [[entities/delmer-s-fahrney|Fahrney]] + [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Keyhoe]]; the case is the kind of credentialed-pilot-large-witness-population event NICAP would foreground. ^[inferred]
- **Predates** the canonical 1960s commercial-aviation UAP cases ([[references/sighting-pan-am-powell-white-1947-08-04|Pan Am-class events]] mostly) and the [[entities/aaro|AARO]]-era (post-2022) corpus.

The 35-passenger witness population would be **very hard to dismiss as misidentification** by the standard AF prosaic-resolution apparatus — possibly a reason the case was clipped and filed at FBI HQ. ^[inferred]
