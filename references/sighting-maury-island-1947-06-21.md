# Sighting — Maury Island WA (21 June 1947)

The 21 June 1947 alleged sighting of six saucer-shaped objects over **Maury Island in Puget Sound, WA**, by **Harold A. Dahl** and **Fred L. Crisman** of Tacoma WA, captured in the FBI corpus across **two serials** of section 1 of file [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894]]: ^[extracted]

- **Serial 32 — 14 Aug 1947 FBI Seattle → Director teletype**: Dahl's confession-of-prospective-hoax (will say it was a hoax if questioned) + AAF intelligence framing of Dahl/Crisman as **publicity-fraud actors** trying to *"make a profitable deal with Fantasy Magazine, Chicago"*. ^[extracted]
- **Serial 49 — 5 Aug 1947 FBI Portland → Director URGENT teletype** (Bobbitt, with cc to Mr. Ladd): the **Davidson/Brown Kelso WA plane crash thread**. ^[extracted]

## What the FBI corpus establishes

The FBI's evidentiary trail captures Maury Island **as a publicity hoax** — but the two AAF investigators dispatched to interview Dahl/Crisman (Capt William L. Davidson and Lt Frank M. Brown of 4th AAF Headquarters Hamilton Field) **died in the return trip B-25 crash at Kelso WA on 1 August 1947**, generating a separate evidentiary thread. ^[extracted]

| Aspect | Source-claim | Status per FBI corpus |
|---|---|---|
| **Original sighting** | 6 saucer-shaped objects, one ejecting "white metal" + "lava-like slag" onto Dahl's salvage boat near Maury Island | extracted from press, not separately verified by FBI ^[inferred] |
| **Physical material** | "Slag" allegedly fell on Dahl's boat, injured his son, killed his dog; Dahl filled boxes with it | analysis identifies fragments as **Tacoma slag-mill product** ^[extracted] |
| **AAF interview** | Capt Davidson + Lt Brown interview Dahl/Crisman 27 Jul 1947 in Tacoma | per Leverett G. Richards, *Oregonian* aviation editor, who tracks Davidson/Brown's wider Pacific-NW interview tour ^[extracted] |
| **B-25 crash** | 1 Aug 1947, Kelso WA — both AAF investigators killed | extracted from FBI Portland teletype to Director ^[extracted] |
| **Cargo allegation** | Tacoma News Tribune (via UP wire) reports the plane was carrying **disc fragments from Maury Island** | extracted; **Dahl denies the fragments came from a disc** ^[extracted] |
| **FBI Seattle conclusion** | Dahl will say "it was a hoax if questioned" but **did not admit hoax to AAF**; "PROBABLY CHRISMAN OR DAHL MADE THE ANONYMOUS PHONE CALL IN THE HOPE OF BUILDING UP THEIR STORY THROUGH PUBLICITY" | ^[extracted] |
| **Final disposition** | *"DAHL AND CHRISMAN WILL NOT BE REINTERVIEWED UNLESS ADVISED TO THE CONTRARY BY THE BUREAU"* (FBI Seattle, 14 Aug 1947) | ^[extracted] |

## The Davidson / Brown Kelso crash thread

Section 1 captures the post-crash investigation through three documents: ^[extracted]

- **5 Aug 1947 FBI Portland → Director URGENT** (Bobbitt) — extracts Maj Gen Twining's by-phone-from-Albuquerque view that the AAF investigation was *"to wash out the disc reports, since they are definitely not of AAF origin"*. ^[extracted]
- **6 Aug 1947 E. G. Fitch → D. M. Ladd** memo — Lt Col C. P. Martin AAF Intelligence informs Reynolds the dead CIC Agent was on a **"top secret mission"**, possibly *en route to or from interview with Mr. Arnold* (i.e. **[[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Kenneth Arnold]]**). Martin's view: *"no flying saucers have been recovered but it was merely an attempt to reinterview an individual who previously had reported seeing one."* ^[extracted]
- **9 Aug 1947 FBI San Francisco → Director URGENT** (Kimball) — Lt Col Donald C. Springer (G-2 SF) *"has no further information"* and the matter is being handled at FBI Seattle / Tacoma. ^[extracted]

The chain links **Maury Island → Kenneth Arnold → Mt Rainier sighting** — an evidentiary loop that the AAF's senior leadership (Twining, Martin) framed as a **"wash out"** investigation, not a substantive recovery. ^[extracted]

## Cross-event linkage

The same 4th AAF investigative team (Davidson + Brown) was tracking **multiple Pacific-NW disc reporters** in late July 1947 per Leverett Richards' extracts: ^[extracted]

- **27 Jul 1947 Portland**: interview Dick Rankin (10 disc formation over Bakersfield, 14 Jun 1947).
- **Same trip**: interviews with Kenneth Arnold (Boise, ID), Capt E. J. Smith + co-pilot Ralph Stevens (United Airlines), Dave Johnson (Idaho Statesman aviation editor) — i.e. **the four most credible early-wave pilot-witnesses** were interviewed by the same AAF team that subsequently met Dahl/Crisman at Maury Island and then died in the Kelso crash. ^[inferred]

This sequencing creates a structural ambiguity in the early FBI / AAF investigative trail: **the same investigators who took the wave's most-credible pilot-witnesses seriously enough to interview them in person** were also routed through the **publicity-fraud Maury Island case** — and the Kelso crash terminated their thread before any synthesis report. ^[inferred]

## Position in corpus

Maury Island is the **first FBI-and-AAF investigated case in the corpus that produced casualties**. ^[inferred] It also generates the corpus's **first "wash out" framing** of UAP investigation — the AAF leadership's public stance that disc reports are *"definitely not of AAF origin"* and the role of investigation is to clear the field of speculation, not to engage substantive evidentiary work. ^[extracted] This is structurally distinct from the AAF's later **Project SIGN / GRUDGE** posture (where investigation produces substantive analytical reports per [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|the 10-element template]]). ^[inferred]
