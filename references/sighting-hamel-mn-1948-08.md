# Sighting — Hamel Minnesota Landing (11 Aug 1948)

A **daylight close-encounter physical-trace landing event** on **11 August 1948 between 12:00 and 12:15 CST**, **R/R 1, Hamel, Minnesota**, filed as **Project SIGN Incident 162** in [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-101-172|the 101–172 incident summary bundle]]. The witnesses were **Jerome Leuer (age 10) and Benne (Benno) Leuer (age 8)**, sons of farmer Philip Leuer. ^[extracted]

This is the **corpus's first 1948 physical-trace landing event** — agent-corroborated ground depression with leveled rocks, plus repeated whistling acoustics during a stop-and-restart sequence. ^[inferred]

## What the witnesses reported

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date / time | 11 Aug 1948, 12:00–12:15 CST |
| Location | R/R 1, Hamel MN (back yard) |
| Number of objects | 1 |
| Distance from observer | A matter of a few feet |
| Altitude | **Rested on ground**, then ascended to 30 ft |
| Direction of departure | Northeast |
| Tactics | Spun once on landing, made whistling noise, shot up 20 ft, halted, whistled again, climbed to 30 ft, **maneuvered around tree branches and telephone wires**, departed NE |
| Sound | **Whistling like a steam whistle** (twice) + **slight clanking on ground impact like metal on metal** |
| Size | 1 ft thick × 2 ft wide |
| Color | **Dull gray** |
| Shape | Round |
| Apparent construction | **Metallic** — no windows, wires, or visible appurtenances |
| Manner of departure | Straight up to 30 ft, then NE |

## Physical-trace signature

The 25-field intake form is followed by a narrative and **AGENTS NOTES** section. The agents note:

> "The spot where the alleged 'flying saucer' had landed was approximately 2 feet in diameter and appeared as though some heavy object had landed there or had been set down — as the ground was dented and protruding rocks had been leveled. The spot was covered by a washtub." ^[extracted]

Mr E.R. Sheridan (Postmaster, Hamel MN) advised the agents that he had **no reason to doubt the story of the boys or of Mr and Mrs Leuer** — Sheridan was the original notifier of "the proper authorities". ^[extracted]

The **physical trace** therefore has three distinct evidentiary components:

1. **Agent-observed 2-ft ground depression** — direct evaluator observation, not witness-claim. ^[extracted]
2. **Leveled protruding rocks** at the trace site — interpretation by the agent that the rocks were "leveled" rather than displaced. ^[inferred]
3. **The Leuers had covered the trace with a washtub** to preserve it for the agent visit. ^[extracted]

This is a stronger physical-trace evidentiary chain than most pre-1980 trace cases in subsequent UAP historiography. ^[inferred]

## Acoustic signature

The "whistling like a steam whistle" + "slight clanking on ground impact like metal on metal" combination is **structurally unique** in the 1948 SIGN corpus. The Inc 162 acoustic profile differs from:

- **The 1948 ball-of-fire / red-glow events** (e.g. Inc 147, 148, 153, 166), all of which report **no sound** — see [[concepts/balls-of-fire-red]].
- **The Inc 144 Chiles–Whitted event** (no sound).
- **The Inc 172 Gorman case** (no sound).
- **The Inc 168 Arnhem event** ("Same as V-2" — i.e. rocket exhaust + sonic-boom-class).
- **The Inc 145 Fielding Lake event** ("loud roaring sound that sharpened to a buzz" — Doppler-class sound profile).

The Hamel acoustic profile is **mechanical/operational**, not propulsive — like a relief valve venting steam. ^[inferred]

## Why the witness-credibility profile is unusual

The two children are the only direct visual witnesses. Two factors raise the credibility profile despite their age:

1. **Mrs Leuer reported the boys "were visibly frightened when they ran into the house and were afraid to tell what they had seen"** — affective trauma signature is consistent with the Sheriff/Bender/Postmaster-corroborated narrative arc, and inconsistent with confabulation. ^[inferred]
2. **The agent-observed physical trace** is independent of the boys' verbal account. ^[extracted]
3. **Postmaster Sheridan** — a federal civil-service witness — corroborated the Leuer family's report behavior on intake. ^[extracted]

The case is **uniquely valuable** for being a pre-1950 SIGN intake of a child-witness physical-trace landing event with civil-service second-line corroboration.

## Cross-references in the bundle

- **Inc 161 (~8 Jul 1948 McKeesport PA)** — adjacent in numbering — "big silver plate" 12–15" diameter that disappeared in cloud bank then reappeared on far side. Same general size class (12-15" Inc 161 vs 24" Inc 162) but the Hamel case is the only landing event.
- **Inc 169 (29 Aug 1948 Maplewood OH)** — adjacent witness profile (farmer) — large silver sphere unraveling silver substance + mid-air disintegration. Different morphology entirely; what's shared is the **rural Midwest farmer-witness intake channel** in late summer 1948.
- **Inc 162 + Inc 169 share an investigator**: Inc 169's narrative notes "while witness was talking to a man from this Command (MCIAXP) when an airplane chanced to fly overhead". MCIAXP is a **subsequent-to-Inc-213's-MCIAXO-3** routing line — implying late-1948 codification of the AMC-internal investigator routing prefix. ^[inferred] The Inc 162 narrative does not name a specific investigator office.

## Open questions

- The "covered by a washtub" detail — was a soil sample ever collected from the depression? Field 24 records "None" for sketches/photographs and the bundle does not contain follow-on lab-analysis correspondence. The contemporaneous SIGN apparatus did include a **Materials Lab** (visible at Inc 211 of the 173–233 bundle) but no Inc 162 referral exists in the OCR. ^[ambiguous]
- The "two boys… visibly frightened" detail — were the Leuer brothers ever interviewed separately by a follow-up evaluator? Project SIGN's standard practice elsewhere in the corpus is single intake. ^[inferred]
- The dimension 1 ft × 2 ft is **smaller than most subsequent close-encounter landing reports** (Hopkinsville KY 1955 reports 2-3 ft tall entities; Pascagoula 1973 reports a ~6-ft craft). The Hamel object is at the very small end of any landing-class case — consistent with a **drone- or probe-class object** rather than a craft-with-occupants. ^[inferred]
