# Sighting — Schmidt Kearney NB Contactee Event (5 Nov 1957)

On the **afternoon of 5 November 1957**, **Reinhold O. Schmidt**, ~50, **a Bakersfield, CA grain dealer** and former Nebraskan, claimed to have encountered a **translucent cigar-shaped space-ship** about **100 feet long, 30 feet wide, and 14 feet high** in a field near **Kearney, Nebraska**, whose crew of **four men + two women in business suits** had paralyzed him with a flashlight-like device, then spoke **High German** with one English-interpreting member, fixed wires and instruments aboard, and departed without explaining their purpose. ^[extracted]
