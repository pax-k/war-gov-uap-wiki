# FBI Detroit File 100-26505 — Krasuski Interview (Nov 1957) + OUFORA Inquiry (Oct 1966)

A **15-page declassified Detroit field office case file**, NW 90290 / DocId `34714592`, FOIA II screening 11-19-2025. The file holds two structurally distinct UAP-adjacent artifacts, **~9 years apart**, both anchored to the same case number (`100-26505`):

1. **November 1957 — Krasuski interview** (pages 1–13). A White-House-routed FBI Director URGENT teletype, the resulting in-person interview by SA Cassius Rathbun, an 11/7/57 AIRTEL back to the Director enclosing the Blank Memo, and ancillary records-check forms.
2. **October 1966 — OUFORA inquiry** (pages 14–15). A letter from Kerry Liesch (Director, [[entities/oklahoma-ufo-research-association|Oklahoma UFO Research Association]]) to FBI Detroit asking for early-1966 sighting information, and SAC Paul H. Stoddard's reply redirecting OUFORA to **USAF Wright-Patterson AFB**.

This is the cross-referenced file `100-26505` whose existence the [[references/fbi-detroit-100-18221-serial-844-1958|17 April 1958 Weaver memo]] noted. The 1958 Weaver serial pre-dates this file's `100-26505-3` AIRTEL by ~5 months — and the Weaver memo's "see also Nos." footer, recorded in retrospect (sub-stamp `5-11-85`), points back here.

**This file is now the earliest FBI-originated UAP-adjacent record in the corpus**, displacing the 1958 Weaver memo by ~5 months. ^[inferred]

## What Krasuski reported (interview, 7 Nov 1957)

**Witness identification.** **[[entities/wladyslaw-krasuski|Wladyslaw Krasuski]]** (aka Walter), residing 5457 Joseph (Jos.) Campau Avenue, Detroit, Michigan; employed since May 1951 at the **Gobel Brewery, Detroit**; arrived in the US at New York on 2 May 1951 via the *S.S. General Stewart* as a Displaced Person; alien registration number redacted under FOIA(b)(6). Brought from Poland to **Gut Alt Golssen, ~30 mi east of Berlin, Germany**, as a **Prisoner of War in May 1942** and held there until a few weeks after the end of WWII. Postwar: DP camps at Kork, Strasburg, Offenburg, Mülheim, and Freiburg; radio-technician school at Freiburg; ~1 year at a textile mill in Laurachbaden. Married Joann Wisniewski in Detroit, 1952; four small children at the time of interview. SA Rathbun explicitly observed "no indication of irrational or otherwise abnormal behavior."

**Why he wrote.** A "news report of mysterious vehicle in Texas causing engines to stall" prompted Krasuski to communicate with the US government concerning a similar phenomenon he had observed in 1944. The "rocket in Texas" almost certainly refers to the **Levelland, TX, 2–3 November 1957 vehicle-engine-stall sightings**, then breaking on US news; Krasuski mentions hearing about it on the **Polish radio program**. ^[inferred] He addressed his letter to Cutler rather than directly to Eisenhower after seeing Cutler's photo and presidential-office identification in a local paper.

**The 1944 observation** — see the consolidated narrative at [[references/sighting-gut-alt-golssen-1944]]. Summary as recorded by SA Rathbun:

- Setting — wartime forced labor, swamp area "a short distance north of Gut Alt Golssen", work crew of 16–18 Russian/French/Polish POWs cutting "hay" under one German civilian and "SS" guards.
- **First episode**: tractor engine stalled on a swamp road; high-pitched whine "similar to that produced by a large electric generator" heard; an SS guard appeared, spoke briefly with the tractor driver; after 5–10 minutes the noise stopped and the tractor restarted normally.
- **Second episode (~3 hours later, same swamp area)**: hidden by trees, Krasuski observed a **circular tarpaulin-walled enclosure ~100–150 yards in diameter, ~50 ft high**. From inside the enclosure, a **circular vehicle ~75–100 yards diameter, ~14 ft tall** rose vertically clear of the wall, then moved horizontally a short distance out of sight. **Top and bottom 5–6 ft sections were stationary dark gray; the middle ~3 ft section was a continuous blur, "similar to an aeroplane propeller"** but extending around the full circumference. Sound similar to but lower-pitched than the earlier whine. Tractor engine stalled again on this occasion until the noise stopped.
- **Cabling**: uninsulated metal (possibly copper) cables 1.5–2 inches in diameter were observed on/under the swamp surface, in places under water, running between the enclosure and a small concrete column-like structure between road and enclosure.
- **Postwar**: Krasuski revisited the area shortly after WWII ended and found the cables removed; the previous site of the concrete column and the enclosure was under water.
- **Co-witnesses**: only [[entities/franciszek-grabowski|Franciszek Grabowski]] (no address known, ~50 years old in 1944) is recalled by name; Krasuski had had no contact since 1945 and presumed Grabowski had returned to Poland.

**Routing detail (1957 thread)**:

- Citizen letter → Cutler (White House special assistant) → forwarded to FBI Director.
- Director URGENT teletype → SAC Detroit, ordering immediate interview ("BUFILES REFLECT NO INFORMATION RE KRASUSKI. SUTEL.").
- SA Rathbun conducts in-person interview the next day (7 Nov 1957) at Krasuski's residence/work.
- AIRTEL back to Director with Blank Memo enclosed, 5 copies for the Bureau, 1 retained Detroit.
- Detroit credit / records check via the **Mich. Merchants Credit Association** (Manager William Recknagel reported only routine 1956–57 credit inquiries on "Walter Krasuski").
- Detroit indices showed no prior record on Krasuski or his wife.

**Disposition**: the file holds no further serials beyond `-4` in this tranche. The interview did not produce a substantive USAF / Project BLUE BOOK referral on the artifact level (the file was classified `IS-X`, not `IR-X`). ^[inferred]

| Reply substance | "this office does not have the information you have requested. It is possible that the United States Air Force, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Dayton, Ohio, may be the proper agency to contact." |
| File serial | `100-26505-6` (FBI's reply) |
| Stamp | "ALL INFORMATION CONTAINED HEREIN IS UNCLASSIFIED — DATE 5/15/83 — Searched / Serialized / Indexed / Filed" |

The 1966 sub-thread establishes that **Detroit's standing disposition for UFO inquiries from civilian research organizations was the same as for citizen phone-ins**: redirect to **USAF Wright-Patterson** (i.e., the institutional home of [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] and its successor [[entities/air-materiel-command|AMC]] / Project BLUE BOOK). ^[inferred] This duplicates the [[references/fbi-detroit-100-18221-serial-844-1958|1958 Weaver]] disposition pattern ("Advise proper air force authorities"), now extended to **organization-level inquiries** as well as citizen-level phone-ins. ^[inferred]

The OUFORA inquiry is the **wiki's first record of a civilian UFO research organization formally approaching the FBI**. ^[inferred]

## Cross-file relationship to 100-18221 (Weaver, 1958)

The two Detroit files share an institutional context but **predate-vs-postdate-each-other in opposite directions** depending on which document is consulted:

- **100-26505 (this file)** — opened on or about 6–7 Nov 1957 with Krasuski's case. ~5 months **earlier** than the 100-18221 Weaver memo of 17 Apr 1958.
- **100-18221** — Weaver was filed as **serial 844** in that file, implying ≥843 prior items by 17 Apr 1958. So 100-18221 itself was opened **earlier than 1957** as a broader/standing case file (its `844`th item is the Weaver UFO call).

The 1958 Weaver memo's "see also" cross-reference to 100-26505 thus pointed back at this **already-existing UFO-adjacent file** in the same field office. The Weaver memo's `5-11-85` retrospective stamp ("see also Nos. — Charest, Michael R., 245,974, 5-11-85") is from a 1985 NARA / FOIA-era cross-indexing, not from 1958 itself. ^[inferred]

**Two distinct FBI routing patterns** are now visible side-by-side in Detroit, ~5 months apart:

| Pattern | Trigger | Director-level involvement | Recording instrument | FBI substantive engagement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **WH-routed citizen letter (1957 Krasuski)** | citizen → White House → FBI Director URGENT teletype | **Yes** — Director ordered immediate interview | Blank Memo (3-page narrative, witness interview) + AIRTEL | **Sit-down interview** by SA, records check, no USAF referral on this artifact |
| **Citizen phone-in (1958 Weaver)** | citizen → FBI Detroit phone | No — local SA → SAC only | SF-64 Office Memorandum (routing instrument) | **None** — disposition "Advise proper air force authorities" |

The 1957 case is **substantively investigated by the FBI**; the 1958 case is **routed to USAF**. Same field office, same `100-` series, ~5 months apart. The differentiator is the **letter-to-the-White-House** entry path: when the President's special assistant forwards an inquiry to the Bureau, the Director responds with an URGENT teletype that compels a real interview. ^[inferred]

## What this file establishes / does not establish

**Establishes:**

- A **1957 White-House-routed citizen letter** can compel the FBI Director to URGENT-teletype a field office for immediate interview on a UFO-adjacent matter — and that Detroit performed the interview the next day with no apparent objection. ^[inferred]
- The FBI's 1957 administrative response to a Director-routed letter was **substantive** (interview, records check, AIRTEL with enclosed memo to the Bureau) — distinct from the 1958 phone-in routing-only response.
- **Engine-stall phenomenology** ([[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon]]) was already a triggering signal in late-1957 popular consciousness, via the **Levelland TX 2–3 Nov 1957** events. ^[inferred] Krasuski's letter explicitly invokes the Texas case as the trigger for reporting his own 1944 experience.
- The FBI Detroit standing disposition for UFO-related external inquiries (citizen phone-ins, civilian research orgs) was **redirect to USAF Wright-Patterson** by at least 1958–1966.
- The "**100-26505**" cross-reference on the 1958 Weaver memo was **not** an unrelated person-of-interest file. It was a **prior UFO-adjacent case file** in the same office. ^[inferred]

**Does not establish:**

- Whether USAF / Project BLUE BOOK opened a parallel case on Krasuski's account. ^[open]
- Whether **Cutler / White House** sent any other UFO-adjacent letters to the Bureau in this era. ^[inferred]
- Anything about the 1944 Gut Alt Golssen object's actual nature. The witness account is a **single, delayed-13-years recollection** without any corroboration on the artifact level (the only named co-witness, Franciszek Grabowski, was last seen by Krasuski in 1945 and presumed returned to Poland).
- Whether 100-26505 is **subject-named** ("UFO" / "IS-X") or is a person-named file on Krasuski himself. The visible `100-26505-1/4` suggests an item-numbered case file; the `Charest, Michael R. — 245,974 — 5-11-85` cross-index entry suggests later cross-references to other cases. ^[ambiguous]

## Provenance pitfalls

- Page 4 of the OCR'd file contains a handwritten note dated `WA 27516 - 11/7/59`. The rest of the 1957 thread is internally dated 11/7/57. This is almost certainly an OCR misread of `11/7/57` (pen-stroke ambiguity between "7" and "9"). ^[ambiguous]
- The endorsement on the URGENT teletype reads `Lt. Col (Bermnie) E. Prater advised…` on the **1958 Weaver memo** — that is in the sister file 100-18221. The 1957 Krasuski thread has its own AF coordination ("Major Golser, 50' high"-type handwritten notes on page 4) that are too thin to interpret. ^[ambiguous]
- The 1957 records-request form (FD-125) shows "Race W; Sex Male/Female checkbox both empty; Birth date / Birthplace blank" — i.e., a half-completed routing form. Reflects FBI procedural norms more than Krasuski biography.

## HQ-side cross-filing — 62-HQ-83894 serials 382-384 (Nov 1957) — added in section 8 batch

The wiki now also holds the **HQ-side filing** of the same case, captured in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-8|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 8]] serials 382-384 (this batch, 4-7 Nov 1957). ^[extracted] The HQ-side adds: ^[inferred]

- **5 Nov 1957 NSC memorandum from J. Patrick Coyne to A. H. Belmont (FBI Asst Director)** enclosing Krasuski's letter to Robert Cutler, with the language *"It would be appreciated if the Bureau, at its convenience, would arrange for a representative to interview Mr. Kiasuski."* ^[extracted] Coyne's ADDENDUM: *"I would appreciate the return of Mr. Kiasuski's letter when it has served your purpose."* ^[extracted] — confirming **the NSC retained the original** and the FBI worked from a copy.
- **The Director URGENT teletype as 62-HQ-83894-382** (6 Nov 1957) — the HQ-side serial number ties the case to the 62-HQ-83894 *UAP-topic* file, in addition to the Detroit-side `100-26505` *Domestic Security IS-X* file.
- **Detroit AIRTEL serial 384** (7 Nov 1957) — file references SAC Detroit `(100-20505)` ^[ambiguous] — likely OCR ambiguity for the existing `100-26505` (third-digit 0/6 OCR drift), since the substance is identical (same date, same SA Rathbun, same Blank Memo).
- **15 Nov 1957 Hoover → Coyne formal NSC reply with inter-agency cc**: State (Office of Security); CIA (Deputy Director Plans); Army G-2 (Chief Security Division); Director of Naval Intelligence; OSI/Air Force; Immigration and Naturalization Service. ^[extracted] **6 inter-agency security recipients** — i.e., full IC-side distribution.

This is the **first NSC-routed UAP case** in the FBI corpus ^[inferred] — establishing that **a White House Special Assistant memo to the FBI Asst Director can compel an URGENT teletype to a SAC**, the same way the Detroit-side anchor noted that a White-House-routed citizen letter compelled an URGENT teletype. The HQ-side filing adds **quantitative scope** (6 inter-agency cc recipients) the Detroit-side filing did not preserve.
