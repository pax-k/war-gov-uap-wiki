# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Serial 164 (Cabell AIRMEM #4 'Unconventional Aircraft', 15 Feb 1949 — 17 OCR-identical carbon copies)

A **137-page Mistral-OCR'd declassified artifact** from the **FBI Central Records Center / FBI HQ**. Filed at **Class 0062 / Case 83894 / Sub —/ Vol 1 / Serial 164 ONLY** with secondary marker **62-HQ-83894-EBF 164 EBF** (cover slip) and document-control number **C9-4892,AF** (memorandum body). FOIPA reference **# 1142292** ^[ambiguous] — the same case-number that appeared on [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-403|serial 403]] under the OCR'd label *"DIPA"*; **the FOIPA/DIPA acronym distinction may be an OCR ambiguity** rather than a substantive difference, with `1142292` being the **shared FOIPA case number** across multiple serials in this file. ^[inferred] *DO NOT DESTROY* stamp; declassification authority *"derived from the FBI Automatic Declassification Guide, issued May 24, 2007"*. ^[extracted]

This is the **fifth artifact ingested in the planned 17-serial 62-HQ-83894 wiki series** (after [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-220|220]], [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-403|403]], [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438|438]], [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449|449]]). At **15 February 1949**, **serial 164 displaces serial 220 (Apr 1950) as the earliest-dated artifact in the entire FBI-corpus** ^[inferred] — the wiki's FBI-corpus floor moves back **~14 months** to 15 Feb 1949. The parent-file hub is [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file]].

## What's in the artifact

The 137 OCR'd pages decompose into **two documentary layers**:

1. **Page 1 (artifact index 0) — FBI Records Center cover slip.** Carries the Class–Case–Sub–Vol–Serial filing block (`0062 83894 — 1 — 164 ONLY`), the secondary marker `62-HQ-83894-EBF 164 EBF`, FOIPA reference `1142292`, the *DO NOT DESTROY* stamp, the OCR'd processing tag *"8/11/1274166"*, the *"ENCL BEHIND FILE 62-HQ-83894 / Serials EBF 164"* notation, and the FBI 2007 Automatic Declassification Guide note. ^[extracted] The processing-tag prefix **`8/11/12...`** matches the Aug-2012 prefix on serials 220, 438, and 449 — placing serial 164 in the **same FOIPA processing tranche** as those three. ^[inferred] (Serial 403 carries a Sep-2022 `9/18/22...` prefix, distinguishing it as a separate DIPA tranche.)

2. **Pages 2–137 (artifact indices 1–136) — 17 OCR-identical carbon copies of the same 8-page document**: **AIR INTELLIGENCE REQUIREMENTS MEMORANDUM NUMBER 4** ("AIRMEM #4"), subject **"UNCONVENTIONAL AIRCRAFT"**, issued **15 February 1949** by **DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE / HEADQUARTERS UNITED STATES AIR FORCE / DIRECTORATE OF INTELLIGENCE / WASHINGTON 25, D. C.** Signed **C. P. CABELL, Major General, USAF, Director of Intelligence, Office of Deputy Chief of Staff, Operations**. ^[extracted] Each 8-page copy carries the document-control number **C9-4892,AF**, the **62-83894-164 ENCLOSURE** stamp on its first page, and *RESTRICTED* classification on every page (a declassification artifact). The first copy uniquely carries the additional footer *"additional copies behind file"* — the structural marker explaining why the artifact totals **17 × 8 = 136 + 1 cover = 137 pages**. ^[inferred] The remaining 16 copies are byte-equivalent (modulo trivial OCR drift such as `C9-4892,AF` vs `C9-4892.AF` and one page carrying the OCR'd file marker as `65-83894-164` vs `62-83894-164`). ^[extracted] **Whether each carbon was directed to a specific intra-FBI component (Director / DCI-via-courtesy / DI / SOG / etc.) or all 17 were retained as bulk reserve at HQ Records Center is not visible in the OCR** ^[ambiguous] — there are no per-copy distribution stamps, routing slips, or initials.

## What AIRMEM #4 is

A standing **HQ-USAF Directorate-of-Intelligence directive** that *replaces the residual Army-side intake doctrine* for sightings of unconventional aircraft / "Flying Discs" with a unified **Air Force** instrument. Its rescission clause is the load-bearing institutional move: it **supersedes**:

- **Department of the Army Collection Memorandum Number 7**, dated 21 January 1948
- Letter **CSGID 425.1**, dated 25 March 1948

— *"both subject as above, which have been transferred to Air Force agencies for action."* ^[extracted] The phrasing **"have been transferred"** in the past perfect documents the **completed Army → USAF doctrinal handover** for the disc-reporting requirement, ~18 months after the [[entities/usaf|USAF]] became an independent service (18 Sep 1947). ^[inferred]

AIRMEM #4 is **not in the supersession lineage of [[concepts/fsr-200-4-reporting-regulation|FSR 200-4 (2 Nov 1948)]]** — the two instruments operate on **different command channels**:

- **AIRMEM #4** is an **HQ USAF Directorate of Intelligence** memorandum, addressed to *Major Air Commands (ZI and overseas)*, *all US Air Attaches*, *Director of Central Intelligence*, *State / GSUSA / ONI / USCG / FBI*. Its purpose is to **enunciate Air Force intelligence requirements** and direct routing through the AFOIR / D/I channel.
- **FSR 200-4** is a **Flight Service Regulation** binding **Flight Service Centers** (Air Transport Command / [[entities/military-air-transport-service|MATS]] component) to file sighting reports under their own regulatory hierarchy. ^[extracted from existing [[concepts/fsr-200-4-reporting-regulation]] page]

The two instruments **co-exist** through 1949 and converge on the same destination — **Commanding General, [[entities/air-materiel-command|Air Materiel Command]], Wright-Patterson AFB, Attn: MCIAXO-3** — but they enter through different chains of command. ^[inferred] The 1949 sighting reports preserved in [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|NARA box 186 file 319.1]] all cite the **6 Feb 1948 Cabell letter + FSR 200-4** combination — *not* AIRMEM #4 — confirming that AIRMEM #4 governed the **D/I / Air Attache / ZI Major Air Command intake stream** in parallel to the Flight-Service / MATS stream. ^[inferred]

## Structural content of AIRMEM #4

The memorandum has two parts.

### PART I — GENERAL

Sections 1 (Purpose), 2 (Rescission), 3 (Reporting). Section 3 distinguishes:

- **3.b. Major Air Commands (Overseas) and Air Attaches** — initial cabled reports go to *Director of Intelligence, HQ USAF* with mandatory cable phrase *"Pass to COMGENAMC WRIGHT-PATTERSON AFB, DAYTON, OHIO, ATTN: MCIAXO-3"*. ^[extracted]
- **3.c. Major Air Commands (ZI)** — reports go directly to *CG, AMC, Wright-Patterson, Attn: MCIAXO-3*, with copy to *Director of Intelligence, HQ USAF*; written reports on **AF Form 112**.
- **3.d. Non-Air Force Agencies** — addressees other than USAF are *requested* to forward direct to AMC.

The MCIAXO-3 attention line is **codified at the directive level here** — earlier the 26 Oct 1948 Hq USAF TWX had introduced it as a modifier to the [[entities/c-p-cabell|6 Feb 1948 Cabell letter]]; AIRMEM #4 promotes it to standing requirement. ^[inferred]

### PART II — REQUIREMENTS

A vastly expanded observational template — a **substantive evolution** of the 1948 [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element Cabell template]]. AIRMEM #4 specifies:

- **20 numbered fields on the object itself** (vs. 10 in the 1948 template): location/time, weather, ground/air/sea coordinates, number/formation, celestial-phenomena ruling-out, distance/elevation/altitude, time in sight, appearance (color/shape/construction/size), direction, tactics/maneuvers (vertical / horizontal / oscillating / fluttering / evasive / aggressive / erratic), evidence-of-exhaust (color/length/odor/evaporation/sound-correlation), effect-on-clouds, lights (reflected/luminous/blinking-with-speed), support (wings / aerodynamic-list / vertical-jet / rotating cylinder or cone / aerostatic-lift), propulsion (propeller/jet/rotor/aerodynamic-vanes/visible-exhaust), control-and-stability (fins / horizontal & vertical stabilizers), air-ducts, speed M.P.H., sound (continuous-whine-or-buzz / roar-whistle-whoosh / intermittent), manner-of-disappearance (explode-with-fragments / fade / disappear-behind-obstacle). ^[extracted]
- **A "Relative to the Observer" sub-block** with 10 numbered observer-attribute fields: name, address, occupation, place of business, pertinent hobbies (amateur astronomer / pilot / engineer + length of experience), ability to determine color/speed/size at distance, **reliability of observer** (with explicit sources: *neighbors, Police Dept, FBI records, employer*), notes on how attention was drawn (sound/motion/glint), **fatigue and duration of flight** (for airborne observers), witnesses (addresses/occupations/reliability), and **interrogator's comments on intelligence and character of person interrogated**. ^[extracted]
- **A "Relative to Radar Sightings" sub-block** with separate ground-radar and airborne-radar fields, including target-turn radius/speed measurement and "any separation of distant target into several targets upon approach". ^[extracted]
- **A "GENERAL" sub-block** of 10 numbered post-incident requirements: weather teletype sequences, winds-aloft, **local flight schedules of commercial / private / military aircraft (incl. Canadian)**, possible **testing-device releases by Ordnance, Navy, Air Force, Army, Weather Units, Research Organizations**, **soil samples** (within and without depression where object landed), **Geiger counter readings** for radioactivity comparing affected vs unaffected aircraft, **photographs (or original negatives)** with sketches of object / terrain / ground-contact-point / maneuvers / formation, **signed statement**, **fragments or physical evidence**, and any **radio antenna or projection**. ^[extracted]

The 1948 → 1949 expansion is a **3× enlargement** of the observational template (10 → ~50 fields across four sub-blocks). ^[inferred] Particularly notable additions:

- The *radioactivity* check (Geiger counter) is **explicit physical-trace methodology** — anticipating the on-scene physical-trace investigation later carried out by [[entities/d-arthur-byrnes-jr|SA Byrnes]] at Socorro NM in 1964. ^[inferred] Cf. [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438]].
- The *"reliability of observer"* sub-section explicitly names **FBI records** as a source — the only declared reverse-pull on the FBI in the 1948-49 protocol stack. ^[extracted] This is the institutional reason FBI maintained an indexed UAP-topic file: AIRMEM #4 itself names the Bureau as a witness-credibility check provider for the Air Force.
- The *"intelligence and character of person interrogated"* line institutionalizes the witness-character note that recurs in every substantive FBI artifact in the corpus (Rathbun on Krasuski 1957, Byrnes on Zamora 1964). ^[inferred]
- The *"foreign-balloon"* speculation seen on the [[entities/oklahoma-ufo-research-association|Moxon 8 Feb 49 San Andres Islands]] report (about Russian sloops Omar / Blesk) is **directly enabled** by AIRMEM #4 Part II GENERAL item 4 (testing-device releases). ^[inferred]

## Distribution list (verbatim)

The signing-page distribution list is **load-bearing**. ^[inferred] Verbatim:

- *Commanding Generals, Major Air Commands, ZI and Overseas*
- *All United States Air Attaches*
- **Director of Central Intelligence**
- *Special Assistant for Research and Intelligence, Department of State*
- *Director of Intelligence, GSUSA* (General Staff United States Army)
- *Chief of Naval Intelligence*
- *Commandant (INT), United States Coast Guard*
- **Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation**

^[extracted] (Final line OCR-truncated on the cover-list — *"Director of Inte"* — but reproduced in full in subsequent carbon copies.)

This is **why 17 carbon copies of AIRMEM #4 are filed at FBI HQ as serial 164**: the FBI Director was a **named addressee on the original distribution**, and HQ retained one or more carbons in the topic-anchored Class-62 file. ^[inferred] The 17-copy count is structurally distinct from the 4-carbon distribution on serial 438 (FBI-internal AMQ → HQ + 3 named addressees) — here the multiplicity is **inter-agency**, with each carbon plausibly intended for a different intra-FBI receiver (Director, DI, SOG, divisional files, field-office pre-positioning, etc.) although **no per-copy distribution markers are visible** in the OCR. ^[ambiguous]

## What 62-HQ-83894 contains here, summarized

- A **routing/cover slip** (FBI Records Center filing instrument, no FBI investigative content).
- **17 OCR-identical carbon copies** of an **inbound USAF intelligence directive** (AIRMEM #4), distributed by USAF D/I to the FBI Director under the directive's own distribution list.

There is **no FBI-authored substantive content** on serial 164. The FBI's only operational gesture on this serial is the **filing-and-retention** of an inbound directive that **named the Director as a distribution recipient and named "FBI records" as a witness-credibility source within its own Part II protocol**. ^[inferred]

## Channel discovery

Serial 164 introduces a **fifth structural intake channel** to the parent file's content-accumulation function:

> **(e) Inter-agency distribution of USAF intelligence directives to the FBI Director, retained at HQ in the topic-anchored Class-62 file.**

This is structurally distinct from the four channels surfaced by serials 220, 403, 438, 449:

| Channel | Anchor serial | Year | Provenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Clipped public publication | 403 | 1956 | external — book jacket |
| (b) Unsolicited inbound civilian correspondence (FBI-translated) | 220 | Apr 1950 | external — Mexican civilian |
| (c) FBI field-office investigative report routed to HQ | 438 | May 1964 | internal — Albuquerque |
| (d) Citizen political-tip → field-office indexing → HQ filing | 449 | Oct 1966 | external — Philadelphia citizen |
| **(e) Inter-agency distribution of USAF intelligence directive** | **164 (this batch)** | **15 Feb 1949** | **inter-agency — USAF D/I → FBI Director** |

Channel (e) is **doctrinal-foundation** in character — the FBI's UAP-topic file at HQ holds not just *content* about UAP but also the *intake doctrine that other agencies use*. ^[inferred] It establishes that the FBI's posture as a topic-anchored Class-62 accumulator was *recognized externally* by the Air Force well before the FBI generated its own first investigative product on the topic (1957 Krasuski Detroit / 1964 Byrnes Albuquerque). ^[inferred]
