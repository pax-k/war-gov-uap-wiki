# Fitch → Ladd Memo on Whedon Instrument and Operation Mogul (23 September 1947)

A **single-page Office Memorandum** from **E. G. Fitch (FBI Inspector)** to **Mr. Ladd**, subject *"INSTRUMENT FOUND ON FARM NEAR DANFORTH, ILLINOIS"*, dated **23 September 1947**, drafted by **`SWR:bmm`** (i.e., SA S. W. Reynolds), filed at **62-83894-122**, recorded at FBI **2 October 1947**, in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR page 161 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]]. ^[extracted] The memo is the **earliest by-name "Operation Mogul" reference in the wiki UAP corpus** ^[inferred] — invoking the program-name three times in the course of a four-stage investigation-and-denial chain that classifies a civilian-recovered instrument as a hoax. ^[extracted] See [[concepts/operation-mogul]] for the cross-cutting analysis.

## Verbatim — the memo body

The memo's body, transcribed in full from OCR page 161:

> Reference is made to a memorandum to Mr. Harbo from Mr. Baughman on the above-captioned matter, dated September 3, 1947. The reference memorandum indicates that the instrument has been examined by the Laboratory and the Laboratory had contacted **a Mrs. Whedon of the Army Engineers** and she indicated that the instrument had been **used by the Air Forces on tests which were classified as "Top Secret."**
>
> The memorandum indicates that **Special Agent S. W. Reynolds** of the Liaison Section contacted the Intelligence Division of the Air Forces and was advised that **Mrs. Whedon alluded that the instrument was used in "Operation Mogul."** The instrument was loaned to the Intelligence Division of the Air Forces who in turn forwarded it to **Wright Field**. The report was received from Wright Field indicating the **instrument had no connection with "Operation Mogul" or any other operation at Wright Field.** It was classified as a hoax in view of the apparent discrepancy between information developed from Mrs. Whedon and information received from Intelligence Division of the Air Forces that the Bureau might wish to pursue this matter further.
>
> **Mr. Zimmers** of the Technical Laboratory advised Mr. Reynolds of the Liaison Section that **Mrs. Whedon had told him that a Major Hopkins handled the Liaison in Washington for the tests in which this instrument was used.** **Lieutenant Colonel George Garrett** of the Intelligence Division of the Air Forces advised Special Agent Reynolds that he contacted Major Hopkins. He advised that **Major Hopkins has had a great deal of experience in radio and along technical lines.** Major Hopkins advised Colonel Garrett that **the instrument could not have been used in the "Operation Mogul."** Hopkins indicated that from the pictures and particles recovered, **it appeared to him to be a part or portions of an old-type radio loud speaker.**
>
> In view of the information received from Major Hopkins, Colonel Garrett stated that **he was at a loss as to how to explain the actions of Mrs. Whedon other than to say that she perhaps gave a false impression as to her knowledge of the instrument and the "Operation Mogul."** Garrett pointed out that in view of the report received from Wright Field, together with the statement made by Major Hopkins, he felt that there was sufficient evidence that **this instrument was not used in any War Department classified project and that in all probability it was just a hoax.**
>
> **RECOMMENDATION: It is recommended that this memorandum be forwarded to the Technical Laboratory for their information.** ^[extracted]

## The instrument — referencing back to section 4 / serial 130 inventory

The "instrument found on farm near Danforth, Illinois" is the **Whedon Watskeka IL package** described in the [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|serial 130 master inventory]]: ^[extracted]

- **Plaster-of-paris body**
- **Nathaniel Baldwin Inc Salt Lake City power microphone** (Pat May 10 1910 + Sept 14 1916)
- **Polymet filter condenser** (filter capacitor, made by Polymet Manufacturing Co. NY)
- **Bakelite-coil cylinders** (skillfully wrapped with thin enameled copper wire)
- **Magnetic-metal fragment** (metallic magnetic ring)
- Routed via Office Memo, Mr Moxom Regional Office No. 1 NY → Mr Wright WBI; brought to FBI Lab by Major Whedon's wife. ^[extracted]
- Farmer-says-burned-2½-ft-weeds-in-1½-ft-diam grass-circle on the Danforth IL farm. ^[extracted]

The patent dates (1910 and 1916) are **structural confirmation of Hopkins's "old-type radio loud speaker" determination** — a 1910s-era radio component cannot have been used in a 1947 top-secret AAF project regardless of program identity. ^[inferred]

## The four-stage attribution-and-denial chain

The memo records a structured **four-stage chain**: ^[extracted]

1. **Mrs. Whedon → FBI Lab (Mr. Zimmers)**: instrument was used in Top-Secret AAF tests known as "Operation Mogul"; Major Hopkins handled the Liaison in Washington.
2. **FBI Lab → Air Forces Intelligence Division → Wright Field T-2**: report from Wright Field denies any connection with Operation Mogul or any other Wright Field project.
3. **Lt Col [[entities/g-d-garrett|George Garrett]] → Major Hopkins (radio/technical expert) → Garrett → Reynolds**: Hopkins denies the instrument could have been used in Operation Mogul; identifies it as a part or portions of an old-type radio loudspeaker.
4. **Garrett → Reynolds (final disposition)**: Mrs. Whedon perhaps gave a false impression; the instrument was not used in any War Department classified project; in all probability just a hoax.

The **terminal hoax-classification** is institutionally definitive — Garrett, on the strength of Hopkins's denial and Wright Field's denial, treats the case as closed and recommends FBI Technical Laboratory be informed. ^[inferred]

The 23 Sep 1947 memo sits in a **structurally ambiguous position**: ^[inferred]

- Garrett **admits** to Reynolds (~22 Sep, privately) that real classified saucer-projects exist.
- Garrett **denies** to Reynolds (23 Sep, in writing) a specific Mogul-attribution for a specific instrument.

The denial may be: ^[ambiguous]

- **Genuine.** The Watskeka instrument is genuinely an old radio loudspeaker, Mrs. Whedon was genuinely confused, and Hopkins's read-in technical expertise correctly rules out Mogul-association.
- **Compartmentation-protective.** The Watskeka instrument may or may not have been Mogul-related, but admitting Mogul-association to FBI Lab would breach compartmentation; denial is procedurally required regardless of artifact identity.
- **Mixed.** The instrument is genuinely an antique radio component, but the *denial procedure* (Wright Field T-2 → Hopkins → Garrett) is a standardized AAF anti-attribution protocol triggered by any civilian-recovered-instrument-with-classified-program-naming.

The corpus does not at present resolve this ambiguity. ^[open]

The memo's wide internal distribution **at executive level** indicates the topic was **not compartmented within FBI HQ**. ^[inferred] This is structurally compatible with FBI HQ holding **outside-the-program** access to the project-name only — i.e., FBI HQ is not read into Mogul technical content, but its executive layer is aware that **Mogul exists as an AAF project name** that has been mentioned in the context of a recovered-instrument case. ^[inferred]
