# Sighting — Kansas City KS / Olathe KS (6 Jan 1950)

A civilian-witness sighting of two stationary aerial objects over Kansas City and Olathe, Kansas, on the night of **6 January 1950**, formally reported to [[entities/air-materiel-command|AMC]] by Lt Col Robert M. McLeod, Commanding, Lowry Flight Service Center (Lowry AFB, Denver), file **RY 319.1**, dated **9 January 1950**.

This is the **chronologically latest** report in [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|NARA box 186 file 319.1]] — and the first 1950-dated UAP item in the corpus.

## Witnesses

- **James F. Grey** — pilot, Bendix Aviation, Kansas City KS; resident of 6200 Hadley St., Raytown, MO.
- **Robert Van De Vyvere** — Bendix Aviation employee; resident of 5532 Raytown Road, MO.

Both witnesses are civilian employees of Bendix Aviation in Kansas City. The named pilot grants the report some additional internal credibility under the Cabell intake template's "ability to determine speed" criterion. ^[inferred]

## Object signature

| Field | Reported value |
|---|---|
| Number | Two |
| Shape | Spherical |
| Apparent size | "Old-fashioned street lights about two blocks distant" |
| Color | Brilliant white, emanating orange and red flashes |
| Altitude | 7,000 – 8,000 feet (estimated) |
| Sound | None |
| Exhaust | None apparent |
| Tactics | Motionless over Olathe for 10–15 minutes, then "moved off very fast" SW |
| Weather | Clear, 12 mi visibility |

## Routing

The report was given to **Fairfax AFB Operations** (Kansas City KS), transmitted to **Lowry Flight Service**, and forwarded to:

- CG, [[entities/air-materiel-command|Air Materiel Command]], Wright-Patterson AFB
- C/S, USAF, Washington DC, Attn: Director of Intelligence
- Comdr, [[entities/military-air-transport-service|MATS]], Washington DC, Attn: Chief, Intelligence Division
- CO, Flight Service, Washington DC

The report explicitly invokes [[concepts/fsr-200-4-reporting-regulation|FSR 200-4]] dated 2 November 1948 as the governing instrument. Sketches were promised as "enroute" to Lowry Flight Service for forwarding. ^[extracted]

## Significance

- **Two-object paired stationary configuration**: a morphology class that recurs in the modern corpus (cf. the 2025 [[references/sighting-helicopter-orb-encounter-2025|helicopter orb encounter]] with multiple co-orbiting bright orbs). The 1950 KS report is an early-cold-war analog. ^[inferred]
- **Motionless hover for 10–15 minutes** above an urban area, then high-speed egress, with **no sound and no exhaust trail**, is the signature class that AMC's 1948 conventional-explanation effort (the [[concepts/horten-flying-wing|Horten thesis]]) cannot accommodate. A flying-wing aircraft cannot hover. ^[inferred]
- **Pilot witness** ([[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element template]] item c) lifts this report above the typical civilian-only disc reports.
- Date proximity (6 Jan 1950) to the **11 Jan 1950 rescission** of the 26 Oct 1948 worldwide-flying-disc-reporting TWX (see [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949]]) is suggestive but the source does not state a causal link. ^[ambiguous]
