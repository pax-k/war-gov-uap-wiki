# DoW-UAP-D23 — Mission Report (UAE Al Dhafra, 24 Oct 2023, Misrep 9319618)

A **9-page Mistral-OCR'd artifact** (~13,607 bytes; SHA-256 `ccc2741021c79bd6…`) — the **28th artifact** in the [[entities/dow-uap-foia-release|DoW-UAP FOIA release series]] and the **THIRTEENTH FULL USMTF MISREP** in the dow-uap corpus. Misrep 9319618 — 24 Oct 2023 mission, first UAE artifact, first OP SPARTAN SHIELD attestation, first NAVCENT-return after the 2022 OIR/EUCOM shift, anchors **NEW Block E release framework at N=1**.

## Headline findings

**1. NEW BLOCK E release framework anchored at N=1.** d23 carries `USCENTCOM MDR 25-0104` + `Approved for Release to AARO - FOLIO+PAPER` + `09/12/25 000001 → 000008` (6-digit page-stamps). Distinct from all four prior blocks:

| Block | MDR | Release date | Sequence digit |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 26-0038 to 26-0046 (9-case) | 03/27/26 | 6-digit `000001` |
| B | 26-0019 (single) | 01/26/26 | 3-digit `001` |
| C | 26-0028 (single) | 03/16/26 | 6-digit `000001..N` |
| D | 25-0093 to 25-0099 (7-case) | 10/17/25 or 10/20/25 | 3-digit `001..N` and 6-digit `000001..N` mixed |
| **E** | **25-0104** (single) | **09/12/25** | **6-digit `000001..000008`** |

**Block E is the EARLIEST release event in corpus by 35 days** (09/12/25 vs Block D's 10/17/25). MDR case number 25-0104 falls AFTER Block D's 25-0093..25-0099 range — **MDR cases are NOT strictly sequential by event/release date**. Refines the case-allocation framework. ^[inferred] At N=18 release-block-testable: A 5 + B 3 + C 3 + D 6 + E 1.

**2. First OP SPARTAN SHIELD attestation.** US Army-led training and security cooperation operation in USCENTCOM AOR (Jordan + UAE focus; succeeded OP SPARTAN HARVEST). d23 anchors first dow-uap corpus reference to this operation. ^[inferred]

**3. NAVCENT return after 2022 OIR/EUCOM shift.** GENTEXT/ISR line: `1.4a CONDUCTED IMINT SUPPORTING NAVCENT ISO OP SPARTAN SHIELD IVO 40RBP52`. After d10-d18 NAVCENT-absent OIR/EUCOM tasking, d23 returns to NAVCENT-supported ISR — though under a different Operation (SPARTAN SHIELD vs cluster's redacted NAVCENT-cluster operation, plausibly Op INHERENT RESOLVE). Plausibly: 432 AEW POC Wing flies BOTH NAVCENT-supported (cluster + d23) AND non-NAVCENT (OIR Iraq/Syria d10-d18 + EUCOM d14) tasking concurrently. ^[inferred]

**4. First UAE theater anchor + first OMAM (Al Dhafra AB) launch base.** d23 takeoff `OMAM` = Al Dhafra Air Base (UAE host of US 380 AEW). First UAE launch base; first UAE filename internally verified. Body MGRS coords 39RYJ + 39RXK + 40RBP all decode to Persian Gulf vicinity (Iranian coast). 6th distinct theater after Persian Gulf interior (cluster) + Gulf of Aden (d44/d57) + Strait of Hormuz (d62/d63) + Arabian Sea (d56) + Iran AOR (d64) + UAE Persian Gulf (d23).

**5. First explicit US pilot response to Iranian GUARDCALL.** d23 GUARDCALL: Iranian Air Defense radio call `"MAINTAIN SAFE DISTANCE FROM BORDER"` (first explicit Iranian-side GUARDCALL phrase captured); US response `"THIS IS A COALITION AIRCRAFT CONDUCTING ROUTINE OPERATIONS IN INTERNATIONAL AIRSPACE"` (first explicit US pilot response to GUARDCALL in corpus). Both sides' verbatim text preserved through OCR/redaction — anchors the GUARDCALL dialogue as **bilateral airspace-claim assertion** rather than one-sided hailing. ^[inferred]
