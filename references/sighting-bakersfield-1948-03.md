# Sighting — Bakersfield, CA (March 1948)

Two civilian sightings of objects falling to earth, reported to the 4th Air Force at Hamilton Field, California, by Sgt A. M. Larsen of the Sheriff's office, Bakersfield, on 9 March 1948 (telephone). Forwarded to Chief of Staff, USAF, by Lt Col Donald L. Springer (AC of S, A-2, 4th AF) on 11 March 1948 — file 4AFDA-3/1208-I.

## Witnesses and observations

| Date | Time | Witness | Observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 March 1948 | 1610–1655 hrs | Mr. Les Buchner, Bakersfield, CA | Two objects falling toward earth from unknown source, southwest toward Buena Vista Lake. Description "similar to falling aircraft with smoke and debris trailing." |
| 8 March 1948 | (unspecified) | Mr. Denio, Pacific General Electric Company employee, Bakersfield | Two objects falling to earth north of Bakersfield. One object "seemed to be on fire with red and black smoke trailing behind." |

^[extracted]

## Investigation

Larsen reported that "searching parties, aircraft and rescue units have made numerous attempts to locate these reported objects without success." 4th Air Force initiated its own investigation; a follow-up report was promised but is not included in this bundle. ^[extracted]

## Distribution

The report was copied to:

- ADC (Air Defense Command), Mitchel Field, NY
- FBI, San Francisco
- G-2, Sixth Army
- DIO, San Francisco (12th Naval District)

The cross-service distribution shows that even routine "falling object" reports were being widely circulated in early 1948. ^[inferred]
