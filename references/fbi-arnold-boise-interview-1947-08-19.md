# FBI Arnold Boise Resident-Agency Interview (SA Joseph E. Jette, 19 August 1947)

A **multi-page FBI deposition** of **Kenneth Arnold** conducted on **19 August 1947 at the FBI Boise Resident Agency, Boise, Idaho** by **SA Joseph E. Jette**, transmitted to FBI HQ via the SAC Butte → Director memo of **27 August 1947** ("Re Seattle tels August 7, 1947, and August 12, 1947"; Butte file 65-477), filed at **62-83894-111** in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR pages 88-110 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]]. ^[extracted]

This is the **FBI's own-channel Arnold deposition** — structurally distinct from the **4th Air Force Brown / Davidson channel** Arnold deposition preserved in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|serial 130]] (the 12 Jul 1947 Boise interview by Brown S/A CIC). ^[inferred] The FBI deposition is conducted **8 weeks after the 24 Jun 1947 sighting** (vs. ~2.5 weeks for the Brown 4AF interview), under the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]], and includes Arnold's **direct correspondence with Ray Palmer of Venture Press** as documentary evidence. ^[extracted]

The FBI deposition **predates the FBI Seattle Maury Island case-closing report** ([[references/fbi-seattle-maury-island-report-1947-08-18|18 Aug 1947]]) by 1 day, and is operationally tied to it: the deposition's purpose was to verify the Maury Island Crisman + Dahl admission that **Arnold had been paid by Palmer to come to Tacoma**. ^[inferred]

## Verbatim — the Palmer correspondence opening

The deposition's opening establishes Arnold's documentary contact with Palmer: ^[extracted]

> KENNETH ARNOLD, Route 1, Mountain View Drive, Boise, Idaho, was interviewed at the Boise Resident Agency, Boise, Idaho, by SA JOSEPH E. JETTE on August 19, 1947. Mr. ARNOLD advised that he received a letter from RAYMOND A. PALMER, Editor, Venture Press, Evanston, Illinois, dated June 26, 1947, in which Mr. PALMER advised that he was interested in publishing an article in his magazine concerning the flying discs seen by Mr. ARNOLD near Mount Rainier on June 24, 1947, which letter is be[ing furnished to the Bureau].

The 26 Jun 1947 Palmer-to-Arnold letter is the **earliest documented Palmer-Arnold contact** in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] It establishes that Palmer made contact with Arnold **within 48 hours of the Mt. Rainier sighting** — earlier than the 22 Jul 1947 Palmer-to-Arnold letter [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR p. 17 separately preserves (which is the **second** Palmer-Arnold letter, asking Arnold to investigate Tacoma).

## Arnold's autobiographical statement

The deposition includes **a multi-page autobiographical statement** authored by Arnold. As preserved at OCR pages 105-110: ^[extracted]

- **Boyhood in Minot, North Dakota**: dog-sled racing, Lions Club Dog Derby winner 1930. ^[extracted]
- **Career**: 1938 Red Comet, Inc. (automatic fire-fighting apparatus, Littleton CO); 1939 district manager (5 western states); 1940 founded Great Western Fire Control Supply (independent fire-control engineer). ^[extracted]
- **Pilot's license number**: **333467**. ^[extracted]
- **Aircraft**: **Callair**, three-place single-engine. ^[extracted]
- **Flying experience**: started as a boy in Minot, North Dakota. ^[extracted]

The autobiographical content **substantially overlaps** with the 4AF Brown agent's-notes character assessment preserved in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|serial 130]] and [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|the existing Arnold sighting page]]. ^[inferred] The structural difference: the FBI deposition is **Arnold-narrated in first-person**, while the 4AF report is **Brown-summarized in third-person**. ^[inferred]

## Verbatim — Arnold on Mt. Rainier (the FBI-channel narrative)

OCR page 105 preserves Arnold's first-person retelling: ^[extracted]

> The sky and air were as clear as crystal. I had not flown more than two or three minutes on my course when **a bright flash reflected on my airplane**. It startled me as I thought I was too close to some other aircraft. I looked every place in the sky and couldn't find where the reflection had come from until I looked to the left and the north of Mt. Rainier where I observed **a chain of nine peculiar looking aircraft flying from north to south at approximately 9,500 feet elevation and going, seemingly, in a definite direction of about 170 degrees north to south.**
>
> They were approaching Mt. Rainier very rapidly, and I merely assumed th[ey were jet aircraft]…

The narrative is structurally consistent with the canonical Arnold sighting account ([[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|existing wiki page]]), preserved in this deposition as **Arnold's own first-person rendering** rather than via a third-party CIC interview filter. ^[inferred] The 9-object chain, 9,500-ft elevation, 170° direction, the bright-reflection-startle-event, all match. ^[inferred]

## Arnold's frustration with institutional non-engagement

OCR page 110 preserves a striking institutional observation by Arnold: ^[extracted]

> [I had no contact from the FBI or Military Intelligence] as to the authenticity of my story or a mental and physical examination as to my capabilities, **I received no interest from these two important protective forces of our country until two weeks after my observation.** I will go so far as to assume that **if our Military Intelligence was not aware of what I observed and reported to the United and Associated Press and over the radio on two different occasions which apparently set the nation buzzing, they would be the very first people I could expect as visitors.**
>
> I have received lots of requests from people who told me to make a lot of wild guesses. **I have based what I have written here in this article on positive facts and as far as guessing what it was I observed, it is just as much a mystery to me as it is to the rest of the world.**

This passage is **paradigm-defining for the corpus's institutional-engagement narrative**: ^[inferred]

- Arnold's expectation was that **Military Intelligence would have visited him within days** of the United/Associated Press wire coverage. ^[extracted]
- The actual delay (~2 weeks per Arnold; first Brown 4AF interview was 12 Jul 1947, ~18 days post-sighting) **surprised** Arnold. ^[extracted]
- Arnold's **inferred-by-omission reading** is that **if Military Intelligence already knew what he observed**, they would have visited promptly — the absence of prompt visits implies that **MI was as ignorant as he was**. ^[extracted]

This Arnold reading — that institutional silence implies institutional ignorance — is the **mirror-image** of the [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett admission's]] reading, which appeared ~3 weeks after this deposition: that institutional unconcern at "high brass" level implies institutional knowledge of a USG-classified project. ^[inferred] The two readings are structurally opposed but generated from the same observational data (institutional response cadence). ^[inferred]

## Why the FBI conducted its own deposition

The deposition was triggered specifically by the **Maury Island case** (per the cover memo's *"Re Seattle tels August 7, 1947, and August 12, 1947"* reference). ^[extracted] The FBI Seattle teletypes had reported the Crisman + Dahl signed admission that **Arnold was paid by Palmer to come to Tacoma**. ^[extracted] The FBI's own deposition served three purposes: ^[inferred]

1. **Verify the Palmer-Arnold financial relationship** independently (yes — the 26 Jun 1947 Palmer letter is documentary evidence). ^[extracted]
2. **Capture Arnold's first-person narrative** of the 24 Jun 1947 sighting under FBI questioning, separate from the 4AF Brown channel. ^[inferred]
3. **Assess Arnold's witness credibility** for FBI HQ purposes — the deposition is the FBI's institutional record on Arnold, distinct from the 4AF's. ^[inferred]

The deposition's transmittal via SAC Butte (rather than SAC Seattle) reflects the **Boise Resident Agency's geographic location** under SAC Butte's territorial jurisdiction. ^[inferred]
