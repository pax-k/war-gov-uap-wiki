# Sighting: Transparent Kite (Western US, Pre-dawn)

Event 4 of the [[references/western-us-event-2026|Western U.S. Event]] cluster.

## Quick facts

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Western U.S., a few hundred meters from [[references/sighting-dark-kite-western-us|Dark Kite]] site |
| Time of day | Pre-dawn (~30 min after Event 3) |
| Witnesses (saw) | USPER5 (NVGs), USPER6 (bare eye) |
| Witness (did not see) | USPER7 |
| Object altitude | ~6 m off the ground |
| Orientation | Canted lower-right to upper-left |
| Motion | "Floating slowly with the wind" |

## Description

About 30 minutes after the [[references/sighting-dark-kite-western-us|Dark Kite]] sighting, the same two agents (USPER5, USPER6) returned to the same general area with a colleague (USPER7) responding to a report of **another unauthorized object**.

Within a few hundred meters of the prior sighting:

- USPER5 and USPER6 observed a **kite-shaped object** with a **lighting pattern similar** to the Dark Kite. ^[inferred] (continuity with Event 3)
- The object was **about 6 m off the ground**, **canted from lower right to upper left**, and appeared to be **floating slowly with the wind**.
- USPER7 **did not see** the object — even though USPER5 (NVGs) and USPER6 (bare eye) both reported it.

(Image 2 in the slide is a recreation of USPER6's bare-eye drawing. Note in the slide: "Outline was not definitive, but location of lights stayed in exact formation.")

## Transparency observation

USPER5 (using NVGs) reported they could **"vaguely see a bright star or two in the distance through the object, though somewhat more faint."** This is a direct claim of **partial optical transparency** — see [[concepts/transparent-uap]].

## Spotlight beam-blocking observation

After losing visual contact, USPER5 used a spotlight to try to reacquire the object:

> "at one point my beam went from shining far into the distance to stopping about 50 yards away on nothing in particular, it just was not projecting into the distance and then it was."

When the witness pointed the light **back at the same spot**, the beam was **"projecting into the distance again."** The team did not reacquire the object.

This is a notable signature: **a transient localized beam-blocking effect** without a clearly visible occluder. ^[inferred] (whether attributable to the same object is the witness's interpretation, not a measured fact)

## Why it matters

- **Multi-modal observability**: object visible in both NVG and bare-eye for two of three witnesses, but invisible to the third. Suggests the relevant signature is on the edge of human perceptual thresholds. ^[inferred]
- **Partial transparency + beam-blocking + same-location reappearance** within 30 min stack into one of the more anomalous reports in the cluster.
- Selectivity of who sees it (USPER7 missed it entirely) is a recurring UAP-witnessing pattern that resists simple shared-illusion or misidentification explanations. ^[inferred]
