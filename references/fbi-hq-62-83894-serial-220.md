# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Serial 220 (García Macías unsolicited letter + Robles Jr. Durango clipping, Mar 1950)

A **15-page Mistral-OCR'd declassified artifact** from the **FBI Central Records Center / FBI HQ**. Filed at **Class 0062 / Case 83894 / Sub —/ Vol 1 / Serial 220 ONLY** with secondary marker **62-HQ-83894-EBF 220 EBF**, FOIPA reference **#129A982**, *DO NOT DESTROY* stamp, and the *"declassification authority derived from FBI Automatic Declassification Guide, issued May 24, 2007"* note. ^[extracted] OCR-visible Records Center processing tag parses as **8/11/12 74164 RRP003IXG6** — likely an internal Records Center processing tag. ^[ambiguous] The artifact carries an **FBI New York stamp dated APR 7 1950**. ^[extracted]

This is the **second artifact ingested in the planned 17-serial 62-HQ-83894 wiki series** (serial 403 was first; see [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-403]]). ^[inferred] It is, however, ~6 years **earlier** than serial 403. The parent-file hub is [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file]].

> **Cross-section confirmation (added 2026-05-10):** [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|Section 5]] (this ingest) contains the **NY SAC → Director originating filing** for serial 220 (filed 11 Apr 1950 by Tony G. Stetler with English translation by Mrs. Sophia Saliba), confirming the artifact's intra-file position at serial 220 between serial 219 (Hoover-AEC Adcock letter, 14 Mar 1950) and serial 221 (Hoover-McClaskey acknowledgment, 11 Apr 1950). ^[extracted] Section 5 also contains the **Hoover 17 May 1950 forward to OSI USAF** of the García Macías material — completing the institutional disposition: NY SAC intake → translation → HQ filing → AF referral. ^[extracted]

## What's in the artifact

The 15 OCR'd pages decompose into **four documentary layers**:

1. **Page 1 (artifact index 0) — FBI Records Center routing/cover slip.** Carries the Class–Case–Sub–Vol–Serial filing block (`0052 [sic] 83894 — 1 — 220 ONLY`), the secondary marker `62-HQ-83894-EBF 220 EBF`, FOIPA reference `129A982`, the *DO NOT DESTROY* stamp, the OCR'd processing tag *"8/11/12 74164 RRP003IXG6"* and *"ENCL BEHIND FILE 62-83894-220"* notation, and the FBI 2007 Automatic Declassification Guide note. ^[extracted] The cover-table cell shows Class **`0052`** while the page header reads **`62- HQ-83894`** (i.e. `0062`); the **header is authoritative** — the cell appears to be an OCR / digit error. ^[ambiguous] The Vol field on this serial is **`1`** (vs. blank on serial 403) — a small structural signal that the file may be volume-organized. ^[inferred]

2. **Pages 2–4 (artifact indices 1–3) — FBI in-house English translation of the García Macías letter.** Three-page typed translation of the Spanish-language original (pages 7–8 below) by **"Mrs. Sophia Saliba"** (likely an FBI New York field-office contract translator ^[inferred]). The translation reproduces García Macías's Veracruz return address (Av. Pino Suárez Núm. 324), the date (19 March 1950), the addressee (*"the President of the Commission of Scientific Investigation of the United States of North America, New York"* — almost certainly a writer-misunderstanding of the US science apparatus; no such body existed), and the full text of the *"stratospheric aerostats / flying saucers"* thesis. ^[extracted] The translator inserts **three explicit translator's notes** (footnoted with `*`, `**`, `***`) calling out the Spanish neologism *"aereo-astactos extratofericos"*, García Macías's habit of capitalizing words *"when there is no apparent need for them"*, and the literal meaning of *"camiones de volteo"* — i.e., the FBI translator is **annotating with linguistic care** rather than producing a hasty summary. ^[extracted]

3. **Pages 5–14 (artifact indices 4–13) — newspaper clipping + Spanish original of the García Macías letter + envelope + García Macías's hand-drawn diagrams.** Specifically:
   - **Page 5 (index 4)**: English caption-translation of the Mexican-newspaper article *"THE FIRST PHOTOGRAPHS OF A 'FLYING SAUCER', OBTAINED IN DURANGO AT AN ALTITUDE OF 9000 FEET"* — see [[references/sighting-durango-mexico-1950-03]] for the sighting page. Translation by Mrs. Sophia Saliba. The page also lists the diagram captions for the *"Wings and Propellers"* + *"Global-Conic and Semi-Global Stability"* pages and ends with the **FBI – NEW YORK / APR 7 1950** stamp. ^[extracted]
   - **Page 6 (index 5)**: **Second, degraded** OCR pass of the same newspaper-page caption (*"THE FIRST PHOTOGRAPHY OF A 'FILING SAMPLE'"*; Robles → *"George Horacio Rodes Jr."*; altitude → *"3000 to 5000 feet"*). The wiki ignores this pass and uses the cleaner pass on page 5. ^[ambiguous]
   - **Pages 7–8 (indices 6–7)**: Spanish-language original of the García Macías letter (2 sheets, signed *"Miguel Ángel García Macías / El Inventor Ideógrafo / 'POR EL PROGRESO UNIVERSAL'"*). ^[extracted]
   - **Page 9 (index 8)**: OCR'd envelope. Sender block *"Miguel Angel Garcia Macias / Descubridor & Inventor Ideógrafo / Av. Pino Suarez #324 / Veracruz, Texas"* (sic — a Mexico-vs-Texas OCR error of *"Ver."* read as *"Tex."* ^[inferred]); addressee block *"Presidente de la Comisión de Investigación Científica de Estados Unidos de Norte America / U.S. Court House, Foley, Tex. / Nueva York"*. The *"U.S. Court House, Foley, Tex."* annotation is OCR'd and may indicate an **interim re-routing point** for the letter inside the US postal / federal-routing system. ^[ambiguous]
   - **Pages 10, 11, 12, 14 (indices 9, 10, 11, 13)**: García Macías's **hand-drawn diagrams** — *"Estabilidad Lúnica-Elibal y Semi-Elibel"* (sic, OCR-degraded — likely *"Estabilidad Cónica-Global y Semi-Global"* ^[ambiguous]), a propeller / *"captura aérea fuerza"* diagram showing a multi-level oxygen chamber and air-current channels, and additional stability diagrams. ^[extracted]
   - **Page 13 (index 12)**: Mexican newspaper page reproduction (image-rich) with masthead *"FEDERACIÓN DE LA REPÚBLICA / LIC. MIGUEL LANZ DURET / MEXICO, D. F., JUEVES 18 DE MARZO DE **1880** / GERARDO LÓPEZ Y FUENTES / NUMERO 12.896"*. The OCR'd date "1880" is almost certainly **1950** ^[ambiguous] (consistent with García Macías's 19 Mar 1950 cover letter and the FBI Apr 1950 stamp). The masthead's combination of director Lic. Miguel Lanz Duret and issue number 12,896 most plausibly identifies the paper as ***El Universal*** (Mexico City) of Thursday **18 March 1950**. ^[inferred]

4. **(Implicit) original Robles photographs**: not preserved as images in the artifact OCR — only the printed-newspaper reproductions are. ^[inferred]

## What 62-HQ-83894 contains here, summarized

- A **routing/cover slip** (FBI Records Center filing instrument, no FBI investigative content).
- An **unsolicited inbound Mexican civilian letter** in Spanish about flying saucers, with the FBI's in-house English translation by Mrs. Sophia Saliba.
- An **enclosed Mexican newspaper clipping** reporting a **photographically-evidenced UAP sighting** at Durango (18 Mar 1950).
- The original sender's **hand-drawn diagrams** of his proposed propulsion / stability mechanism.

There is **no FBI-authored substantive content**: no FD-302, no Bureau-internal memo, no SAC routing slip, no name-trace, no analytical narrative. ^[extracted] The FBI's only operational gesture on this serial is the **in-house translation work** (Saliba) — a non-trivial commitment of agency labour, but not investigative output. ^[inferred]

## What 62-HQ-83894 appears to be — refined

Serial 403 (1956) framed 62-HQ-83894 as a **topic-clearinghouse for publicly-available materials about UAP** (a clipped book jacket). Serial 220 (1950) **broadens that reading** without contradicting it: ^[inferred]

- The file accumulates **(a) clipped public publications** (serial 403's Barker book jacket), **AND**
- **(b) unsolicited inbound civilian saucer-correspondence**, including **foreign-language items the FBI translates in-house** before filing (this serial).

Both channels are **content accumulation, not investigation** — the FBI is **collecting** UAP-topic material at HQ rather than acting on it. ^[inferred] The two-serial spread suggests 62-HQ-83894 is best read as a **multi-channel UAP-topic accumulation file** spanning at least 1950 → 1956, holding **whatever UAP-topic paper crossed HQ's desk** across multiple intake routes (foreign mail, English-language clipping, etc.). ^[inferred] The file's serial-220 vs serial-403 morphology spread is *consistent* with the topic-clearinghouse hypothesis at the level of **agency posture** (collect, don't investigate), while **expanding** the channel count from 1 to 2.

The file's date span is now **1950 → 1956 (≥ 6 years)** ^[inferred]; the remaining 15 queued serials (164, 438, 449, sections 1–10, sub_a) will resolve the upper / lower bounds and may broaden the channel count further. ^[inferred]

## Position in the FBI corpus

This artifact moves the **earliest FBI-originated UAP-adjacent record in the wiki** from **1956** (serial 403, Barker jacket) to **April 1950** (this serial's intake stamp). ^[inferred] It also distinguishes itself from every other FBI-originated record in the corpus by **intake channel**:

| Era | Office | Intake channel | Instrument | Wiki anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Apr 1950** | **HQ (via FBI New York stamp)** | **Unsolicited inbound foreign civilian mail (Spanish, FBI in-house translated)** | **Records Center cover slip + translated letter + enclosed Mexican newspaper clipping + sender diagrams** | **THIS ARTIFACT** |
| 1956 | HQ | HQ-side clipping of US publication | Records Center slip + clipped book jacket | [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-403]] |
| Nov 1957 | Detroit | WH-routed citizen letter (via Cutler) | Blank Memo + AIRTEL + Director URGENT teletype | [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957]] |
| Apr 1958 | Detroit | Citizen phone-in | SF-64 Office Memorandum (advise USAF) | [[references/fbi-detroit-100-18221-serial-844-1958]] |
| Oct 1966 | Detroit | Civilian UFO-org inquiry | SAC outgoing letter (USAF redirect) | [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957]] |
| 2023 | (redacted) | FD-302 evidentiary intake | FD-302 narrative form ×2 | [[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023]], [[references/fbi-fd302-serial-3-driver-redacted-2023]] |

The **inbound-foreign-civilian-mail** channel is **structurally novel** for the FBI corpus. ^[inferred] The 1957 Krasuski case is the closest analogue (a citizen letter that triggered FBI action), but Krasuski's letter (a) was in English, (b) was routed via the White House Special Assistant, and (c) triggered substantive sit-down investigation. García Macías's letter (a) was in Spanish, (b) arrived via direct mail, and (c) triggered only **filing** + **translation** (no investigation). ^[inferred]

The **secondary-marker scheme differs**: serial 403's `E483` is a single-letter prefix + number; serial 220's `EBF 220 EBF` is a two-instance text-bracket form. ^[inferred] The **FOIPA `129A982`** vs serial 403's **DIPA `1142292`** difference is the most significant — `FOIPA` (Freedom of Information / Privacy Act) is the **FBI's public-release tracking prefix**, suggesting this serial was released via a FOIA request rather than a Records Center release-tranche. ^[inferred] The processing-tag dates (8/11/12 vs 9/18/22) span ~10 years, consistent with **per-serial-tranche** release rather than a single bulk release of the entire 62-HQ-83894 file. ^[inferred] The processing-tag-date hypothesis flagged in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-403]] is now strongly supported. ^[inferred]
