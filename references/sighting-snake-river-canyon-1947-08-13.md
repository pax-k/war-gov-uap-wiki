# Sighting — Snake River Canyon, ID (A. C. Urie + sons, 13 August 1947)

A **low-altitude high-speed daytime flying-saucer sighting** through the **Snake River canyon, Idaho**, on **13 August 1947 at approximately 1:00 PM**, witnessed by **A. C. Urie** and his two young sons **Keith Urie (8 years old)** and **Billy Urie (10 years old)** at or near the **Anger Falls Trout Farm Highway 88 / Blue Lakes ranch** area, **6 miles west of Blue Lakes ranch**, **40 miles southwest of Twin Falls**. ^[extracted] The case is preserved in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR pages 122-132 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]] (filed serial 115; SAC Butte file 65-480) including hand-drawn sketches, the Twin Falls *Times-News* 15 Aug 1947 newspaper coverage, and a Vic Goertzen artist's conception of the object. ^[extracted]

## Verbatim — Urie's narrative

A. C. Urie's hand-written narrative (preserved at OCR page 132): ^[extracted]

> A close up view of the Flying Saucer as it passed by the Anger Falls Trout farm Hwy 88 at 1:00 pm Aug 13 going down Snake River Canyon at a height of about **75 ft from canyon floor** at a speed of about **1000 mph**. I had a side view at a distance of about **300 ft** and almost in a level and two of my boys Keith 8 + Billy, 10 was below me and saw it at about a 45 degree angle they got both a bottom and side view we were all looking at from the south side looking toward the north the boys saw it coming about a 9 mi. up Canyon we all lost sight of it in less than a mile.
>
> **It was all one color sort of a light sky blue with a red tubular fiery glow on the side of hood.** The canyon floor is rough along here and it ro[lled and pitched as it followed contour]…

## The Mormon Poplars vacuum effect

A distinctive feature of the case: ^[extracted]

> As the machine went by the URIE place, **the trees over which it almost directly passed (Mormon Poplars) did not just bend with the wind as if a plane had gone by, but, in URIE's words, "spun around on top as if they were in a vacuum."**

The "spun around on top as if in a vacuum" framing is a **distinctive aerodynamic-effect signature** in the corpus. ^[inferred] It is not a standard wake-vortex effect (which would deflect crowns laterally) but an **azimuthal rotation** of the upper canopy. ^[inferred] No corpus analogue is identified; the effect remains structurally singular. ^[inferred]

## Multi-witness corroboration with chained-age witnesses

The case is corroborated by **three witnesses across two age groups**: ^[extracted]

- **A. C. Urie (adult)** — primary observer, side view from ~300 ft, level perspective from the south side of the canyon.
- **Keith Urie (8 yo)** — first to see the object coming about 9 mi up the canyon; observed at 45° angle from below; got both bottom and side views.
- **Billy Urie (10 yo)** — saw the object almost immediately after Keith; same below-perspective.

The **child-witness independent first-detection** is a distinctive corroboration pattern in the corpus — Keith (age 8) saw the object first, before his father, indicating the children had unprompted attention to the canyon when the object entered visibility. ^[inferred] The mother and daughter (in the house at the time) did not witness the event. ^[extracted] Urie's brother (also living in the canyon) was questioned but had not seen the object. ^[extracted]

The **SAC Butte 20 Aug 1947 memo** at OCR page 122 documents Urie's belief that the **rolled outer edge** was about a foot through and the **tubing/exhaust flame** extended at least to the back end of the device. ^[extracted] The memo's filing reference is to a prior **15 Aug 1947 SAC Butte teletype to the Bureau** (OCR page 84) reporting **AL W. HAWKINS, COUNTY COMMISSIONER AND EX SHERIFF, TWIN FALLS, IDAHO**, and **J. H. BROWN, IDAHO STATE WAREHOUSE INSPECTOR** sighting two disc objects at ~9:30 AM Wednesday morning August 13, 1947 (the same day) while fishing 40 mi SW of Twin Falls. ^[extracted] The Hawkins/Brown sighting is **temporally and geographically adjacent** to the Urie sighting (~3.5 hours earlier, same vicinity), suggesting either two distinct objects on the same day in the same region or two observations of one object hours apart. ^[ambiguous]

## Press coverage

The Twin Falls *Times-News* 15 August 1947 ran the case on its front page under the headline **"Heads Up, Folks! The Discs Are Flying"** with an artist's conception by staff engraver Vic Goertzen, captioned: ^[extracted]

> This is an artist's conception of the flying disc that A. C. Urie saw sweeping through Snake river canyon six miles west of Blue Lakes ranch. **It seemed to be powered by jets emitting a fiery glow on both sides**, and could well be the inspiration for something new in women's hats, such as a **"flying saucer" creation**.

The press caption is paradigm-noteworthy on two counts: ^[inferred]

1. The "powered by jets emitting a fiery glow on both sides" — the press version describes **two-sided fiery glow** while Urie's narrative describes a **single-sided fiery red glow** ("red tubular fiery glow on the side"). ^[ambiguous] Whether the press description reflects Goertzen's artistic license or a different witness account is unclear.
2. The "could well be the inspiration for something new in women's hats" — the **trivializing rhetorical frame** typical of 1947-wave press coverage that is documented in [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|the hoax-default frame]]. ^[inferred]

## Position in the 1947 Idaho-Montana wave

The Urie case sits in the **second-half-of-August 1947 Idaho-Montana sub-wave** documented in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]]: ^[inferred]

- **5 May 1947** — Warden John Henry Montana State Prison Ellensburg-Seattle silver-disc-disintegrating sighting (preserved as a 27 Sep 1947 retrospective at section 3 OCR p. 185)
- **13 Aug 1947 morning** — Hawkins/Brown Twin Falls 40 mi SW disc sighting (15 Aug 1947 SAC Butte teletype, section 3 OCR p. 84)
- **13 Aug 1947 ~1 PM** — **the Urie case** (this page)
- **14 Aug 1947** — Sidney Decker / L. R. Brummett Redmond WA twin-disc sighting (section 3 OCR p. 142)
- **14 Aug 1947** — Switzer Placerville CA "rocket-shaped" object struck ground (section 3 OCR pp. 78-81; 4AF-1208-I report)
- **19 Aug 1947** — Hedstrom Twin Falls mass-formation observation ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]] serial 66 — first explicit BB-42 paragraph-B citation)

The cluster confirms a **regional sub-wave centered on the Idaho-Montana-Pacific Northwest geographic axis** in the second half of August 1947, **6-7 weeks after the [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Arnold sighting]]**. ^[inferred]

## Disposition

The case appears to receive **no formal investigative follow-up beyond the SAC Butte filing**. ^[inferred] No FBI agent visited the Anger Falls Trout Farm site for physical-evidence collection (compare the [[references/sighting-noack-helendale-1948-12|Dec 1948 Noack case]] where Inspector [[entities/h-b-fletcher|Fletcher]] dispatched FBI agents to physically secure a possible disc; the Urie case is **not** so dispatched). The case is filed at HQ as a **passive-accumulation** intake under the cooperation regime. ^[inferred]

This is consistent with the [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|BB-42]] field-office tasking framework, which directs investigation of **first-witness sincerity** rather than **physical-evidence collection**. ^[inferred] Urie's voluntarily-supplied sketches + his stated claim of the Mormon Poplars vacuum effect would, under a physical-evidence regime, prompt site visit and tree-canopy inspection — but no such visit is documented. ^[inferred]
