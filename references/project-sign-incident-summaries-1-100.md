# Project SIGN Incident Summary Sheets 1–100 (May 1947 – Jan 1948)

A 209-page Mistral-OCR'd bundle of **100 sequentially-numbered Incident Summary Sheets** spanning the 1947 *Flying Disc wave* and its operational fallout into early January 1948. Cover stamp **001863** "DECLASSIFIED Authority NND 917033 / D20249 30014 / S78-1 (126) / Incl 194". This is the **earliest segment** of the [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] sequential-incident stream as preserved at NARA Record Group 38 (Records of the Office of the Chief of Naval Operations and Naval Records), File 143685, Box 7. ^[extracted]

It precedes the [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-101-172|101–172 bundle]] (Jan – Oct 1948) and the [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|173–233 bundle]] (Sep 1948 – Jan 1949). Together the three RG 38 Box 7 bundles preserve **continuous numbered coverage from Incident 1 (8 Jul 1947 Muroc) through Incident 233 (1 Jan 1949 Jackson MS)** — 233 numbered incidents over ~18 months. ^[inferred]

## Why this file matters for the wiki

This bundle is the **first primary source in the corpus** to:

- **Document the 25-field "Check-List Unidentified Flying Objects" form in routine use as early as July 1947** (Inc 1, 8 Jul 1947 Muroc AAF) — **6 months before** the [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell]] AFOIR-CO-5 directive of 27 Feb 1948. The 101–172 wiki page had hedged this as "most economical reading"; this bundle promotes it to **extracted fact**: the 25-field form **predates** Project SIGN's formal stand-up by half a year and was carried into SIGN by AMC T-2's pre-existing 1947 intake apparatus. ^[extracted]
- **Document the founding [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Kenneth Arnold sighting]]** (Inc 17, 24 Jun 1947 — 9 mirror-like craft over Mt Rainier WA) as a Project SIGN file artifact, complete with the agent's note that *"if Mr. Arnold could write a report of such a character and did not see the objects he was in the wrong business and should be engaged in writing Buck Rogers fiction"*. ^[extracted]
- **Document the [[references/sighting-mantell-godman-1948-01-07|Mantell incident]]** (Inc 33 + 17 sub-incidents) as a single 12-hour cross-state phenomenon with **17+ correlated sub-files** numbered 30 / 30a / 30b / 30c, 32, 33 / 33a / 33b / 33c / 33d / 33e / 33f / 33g, 48 / 48a / 48b / 48c / 48d. The first **UAP-attributed fatality** in the US institutional record. ^[extracted]
- **Document the [[references/sighting-muroc-1947-07-08|Muroc AAF cluster]]** (Inc 1, 1c, 1e, 2, 4) — multiple silver disc / sphere observations at the **AAF flight-test base** on 8 Jul 1947, including the [[references/sighting-rhodes-phoenix-1947-07-07|J. P. Stapp]] observation truck event during seat-ejection testing. ^[extracted]
- **Document the [[references/sighting-rhodes-phoenix-1947-07-07|William Rhodes Phoenix photo]]** (Inc 40, 7 Jul 1947) — **first photographic evidence in the corpus**, two photos of a 20–30 ft elliptical grey object spiraling 5,000 → 2,000 ft. ^[extracted]
- **Document a 1947 EM-effect close-encounter** (Inc 68, 24 Jun 1947 Cascade Mts) — Fred M. Johnson's combination compass-and-watch *"reacted in a very peculiar manner, the hand waving from one side to the other but that this condition corrected itself immediately after the discs had passed out of sight"*. **Earliest electromagnetic-anomaly datum in the corpus** — same day as Arnold, ~150 mi north. ^[extracted] See [[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon]].

## What is NOT in this file

Two cases the corpus's downstream historiography emphasizes are **absent** from the SIGN sequential-incident stream:

- **Roswell, NM (~early Jul 1947)** — no incident-numbered sheet, no cross-reference, no JONES/JUDGE filing-line. ^[extracted] Project SIGN's 1947 Flying Disc data collection **did not formally incorporate the Roswell event** into its incident-numbered stream as preserved here. ^[inferred] Whether this is a cataloguing absence (Roswell handled outside the SIGN intake) or a gap in the OCR'd file is open.
- **Maury Island, WA (21 Jun 1947)** — also absent from the numbered stream. ^[extracted]

The absence is itself a structural finding: the most-cited 1947 events in modern UAP discourse are **not** the events SIGN's intake apparatus emphasized. The 1947 cases SIGN tracked instead were the **Muroc AAF cluster** (Inc 1–4, 8 Jul 1947) and the **Pacific Northwest patrolman wave** (Inc 5–16, 4 Jul 1947 Portland OR / Vancouver WA). ^[inferred]

## Cross-bundle observations

### The 25-field form pre-existed Project SIGN

Inc 1 (8 Jul 1947 Muroc) uses the **identical 25-field "Check-List Unidentified Flying Objects" form** later seen in the [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-101-172|101–172]] and [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|173–233]] bundles. The form **predates the [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell]] AFOIR-CO-5 directive of 27 Feb 1948 by 7 months**. ^[extracted] AMC T-2's 1947 pre-SIGN intake apparatus carried the form into Project SIGN at SIGN's stand-up. The 101–172 wiki page hedged this as "most economical reading"; **this bundle promotes the claim from `^[inferred]` to `^[extracted]`**. ^[inferred]

### Multi-state cross-correlation pre-Mantell

The Mantell sequence (Inc 30/30a–c, 32, 33/33a–g, 48/48a–d, 49) is the corpus's **earliest multi-state cross-correlation event** — 12 hours of phenomena tracked across **Edwardsville IL → Madisonville KY → Lexington KY → Owensboro KY → Maysville KY → Irvine KY → Godman KY → Lockbourne OH → Wilmington OH → Danville KY**, with **independent observers at each waypoint** and **subsequent astronomer-attempted Venus/comet rationalization** explicitly questioned in the file. ^[extracted]

The 30 Dec 1947 sequence (Inc 94, 95, 96, 97) is a precursor: **simultaneous observations from 4 stations across CA, NV, OR/CA border** at 1925 PST, with **the only ground-fire-correlated 1947 datum**. ^[extracted]

### EM-effect chronology

The corpus's earliest electromagnetic-effect datum was previously **Inc 162 Hamel MN** (101–172 bundle, 11 Aug 1948 — physical-trace landing). **This bundle pushes the EM-effect chronology back 14 months** to **Inc 68 Fred Johnson Cascade Mts (24 Jun 1947)** — same day as Arnold, ~150 mi N: a prospector's combination compass-and-watch deflects during disc passage, corrects after disappearance. ^[extracted] The witness's 40-year prospecting credibility is explicitly attested by the agent.

- **NCOs/airmen** (~15): Nauman, Ruvolo, Livingston, Blackwell, Oliver, Ziegler, etc.
- **Civilian commercial pilots** (~10): UAL Capt Smith (Inc 10), PAA Capts Powell + Griffin (Inc 73, 59), EAL Capts Chiles (later Inc 144)
- **Police officers** (~10): McDowell, Cross, Lissy, Ellis, Patterson, Sullivan, McKay, Krives, Frazier, Rountree, Scott, Kelley
- **Trained scientific/technical observers** (~10): Wood (UC), Stapp (Muroc flight test), Kauke/Zohn/Rockwood (NRL Rocket Sonde), Cole (astronomer), Madden (Pac Tel & Tel), Switzer (UC Berkeley grad), Minozewski/Baron (Weather Bureau pibal)
- **Politicians** (1): Lt Gov Whitehead (Idaho, Inc 28)

- **Civilian misc** (~70): farmers, housewives, prospectors, train crews, harbor patrolmen, postmasters, etc.

The pool is **dominated by aviation-trained observers** during 1947 — a structural feature of the SIGN intake apparatus that selects for credibility rather than randomness in the witness population. ^[inferred]

## Open questions

- **Why no Roswell or Maury Island in the SIGN sequential-incident stream?** Are these handled in adjacent unfiled correspondence at NARA RG 38 Box 7 / 6 / 8 that has not been digitized, or were they routed outside the AMC T-2 intake apparatus through non-summary-sheet channels (CIC, FBI liaison, AFSWP, etc.)? ^[ambiguous]
- **Inc 31 K. D. Wood (Dec 1946 N Arizona)**: Is this the corpus's earliest event in a SIGN file? It is **6 months before Arnold** but filed retroactively — the bundle's data-collection window evidently absorbed witness recollections from before the 1947 wave proper. ^[extracted] Whether the 1946 backdated case is unique or there are others is open.
- **Inc 79 Minozewski / Baron (Apr 1947 Weather Bureau pibal Richmond VA)**: This is a **3–4-event over 6 months** weather-theodolite-observed UAP record — suggesting routine WB observation of unidentified objects pre-Arnold. The relationship to the post-Arnold formal SIGN stream is open. ^[ambiguous]
- The **Mrs Evans Wildwood NJ multi-month log** (Inc 50 + Feb 19, Feb 21, Mar 11, Apr 2, Jul 1 1948) was annotated *"Individual appears to be 'crackpot'"* by the agent. This is the **first agent-side dismissal-language** in the SIGN intake stream — earlier than e.g. the [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|173–233]] agent-skepticism examples. ^[extracted] How frequent / how consequential agent-side credibility annotations are across the full SIGN run is open.
