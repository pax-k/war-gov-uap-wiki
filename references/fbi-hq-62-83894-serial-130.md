# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Serial 130

A **single FBI HQ serial** filed at **62-HQ-83894-130**, declassified per **HND 90965** ^[extracted], comprising **126 OCR pages / ~188 KB markdown** of multi-document AAF intelligence material centered on the **30 July 1947 AFBIR-CO 18-sighting statistical analysis** ^[extracted] (see [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30]]). The serial is **not an FBI-authored compilation** — it is an **AAF-side analytical packet** plus its source-document corpus, retained as-received at FBI HQ. ^[inferred] The packet's coverage page is dated **12 September 1947 at HEADQUARTERS, AIR DEFENSE COMMAND, MITCHEL FIELD, NEW YORK**, conveying the contents at HQ-ADC level via Maj-A.O.-Powell-interview report. ^[extracted]

## What this serial is — structurally a NEW intake channel

Serial 130 introduces a **structural channel that did not appear in N=7 prior ingested artifacts** of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894]]. ^[inferred] Prior serials were either single-instance intake events (220 letter, 403 jacket, 438 Albuquerque report, 449 LA memo + magazine), bulk multi-carbon directives (164's 17 Cabell AIRMEM #4 carbons), or operational sequential-serial sections (1-52, 53-100). **Serial 130 is the first FBI-corpus example of an externally-compiled multi-document analytical packet retained at HQ as a single discrete serial.** ^[inferred] Channel inventory now extends to:

- **(h) NEW — Inter-agency analytical compilation product retained at HQ as a discrete serial** (AAF-compiled multi-document statistical package + source documents forwarded as a unit; distinct from individual serial-by-serial accumulation)

The packet is paginated **as one item** at FBI HQ (a single `62-83894-130` filing) but contains ≥ 16 distinct source documents originating at ≥ 8 different commands (15th AF Colorado Springs, 4AF Hamilton Field, NRL ARO, Newfoundland Base Command/ATC, ADC Mitchel Field, 14th AF Orlando + Birmingham AAF Reserve, TAC Langley, AAF HQ Pentagon, OCAMA Tinker Field, CAP Wisconsin Wing, SBAMA San Bernardino, A.M. London, etc.). ^[extracted]

## The titular document — AFBIR-CO 30 July 1947

OCR page 73 carries the **only "62-83894-130" inline filing-marker in the body** ^[extracted]:

> CONFIDENTIAL / 62-83894-130 / FLYING DISCS / 30 July 1947
> For purposes of analysis by **AFBIR-CO**, eighteen reported sightings of "Flying Discs" were selected for breakdown into detailed particulars.

This is the **paradigm-defining content** of the serial. AFBIR-CO is **AC/AS-2 Brigade Intelligence Requirements Branch — Collection** at AAF Headquarters Washington (Pentagon), the analytical office tasked by [[entities/g-d-garrett|Lt Col G. D. Garrett]] with running the disc-collection regime. ^[inferred] The 30 Jul 1947 analysis is the **earliest formal AAF statistical-analytical exercise on the 1947 flying-disc wave** in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] It **predates the famous 23 Sep 1947 Twining "AMC Opinion" memo by ~8 weeks** and the **5 Sep 1947 Schulgen formal denial letter to Hoover** by ~5 weeks, sitting **immediately after the 30 Jul 1947 issuance of [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]]** — i.e. AFBIR-CO and BB42 are **same-day-issued instruments of the AAF-FBI cooperation regime**. ^[inferred]

See [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30]] for the 18-witness inventory + 21-field breakdown.

## Key paradigm-defining content

### 1. AFBIR-CO 30 Jul 1947 18-sighting analysis — the corpus's earliest formal AAF analytical instrument

See [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30]]. The 21-field breakdown is **structurally identical in spirit to the 10-element [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|Cabell directive]] of Feb 1949** — but **issued 18 months earlier**, at the same date as [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]] (30 Jul 1947). ^[inferred] The two instruments together form the **synchronous AAF + FBI analytical apparatus** of the cooperation regime: AFBIR-CO collects + statistically breaks down upstream sightings; BB42 organizes the FBI field-office investigation channel. ^[inferred]

The 18-witness inventory (table summary): 16 distinct witness clusters spanning 19 May 1947 (Pikes Peak) → 12 Jul 1947 (Elmendorf AK), heavily weighted toward AAF officers (5 out of 16 witnesses), pilots (5 out of 16), and ground-civilian observers in CAVU conditions. Reports 7 (Bakersfield CA 1 Jul 1947) + 2 (Oklahoma City 22 May) + 4 (Mt Rainier 24 Jun = Arnold) + 17 + 18 are unanalyzed. ^[extracted] See [[references/afbir-co-18-sighting-analysis-1947-07-30]] for the full 21-field per-report breakdown.

### 2. NRL "Flying Saucer Mystery Solved" 12 Jul 1947 — earliest formal skeptical-debunking framework in the corpus

See [[references/nrl-flying-saucer-mystery-solved-1947-07-12]]. The 7-page memo proposes that **flying-disc sightings are sun-reflection artifacts on automobile ventilation-wing windows** (for ground-level civilian observers) and **electric-light-bulb reflections in plane cockpits** (for airborne observers). ^[extracted] Distinct from the [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|hoax-default frame]] (which assumes deliberate prankster fabrication) — the reflection-theory frame **doesn't require any physical disc artifact at all**. ^[inferred] This is the **earliest formal optical-skepticism framework** in the corpus, predating both the AFBIR-CO analysis (by 18 days) and Project SIGN's stand-up (by ~5 months). ^[inferred]

### 3. Pan Am Powell-White 4 Aug 1947 — earliest commercial-airline-pilot UAP encounter in the corpus

See [[references/sighting-pan-am-powell-white-1947-08-04]]. **First pilot Alpheus O. Powell** (Maxwell Class 41-C, 4,000+ command-pilot hours) and **navigator Walter I. White** (sitting in copilot seat) both observed a 15-ft elliptical deep-gold object on the Gander Newfoundland → La Guardia run, 1,000 ft below the Constellation, 10 mi NW of Boston, 1600 EDT 4 Aug 1947. ^[extracted] **Powell-White is institutionally significant because it sits 41 days after [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Arnold]] and 31 days after the [[references/sighting-rhodes-phoenix-1947-07-07|Rhodes]] photographic case** — i.e. it falls within the ~6-week window when AAF intelligence would have been most interpretation-sensitive, yet ADC's Mitchel Field interview (12 Sep 1947) is **AAF-side institutional engagement of a commercial-pilot witness**. ^[inferred]

### 4. Browning + Burniston + Kayko et al. — first sworn AAF-officer statements in the corpus

Three discrete AAF-officer sworn statements appear in serial 130: ^[extracted]

- **Maj Archie B. Browning AC** sworn statement at AAF HQ Pentagon (16 Jul 1947) — 6 Jul 1947 B-25 sighting 100 mi W of Kansas City; received AFBIR-CO same day.
- **Capt James H. Burniston AC** 24th-Article-of-War sworn statement to Capt Frazier + Sgt Stewart at Fairfield-Suisun AAB Intelligence Office — 6 Jul 1947 sighting (rolled-from-side-to-side three-times-across-sky behavior).
- **Capt Wilson H. Kayko + Capt John H. Cantrell + 1st Lt Theodore Dewey + Capt Redman** TAC Langley report — 28 Jun 1947 Maxwell Field AL 2120 CST sighting (zigzag waterbug behavior) + verified-no-experimental-aircraft via Maxwell Field Operations.

The combined Browning + Burniston + Kayko cluster is the corpus's **first datapoint of AAF officers swearing under oath to having witnessed flying discs**, forming **the analytical priors** that AFBIR-CO drew from (Browning is Report #9, Burniston is Report #10, Kayko et al. is Report #5 in the AFBIR-CO inventory). ^[extracted] [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement|UAP-aircraft engagement]] should fold these in.

### 5. Newfoundland cluster — earliest multi-witness UAP cluster outside the continental US in the corpus

See [[references/sighting-newfoundland-cluster-1947-07]]. **6 distinct sightings on the Newfoundland-Stephenville-Cordroy-Grand Falls cluster between 9-23 Jul 1947**, witnessed by Pan American Airways + TWA mechanics (Mehrman, Woodruff, Leidy — Kodachrome photographs taken), Newfoundland civilian villagers (John P. Legge, Wm Evans, Albert Samms (10 yo), Mrs John Samms), and Newfoundland Constabulary officer Constable Eric Kearsey + family. ^[extracted] Both 4AF Mitchel-Field-routed and Newfoundland Constabulary-routed channels arrive at FBI HQ via the serial-130 packet. **The Newfoundland tranche introduces the earliest "blueish-black trail / 15-mile-long beam-light residue" signature in the corpus** ^[inferred] — distinct from the Cascade Mts EM-effect signature.
