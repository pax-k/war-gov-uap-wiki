# Sighting: Large, Fiery Orb (Western US, Dusk)

Event 2 of the [[references/western-us-event-2026|Western U.S. Event]] cluster.

## Description

USPER5 and USPER6 observed a **glowing orange orb** at the witness-estimated 500–600 m range, perched close to a rock pinnacle. The witnesses described:

- Apparent size **similar to a "small helicopter cockpit"** (witness estimate).
- No clearly visible structure other than the glowing orb.
- USPER6 noted the object "did almost appear it might have had a small spindle or something connecting it from underneath to the rock formation."
- **No noticeable sound.**
- Visual analogies provided by witnesses: "**similar to the Eye of Sauron from Lord of the Rings, except without the pupil**, or maybe an orange Storm Electrify bowling ball."
- USPER6: object "seemed to be hovering with zero resistance or movement, or to be suspended."

## AARO follow-up measurements

The slide flags two AARO-assessed corrections via footnote markers:

- *True range*: ~**1050 m** (witnesses underestimated by roughly half).
- *True diameter*: **12–18 m** (substantially larger than a "small helicopter cockpit," ~2–3 m).

This means the witness-perceived "small helicopter cockpit" object at "500–600 m" was actually a **bus-to-house-sized luminous body** at over a kilometer's distance. ^[inferred] — the inference that this is a post-hoc instrumented site survey is implicit; the slides do not specify methodology.

## Why it matters

- The AARO range/size correction is one of the few **calibrated corrections to witness estimates** in the deck and a useful data point for the systematic underestimation of UAP range/size by even trained observers. ^[inferred]
- The "spindle to the rock" suggestion is unusual — most "orb" reports describe free-floating bodies. Worth tracking for repeat occurrences. ^[inferred]
- The ~1 minute duration is **far longer** than the 1–2 second orange orbs in [[references/sighting-orbs-launching-orbs-western-us]]; same color but different temporal profile.
