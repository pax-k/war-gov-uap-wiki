# Drew Pearson — "Reports Plentiful, Saucers Nil" (25 Nov 1950)

A syndicated column by **Drew Pearson** (the Washington-Merry-Go-Round columnist) dated **25 Nov 1950**, published in the Washington Post (filed by FBI HQ Central Research Section ~17 Dec 1950 — "162 83894-A 8 DEC 19 1950"). ^[extracted] Pearson explicitly states *"This column has now been able to examine Air Force files"* and walks through several confidential AF saucer-investigation outcomes. ^[extracted]

## Pearson's claims

| Claim | Pearson's source | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| The Air Force has spent *"hour upon hour checking reports"* but *"has not been able to track down a single bona fide saucer"* | unattributed | extracted |
| **Bruce Cabot / Wyandotte Echo / Coulter / 3-foot blond beardless men from Venus** story = a hoax. The "mysterious Mr. Coulter" turned out to be **George Koehler of Denver** (advertising salesman, radio station KMVR). The "flying saucer gear" was stamped with **Roman numeral VI**; Koehler later denied any saucer/occupant claims | "the confidential Air Force report" | extracted |
| **Warren MN "flying saucer crash"** = a hoax. **Walter Sirek**, service-station operator, directed AF investigators to look behind Nish's Tavern. The "machine was obviously made from various objects such as an old washing-machine cover, part of a radio set chassis and a spent insecticide bomb." Built as a joke by **Ted Heyen + Robert Schaeffer**, local hardware-store operators | "secret Air Force files" | extracted |
| **Mariana Great Falls MT film** (Nick Mariana, park manager): silvery discs over the baseball park 11:20-11:35 am 15 Aug 1950 — **AF investigators found that two silvery F-94 jet fighters from "Land Air Force Base, Alaska"** flew high over Great Falls at exactly that time, sun reflected off them as silvery saucer-blurs ^[ambiguous] | "Air Force investigators" | extracted (note: "Land Air Force Base" is likely an OCR error or Pearson misnaming — Malmstrom AFB / Great Falls AFB or Larson AFB are candidates) ^[ambiguous] |
| **Wright Field, Ohio radar** picked up a saucer drifting eastward at 20 mph — "prompt investigation showed it to be only a dense black cloud so charged with electronic particles that it appeared on radar" | "Air Force radar screen" | extracted |
| **Air Force currently receiving 5-6 saucer reports per day** | unattributed | extracted |

## Cross-corpus position

### A FIFTH Aztec-myth-seed laundering channel

The wiki currently anchors [[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950|the Aztec saucer myth-seed]] on **four laundering channels** in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|section 5]] of the main file (Wyandotte Echo OSI / Denver SAC / Hottel WFO / New Orleans SAC, Jan-Mar 1950). Pearson's column is a **fifth channel — press syndication via confidential-AF-files retention** — within sub-A's parallel filing track, eight months later (Nov 1950). ^[inferred] Specifically, Pearson's reproduction of:

- **Bruce Cabot + Wyandotte Echo + Coulter** is the **same intake pattern** as section 5 serial 203 (Wyandotte Echo OSI 13th Dist TWX, Jan 1950)
- **George Koehler of Denver radio KMYR** is the **same person + venue** as section 5 serial 206 (Denver SAC URGENT on Denver U lectures, Mar 1950)
- The **Roman-numeral-VI stamp** is a new datapoint not in any of the four section-5 channels

The Pearson column thus **closes the Coulter-Koehler identity-conflation question** publicly in November 1950 — eight months after FBI HQ first received it. ^[inferred] **Sub-A is therefore the press-shadow that publishes the AF's internal resolution to the same information that section 5 received via internal channels.** ^[inferred]

### First FBI-corpus retention of journalism citing classified AF files

This is the **first sub-A item — and the first FBI-corpus item generally — to retain a press article that explicitly cites confidential Air Force files**. ^[inferred] The retention pattern is unusual: HQ Central Research Section files journalism that **publicly discloses what AF investigators found in their classified record**, including the Mariana Great Falls film resolution (F-94-reflection) and the Wright Field radar-as-electronically-charged-cloud explanation.

Possible institutional readings: ^[inferred]

- **Counter-narrative reinforcement**: the FBI files Pearson's column to track public counter-narratives that align with the [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|saucer-as-hoax-default]] posture.
- **Source-pattern documentation**: Pearson's stated access to *"confidential Air Force files"* would itself be of FBI counter-intel interest if the access was unauthorized.
- **Doctrinal-mirror behaviour**: the file documents what the FBI/AF *say in public* about saucers, parallel to what the FBI *receives privately*.

### Mariana film disposition

The **Mariana Great Falls film resolution as F-94-reflection** is itself a noteworthy artifact. The AF's later [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|section 7]] retention of the **27 Oct 1952 ATIC unexplainable-creditable Navy-photographer-film admission** (Tremonton UT 2 Jul 1952) suggests the AF's posture on UAP films was inconsistent across the 1950-1952 window: Mariana = explained-away as F-94 reflection; Tremonton = "completely at a loss to explain". ^[inferred] The candidate "creditable Navy-photographer film" referenced in section 7 is therefore unlikely to be Mariana (already publicly debunked by Pearson Nov 1950); supports the WO Delbert C. Newhouse Tremonton hypothesis. ^[inferred]
