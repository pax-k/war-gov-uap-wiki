# SHAEF Foo Fighter / Night Phenomena File — 1944–1945

A 17-page Mistral-OCR'd US National Archives bundle (Record Group 331, Authority NND 947020, "FILE COPY GSA"), filed under SHAEF reference numbers **37153** and **SHAEF Air/TS.37153/A-2**. It is the only WWII-era primary-source bundle currently in the [[projects/uap/uap|UAP project]] and predates the [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948|1948 USAF Flying Disc correspondence]] by roughly three years.

## What the bundle contains

The file collates two parallel intelligence threads that SHAEF Air Staff treated together:

### Thread A — "Night Phenomena" / "Foo Fighters"

A chain of memoranda originating with the [[entities/415th-night-fighter-squadron|415th Night Fighter Squadron]] and routed up through XII Tactical Air Command, First Tactical Air Force (Prov), [[entities/shaef-air-staff|SHAEF Air Staff]], and the [[entities/uk-air-ministry-ddi2|UK Air Ministry (DDI2)]]:

- **16 Jan 1945 — XII TAC A-2** (Lt Col Leavitt Corning, Jr.) forwards a December 1944 squadron tactical-information extract reporting "a phenomenon which we cannot explain; crews have been followed by lights that blink on and off changing colors etc. The lights come very close and fly formation with our planes." ^[extracted]
- **20 Jan 1945 — First Tac AF (Prov), Maj S. V. Boykin** asks for more detail (colours, intensity, size, duration, altitude, hours, whether they cross Allied lines, on what part of the aircraft they ride). ^[extracted]
- **30 Jan 1945 — 415th NFS Capt F. B. Ringwald** returns ten dated sortie-report extracts from the nights of 14–15 Dec, 16–17 Dec, 22–23 Dec, 23–24 Dec (×2), 26–27 Dec (×4), 27–28 Dec (×2), 30–31 Dec, 1–2 Jan, 14–15 Jan, 29–30 Jan. The cover note adds: *"Foofighters is the name given these phenomenon by combat crews of this Squadron."* ^[extracted]
- **11 Feb 1945 — SHAEF Air Staff (A/Cmdr [[entities/c-m-grierson|C. M. Grierson]])** routes the bundle (a) back to First Tac AF stating SHAEF has no information that explains the phenomena and is referring it to the Air Ministry, and (b) onward to USSTAF (attn Col Bradley) noting "there must be something more than mere imagination behind the matter, and in view of the fact that pilots and crews are becoming slightly worried by them, it is considered that everything possible should be done to get to the root of the matter." ^[extracted]
- **17 Feb 1945 — A.D.I.(K) (W/Cdr S. D. Felkin)** replies that the matter "probably refers to *Flak Bombe* mentioned in A.D.I.(K) Report No. 562/1944, paragraphs 128 and 129." ^[extracted] — see [[concepts/flak-bombe-hypothesis]].
- **13 Mar 1945 — Air Ministry DDI2 (Group Captain E. D. M. Hopkins)** under file reference *Balls of Fire — Red* / 111/45/DDI2 / 6c A 37153, replies that "Bomber Command crews have for some time been reporting similar phenomena. A few of the alleged aircraft may have been Me. 262 and for the rest, flak rockets are suggested as the most likely explanation. The whole affair is still something of a mystery and the evidence is very sketchy and varied so that no definite and satisfactory explanation can yet be given." ^[extracted]
- **18 Mar 1945 — SHAEF Air Staff (Grierson)** closes the loop with First Tac AF: "no further, or more definite, information can be given." ^[extracted]

### Thread B — "Long Cylindrical Objects" (1 March 1945)

A separate 1 March 1945 sighting near grid F-5710, treated in the same SHAEF file (37153) but routed independently:

- **1 Mar 1945, 1030 hrs — pilots of 67 TAC/R Group / 107 PR Squadron** observe "an aluminum colored cylinder shaped object, about 12 ft long and 1 ft in diameter ... floating in the air at 9,000 ft ... suspended vertically with small fins and a mast projecting from the lower end." Attacked, partially deflated, "a red flame resulted without smoke. The cylinder did not disintegrate." ^[extracted]
- **2 Mar 1945 — IX TAC (Quesada)** and **4 Mar 1945 — SHAEF Air Staff** circulate the report and request photos. ^[extracted]
- **5 Mar 1945 — IX TAC (Quesada)** reports the photos taken by the 107 SQ pilot were unsuccessful; written report to follow. ^[extracted]

The file also contains a separate 4–5 March 1945 message thread on **bomb torpedoes recovered from the Trippelwerke at Molsheim** (sent to Hornchurch via DDI2). This is a German-armaments collection thread, not directly part of the foo-fighter or cylindrical-object investigations, and is included in the file because it shares routing officers (Grierson, DDI2). ^[inferred] See standalone page [[references/sighting-cylindrical-object-vicinity-f5710-1945-03-01]].

## File-naming and provenance

The source filename `331_120752_numeric_files_1944–1945_37153_german_armament_equipment_documents.json` encodes:
- `331` — NARA Record Group 331 (Allied Operational and Occupation Headquarters, World War II — i.e. SHAEF). ^[inferred]
- `120752` — finding-aid / digitization sequence number. ^[inferred]
- `37153` — SHAEF subject file number (which appears verbatim on most pages of the bundle). ^[extracted]
- `german_armament_equipment_documents` — the broader umbrella series; the foo-fighter and cylindrical-object correspondence sits inside it because at the time it was treated as **possible enemy weaponry**. ^[inferred]

## Why this matters for the wiki

- It is the **earliest UAP-adjacent primary source** currently in the wiki, predating [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948]] by ~3 years.
- It establishes that **institutional collection of unexplained-aerial-phenomena reports in Allied air forces is at least as old as WWII**, not a postwar phenomenon. ^[inferred]
- It provides the WWII conventional-explanation menu (**[[concepts/flak-bombe-hypothesis|Flak Bombe]], [[entities/horten-brothers|Me 262]] sightings, flak rockets**) that anticipates the structurally similar 1948 conventional-explanation menu (**[[concepts/horten-flying-wing|Horten flying wing]], Soviet derivatives, Loedding LD-2**). ^[inferred] See [[synthesis/foo-fighters-to-flying-discs]].
- It documents the **first known appearance of the term "foofighter" in an official military intelligence file**, attributed to [[entities/415th-night-fighter-squadron|415th NFS]] combat crews. ^[extracted]
