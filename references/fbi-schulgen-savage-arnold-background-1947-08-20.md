# Schulgen Background-Investigation Request on Savage and Arnold (Fitch → Ladd, 20 August 1947)

A **single-page Office Memorandum** from **E. G. Fitch (FBI Inspector)** to **D. M. Ladd**, dated **20 August 1947**, drafted by **`SWR:rhr`** (i.e., SA S. W. Reynolds), filed at **62-83894-116**, recorded **23 September 1947**, in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR page 134 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]]. ^[extracted] The memo records a Schulgen **second institutional channel** to the FBI, structurally distinct from the famous 5 Sep 1947 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|Schulgen-to-Hoover formal denial letter]] (section 2 serial 84). ^[inferred]

In this earlier channel, **[[entities/george-f-schulgen|Brig Gen Schulgen]] requests the FBI conduct a background investigation on two specific witness-subjects** — Byron B. Savage (RCA Field Engineer, Oklahoma City) and Kenneth Arnold (Boise, Idaho) — to **ascertain whether they have any subversive background and whether they had any ulterior motives for reporting these sightings**. ^[extracted] The two memoranda Schulgen attached are dated **24 July 1947** and **16 July 1947** — predating the [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]] codification of the cooperation regime (30 Jul 1947) by ~6-14 days. ^[extracted]

## Verbatim — the request body

The memo's full body: ^[extracted]

> There is attached hereto copies of two memoranda dated **July 24, 1947, and July 16, 1947**, concerning **Byron B. Savage**, Field Engineer, Radio Corporation of America, Dallas, Texas, 416 N. W. 29th Street, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, and **Kenneth Arnold**, Box 387, Boise, Idaho. These memoranda were made available to **Special Agent S. W. Reynolds of the Liaison Section by General Schulgen of the Army Air Forces Intelligence**.
>
> **General Schulgen requested that the Bureau conduct a background investigation of these two individuals** mentioned in the attached memoranda since they were among the first to sight the alleged flying discs. **He indicated that he desired that the investigation be directed toward ascertaining whether or not either of these individuals have any subversive background and to ascertain whether or not they had any ulterior motives for reporting these sightings.**
>
> RECOMMENDATION
>
> It is recommended this memorandum be forwarded to the Internal Security Section.
>
> Attachment
> SWR:rhr
>
> RECORDED
> E. G. Fitch
> SEP 23 1947

## What this Schulgen channel does

Three observations are paradigm-defining: ^[inferred]

### 1. Schulgen's witness-credibility tasking is concrete and named

The famous 5 Sep 1947 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|Schulgen-to-Hoover formal denial letter]] (serial 84) is a **structural-policy** instrument — it tells Hoover the AAF has no project matching the discs, closing the prosaic-explanation hypothesis. ^[extracted] This 20 Aug 1947 channel is **operationally different**: Schulgen names **two specific witnesses** and asks for **subversive-background-and-motive determinations** on them. ^[extracted]

Both witnesses are **first-generation 1947-wave figures**: ^[extracted]

- **Byron B. Savage** — RCA Field Engineer, Oklahoma City; **17-21 May 1947 sighting** (private pilot, frosty white object, swishing sound, 350° to north). His OCAMA Tinker Field memorandum is preserved in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|serial 130]] OCR pp. 53-54. ^[extracted]
- **Kenneth Arnold** — the **24 June 1947 Mt. Rainier observer**; the original 1947-wave catalyst. ^[extracted]

The dates Schulgen attaches (16 + 24 Jul 1947) are likely the **AAF's working memos on Savage and Arnold respectively**: 16 Jul 1947 corresponds to the date Savage was AFBIR-CO-input-received (per [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|serial 130]] AFBIR-CO inventory), and 24 Jul 1947 corresponds to the OCAMA Tinker Field 24 Jul 1947 Savage memorandum. ^[inferred]

### 2. The "subversive sympathies + ulterior motives" framing is the BB-42 framing

Schulgen's framing — *"subversive background"* + *"ulterior motives for reporting"* — is **structurally identical** to the [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|BB-42]] field-office tasking: investigate sightings to determine if they are *"a bona fide sighting, an imaginary one, or a prank"* (i.e., truth/honest-error/fabrication, with the fabrication being the subversive-or-publicity-driven sub-class). ^[inferred] The 20 Aug 1947 Schulgen request is therefore the **first concrete operational invocation of BB-42's investigative framework on named individuals**. ^[inferred]

The framing is **not** identical to the FBI's own operational priorities at this date: the [[references/fbi-ladd-proposed-bulletin-1947-09-25|25 Sep 1947 Ladd proposal memo]] retroactively notes that the FBI's 9 weeks of BB-42 investigations *"have failed to reveal any indication of subversive individuals being involved in any of the reported sightings"* — i.e., **the subversive-motive hypothesis was empirically refuted by the cooperation regime's own work**. ^[extracted] Schulgen's 20 Aug 1947 request is the **deepest institutional commitment to the subversive-motive frame** that the corpus preserves. ^[inferred]

### 3. The memo's recommendation routes to Internal Security Section

The memo's recommendation (*"It is recommended this memorandum be forwarded to the Internal Security Section"*) places the Savage + Arnold background-investigation **under the FBI's Internal Security Section** — i.e., the same FBI sub-organization that handled subversive-individual loyalty investigations. ^[extracted] This is structurally **the operational routing the 25 Sep 1947 Ladd memo would later document had failed** to find any subversive individuals across all 9 weeks of BB-42 work. ^[inferred]

## Routing through Reynolds

The memo records that **Reynolds personally received the two memoranda from Schulgen** ("**Special Agent S. W. Reynolds of the Liaison Section by General Schulgen of the Army Air Forces Intelligence**"). ^[extracted] This is consistent with Reynolds being the FBI's primary AAF-channel interlocutor since 9 Jul 1947 (the original [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]] negotiation). ^[extracted] The Reynolds → Fitch → Ladd channel for routing AAF-originated background-investigation requests is **the standard FBI pathway** for AAF-tasked work under the cooperation regime. ^[inferred]

## Implications for the cooperation-regime narrative

The 20 Aug 1947 Schulgen request demonstrates that **Schulgen's confidence in the subversive-motive frame remained high through 20 Aug 1947** — i.e., 8 weeks into the cooperation regime, he was still actively naming specific witnesses for FBI-side subversive-background investigation. ^[extracted] This is **temporally compatible** with: ^[inferred]

- the 5 Sep 1947 Schulgen-to-Hoover formal denial letter (admitting the AAF has no matching project, closing the prosaic-explanation frame);
- the ~22 Sep 1947 [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett admission]] (privately admitting discs are likely USG-classified);
- the 27 Sep 1947 [[references/fbi-hoover-mcdonald-letter-1947-09-27|Hoover → McDonald termination letter]] (announcing FBI exit).

The institutional arc is: **Schulgen-confident-in-subversive-frame (20 Aug)** → **Schulgen-formally-disclaiming-AAF-project-knowledge (5 Sep)** → **Garrett-admitting-USG-classified-project (22 Sep)** → **Hoover-terminating-cooperation (27 Sep)**. ^[inferred] The 20 Aug 1947 memo is the **starting datapoint** of this arc — the pre-uncertainty AAF-Intelligence working assumption. ^[inferred]
