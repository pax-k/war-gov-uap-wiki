# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Section 2 (Serials 53-100)

The **second section** of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894]] — 194 OCR pages of declassified documents indexed serials **53 through 100** ^[extracted], covering ~6 August 1947 through ~17 September 1947 ^[inferred] (date stamps on individual filings range 6 Aug → 17 Sep 1947, with retroactive copy stamps from later years on receipt-slip pages). Section 2 is the **direct continuation of [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-1|section 1]]** (serials 1-52, 6 Jul - 12 Sep 1947 ^[ambiguous]), confirming that "section" boundaries in this file are mechanical / paginal, not topical.

## Cover-slip self-identification

The first OCR page of this artifact carries the **FBI Records Center cover slip** with the legend **"62-HQ-83894 / SERIALS 53-100 / 62-HQ-83894-2W / SECTION 2"** — i.e. the section is identified by the file's own indexing system. ^[extracted] FOIPA tag: **`#993087`** (distinct from section 1's `#1366404` and from the individual-serial releases' `#1142292`); Records Center copy-tag dates: `MAY 14 1977 BY ... FOIPA / MAY 2 1977 BY ... 8/11/1274197`. The "1977" copy-stamp is **3 decades earlier than the Aug-2012 / Sep-2022 prefixes** on the individually-released serials — section-level releases used a much earlier FOIPA processing pipeline. ^[inferred]

## Structural function

Section 2 sits **chronologically wedged between BB42's distribution (30 Jul 1947, section 1 serial 33)** and the **Cabell-axis takeover (Feb 1948 → Feb 1949)**. ^[inferred] Three structurally novel events occur inside section 2 that reframe the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]]:

### 1. The Garrett admission — AAF Intelligence's own line cracks (mid-Sep 1947)

The **single most institutionally consequential event** in section 2: ^[inferred] Lt Col **[[entities/g-d-garrett|G. D. Garrett]]** of AAF Intelligence privately tells [[entities/s-w-reynolds|SA S. W. Reynolds]] that he **personally believes flying discs are a classified US Government experiment**, and that "**a Mr. Carroll, who is a scientist attached to the Air Forces Intelligence, was of the same opinion**". ^[extracted] Garrett's reasoning (per Fitch → Ladd memo at section 2 OCR page 128, serial 86):

> Colonel Garrett pointed out that when flying objects were reported seen over Sweden, the "high brass" of the War Department exerted tremendous pressure on the Air Forces Intelligence to conduct research and collect information in an effort to identify these sightings. Colonel Garrett stated that, in contrast to this, we have reported sightings of unknown objects over the United States, and the "high brass" appeared to be totally unconcerned. He indicated this led him to believe that they knew enough about these objects to express no concern.

Reynolds escalates to **Col L. R. Forney** (MID, who confirms with **Generals Chamberlin and Todd** that the Army runs no such project) and Garrett raises the issue with [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]]. ^[extracted] Schulgen agrees a memo will go from Maj Gen McDonald (A2) to **Gen LeMay** (R&D) requesting confirmation. ^[extracted] The result is **Schulgen's 5 September 1947 letter** to FBI Director (section 2 OCR page 123, declassified per **NND 90986**): ^[extracted]

> Attention: Liaison Section. In answer to a verbal request of your Mr. S. W. Reynolds, a complete survey of research activities discloses that the Army Air Forces has no project with the characteristics similar to those which have been associated with the Flying Discs.

The institutional consequence: **the AAF formally certifies to the FBI in writing** that flying discs are **not** a US Government project. ^[extracted] This is the **closure of the AAF-side prosaic-explanation hypothesis as a working frame for the cooperation regime** — at exactly the moment Lt Gen Twining's Wright Field staff is drafting the famous **23 Sep 1947 Twining memo** (the "AMC Opinion" memo concluding the discs are *"real and not visionary or fictitious"*). ^[inferred] Section 2 thus **anchors the FBI's institutional record of the AAF's internal pivot from "secret-project" to "real-but-unidentified"** — the doctrinal substrate of [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]]'s creation in late 1947 / early 1948. ^[inferred]

### 2. The Fugate incident — Hoover BLOCKS joint investigation (30 Aug 1947)

Section 2 captures the **first formal FBI rejection of joint AAF investigation** under [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]]. ^[extracted] The case: **William Rhodes' Phoenix photographs** (see [[references/sighting-rhodes-phoenix-1947-07-07]]). Mr. **George Fugate Jr.** of A-2 4th AAF Hamilton Field was sent under orders from Lt Col Springer to interview Rhodes jointly with the FBI; Fugate **insisted on being introduced as merely a "representative of the United States government" and not as AAF**, and said he doubted Rhodes' photographic negatives would be returned. ^[extracted] SA J. Bailey Brower of Phoenix flagged this as "peculiar procedure"; the negatives were ultimately handed over **only after Rhodes was explicitly told they were going to AAF and would not be returned**. ^[extracted]

Hoover's response — section 2 OCR page 65, serial 68 (30 Aug 1947, FBI HQ → SACS San Francisco and Phoenix): ^[extracted]

> RE SANF MEMO AUGUST TWENTY EIGHT LAST CONCERNING INTERVIEW WITH WILLIAM RHODES, PHOENIX, ARIZONA. SANF ADVISE A-TWO AIR FORCE NOT NECESSARY FOR FUGATE TO PROCEED TO PHOENIX, THAT BUREAU WILL HANDLE INTERVIEW IF DESIRED, BUT JOINT INVESTIGATION NOT DEEMED NECESSARY. IF FUGATE PROCEEDS PHOENIX IN SPITE OF ABOVE, ADVISE HIM YOU ARE NOT AUTHORIZED TO CONDUCT JOINT INVESTIGATION AND NOT POSSIBLE FOR FUGATE TO SIT IN ON INTERVIEW WITH RHODES.

By the time the teletype reaches Phoenix, **the joint interview has already happened**. ^[extracted] The cooperation regime's "**full FBI access**" precondition (Hoover's marginal note from section 1 serial 33) is **operationally inverted**: instead of FBI getting full access to AAF-recovered discs, the AAF representative is on-scene before the FBI's prohibition arrives, **with the Bureau's agent participating** but the AAF taking the negatives. ^[inferred] This is the **first documented operational erosion of BB42** — not termination, but **competitive-channel friction** that slowly delegitimizes the regime. ^[inferred]

### 3. The first BB42-cited field-office report (Hedstrom Twin Falls, 19 Aug 1947)

Section 2 contains the **first explicit field-office citation of "Bureau Bulletin No. 42, paragraph B"** in operational reporting. ^[extracted] FBI Butte SAC **Banister** files a 20 Aug 1947 URGENT teletype on the **Hedstrom / Schultz Twin Falls Idaho mass-formation observation** (serial 66, OCR page 56): ^[extracted]

> INVESTIGATION CONTINUING IN LINE WITH BUREAU INSTRUCTIONS CONTAINED BUREAU BULLETIN NO. FORTY TWO, PARAGRAPH B, DATED JULY THIRTY LAST. IN EVENT BUREAU IN POSSESSION OF ANY INFORMATION CONCERNING EXPERIMENTAL ACTIVITIES ON PART OF ARMY AIR FORCES WHICH MAY EXPLAIN THESE PHENOMENA, ADVICE WOULD BE GREATLY APPRECIATED. IT IS BELIEVED CONTINUED APPEARANCE OF SUCH OBJECTS WITHOUT OFFICIAL EXPLANATION MAY RESULT IN HYSTERIA OR PANIC TWIN FALLS, IDAHO.

Hoover's reply (25 Aug 1947, Butte teletype): "**EXTENSIVE INQUIRIES AT ARMY AIR FORCES REVEAL THAT NO RESEARCH OR EXPERIMENTS WERE BEING CONDUCTED BY AAF IN THE VICINITY OF TWIN FALLS, IDAHO ON OR ABOUT AUGUST NINETEEN.**" ^[extracted] **The 25 Aug AAF denial sits in section 2 ~10 days before Schulgen's 5 Sep blanket denial** — the latter is in fact the **system-wide formalization** of what the Twin Falls case demanded as a single-event answer. ^[inferred]

The N=47 inventory shows **profound shift from section 1's pattern**: ^[inferred]

- **Civilian mail volume drops** — section 2 has ~5 civilian-channel items (Williams AL, Sarbanis cryptanalysis, Wenyon Delaware, Parker VT, Merchant via Winchell) vs section 1's ~12.
- **AAF inter-agency forwards rise** — section 2 has ~4-5 multi-MOIC packages from 4AF / SF SAC (serials 53, 95, 97, 98) plus the **Schulgen 5 Sep formal denial letter** (serial 84) — section 1 had 1 (the 4 Aug Graham forwards = serial 52).
- **Pilot/LE-witness sighting reports rise** — section 2 has Hedstrom/Frazier (Twin Falls PD), Jenkins/Raney (Portland PD), Hatfield (Atlantic ASW pilot), Ryherd (4AF instructor pilot), Stockton/Jennings (TN), Bartl (Agate Beach OR), Dupuis (Oswego OR), Johnson (Cascade Mts) — **8 sightings vs section 1's ~4 substantive sightings**.

The operational pattern: **section 2 is the BB42 regime running at full throttle**, with civilian-mail backwash declining as **AAF cooperation forwards saturate the channel** while **AAF Intelligence's own internal credibility collapses on the secret-project hypothesis**. ^[inferred]

## Themes

### Press posture: "no censorship" but "no leads run out"

Serial 99 captures **Maj Gen Hale's 25 Aug 1947 letter to Henry McLeod, Seattle Times city editor**: *"as far as the Army Air Force was concerned, they had no information which would definitely clear up the situation; that as far as interviewing persons, Mr. McLeod could feel free to interview whomsoever he desired, in that **there was no censorship at the present time**; that **in the interest of economy, the Fourth Air Force Headquarters would not run out every lead or report of flying discs**, but that if well-founded and apparently reliable information were received, that such information would be given attention."* ^[extracted]

This is the **earliest FBI-corpus capture of the AAF's deliberate "no-censorship-but-no-investigation" posture** — the institutional precursor to the [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] / GRUDGE public-debunking arc. ^[inferred]

### The "Mr. Carroll" lead

Garrett's admission (serial 86) names a **scientist "Mr. Carroll" attached to Air Forces Intelligence** as also believing discs are a classified USG experiment. ^[extracted] The corpus does not name this Carroll further; he may be **Wallace W. "Doc" Carroll**, an OSS-era propaganda-and-information-warfare scientist, or a different Carroll on the AAF Intelligence civilian-scientist staff. ^[ambiguous] **Open thread: identify "Mr. Carroll".**

### Soviet-espionage cover-story

The 14 Aug 1947 LA-papers Soviet-espionage story (serial 62-63) is **flagged by Hoover personally** and explicitly denied across G-2 / ONI / Air Corps Intelligence / CIG. ^[extracted] The story's structural function: **a counter-prosaic frame the press circulated** in parallel to the AAF's wire-released-disc cover-story (BB42) — both serve to **anchor disc-discussion in a security-state vocabulary** rather than physical-object investigation. ^[inferred] Compare BB42's "subversive individuals creating mass hysteria" framing.
