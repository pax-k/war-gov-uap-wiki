# USPER Statement: Helicopter Orb Encounter (2025)

A redacted three-page SECRET//NOFORN witness statement (Mistral-OCR'd) describing an aerial-search and orb-encounter incident "on 2025" at a redacted facility. The narrating witness is identified only as `[WITNESS 1]`, **a senior US intelligence official**.

- **File**: `sources/usper-statement-redacted.json` — three OCR'd pages, headed and footed `SECRET//NOFORN`. The version we are reading is the public/redacted form: locations, call signs, federal partners, and times-of-year are all bracket-redacted.

## What the statement covers

The statement describes a single multi-hour event:

- **Initial context**: a daytime aerial search (helicopter `[CALL SIGN 1]`) over a mountain range west of `[SITE CODE NAME]` for **debris from earlier eyewitness reports** of orbs/lights and "thuds as if something had fallen and hit the ground." A short orbit identifies a **large cavern entrance** at `[COORDINATES]`.
- **Hand-off and refuel cycle**: dropoff of `[WITNESS 2]` to `[FEDERAL PARTNER 3]` personnel; helicopters cycle through refueling at `[SITE CODE NAME]`.
- **First contact (~22:02)**: an LP/OP team using FLIR sees an orb described as **"super-hot"** hovering at ground level, then ascending and **breaking into two objects**. The pilot's co-pilot also reports "something emerging from the two objects" under NVG and traveling in a different direction at high speed. See [[references/sighting-helicopter-orb-encounter-2025]].
- **Close approach (~22:18)**: orb reportedly came within **~10 feet** of `[CALL SIGN 1]`.
- **Repeated flare-up / flare-down formations (~22:27 onward)**: multiple horizontal-line orb formations flaring up one at a time, then flaring down in reverse order — see [[concepts/orb-flare-formation]].
- **Apparent engagement of military aircraft**: orbs "broke off" from the helicopter to pursue five `[MILITARY AIRCRAFT]` conducting a training mission, including a flare-up over the descending aircraft — see [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement]].
- **Triangle formation**: at ~22:49 a swarm of lights with three distinct orbs in a triangle formation.

## Limitations

- All location, time-of-year, callsign, and partner-organization identifiers are redacted; the statement cannot be geolocated or precisely dated from this artifact alone.
- The statement is single-author (`[WITNESS 1]`); the helicopter pilots' independent reports and the LP/OP FLIR records are referenced but not attached.
- No instrumental data (FLIR captures, NVG video, JOC logs) are included with the OCR'd file as ingested.
- The "thuds / fallen debris" framing implies a **prior recovery effort** and a possibly larger campaign of UAP encounters at this facility. ^[inferred]
- The redacted SECRET//NOFORN classification on the document confirms the underlying incident is treated as a national-security matter, but the version we hold is the **redacted** one.
