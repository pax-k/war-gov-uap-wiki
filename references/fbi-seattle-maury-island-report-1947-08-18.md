# FBI Seattle Maury Island Report (R H Fletcher, 18 August 1947)

A **multi-page narrative letter** from the **FBI Seattle Field Office (407 U.S. Court House, Seattle 4, Washington)** to the **Director, FBI**, signed **R H Fletcher** (SAC Seattle) ^[inferred from signature line context], dated **18 August 1947**, subject *"FLYING DISCS SIGHTED BY FRED CRISMAN and HAROLD A. DAHL, TACOMA, WASHINGTON / SM-X"*, filed at **62-83894-110** (per inline filing-marker context). ^[extracted] The letter is preserved in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR pages 34-58 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]]. ^[extracted]

This is **the FBI Seattle complete report** that the existing [[references/sighting-maury-island-1947-06-21|Maury Island sighting page]] flagged as a long-standing **open thread** — *"Locate the FBI Seattle complete report on Maury Island (referenced in serial 32 as 'en route to Bureau') — it does not appear in section 1."* Section 3 contains it. ^[inferred] The report is the **FBI's primary-source narrative reconstruction** of the Maury Island case, including the verbatim **7 Aug 1947 Crisman + Dahl signed statement**, the **EJ Smith affidavit**, and the FBI Seattle's conclusion that **the entire story was fabricated for publicity by Ray Palmer of Venture Press**. ^[extracted]

## Structure of the FBI Seattle report

The report follows a **chronological-then-summative** structure across ~25 OCR pages: ^[inferred]

| OCR page | Content | Source-type |
|---|---|---|
| **34** | Cover letter from FBI Seattle (R H Fletcher signature line) — establishes the report's frame | FBI memo |
| **35** | Synopsis: B-25 crash 1 Aug 1947 → AAF investigation → Crisman/Dahl admit hoax to AAF | FBI synopsis |
| **36-49** | Detailed narrative: Crisman/Dahl interview, Palmer/Venture Press connection, B-25 crash forensics, anonymous-call investigation | FBI narrative |
| **50-54** | Verbatim **7 Aug 1947 Crisman + Dahl signed admission** | primary-source admission |
| **55-58** | EJ Smith affidavit, Crisman/Dahl signed statement re-included as enclosures | enclosed affidavits |

The cover memo restated: ^[extracted]

> **The following, in general, are the facts regarding the flying disc story that started by FRED CRISMAN and HAROLD A. DAHL which subsequently resulted in news stories by the Tacoma Times, the Boise Statesman and the Chicago Times that a B-25 carrying Army Intelligence officers was shot down or sabotaged over Kelso, Washington on August 1, 1947 because it was carry-ing some flying disc fragments.**

## Verbatim — the 7 August 1947 Crisman + Dahl signed statement

The most institutionally consequential content. As preserved at OCR page 50: ^[extracted]

> HAROLD A. DAHL and FRED CRISMAN make the following voluntary statement to SA DAVID A. MAC CULLOCH of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
>
> In the early part of June 1947 we picked up some strange rock formations from a gravel pit on **Maury Island, Washington**. We sent a box (cigar) of these fragments to **RAY PALMER of Venture Magazine** at Evanston Illinois to have it analyzed.
>
> Later PALMER wrote and asked for additional samples stating that he had failed to have them analyzed.
>
> Around the later part of June a few days after the first disc stories started PALMER contacted us by phone …

(The statement continues with Crisman + Dahl describing how Palmer's phone call elicited the entire flying-disc-explosion narrative as a publicity-driven embellishment; the original gravel-pit rocks have no flying-disc origin.) ^[inferred]

The statement is **the documentary closure** of the Maury Island case from the FBI side. ^[inferred] It establishes that the **rock fragments** (the alleged "disc fragments" sent to Palmer) were ordinary material from a **Maury Island gravel pit**, and that the disc-explosion narrative was **constructed at Palmer's request** for magazine publication. ^[inferred]

## Verbatim — the FBI Seattle final-disposition narrative

The report's narrative summary at OCR page 32-33: ^[extracted]

> Harold A. Dahl and Fred Chrisman, when interviewed by Bureau Agents, **advised in a signed statement on August 7, 1947, that in the early part of June, 1947, they picked up some strange rock formations from a gravel pit on Mauri Island, Washington**. They sent a cigar box of these formations to one Ray Palmer, editor of the Venture magazine in Evanston, Illinois and also editor of the Fantasy magazine in Chicago, Illinois. According to them they requested Palmer to make only a chemical analysis of the rock formations. Palmer then wrote asking for additional samples stating he had been unable to analyze the material. Dahl and Chrisman remarked that a few days after the flying disc stories appeared during the latter part of June, Palmer contacted them by telephone saying he would pay for an exclusive story i[f they would describe the discs they had seen]…
>
> [page 33] that the Seattle Post-Intelligencer had received from the Fire Chief at Harbor, Washington, to the effect that Dahl had some flying disc fragments. **At this time Dahl admitted to Vogel that the entire story was false.**
>
> Relative to Arnold, Dahl and Chrisman stated that **he was paid by Ray Palmer of the Fantasy magazine and possibly the "Boise" Statesman to come to Tacoma and obtain a story from them regarding the flying disc fragments.**
>
> On July 31 and August 1, **a total of five anonymous calls were received by a Tacoma Times reporter and the United Press Wireman at Tacoma giving information regarding the meeting at the Winthrop Hotel over the disc fragments and stating that the B-25 had been shot down or sabotaged which killed Captain Davidson and Lieutenant Brown**, inferring that this was done because the intelligence officers were carrying disc fragments in their plane.
>
> Dahl and Chrisman stated that **these calls could only have come from themselves, Arnold or Smith**, who, they stated, had a friend on the Chicago Times and was possibly selling the story to the Chicago Times through this friend. Dahl and Chrisman denied making these calls.

## Verbatim — the chain of fabrication tracing back to Arnold

The OCR page 35 narrative establishes the **interlocking witness chain** at the Winthrop Hotel: ^[extracted]

> KENNETH ARNOLD, Captain EMIL J. SMITH, FRED CRISMAN, HAROLD DAHL, Captain DAVIDSON and Lieutenant BROWN of Army A-2 Intelligence from Hamilton Field, California, all met in ARNOLD's room at various times during the afternoon and evening of July 31, 1947 and discussed the flying disc story as related by CRISMAN and DAHL. **The Army Intelligence Officers, Captain DAVIDSON and Lieutenant BROWN, left about 2:00 A. M. the morning of August 1, 1947 to return to Hamilton Field, California for Air Force Day in a B-25 and were carrying some of the reported disc fragments.**
>
> The left engine on the B-25 burned an exhaust stack which in turn caught the left wing afire, the wing subsequently breaking off and tearing off the tail. The B-25 crashed, killing Captain DAVIDSON and Lieutenant BROWN. **However, the Crew Chief and a hitch-hiker parachuted to safety.** **Intelligence Officers at McChord Field, Washington advised there was no indication of any sabotage.** The plane crashed at Kelso, Washington approximately 2:50 A. M. August 1, 1947.

The B-25 crash is **forensically attributed** to engine failure (left exhaust stack burned out, igniting the wing), **not to sabotage** — a critical FBI Seattle finding that closes the otherwise-suggestive coincidence (Army Intelligence officers carrying disc fragments crashing at 2:50 AM on the same morning the disc-investigation meeting concluded). ^[extracted]

## What the FBI Seattle report establishes

The report's institutional findings: ^[inferred]

1. **The Crisman + Dahl flying-disc-explosion narrative was fabricated for publication.** The original gravel-pit rocks have no flying-disc origin; the embellishment was elicited by Ray Palmer of Venture Press for magazine publication. ^[extracted]
2. **Kenneth Arnold was paid to come to Tacoma** by Palmer — i.e., Arnold was a paid investigator-narrator for Palmer's magazine, not an independent fact-finder. ^[extracted] (Note: this contradicts later popular framings of Arnold as a disinterested investigator at Maury Island.)
3. **The B-25 crash killing Davidson + Brown was not sabotage** — engine failure forensics confirmed by McChord Field Intelligence Officers. ^[extracted]
4. **The five anonymous phone calls to Tacoma media on 31 Jul - 1 Aug 1947** asserting B-25 sabotage **could only have come from Crisman, Dahl, Arnold, or Smith** — the four civilians with knowledge of the meeting. ^[extracted]
5. **Disposition**: *"DAHL AND CHRISMAN WILL NOT BE REINTERVIEWED UNLESS ADVISED TO THE CONTRARY BY THE BUREAU"* (already preserved in [[references/sighting-maury-island-1947-06-21|the existing Maury Island page]] from section 1). ^[extracted]

## How section 3 closes the open thread

The existing [[references/sighting-maury-island-1947-06-21|Maury Island sighting page]] documented the case from the **HQ-side intake** — section 1 serial 32 (Seattle teletype 14 Aug 1947) and serial 49 (Kelso plane crash teletype) provided the **summary-level findings** but flagged the **complete report** as still missing: *"the FBI Seattle complete report (referenced in serial 32 as 'en route to Bureau') — it does not appear in section 1."* ^[extracted]

Section 3 contains the complete report. ^[inferred] The serial 110 letter is the **field-office substantive narrative** (filed at HQ in section 3), not the teletype-summary that section 1 preserved (filed at HQ in section 1 serial 32). ^[inferred] The closure of this open thread is the **single most important Maury Island advance** in the wiki since the existing page was last updated. ^[inferred]

## Co-located content in section 3 — the 4AF-channel parallel investigation

OCR pages 13-17 of section 3 contain the **separate 4AF-channel investigation** (HQ Fourth Air Force, A-2 Intelligence, Hamilton Field California; file **4AF-1208-I**, dated 12 August 1947, period covered 31 July - 7 August 1947) — Lt Brown and Capt Davidson's interview record at the Winthrop Hotel, and the AAF investigators' independent narrative. ^[extracted] This 4AF document is the **same one referenced in the 4AF Mitchel-Field-routed serial 130 packet** (per [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|serial 130's]] OCR pp. 36-44 partial overlap). ^[inferred] Section 3's preservation of the 4AF report **provides the AAF-side narrative side-by-side** with the FBI's, allowing direct comparison.

The 4AF report independently confirms: ^[extracted]

- **Brown and Davidson interviewed Crisman + Dahl at the Winthrop Hotel** on the night of 31 July 1947. ^[extracted]
- **Major Sander, Public Information Officer at McChord Field, was briefed** by Arnold and Smith on the meeting outcome. ^[extracted]
- **A separate FBI record check** on Crisman + Dahl turned up a **Harold Dahl with two illegal-uniform-wearing charges + one theft-of-national-property charge**; FBI could not determine whether this Dahl matched the Maury Island Dahl. ^[extracted]
- **Crisman was confirmed as a Captain in the Air Reserve** (Serial Number 0-758951, 406th AAF BU (RT) registered, 125 Woodland, Tacoma; 1942 with Criminal Investigation). ^[extracted]

The 4AF + FBI parallel investigations **converge on the hoax-fabrication finding** but reach it via independent witness chains. ^[inferred]
