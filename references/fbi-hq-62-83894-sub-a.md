# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Sub-File A

The **press-clippings appendix** to [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894]]. Filed at FBI Central Records Center as `Class 0062 / Case 83894 / Sub A / Vol 1 / Serial 1 OPEN`. ^[extracted] **287 KB / 124 OCR pages** (Mistral OCR), tagged `8/11/1274151 / RRP003XKT` — same Aug-2012 FOIPA-tranche prefix as serials 164 + 220 + 438 + 449 of the main numerical sequence. ^[inferred]

## HEADLINE — sub-file A is a single perpetually-OPEN serial slot acting as the file's press-shadow reservoir

Sub-A is **structurally novel** for the FBI corpus in three ways: ^[inferred]

1. **It is a sub-file, not a serial.** The main numerical sequence in 62-HQ-83894 closes serials at issuance — each clipping or memo gets its own incrementing number (e.g. serial 130, serial 220, serial 403). Sub-A instead is **one open-ended sub-classification** (`62-HQ-83894-A`) that **stays "OPEN"** as a single serial slot, accumulating ~120 distinct items over **12 years** (5 Jul 1947 → 25 May 1959) without ever closing. The cover slip's literal `Class / Case # / Sub / Vol / Serial #` reads `0062 / 83894 / A / 1 / 1 OPEN`. ^[extracted]

2. **It is the file's press-shadow.** Every item is a clipped press article, AP/UPI wire copy, or foreign newspaper cutting — routed via Bureau forms `0-19` (Rev. dates 1955-1959) and `0-20`, with HQ executive distribution lists (Tolson / Ladd / Nichols / Belmont / etc.) and a stamp `[paper] / Date: [date] / 162-83894-A NOT RECORDED`. ^[extracted] The volume is **industrial scale**: ~120 distinct clippings vs. the main file's prior single book-jacket clipping at [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-403|serial 403]]. ^[inferred]

3. **It refines (does not extend) the [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|file's]] channel inventory.** The prior wiki framing of channel (a) — *Clipped public publication* — was anchored on serial 403 (one book jacket, "Filed only"). Sub-A reveals that channel (a) is **continuous, high-volume, and operationally routine**: HQ Central Research Section ran a press-clipping pipeline against the saucer topic from at least Jul 1947 through May 1959. ^[inferred] Channel (a) is therefore not "filed only" — it has its own dedicated sub-file slot in the FBI's records architecture. **The intake-channel inventory does not grow from 10 to 11**; channel (a)'s scale is rewritten upward by an order of magnitude. ^[inferred]

## Cross-cuts

### Channel (a) industrial scale

The wiki's prior framing of channel (a) as "filed only" (anchored on serial 403) **understated channel (a) by ~120×**. ^[inferred] HQ Central Research Section ran a press-clipping pipeline at industrial scale across ~12 years. Specific implications:

- Channel (a) is **continuous**, not episodic.
- The HQ executive distribution list (Tolson / Belmont / DeLoach / Mohr / Holloman / Gandy / etc., with Hoover-era principals stable across the 1947-1959 window) means the press-clipping shadow was **read at the executive level**, not just filed. ^[inferred]
- Channel (a) is the **doctrinal mirror** of the main file: if the main numerical sequence is what the FBI received from outside (channels b-i) plus what the FBI generated (channel c), sub-A is what the FBI **chose to clip from the public press** about its own institutional posture and the wider topic. ^[inferred]

### Late-period file activity

Sub-A's latest items (1957-59) are particularly informative because the main numerical sequence in the wiki gap between [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|section 7]] (ends Jun 1954) and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438|serial 438]] (May 1964) is currently uningested (sections 8-10 still queued).
