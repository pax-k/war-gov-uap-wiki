# Loedding Flying Disc (drawing LD-2)

A US Air Materiel Command in-house concept drawing of a flying-disc-shaped aircraft, designated **LD-2**. Referenced in a 24 September 1947 memo (TSNAD-2B, "Flying Disc") from [[entities/wright-patterson-afb|Wright-Patterson AFB]] to the Commanding General, Army Air Forces, attention AC/AS-2, Maj Gen George McDonald.

## What the memo says

> "In compliance with General McDonald's telephone conversation, this date, to Colonel H. M. McCoy, T-2, Hq. AMC, attached herewith is a copy of a drawing entitled, 'Loedding Flying Disc', designated LD-2. Because of patent rights involved, it is requested that a record be kept of all persons reviewing this drawing by witnessing with signatures and dates directly on the drawing."

> "This drawing should be returned to this office when it has served its purpose so that it can be turned over to the Patent Office Hq. AMC for official record." ^[extracted]

## What we can infer

- The drawing was treated as **patent-sensitive intellectual property**, suggesting it was a real engineering design proposal — not a sketch made to characterize sightings. ^[inferred]
- "Loedding" almost certainly refers to **Alfred Loedding**, an [[entities/air-materiel-command|AMC]] aerodynamicist who was active at Wright-Patterson on disc-shaped aircraft concepts in this period. ^[inferred] *(Identification not stated in the source.)*
- The handling protocol — sign-in log directly on the drawing — implies extremely small distribution. The drawing existed in (probably) a single physical copy. ^[inferred]

## Why it matters

The Loedding disc places a **plausible US-side disc-shaped aircraft program inside the same office** that was simultaneously evaluating sighting reports about flying discs. This complicates any clean separation between "what was being built" and "what was being reported" in 1948. ^[inferred]

It also pairs naturally with the [[concepts/horten-flying-wing|Horten thesis]]: USAF intelligence was tracking foreign flying-wing programs (Horten / Soviet) **and** working on a US disc concept of its own. The picture of the era is multi-sourced disc-shape engineering activity, not a single mystery. ^[inferred]
