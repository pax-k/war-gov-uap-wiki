# FBI FD-302: Lead-Driver UAP Witness (Sept 2023, redacted, serial-3)

A 2-page redacted Mistral-OCR'd [[entities/fbi|FBI]] FD-302 (Rev. 5-8-10) recording the **second witness interview** for the same September 2023 UAP incident already documented in [[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023|serial-4]]. This witness was the **female lead-vehicle driver** of the contractor convoy (serial-4's witness was the front-seat passenger). The form was drafted **2 October 2023** — same date as serial-4 — by an FBI Special Agent and a second interviewer in the witness's office (in person, building number redacted), with the witness's supervisor sitting in.

## Provenance

- **Source bundle**: `sources/serial-3_redacted.json` (Mistral-OCR; the previously open thread "locate serial-3" closes with this ingest).
- **Form**: FBI **FD-302** (Rev. 5-8-10), 2 pages (page 1 body + page 2 FD-302a continuation).
- **Date drafted**: 2 October 2023 (same day as serial-4).
- **Investigation date**: September 2023 (specific day redacted, but cross-reference with serial-4 narrative implies the same morning).
- **Interview medium**: **In Person** at the witness's office in Building [REDACTED] — *distinct from* serial-4's FaceTime interview. ^[extracted]
- **Observers**: the witness's "supervisor sat in on the interview" — first observer-of-observer corroboration in the corpus's interview infrastructure. ^[inferred]
- **All signatures, file numbers, witness identifiers, location names, and contractor identifiers are redacted.**

## Document structure

### Page 1 (FD-302 body)

- Form header: "FD-302 (Rev. 5-8-10) — 1 of 2 — FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION — Date of entry 10/2/2023".
- **Interview block**: "On September 2023, [REDACTED] and FBI Special Agent [REDACTED] interviewed [REDACTED] office and [REDACTED] cell), in Building [REDACTED] the [REDACTED] and [REDACTED] supervisor sat in on the interview…"
- **Witness background**: identified as the "[REDACTED] / [REDACTED] at [REDACTED] for [REDACTED] years" — a long-tenured site role. The role redaction is consistent with a **range/site supervisor or test director** — she "had restricted the air space in [REDACTED] for the upcoming tests" and was in the **first vehicle**. ^[inferred]
- **Convoy structure**: 7:02 am standard base brief; three vehicles departing for the test site. **Witness drove the first vehicle with [REDACTED] as front passenger** (the front-seat passenger maps to serial-4's drone-pilot witness, ^[inferred] — the witness count and seating order match). Two more contractors were in vehicles two and three.
- **Gate anomaly** (pre-sighting): between 7:15–7:30 am she drove down [REDACTED] to a gate that restricted access; she tried to open it remotely with a fob — the gate "**opened just a little and then closed on three separate tries**." On the **fourth attempt** the gate opened completely and stayed open. The witness explicitly notes "there were no prior operational problems with that particular gate and there were no operating issues with the gate after September [REDACTED]." This is a **bounded electromagnetic-or-mechanical anomaly** strictly co-temporal with the sighting. ^[inferred] Serial-4 mentioned the gate giving trouble but did not record this 3-tries-then-4th-attempt detail or the no-prior/no-after claim. ^[inferred]
- **Sighting onset**: "As [REDACTED] was driving through the gate she looked up and saw a [continued on page 2]…"
- **Footer**: "Investigation on 09/2023 at [REDACTED] United States (In Person). File # [REDACTED]. Date drafted 10/2/2023 by [REDACTED]." Standard FBI control disclaimer in footer.

### Page 2 (FD-302a continuation)

- Form header: "FD-302a (Rev. 5-8-10)" with redacted banner image at the top.
- **Object description**: full sighting narrative (see [[references/sighting-fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-2023-09]] for the consolidated multi-witness account).
- **Witness reaction**: "initially annoyed when she saw the object because she had restricted the ranges for the tests they were going to conduct that morning but then she realized the object was not an aircraft or drone." This matches serial-4's "the lead-vehicle driver was reportedly annoyed" detail — the *driver* in serial-4 is *this witness*. ^[inferred]
- **Cross-witness corroboration** (extracted): "[REDACTED] in the second vehicle said he saw the object too." Serial-4 reported "the second-vehicle occupant tried unsuccessfully to see the object" — these two statements are **superficially in tension**. ^[ambiguous] Possible reconciliations: (a) two different second-vehicle occupants — driver vs passenger — with different sighting outcomes; (b) the second-vehicle witness saw the object **only briefly** before serial-4's witness turned to look; (c) one of the two FD-302s captures the second-vehicle account incorrectly. The wiki preserves both verbatim and flags the contradiction.
- **Reporting reluctance**: the witness "would not have reported the object if she had seen it by herself. Several of her co-workers subsequently made fun of her due to her report." This is **first explicit corpus-level evidence of the social cost of reporting** within the contractor community. ^[inferred]
- **No photos/video**: explicitly noted in the narrative.
- **Witness baseline**: "had never seen anything like the object she observed" — same negative-space framing as serial-4 but from a different witness class (range/test director, not drone pilot). ^[inferred]

## Why this artifact matters

- **Closes the "locate serial-3" open thread** ([[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023]] and [[projects/uap/uap]] both flagged this). Confirms that **the same FBI release tranche contains at least two FD-302s on the same incident** — interview infrastructure produced parallel records, not a single witness-rollup. ^[inferred]
- **Promotes the Sept 2023 sighting from single-witness to multi-witness multi-vantage.** The lead-vehicle driver and the lead-vehicle passenger both saw the object; a second-vehicle occupant claim is now contested across the two FD-302s. ^[inferred] See [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]] for the corroboration upgrade.
- **Provides a parallel morphology description from a non-pilot trained-observer**, allowing direct comparison: see [[references/sighting-fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-2023-09]] for the side-by-side morphology table. The two descriptions agree on east-to-west motion, helicopter-class length, intense single light on the eastern end, and silent operation — and **diverge** on cigar/linear shape, bronze/gray color, and "almost hovering" vs. "moving parallel to the ground." This is a **textbook case of multi-vantage morphology drift** of the kind documented for the [[references/sighting-dark-kite-western-us|2026 Dark Kite]] (single object, multiple shape descriptors). ^[inferred]
- **First in-person FD-302 in the corpus** — distinct from serial-4's FaceTime modality. The interview was conducted in the witness's own office, with her supervisor present, suggesting the FBI considered her **in a chain of custody at the test site itself** (range owner / restricted-airspace authority). ^[inferred]
- **First explicit "social-cost-of-reporting" entry** in the modern corpus — co-workers ridiculed the witness after she reported. This is structurally consistent with the witness-class arguments in [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]]: testimony costs reputation, raising the prior that what is reported was not casually invented. ^[inferred]
- **Gate anomaly as a co-temporal physical effect**: the 3-tries-then-4th-attempt-and-stays-open gate is a discrete, bounded mechanical/electromagnetic event in the **same minutes** as the sighting, with explicit before/after baselines. The FD-302 records it without endorsing it as causal. ^[inferred] This is the **first co-temporal physical-effect record** in the modern (2023+) corpus.
