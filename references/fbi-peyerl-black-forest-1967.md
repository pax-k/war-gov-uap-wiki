# Peyerl WW2 Black-Forest Saucer Disclosure (FBI Miami, 26 Apr 1967)

A **26 April 1967 walk-in interview** at the **FBI Miami Field Office** by **Paul L. Peyerl** (born 3 May 1924, Austria; in 1967, Eastern Airlines mechanic at Miami FL; ex-Luftwaffe test pilot 1944-1945; ex-British POW 1945-1946; naturalized US citizen 1958). ^[extracted] Peyerl disclosed his alleged personal involvement in a **secret 1944 Black Forest saucer-shaped aircraft test program** and supplied a Xerox copy of an in-flight negative + a still photograph of the craft in a hangar. ^[extracted]

The interview is documented in a **Letterhead Memorandum** dated 8 June 1967 ^[extracted], which the Miami SAC forwards to FBI HQ on **27 June 1967** with four enclosures (memorandum + two photos + Peyerl's autobiographical statement). HQ files the LHM at **62-HQ-83894 serial 460** and forwards a copy to the **Inspector General, USAF, by Form 6-11-67**. ^[extracted]

This is the **first FBI-corpus walk-in saucer disclosure tied to WW2 German aerospace engineering** ^[inferred] — and an early primary-source datapoint on the **post-1947 disc-as-Nazi-prototype hypothesis** that runs as a recurring civilian-research thread through later UAP literature. ^[inferred]

## Peyerl's biographical claims (per the LHM)

- **Born**: 3 May 1924, Austria. ^[extracted]
- **Service**: Graduated from the German Air Academy in 1943; assigned to the **Luftwaffe** on the Russian Front. ^[extracted]
- **End of 1944**: Released from front duty; assigned as **test pilot to a top secret project in the Black Forest of Austria** ^[extracted] (note: the geographic ambiguity — Black Forest is conventionally located in southwest Germany, not Austria — is in the OCR; Peyerl may have meant a different Austrian forest, or the OCR may have transposed nation-of-origin). ^[ambiguous]
- **March 1945**: Reassigned to the Western Front; shot down by the British **14 March 1945**. ^[extracted]
- **1945-1946**: Held prisoner by the British in London and Brussels; released 1946. ^[extracted]
- **26 December 1951**: Departed Bremerhaven, Germany for the US. ^[extracted]
- **7 January 1952**: Entered the US in New Jersey. ^[extracted]
- **1958**: Naturalized in Miami. ^[extracted]
- **1967**: Eastern Airlines mechanic, 1430 Southwest 76th Street, Miami, FL. ^[extracted]

## The aircraft Peyerl claims to have photographed

> *"During this period he observed the aircraft described above. It was saucer shaped, about twenty-one feet in diameter, radio controlled, and mounted several jet engines around the exterior portion of the craft. He further described the exterior portion as revolving around the dome in the center which remained stationary."* ^[extracted]

Operational claims ^[extracted]:

- **Photographed at 7,000 m (~20,000 ft) altitude**, 30-second time exposure.
- A still photograph of the craft *"parked in a hanger"* — *"taken at the risk of my life"* per Peyerl.
- Peyerl claims to retain the **original negatives**.
- *"This type of aircraft was responsible for the downing of at least one American B-26 airplane."*

Engineering claims (per Peyerl, copied from a board in the hangar) ^[extracted]:

> *"Fuel mixture of N₂H₄O in Methyl Alcohol (CH₃OH) rather than 'oxygen-holding' mixture of hydrogen peroxide H₂O₂ in water. 7m 1,3m high two rocket motors; smooth flow, rotary drive over 2,000 meters per second…"*

The fuel-chemistry detail is structurally significant ^[inferred] — N₂H₄O is **not a standard chemical formula** (likely OCR-degraded from N₂H₄·H₂O = hydrazine hydrate, or N₂H₄ = hydrazine, both used in WW2 German rocketry). ^[inferred] The CH₃OH (methanol) reference is consistent with the **Walter HWK 109-509** rocket engine fuel chemistry used in the Me 163 Komet and similar German rocket aircraft (which used a hydrazine hydrate / methanol blend called *C-Stoff* with hydrogen peroxide *T-Stoff* as oxidizer). ^[inferred — historical-cross-reference] The **rotary drive over 2,000 m/s** specification is unphysical for any conventional WW2 propulsion system. ^[inferred]

## Engineer attribution

Peyerl attributes the design to **(First Name Unknown) KUEHR**, *"a German engineer whose present whereabouts is unknown to him"* ^[extracted]:

- KUEHR *"unsuccessfully attempted to avoid the German draft, but was apprehended by the Gestapo in Vienna, Austria, sometime during late 1943 or early 1944."* ^[extracted]
- Peyerl assumes KUEHR *"was taken into custody by Allied Forces upon the termination of hostilities."* ^[extracted]
- Peyerl assumes *"the secrets pertaining to this aircraft were captured by Allied Forces."* ^[extracted]

KUEHR is **not identifiable** in the standard German aerospace engineering literature on WW2 disc-shaped aircraft ^[inferred — he does not match Heinrich Focke, Andreas Epp, Rudolf Schriever, or Klaus Habermohl, the conventionally cited names]. The **Gestapo-apprehension-for-draft-avoidance** detail is structurally distinctive — most WW2 German aerospace engineers were either drafted normally, exempted via aircraft-industry priority, or were post-1945 Operation Paperclip targets. ^[inferred]

## Peyerl's stated motivation

> *"PEYERL stated he has withheld this information because he assumed the United States possessed it. He has become increasingly concerned because of the unconfirmed reports concerning a similar object and denials the United States has such an aircraft. He feels such a weapon would be beneficial in Vietnam and would prevent the further loss of American lives which was his paramount purpose in contacting the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)."* ^[extracted]

The **Vietnam-utility framing** dates the disclosure to the post-Tet-offensive UFO-publicity-spike context of 1967. ^[inferred] Peyerl's reasoning chain — *"if these UFOs are real and the US has the technology, why are we losing pilots in Vietnam?"* — is structurally novel within the FBI corpus ^[inferred] and reflects the period's tendency to read UFO sightings as evidence of an **undisclosed US aerospace program**. ^[inferred]

## FBI's institutional handling

The Miami SAC's 8 Jun 1967 communicative chain ^[extracted]:

- **Memo**: *"In view of recent publicity afforded UFO sightings, some apparently by responsible sources, this information is submitted to the Bureau for consideration of transmittal to the U. S. Air Force."* — i.e., field-office defers HQ on referral decision.
- **HQ disposition**: forwards to **Inspector General, USAF** by Form 6-11-67. ^[extracted]
- **No FBI investigation conducted.** ^[extracted] Standard policy ([[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|hoax-default]]) applies.

## Prior Peyerl FBI contact

The Miami indices show two prior Peyerl visits ^[extracted]:

- **24 Jul 1959** — appeared at Miami Office, *"volunteered to return to Austria as an intelligence agent. His expressed motivation was to do something to repay the debt he felt he owed the United States."* Was given Washington HQ CIA address.
- **4 Jul 1961** — appeared at Miami Office, gave *"an incoherent story of recently meeting an individual who asked him to kill his mother."* Appeared *"somewhat bewildered and continuously gazed away from the interviewing Agent."*
- **26 Apr 1967** — present visit (the saucer disclosure). Per the LHM, *"PEYERL seemed genuinely concerned about the existence of the object he allegedly photographed during November, 1944. He exhibited no emotional nor mental disorder and appeared to be rational."* ^[extracted]

The **prior 1959 + 1961 history** is significant for the Bureau's institutional read of the 1967 disclosure ^[inferred] — Peyerl is a known walk-in with prior interviewer-noted irregularities, and the Bureau's handling of the saucer claim incorporates that history without rejecting the claim. ^[inferred]
