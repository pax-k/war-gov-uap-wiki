# Sighting — Quantico Marine Base Mystery Red Lights (Jan 1954)

In **early January 1954** Marines at the **Quantico Marine Base** in Virginia reported **mysterious red lights flying over the base 22 times in six nights**, prompting press coverage and an internal investigation. ^[extracted] The case resolved as **misidentification of new airliner navigation lights** (specifically the new **DC-7 navigation-light pattern** that the Marines were not yet familiar with). ^[extracted]

The Quantico case is also significant as a **military-base witness population case** — i.e. **Marines themselves** misidentified normal civilian aircraft as UAP for **22 separate observations over six nights**. This complicates the "credentialed-witness" framing that NICAP would adopt three years later under [[entities/delmer-s-fahrney|Fahrney]] + [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Keyhoe]]: military observers at a Marine base failed to recognize normal civilian aircraft. ^[inferred]
