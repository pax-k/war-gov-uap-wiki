# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Section 1 (Serials 1-52)

The **founding section** of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894]] — 185 OCR pages of declassified documents, indexed serials 1 through 52, dated **6 July 1947 through 12 September 1947** ^[extracted]. Section 1 is materially distinct from the numbered-serial individual ingest artifacts already in the wiki (serials 164, 220, 403, 438, 449): those are **single-serial** documents released as standalone Vault items, while a "section" is a **chronological/topical compilation** of contiguous serials reproduced as one bulk PDF. ^[inferred]

## Section vs serial — the structural distinction

Sections 1-10 (plus sub-a) of the 62-HQ-83894 release each contain dozens of contiguous serials in OCR-page order. The structural relationship: ^[inferred]

```
file 62-HQ-83894
├── section 1   = serials 1   - 52    (this artifact)
├── section 2   = serials ~52 - ?
├── section 3-10 + sub_a       (queued)
└── individual numbered serials (164, 220, 403, 438, 449) = standalone single-serial Vault releases
```

Within section 1, the serial markers `62-83894-1` through `62-83894-52` appear inline in the OCR text on the lower-right of each filed item. ^[extracted] Several serials carry **enclosure trailers** (newspaper clippings filed under the same serial number — e.g. serial 1 includes Washington Post, Milwaukee Sentinel, Milwaukee Journal, AP wire enclosures all marked *"62-83894-1 Enclosure"*). ^[extracted]

## HEADLINE: Section 1 moves the file's date-floor back ~19 months

Before this ingest, the wiki's earliest record in 62-HQ-83894 was [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|serial 164 (15 Feb 1949)]] — Cabell's AIRMEM #4. Section 1 displaces that floor by **~19 months** to **6 July 1947** — i.e. **the first day of the FBI's saucer engagement**. ^[extracted] Critically, **the file was opened in direct response** to: ^[inferred]

- the 24 June 1947 [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Kenneth Arnold sighting]] over Mt Rainier;
- the resulting national press wave that broke between Jun 25 and Jul 6;
- a 9 July 1947 in-person request from **[[entities/george-f-schulgen|Brigadier General George F. Schulgen]]** (Chief, Requirements Intelligence Branch, AAF Intelligence) to **[[entities/s-w-reynolds|SA S. W. Reynolds]]** of the FBI Liaison Section, asking the Bureau to **investigate first-witness sincerity** on every disc-sighting report — the request that Hoover initially declined but then accepted with the marginal note: *"I would do it but before agreeing to it we must insist upon full access to discs recovered. For instance in the La. case the Army grabbed it and would not let us have it for cursory examination."* ^[extracted]

This corrects an earlier framing bias on [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file]] — the file is **not** a topic-clearinghouse that grew topical retroactively. It was **operational from day 1** of the FBI's UAP engagement, opened as the documentary infrastructure for the **[[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|FBI-AAF saucer cooperation arrangement]]** finalized that same month and issued as **[[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42, Series 1947]]** on **30 July 1947**. ^[inferred]

## What section 1 reveals about file function

The 1949–1966 N=5 ingest had inferred a **5-channel** intake pattern (a) clipped publications, (b) inbound civilian mail, (c) FBI field-office investigative product, (d) citizen-political-tip → counterintelligence indexing, (e) inter-agency intelligence directives. Section 1 introduces **two structurally novel earlier channels** (re-lettered): ^[inferred]

| Channel | Section 1 instance | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(f) NEW — Civilian mass-correspondence to the Director** | ~12 letters from members of the public — Reibold (Omaha), Hoersch (Asbury Park NJ), Norris (Chicago), Pluskat (Brooklyn), Mrs. Anna Benys (Yoakum TX), Mrs. J.M. Clark (Westport CT), Mrs. Anderson, Sroka (Waukesha WI), Cope (San Marcos TX), Watts (Darlington SC), Bailey (Stamford CT), Demmerman (Daly City CA), Mrs. Mae Mass via Woodstock IL anon letter, "Combined and Amalgamated Committee of Sky-Scanners" advertising mail, Mrs. Merchant (El Paso) | Jul-Aug 1947 | Each acknowledged with a Hoover form-letter and forwarded to the War Department; **the file's intake volume in the founding section is dominated by civilian mail, not by Bureau-authored content** ^[inferred] |
| **(g) NEW — AAF-cooperation-doctrine via Liaison Section** | Schulgen → Reynolds 9 Jul 1947 meeting (serial 33); Hoover 24 Jul 1947 follow-up agreeing on full-disc-access precondition; Bureau Bulletin No. 42 of 30 Jul 1947 distributing the protocol to all field offices | Jul-Aug 1947 | The **doctrinal bargain** that set the FBI's UAP role for the next ~14 months until [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol\|AIRMEM #4]] reorganized it ^[inferred] |

The **at-N=6** total channel inventory is now: ^[inferred]

```
(a) clipped public publication                        — serial 403  (1956)
(b) unsolicited inbound foreign civilian mail         — serial 220  (1950)
(c) FBI field-investigation product                   — serial 438  (1964)
(d) citizen-political-tip → CI indexing               — serial 449  (1966)
(e) inter-agency USAF intelligence directive          — serial 164  (1949)
(f) NEW — civilian mass-correspondence to Director    — section 1   (1947)
(g) NEW — AAF-cooperation-doctrine via Liaison        — section 1   (1947)
```
