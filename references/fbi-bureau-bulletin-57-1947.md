# Bureau Bulletin No. 57, Series 1947 (1 October 1947)

The **FBI HQ all-SAC bulletin** that **terminated FBI investigation of flying discs** ~9 weeks after [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42, Series 1947]] (30 Jul 1947) opened it. ^[inferred] BB-57 is the **structural closure** of the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|FBI-AAF saucer cooperation regime]] — the institutional bargain SA Reynolds had negotiated with Brig Gen Schulgen between 9 Jul and 24 Jul 1947. ^[inferred]

These documents close several previously-open threads on this page (see Open Threads section, below): ^[inferred]

- **Bulletin's originator**: SA Reynolds drafted, Asst Director Ladd signed, Hoover personally approved 27 Sep 1947. ^[extracted]
- **Proximate trigger**: the ADC D333.5EX letter's unilateral redefinition + insulting "ash can covers" framing. ^[extracted]
- **Why issued ~9 weeks after BB-42**: Ladd's recommendation 1 (*"protest vigorously"*) + recommendation 2 (*"discontinue all activity"*) directly responds to the ADC letter; the Garrett admission was the parallel-but-deeper trigger held off the documentary record. ^[inferred]
- **External-facing announcement**: confirmed via the same-serial-filed 27 Sep 1947 Hoover-McDonald letter — closure was **announced to the AAF originating channel** at Maj-Gen level, not silently superseded. ^[extracted]

The bulletin's **literal text remains unrecovered**. ^[extracted] The proposed Bulletin attached to the Ladd 25 Sep 1947 memo is referenced in the recommendation block (*"A proposed Bulletin is attached for your approval"*) but is **not separately preserved** in section 3's OCR output. ^[ambiguous] Recovering the verbatim BB-57 text remains an open thread for unrescued sections 6, 9, 10. ^[open]

## Operational substance — reconstructed

From the citations, BB-57 issued the following directives to all FBI field offices: ^[inferred]

1. **Effective date 1 Oct 1947**, the Bureau **discontinued its investigative activities** in connection with flying discs. ^[extracted]
2. **All future reports** concerning flying discs received in the field **should be referred to the Air Forces**. ^[extracted]
3. **No investigative action** is to be taken by Bureau agents in flying-disc matters. ^[extracted]
4. The Bureau will **receive any information which the Air Forces volunteer** but **conduct no investigations**. ^[extracted, paraphrased from 20 Feb 1948 Hoover reaffirmation]

The bulletin **does not** appear to have explicitly:

- Repealed [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]] by name. ^[inferred]
- Cited the [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett admission]] (~22 Sep 1947) as the proximate trigger. ^[inferred]
- Identified the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]] by name. ^[inferred]

The wiki's reconstructed reading is that BB-57 was a **brief operational directive** rather than a comprehensive policy paper — a one- or two-paragraph all-SAC instruction sufficient to be paraphrased identically across multiple later citations. ^[inferred]

## Why BB-57 was issued — the institutional context

BB-57 follows ~9 weeks after [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|BB-42]] established the cooperation regime. The trigger is plausibly: ^[inferred]

1. **The Garrett admission** (~22 Sep 1947, [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2 serial 86]]) — Lt Col Garrett tells SA Reynolds privately that flying discs are likely a *"highly classified US Government experiment"*. Reynolds explicitly says: *"if it is a fact experimentations are being conducted by the United States Government, then it does not appear reasonable to request the FBI to spend money and precious time conducting inquiries with respect to this matter."* ^[extracted]
2. **The Schulgen 5 Sep 1947 formal denial letter** (serial 84) — closes the AAF-side prosaic-explanation framework but **does not** restore the cooperation regime's operational rationale. ^[extracted]
3. **The Fugate negative-handover incident** (29-30 Aug 1947, serials 68 + 80) — operationally inverts the *"full FBI access"* precondition Hoover insisted on at regime founding. ^[extracted]

By ~end-September 1947, the cooperation regime's structural problem (FBI investigating something the AAF privately suspects is its own classified project) had become institutionally explicit. ^[inferred] BB-57's 1 Oct 1947 issuance is **structurally consistent** with closing the regime ~7-9 days after the Garrett admission propagated to the FBI Liaison Section, ^[inferred] giving Reynolds and Ladd time to brief Hoover and draft the bulletin.

## What BB-57 did NOT do — confirmed by section 4 evidence

Section 4 of 62-HQ-83894 establishes that BB-57 was **not absolute** in operation: ^[extracted]

- **The Stratemeyer ADC letter (4 Feb 1948)** — see [[references/adc-stratemeyer-flying-disc-1948-02-04]] — directs USAF Commanding Generals to *"coordinate investigations with the FBI Office concerned"*, **directly contradicting BB-57**. SAC SF Kimball flags the conflict; Hoover (via Keay/Reynolds) reaffirms BB-57 still applies; AF agrees Mitchel Field was *"not aware that the Bureau was no longer conducting investigations"*. ^[extracted]
- **The Noack Houston case (Dec 1948)** — see [[references/sighting-noack-helendale-1948-12]] — Inspector [[entities/h-b-fletcher|H. B. Fletcher]] **actively dispatched** Houston SAC + LA SAC to recover and guard a possible disc, despite BB-57 having been in force for 14 months. ^[extracted] The case resolves before BB-57 is invoked (Olson and Rice tow-kite ID), but the FBI's operational stance was clearly **not** "refuse to investigate" at that moment.
- **The SAC Letter No. 38, Series 1949 (25 Mar 1949)** — see [[references/fbi-sac-letter-38-1949]] — partially reactivates a non-investigative information-gathering channel: *"although no active investigation will be conducted by the Bureau, it is believed that the captioned matter is of sufficient importance to the internal security of the country that our field offices should secure as much information as possible from complainants in order to assist the Department of the Air Force."* ^[extracted]

## Numbering

BB-42 (30 Jul 1947) and BB-57 (1 Oct 1947) span **~9 weeks** with **~15 intervening bulletins**. Without bulletin-by-bulletin recovery, we cannot say whether the 15-bulletin gap is **dense** (FBI HQ issuing ~2 bulletins/week) or **sparse** (other UAP-adjacent bulletins issued in the gap). ^[inferred] The bulletin numbering is sequential across all topics, not topic-specific. ^[inferred]

## Confidence

This page is **constructed from references**, not from primary-source transcription. ^[extracted] The bulletin itself has not been located in any ingested source. The reconstructed content is **operationally well-attested** (5 independent citations spanning 28 months), but the **literal text remains unrecovered**. Future ingest of unrescued sections of 62-HQ-83894 (sections 3, 6-10, sub_a) may recover the bulletin verbatim — a high-priority open thread.
