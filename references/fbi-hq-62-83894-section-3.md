# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Section 3 (Serials 101-130)

A **190-OCR-page** declassified tranche of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]] (size 308 KB; SHA-256 `46cdab03219483eb15bf2ae93046bfa8154d871fb151278e88c5565f0898ef55`), the **fourteenth ingested artifact** in the file's 17-section ingest series. ^[inferred] Section 3 sits **between [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2 (serials 53-100)]] and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-4|section 4 (serials ~113-185)]]** with **mild serial-overlap** in the 113-130 range, but section 3's distinct contribution is its **paradigm-defining late-Aug to late-Sep 1947 institutional-decision content**: the BB-57 birth documents, the FBI Seattle Maury Island complete report, and the earliest by-name "Operation Mogul" reference. ^[inferred] The cover page (OCR p. 0) confirms section 3 = **"SERIALS 101-130 / SECTION 3"** under filing class `0062 / 83894 / 3 / 101-130`. ^[extracted]

The section's date span: ^[extracted]

- **Earliest substantive document**: 7 July 1947 (4AF-1208-I Muroc affidavits cluster, contemporaneous with Inc 1; preserved here as 4AF channel, OCR pp. 60-72)
- **Latest substantive document**: 9 October 1947 (Reynolds → Ladd photograph-transmittal memo serial 130, OCR p. 187 — the file's final serial in this section)
- **Primary span**: ~14 August 1947 to 27 September 1947 — i.e., the **9-week window** in which the FBI-AAF cooperation regime ran at full throttle and then closed.

Section 3 is **paradigm-defining for the FBI's 1947 termination decision**. ^[inferred] It contains the **three documents that triggered, justified, and announced** the FBI's 1 October 1947 [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 57]] termination of saucer investigation, all filed at **62-83894-121** (the BB-57 anchor serial) and **62-83894-122** (the ADC-letter enclosure serial): ^[extracted]

These three documents **promote substantial parts of the BB-57 narrative** from `^[inferred]` to `^[extracted]` in the wiki corpus: ^[inferred]

- **Birth document identified**: the Ladd 25 Sep 1947 memo with Hoover's handwritten "I agree" and "A proposed Bulletin is attached for your approval" is the literal proposal-vehicle for BB-57.
- **Originator identified**: SA S. W. Reynolds drafted, D. M. Ladd signed, Hoover personally approved 27 Sep 1947.
- **Proximate documentary cause identified**: the ADC Stratemeyer-by-command D333.5EX letter of 3 Sep 1947 — its "ash can covers, toilet seats and whatnot" framing is cited verbatim by Hoover in the McDonald letter.
- **External-facing announcement preserved**: the McDonald letter shows the FBI exit was **announced to the AAF originating channel** (AC/AS-2 at Maj-Gen level), not silently superseded.

The literal text of BB-57 itself is **still not transcribed** in section 3 — the proposed Bulletin is referenced as an attachment to the Ladd memo but is not separately preserved in the OCR output. ^[ambiguous] Recovering the verbatim BB-57 text remains an open thread for unrescued sections 6, 9, 10. ^[open]

## HEADLINE — Earliest by-name "Operation Mogul" reference in the corpus

Section 3 OCR page 161 preserves a **23 September 1947 Fitch → Ladd memo** (filed **62-83894-122**) containing the **earliest by-name "Operation Mogul" reference in the wiki UAP corpus**. ^[inferred] See [[references/fbi-fitch-ladd-mogul-1947-09-23]] and [[concepts/operation-mogul]] (NEW).

The structural inversion is paradigm-noteworthy: ^[inferred]

- **In 1947 (this memo)**: an FBI Lab inquiry attempting to attribute a civilian-recovered Illinois farm instrument to **"Operation Mogul"** is **explicitly denied** by Wright Field T-2 + Major Hopkins (radio/technical-experience expert) + Lt Col [[entities/g-d-garrett|George Garrett]]; the case is closed as a hoax involving an old-type radio loudspeaker. ^[extracted]
- **In 1994 (USAF Roswell Report)**: Mogul is *affirmed* as the primary explanation for the [[references/sighting-roswell-1947-07-08|Roswell debris recovery]]. ^[inferred]

The 47-year inversion is institutionally noteworthy. The four-stage attribution-and-denial chain (Mrs. Whedon → FBI Lab → Wright Field → Hopkins → Garrett → final hoax-classification) provides the **structural template** the AAF appears to have used for any civilian instrument-recovery claim that named a classified program. ^[inferred] Whether the **Whedon attribution was a deliberate AAF deception** (a security-leak test of FBI's response cadence) or a genuine error remains open. ^[open]

## HEADLINE — The FBI Seattle Maury Island complete report

Section 3 OCR pages 34-58 preserve the **FBI Seattle field-office complete narrative report** (filed at **62-83894-110**, signed R H Fletcher, 18 August 1947) — the document the existing [[references/sighting-maury-island-1947-06-21|Maury Island sighting page]] flagged as a long-standing **open thread** ("the FBI Seattle complete report — referenced in serial 32 as 'en route to Bureau' — does not appear in section 1"). ^[extracted] Section 3 closes this thread.

The report's institutional findings: ^[extracted]

1. **Crisman + Dahl signed admission, 7 August 1947** — the original "rock formations" came from a Maury Island gravel pit; the disc-explosion narrative was constructed at Ray Palmer (Venture Press)'s telephonic request for magazine publication.
2. **Kenneth Arnold was paid by Palmer** to come to Tacoma to "investigate" the case — i.e., he was a paid investigator-narrator, not a disinterested fact-finder.
3. **The B-25 crash killing Davidson + Brown was forensically attributed to engine failure** (left exhaust stack burned out, ignited the wing) — not sabotage. McChord Field Intelligence Officers verified no sabotage indication.
4. **The five anonymous phone calls to Tacoma media on 31 Jul - 1 Aug 1947 asserting B-25 sabotage** could only have come from Crisman, Dahl, Arnold, or Smith — the four civilians with knowledge of the Winthrop Hotel meeting.
5. **Final disposition**: Dahl and Crisman will not be reinterviewed.

See [[references/fbi-seattle-maury-island-report-1947-08-18]] for the full discussion. ^[inferred] Section 3 also preserves the **parallel 4AF-channel investigation report** (file 4AF-1208-I, 12 August 1947, by Lt Brown and Capt Davidson) at OCR pages 13-17 — providing the AAF-side narrative side-by-side with the FBI's. ^[extracted] The two parallel investigations converge on the hoax-fabrication finding via independent witness chains. ^[inferred]

## HEADLINE — Pre-cooperation-regime Schulgen subversive-background-investigation request

Section 3 OCR page 134 preserves a **20 August 1947 Fitch → Ladd memo** (filed **62-83894-116**) — the **earliest documented Schulgen institutional channel** to the FBI on specific witness-subjects. ^[inferred] [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]] requests **FBI background investigations on Byron B. Savage (RCA Field Engineer, Oklahoma City)** and **Kenneth Arnold (Boise, Idaho)** to *"ascertain whether or not either of these individuals have any subversive background and to ascertain whether or not they had any ulterior motives for reporting these sightings."* ^[extracted]

This 20 Aug 1947 channel is **structurally distinct** from the famous 5 Sep 1947 Schulgen-to-Hoover formal denial letter (section 2 serial 84) and operates at a deeper institutional level: ^[inferred]

- **20 Aug 1947 (this memo)**: Schulgen requests subversive-background investigations on **named individual witnesses** under the [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|BB-42]] framework.
- **5 Sep 1947 (section 2 serial 84)**: Schulgen formally tells Hoover the AAF has no project matching the discs (closes the prosaic-explanation hypothesis).

The 20 Aug 1947 memo is the **deepest institutional commitment to the subversive-motive frame** the wiki preserves. ^[inferred] By the 25 Sep 1947 Ladd proposed-bulletin memo, the FBI had concluded that 9 weeks of BB-42 work *"have failed to reveal any indication of subversive individuals being involved in any of the reported sightings"* — i.e., **the subversive-motive hypothesis was empirically refuted by the cooperation regime's own work**. ^[extracted] See [[references/fbi-schulgen-savage-arnold-background-1947-08-20]].

## HEADLINE — The FBI's own-channel Arnold deposition

Section 3 OCR pages 88-110 preserve the **FBI Boise Resident Agency 19 August 1947 deposition of Kenneth Arnold** by **SA Joseph E. Jette**, transmitted via SAC Butte (Banister) → Director memo of 27 August 1947 (Butte file 65-477). ^[extracted] This is the **FBI's own-channel Arnold deposition** — distinct from the 4th Air Force Brown / Davidson channel (12 Jul 1947 interview, preserved in section 2 serial 95 and serial 130). ^[inferred]

The deposition's distinctive content: ^[extracted]

- **The 26 June 1947 Palmer-to-Arnold letter** — the earliest Palmer-Arnold documentary contact in the wiki corpus, transmitted to Bureau as documentary evidence.
- **Arnold's first-person autobiographical statement** — including pilot's-license-number 333467, Callair aircraft, Red Comet / Great Western Fire Control Supply career history.
- **Arnold's Mt. Rainier first-person narrative** — 9-object chain at 9,500 ft, 170° N→S direction, the bright-reflection startle event.
- **Arnold's frustration with institutional non-engagement** — *"I received no interest from these two important protective forces of our country [FBI and Military Intelligence] until two weeks after my observation. I will go so far as to assume that if our Military Intelligence was not aware of what I observed … they would be the very first people I could expect as visitors."*

The deposition's institutional purpose was to **verify the Maury Island Crisman + Dahl claim that Arnold was paid by Palmer** to come to Tacoma — the Palmer-Arnold financial relationship is documentary-confirmed by the 26 Jun 1947 Palmer letter. ^[extracted] See [[references/fbi-arnold-boise-interview-1947-08-19]].

The Discs Are Flying" article + Vic Goertzen artist's conception), and a distinctive aerodynamic-effect signature (**Mormon Poplars "spun around on top as if they were in a vacuum"**). ^[extracted] See [[references/sighting-snake-river-canyon-1947-08-13]] (NEW).
