# ADC D333.5EX Letter — Cooperation of FBI with AAF on Investigations of Flying Disc Incidents (3 September 1947)

A **multi-paragraph letter from HEADQUARTERS, AIR DEFENSE COMMAND, Mitchel Field, New York**, file reference **D333.5EX**, dated **3 September 1947**, subject **"Cooperation of FBI with AAF on Investigations of 'Flying Disc' Incidents"**, signed **"By Command of Lieutenant General Stratemeyer"** by **Col R. H. Smith, Assistant Chief of Staff Intelligence, Headquarters, Air Defense Command**, addressed **TO: Commanding Generals, First, Second, Fourth, Tenth, Eleventh and Fourteenth Air Forces, ATTENTION: Assistant Chief of Staff, A-2**. ^[extracted] Filed at **62-83894-122** (OCR page 165 ENCLOSURE marker is for serial 122) and reproduced as enclosures at OCR pages 160 and 183 of [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]]. ^[extracted]

This letter is **the proximate documentary trigger of the FBI's 1 Oct 1947 termination of saucer investigation** — the [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|BB-57]] decision. ^[inferred] Hoover's [[references/fbi-hoover-mcdonald-letter-1947-09-27|27 Sep 1947 letter to Maj Gen McDonald]] cites this letter's "ash can covers, toilet seats and whatnot" framing verbatim as the institutional offense that prompted Bureau exit. ^[extracted]

## Verbatim — the letter text

The letter's substantive paragraphs (reconstructed from OCR pages 160 + 183):

> **HEADQUARTERS**
> **AIR DEFENSE COMMAND**
> **Mitchel Field, New York**
>
> In reply
> refer to:
> D333.5EX
>
> 3 September 1947
>
> SUBJECT: Cooperation of FBI with AAF on Investigations of "Flying Disc" Incidents.
>
> TO: Commanding Generals, First, Second, Fourth, Tenth, Eleventh and Fourteenth Air Forces
> ATTENTION: Assistant Chief of Staff, A-2
>
> 1. The Federal Bureau of Investigation has agreed to assist Air Force Intelligence personnel in the investigation of "flying disc" incidents in order to quickly and effectively rule out what are pranks and to concentrate on what appears to be a genuine incident.
>
> 2. It was the original intent of the AC/AS-2, Headquarters, Army Air Forces that whereas the ADC Air Forces would interview responsible observers whose names would be furnished by AAF, **the FBI would investigate incidents of so called "discs" being found on the ground.** The services of the FBI were enlisted in order to relieve the numbered Air Forces of the task of tracking down all the many instances which **turned out to be ash can covers, toilet seats and whatnot.**
>
> 3. It is requested that each A-2 informally coordinate and cooperate with the FBI, generally keeping the FBI informed of any proposed calls th[e Air Forces would make on FBI investigative resources] ... ^[OCR-truncated]

^[extracted]

The letter's effective directive: **division of labor** under which AAF interviews "responsible observers" and FBI investigates **ground-recovered disc incidents**, with AAF A-2 informing FBI of any calls on FBI resources. ^[inferred]

## Why the letter triggered FBI exit

Three structural problems with the letter, viewed from FBI HQ: ^[inferred]

### 1. Unilateral redefinition of the cooperation regime

The original [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]] negotiated 9-24 Jul 1947 between [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]] and [[entities/s-w-reynolds|Reynolds]] tasked the FBI with investigating **first-witness sincerity** — i.e. determining whether disc reports were bona-fide observations, imaginary, or pranks. ^[extracted] The FBI's investigative scope was the **witness's mental state** ("subversive sympathies", "publicity-seeking", "honest mistake"). ^[inferred]

The ADC letter **redefines the FBI's role** as **investigating physical disc artifacts found on the ground** — a tasking the FBI never agreed to. ^[inferred] [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42, Series 1947]], the FBI's own operational instrument codifying the cooperation regime, instructs FBI field offices to investigate **sightings** for sincerity-and-prank-or-bona-fide-determination — not to investigate physical artifacts. ^[extracted]

### 2. The "ash can covers, toilet seats and whatnot" framing

The letter's paragraph 2 reduces the investigative output to date as **"ash can covers, toilet seats and whatnot"** — a deeply institutionally insulting framing of **the FBI's 9-week effort under BB-42** as having produced trivial junk. ^[inferred] This framing is what Hoover protests at length in his [[references/fbi-hoover-mcdonald-letter-1947-09-27|27 Sep 1947 letter to McDonald]] (*"I cannot permit the personnel and time of this organization to be dissipated in this manner"*). ^[extracted]

### 3. Unilateral issuance without FBI coordination

The letter is **distributed to six numbered Air Forces** as a directive — but is not coordinated with the FBI before issuance. ^[inferred] The FBI surfaces the letter only because **SAC SF Kimball secured a "restricted" copy from Lt Col Donald L. Springer (4AF A-2 Hamilton Field)** on or around 12 Sep 1947, who passed it confidentially to the SAC. ^[extracted] SAC SF transmits to FBI HQ via the [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR page 180 SAC SF letter of 19 Sep 1947 to Director-Attn-Ladd. ^[extracted]

The combination — **FBI is named in an AAF-issued operational directive that unilaterally redefines the cooperation regime; FBI learns of the directive only through a confidential leak from a 4AF officer; the directive treats the FBI's prior work as junk-recovery** — is institutionally untenable for FBI HQ. ^[inferred] Within ~13 days of the FBI's receipt of the directive, [[references/fbi-ladd-proposed-bulletin-1947-09-25|Ladd drafts the BB-57 proposal memo]] and Hoover signs the [[references/fbi-hoover-mcdonald-letter-1947-09-27|McDonald termination announcement]]. ^[inferred]

## What the letter signals about ADC's posture

The letter's existence reveals that **HQ Air Defense Command at Mitchel Field** had been operating under a **substantively different understanding** of the cooperation regime than HQ AAF AC/AS-2 (the Schulgen channel that originally negotiated it): ^[inferred]

- **AC/AS-2 understood the regime** as Schulgen-Reynolds: FBI investigates first-witness sincerity nationwide, AAF retains scientific-analytic authority, AAF commits to disc-recovery access for the FBI per Hoover's precondition. ^[inferred]
- **ADC understood the regime** (per this 3 Sep 1947 letter) as: AAF interviews "responsible observers", FBI investigates ground-recovered junk discs (the "ash can covers" tasking), with AAF A-2 informing FBI of resource calls. ^[inferred]

The letter is signed *"By Command of Lieutenant General Stratemeyer"* — i.e., **Maj Gen George E. Stratemeyer** as Commanding General of ADC Mitchel Field. ^[inferred] Stratemeyer's organization (ADC) had been the AAF's air-defense command since 1946; the cooperation regime's negotiation had occurred at **AAF HQ AC/AS-2 Pentagon level** (Schulgen → Reynolds), **not** at ADC level. ^[inferred] The letter therefore reflects **ADC reading-in to a regime negotiated above its head** — interpretation drift. ^[inferred]

The same Stratemeyer reappears 4 months later as **the named Commanding General of [[references/adc-stratemeyer-flying-disc-1948-02-04|the 4 Feb 1948 ADC directive]]** that **retries the FBI-coordination posture after BB-57 has terminated it** — confirming ADC institutional preference for treating the FBI as a sub-tasked investigative resource is **structurally durable** and not eliminated by BB-57. ^[inferred]

## OCR retention pattern in section 3

The letter is **preserved in section 3 in TWO distinct copies** — at OCR pages 160 and 183. ^[extracted] The dual retention reflects: ^[inferred]

- **OCR page 160** — copy attached to the [[references/fbi-ladd-proposed-bulletin-1947-09-25|Ladd 25 Sep 1947 memo to Hoover]] (filed 62-83894-121); the Ladd memo's *"a copy of which is attached hereto"* directly references this attachment. ^[extracted]
- **OCR page 183** — copy attached to the [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR page 180 SAC SF Kimball-to-Director letter of 19 Sep 1947 (the original FBI-side intake of the letter from Lt Col Springer). ^[inferred]

The two copies are **textually identical** but represent **two independent FBI institutional retentions** — the field-office intake (SAC SF) and the HQ-level decision-vehicle (Ladd's proposal). ^[inferred]

## Open threads

- **Recover the letter's paragraph 3-end** — section-3 OCR is truncated mid-paragraph 3. Whether the letter contains additional paragraphs about scientific-analysis-channel division or disc-recovery-handover is open. ^[open]
- **Verify Stratemeyer's organizational role on 3 Sep 1947** — at ADC Mitchel Field commanding general level, signing under "Lieutenant General" but a Maj-Gen at this date in some references. ^[ambiguous]
- **Determine whether the letter was issued in response to a specific AC/AS-2 instruction** or as ADC's own initiative. ^[open] The letter's paragraph 1 ("The FBI has agreed to assist") suggests it was downstream of an AC/AS-2 announcement; whether ADC interpreted that announcement correctly remains open.
- **Cross-reference whether the 4 Feb 1948 [[references/adc-stratemeyer-flying-disc-1948-02-04|ADC Stratemeyer directive]] also carries D333.5EX as its reference number** — would establish ADC's persistent file-identity for the cooperation-coordination posture across the BB-57 termination boundary. ^[open]
