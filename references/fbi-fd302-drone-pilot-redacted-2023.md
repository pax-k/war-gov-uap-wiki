# FBI FD-302: Drone-Pilot UAP Witness (Sept 2023, redacted)

A 2-page redacted Mistral-OCR'd [[entities/fbi|FBI]] FD-302 form (Rev. 5-8-10) recording **the contractor drone-pilot witness interview** — one of **at least two FD-302s** drafted 2 October 2023 about the same September 2023 UAP incident. The interview was conducted via FaceTime by an FBI Special Agent (with one observer) in late September 2023; the form was drafted 2 October 2023. The **sister FD-302** for the lead-vehicle driver of the same convoy is at [[references/fbi-fd302-serial-3-driver-redacted-2023]] (serial-3 — recovered after this artifact was first ingested as serial-4). The consolidated multi-witness sighting narrative is at [[references/sighting-fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-2023-09]].

- **Continuation observations**: object stayed constant size and intensity; first noticed coming over a small bridge before the gate; **no vehicle interference**; the lead-vehicle driver was annoyed at the sighting given the deconflicted airspace; the second-vehicle occupant tried unsuccessfully to see the object.
- **Witness aftermath**: that night a storm caused a TV outage; the witness, "still freaked out," went downstairs to verify the outage was building-wide; weird dreams and 2-night sleep difficulty.

## Why this artifact matters

- **First FBI-originated UAP-adjacent record in the wiki corpus.** Until now, federal LE UAP testimony in the corpus was [[entities/aaro|AARO]]-handled (the [[references/western-us-event-2026|2026 Western US event]]). The FBI as the *intake* agency, recording on FD-302 rather than an AARO intake form, is a **distinct intake pipeline**. ^[inferred]
- **Daylight sighting** — relatively rare in the corpus (most modern UAP reports here are dusk / pre-dawn / multi-hour-night). The "good visibility, sun in east, clear sky" ground truth makes the sighting harder to attribute to night-vision optical artifacts.
- **Drone pilot as witness** — a witness whose professional negative space includes "what drones look like at altitude" provides moderately stronger size/distinguishability evidence than a generic civilian witness. See [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern]] for the broader trained-observer-witness argument; this case extends it to **contractor drone pilots in deconflicted airspace**. ^[inferred]
- **Filename pattern** — the source filename `serial-4-redacted_redacted.json` plus the existence of `sources/serial-3_redacted.json` in the sources/ directory suggests this is part of a **numbered serial release** of FBI redacted UAP-adjacent records. ^[inferred]

## FD-302 form notes (for cross-reference)

The FD-302 is the standard FBI narrative interview form. Key distinguishing features visible in this artifact:

- **Two-page structure** (FD-302 + FD-302a continuation) with persistent revision date `Rev. 5-8-10`.
- A **standard non-distribution-and-no-conclusions disclaimer** appears verbatim in the footer.
- The form makes the *interview event* a discrete piece of evidence — date drafted, file number, location, medium are all explicit fields.
- This document type was almost certainly **never primarily intended for UAP collection** ^[inferred] — its presence here means an FBI investigative file is treating UAP-adjacent witness reports under the same evidentiary standard as any other interview.
