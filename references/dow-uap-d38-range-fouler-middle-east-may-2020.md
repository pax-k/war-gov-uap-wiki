# DoW-UAP-D38 — Range Fouler Debrief Form (ISR mission, 14 May 2020, Persian Gulf coast)

A **1-page Mistral-OCR'd artifact** (`sources/dow-uap-d38-range-fouler-debrief-middle-east-may-2020.json`, 3,771 bytes) — the **twelfth artifact** in the [[entities/dow-uap-foia-release|DoW-UAP FOIA release series]] (12-of-40) and the **third range-fouler-debrief ingest**, extending the document class anchored at N=1 by [[references/dow-uap-d58-range-fouler-debrief-2020-10|d58]] (27 Oct 2020) and validated at N=2 by [[references/dow-uap-d44-range-fouler-arabian-sea-october-2020|d44]] (15 Oct 2020 Gulf of Aden). d38 is **the earliest range-fouler in the corpus by ~5 months** — predates d44 by 154 days and d58 by 166 days. d38 confirms the class on **structural axes** (form schema, SPEAR intake, Navy-aviation form-template attribution) but **inverts the release-framework-recurrence finding** previously anchored at N=3: d38's release block carries a **single MDR case** (`USCENTCOM MDR 26-0019`, not the `26-0038 to 26-0046` range) and a **different release-stamp date and sequence** (`01/26/26 001`, not `03/27/26 000001`). **Major narrative signals**:

1. **ISR tasking explicit** — first body-text ISR-tasking attestation in the dow-uap range-fouler subset; **confirms the ISR mission-descriptor in the structured field**. ^[extracted]
2. **Sensor-mode string `ULTN/Black Hot/Lin`** — a three-token sensor-channel configuration ^[inferred]:
   - **`ULTN`** — unclear; possible expansions: ULtra-Thermal Narrow, Ultra-LonG-wave-Thermal-Narrow, or a unit/system-specific gain mode. Not a standard FLIR-vocabulary token. ^[ambiguous]
   - **`Black Hot`** — IR sensor polarity setting where cold objects render bright/white; confirms thermal-imaging acquisition. ^[inferred]
   - **`Lin`** — most probably **Linear gain mode** (vs Logarithmic / Histogram-Equalization / Auto-Gain). ^[inferred]
3. **`solid white object`** — visual signature on the IR channel; with `Black Hot` polarity this is the IR-cold reading (object colder than ambient scene). ^[inferred] Matches d44's `bright-white-on-black-hot` IR-cold signature at N=2.

The dow-uap range-fouler subset is therefore distributed across **at least 2 distinct USCENTCOM MDR allocations** (`26-0019` for the May-2020 artifact + `26-0038 to 26-0046` for the Oct-2020 artifacts). ^[inferred] At least two USCENTCOM declassification reviewers processed the range-fouler subset in different batches; alternatively, the range-fouler subset spans multiple MDR cases because different events were submitted to MDR at different times.

3. **The `000001` vs `001` sequence number divergence is decisive on the per-batch vs per-MDR-case readings.** The d44 page raised three readings for the identical `000001` sequence across d55/d58/d44: (a) per-MDR-case (each document = `000001`), (b) per-batch (all 9 cases share `000001`), (c) OCR artifact. d38 carries `001` (3-digit format) in a different MDR case — which is **consistent with both (a) and (b) but inconsistent with (c)** (three artifacts producing identical `000001` corruption while a fourth produces clean `001` rules out a uniform corruption pattern). The **per-batch reading strengthens** at N=4: d55/d58/d44 are the `000001` of one batch; d38 is the `001` of a different batch. ^[inferred] The format-difference (`000001` 6-digit zero-padded vs `001` 3-digit zero-padded) is consistent with two different reviewer/clerical conventions per batch.
