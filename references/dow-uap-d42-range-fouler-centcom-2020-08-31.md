## OCR ambiguities

- **`Decdified by MG Richard A. Harrison`** — almost certainly `Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison` with OCR truncation. ^[inferred]
- **`USG 1000...0` long zero string** — most parsimonious reading is `USCENTCOM Chief of Staff` collapsed with a numeric `Declassified on:` date line where the date digits OCR'd as repeated zeros. ^[ambiguous] Alternative readings: (a) USG = `U.S.G.` abbreviation for `United States Government`; (b) zero string is a literal page-template artifact. The visual-collision-with-date-line reading is preferred.
- **`(b)(1)1.1a` portion-redaction in position-grid table** — possible OCR digit-swap `1.4a → 1.1a` (recurring `1.4a` style at d44/d56/d58/d38 makes a one-digit corruption plausible); alternatively a genuine `§1.1(a)` citation (EO 13526 §1.1(a) is the broader classification-authority section). ^[ambiguous] At N=1 a per-document interpretation cannot disambiguate.
- **MGRS `39RWL26(1)1.1a`** — last 4 characters appear to be portion-redaction shorthand collapsed into the MGRS string. The actual MGRS is `39RWL26` (a 4-digit easting+northing within the WL square) with the redaction stamping the precise location. ^[inferred] Alternative: full MGRS is `39RWL26<digit>1.1a<more>` with the precise easting+northing partially redacted at sub-10-km precision. ^[ambiguous]
- **Hyperlink `https://39RWL2(1~.ic1i1.M}c1i1.Ja`** — OCR garble of the same MGRS string with redaction-markers; not a real URL. ^[inferred]
- **Form Date field `08/31/20`** — clean OCR; first range-fouler with `Date (mm/dd/yy)` label clearly followed by the value rather than preceded by it. ^[extracted]
- **EA Indications line-breaks** — `Letter Identifier` and `False Trackfiles` rendered as separate lines (matches d38 OCR pattern). The underlying form's 5-row block is the same. ^[inferred]
- **`3.5c. (b)(6)` redaction prefix with period (vs d38 `3.5c, (b)(6)` with comma)** — OCR glyph variance on the prefix delimiter. The underlying redaction-authority citation `EO 13526 §3.5(c)` ^[inferred] is the same.
- **`Direction/Speed (Ex: 090/15) 150/230`** — the form's pre-printed example `(Ex: 090/15)` provides no unit indication; d42's `150/230` is similarly unit-agnostic. ^[ambiguous] Reading: KT (form-convention at d58 form-version) vs mph (d44 witness-supplied) vs unit-blank (d56). The 230 value if KT = 425 km/h is fast but operationally plausible for sensor-tracked objects; if mph = 370 km/h is slower but still moderate-speed.
