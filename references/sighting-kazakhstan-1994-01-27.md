# Sighting — Tajik Air 747SP over Kazakhstan (27 Jan 1994)

A **~40-minute multi-witness commercial-cockpit UAP encounter** at flight level 410 over Kazakhstan, with a follow-on **~100,000-ft contrail observation** at sunrise approximately 45 minutes after the initial sighting. The single-source artifact is [[references/dos-cable-kazakhstan-1994-01|DUSHANBE 00259]] of 31 Jan 1994.

## When and where

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | 27 January 1994 |
| Reported to embassy | 29 January 1994 |
| Cabled | 31 January 1994 (`R 310258Z JAN 94`) |
| Flight level | 41,000 feet (FL410) |
| Position (initial sighting) | ~45° N latitude, 55° E longitude — Kazakhstan, ~north of the Caspian / Aral basin |
| Lighting | Dark; sun rose during the ~45 min between the initial sighting and the contrail observation |
| Aircraft | [[entities/tajik-air|Tajik Air]] Boeing 747SP |
| Witnesses | Capt **Ed Rhodes** (chief pilot; AMCIT) + 2 American pilot colleagues. All three are described in cable as having flown passenger aircraft for **PanAm** prior to Tajik Air. |

## What was seen

### Initial encounter (~45° N, 55° E, dark)

- The object **first appeared as a "bright light of enormous intensity"** approaching from over the eastern horizon "at a great rate of speed and at a much higher altitude" than the 747SP.
- Watched for **"some forty minutes"**. During this period the object manoeuvered:
  - **circles**;
  - **corkscrews**;
  - **90-degree turns** at "rapid rates of speed and under very high G's."
- The crew were **unable to discern its shape** because of darkness.
- Light emitted was described as having a **"bow wave"** and as **"resembling a high-speed photo of a bullet in flight, in which a very small object gives off a much larger trailing wave of heat/light."** ^[ambiguous] The "bow wave" descriptor is notable: it implies a small physical body and a *much larger optical/thermal trailing wave*, distinct from the steady luminous-orb morphology in the modern corpus. ^[inferred]
- Capt Rhodes took **several photos with a pocket Olympus camera**. He offered to forward copies to the embassy and to Lowry Taylor (Tajikistan Desk, State Department) "if they come out." Whether the photos developed, and where they ended up, is **not in the cable**. ^[open]
- Closing phase: the object "adopted a horizontal high-speed course and disappeared over the horizon."

### Contrail follow-on (~45 min later, sunrise)

- The 747SP continued its course at **>500 knots**.
- About 45 minutes after the initial sighting, as the sun was rising, the aircraft flew **under contrails the object had left behind**.
- Rhodes estimated the contrails at **~100,000 ft altitude** — and explicitly noted that the air/moisture at that altitude is **insufficient for ordinary aircraft to leave contrails**. ^[ambiguous] (This is a flight-deck assessment, not an instrumented reading; ~100,000 ft is well above the contrail-formation envelope of any operational aircraft of the era. ^[inferred])
- The contrail **paths reflected the object's manoeuvers** — i.e., the contrails were themselves "circles, corkscrews, etc."

## Witness assessment

- The embassy proposed the conventional explanation: **a meteor entering and skipping off the upper atmosphere**.
- The crew rejected this. They said in their PanAm careers they had "seen thousands of 'falling stars' and other space junk entering the atmosphere," and that "this, they insisted, was nothing like a meteor."
- On the basis of the object's **speed and manoeuverability**, Rhodes — supported by his crew — stated the opinion that the object was **"extraterrestrial and under intelligent control"**.

This is the **first explicit witness-side ETI attribution** in the corpus's primary-source documents. ^[inferred] It is preserved verbatim in an UNCLAS State Department cable, and is followed by the embassy's flat ¶4 closer **"WE HAVE NO OPINION AND REPORT THE ABOVE FOR WHAT IT MAY BE WORTH."** That two-bookend rhetorical structure — witness ETI claim + embassy posture-of-no-posture — is itself a useful corpus signal: see [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame]].

## Cross-corpus signatures

- **Pacing aircraft → manoeuvring → break-off** is a recurring corpus engagement template — see [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement]]. The 1944–45 [[entities/415th-night-fighter-squadron|415th NFS]] foo-fighter pacing reports, the 30 Jul 1949 [[references/sighting-portland-or-1949-07|Portland OR sighting]] (NWA Capt Thrush attempted intercept), and the 2025 [[references/sighting-helicopter-orb-encounter-2025|helicopter orb encounter]] all share this template across 81 years. The 1994 case differs in that the UAP did **not** approach the 747SP — it manoeuvered at a significantly higher altitude before egressing. ^[inferred]
- **High-G manoeuver descriptor** ("90-degree turns at rapid rates of speed and under very high G's") echoes the 1949 [[references/sighting-mountain-home-id-1949-07|Mountain Home ID]] unbanked-180° turn report. The 1994 cable's contrail evidence is the equivalent of a *physical-trace* corroboration that the 1949 case lacked. ^[inferred]
- **Bow wave / bullet-in-flight optical signature** does not have a clean prior match in the corpus. It may belong to a distinct optical-morphology class — possibly related to plasma/heat shock signatures rather than the steady luminous-orb class. Worth tracking. ^[inferred]
- **Multi-witness cockpit corroboration** with three American commercial pilots is a strong-witness-class data point — comparable to the [[references/sighting-fairfield-suisun-1948-12|3 Dec 1948 Fairfield-Suisun]] AACS-tower multi-witness case but with the witness aggregation happening on the flight deck rather than at the ground station.
- **Multi-vantage and physical-trace combination is rare**: the 24 Jan 1985 [[references/sighting-papua-new-guinea-1985-01-24|PNG case]] had multi-vantage contrail observations from the ground; the 1994 case has **single-vantage initial sighting + single-vantage physical-trace (contrail) follow-on from the same crew**. The contrail observation is the corroboration the visual-only case lacks.
