# Sighting — Muroc AAF Cluster (8 Jul 1947)

A **multi-event same-day witness cluster** at **Muroc Army Air Field, California** (the AAF's experimental flight-test base, later renamed Edwards AFB) on **8 July 1947**. Filed across **Project SIGN Incidents 1, 1c, 1e, 2, 4** in [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-1-100|the 1–100 bundle]], with adjacent **Inc 3** preserving the day-prior 7 Jul 1947 same-base event by Maj J. C. Wise during XP-84 ground run. ^[extracted]

Inside the SIGN sequential-incident stream this is **the founding cluster** — Inc 1 is **literally the first numbered Incident Summary Sheet in the corpus**. ^[extracted] AAF flight-test institutional witnesses observing unidentified objects from the same base over a 3-hour window establishes the early-1947 wave's anchoring institutional pedigree. ^[inferred]

## The cluster's events

| Inc # | Time | Witness | Object | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 0930 | **1st Lt Joseph C. McHenry** (Billeting Officer in Charge) | 2 then 1 silver discs/spheres | First sighting; called other 6 personnel after sighting; *"performed too tight a circle to be any type of known aircraft"* |
| **1c** | 0945 | **S/Sgt Joseph Ruvolo** (NCO Charge Billeting) | 2 saucer/disc-shaped silver objects | "**I am of good health and sound mind and this was no hallucination**" |
| **1e** | 1000 | **Jannette Marie Scott** (Secretary to McHenry) | 3 silver discs (2 first, 1 later) | First **female civilian** witness in the corpus; ruled out balloons by horizontal trajectory |
| **2** | ~1200 | **Maj Richard R. Shoop** (Office of Chief of Tech Engineering Div) + Col Gilkey | 1 thin metallic aluminum-colored object | 5–8 mi N at fairly high altitude; **oscillating descent then climb** to surface |
| **4** | 1150 | **Capt John Paul Stapp** (Flight Test) + Mr Lenz (Wright Field civilian) + 2 others, on observation truck Area #3 Rogers Dry Lake | 1 ovular silver-aluminum 50-ft object w/ 2 fins/nobs crossing | **During preparation of seat-ejection experiment** with formation of 2 P-82s and an A-26 at 20,000 ft; first impression: "**premature ejection of seat and dummy had occurred**" |

The day-prior **Inc 3** preserves a related case: **Maj J. C. Wise** (XP-84 test pilot) observed a 5–10 ft yellowish-white sphere at 10,000–12,000 ft moving 200–225 MPH W→E *"oscillating in a forward whirling movement without losing altitude"* on 7 Jul 1947 at 1010 — **while running up the XP-84 on the ground**. ^[extracted]

## Multi-witness cross-corroboration

The Muroc 0930 sighting (Inc 1) was witnessed by **at minimum 5 of 7 personnel called to the scene** by McHenry; the cluster's three direct witness statements (Inc 1, 1c, 1e from McHenry, Ruvolo, Scott) are **independent first-person accounts of the same approximate phenomenon** with mutually consistent attributes:

- **Number**: 2 silver objects initially, then 1 third object circling
- **Speed**: 300–400 MPH per all three witnesses
- **Altitude**: ~8,000 ft per all three
- **Color**: silver per all three
- **Shape**: disc-like or spherical (McHenry, Scott); saucer/disc-shaped (Ruvolo)
- **Heading**: NW toward Mojave per all three
- **Sound**: none per all three; Scott explicitly *"listened carefully for a few minutes but could hear no drone such as occasioned by any aircraft"*

The Stapp Inc 4 event 2 hours later is **structurally distinct** — different witness pool (flight-test personnel rather than billeting personnel), different geometry (aircraft-formation reference frame at 20,000 ft rather than ground), different morphology (ovular w/ fins/nobs rather than smooth disc). ^[inferred]

## Why this cluster matters

### Inside the SIGN intake stream

- **Inc 1 is the first numbered Incident Summary Sheet in the SIGN sequential stream**. ^[extracted] The fact that it uses the **identical 25-field "Check-List Unidentified Flying Objects" form** as every later 1948–49 SIGN sheet establishes that **the form predates the Cabell directive by ~7 months**. ^[inferred] See [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-1-100]] for the full cross-bundle continuity argument.
- **Sub-incident lettering convention** is established at Inc 1 (1, 1c, 1e). The 173–233 and 101–172 bundles' use of A/B/C lettering for corroborating witness sheets descends from this precedent. ^[extracted]
- **Multi-witness intake protocol** at Muroc — McHenry calling additional witnesses to verify, with subsequent witnesses signing their own intake sheets — is the earliest preserved instance of **systematic AAF witness corroboration** in the SIGN intake stream. ^[inferred]

### The Stapp datum

**Capt John Paul Stapp** is independently notable in aviation safety and biomechanics history (he later set human-tolerance acceleration records at Edwards AFB). His Inc 4 statement is preserved in the file at unusual length:

> *"As this object descended through a low enough level to permit observation of its lateral silhouette, it presented a distinct ovular outline, with two (2) projections on the upper surface which might have been thick fins or nobs. These crossed each other at intervals, suggesting either rotation or oscillation of slow type. No smoke flames, propeller arks, engine noise, or other clausable or visible mens of propulsion were noted. The color was silvery, resembling aluminum painted fabric, and did not appear as dense as a parachute canopy."*

Stapp's three explicit personal opinions in the file:

1. *"I think it was a man-made object, as evidenced distainty by the outline and functional appearance."*
2. *"It's size was not far from 25 feet with a parachute canopy."*
3. *"The path followed by this object appeared as though it might have been dropped from a great height."*

These three opinions **anchor Stapp on the man-made / dropped-from-height side** of the period's interpretation space — distinct from Wise's "**weather balloon at first then noticed it was oscillating in a forward whirling movement without losing altitude**" hedge in Inc 3. ^[inferred]

### As an institutional founding event

The Muroc cluster establishes the institutional pattern that defines [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]]:

- AAF / military aviation personnel as primary witnesses
- Multi-witness corroboration sought at site
- Standardized 25-field form intake
- Speed / altitude / heading recorded numerically with witness uncertainty markers
- Agent's narrative summary attached
- Cross-base correlation across same-day events

The Pacific Northwest patrolman wave 4 days earlier (4 Jul 1947, Inc 5–16) and the Phoenix photographic event 1 day earlier (Inc 40, William Rhodes) **set the public-press baseline** for the wave; the **Muroc cluster sets the institutional baseline**. ^[inferred]

## Open questions

- **Inc 3 vs Inc 1 ordering.** Inc 3 (Maj Wise, 7 Jul 1947 1010) **precedes** Inc 1 (8 Jul 1947 0930) in event-date but is filed third in the bundle. This **filing-order vs. event-order discrepancy** is a recurring feature of the SIGN intake stream and shows the bundle is organized by **filing date** at AMC T-2, not event date. ^[inferred]
- **The "third object" in Inc 1.** McHenry's account states 2 objects were initially observed flying NW, then 5 minutes later a **third silver object circled** at the same altitude over the airfield's north end. Whether this third object was one of the original two having reversed course or a separate vehicle is open. ^[ambiguous]
- **The Stapp seat-ejection experiment context.** The fact that Inc 4 occurred **during preparation for a seat-ejection experiment** (with 2 P-82s and an A-26 already at 20,000 ft) means there was **substantial USAAF aerial activity in progress at the moment of the sighting**, but no aircrew sighting reports are preserved in the bundle. Whether the 4 USAAF aircraft also observed the object is open. ^[ambiguous]

## Section 3 / 4AF-channel addition (this batch)

[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|FBI HQ section 3]] (this batch) preserves the **same six sworn affidavits** at OCR pages 60-72 in their **original 4AF-1208-I CIC-channel form** — the Headquarters Fourth Air Force Investigation Report dated **18 August 1947** (period covered 8 July 1947), classified CONFIDENTIAL, controlling office Air Defense Command Mitchel Field. ^[extracted] The six sworn affidavits in section 3:

| Affiant | Rank | Date given | Authority taken by | Affidavit content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Col Gilkey** | CO Muroc AAF | 11 Aug 1947 (orally) | Capt Harry D. Black, Intelligence Officer | Object he saw, **believed at the time to be paper and of no significance** — does not justify further reports |
| **Maj Richard R. Shoop** | Office of Chief of Tech Engineering Division | 11 Jul 1947 | (self-witness statement) | At ~noon 8 Jul 1947, attention called by Col Gilkey to thin metallic object 5-8 mi N at intermediate altitude in **oscillating descent then climb** |
| **T/Sgt Joseph Ruvolo** | NCO Charge Billeting | 14 Jul 1947 | 1st Lt J. C. McHenry, Billeting Officer | "**Two flying disc or saucer shaped silver colored objects**" NW direction ~350-400 mph at ~7500-8000 ft; *"I am of good health and sound mind and this was no hallucination"* |
| **1st Lt Joseph C. McHenry** | Billeting Officer in Charge | 11 Jul 1947 | Mr. Thomas A. McMillan, CIC S/A this Station | First sighting; pre-conversation skepticism *"Someone will have to show me one of these Disc before I will believe it"*; observed two silver objects then a third |
| **Maj J. C. Wise** | Test Pilot | 13 Aug 1947 | Mr. Thomas A. McMillan, CIC Agent | **7 Jul 1947 ~1010**, while running up the XP-84 on the ground — yellowish-white sphere about 5-10 ft diameter, oscillating forward whirling movement without losing altitude (the day-prior Inc 3 case) |
| **Capt John Paul Strapp** [Stapp] | Flight Test | 12 Aug 1947 | Mr. Thomas A. McMillan, CIC Agent | **8 Jul 1947 1150** while sitting in observation truck Area #3 Rogers Dry Lake; ovular silver object lower than 20,000 ft; first impression was *"premature ejection of the seat and dummy had occurred"*; ~25 ft size, *"appeared as though it might have been dropped from a great height"* |

The 4AF-channel material is **structurally identical content** to the SIGN-channel Inc 1, 1c, 1e, 2, 4 sheets but with **different filing-channel context**: ^[inferred] the 4AF report is a unified investigation document with a synopsis, file number 1208-I, and CIC-Sub-Detachment-commander signature line; the SIGN version atomizes the same affidavits across numbered Incident Summary Sheets. ^[inferred] The 4AF documentation precedes Project SIGN's stand-up by ~5 months, supporting the inference that **SIGN's intake stream re-used the 4AF CIC affidavits as numbered-Incident-template input**. ^[inferred]

The cross-channel preservation strengthens witness credibility — the same affidavits appear in two independent institutional repositories with consistent content. ^[inferred]
