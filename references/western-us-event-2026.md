# Western US Event Slides (May 2026)

A four-slide briefing deck (Mistral-OCR'd) titled by event name. The deck documents a tight temporal-spatial cluster of UAP observations by **seven federal law enforcement special agents** (USPER1–USPER7) operating in pairs/teams across two days at one Western U.S. location, plus follow-up measurements by [[entities/aaro|AARO]]. The witnesses are anonymized as USPER tokens — a common convention in [[entities/aaro|AARO]]-handled reporting.

## Cross-cutting observations

- **Recurring witnesses USPER5 and USPER6** appear in all four events; USPER1–USPER4 appear only in Event 1; USPER7 joins for Event 4 but does not see the object. ^[inferred]
- **AARO post-hoc instrumented measurement** is referenced only on the "Large, Fiery Orb" slide (revising witness range from 500–600 m to ~1050 m and witness "small helicopter cockpit" size estimate to a true diameter of 12–18 m), and the "Dark Kite" slide ("triangular" reclassification). This suggests AARO had access to the site for follow-up survey/photogrammetry. ^[inferred]
- **The "kite" pair** (Events 3 and 4) occur within ~30 minutes and within a few hundred meters of each other, strongly suggesting the same or an identical class of object reappeared.

- Witness count (7) and the multi-vantage-point structure of Event 1 raise the credibility floor, but all four events remain **eyewitness-based**.
