# FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894 — Section 6 (Serials 246-301)

## Structural function — section 6 is the green-fireball / Twinkle / pre-1952-wave passive-accumulation tranche

Where [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|section 5]] documented the **transition** to post-cooperation-regime passive accumulation (Boggs-Hearn memo confirming both AF + FBI exits, Aztec myth-seed laundering, Oak Ridge / Adcock dry-run) and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|section 7]] documents the **operationalization under load** (1952 wave, SAC Letter #83 reaffirmation, CIA-IAC Robertson-Panel run-up), **section 6 is the chronological hinge that sits between them and reframes the period not as institutional silence but as the green-fireball / Project Twinkle / critical-facility-anomaly escalation**. ^[inferred] **Five paradigm-defining strands** in section 6: ^[inferred]

### 1. Project Twinkle is FBI-canonized (Aug-Oct 1950)

The **single most institutionally consequential thread** in section 6 ^[inferred]: serials 246 + 250 + adjacent material introduce **[[concepts/project-twinkle|Project Twinkle]]** to the FBI corpus — the 24-hour USAF observational network at **Vaughn / Holloman AFB, New Mexico**, contracted to **Land-Air, Inc.** by **AMC Geophysical Research Division (Cambridge MA)**, with **Dr. Anthony O. Mirarchi** as Project Engineer. ^[extracted]

- **Serial 246 (21 Aug 1950)** — **Belmont → Ladd memo** *"SUMMARY OF AERIAL PHENOMENA IN NEW MEXICO"* ^[extracted]: documents that OSI has *"expressed concern in connection with the continued appearance of unexplained phenomena described as green fireballs, discs and meteors in the vicinity of sensitive installations in New Mexico"* ^[extracted]; reports **"approximately 150 observations of aerial phenomena since 1948"** ^[extracted]; classifies them into three types (green fireballs, discs, meteors); names **Dr. Lincoln LaPaz**'s 23 May 1950 analysis (the **Seventh Report**, q.v.) concluding *"approximately half of the phenomena recorded were of meteoric origin. The other phenomena commonly referred to as green fireballs or discs he believed to be U.S. guided missiles being tested in the neighborhood of the installations"* ^[extracted]; describes **Project Twinkle**'s establishment at Vaughn NM with a 24-hour day watch instituted by Land-Air personnel on 24 May 1950 (8-10 objects sighted that date). ^[extracted]
- **Serial 250 (9 Oct 1950)** — **Ladd → The Director memo** *"FLYING SAUCERS / FLYING DISCS / GREEN FIREBALLS"* ^[extracted]: 6-week update; *"To date the Air Force has not advised us of any new developments in connection with this project"* ^[extracted]; **"an average of approximately three or four complaints have been received per month from June through September"** ^[extracted]; **"A review of Bureau files does not indicate that there has been any increase in the sightings of these phenomena during or as a result of the war in Korea"** ^[extracted]; and most consequentially: *"Their investigation of these phenomena fails to indicate that the sightings involved space ships or missiles from any other planet or country … the complaints received by them have failed to indicate any definite pattern of activity"* ^[extracted].
- **Enclosed verbatim**: the **25 May 1950 OSI 17th District (Kirtland NM) Summary** to Brig. Gen. Joseph F. Carroll (Director of Special Investigations, USAF) — a ~38-page tabular catalog of **~175 sightings** from December 1948 through May 1950 in the New Mexico area, with the verbatim 23 May 1950 LaPaz Seventh Report attached. ^[extracted] See [[references/osi-17th-district-nm-summary-1950-05-25]] (NEW) and [[references/lapaz-seventh-report-1950-05-23]] (NEW).

**Institutional consequence**: Project Twinkle is the **first FBI-corpus formal AF-contractor critical-facility scientific-study program**. ^[inferred] It is **operationally distinct** from [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]] (which the [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|Boggs-Hearn memo]] declared discontinued in December 1949) — Twinkle is **AMC Geophysical Research Division** rather than AMC Wright-Patterson T-2, and it is **green-fireball-specific** rather than disc-general. ^[inferred] The FBI's Albuquerque Office has been formally **plugged into Mirarchi's reporting chain** for any espionage/sabotage-jurisdiction triggers — establishing a **Bureau-to-AF-contractor liaison** at the scientific-investigation tier. ^[extracted] See [[concepts/project-twinkle]] (NEW).

### 2. The Cabell AFOIC-CC-1 directive (8 Sep 1950) — successor to AIRMEM #4

Serial 249 is the **AFOIC-CC-1 "Reporting of Information on Unconventional Aircraft" directive** of **8 Sep 1950**, signed **Maj Gen [[entities/c-p-cabell|C. P. Cabell]]** as Director of Intelligence, *"By Command of the Chief of Staff"*, addressed to *Commanding Generals, Major Air Commands, ZI and Overseas, [and] All United States Air Attaches* ^[extracted]. The directive is **structurally the successor** to the 15 Feb 1949 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|Cabell AIRMEM #4]] ^[inferred] — same author (Cabell), same office (HQ USAF Directorate of Intelligence), same subject ("Unconventional Aircraft"), similar 9-field EEI schema and Air Form 112 follow-up requirement; **but the routing destination is now MCIS** (not MCIAXO-3 of AIRMEM #4) and the language is **more elaborated**. ^[extracted] **Info copies** are sent to: *Dir/Int G-2 Army; Dir/Naval Int; Commandant (INT) US Coast Guard; Sp Asst for Research & Intel, State; Director FBI; Director of [presumably CIA, OCR-truncated]*. ^[extracted]

The directive's **9-element EEI schema** is the most elaborated **observational protocol** for unconventional aircraft in the FBI corpus prior to the 1952 wave ^[inferred]: (1) object description (shape/size/color/formation/aerodynamic features/trail/exhaust/propulsion/speed/sound/maneuvers/manner of disappearance); (2) time in 24-hour clock zonal time + duration; (3) manner of observation (visual/electronic/from-air/surface) with optical/electronic equipment description; (4) location with lat/long and landmark + distance/direction/altitude of object; (5) observer-reliability identification; (6) weather/winds aloft; (7) any explanatory activity/condition; (8) physical evidence; (9) interception/identification action taken. ^[extracted] Section 6 also imposes **electrical-transmission-then-AF-Form-112-follow-up** within 10 days, *"sketches and signed attested narrative statements of observers"*, and a **publicity-suppression instruction**: *"It is desired that no publicity be given this reporting or analysis activity."* ^[extracted]

**Institutional consequence**: AFOIC-CC-1 confirms the AF's **December 1949 GRUDGE termination** (per [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|Boggs-Hearn]]) was **not** an exit from the reporting-protocol business — the AF rebuilt the protocol in the 9 months between Boggs-Hearn (28 Mar 1950) and AFOIC-CC-1 (8 Sep 1950). ^[inferred] AIRMEM #4 (15 Feb 1949) → AFOIC-CC-1 (8 Sep 1950) is a **third structural reporting-protocol revision** in 19 months — paralleling the FBI's BB-57 (1 Oct 1947) → SAC Letter #38 (25 Mar 1949) → SAC Letter #83 (29 Aug 1952) cadence. ^[inferred] The two policy-bulletin chains run **in lockstep**, each agency revising its passive-intake protocol at ~2-year intervals. ^[inferred] See [[references/cabell-afoic-cc-1-unconventional-aircraft-1950-09-08]] (NEW).

### 3. The LaPaz Seventh Report (23 May 1950) — formal scientific framework for the Urals-missile hypothesis

The section retains the **23 May 1950 LaPaz "Anomalous Luminous Phenomena (Seventh Report)"** in full ^[extracted] — addressed to Lt Col Doyle Rees (CO 17th District OSI), the **definitive scientific articulation of the green-fireball-distinct-from-meteor framework** in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] The report enumerates **11 specific differences** between the December 1948 green fireballs and typical meteors ^[extracted]:

1. Horizontal trajectory (unusual for meteors).
2. Very low altitude (>40 mi typical for meteors).
3. Velocity less than typical meteors but greater than V-2 rockets or jets.
4. Noiselessness (meteorites penetrating to low altitudes invariably make violent noises).
5. No initial-faint-hair-line brightening (genuine meteors brighten progressively).
6. **Pronounced north-half-of-sky approach bearing** (genuine meteors come from all compass points equally).
7. **Curious temporal association with meteor showers** (Quadrantid / Perseid / Geminid) — *"this relationship might indicate an attempt to render the green fireballs less conspicuous by causing them to appear only when there is considerable meteoric activity"* ^[extracted].
8. The 5218Å green color matches **copper-salt blowpipe-flame spectrum** by laboratory test (rare for genuine meteors).
9. 2-3 second duration (longer than ordinary visual meteors at 0.4-0.5 sec, shorter than meteorite-fall duration at 5+ sec).
10. Horizontal infall direction (strict vertical infall also very rarely observed for genuine meteor falls).
11. **Time-distribution maximum at 2030 local time** — coincides with **neither** the meteor maximum (0300) **nor** the meteorite-fall maximum (1600).

The report then introduces the **Urals-missile interpretation**: *"the time interval … extends from about 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. in the Ural region of the USSR. Since missiles moving with velocities of the order of those found for the green fireballs for which real path determinations have been possible would travel from the southern Urals to New Mexico in less than 15 minutes, a possible interpretation [is] guided missiles launched from bases in the Urals."* ^[extracted]

LaPaz's recommendations: **dual-station optical instrumentation** at Roswell + Sacramento NM under Dr. L. A. Manning of Stanford, **radar investigation** under Dr. Millman, and **ground search / dust collection** under Dr. William Crozier (New Mexico School of Mines) — proposed at Major Oder's $40/day consultant rate, which LaPaz declines in favor of **voluntary service**. ^[extracted] **The recommendations are operationally implemented as Project Twinkle ~12 weeks later**. ^[inferred] See [[references/lapaz-seventh-report-1950-05-23]] (NEW).

**Institutional consequence**: the Seventh Report is the **earliest formal post-Roswell critical-facility-threat scientific document** in the FBI corpus ^[inferred] — and the Urals-launch hypothesis is the **structural opposite** of the section-5 Boggs-Hearn debunk-by-default frame. The FBI HQ file thus holds, **side-by-side in the same calendar quarter**, both *"three-fourths misidentifications, hoax-default"* (Boggs-Hearn, 28 Mar 1950) and *"guided missiles from the southern Urals, 15-minute flight time"* (LaPaz, 23 May 1950). ^[inferred]

### 4. The Fort Monmouth radar event (10-11 Sep 1951) — first AN/MPG-1 + SCR-584 tracked target case

Serial ~287 captures a **20 Sep 1951 Newark SAC URGENT teletype** ^[extracted] from G-2 Fort Monmouth (Andrew J. Reid) reporting an **AN/MPG-1 radar set** picking up *"a fast moving low flying target"* at 1110 hours 10 Sep 1951 SE of Fort Monmouth, **changing azimuth too rapidly for full-aided azimuth tracking** (which "normally is fast enough to track jet aircraft"). ^[extracted] A second incident at 1515 hours 10 Sep 1951 with an **SCR-584** radar at ~32,000 yards N of Ft. Monmouth, **at ~31,000 ft altitude**, **speed exceeded the maximum aided tracking rate of 700 mph**, requiring manual range tracking. ^[extracted] A third incident 11 Sep 1951 with the target **rising nearly vertically**, then proceeding at *"extremely rapid rate in range in a southerly direction."* ^[extracted] Above **three witnesses** present at the second + third events. Reid's request: *"info be co-ordinated with Air Force"*. ^[extracted]

**Institutional consequence**: the Fort Monmouth case is the **first AN/MPG-1 + SCR-584 radar-tracked UAP case in the FBI corpus** ^[inferred] — predating the famous July 1952 Washington DC radar incidents by ~10 months. It is **structurally distinct from** the [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-adcock-1950-03|Oak Ridge / Adcock]] case (Adcock was a civilian amateur with a surplus APN-7; Ft. Monmouth is institutional G-2 with military radar). The 1951 case is documented in classified Army intelligence channels and routed laterally to the FBI through the Newark SAC, **not** through the BB-57 / SAC Letter #38 OSI-handoff protocol — i.e. the **Army G-2 → FBI HQ direct channel**, an extension of channel (e). ^[inferred] See [[references/sighting-fort-monmouth-radar-1951-09]] (NEW).

### 5. The Oak Ridge NEPA / Y-12 / 663rd AC&W radar+visual sequence (Dec 1950 - Jan 1951) — extends the Adcock thread

Serials ~258-265 (Dec 1950 - Feb 1951) capture an **Oak Ridge multi-event sequence**: ^[extracted]

- **18 Dec 1950 Hood / Hribar / Steele / Carss / Gray / Frey 0827 sighting** at S-50 area / Y-12 plant ^[extracted] — six NEPA Division (Fairchild Engine and Airplane Corp.) employees observe a brightly-reflecting object via car windshield; later detached by 663rd AC&W Squadron radar tracking at 0832-0845 hours, ending in fighter-intercept attempt with negative results.
- **20 Dec 1950, 14-16 Jan 1951 additional 663rd AC&W radar events** — Captains Clevenger + Aiken (Controllers, 663rd AC&W); 17 January 1951 letter "Report of Unusual Incident 2145 hours 16 January 1951" by CO 663rd. ^[extracted]
- **5 Dec 1950 Hoover-to-SAC Knoxville teletype** ^[extracted]: *"REGARDING POSSIBLE RADAR JAMMING AT OAK RIDGE. ARRANGEMENTS SHOULD BE MADE TO OBTAIN ALL FACTS CONCERNING POSSIBLE RADAR JAMMING BY IONIZATION OF PARTICLES IN ATMOSPHERE. CONDUCT APPROPRIATE INVESTIGATION TO DETERMINE WHETHER INCIDENT OCCURRING NORTHEAST OF OLIVER SPRINGS, TENNESSEE, COULD HAVE HAD ANY CONNECTION WITH ALLEGED RADAR JAMMING."* ^[extracted] Original copy filed at **65-58300-141** ([[references/fbi-san-antonio-vital-installations-1949|Vital Installations file]]) — confirms the **cross-class filing pattern**.

**Institutional consequence**: the Oak Ridge NEPA sequence is the **continuation of the Adcock dry-run** under operationalized institutional response. ^[inferred] The 8 Dec 1950 Richmond SAC URGENT ^[extracted] — *"Army Intelligence Richmond … put on immediate high alert for any data whatsoever concerning flying saucers"* — confirms the **Army-Intelligence-side** was reactivated to high-readiness in early December 1950, **coincident with the Oak Ridge radar-jamming hypothesis investigation** at the AEC's Y-12 critical facility. ^[inferred] See [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-nepa-1950-12-1951-01]] (NEW).
