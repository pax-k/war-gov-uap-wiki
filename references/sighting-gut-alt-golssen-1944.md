# Sighting — Gut Alt Golssen, Germany (1944, ~30 mi east of Berlin)

A **wartime forced-labor sighting**, reported to the FBI **13 years after the fact** by [[entities/wladyslaw-krasuski|Wladyslaw Krasuski]] (Polish DP, then resident in Detroit) on **7 November 1957**. The artifact-level account is preserved in [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957]] (FBI Detroit file 100-26505, Blank Memo, SA Cassius Rathbun's interview).

## Setting

- **Location**: swamp area "a short distance north of Gut Alt Golssen", ~30 mi east of Berlin, Germany. Modern coordinates: roughly Brandenburg state, ~50 km SE of Berlin. ^[inferred]
- **Date**: 1944, month not recalled.
- **Personnel**: Krasuski (Polish POW since May 1942), one German civilian driving the tractor, "SS" guards, and a 16–18-man work crew of Russian / French / Polish POWs cutting "hay" in the swamp. The only named co-witness recalled by Krasuski was [[entities/franciszek-grabowski|Franciszek Grabowski]] (~50 years old in 1944).
- **Concealment posture**: the area was deserted apart from the work crew and the German guards; Krasuski's observation of the second-episode vehicle was **surreptitious** because of the SS presence.

## First episode — tractor stall + auditory cue

While en route to work, the **tractor engine stalled** on a swamp road. Witnesses heard a **high-pitched whine "similar to that produced by a large electric generator."** An **"SS" guard appeared** and spoke briefly with the German tractor driver, who waited 5–10 minutes. After the noise stopped, the **tractor engine started normally**. ^[extracted]

This first episode is the **earliest specifically engine-stall–coupled-to-acoustic-cue UAP-adjacent observation** in the corpus, predating the [[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon|Levelland TX 1957]] cluster by ~13 years and the modern [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement|aircraft-engagement]] morphology by ~80 years. ^[inferred]

## Second episode — the vehicle (observed ~3 hours later, same swamp area)

While the work crew was cutting "hay" away from the road, Krasuski **surreptitiously observed** (because of the German civilian-in-charge and the SS guards):

- A **circular tarpaulin-walled enclosure**, ~100–150 yards in diameter, ~50 ft tall, set in the otherwise-deserted swamp.
- A **vehicle** that **rose vertically** from inside the enclosure, **clearing the 50 ft wall**, then **moved slowly horizontally** a short distance out of view (sightline obstructed by trees).

**Vehicle morphology** (observed from ~500 ft):

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| Plan-view shape | Circular |
| Diameter | **75–100 yards** |
| Height | **~14 ft** |
| Top section | ~5–6 ft, **stationary, dark gray** |
| Bottom section | ~5–6 ft, **stationary, dark gray** |
| Middle section | **~3 ft, continuously rotating "blur" — "similar to an aeroplane propeller"** but extending around the full circumference |
| Sound | Similar to but **lower-pitched** than the earlier whine |
| Engine effect | Tractor engine stalled again; the German driver did not attempt to start the engine until the noise stopped, after which it started normally |

The **rotating-middle-section morphology** is structurally distinct from the foo-fighter / ball-of-fire morphology of [[concepts/foo-fighter-phenomenon|the same wartime corpus]] and from the disc / cylinder / V-formation deltas that dominate 1948–49. ^[inferred] It is closer in profile to **descriptions of post-1947 "saucer with rotating rim" drawings** than to anything else in the wiki's WWII corpus — but this is **delayed-13-years recollection by a single witness**, not a corroborated wartime engagement record. ^[ambiguous]

## Cabling and ground infrastructure

Krasuski observed **uninsulated metal cables (possibly copper)**, **1.5–2 inches in diameter**, on and under the swamp surface, in places under water, running between the tarpaulin enclosure and a **small concrete column-like structure** between the road and the enclosure. The cabling implies an **external power feed or signal coupling** to whatever was housed inside the enclosure. ^[inferred] The wiki holds no other 1940s artifact mentioning a comparable physical-infrastructure context.

## Postwar revisit

Krasuski revisited the area shortly after the end of WWII. He found:

- The cables had been **removed**.
- The previous locations of the concrete column-like structure and the tarpaulin enclosure were **covered by water**.

No subsequent contact with any of the 16–18-man work crew is recorded, except that Krasuski "presumed" Grabowski had returned to Poland.

## Why the witness reported in 1957 (not 1944)

Krasuski was **prompted to report by a contemporaneous (Nov 1957) news story** — "a news report of mysterious vehicle in Texas causing engines to stall," heard via the **Polish radio program**. The Texas case is almost certainly the **[[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon|Levelland, TX November 2–3, 1957]] vehicle-engine-stall sightings**, then breaking on US news. ^[inferred] The phenomenology match (engine stall + ascending circular object) is what triggered the 13-year-delayed report.

The reporting chain:

1. Krasuski writes to **[[entities/robert-cutler|Robert Cutler]]** (Special Assistant to President Eisenhower), 4 Nov 1957.
2. Cutler's office forwards to FBI HQ.
3. FBI Director URGENT teletype to SAC Detroit, 6 Nov 1957, 5:40 PM.
4. SA Cassius Rathbun interviews Krasuski **in person** the next day, 7 Nov 1957 (3 hours after the AF-channel event also being investigated). ^[inferred]
5. Detroit AIRTEL back to Director with Blank Memo enclosed, 7 Nov 1957.

This is the **wiki's first White-House-routed citizen UFO letter**, and the **FBI's first sit-down interview** for a UAP-adjacent witness in the corpus. ^[inferred] See [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957]] for the routing-level analysis.

## Provenance and credibility caveats

- **Single-witness, delayed-13-years recall.** The vehicle morphology (75–100 yd, 14 ft, rotating middle section) and the auditory/engine-stall coupling are recorded only in Krasuski's account. The named co-witness (Grabowski) was uncontactable in 1957. The FBI did not attempt corroboration via Russian/French/Polish POW networks. ^[inferred]
- **No physical or photographic evidence.** The artifact-level record is a written interview narrative, not a sketched track or a photograph.
- **SA Rathbun's character assessment** explicitly states "no indication of irrational or otherwise abnormal behavior by KRASUSKI." This is the FBI's best-effort credibility note in 1957 terms. It does not address memory drift over 13 years.
- **Wartime-secrecy frame.** The tarpaulin enclosure, SS guards, copper cabling, and concrete column-like structure are *consistent with* a Reich-era secret-weapons site — and would explain why the work crew was kept on a swamp road behind tarpaulin walls. But the morphology Krasuski reports is **inconsistent** with the known German programs (Me-262, V-1, V-2, [[concepts/horten-flying-wing|Horten flying wing]], Flak Bombe). The wiki's [[synthesis/horten-thesis-vs-disc-sightings|Horten thesis]] does not naturally accommodate a 75-yard vertically-ascending platform with rotating mid-section. ^[inferred] Treat this account as a **second-order witness datum** for the wartime track, not as confirmation of any specific German-program hypothesis.
