# Hoover → Maj Gen McDonald Letter Announcing FBI Termination of Saucer Investigation (27 September 1947)

A **single-page letter** signed **J. Edgar Hoover, Director** dated **27 September 1947**, addressed to **Major General George C. McDonald, Assistant Chief Air Staff - 2, The Pentagon, Washington, D. C.**, filed at **62-83894-121** in [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|FBI HQ File 62-HQ-83894]] [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR page 156. ^[extracted] This is **the FBI's formal external-facing announcement** that the Bureau is **discontinuing all investigative activity regarding flying discs**, sent **four days before** the parallel internal-facing instrument [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 57, Series 1947]] issues to all FBI field offices on 1 October 1947. ^[inferred]

The letter's existence is **paradigm-defining for the FBI-AAF cooperation regime closure**: prior to this serial's recovery, the FBI's exit from the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]] was reconstructed as an **internal-only** event (BB-57 to all SACs, plus subsequent operational citations). ^[inferred] The McDonald letter establishes that Hoover **simultaneously** notified the AAF interlocutor at AC/AS-2 (the AAF office that originated the cooperation request through Schulgen on 9 Jul 1947). ^[inferred]

## Verbatim — the letter text

The letter's three substantive paragraphs:

> The Federal Bureau of Investigation has been requested by your office to assist in the investigation of reported sightings of flying discs.
>
> My attention has been called to instructions disseminated by the Air Forces relative to this matter. I have been advised that these instructions indicate that the Air Forces would interview responsible observers while the FBI would investigate incidents of discs found on the ground, thereby relieving the Air Forces of running down incidents which in many cases turned out to be **"ash can covers, toilet seats and whatnot."**
>
> In view of the apparent understanding by the Air Forces of the position of the Federal Bureau of Investigation in this matter, **I cannot permit the personnel and time of this organization to be dissipated in this manner.**
>
> I am advising the Field Divisions of the Federal Bureau of Investigation **to discontinue all investigative activity regarding the reported sightings of flying discs**, and am instructing them to refer all complaints received to the appropriate Air Force representative in their area.
>
> Sincerely yours,
> J. Edgar Hoover
> Director

^[extracted]

## What the letter signals

Three observations are paradigm-defining:

### 1. The "ash can covers, toilet seats and whatnot" framing is the documented trigger

Hoover's letter explicitly cites the **AAF-disseminated instructions' "ash can covers"** framing as the institutional offense that prompted FBI exit. ^[extracted] This is the **same framing transcribed verbatim** in the [[references/adc-d333.5ex-cooperation-1947-09-03|ADC D333.5EX letter of 3 September 1947]] (signed *"By Command of Lieutenant General Stratemeyer"* by Col R. H. Smith) preserved at [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-3|section 3]] OCR pages 160 + 183. ^[extracted] Hoover's letter does not name the ADC document directly, but the verbatim phrasing match is unambiguous. ^[inferred]

This makes the **ADC letter the proximate documentary cause** of FBI exit — the [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett admission]] (~22 Sep 1947, [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]] serial 86) was the deeper structural cause but is not memorialized in the official outgoing letter. ^[inferred] The McDonald letter constructs the **least-embarrassing public-record reasoning** for the termination — institutional dignity ("personnel and time … dissipated") rather than secret-project doubts. ^[inferred]

### 2. The recipient is McDonald, not Schulgen

The letter is addressed to **Maj Gen George C. McDonald** (Assistant Chief Air Staff - 2), not to **Brig Gen [[entities/george-f-schulgen|George F. Schulgen]]** (Requirements Intelligence Branch under McDonald). ^[extracted] McDonald is the **formal AC/AS-2 of record** at the time — Schulgen had been the operational interlocutor for the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime]] from 9 Jul 1947 onward, but the formal-correspondence-of-record is to the senior officer. ^[inferred] This is consistent with FBI institutional practice in cross-agency policy correspondence (Director-to-Director-equivalent rather than Director-to-Branch-Chief). ^[inferred]

McDonald (whose name later appears in [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett's admission]] as the AAF officer who would issue the McDonald → LeMay R&D-confirmation memo) is the **structural recipient of both the Garrett-admission proposed action and the FBI-termination announcement** — an institutional convergence that the corpus did not previously document at this level of precision. ^[inferred]

### 3. The 4-day FBI-internal-vs-external lead

The chronology is precise: ^[inferred]

| Date | Event | Document |
|---|---|---|
| **25 Sep 1947** | Ladd → Hoover memo recommending termination + proposed Bulletin attached | [[references/fbi-ladd-proposed-bulletin-1947-09-25\|62-83894-121 Ladd memo]] |
| **27 Sep 1947** | Hoover writes "I agree" approval; signs the McDonald letter | this letter (62-83894-121); approval handwriting on Ladd memo |
| **27 Sep 1947 (same day)** | Hoover personally signs the external-facing letter to AAF | this letter |
| **1 Oct 1947** | [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947\|BB-57]] issues to all FBI field offices | reconstructed from section 4 + 5 citations |

The McDonald letter and BB-57 are filed **at the same serial** (62-83894-121) — both are **filings of the same termination decision** but on different communication channels (external policy letter vs internal operational bulletin). ^[inferred] The 4-day lead between McDonald letter (27 Sep) and BB-57 (1 Oct) reflects standard FBI HQ → SAC bulletin-distribution mimeograph + mailroom turnaround. ^[inferred]

## Implications for the cooperation-regime narrative

Three implications follow:

1. **The cooperation regime was closed by formal cross-agency notification, not by silent supersession.** Prior wiki framing (e.g., [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947]]) treated BB-57 as "internal-only" without external notification of the closure. The McDonald letter establishes that closure was **announced to the originating AAF channel** at Maj-Gen level on the same day as the internal-bulletin approval. ^[inferred]

2. **The "ash can covers" framing is the official record of why the regime closed.** The deeper trigger (Garrett's admission of likely-USG-classified-project) is **not on record** — it appears only in the Sep-22 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2 serial 86]] internal-FBI memo. ^[inferred] The McDonald letter therefore serves as the **public-facing institutional cover-story** for a privately-much-deeper rupture. ^[inferred]

3. **The Stratemeyer ADC re-enlistment attempt of 4 Feb 1948** (preserved in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-4|section 4]] page 36, the [[references/adc-stratemeyer-flying-disc-1948-02-04|ADC Mitchel Field directive]]) takes on **new institutional meaning** in light of the McDonald letter — Stratemeyer's organization was explicitly named in Hoover's reasoning (the ADC directive Hoover protests was *"By Command of Lieutenant General Stratemeyer"*). When ADC reissues the cooperation-coordination directive 4 months later, **Stratemeyer is structurally retrying the same posture Hoover already terminated** — explaining why Hoover (via Keay/Reynolds) so swiftly reaffirms BB-57 still applies in Feb 1948. ^[inferred]
