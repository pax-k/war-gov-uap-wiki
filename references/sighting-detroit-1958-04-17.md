# Sighting — Detroit, MI (17 April 1958, ~0408 LT)

A **single-witness, pre-dawn, citizen-reported UFO sighting** over Detroit, Michigan, recorded as **serial 844 of FBI Detroit file 100-18221** on the morning of the event. See [[references/fbi-detroit-100-18221-serial-844-1958]] for the artifact-level analysis.

## Witness

- **Name**: David Weaver, age 23.
- **Address**: 15801 Decosta, Detroit, Michigan.
- **Telephone**: KEnwood 6-7546.
- **Background**: Son of a Detroit policeman; prior Civil Air Patrol (CAP) experience but self-described as "**not too familiar with the identification of aircraft**". On his way home from his Detroit employment at the time of the observation. ^[extracted]

This is the **first civilian-class witness** in the corpus's modern (post-1949) thread — distinct from the trained-observer / federal-LE / intel-witness classes that dominate later [[synthesis/federal-le-uap-witness-pattern|federal-LE/intel-witness]] sources. ^[inferred]

## Morphology (as reported)

- **Shape**: "**circular object**" ^[extracted]
- **Distinctive feature**: a "**crystal-type dome that reflected lights**" ^[extracted]
- **Self-luminous?**: Not stated; "reflected lights" implies the dome was reflecting **ground / city lights** rather than emitting its own. ^[inferred]
- **Sound**: Not addressed in source. ^[ambiguous]
- **Trajectory**: Linear, SW → N, no manoeuvre details recorded. ^[extracted]

The descriptor is **on-thesis for classic 1950s "saucer with dome"** morphology — the canonical popular-UFO archetype of the era — without the witness having been pre-loaded with any 1948 [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element template]] structure. ^[inferred]

## Citizen routing behaviour

The witness's call sequence is itself part of the record:

1. **First attempt**: telephoned **Selfridge Field** (USAF base NE of Detroit) to reach the Air Force; calls "unproductive."
2. **Fallback**: telephoned the **FBI Detroit field office** (0408 LT).
3. SA Reynolds takes the call, drafts an Office Memorandum to SAC Detroit, recommends "Advise proper air force authorities," and a Lt. Col. (likely "Bernie") E. Prater is **telephonically advised the same day**. ^[extracted]

This sequence corroborates that, **a decade after [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|Cabell's 1948 directive]]**, the **Air Force was still the citizen-recognised lead authority** for UFO sightings, with the FBI functioning as a **fallback channel**. ^[inferred] The civilian-recognition path predates [[entities/aaro|AARO]] by 65+ years. ^[inferred]

## What this sighting does *not* contain

- **No multi-witness corroboration.** Single witness, single observation point.
- **No sensor data.** Naked-eye only; no radar, no photographs, no NVG / FLIR (the 2025 [[references/sighting-helicopter-orb-encounter-2025|helicopter orb encounter]]'s multi-sensor stack is far in the future).
- **No range / size / altitude estimate.** The artifact gives a track (3 blocks S of Six Mile at Lamphere) but no altitude.
- **No object behaviour** beyond a single linear pass. Compare with the rich behavioural envelopes recorded in 1948–49 ([[references/sighting-portland-or-1949-07|Portland chase]], [[references/sighting-mountain-home-id-1949-07|Mountain Home V-formation]]) and 2023+ events ([[references/sighting-fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-2023-09|FBI Sept 2023]]).
- **No follow-up.** The endorsement records "no letter rec." from the Air Force.

The thinness of the report is itself diagnostic of the **routing instrument** the FBI used: a Standard Form 64 office memorandum is for case-file routing, not for evidentiary sighting reconstruction. ^[inferred] Compare with the FD-302 instrument, which 65 years later captured **paragraphs** of morphology and behavioural detail per witness ([[references/fbi-fd302-drone-pilot-redacted-2023|serial-4]] / [[references/fbi-fd302-serial-3-driver-redacted-2023|serial-3]]).

## Cross-event observations

- **Earliest civilian-class witness** in the modern UAP corpus thread (post-1949).
- **Earliest "domed disc" morphology** in the corpus — the 1948–49 corpus is dominated by **delta wings, cylinders, balls of fire, glowing orbs**, not classic saucer-with-dome forms.
- **Earliest record where the witness's first call goes to the Air Force** rather than to local LE — a small but durable signal about **public knowledge of the AF's UFO desk** in the late 1950s. ^[inferred]
- **Site geography**: Six Mile (W. McNichols) and Lamphere St is in NW Detroit; the witness's home address (15801 Decosta) is also in NW Detroit, consistent with the witness reporting the object on his commute home. ^[inferred]
