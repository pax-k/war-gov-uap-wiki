# Sighting — Norcatur Kansas (18 Feb 1948)

A **two-track event** filed as **Project SIGN Incident 101** in [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-101-172|the 101–172 incident summary bundle]]:

1. **A confirmed meteor explosion** at ~17:01 over Norcatur KS (visible in OK, NM, CO, KS, NE) with a **109-pound achondrite recovered** in a clover field on 24 April 1948. ^[extracted]
2. **A separate, simultaneous, *unrelated*** close-encounter account by **Leland Sammons** (farmer, 14 mi west of Stockton + 1 mi N of US 24) of a 4-foot funnel-shaped object hovering near his hog-pen, fire belching out and sucking back in, while pheasants and chickens reacted in distress. ^[extracted]

Project SIGN's evaluators preserved both accounts in the same incident sheet, **flagging the Sammons account internally** ("A determined effort is under way to check up on Sammons' veracity (and sanity!)" — [[entities/lincoln-la-paz|La Paz]] 11 April 1948). ^[extracted]

## What the witnesses reported

### The meteor (verified)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date / time | 18 Feb 1948, 17:01 |
| Location | Norcatur, KS |
| Witness | M.R. Krehbiel (Editor, Norton KS — newspaper account) |
| Number of objects | 1 |
| Altitude | 30–35 mi above earth |
| Sound | Big explosion, then "lots of little explosions" |
| Color | Bluish-white smoke smudge |
| Shape | Mushroom |
| Apparent construction | **Meteor** (achondrite) |
| Manner of disappearance | Disintegration |
| Photo | Vapor trail photo by Duane W. Wray, 9 mi N of Norton, taken 4 min after explosion |
| Physical recovery | **109-lb achondrite + smaller fragments**, recovered starting 24 Apr 1948 (per Oscar Monnig, Ft Worth Texas Observers Amateur Astronomy) |

This portion of Inc 101 is unambiguously **a confirmed meteorite fall**. ^[extracted]

### The Sammons close-encounter (contested)

Per Sammons' EXHIBIT B statement (taken at his Stockton-area farm):

> "On February 18 at about 5 P.M., I was standing near my hog-pen about 100' east of my house, when I heard the pheasants raising a disturbance and the chickens all rushed to the chicken-house. I looked around toward the house to see what was causing it and saw something hovering just above the house. I ran toward the house, and it then lowered over the north end of the house and settled toward the ground. I was then very near it, approximately 6' when it stopped about level with my face, and just wobbled around for an instant, fire belching out of it and sucking back in. The thing was about 4' long, shaped something like a funnel. There was a pipe sticking out the back of it, and once as it wobbled around, the pipe was sticking right…" ^[extracted]

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Witness | Leland Sammons (farmer, 14 mi W Stockton + 1 mi N US 24) |
| Object size | ~4 ft long |
| Shape | "Something like a funnel" |
| Behavior | Hovering above house, lowering to N end of house, settling toward ground, wobbling at face level (~6 ft from observer) |
| Acoustic effects | **Pheasants and chickens distressed** — first wildlife-alert UAP signature in the 1948 SIGN corpus |
| Apparent construction | "Pipe sticking out the back" |
| Behavioral signature | "Fire belching out and sucking back in" |

The **wildlife-alert signature** (chickens running to the chicken-house + pheasants raising a disturbance) is what attracted Sammons' attention — making it a **proximate-effect-class** observation. ^[inferred]

## The April 11, 1948 La Paz letter (Inc 101n)

**The most consequential document in this incident** is **Inc 101n**: an excerpt of a letter dated **11 April 1948** from **Dr Lincoln La Paz**, Director of the Institute of Meteoritics at the University of New Mexico, **to the Deputy Executive Director, Committee on Geophysical Sciences, Research and Development Board**. ^[extracted]

The letter responds to material forwarded under date of 30 March 1948 — primarily Markham's "[[concepts/eti-attribution-statements|space-ship from the moon]]" letter (Inc 101's supplement, dated 14 Mar 1948 from Pueblo CO) and the Sammons close-encounter EXHIBIT B. La Paz's letter is **8 months earlier** than [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|Inc 227]] — which the wiki had previously anchored as La Paz's first preserved SIGN-orbit engagement.

La Paz's substantive points in the 11 April 1948 letter:

1. **Markham's "invasion by beings from Venus or the Moon" theory is "fantastic"** — but Markham is "justified in calling attention to certain incidents as unexplained, for example, the 'flying lenses' (in my opinion 99% hoax and imagination and 1% real)." ^[extracted] **This is the corpus's earliest documented La Paz framing of "flying lenses" / disc reports as 99%-hoax-1%-real**, eight months before the December 1948 NM green fireball wave. ^[inferred]
2. **The Norcatur fall remains "a genuine meteorite fall, although one of exceptional size."** La Paz preserves the meteoritic explanation. ^[extracted]
3. **"...not a trace of meteoritic material has so far been found"** at the time of writing (11 Apr 1948). The 109-lb achondrite was recovered **two weeks later** on 24 April. ^[extracted]
4. **A "determined effort" was under way to "check up on Sammons' veracity (and sanity!)"** — La Paz had not personally interviewed Sammons. Dr D.M. Gragg (Math Instructor at Institute of Meteoritics) had interviewed Sammons and believed him "to be sincere and very badly scared". ^[extracted]
5. **La Paz cites a 29 November 1945 great fall** ("from which no meteorites have been recovered either!") and the "Four Corners and Norcatur incidents… coupled with such things as the Ussuri incident" as a pattern that "convinces me that either the earth is under a most unusual cosmic bombardment or many of the fireballs are not meteorites at all." ^[extracted] **This is La Paz's earliest preserved "or-not-meteorites" hedging in the corpus.** ^[inferred]

## Why this rewrites the corpus

Two prior wiki claims need correction:

1. **[[entities/lincoln-la-paz]]** previously anchored La Paz's first SIGN-orbit contact at **Inc 227 (Dec 1948)** — the "Significant Differences between the Fireballs Observed in the Interval Dec 5–13 and Typical Meteors" memo. The Inc 101n letter pushes that anchor back to **11 April 1948** — 8 months earlier.
2. **[[concepts/green-fireballs-new-mexico]]** previously framed the Dec 1948 NM cluster as the corpus's first multi-event single-region UAP cluster analyzed in real-time by La Paz. **Inc 101n shows La Paz had been hedging the "or-not-meteorites" pattern across the 29 Nov 1945 / Four Corners / Norcatur / Ussuri sequence by April 1948** — a 4-event hedging pattern earlier than the NM cluster.

The Norcatur Inc 101 file therefore proves La Paz was not pulled into SIGN's evaluation loop because of the Dec 1948 NM wave; **he was already engaged with SIGN-orbit material 8 months earlier**, and the NM wave was the first cluster he could investigate with first-person field access (UNM is in Albuquerque). ^[inferred]

## Markham's letters (the "moon space-ship" thread)

The Inc 101 supplement preserves a series of letters from **Norman G. Markham** (Pueblo, Colorado, MAIN HOTEL) to the Chief Signal Officer (14 Mar 1948) and others, theorizing that **on June 24, 1947, "something like a space-ship came here from the moon and upon February 18, 1948, returned to the moon"** — i.e. that the [[concepts/eti-attribution-statements|ETI-attributed]] explanation for the 24 Jun 1947 [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24|Kenneth Arnold]] "flying disc" sighting and the 18 Feb 1948 Norcatur fireball is a **single ETI mission with a return trip**. ^[extracted]

Markham's framing is rejected by La Paz as "fantastic" — but its inclusion in the SIGN incident summary file is itself notable: **the SIGN intake stream preserved civilian ETI-attribution letters as part of incident packets**, not just witness-statement intake forms. This is one of the **earliest preserved civilian-ETI-attribution letters retained inside the SIGN paperwork stack** in the corpus. ^[inferred]

## Open questions

- The Sammons close-encounter — was it ever evaluated as a separable incident from the meteor? The Inc 101 sheet **conflates** the two; the EXHIBIT-style attached statements suggest SIGN treated Sammons' funnel-shape account as supplementary detail of the same fireball event. ^[inferred]
- Did La Paz ever revise his "99% hoax / 1% real" estimate post-NM-wave? The 173–233 bundle's Inc 227 La Paz memo does not contain such a revision in its excerpts. ^[inferred]
- The Sammons account is similar in form to the [[references/sighting-hamel-mn-1948-08|Inc 162 Hamel MN]] case (small object, low altitude, close to observer, nearby animals reactive) — but Inc 162 has agent-corroborated physical trace, while Inc 101 Sammons has only witness statement + agent skepticism. The two events are 6 months apart with Inc 162 in the bundle's later half — **Project SIGN does not appear to have cross-correlated these two child/farmer-witness landing-or-near-landing accounts**. ^[inferred]
