## Pre-incident context

The mission departed at ~17:00 with the explicit purpose of conducting a **daytime aerial search for debris** from prior eyewitness reports of orbs/lights at `[COORDINATES]`. Earlier reporters had described **"thuds as if something has fallen and hit the ground"** — implying a recovery effort prior to this incident. ^[inferred]

The same day, the Office completed a successful test at `[SITE CODE NAME]` (nature of the test redacted). Whether the test is causally relevant to the encounter is not stated. ^[ambiguous]

At 17:51 hours, the helicopter spotted a **large cavern entrance** during a short orbit. This is recorded but not subsequently linked to any object observed.

## Key descriptors

- **Object morphology**: oval/spherical, orange, **white or yellow center**, light emission "in all directions."
- **Size class**: not numerically estimated by the witness; relative to the helicopter rotor disk in the close-approach event.
- **Behavior signatures**:
  - **Sequential flare-up / flare-down** in lines or stacks — a distinct morphological-temporal signature, distilled separately at [[concepts/orb-flare-formation]].
  - **Stationary while flaring** — orbs "appeared to be stationary" during the cycle, even as new ones lit below or beside.
  - **Pursuit/engagement** — at least once "the orbs broke off from `[CALL SIGN 1]` and pursue[d] the `[MILITARY AIRCRAFT]`"; multiple flare-ups occurred above the military aircraft track. See [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement]].
  - **High-speed splitting** — initial FLIR contact "broke into two objects"; "something emerge[d] from the two objects" under NVG. ^[ambiguous] (interpretation of what "split" means is left open)

## Witness's own caveats

The statement closes with three witness notes:

1. The pilots indicated they were **recording** sightings that occurred above the helicopter — outside the FLIR camera angle. Implies recordings exist on the NVG / helmet-cam channel; not attached. ^[inferred]
2. `[WITNESS 1]` could not see all sightings with the naked eye; some were called out by pilots on NVG.
3. `[WITNESS 1]` notes the orbs **broke off from the helicopter to pursue** the military aircraft.

## Why it matters

- **Multi-sensor multi-vantage corroboration**: FLIR (independent ground team) + NVG (two pilots) + naked eye (`[WITNESS 1]`) all on the same time-stamped events. This is a higher-bandwidth witness configuration than the 2026 [[references/western-us-event-2026|Western U.S. event]] in some respects (sensor channels), and lower in others (single primary witness statement, single facility, single night).
- **First wiki page documenting an apparent UAP-aircraft engagement** rather than passive hover — see [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement]].
- **First wiki page documenting the sequential flare-formation morphology** — see [[concepts/orb-flare-formation]].
- **Prior debris/"thuds" reports** at the same facility imply this is **not the first incident** at this location. ^[inferred]
