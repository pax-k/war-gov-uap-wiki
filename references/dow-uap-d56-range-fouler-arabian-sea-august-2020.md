**Witness narrative (verbatim):**

> *1.4a observed 3x possible unidentified small air contacts while conducting routine operations in the North Arabian Sea. Negative ES, radar track, and IFF track. Distance to contact was unknown. **Major narrative signals**:

1. **`North Arabian Sea`** — explicit body-text theater label. **Confirms the filename `arabian-sea` at the body-text level** at the regional level. The North Arabian Sea is the sea region between the Arabian Peninsula (Oman / Yemen / Pakistan coastlines) and the Indian subcontinent, distinct from the **Gulf of Aden** (d44's body theater, which is the western/southwestern margin of the same basin). ^[inferred] **First explicit North Arabian Sea body-text anchor in the dow-uap corpus.**

2. **`3x possible unidentified small air contacts`** — **first explicit "small" size descriptor + first explicit "air contacts" framing** in the range-fouler class at N=4. The `small` descriptor is consistent with the Round + Wings/Airframe morphology call and with the distance-unknown caveat. The `air contacts` framing (vs `surface contacts`) confirms the contacts were airborne, not on the water surface. ^[extracted]

3. **`Negative ES, radar track, and IFF track`** — **first explicit triple-negative-sensor-channel datum in the range-fouler class** ^[inferred]:
   - **ES** = **Electronic Support** (passive RF detection, e.g. radar warning, signal direction-finding) ^[inferred] — the helicopter's ES suite detected no RF emissions from the contacts. Distinct from `EA` (Electronic Attack, the form's pre-printed reception schema for jamming / arcing / false trackfiles).
   - **Radar track** = the AN/APS-153 surface-search radar produced no track on the contacts ^[inferred] — consistent with `Stable Trackfile = No`.
   - **IFF track** = **Identification Friend or Foe** transponder track ^[inferred] — the contacts did not squawk a recognizable IFF Mode 1/2/3/A/C/S code. Non-squawking contacts are the classic range-fouler precursor.

4. **`No interaction took place`** — **first explicit no-interaction attestation in the dow-uap corpus** ^[inferred]. Confirms passive non-engagement posture (no closure attempt, no intercept, no fire-control pipeline, no EW exchange).

5. **Lost-then-regained track + 2X additional contacts due east of initial location** — narrative structure parallels d38's `temporarily lose of the object but re-acquired shortly thereafter` ^[inferred] but with a more dramatic outcome: when contact is regained, **two additional contacts appear** (3X total). The 2X additional contacts appearing **due east** of the original contact's location implies a multi-element formation or a spread of contacts on similar headings. The narrative explicitly states `All 3x unknown air contacts appeared to maintain their relative course, speed, and altitude` — formation-flying behavior. ^[extracted]

6. **`relative course, speed, and altitude`** — formation-flying language. The contacts maintained a coordinated kinematic state across the encounter (all on westerly headings, all maintaining altitude, all moving at the same unknown speed). ^[inferred] This is **the first formation-flying attestation in the dow-uap range-fouler subset** and contrasts with d58's `one circling around the other` intra-group kinematic interaction.

7. **No range fouler vocabulary in narrative** — d56's narrative uses `air contacts`, `unknown air contacts`, `1x / 2x / 3x` only. Matches d44 + d38 vernacular pattern; contrasts d58. **Strengthens the institutional-form-vocabulary reading at N=3** (d56 + d44 + d38 narratives use generic language; only d58 uses `range fouler`). ^[inferred] 3-of-4 witness narratives at N=4 use the generic vernacular; only d58 picks up the form's headline term.
