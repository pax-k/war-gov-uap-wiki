# Dallas Anonymous Informant ETI Claims (FBI Dallas, 9 Oct 1967)

A **9 October 1967 walk-in interview** at the **FBI Dallas Field Office** by **a young white female who refused to give her name**. ^[extracted] The informant was interviewed by **SA Earl O. Cullum** ^[extracted] and made a series of specific extraterrestrial-contact claims, which Dallas SAC forwarded to FBI HQ as a Letterhead Memorandum dated **10 October 1967** with copy to **OSI Tinker AFB Oklahoma**. ^[extracted] HQ filed the LHM at **62-HQ-83894 serial 464**. ^[extracted]

This is the **first FBI-corpus walk-in claim of active ongoing telepathic ETI contact** with specific kinematic UAP claims attached ^[inferred] — and a structurally novel datapoint at the intersection of [[concepts/contactee-movement-1952-1954|contactee-movement narratives]], [[concepts/eti-attribution-statements|ETI attribution]], and the FBI's anonymous-informant tradition. ^[inferred]

## The informant's stated rationale for anonymity

> *"She stated she will not reveal her identity as she would feel like a fool if this information is not true. She stated if it is true, however, she will meet with interested officials and furnish all the information she has, provided nothing is done to endanger her safety."* ^[extracted]

The **conditional-disclosure** structure (anonymity until verified, then in-person willingness contingent on safety) is structurally distinctive within the FBI corpus ^[inferred] — most contactee-movement walk-ins (cf. AFSCA convention testimony, Frances Swan, George Adamski) provide their names openly. The anonymity protocol implicitly anticipates [[concepts/three-men-in-black|MIB silencing]] without explicitly invoking it. ^[inferred]

## The informant's contact narrative

> *"She stated in July, 1967, she met a being from another planet who had assumed earthly form. He gave her certain information, then he was picked up and departed from the earth on August 21, 1967. She stated she then received messages from non-earthly sources in a manner she refused to discuss."* ^[extracted]

Three structural features ^[inferred]:

1. **In-person contact in human form (Jul 1967)** — consistent with the [[concepts/contactee-movement-1952-1954|Adamski-tradition contactee model]] (extraterrestrial assumed Earthly form, met witness on Earth).
2. **Specific departure date (21 Aug 1967)** — unusually precise for a contactee narrative; the date coincides with **a UFO event the informant separately claims occurred over Antarctica that day** (see below), creating an internal narrative consistency. ^[inferred]
3. **Continued telepathic contact post-departure** — *"received messages from non-earthly sources in a manner she refused to discuss"* — structurally novel for the FBI corpus, the closest analog being [[entities/frances-swan|Frances Swan]]'s 1954 automatic-writing case (FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 serial 346, [[references/sighting-frances-swan-1954-07]]). ^[inferred]

## The four specific UAP claims

The informant reports four specific UAP events allegedly conveyed to her by the *"non-earthly sources"* ^[extracted]:

| # | Date | Event | Geographic anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **22 May 1962** | *"An antimissile missile was fired at a UFO over Africa, but the UFO was protected by its 'force field.'"* | Africa |
| 2 | **~6 Aug 1967** | *"A UFO was detected 22,000 miles from earth by radar."* | Earth orbit (geosynchronous altitude) |
| 3 | **20 Aug 1967** | *"A UFO was detected over Antarctica."* | Antarctica |
| 4 | **late Aug or early Oct 1967** (*"in the past week"*) | *"A UFO was detected over the 'Dewline' and was shot down, and beings from outer space are trying to recover it."* | DEW Line (North American Aerospace Defense Command early-warning radar line) |

The **22,000-mile detection range** (claim 2) is **geosynchronous-orbit altitude** (35,786 km ≈ 22,236 mi). ^[inferred — 1967 the 22,000-mile figure is recognizable as geosync) The **DEW Line shootdown + recovery operation** (claim 4) is the only claim in which the informant attributes *active extraterrestrial recovery operations* on Earth — structurally similar to later (post-1980s) crash-retrieval narratives. ^[inferred]

## What the FBI did

The Dallas SAC's disposition is minimal ^[extracted]:

- **No further information being taken.**
- **No investigation conducted.**
- **Distribution**: 5 copies to Bureau, 1 to Dallas, 1 to OSI Tinker AFB Oklahoma. ^[extracted]
- **HQ filing**: 62-HQ-83894 serial 464, with the LHM under the standard *"This document contains neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI"* boilerplate. ^[extracted]

The Bureau's [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default|standard hoax-default]] applies. The OSI cc means the LHM enters the AF channel as well. The informant is not interviewed further. ^[inferred]

## What is structurally novel

The Dallas Anonymous Informant LHM is the **first FBI-corpus retention of an extraterrestrial-recovery narrative tied to a specific date and geographic anchor** ^[inferred]:

- The May 1962 Africa antimissile claim cannot be cross-referenced to any documented antimissile test program. ^[inferred — the US ABM program at the time was Nike-Zeus testing at Kwajalein, not African theatre]
- The August 1967 Antarctica claim cannot be cross-referenced to any contemporaneous AF UFO file. ^[inferred]
- The DEW Line shootdown claim is the **earliest dated narrative in the FBI corpus of an active ETI-vehicle-recovery operation on Earth** ^[inferred] — predating the well-known Kecksburg PA Dec 1965 narrative chain and the 1980s crash-retrieval literature.

## Position in the corpus

The Dallas Anonymous Informant case sits at the intersection of three corpus threads ^[inferred]:

- **[[concepts/contactee-movement-1952-1954|Contactee movement]]** — the Adamski-tradition human-form ETI-meeting structure
- **[[concepts/psychic-channeling-uap]]** — telepathic post-departure contact (cf. [[entities/frances-swan|Swan]] 1954)
- **[[concepts/eti-attribution-statements|ETI attribution]]** — the explicit *"beings from outer space"* framing the informant uses

It is the **most kinematically detailed** anonymous-informant case in the FBI corpus ^[inferred] — most anonymous-informant filings in 62-HQ-83894 stop at *"saw a saucer"* without attaching specific recovery, shootdown, or geosynchronous-altitude claims.
