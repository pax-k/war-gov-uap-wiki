# Cabell AFOIC-CC-1 Directive — "Reporting of Information on Unconventional Aircraft" (8 Sep 1950)

A standing **HQ USAF Directorate of Intelligence directive**, document control number **AFOIC-CC-1**, dated **8 September 1950**, subject *"Reporting of Information on Unconventional Aircraft"*, signed **[[entities/c-p-cabell|C. P. Cabell]], Major General USAF, Director of Intelligence**, *"By Command of the Chief of Staff"*. ^[extracted] Addressed to *Commanding Generals, Major Air Commands, ZI and Overseas, [and] All United States Air Attaches*. ^[extracted] Filed in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-6|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 6]] at serial 249 as an **enclosure** to the AF's 12 Sep 1950 covering letter to Hoover (*"forwarded for your information and any cooperation you may be able to extend … desired to place this subject in its proper perspective as part of the normal USAF requirement for information of air technical intelligence interest"*). ^[extracted]

## Structural function — successor to AIRMEM #4

AFOIC-CC-1 is the **structural successor** to the 15 Feb 1949 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|Cabell AIRMEM #4]] ^[inferred]:

| Feature | AIRMEM #4 (15 Feb 1949) | **AFOIC-CC-1 (8 Sep 1950)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Author** | Maj Gen C. P. Cabell, Director of Intelligence, HQ USAF D/I | Maj Gen C. P. Cabell, Director of Intelligence, HQ USAF, "By Command of the Chief of Staff" |
| **Subject** | "Unconventional Aircraft" | "Reporting of Information on Unconventional Aircraft" |
| **Addressees** | Major Air Commands (ZI / overseas), US Air Attaches | Major Air Commands (ZI / overseas), US Air Attaches |
| **Routing destination** | CG Air Materiel Command, Attn: **MCIAXO-3** | CG Air Materiel Command, Attn: **MCIS** |
| **EEI schema** | 9-field | **9-field** (elaborated) |
| **Follow-up form** | AF Form 112 | **AF Form 112** |
| **Info-copies** | (not preserved in section-1) | Dir/Int G-2 Army; Dir/Naval Int; Commandant (INT) US Coast Guard; Sp Asst for Research & Intel, State; **Director FBI**; Director of [CIA, OCR-truncated] ^[ambiguous] |
| **FBI receipt** | 17 OCR-identical carbon copies (serial 164) | 1 copy enclosed with AF covering letter to Hoover (serial 249) |
| **Publicity-suppression clause** | (not on extant copy) | *"It is desired that no publicity be given this reporting or analysis activity."* ^[extracted] |

The two directives share: ^[inferred]

- **Same author and office** (Cabell, HQ USAF D/I).
- **Same subject scope** ("Unconventional Aircraft").
- **Same routing channel** (Major Air Commands + Air Attaches → AMC).
- **Same FBI-on-distribution** (Director FBI as info-copy recipient).

They differ in: ^[inferred]

- **Routing destination office** (MCIAXO-3 → MCIS) — possibly a reorganization at AMC between Feb 1949 and Sep 1950.
- **Publicity clause** — AFOIC-CC-1 explicitly suppresses publicity, AIRMEM #4 did not.
- **Issuance authority** — AFOIC-CC-1 is *"By Command of the Chief of Staff"* (USAF CoS Hoyt S. Vandenberg), AIRMEM #4 is a Directorate-level instrument.

The **AIRMEM #4 → AFOIC-CC-1 chain** is structurally parallel to the FBI's **BB-57 (1 Oct 1947) → SAC Letter #38 (25 Mar 1949) → SAC Letter #83 (29 Aug 1952)** cadence ^[inferred] — each agency revising its UAP passive-intake protocol at ~19-24 month intervals through the 1949-52 period. AFOIC-CC-1 represents the **third reporting-protocol revision** on the AF side in 22 months — following [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|AIRMEM #4]] (15 Feb 1949) and the residual Army DA Coll Memo #7 (21 Jan 1948) which AIRMEM #4 had superseded. ^[inferred]

## The 9-element EEI schema

AFOIC-CC-1 defines an **"unconventional aircraft"** as *"any aircraft or airborne object which by performance, aerodynamic characteristics, or unusual features, does not conform to any presently known aircraft type."* ^[extracted] The 9-element Essential Elements of Information (EEI) schema for reports ^[extracted]:

1. **Object description** — shape, size, color, number, formation if more than one, aerodynamic features, trail or exhaust, propulsion system, speed, sound, maneuvers, manner of disappearance, and other pertinent or unusual features.
2. **Time sighted** in 24-hour clock zonal time, and length of time observed.
3. **Manner of observation** — visual or electronic, from air (give speed, altitude, and type of aircraft), or surface. Any type of optical or electronic equipment used should be described.
4. **Location of observer** — exact latitude and longitude as closely as feasible, and/or reference to a known landmark. Location of object(s) with respect to observer, giving distance, direction, and altitude.
5. **Observer identification** — identifying information on observer(s) and witnesses, estimate of reliability and experience, and any factors bearing on estimated reliability of the sighting.
6. **Weather and wind conditions** (teletype sequences) at time and place of sightings.
7. **Explanatory activity / condition** — any activity or condition, meteorological or otherwise, which might account for the sighting.
8. **Physical evidence** — existence of any physical evidence; fragments, photographs and the like, of the sighting.
9. **Interception / identification action** — interception or identification action taken (such action should be taken whenever feasible, complying with existing air defense directives).

## Reporting workflow

The directive establishes a **two-stage reporting workflow** ^[extracted]:

1. **Electrical transmission** with priority *"appropriate in the judgment of the forwarding agency, according to its apparent authenticity and importance as intelligence"* — containing the 9-element EEI schema *"as far as possible"*.
2. **Written follow-up on AF Form 112** within **10 days** — containing the same information *"in greater detail"*, including *"sketches and signed attested narrative statements of observers"*.

Where no electrical transmission has been submitted, the **written report alone** follows the same AF Form 112 format. ^[extracted]

**Physical evidence** is to be forwarded by *"most expeditious means to Commanding General, Air Materiel Command, Attn: MCIS, under cover of a letter identifying the shipment with the report of sighting."* ^[extracted]

## Publicity-suppression clause

Paragraph 3 of AFOIC-CC-1 ^[extracted]:

> It is desired that no publicity be given this reporting or analysis activity.

This is the **first explicit publicity-suppression clause** in an AF UAP reporting directive in the wiki corpus ^[inferred]. AIRMEM #4 (Feb 1949) did not carry it; SAC Letter #38 (Mar 1949) did not carry it; [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|BB-57]] (Oct 1947) was an internal-FBI bulletin, not subject to publicity. The clause's institutional context: by September 1950, the AF was **simultaneously**:

- Operating [[concepts/project-twinkle|Project Twinkle]] at Vaughn NM (publicly unacknowledged, instrumented scientific study).
- Maintaining the [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|December 1949 GRUDGE-termination public posture]] ("after two years … three-fourths of the incidents … misidentifications").
- Issuing AFOIC-CC-1 internally to renew the reporting infrastructure for unconventional aircraft.

The publicity-suppression clause is **operationally consistent** with maintaining the public posture intact while the internal reporting infrastructure runs at full strength. ^[inferred]

## Info-copy distribution

The directive's info-copy distribution names 6 inter-agency recipients ^[extracted]:

- **Dir/Int, G-2, Army**
- **Dir/Naval Int**
- **Commandant (INT) US Coast Guard**
- **Sp Asst for Research & Intel, State**
- **Director FBI**
- **Director of [CIA, OCR-truncated]** ^[ambiguous]

The FBI Director-on-distribution is the **second documented AF-D/I-to-FBI inter-agency directive routing** in the wiki corpus ^[inferred] — confirming the **channel (e)** (inter-agency directive accumulation) introduced by [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|serial 164]] continues operationally in 1950. The 17-carbon-copy distribution pattern from AIRMEM #4 is **not** repeated for AFOIC-CC-1; section 6 holds **one copy** of the directive plus the AF covering letter. ^[inferred]

## Institutional consequence

AFOIC-CC-1 is the **structural confirmation** that the December 1949 [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|GRUDGE termination]] was **not** an exit from the unconventional-aircraft reporting business ^[inferred]:

- The AF rebuilt the protocol within **~9 months** of GRUDGE's termination (28 Mar 1950 Boggs-Hearn → 8 Sep 1950 AFOIC-CC-1).
- The directive issues *"By Command of the Chief of Staff"* — i.e. at the **CoS-Vandenberg level**, an elevation from the Directorate-level AIRMEM #4.
- The publicity-suppression clause confirms the **public-vs-internal posture split** is the operational frame.

The corpus's prior reading of GRUDGE termination as **AF exit from UAP investigation** is thus **partially refuted by AFOIC-CC-1** ^[inferred] — the more accurate framing is: GRUDGE-as-named-program ended, but the **doctrinal reporting infrastructure persisted**, recapitulated in a more elaborated form, signed at higher rank, and with explicit publicity-suppression. The FBI's parallel posture is unchanged — passive intake under BB-57 / SAC Letter #38 — with the **FBI Director on the info-copy distribution** preserving the FBI as a **recognized institutional touch-point** for AF UAP reporting doctrine.
