# USAF UFO Fact Sheet (McClanahan-Malmfeldt, 14 Jan 1977)

A **3-page USAF UFO Fact Sheet** transmitted on **14 January 1977** by **Lt Col H. A. McClanahan** (Chief, Civil Branch, Community Relations Division, Office of Information, **Department of the Air Force**, Washington DC 20330) to **Mr. Malmfeldt** (FBI Room 7825). ^[extracted] HQ filed both the cover letter and the Fact Sheet at **62-HQ-83894 serial 483** with attached **Total UFO Sightings 1947-1969** chart and **UFO Materials** list. ^[extracted]

The Fact Sheet is **the FBI corpus's canonical primary-source artifact of [[concepts/project-blue-book-termination-1969|Project Blue Book's 17 December 1969 termination]]** ^[inferred] — the file's only retained verbatim text of the termination announcement, the three Blue Book conclusions, and the post-closure NARA-transfer disposition.

## The cover letter (McClanahan → Malmfeldt)

> *"Federal Bureau of Investigation*
> *Attention: Mr. Malmfeldt, Room 7825*
> *Washington, D. C. 20535*

> *Dear Mr. Malmfeldt:*
> *This office recently received a public inquiry regarding UFOs, referred to us from the Bureau. As the inclosed fact sheet indicates, the Air Force's "Project Blue Book" investigation of UFOs was terminated on December 17, 1969, and all related documentation was turned over to the National Archives and Records Service.*
> *The inquirer referred to us has been apprised of these events. Hopefully, the inclosed fact sheet will be of help in responding to any future inquiries on this subject.*
> *Sincerely,*
> *H. A. McClANAHAN, Lt Col, USAF*
> *Chief, Civil Branch*
> *Community Relations Division*
> *Office of Information"* ^[extracted]

The **trigger event** is a citizen UFO inquiry that the FBI had referred to the AF — the AF then sends the Fact Sheet to FBI for re-use on **future** inquiries. The cover letter is therefore **a transmittal-for-distribution-precedent** rather than a one-time courtesy. ^[inferred]

## The Fact Sheet content (3 pages)

The Fact Sheet's three components ^[extracted]:

### Page 1 — Termination announcement

> *"On December 17, 1969 the Secretary of the Air Force announced the termination of Project Blue Book, the Air Force program for the investigation of UFOs."* ^[extracted]

### Page 1-2 — Decision basis

> *"The decision to discontinue UFO investigations was based on an evaluation of a report prepared by the University of Colorado entitled, 'Scientific Study of Unidentified Flying Objects;' a review of the University of Colorado's report by the National Academy of Sciences; past UFO studies; and Air Force experience investigating UFO reports during the past two decades."* ^[extracted]

### Page 2 — The three Blue Book conclusions

> *"As a result of these investigations and studies, and experience gained from investigating UFO reports since 1948, the conclusions of Project Blue Book are: (1) no UFO reported, investigated, and evaluated by the Air Force has ever given any indication of threat to our national security; (2) there has been no evidence submitted to or discovered by the Air Force that sightings categorized as 'unidentified' represent technological developments or principles beyond the range of present day scientific knowledge; and (3) there has been no evidence indicating that sightings categorized as 'unidentified' are extraterrestrial vehicles."* ^[extracted]

### Page 2 — Post-closure disposition

> *"With the termination of Project Blue Book, the Air Force regulation establishing and controlling the program for investigating and analyzing UFOs was rescinded. All documentation regarding the former Blue Book investigation has been permanently transferred to the Modern Military Branch, National Archives and Records Service, 8th and Pennsylvania Avenue, Washington, D.C. 20408, and is available for public review and analysis."* ^[extracted]

### Page 2 — No-resumption justification

> *"Since the termination of Project Blue Book, no evidence has been presented to indicate that further investigation of UFOs by the Air Force is warranted. In view of the considerable Air Force commitment of resources in the past, and the extreme pressure on Air Force funds at this time, there is no likelihood of renewed Air Force involvement in this area."* ^[extracted]

### Page 2 — Civilian-research overlay

> *"There are a number of universities and professional scientific organizations such as the American Association for the Advancement of Science, which have considered UFO phenomena during periodic meetings and seminars. In addition, a list of private organizations interested in aerial phenomena may be found in Gale's Encyclopedia of Associations (Edition 8, Vol I, pp. 432-3). Such timely review of the situation by private groups insures that sound evidence will not be overlooked by the scientific community."* ^[extracted]

## Attachment 1 — Total UFO Sightings 1947-1969

The chart (filed as `tbl-3.md` in the OCR but not preserved in the JSON output) summarizes sightings totals year-by-year for 1947-1969. ^[extracted] The OCR reference suggests **22 rows + total** with columns for year, total sightings, and unidentified count. The Fact Sheet's textual reference to *"Air Force experience investigating UFO reports during the past two decades"* implies the chart shows ~12,000+ total sightings with ~700 unidentified (consistent with the well-documented Blue Book final figures, 12,618 sightings / 701 unidentified). ^[inferred]

## Attachment 2 — UFO Materials (Page 3)

The materials-list attachment provides ordering information for **the actual primary documents** ^[extracted]:

> *"Scientific Study of Unidentified Flying Objects. Study conducted by the University of Colorado under contract F44620-76-C-0035 [sic — likely F44620-67-C-0035 with OCR-substituted-digit]. Three volumes, 1,465 p. 68 plates. Photoduplicated hard copies of the official report may be ordered for $6 per volume, $18 the set of three, as AD 680:975, AD 680:976, and AD 680:977, from the National Technical Information Service, U.S. Department of Commerce, Springfield, VA 22151."*

> *"Review of University of Colorado Report on Unidentified Flying Objects. Review of report by a panel of the National Academy of Sciences. National Academy of Sciences, 1969, 6p. Photoduplicated hard copies may be ordered for $3 as AD 688:541 from the National Technical Information Service, U.S. Department of Commerce, Springfield, VA 22151."* ^[extracted]

The contract number (F44620-67-C-0035, after OCR correction) and the AD accession numbers identify the **canonical Condon Report citation form**. ^[inferred]

## What the Fact Sheet does NOT contain

Three structurally significant omissions ^[inferred]:

1. **No mention of the [[concepts/robertson-panel-runup-1952|Robertson Panel of 1953]]** — the AF's prior CIA-influenced UFO review. ^[extracted]
2. **No mention of the [[concepts/sputnik-saucer-reactivation-1957|Sputnik-era reactivation]] of 1957** or the **Levelland TX engine-stall wave** — events that the FBI corpus's [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-9|section 9]] documents extensively. ^[inferred]
3. **No mention of any specific UAP case** — the Fact Sheet operates at full institutional-aggregate level, never naming an individual sighting, witness, or case. ^[inferred]

The omission of specifics is a **deliberate institutional-aggregate framing** ^[inferred] — the Fact Sheet's purpose is to terminate further inquiry by directing the inquirer to NARA / NTIS for raw data and to the AAAS / Gale's Encyclopedia for civilian channels.

## The 24 January 1977 FBI reply (Moore → McClanahan, serial 484)

The FBI's reply, dated **24 January 1977**, signed by **Donald W. Moore Jr.** (Asst Director, External Affairs Division) ^[extracted]:

> *"Dear Colonel McClanahan:*
> *Mr. Malmfeldt has brought your letter of January 14th, with enclosure, to my attention. I join him in thanking you for sending us a copy of your UFO Fact Sheet and for apprising us of the termination of your project."* ^[extracted]

The phrase *"apprising us of the termination of your project"* — written in 1977 about a December 1969 event — is structurally significant ^[inferred] (see the [[concepts/project-blue-book-termination-1969|termination concept page]] for full analysis).

## Position in the corpus

The Fact Sheet is the **only FBI-corpus retention of the verbatim 1969 Blue Book termination text** ^[inferred]. Earlier FBI corpus references to Blue Book closure are summary-level only. The Fact Sheet:

- Anchors [[concepts/project-blue-book-termination-1969]] (the termination concept page).
- Provides the AF-side institutional-narrative for the [[concepts/condon-committee|Condon Committee]] (and is the FBI corpus's only reference to the Condon Report's contract number F44620-67-C-0035).
- Pre-figures (by 5 months) the [[references/fbi-cochran-schneider-1977|Carter White House coordination question]] — the Fact Sheet's "no successor" statement is exactly the gap Powell asks about.
- Establishes the **transfer-to-NARA disposition** which the FBI corpus inherits when later FOIA requests on Blue Book material are routed.
