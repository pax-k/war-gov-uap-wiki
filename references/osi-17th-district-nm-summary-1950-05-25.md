# 17th District OSI — Summary of Observations of Aerial Phenomena in the New Mexico Area, December 1948 - May 1950

A multi-section report dated **25 May 1950**, file number **(24-8)-28**, signed via the **17th District Office of Special Investigations (IG) USAF, Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico**, addressed to **Brigadier General Joseph F. Carroll, Director of Special Investigations, Headquarters USAF, Washington 25, D. C.** ^[extracted] Filed in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-6|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 6]] as the **enclosure stack** supporting the [[references/belmont-ladd-aerial-phenomena-nm-1950-08-21|21 August 1950 Belmont → Ladd memo]] (serial 246) ^[extracted]. The summary is approximately **38 OCR pages** (pages 23-70 of the section's 271 OCR pages, of which pages 33-70 are the tabular catalog itself), held under CONFIDENTIAL / *Declassified Authority NND 90986*. ^[extracted]

## Cover narrative

The summary opens with a 5-paragraph framing narrative ^[extracted]:

1. **Inception of OSI 17th District's NM aerial-phenomena reporting**: *"In a liaison meeting with other military and government intelligence and investigative agencies in December 1948, it was determined that the frequency of unexplained aerial phenomena in the New Mexico area was such that an organized plan of reporting these observations should be undertaken. The organization and physical location of units of this District were most suitable for collecting these data, therefore, since December 1948, this District has assumed the responsibility for collecting and reporting basic information with respect to aerial phenomena occurring in this general area."* ^[extracted]
2. **The compilation**: not a complete record, but *"only those in which sufficient information was available to justify their inclusion. The observers of these phenomena include scientists, Special Agents [redacted]"* ^[extracted].
3. **LaPaz's role**: *"Director of the Institute of Meteoritics and Head of the Department of Mathematics and Astronomy at the University of New Mexico. He was Research Mathematician at the New Mexico Proving Grounds under an OSRD appointment in 1943 and 1944, and Technical Director of the Operations Analysis Section, Headquarters, Second Air Force, 1944-45. Since 1948, Dr. LaPaz has served on a voluntary basis as consultant for this District in connection with the green fireball investigations."* ^[extracted]
4. **The two 1949 Los Alamos conferences** (17 February + 14 October 1949): *"Representatives of the following organizations were present at these meetings: Fourth Army, Armed Forces Special Weapons Project, University of New Mexico, Federal Bureau of Investigation, U. S. Atomic Energy Commission, University of California, U. S. Air Force Scientific Advisory Board, Geophysical Research Division Air Materiel Command USAF, and the Office of Special Investigations (IG) USAF. A logical explanation was not proffered with respect to the origin of the green fireballs. It was, however, generally concluded that the phenomena existed and that they should be studied scientifically until these occurrences have been satisfactorily explained. Further, that the continued occurrence of unexplained phenomena of this nature in the vicinity of sensitive installations is cause for concern."* ^[extracted]
5. **The Land-Air contract**: *"The Geophysical Research Division, Air Materiel Command, Cambridge, Massachusetts, has recently let a contract to Land-Air, Inc., Holloman AFB, Alamogordo, New Mexico, for a limited scientific study of greenness fireballs"* — establishing the **AMC Geophysical Research Division (Cambridge) → Land-Air Inc. (Alamogordo)** contract architecture for [[concepts/project-twinkle|Project Twinkle]]. ^[extracted]

The narrative confirms ^[inferred]:

- **OSI 17th District is the operational owner** of the NM green-fireball reporting program from December 1948 onward.
- **Two 1949 inter-agency conferences** (Feb + Oct 1949) included **the FBI as a representative-attended organization** — the FBI is institutionally networked into the green-fireball investigation at the conference tier, not just at the BB-57 / SAC-Letter-#38 passive-intake tier.
- **AEC + AFSWP + Fourth Army + UNM + UC + AF Scientific Advisory Board + AMC Geophysical Research Division + OSI** are the standing-cast institutional matrix for the green-fireball portfolio in NM.

**The FBI receives 2 of 16 copies** (El Paso + Albuquerque field offices) ^[inferred] — confirming the FBI is on the **summary's standing-distribution list** as a recognized institutional touch-point. This is a **second documented OSI-to-FBI standing-distribution channel** in the wiki corpus ^[inferred] — alongside the [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|AIRMEM #4]] / [[references/cabell-afoic-cc-1-unconventional-aircraft-1950-09-08|AFOIC-CC-1]] HQ-to-Director routing. The FBI's path into section 6's section-opener (the 21 Aug 1950 Belmont memo) thus runs through the **Albuquerque field office's receipt of the El Paso distribution copy** ^[inferred].

## Contents

The summary's body is a **tabular catalog** spanning OCR pages 33-70 of the section ^[extracted] — approximately **38 tables (tbl-0.md through tbl-37.md)** referenced by the OCR but not rendered as text content. ^[ambiguous] The catalog covers:

- **Reliability of Observers** column: VR (Very Reliable), R (Reliable), Unk (Unknown Reliability). ^[extracted]
- **Evaluation** column: (1) "Green Fireball Phenomena", (2) "Disk" or Variation, (3) Probable Meteor. ^[extracted]
- **Sighting No. 175** explicitly references the **24-25 Feb 1950 Cpl Lertis E. Stanfield Datil NM photograph** + LaPaz astrometric analysis. ^[extracted]

The catalog runs the full December 1948 - May 1950 chronology of OSI-collected NM-area aerial-phenomena reports. The **terminal sighting count of ~175** ^[inferred] matches Belmont's *"approximately 150 observations"* count (21 Aug 1950) modulo the ~3 months of additional accumulation between 25 May 1950 (summary cutoff) and 10 August 1950 (Belmont's last-prior FBI-Albuquerque update reference). ^[inferred]

## LaPaz Seventh Report attachment

The summary is bound with **LaPaz's 23 May 1950 [[references/lapaz-seventh-report-1950-05-23|Seventh Report]]** ^[extracted] — the **5-page analytical addendum** that supplies the cumulative-incompatibility scientific framework distinguishing the green fireballs from typical meteors (11-criteria taxonomy) and articulating the **Urals-missile hypothesis**. The Seventh Report is filed as part of the same 25 May 1950 enclosure stack, with LaPaz signing as Director of the Institute of Meteoritics.

## Institutional significance

The summary is the **first formal multi-agency consolidated catalog of NM-area aerial phenomena** in the wiki corpus ^[inferred] — and the **first FBI-corpus retention of a primary OSI tabular catalog with declared methodology** (reliability ratings, evaluation categories). It establishes:

- **Provenance of Twinkle**: the Land-Air contract is sponsored by AMC Geophysical Research Division (Cambridge MA), structurally distinct from AMC Wright-Patterson T-2 (the [[concepts/project-grudge|GRUDGE]] home). ^[extracted]
- **Provenance of the FBI's section-6 engagement**: the FBI is an institutional recipient at both the field-office tier (El Paso + Albuquerque) and via separate HQ-to-Director routing.
- **The continuity of investigation**: the December 1948 wave's investigation **did not pause** with [[concepts/project-grudge|GRUDGE]]'s December 1949 termination — OSI 17th District continued data collection through May 1950 and beyond.
- **Inter-agency conference history**: the 17 Feb 1949 + 14 Oct 1949 Los Alamos conferences are documented as **standing inter-agency engagements** on the green-fireball portfolio. The 14 Oct 1949 conference is a **previously-undocumented NM-area inter-agency event** in the wiki corpus ^[inferred].
- **LaPaz's institutional role**: serving voluntarily as 17th District consultant from 1948 onward; previously OSRD-appointed at NM Proving Grounds (1943-44) and Technical Director Operations Analysis Section HQ Second Air Force (1944-45) — i.e. a **WWII-era institutionally-credentialed analyst** who held the green-fireball portfolio for the AF on $0/day. ^[extracted]
