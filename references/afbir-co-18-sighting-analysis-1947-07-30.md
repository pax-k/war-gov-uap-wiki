# AFBIR-CO 18-Sighting Analysis (30 July 1947)

A **6-page tabular analytical document** filed at FBI HQ as the titular content of [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-130|62-HQ-83894 serial 130]], dated **30 July 1947** at AAF HQ Washington. ^[extracted] The document opens: *"For purposes of analysis by **AFBIR-CO**, eighteen reported sightings of 'Flying Discs' were selected for breakdown into detailed particulars. Each report was assigned a number and each number appears in the left-hand column of the data on the following pages."* ^[extracted]

This is the **earliest formal AAF statistical-analytical exercise on the 1947 flying-disc wave** in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] It **predates the famous 23 Sep 1947 Twining "AMC Opinion" memo by ~8 weeks**, predates [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN's]] Jan 1948 stand-up by ~5 months, and predates the [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element Cabell directive]] by ~18 months. ^[inferred]

## What "AFBIR-CO" stands for

**AFBIR-CO** is the Pentagon-level cable address for **AAF Brigadier-Air-Force-level Intelligence Requirements / Collection Branch** at AC/AS-2. ^[inferred] The "AFBIR" prefix is the AAF Field-Brigade-Intelligence-Requirements identifier; the "CO" suffix denotes the Collection Branch. ^[inferred] The same identifier appears on the **Browning sworn statement** ("Received AFBIR-CO 16 July 47") in the same serial-130 packet ^[extracted] — confirming AFBIR-CO is the **organizational receiver** for AAF disc-witness statements arriving at HQ. The branch sits **operationally under** [[entities/g-d-garrett|Lt Col G. D. Garrett]], who is cited explicitly on serial-130's NRL-Mellen memos as the AAF A-2 staff officer requesting NRL interviews. ^[extracted]

The name does not appear elsewhere in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] By the time of [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|Cabell's 15 Feb 1949 AIRMEM #4]], the disc-collection function has been **rebranded under** the post-1947 USAF D/I (Cabell line). ^[inferred] AFBIR-CO is the **pre-USAF (AAF-era) institutional ancestor** of the Cabell channel.

## The 21-field breakdown

Each report's analysis covered: ^[extracted]

> Date / Hour (Local Standard Time) / Location / Observer's Name / Observer's Occupation / Observed from Ground or Air / Number of Objects Sighted / Altitude / Direction of Flight / Speed / Distance Covered / Length of Time in Sight / Deviation from Straight Flight / Color / Size / Shape / Sound / Trail / Weather / Manner of Disappearance / Remarks

**Compare to the [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|Cabell 10-element directive]] of Feb 1949**: AFBIR-CO's 21-field schema is a **superset** of the Cabell 10-element schema — i.e. AFBIR-CO retains observational fields the Cabell directive **drops** (e.g. Manner of Disappearance) and **expands** witness-occupation tracking. ^[inferred] The 1947 → 1949 instrument-evolution is therefore **one of consolidation toward a leaner standardized form** rather than expansion. ^[inferred]

## Selected morphological data extracted

The 21-field breakdown captures observational data that, aggregated, yields recurring signatures. From AFBIR-CO Tables 4-6 (the cross-witness fields): ^[extracted]

### Color
- "Silver" / "very bright silver" / "silvery" — Reports 1, 3, 9, 13, 15 (5 of 16)
- "Brilliance slightly greater than a star" — Report 5 (Maxwell Field at night)
- "Solar specular reflection" — Report 6 (NRL White Sands)
- "Reflection from sun" — Report 10 (Burniston)
- "Phosphorous color" — Report 14 (Newfoundland Constabulary)
- "Resembled a grayish balloon" — Report 16 (Elmendorf)

### Shape
- "Round, disc-shaped" — Reports 9 (Browning), CAP-references "saucer" (#11, #12)
- "Flat object … without vertical fin or any visible wings, with apparent depth of a P-51" — Report 13 (Moorman)
- "Flat on base with the top slightly rough in contour" — Report 8 (Smith UA)
- "Egg-shaped, or like barrel head" — Report 14 (Newfoundland)
- "Circular like wheel" — Report 15 (Harmon Field)
- "No definite shape could be determined" — Reports 1, 5, 10, 16

### Size
- "30-50' in diameter" — Report 9 (Browning B-25)
- "Comparable to a C-54 at 10,000'" — Reports 10 (Burniston), 15 (Harmon)
- "Apparent depth of a P-51" — Report 13 (Moorman)
- "Approx. 10' in diameter" — Report 16 (Elmendorf)
- "Apparently small" — Report 1 (Pikes Peak)

### Sound + Trail
- **All 16 reports**: **No sound** (where data analyzed). ^[extracted]
- Trail observed: only **2 reports** (#6 Zohn "Possible vapor trails", #15 Harmon Field "Bluish black trail approx. 15 mi. long"). ^[extracted]
- **The "no sound + no trail" signature dominates the analytical sample**. ^[inferred]

### Weather
- **CAVU**: Reports 1, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14 (7 of 16) — observation skew strongly toward clear-air observation. ^[extracted]
- "Clear moonlight" (#5 Maxwell night), "Sunny" (#10), "Clear with scattered cumulus 8-10000'" (#15).

### Manner of disappearance
- "Climbed very fast and out of sight" — Report 1
- "Obscured by a cloud bank" — Report 3
- "Lost in brilliancy of the moon" — Report 5
- "Cannot explain, except that reflection angle may have changed abruptly" — Report 6
- "Don't know whether they put on a tremendous burst of speed, or disintegrated" — Report 8 (Smith UA)
- "Unexplained" — Reports 9, 11, 12, 14, 15
- "Disappeared at an angle of about 30° above earth's surface" — Report 10
- "Pilot (at 300 MPH) attempted to keep object in sight, but unable to do so" — Report 13 (Moorman)

The dominant disappearance signature is **"unexplained"** (5 of 16) followed by **rapid acceleration / climb-out** (3 of 16). ^[inferred] No reports describe the object **landing** (consistent with AFBIR-CO's selection bias toward in-flight-witnessing). ^[inferred]

## Why this analysis matters institutionally

### Earliest formal AAF analytical instrument on the 1947 wave

AFBIR-CO's 30 Jul 1947 analysis is the **first known AAF document that processes UAP observations as a statistical population** rather than as individual case files. ^[inferred] The structurally-equivalent AAF-side documents that postdate it: ^[inferred]

1. **5 Sep 1947 [[entities/george-f-schulgen|Schulgen]] formal denial letter to Hoover** ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2 serial 84]]) — concludes "Army Air Forces has no project with characteristics similar to those associated with Flying Discs."
2. **23 Sep 1947 Twining AMC "Opinion" memo** — concludes "real and not visionary or fictitious".
3. **Jan 1948 [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] formal stand-up** — successor analytical apparatus.
4. **15 Feb 1949 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|Cabell AIRMEM #4]]** — supersedes residual Army instruments and re-codifies the AAF/USAF intake schema.

AFBIR-CO's 18-sighting analysis is the **upstream analytical priors** that fed into 1-3 above. ^[inferred] The Schulgen denial, the Twining "real-not-visionary" determination, and the Project SIGN stand-up are all **downstream consequences** of AFBIR-CO's having concluded by 30 Jul 1947 that **18 selected reports yielded no clearly conventional explanation**. ^[inferred]

### Synchronicity with Bureau Bulletin No. 42

AFBIR-CO's analysis is **dated 30 July 1947** — the **same day as [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]]** (also 30 Jul 1947, FBI HQ → all SACs). ^[extracted] **The two instruments are synchronous parts of the AAF + FBI cooperation regime**: ^[inferred]

- AFBIR-CO collects + statistically analyzes **upstream** sightings (incoming from across the AAF + civilian + foreign-liaison channels);
- BB42 organizes the **downstream** FBI field-office investigation into witness sincerity.

The two operate as a **closed analytical loop**: BB42 feeds witness-credibility verdicts into AAF intelligence; AFBIR-CO feeds analytical priors into BB42 instructions. ^[inferred] The synchronous date (30 Jul 1947) suggests the two were **deliberately co-published** by Garrett-Reynolds-Ladd-Schulgen at the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|cooperation regime's]] founding.

### Selection-bias that pre-loads the SIGN intake

AFBIR-CO's **deliberate selection of 18 sightings** for analysis (out of presumably hundreds of incoming reports by 30 Jul 1947) means the analytical priors are **pre-curated for credibility**. ^[inferred] This is **not a random sample**:

- 5 of 16 clusters are AAF officers (highest-credibility class);
- 5 of 16 clusters are pilots;
- Hoax cases and fragment-recovery cases are **excluded entirely**;
- All 18 reports are **observation-based** (no physical evidence).

The selection bias **amplifies the "real-but-unidentified" hypothesis** — i.e. by curating only the most credible witness reports, AFBIR-CO's analytical output is **structurally pre-disposed** to conclude (as the page-35 Newfoundland-tranche analytical wrap-up does, in the same packet): "*Something is really flying around. … 1. The surface of these objects is metallic, indicating a metallic skin, at least.*" ^[extracted] This **anticipates Twining by 8 weeks** and is a **paradigm-defining finding**. ^[inferred] The selection-bias mechanism partly explains how AFBIR-CO arrives at materially-the-same conclusion as Twining despite drawing on a much smaller analytical sample.
