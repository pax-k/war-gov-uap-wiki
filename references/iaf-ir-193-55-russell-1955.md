# USAIRA Prague IR 193-55 — Russell Trans-Caucasus Flying Disc Report (Oct 1955)

A 9-page Mistral-OCR'd declassified **SECRET (NOFORN)** US Air Force **Air Intelligence Information Report** filed on **14 October 1955** by **[[entities/thomas-s-ryan|Lt Col Thomas S. Ryan]]**, US Air Attaché ([[entities/usaira-prague|USAIRA Prague]]), Czechoslovakia. Report number **IR 193-55**, on US AF Form 112 / 112a, **TS Control # 3-2500** (with continuation `T.S. Cont. # 3-2500` on cover), routing dual-classified to **HQ USAF, USAFE, and USAIRA Moscow**. Declassification authority **NND 857013**, BT NARA, **11-12-96**. Document IDs **NW 38537** / **DocID 5916784**.

The artifact is the **debriefing record** of a 13 October 1955 meeting in [[entities/usaira-prague|USAIRA Prague]]'s and US Army Attaché Col. Thomas Dooley's offices, at which **[[entities/e-u-hathaway|Lt Col E. U. Hathaway]]** (US Army, Senate Armed Services Committee staff) and **[[entities/ruben-efron|Mr. Ruben Efron]]** (Committee Consultant) related to USAIRA the multi-segment USSR-internal trip just completed by their party — which also included **[[entities/richard-russell|Senator Richard B. Russell]]** (Chairman, Senate Armed Services Committee). Senator Russell himself was unavailable for the formal debrief (engaged with Chief of Mission Harold Vedler) but is named as the **first witness** of the central sighting.

The bundle subsumes a previously dispatched **USAIRA Cable** (TOP SECRET, ref **C-103**, dated 13 Oct 1955) — the cable text is quoted verbatim on page 2 of the report.

## What the bundle contains

The body of the report is structured into named **Parts A–F** plus the verbatim cable text and a sketch page:

### Cover narrative (page 1)

USAIRA's summary: three highly reliable US observers travelling by rail saw two flying discs ascend almost vertically and depart north at high speed at **1910 hours, 4 October 1955**, in the Trans-Caucasus region of the USSR between **ATJATY** and **ADZHLJABUL** (Azerbaijan SSR). Ryan's USAIRA comment closes:

> "The significance of this report re the USAF project 'Unidentified Flying Objects' is remarkable and lends credence to many 'saucer' reports."

This is a **direct USAIRA institutional endorsement** of the witnesses' interpretation — distinct from the typical 1948–49 AFOIR-CO-track posture of *recording without endorsing*. ^[inferred]

### USAIRA cable text C-103, 13 Oct 1955 (page 2)

The TOP SECRET cable to **DINTA, WASH. DC.** (USAF D/I), routed `OPERATIONAL IMMEDIATE` and crypto-passed to **CINCUSAFE** and **USAIRA Moscow**. Reports the sighting as the lead, then summarizes:

- **Dnepropetrovsk Airfield, 9 Oct 55** — 42 stubby jet fighters (not Fagots) + one multi-jet bomber with novel features (canvas-covered buried-wing engines, swayback fuselage top, tailcone silver bulbous bulge).
- **Baku** — long flatcar train loaded with platoon-size landing craft.
- **Moscow-Minsk highway, 42 km marker** — radar site with two radars, tentatively `Woodgage`/`Gage` + square-pole `Freya`.
- Recommends **"complete debriefing of Russell group upon return and... commendation from D/I."**

Signed `T.S. RYAN`.

### Part A — Sighting debrief (pages 2–4)

The 0900–1200 13 Oct interview at the Army Attaché's office. **Hathaway did most of the explaining; Efron supplemented with his contemporaneous notes**. The 18 **raw notes** (numbered 1–18) cover ascent profile, rotation behavior, light positions, take-off-area searchlights, sparking/flame at base, Soviet trainmen's reaction, and witness-positioning details. ^[extracted] These are the wiki's primary input for the [[references/sighting-trans-caucasus-1955-10-04|sighting page]].

Ryan inserted **bracketed clarifications** in italics where the raw quote was ambiguous — e.g., the ascent angle was clarified as "a slight arc just off the vertical" rather than "straight up", and the rotation was clarified as "clockwise / to the right" with a slow rate during ascent vs. a "whirring" cruise.

### Part B — Observations at Baku (pages 4–5)

Long railcar train of platoon-sized landing craft outside Baku; one high-altitude jet (unidentifiable); civilian airfield ~1 hour from city; Intourist guide stated the airfield they passed was a **military training airfield**, with green transports parked.

### Part C — Observations at Dnepropetrovsk Airfield (page 5)

**42 stubby jet fighters**, smaller than [[concepts/horten-flying-wing|conventional]] reference aircraft. Hathaway compared against a **Fagot model** in USAIRA Prague's office and rejected the Fagot identification due to fuselage stubbiness; Ryan flagged **SRI No. TIC 6244** as the candidate composite-fighter configuration (one-third smaller than MIG-15). One **multi-jet bomber** with morphology drawn on page 8: cockpit set forward, canvas-covered wing-buried engines, severe sweep to mid-wing, swayback fuselage top, silver bulbous bulge at tailcone aft of rudder line. ^[extracted]

### Part D — Observations near Moscow (page 5)

42-km marker on the Moscow-Minsk highway, 200–250 yards south of road, **radar site with two radars** — sketches identified by Ryan as one Woodgage/Gage and one square-type Freya. ^[extracted]

### Part E — Observations Tiflis to Sochi (pages 5–6)

- Tiflis (~5 Oct 55): **banana-shaped helicopter with two main rotors at each end**, more windows than analogous US type. ^[extracted]
- Tiflis → Sochi rail leg: chance encounter with **`EGOROV`**, ~55-year-old Russian who claimed to be one of the original pilots of the **trans-polar Russian flight to Washington State (1935 or 1936)**, accompanied by a younger engineer "whose job was to keep him supplied with spirits." Ryan's USAIRA comment: *"It is difficult for me to believe that this experienced pilot in polar navigation would be retired and doing nothing but travelling around the Trans Caucasus region with a young engineer."* ^[extracted] Russell extended an invitation for Egorov to visit his US Senate office. **17 German aircraft** WWII shootdown claim. ^[extracted]
- Outside Sochi: **T-34 tank with muzzle brake on flatcar**, "looked very different" from the Korea-tour comparison set. ^[extracted]

### Part F — Observations at COP and Czech railroads (pages 6–7)

The party crossed the **COP** (Chop) USSR/Czechoslovakia rail border on 12 Oct 55 at ~0630, where Hathaway observed the **gauge-switching operation** — Russian broad gauge → Czech standard gauge — using a 2-axle truck swap inside a long border building (40–45 minutes per car). Ryan included a sketch on page 9 (also reproduced in [[concepts/usaira-reporting-channel|USAIRA reporting channel]]'s methodology — sketches consume a measurable fraction of every USAIRA report). The party then proceeded **COP → KOSICE → POPRAD → ZILINA → Prague** by train; Hathaway noted prefabricated Czech rail-electrification construction, a long fenced/guarded area at dusk (concentration-camp / labor-camp pattern unstated), and large RR marshaling yards with manned guard towers. ^[extracted] Off-topic to UAP, but corroborates Hathaway's competence as a structured ground observer (relevant to the sighting's witness-credibility floor). ^[inferred]

### Closing USAIRA comment (page 7)

> "Due to the time available for debriefing and the physical limitations of our office in Prague, no more can be reported from these excellent sources."

The interview ended at noon 13 Oct because the Russell group was departing Prague aboard **Swissair for Rome at 1300+ hours** the same day.

## What the report does and does not preserve

**Preserved:**

- A **primary contemporaneous record** of three named US witnesses' independent multi-light, multi-vantage sighting deep inside the USSR. ^[extracted]
- The **USAIRA channel's** institutional posture toward the report: explicit endorsement (*"remarkable... lends credence"*) plus a recommendation for D/I commendation of the witnesses. ^[extracted]
- A **full collateral observation set** — Soviet airfield order-of-battle, radar sites, tank type, RR gauge-switching, Egorov contact — none of which is UAP-adjacent but all of which supports the **witness-quality argument** for the disc account. ^[inferred]
- A **B-2 evaluation** on the reporting subsource (Hathaway). On the AF Form 112 evaluation rubric, **B = "usually reliable"** and **2 = "probably true"** — a high but not maximal rating. ^[inferred]

**Not preserved:**

- The **actual cable C-103 routing record** at HQ USAF DINTA — only the body text is in the bundle.
- Any **D/I reply** acknowledging or actioning Ryan's commendation recommendation. ^[open]
- Any **subsequent debriefing** of the witnesses on US return (Ryan recommended *"complete debriefing"*). ^[open]
- The **Senator Russell first-person account** — Russell did not attend the 13 Oct debrief; everything attributed to Russell in the report is via Hathaway and Efron. ^[ambiguous]
- The **TS-track parent file** — `T.S. Cont. # 3-2500` implies TS-track entries `3-2500 ± N`. ^[inferred]
- Any **USAIRA Moscow follow-on** record from the cable's `INFO` distribution. ^[open]
- The cable's **HQ USAF redistribution slip** (analogous to the 8 Nov 1948 AFOIR-CO disposition slip on [[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11|USAFE TT 1524]]). ^[open]

## Significance

- **First 1950s-era artifact in the corpus.** The wiki previously had a 1944–45 / 1948–50 / 1957–58 / 1963 / 1985 / 1994 / 2023 / 2025–26 chronology. This document closes the **1950–56 gap**. ^[inferred]
- **First USAIRA-originated UAP-adjacent record in the corpus**. The 1948 [[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11|USAFE TT 1524]] dispatch was a **major-command** TS-track artifact; this 1955 IR 193-55 is an **air-attaché HUMINT** SECRET-track artifact, structurally distinct. ^[inferred]
- **First UAP sighting witnessed by Americans physically inside the USSR**. ^[inferred] Every other sighting in the corpus is in the US, allied territory, contested airspace (PNG, Kazakhstan), or US-base-overseas — never inside the USSR proper.
- **First Senatorial-class witness** in the corpus. Russell was Chairman of the Senate Armed Services Committee at sighting; the political weight of the report's witness party is unmatched elsewhere in the corpus. ^[inferred]
- **First USAF institutional posture endorsing a witness sighting** in the corpus. AFOIR-CO-track 1948–49 reports adopt a recording-without-endorsing posture; USAIRA-track 1955 here uses *"remarkable... lends credence"*. The endorsement comes from the air-attaché HUMINT side, not the operational-investigation side. ^[inferred]
- **First "non-US-military hostile-territory operational suppression behavior"** datum: Soviet trainmen lowered curtains and refused window-access after the sighting. See [[concepts/observer-suppression-behavior]]. ^[extracted]
- **Confirms ascending-disc / rotating-outer-surface morphology** as a tracked behavior in 1955, three years before the 1958 Detroit phone-in cluster. The Trans-Caucasus discs match the 1948 [[references/sighting-fairfield-suisun-1948-12|Fairfield-Suisun]] vertical-ascent profile and elements of the [[references/sighting-mountain-home-id-1949-07|Mountain Home]] silent-high-G envelope-break. See [[concepts/rotating-disc-morphology]]. ^[inferred]
- **First "ground-level launch site with searchlights"** datum in the corpus: two operating searchlights pointing near-vertical near the take-off area 1–2 miles south of the rail line. Implicates a **deliberate Soviet ground installation** as the ascent origin, in the witnesses' framing. ^[extracted] No other corpus artifact contains a witnessed launch installation alongside the sighting.

- Locate **USAIRA Cable C-103, 13 Oct 1955** as a standalone TS-classified message (the body text is preserved here, but the routed cable would be in HQ USAF / USAFE / USAIRA Moscow files). ^[open]
- Locate the **HQ USAF D/I (DINTA)** reply to Ryan and any **USAIRA Moscow** follow-on traffic. ^[open]
- Locate the **post-return Russell-group complete debriefing** Ryan recommended. ^[open]
- Locate other items in the **TS Control range `3-2400 – 3-2599`** at NARA RG 341 / FOIA 110677 — the file naming `numerical_file_5-2500` and the cover marking `T.S. Cont. # 3-2500` together imply a numbered TS-track series. ^[inferred]
- Identify the **Russell-group sleeper-car identifier** and verify the Trans-Caucasus rail leg (Baku → Tbilisi → Sochi → Kiev → COP → Prague) against contemporary 1955 timetables. ^[open]
- Resolve **`ATJATY` / `ADZHLJABUL`** as Azerbaijani / Russian-romanization placenames — likely **Adzhi-Kabul** (Adschi Kabul / Hadzhikabul) on the Baku-Tbilisi line, with `ATJATY` as a station to its south. ^[ambiguous]
- Identify **`EGOROV`** the trans-polar pilot — candidates include **Andrei Yumashev** (1937 Moscow-California flight crew) or **Mikhail Gromov** (1937 Moscow-California, but as captain not retired-with-handler in 1955). ^[ambiguous] Note: the trans-polar **1935** flight Egorov claimed appears in error or compressed; the actual Soviet trans-polar Moscow-North America flights occurred in **June and July 1937** (Chkalov to Vancouver WA; Gromov to San Jacinto CA). ^[inferred]
- Verify **AF Form 112 B-2** evaluation rubric: source reliability `B = "usually reliable"` and information `2 = "probably true"`. ^[inferred]
- Check whether the **42-km Moscow-Minsk radar site** is recorded in any post-1955 NATO Soviet radar order-of-battle. ^[open]
- Identify the **Soviet jet-fighter type** matching the Dnepropetrovsk 42-aircraft order-of-battle that Hathaway distinguished from MIG-15 / Fagot — candidates: **MIG-17 Fresco**, **MIG-19 Farmer**, or per Ryan, the SRI TIC 6244 composite. ^[ambiguous]
