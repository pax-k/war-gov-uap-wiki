# Sighting — Shell Oregon (27 May 1949)

A **27 May 1949** UAP sighting by **LtJG Joseph C. Shell, A5(L), USNR (Inactive), Naval Aviator's Certificate #0-17945** ^[extracted], during a private SNJ-aircraft flight from Red Bluff CA to Burns OR. Documented in **DIO-11ND Confidential Intelligence Report Serial 23-09** dated 23 June 1949, **filed in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 5]] as serial 197**. ^[extracted] The earliest 1949 **Navy-originated** UAP report in the wiki FBI corpus. ^[inferred]

## Source quality

DIO-11ND assigned the source the evaluation **B-3** ("Usually Reliable / Possibly True") ^[extracted]. Source qualifications: ^[extracted]

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Naval Aviator's Certificate | #0-17945 |
| Senior pilot rating | Ferry Wing of Naval Air Transport Command |
| Commercial ratings | Single + multi-engine + flight-instructor; commercial pilot license #184256 (1945 reissue) |
| Total flight hours | **>5,000** including 1,300 in U.S. Navy |
| Pilot training | Cal-Aero (primary instructor) + Lancaster CA (basic instructor), Mar 1942 - Sep 1943 |
| Operational service | Corpus Christi 30 days AV(T); Dallas 2 mo VHF-2(D); ferried BBJ's from North American plants; check-out base for ferry squadron VHF(2) Columbus Aug 1945; legal+personnel officer VHF(3) Terminal Island CA |
| Discharge | January 1946 as LtJG |
| Civilian position (1949) | Vice-President of Royal Petroleum Company of California (Long Beach); part-owner of three other oil-producing concerns |
| Education | B.S. + B.A. + 2 yrs law from University of Southern California |
| Age (1949) | 30 |
| DIO-11ND character note | *"believed to be conservative, sincere, absolutely responsible, reliable, and very competent"* |

The **>5,000-flight-hour pilot-witness profile** establishes Shell as a **top-quartile pilot-witness** in the wiki FBI corpus. ^[inferred] DIO-11ND's "Source secured a private license in 1935" predates pilot-licensing standardization in many states; combined with the WW2 instructor-then-ferry-pilot-then-private-pilot trajectory, the 14-year flight history makes Shell **uniquely well-qualified** among the corpus's 1949 sighting witnesses. ^[inferred]

## The sighting

Per Shell's deposition (DIO-11ND Serial 23-09): ^[extracted]

| Aspect | Value |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Friday 27 May 1949 |
| **Time** | 1425 Pacific Standard Time |
| **Location** | Hart Mountain valley, Oregon — between Red Bluff CA (departed 1332) and Burns OR (arrived 1458) |
| **Coordinates** | First seen: 42°38'N 119°43'W; lost: 42°28'N 119°48'W |
| **Witness aircraft** | Personal SNJ (Navy trainer) — "demilitarized" (~600 lbs lighter), normal max 170 mph upgraded to ~200 mph; making 212 mph ground speed (15-18 mph tail wind) |
| **Object size** | Less than 20 ft length |
| **Shape** | Elongated oval (~2× as long as wide, ~5× as long as thick); could possibly be egg-shaped or perfectly oval; "no break in the outline" — solid configuration |
| **Number** | Definitely 6-7 separate objects (no less than 5, no more than 8) |
| **Speed** | 190-260 mph air-speed; "definitely traveling … faster than a group of birds, and slower than jet planes" |
| **Direction** | South/SSW (opposite to Shell's NNE course) |
| **Altitude** | ~8,000 ft MSL; ~3,500 ft above valley floor |
| **Formation** | **File formation** (one behind the other); **constant inter-object spacing**; "appeared to keep their formation … as if they might have been under tow by the leading object" |
| **Color** | "Unpainted metal"; reflected sun toward Shell |
| **Trail** | No smoke or exhaust |
| **Sun position** | Rear of Shell (Shell between sun and objects) |

DIO-11ND **reporting officer's comment**: ^[extracted]

> It is believed that conventional aircraft, birds, balloons, astronomical phenomena, and auto-hypnosis can be eliminated as an explanation for the objects sighted by source. Since source was not flying into the sun and since he is an expert pilot-instructor, vertigo does not appear to be a reasonable explanation; nor do reflections on, or irregularities of, the plexi-glass canopy through which source viewed the objects offer a reasonable explanation, because of the wide range of observance of the objects. As far as the reporting officer's comment is concerned (as well as source's own comment) the explanation of source's sighting of the strange flying objects remains a question mark.

## Why this sighting matters

1. **Earliest 1949 Navy-originated UAP report in the wiki FBI corpus** ^[inferred] — predates the [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|1949 Box-186/319.1 file]]'s ONI Kodiak material (DIO-17ND Serial 13-49, 23 Apr 1949) by ~5 weeks ^[inferred].
2. **Top-quartile pilot-witness credibility** — 5,000-hour flight time, Naval Aviator + civil-aviation instructor.
3. **File formation morphology** — 6-7 elongated-oval objects in tight constant-spacing file flight is structurally distinctive: it predates the [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|section 2]] formation-flight reports (Hedstrom Twin Falls Aug 1947, Dupuis Oswego Sep 1947) by ~2 years and confirms the morphology as a stable cross-era pattern. ^[inferred]
4. **Hart Mountain Oregon location** — anchors a documented Pacific Northwest 1949 wave that the wiki has not previously located precisely.
5. **DIO-11ND filing in the FBI 62-HQ-83894 file** demonstrates **inter-agency distribution channel (e)** ([[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|see file-function entry]]) — i.e. Navy-originated intelligence reports retained as discrete serials in the FBI HQ topic file, parallel to the AF-side AIRMEM #4 channel (serial 164). ^[inferred]
