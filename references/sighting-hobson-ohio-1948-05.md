# Sighting — Hobson, OH (8 May 1948)

Night-time multi-witness sighting of phosphorescent disc-shaped objects over Hobson, Ohio. Reported on 27 May 1948 to the 11th Air Force, Harrisburg, PA by Special Agent B. K. Brown, [[entities/fbi|FBI]] Cleveland Office. Forwarded to Chief of Staff, USAF (Director of Intelligence, Requirements Division) on 14 June 1948 by Lt Col H. B. Wax, AC of S, A-2.

## Witnesses (NY Central System employees)

| Name | Role |
|---|---|
| Ben Rope | Car Inspector |
| Earl Roush | (same role) |
| Bob White | Yard Clerk |
| C. K. Hite | Patrolman |

Addresses listed as unknown to the originating headquarters. ^[extracted]

## Routing

- 11th Air Force → Chief of Staff, USAF (Director of Intelligence) — 14 June 1948
- Forwarded to [[entities/air-materiel-command|AMC]], [[entities/wright-patterson-afb|Wright-Patterson]], Attn: MCI — 16 June 1948 (1st Ind, AFGIR-CO-5, "L-6-29")
- Copies sent to Hq AMC and Hq Air Defense Command per ADC Letter 45-5, ¶1a (25 March 1948).
- Marked **CONFIDENTIAL**.

The 11th AF noted: "This Headquarters has no further information and is not initiating any investigation in accordance with paragraph 2, ADC Ltr. 45-5." Any further information was to be forwarded immediately. ^[extracted]

## Notes on the data quality

- Estimating an apparent size ("nine inches in diameter from ground level") of an object reported at 6–8 miles altitude is internally inconsistent — at that range a 9-inch apparent disc implies a true diameter of several hundred feet. ^[inferred]
- The witnesses were occupational observers (railroad night-shift staff) — not random pedestrians. This is the kind of observer profile that later UAP analysts (e.g. NICAP, AARO) preferentially weight. ^[inferred]
