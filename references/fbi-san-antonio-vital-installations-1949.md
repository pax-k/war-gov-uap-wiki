# SAC San Antonio → Director Vital Installations Memos (Jan-Mar 1949)

A **two-memo sequence** from the FBI San Antonio field office Special Agent in Charge to Director Hoover, **filed under Bureau File 65-58300, *Protection of Vital Installations*** rather than 62-HQ-83894 — captured in 62-HQ-83894 section 4 as enclosures or cross-references. ^[extracted] The memos are the wiki's **first primary-source evidence of cross-class FBI filing of UAP material**, ^[inferred] and contain the wiki's **earliest by-name reference to "Project Grudge"** (22 Mar 1949). ^[extracted]

## Document particulars

| Memo | Date | From | To | Subject | Bureau File | Section-4 page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| #1 | **31 January 1949** | SAC, San Antonio | DIRECTOR, FBI | PROTECTION OF VITAL INSTALLATIONS | **65-58300** | 114-116 |
| #2 | **22 March 1949** | SAC, San Antonio | DIRECTOR, FBI | PROTECTION OF VITAL INSTALLATIONS — Flying Discs | **65-58300** | 119-120 |

Both memos are classified CONFIDENTIAL. Both are filed primarily under 65-58300 but appear in 62-HQ-83894 section 4 as part of the post-BB-57 saucer-monitoring intake. The exact mechanism of cross-filing (carbon distribution, Liaison Section forwarding, or HQ Records Center cross-class indexing) is **not specified** in the visible OCR. ^[inferred]

## Memo #1 — 31 January 1949

### Cross-agency framing

> At recent Weekly Intelligence Conferences of G-2, ONI, OSI, and F.B.I., in the Fourth Army Area, Officers of G-2, Fourth Army have discussed the matter of "Unidentified Aircraft" or "Unidentified Aerial Phenomena" otherwise known as "Flying Discs", "Flying Saucers", and "Balls of Fire". This matter is considered top secret by Intelligence Officers of both the Army and the Air Forces. ^[extracted]

This is the wiki's **first primary-source documentation of UAP as a regular WIC (Weekly Intelligence Conference) topic** in the Fourth Army Area — i.e. **routinized cross-agency UAP coordination at G-2/ONI/OSI/FBI** by January 1949. ^[inferred]

### Historical framing (the memo's overview)

> It is well known that there have been during the past two years reports from the various parts of the country of the sighting of unidentified aerial objects which have been called in newspaper parlance "flying discs" and "flying saucers". The first such sightings were reported from Sweden, and it was thought that the objects, the nature of which was unknown, might have originated in Russia. ^[extracted]

The "first sightings reported from Sweden" framing is the **Swedish ghost-rocket / 1946-47 Russian-missile attribution** as it survived in institutional memory by Jan 1949. ^[inferred] See [[entities/swedish-air-intelligence-service]] and [[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11]].

### The Eastern Air Lines Montgomery sighting

> In July 1948 an unidentified aircraft was "seen" by an Eastern Airlines Pilot and Co-Pilot and one or more passengers of the Eastern Airlines Plane over Montgomery, Alabama. This aircraft was reported to be of an unconventional type without wings and resembled generally a "rocket ship" of the type depicted in comic strips. It was reported to have had windows; to have been larger than the Eastern Airlines plane, and to have been traveling at an estimated speed of 2700 miles an hour. It appeared out of a thunderhead ahead of the Eastern Airlines plane and immediately disappeared in another cloud narrowly missing a collision with the Eastern Airlines plane. No sound or air disturbance was noted in connection with this appearance. ^[extracted]

The "EAL pilots over Montgomery AL July 1948" event is the [[references/sighting-chiles-whitted-1948-07|Chiles-Whitted DC-3 24 Jul 1948]] case. The 31 Jan 1949 SAC San Antonio framing **promotes Chiles-Whitted to a Vital-Installations-grade institutional reference** ~6 months after the event, and **5 months before the SIGN Estimate of the Situation rejection** by Vandenberg. ^[inferred] This is the **first FBI-internal documentation** of the Chiles-Whitted case in the wiki corpus. ^[extracted]

### Spectroscopic + speed analysis

> Night-time sightings have taken the form of lights usually described as brilliant green, similar to a green traffic signal or green neon light. ... The spectrum analysis of one light indicates that it may be a copper compound of the type known to be used in rocket experiments and which completely disintegrates upon explosion, leaving no debris. It is noted that no debris has ever been known to be located anywhere resulting from the unexplained phenomena. ^[extracted]
>
> Recent observations have indicated that the unidentified phenomena travel at a rate of speed estimated at a minimum of three miles per second and a maximum of twelve miles per second, or a mean calculated speed of seven and one-half miles a second, or 27,000 miles an hour. Their reported course indicates that they travel on an East - West line with probability that they approach from the Northern Quadrant, which would be the last stage of the great circle route... ^[extracted]

The **spectroscopic copper-compound analysis** is a **physically diagnostic claim** unique in the wiki corpus — possibly performed by Dr Lincoln La Paz or by AEC instruments. ^[inferred] The **27,000 mph mean speed** estimate is **structurally inconsistent** with a "Russian missile" hypothesis (no 1949-era rocket engine could sustain such speeds), ^[inferred] yet the memo proceeds with the Russian-attribution framing without resolving this tension. The "great-circle from the northern quadrant" trajectory framing is **identical** to the Gasser hypothesis transmitted via [[references/fbi-sac-letter-38-1949|SAC Letter No. 38]] of 25 Mar 1949 (~8 weeks later) — suggesting **shared institutional source** (likely AMC Engineering Field Officer Gasser feeding both channels). ^[inferred]

### The Madeline Gwynne Merchant note

> Mrs. MADELINE GWYNNE MERCHANT was the subject of a letter from the Atlanta office to the Bureau dated August 10, 1948... She has generally been considered unreliable and possibly mentally unbalanced. She, however, has submitted to Military authorities the only theory thus far known that has any credibility at all, namely, that the lights are manifestations of cosmic rays which are directed toward a specific point. She further theorizes that such rays may interfere with the ignition of motors and may account for various unexplained air crashes. ^[extracted]

The Bureau's institutional simultaneously holding *"considered unreliable and possibly mentally unbalanced"* with *"the only theory thus far known that has any credibility at all"* is **structurally novel** in the wiki — the first explicit Bureau acknowledgment of a civilian theory carrying explanatory weight despite the originator's discredited status. ^[inferred] The Merchant cosmic-ray-EM-interference hypothesis structurally anticipates **EM-effect signatures** documented later in the corpus (engine stall, aircraft instrument failure). ^[inferred] See [[concepts/orb-phenomenon]] and [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement]] for related EM-effect cases.

### Vital-installations protective scope

> It is noted that the Fourth Army has the responsibility of protecting vital installations at Los Alamos, New Mexico, Sandia Base, New Mexico, and Camp Hood, Texas. Any information developed should be expeditiously transmitted to G-2, Fourth Army. ^[extracted]
>
> It is further noted that G-2 and O.S.I. are actively engaged in investigating this... ^[extracted]

The **Los Alamos / Sandia / Camp Hood triangle** is the institutional protective scope. **AEC** (Atomic Energy Commission) and **AFSWP** (Armed Forces Special Weapons Project) are both based in this airspace. The memo establishes that **G-2 4th Army has the operational lead** on UAP protective intelligence in the southwestern triangle — distinct from but coordinated with FBI / OSI / ONI participation. ^[inferred]

## Memo #2 — 22 March 1949

### "Project Grudge" by-name

> G-2, 4th Army, has now advised that the above matter is now termed "Unconventional Aircraft" and **investigations concerning such matters have been given the name "Project Grudge"**. ^[extracted]

This is the wiki's **earliest by-name reference to "Project Grudge"** — antedating the prior wiki anchor (28 Mar 1950 Boggs-Hearn memo) by **~12 months**. ^[inferred] The memo synchronizes:

- **"Unconventional Aircraft"** = the AIRMEM #4 / SAC Letter No. 38 nomenclature (15 Feb / 25 Mar 1949)
- **"Project Grudge"** = the AF program label

…confirming both labels were institutionally active by **G-2 4th Army's WIC channel** by **late March 1949**. ^[inferred] See [[concepts/project-grudge]] for the synchronizing update.

### The 16 Feb 1949 Los Alamos green-fireball conference

> G-2, 4th Army, advised on February 16, 1949, a conference was held at Los Alamos, New Mexico, to consider the so-called "Green fire ball phenomena" which began about December 5, 1948. It was brought out this question has been classified "secret" and that investigation is now the primary responsibility of the U.S. Air Force, Air Materiel Command, T-2.
>
> Dr. LINCOLN LA PAZ of the University of New Mexico, discussed one sitting which he himself ha[d witnessed]... ^[extracted]

The **16 Feb 1949 Los Alamos AEC/AFSWP/4th Army conference** is the **proximate institutional response** to the Dec 1948 NM green-fireball wave + the [[references/sighting-kirtland-mass-1949-01-30|Kirtland 30 Jan 1949 mass-event]] (~17 days earlier). ^[inferred] The conference assigns operational responsibility to **AMC T-2** at Wright-Patterson AFB — i.e. the same office running [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]]. ^[inferred] Project GRUDGE thus inherits not just the SIGN caseload but **also** the green-fireball portfolio, **before** GRUDGE has been formally branded by name (as confirmed by this same memo). ^[inferred]

### Wave statistics

> Since December 5, 1948 there have been more than ten incidents analogous to the "green fireball" above described and some twenty others with miner deviations from the above. It is also pointed out that the only sittings which had occurred seemed to have been confined to the Los Alamos, Las Vegas [NM], and West Texas triangle. ^[extracted]

This is the **first primary-source statistical summary** of the Dec 1948 - Mar 1949 NM green-fireball wave: **>10 prototype events + ~20 deviation events**, all confined to the Los Alamos / Las Vegas NM / West Texas triangle. ^[extracted]

### Camp Hood Killeen flares

> About 7:30 p.m., March 6, 1949, what was at first thought to be a flare was seen approximately one-half mile north of Killeen Base in the area of the Vital Installation at Camp Hood, Texas, and a second flare was noticed at 1:45 a.m., March 7, 1949, approximately three miles from Killeen Base. It has since been concluded that the flares seen near Killeen are probably similar to the phenomena previously noted in the Los Alamos, Sandia Base Area although these are the first sittings... ^[extracted]

The **Camp Hood / Killeen 6-7 Mar 1949** events are the **wiki's first preserved Camp Hood-area UAP datapoints**. ^[inferred] Killeen Base is the [[entities/afswp|AFSWP]] National Atomic Stockpile Site #2. ^[inferred] The memo's framing — *"probably similar to the phenomena previously noted in the Los Alamos, Sandia Base Area"* — links the green-fireball wave's institutional reading to the **AFSWP nuclear-stockpile threat-vector** rather than to the meteoric / atmospheric interpretation La Paz had been pursuing. ^[inferred]

## Why these memos matter — taxonomy

The two memos establish three structural points: ^[inferred]

1. **Cross-class FBI filing**: UAP-relevant material was being **filed under counterintelligence Class-65 (Vital Installations) in parallel** with the topic-anchored Class-62 file 62-HQ-83894 from at least January 1949. The 1966 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449|serial 449]] dual-class filing is **not the first** such pattern; this Jan 1949 cross-class filing is **17 years earlier**. ^[inferred] See [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file]] update.
2. **WIC channel**: G-2 / ONI / OSI / FBI **Weekly Intelligence Conferences** were a routinized cross-agency UAP coordination instrument by Jan 1949 in at least the Fourth Army Area. ^[extracted] No 1947-era equivalent is documented in the wiki corpus.
3. **Project GRUDGE timing**: GRUDGE was **named by 22 March 1949** at the G-2 4th Army WIC level — antedating the [[concepts/project-grudge|page's prior anchor]] (28 Mar 1950 Boggs-Hearn memo) by **~12 months**. ^[extracted] GRUDGE's operational scope by 22 Mar 1949 already includes the green-fireball wave (assigned to AMC T-2) — i.e. GRUDGE inherits a non-disc UAP portfolio at founding. ^[inferred]
