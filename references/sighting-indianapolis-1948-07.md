# Sighting — Indianapolis Cluster Toney/Huggins + Swigert (29 Jul + 31 Jul 1948)

A **two-event cluster in Indianapolis IN over a 48-hour window** in late July 1948, both investigated by the same field intelligence officer at Stout Field:

- **Inc 151** — 29 Jul 1948 0955; broad-short-propeller-shaped 6–8 ft aluminum object at 30 ft above tree-tops; civilian truck-driver witnesses **Mr James W. Toney + Mr Robert Huggins**.
- **Inc 152** — 31 Jul 1948 0825; cymbal-shaped 20-ft white flat-disc object at 2000 ft; husband-wife witnesses **Mr and Mrs Vernon Swigert**.

Both cases filed under **CONFIDENTIAL** classification in [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-101-172|the 101–172 incident summary bundle]]. ^[extracted]

The investigating officer for both incidents was **1st Lt Russell J. Carey Jr.**, Base Intelligence Officer, **331st AFBU, Reserve Training, Stout Field, Indianapolis**. Carey signs the morphology sketches and location diagrams attached to both cases — these are the **only hand-signed officer-drawn morphology sketches** in this part of the bundle. See [[entities/russell-j-carey]]. ^[extracted]

## Inc 151 — Toney + Huggins, 29 Jul 1948, NE Indianapolis

### Witnesses

- **Mr James W. Toney**, 235 So Bradley, Indianapolis
- **Mr Robert Huggins**, 5040 Indianapolis, Indianapolis

Both civilians. The 25-field intake form does not record occupations beyond addresses. From the narrative, they were riding in a truck approaching a cantilever bridge in northeastern Indianapolis when the object was sighted directly ahead above the trees. ^[extracted]

### Search

The witnesses stopped on the other side of the cantilever bridge but **could not relocate the object**. A subsequent **search by Lt John E. Hoskins (Base PIO) and Lt Russell J. Carey Jr. (Base Intel Officer, 331st AFBU, Reserve Training, Stout Field) produced no result**. The narrative notes the area where the object was believed to have fallen was **"densely wooded with many hills and valleys."** ^[extracted]

### Morphology distinctiveness

The "broad short propeller" shape with **round protruding cups on the upper side of each blade** is, structurally, unlike any other morphology in the 101–172 bundle. It is **not disc-class** (the standard 1948 morphology), **not cigar/cylinder-class** (Chiles-Whitted Inc 144), and **not orb-class** (Inc 130 Bonneville). The 6–8 ft / 1½–2 ft sub-meter dimensions and the 25–30 MPH glide speed put it closer to **drone / model-aircraft scale** than to manned aircraft — but the cup-protrusion feature is the morphology marker that differentiates it from any obvious prosaic explanation. ^[inferred]

The Carey-signed sketches preserve the object as **two equal blades meeting at a center hub**, oriented like an aircraft propeller laid horizontal, with **six visible cup-shaped protrusions on each blade's upper face** ("These are round cups which protrude" — Carey's annotation). ^[extracted]

## Inc 152 — Swigert + wife, 31 Jul 1948, S-Central Indianapolis

### Witnesses

- **Mr Vernon Swigert** — electrician
- **Mrs Vernon Swigert** — housewife

Both at home, **2020 Boyd St., Indianapolis**. Mr Swigert was standing at an open bathroom window facing west when he was attracted by the object; he rushed into the adjoining kitchen and showed his wife the object through the kitchen window facing south. ^[extracted]

| Exhaust trails | None |

| Sketches | **1 — signed by 1st Lt Russell J. Carey Jr., USAF, Intel Officer** |
| Apparent capacity | "Object appeared large enough to carry one person" (Swigert) |

### Investigation method

The narrative explicitly states: **"Investigation conducted by Intel Officer, 1st Lt Russell J. Carey, Jr., on a call from Mr. Swigert. It was noted that visibility was good from both windows for such observation."** ^[extracted]

This is the wiki's first explicitly-documented case of **a Project SIGN field-intel officer making a same-day-callout site visit to a civilian residence** to verify witness sight-lines as part of intake validation. ^[inferred] The two-window-confirmation methodology (bathroom window facing W + kitchen window facing S, witnesses each describing what they saw through their respective windows) is a viewing-angle-cross-check that **constrains an east-bound trajectory at the altitude reported**.

The narrative closes: **"No other reports received on this object."** ^[extracted]

## Why this cluster matters for the wiki

### 1. The Carey hand-signed sketch artifacts

Carey's two morphology sketches (Inc 151 broad-propeller-with-cups + Inc 152 cymbal-shape side-and-top-view) and one location diagram (Inc 151 cantilever-bridge-Fall-Creek path-of-object map) are the **only hand-signed officer-drawn morphology illustrations** in the central section of the 101–172 bundle. ^[inferred] They are evidence that **AMC's field-intel cadre was producing visual-record material from witness interviews at the local AFBU level**, not waiting for AMC HQ T-2 to commission illustrations.

### 2. Same-week morphology heterogeneity

The two cases sit **48 hours and ~8 miles apart in the same city**, yet present **radically different morphology classes**:

- Inc 151: low-altitude, slow, sub-meter dimensions, propeller-with-cups, "went down in woods"
- Inc 152: high-altitude, supersonic-estimated, 20-ft cymbal, "level course, no descent"

The cases are **not a same-object cluster** — they are independent intake at one field-intel office in one city. The morphology heterogeneity is data: **the 1948 corpus does not converge on a single object class even within one investigator's same-week pool**. ^[inferred]

### 3. The 331st AFBU at Stout Field

The investigations document **Stout Field (Indianapolis)** as an active Project-SIGN-pathway field-intel post in summer 1948. The 331st AFBU (Air Force Base Unit) is the reserve-training organization on station; Carey is the resident intel officer. ^[extracted]
