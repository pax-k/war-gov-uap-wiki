# Sighting — Savannah River Plant AEC, 8 August 1952

A flying-saucer-shaped object reported by **two employees of the E. I. du Pont Company** over the **400 area of the Savannah River Plant** at approximately **9:30 PM EST on 8 August 1952**. ^[extracted] First AEC critical-facility UAP event documented in the FBI corpus after the 1950 [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-adcock-1950-03|Oak Ridge / Adcock]] case. ^[inferred] Captured in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-7|FBI HQ 62-HQ-83894 section 7]] serial 302 (page 2). ^[extracted]

## The teletype

The 9 Aug 1952 6:46 PM EST URGENT teletype from FBI Savannah to Director: ^[extracted]

> FLYING SAUCERS, SAVANNAH RIVER PLANT, AEC. SECURITY OFFICE OF AEC ADVISED THIS DATE THAT TWO EMPLOYEES OF THE E. I. DU PONT COMPANY SAW A BLUE LIGHT WITH AN ORANGE FRINGE SHAPED LIKE A SAUCER FLY OVER THE FOUR HUNDRED AREA OF THE SAVANNAH RIVER PLANT AT APPROXIMATELY NINE THIRTY PM AUGUST EIGHT, FIFTYTWO. OBJECT FLYING AT A HIGH RATE OF SPEED AND TRAVELING IN A NORTHEASTERN DIRECTION.
>
> SCHLENKER. ACK AND HOLD. 7-48 PM OK FBI WA NRB.

The teletype is filed under serial **62-83894-302**. ^[extracted] Acknowledgment by Capt Schlenker noted on the cover. ^[extracted]

## Why this matters institutionally

The Savannah River Plant in Aiken, SC was an **AEC nuclear materials production site**, operated by **E.I. du Pont de Nemours under AEC contract**, primarily producing **tritium and plutonium-239** for the H-bomb program. ^[inferred] Construction had begun in 1950; production was ramping up in 1952. ^[inferred] Critical-facility designation places the sighting in a **structurally distinct category** from civilian-channel sightings of the same wave. ^[inferred]

The event extends the **AEC critical-facility UAP precedent** ^[inferred]:

| Date | Facility | Event | FBI corpus anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-8 Mar 1950 | Oak Ridge TN | [[references/sighting-oak-ridge-adcock-1950-03\|Stuart Adcock APN-7 radar over AEC site]] | Section 5 serials 215-219 |
| 30 Jan 1949 | Kirtland AFB / Los Alamos AEC | [[references/sighting-kirtland-mass-1949-01-30\|Multi-state mass-event "AEC, AFSWP, 4th Army perturbed"]] | Section 4 |
| **8 Aug 1952** | **Savannah River Plant SC** | **Two du Pont employees, blue-light-orange-fringe saucer** | **Section 7 serial 302 (THIS PAGE)** |

The 1952 SRP event differs from both predecessors in two respects: ^[inferred]

- **Civilian witnesses (du Pont contractors), not military** — unlike Oak Ridge (Adcock = WROL Knoxville radio engineer / amateur radar) or Kirtland (military bases) — du Pont employees are civilian-contractor witnesses.
- **Reported via AEC Security Office to FBI**, not directly by witnesses to FBI — the AEC's **internal-security routing** brings the sighting to FBI HQ, illustrating that **AEC-facility sightings flow through the AEC security channel first, even after BB-57**. ^[inferred]

The 1952 wave + post-cooperation-regime context: the FBI had reaffirmed [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-57-1947|BB-57]]'s passive-intake-only posture in [[references/fbi-sac-letter-83-1952|SAC Letter #83]] just **3 weeks after** this sighting — i.e. SRP serial 302 is one of the inbound items that prompted the reaffirmation. ^[inferred] FBI Savannah's URGENT routing reflects compliance with BB-57 paragraph D's "promptly furnish to OSI locally" mandate. ^[inferred]
