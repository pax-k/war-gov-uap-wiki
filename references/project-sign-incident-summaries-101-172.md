# Project SIGN Incident Summary Sheets 101–172 (Jan – Oct 1948)

A 178-page Mistral-OCR'd bundle of **66 Project SIGN incident summary sheets** numbered 101 through 172 (gap at 111 and 131–136), filed under cover routing **EXHIBIT 8530, Op 51, Serial 00196957 R/S, S-06200 dated 4-23-49**, subject "Flying Discs", filing line **"REPORT FILED M-5 AIRCRAFT - UNIDENTIFIED"** (signed JUDGE, 9-27-50). Declassification authority **NND 917033**.

This is the **middle segment** of the [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] sequential-incident stream as preserved at NARA Record Group 38 (Records of the Office of the Chief of Naval Operations and Naval Records), file 143685, Box 7. It bridges the [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1948|Feb–Jun 1948 AFOIR-CO startup file]] (which carries reports without standardized incident numbers) and the [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|Sep 1948 – Jan 1949 173–233 bundle]] (which uses the same 25-field intake form at scale). ^[inferred] The RG 38 placement reflects an ONI archival cross-file, not a Navy-originated investigation. ^[inferred]

## Why this file matters for the wiki

This bundle is the **first primary source in the corpus** to:

- **Document the 25-field "Check-List Unidentified Flying Objects" form in routine use as early as 18 February 1948** (Inc 101). The 173–233 page implied this form was a later development; this file proves it was operational at SIGN startup, less than a month after the [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell]] AFOIR-CO-5 directive of 27 Feb 1948. ^[extracted]
- **Establish [[entities/lincoln-la-paz|Dr Lincoln La Paz]]'s engagement with SIGN-orbit cases as early as 11 April 1948** (Inc 101n contains his letter to the Research and Development Board on the Norcatur Kansas incident). This pushes La Paz's first preserved SIGN-orbit contact back **8 months** from the prior Dec 1948 anchor in [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|Inc 227]]. ^[extracted]
- **Document the Chiles-Whitted Eastern Air Lines cigar-craft sighting** (Inc 144, 24 Jul 1948) — a corpus landmark that puts a pilot-witness engagement-class event five months **before** [[references/sighting-andrews-1948-11|Andrews Inc 207]]. See [[references/sighting-chiles-whitted-1948-07]]. ^[extracted]
- **Document the Gorman dogfight** (Inc 172, 1 Oct 1948 Fargo ND) — 27 minutes of mutual maneuvering between an F-51 pilot and a 6–8" white round object, including diving and climbing. See [[references/sighting-gorman-fargo-1948-10]]. ^[extracted]
- **Document the corpus's first 1948 physical-trace landing event** (Inc 162, 11 Aug 1948 Hamel MN) — a 2-ft dull-gray metallic object that landed within feet of two children, made whistling noises, and left a 2-ft ground depression with leveled rocks (corroborated by the field agent on visit). See [[references/sighting-hamel-mn-1948-08]]. ^[extracted]
- **Document the earliest Pacific-theater Project SIGN incident** (Inc 167, 6 May 1948) — Capt Floyd Barnes (USAF MATS Pacific Division) sees a white ball-of-fire explode 4–5 mi ahead of his aircraft between Kwajalein and Hickam, with subsequent unanswered VHF radio attempts to a reciprocal-course aircraft 1000 ft below. This **predates** [[references/sighting-honolulu-1948-10|Inc 178 Honolulu (18 Oct 1948)]] by 5 months as the corpus's earliest Pacific-theater SIGN datum. ^[extracted]

## Routing apparatus and incident-numbering

The 101–172 batch establishes that the **25-field "Check-List Unidentified Flying Objects" form was already operational at SIGN startup** — Inc 101 (18 February 1948) uses the identical form that the 173–233 bundle later uses at scale. This means the form was either:

1. Designed in parallel with the 27 Feb 1948 [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell]] [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|10-element template]] but expanded — or —
2. Backwards-applied retrospectively after Cabell's directive to standardize earlier 1948 reports. ^[ambiguous]

The first hypothesis is more economical: AMC T-2 had been intaking sightings since 1947 and likely pre-existed Cabell's formal standing-order directive. ^[inferred]

The bundle preserves four forms in addition to the 25-field Check-List:

1. **The 25-field "Check-List Unidentified Flying Objects" form** — primary intake instrument across nearly every incident.
2. **The 23-field "Incident Summary Sheet"** — used for Incs 137, 138, 142, 143, 170, 171 — likely a regional-office variant adopted at Wright-Pat. ^[inferred]
3. **EXHIBIT-style narrative supplements** — full witness statements (e.g. Inc 101 EXHIBIT B Sammons; Inc 144's attached EAL pilot statement; Inc 110's FBI-Baltimore-routed letter via SAC A-2B).
4. **Letters from named scientific consultants** — Inc 101n (Lincoln La Paz to the Research and Development Board), Inc 117 (Dr Paul ?? trip-report supplement, Memphis).

This file completes the **mid-1948 SIGN intake stream** in the corpus. With the 173–233 bundle (Sep 1948 – Jan 1949) now bracketed by this 101–172 bundle (Jan – Oct 1948), the wiki holds **continuous primary-source coverage of the SIGN sequential incident stream from January 1948 through January 1949** — 124 numbered incidents over 13 months.

The most consequential wiki-impacting findings from this file:

1. **The 25-field intake form predates the Cabell directive's mandate** — establishing AMC T-2's pre-1948 institutional readiness to intake civilian-and-military disc reports.
2. **La Paz's first SIGN-orbit engagement is now anchored at 11 April 1948**, not December 1948. This rewrites [[entities/lincoln-la-paz]] and [[concepts/green-fireballs-new-mexico]].
