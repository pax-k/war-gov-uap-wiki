# Project GRUDGE

The **second formal USAF flying-saucer project**, the institutional successor to [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] (initiated 30 Dec 1947 / formally Project SIGN 22 Jan 1948 ^[ambiguous]) and the predecessor to **Project BLUE BOOK** (1952). ^[inferred] GRUDGE ran from approximately **February 1949 through December 1949** ^[inferred], with formal **public termination announced December 1949** in a press release **"concurred in by the Army and Navy"** per [[entities/usaf|AF Intelligence]] (Major Boggs, Lt Col J. V. Hearn) as relayed to [[entities/d-m-ladd|FBI Asst Dir Ladd]] in the [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|28 March 1950 Boggs-Hearn memo]] (62-HQ-83894 serial 212). ^[extracted]

## What this page can document

The wiki's primary source for GRUDGE is **a single secondary FBI memo** (the Boggs-Hearn memo). The full primary-source GRUDGE record (the *Final Report on Project GRUDGE*, August 1949, "Special Report No. 102 AC 49/15-100" by AMC Wright-Patterson) is **not yet ingested** in the wiki corpus. ^[inferred] This page is therefore a **placeholder hub** for cross-referencing GRUDGE-adjacent material across the corpus, and will be enriched by future ingest.

## Founding posture

GRUDGE inherits the [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] caseload but reverses SIGN's late-1948 ETI-curious institutional posture. ^[inferred] [[references/project-sign-incident-summaries-173-233|SIGN's Sep 1948 - Jan 1949 caseload]] includes the [[references/sighting-chiles-whitted-1948-07|24 Jul 1948 Chiles-Whitted DC-3 encounter]] (which contributed to SIGN's "Estimate of the Situation" recommending the ETI hypothesis to the Pentagon, famously rejected by Vandenberg in late 1948) ^[ambiguous]; GRUDGE's intake reorganization on the basis of that rejection drives the **debunking-default posture** of 1949. ^[inferred]

The **operational substrate** of GRUDGE-era reporting is captured by:

- **[[concepts/fsr-200-4-reporting-regulation|Flight Service Regulation 200-4 (2 Nov 1948)]]** — the Flight-Service-side reporting instrument routing UFO reports to AMC at Wright-Patterson
- **[[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|HQ USAF AIRMEM #4 "Unconventional Aircraft" (15 Feb 1949)]]** — [[entities/c-p-cabell|Cabell]]'s D/I-side reporting instrument expanding the 10-element template into a ~50-field EEI schema; runs in parallel with FSR 200-4 throughout GRUDGE's lifetime
- **[[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|USAF Flying Discs Correspondence — NARA Box 186 / 319.1]]** — the 1949 continuation of the SIGN-era Flying-Service routing file (Oct 1948 - 9 Jan 1950 — i.e. **the file closes its outer routing instrument 11 weeks before the Boggs-Hearn memo confirms GRUDGE's December 1949 termination**) ^[inferred]

## Termination — the December 1949 press release

The Boggs-Hearn statement to Reynolds (28 Mar 1950): ^[extracted]

> The Air Force discontinued their intelligence project to determine what flying saucers are the latter part of last year. They publicly announced to the press in December, 1949, that the project had been discontinued. They advised that the press release had been concurred in by the Army and Navy.

The stated rationale: ^[extracted]

> after two years of investigation over three-fourths of the incidents regarding flying saucers proved to be misidentifications of a wide variety of conventional items such as lighted weather balloons and other air-borne objects.

The "two years of investigation" framing **collapses SIGN + GRUDGE into a single 2-year period (Dec 1947 - Dec 1949)** and **back-dates Boggs-Hearn's recap of public AF posture by ~2-3 months** vs. AF Director of Intelligence's actual statement (the literal AF statement of 27 Dec 1949 — the public end of GRUDGE — pre-dates this memo by 3 months). ^[inferred] The "75% misidentifications" figure is structurally identical to **Eddie Rickenbacker**'s 16 May 1950 quote captured in the [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|section 5]] D-3 briefing (serial 230). ^[inferred]

The replacement reporting protocol per Hearn: ^[extracted]

> Reports concerning flying saucers received at this time will be investigated by the Area Commander and his report submitted to the Air Force Intelligence Division as an intelligence item.

This is **decentralized residual processing**: HQ-USAF Intelligence Division will receive UFO reports as **incidental intelligence items** rather than systematic input via FSR 200-4 or AIRMEM #4. ^[inferred] The decentralization is the institutional substrate for **GRUDGE's transition to "passive" mode** (1951) before BLUE BOOK reactivates centralized investigation in 1952. ^[inferred]

## Why GRUDGE matters in the wiki corpus

- It defines **the second institutional posture** USAF takes toward UAP — debunking-default replacing SIGN's ETI-curious. ^[inferred]
- Its **December 1949 termination** is the **AF-side closure of the [[concepts/fbi-aaf-saucer-cooperation-1947|FBI-AAF cooperation regime]]** — together with the FBI's October 1947 internal closure (referenced in [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|the Boggs-Hearn memo]] as 62-83894-141-160), it formalizes the **dual-agency exit** by 28 March 1950. ^[inferred]
- Its **press-attribution rhetoric** ("recent magazine articles") sets the institutional posture that **reframes the post-1949 saucer-reporting wave as press-induced** — the [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Keyhoe]] / [[entities/frank-scully|Scully]] / [[concepts/aztec-saucer-myth-1950|Aztec-myth]] axis becomes the explanatory frame for AF + FBI + Army + Navy all simultaneously. ^[inferred]
- Its operational substrate (FSR 200-4 + AIRMEM #4) defines the *parallel channels* the wiki has documented in [[references/usaf-flying-discs-1949|the 1949 USAF correspondence file]] and [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-164|the Cabell directive carbons]] respectively. ^[inferred]
