# Four-Phase Contact Strategy (COMETA, 1999)

A thought-experiment framework introduced in the [[references/cometa-report-1999|COMETA Report]] (Chapter 13, "Political and Religious Implications") for modeling **how an extraterrestrial civilization might approach a less-advanced civilization like ours**. The committee imagines themselves in the shoes of the visitors, then derives implications for what Earth might experience and how Earth should prepare.

The framework is **inverse first-person**: COMETA writes the model from the *visiting* civilization's planning perspective, then asks what each phase would look like *to us as the observed civilization*. This rhetorical move is unusual in the policy-track corpus — [[references/hunter-1963-space-alien-race-memo|Hunter 1963]] writes from Earth's perspective only. ^[inferred]

## The four phases

| Phase | What the visitors do | What Earth sees | COMETA section |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Observation from a distance** | Remote sensing, electronic surveillance, language-decryption, recording. No physical/material contact. Could last "1 year, 10 years, a century, or a thousand years." | Background radiation only; no direct visual evidence. | §13.1 |
| **2. In-situ sampling and furtive appearances** | Sampling minerals, plants, animals, possibly evolved beings. Furtive contacts possible — covert or visible. | Sporadic UFO sightings, brief encounters; no sustained presence. Religious/political impact varies by civilization stage. | §13.2 |
| **3. Influences on local civilizations** | Modifying environment (seeding plants, organisms); influencing select individuals; possibly causing genetic mutations; reorienting religion/morals/law. | Mythologies of gods/visitors; sudden civilizational leaps; selectively-influenced leaders. | §13.3 |
| **4. Direct contacts** | Open encounter with select individuals, leaders, or full populations. Possibly via bionic robots or human-like avatars first. | Contact event proper; political/religious upheaval; sectarian responses. | §13.4 |

## Sub-frames

### Phase 2 distinguishes preindustrial vs. industrial civilizations

For **preindustrial-age civilizations** (§13.2.1), apparitions of spaceships or astronaut-class beings would create lasting religious / political impacts — COMETA cites parallels with biblical / mythological traditions:

- Ezekiel's flying machines.
- The aerial war of the Ramayana.
- The Epic of Gilgamesh.
- The "Elohim" of Genesis and the "Watchmen of the Sky" mixing with daughters of men, begetting giants (Enoch).
- The Immortals, Sons or Kings of the Sky (Orient, China, Japan).
- The Viracochas of South America; the Incas; ancient Egyptian gods.
- Various Greek and Roman pantheon figures.

Modern industrial-age civilizations (§13.2.2), by contrast, are "more skeptical… have more difficulty envisioning what is not a product of the immediately explainable" — but irrefutable proof of extraterrestrials would still leave a profound mark.

### Phase 3 discusses civilizational acceleration

For preindustrial civilizations under Phase-3 influence (§13.3.1), COMETA proposes alien intervention could accelerate technical/moral development by "playing the role that these populations would have willingly reserved for gods" — sacred texts, moral reorientation, perhaps genetic modification. The committee gestures toward the **concomitant appearance of the great civilizations of the Indus, Mesopotamia, and Egypt** (cities, architecture, writing, calendar, astronomy) as suggestive but not asserted. ^[ambiguous]

### Phase 4 distinguishes target civilizations

For **industrial-age civilizations** (§13.4.2), COMETA imagines the visitors selecting *specific states or leaders* for discreet, secret contact — with mutual benefit for the chosen leaders ("information, disinformation, and counterinformation campaigns to maintain the privileged nature of these relations"). This tracks directly with Chapter 10's [[concepts/uap-state-knowledge-classes|state-knowledge taxonomy]]: COMETA's class **(d) States that have entered into contact with one or more extraterrestrial civilizations** is the fully-realized Phase-4 target state. ^[inferred]

The committee's wry concluding move: by stepping through this thought experiment, "we thus return to our concerns and our current questions" — i.e., the model of how *we* might explore is the same model under which *they* are observing *us*.

## Grounding in Appendix 4 ("Colonization of Space")

COMETA's [[references/cometa-report-1999|Appendix 4]] is the **engineering thought experiment that grounds Chapter 13**. The four-phase model maps onto a future-human colonization plan that COMETA itself uses as the visitors' planning template:

- **Russian closed-ecosystem experiments since 1961**; American **Biosphere 2** (1.3 ha greenhouse, Arizona, 1991–1993 first run + six-month follow-on); subsequent University of Columbia takeover.
- **Permanent orbital station Alpha** (then-imminent successor to Mir).
- **Permanent moon base** as Antarctic-base analog; in-situ resource use for spacecraft construction (low gravity + no atmosphere advantages).
- **American physicist O'Neill's cylindrical space-city design** (30 km × 6 km, rotating for artificial gravity, supporting millions); constructed in the asteroid belt using local materials.
- **Antimatter-propelled "ship-worlds"** for interstellar transit, generations succeeding aboard during voyages of several centuries.
- **MHD propulsion** (cf. [[concepts/mhd-propulsion-uap]]) explicitly proposed for *"high-speed and silent craft to move through the planet's atmosphere"* in a destination star system.
- **Non-lethal weapons** to handle untimely encounters — *"the paralyzing effect of pulsed microwaves is under study in several countries."*

The structural read: COMETA writes Phase 1–4 as the alien plan but **engineers it as the human plan**. The four-phase model is internally consistent because it is the human space-exploration roadmap projected through Phase-1 "first-person inversion." ^[inferred] The committee uses this mirror move to keep the framework empirically anchored — every alien capability discussed in Phase 1–4 is one humans are explicitly planning or already prototyping. ^[inferred]

## What this concept does for the wiki

- **First explicit timed-phase contact-strategy framework** in the corpus. [[concepts/three-class-alien-taxonomy|Hunter's three-class taxonomy]] is a *type* taxonomy; this is a *time-sequence* taxonomy. Orthogonal axes. ^[inferred]
- **Explicit modeling of Earth's current state in the framework**: COMETA places us at "between Phase 1 and Phase 2" — observation has clearly happened (the report's own UAP D casework), and sampling/furtive appearances are documented but not yet fully validated. ^[inferred]
- **Consistent with the [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame|prepared-skepticism posture]]**: COMETA does not assert "we are in Phase X." It builds a framework and offers it as a planning instrument.
- **Resonates with [[concepts/space-alien-policy-question|the space-alien policy question]]**: this is the fully-elaborated alternative to Hunter's "if first contact, then…" 1963 framing. The framing has *expanded* in 36 years from a one-event conditional to a multi-decade-or-multi-century process model. ^[inferred]
