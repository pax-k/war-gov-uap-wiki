# Orb Flare Formation

A distinct **morphological-temporal signature** within the broader [[concepts/orb-phenomenon|orb phenomenon]]: a group of orbs that **flare up sequentially** along a line (horizontal or vertical), hold stationary while illuminated, then **flare down in reverse order** after a brief interval.

## Operational definition (working)

A UAP report fits the *flare-formation* pattern when the witness account includes:

1. **Multiple orbs** appearing in close spatial proximity (typically 3–6).
2. **Geometric arrangement** — most commonly a horizontal line, but vertical stacks and triangular formations have been reported. ^[inferred]
3. **Sequential ignition** — orbs flare up one at a time in the formation, not simultaneously.
4. **Stationary hold** — orbs appear stationary while lit (typically 10–15 seconds in the source).
5. **Reverse extinguishing** — orbs flare *down* in the opposite order from the flare-up sequence.

## Source observations

The pattern is documented in [[references/sighting-helicopter-orb-encounter-2025]] across at least **six distinct flare-formation events** in roughly 30 minutes:

| Approx. time | Configuration | Direction from helicopter |
|---|---|---|
| ~22:27 | 2 → 4–5 orb **vertical stack**, oval orange with white/yellow centers | West, above rotor disk |
| ~22:28 | 4–5 orb **horizontal line** | West, over mountains and above military aircraft |
| ~22:33 | 4–5 orb **horizontal line** (similar) | East, toward nearby town |
| ~22:41 | Single orb flare | West |
| ~22:52 | 5–6 orb **horizontal line** | East of facility, near local town |
| ~22:57 | 4 orb **horizontal line** | Above descending military aircraft |

The 2026 [[references/western-us-event-2026|Western U.S. Event]] does not describe this pattern — its "orbs launching orbs" event has a *launch* mechanic (one orb emits other orbs that move away), not a flare-up/flare-down formation.

## Distinguishing features vs. other orb sub-classes

| Sub-class | Defining behavior | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Brief flash-orb | 1–2 s appearance, **emits/launches** smaller orbs that move away | [[references/sighting-orbs-launching-orbs-western-us]] |
| Sustained-glow orb | Single orb, ~1 min hover near terrain | [[references/sighting-fiery-orb-western-us]] |
| **Flare formation** | Multiple orbs, sequential up/down, formation stationary | [[references/sighting-helicopter-orb-encounter-2025]] |

## Why it's a distinct concept (vs. lumping into orb-phenomenon)

- **Coordination signature**: the sequential up/reverse-down pattern is *non-trivial to produce* with conventional sources. Pyrotechnics produce simultaneous ignition or clustered random flares; drone formations produce simultaneous lights, not sequential ones with reverse extinguishing. ^[inferred]
- **Temporal closure**: the formation comes back down — the orbs do not depart laterally. This rules out the "launched submunition" hypothesis that fits Event 1 of the 2026 cluster. ^[inferred]
- **Reproducibility**: in the source, the same configuration appeared **at multiple times in multiple compass directions**, suggesting it is a stable behavior of whatever the underlying objects are. ^[inferred]

## Open hypotheses (not endorsed)

- **Coordinated drone display**: would explain sequential ignition and reverse extinguishing — but the close-approach behavior (≤10 ft from helicopter) and apparent pursuit of military aircraft in the same source raise the bar on payload and intent. See [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement]].
- **Optical/atmospheric artifact**: stretched by multi-sensor multi-channel observation (FLIR + NVG + naked eye) on the same event. ^[inferred]
- **Genuine unknown phenomenon**: held open by the SECRET//NOFORN classification of the underlying statement.

## Open questions

- Does the **direction of flare-up** correlate with the direction the formation later "engages" or moves? Source data is too sparse for this. ^[inferred]
- Are the vertical-stack and horizontal-line configurations the same phenomenon viewed from different aspects, or genuinely different formations? Source supports both interpretations. ^[ambiguous]
- Does this pattern recur in any of the publicly available UAP corpora (Project BLUE BOOK, AARO releases, civilian databases)? **Open thread for future ingests.**
