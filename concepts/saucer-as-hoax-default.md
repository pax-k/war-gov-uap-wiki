# Saucer-as-Hoax Default

The **operating presumption** embedded in the FBI's first phase of saucer-engagement (July-August 1947): **any recovered "flying disc" is a prankster fabrication until proven otherwise**. ^[inferred] The presumption is encoded in [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42's]] field-office instruction to *"investigate each instance which is brought to your attention of a sighting of a flying disc in order to ascertain whether or not it is a bona fide sighting, an imaginary one or a prank"* — and it is **confirmed empirically** within section 1 of [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|file 62-HQ-83894]] across at least **9 distinct prank-disc events** in 10 weeks. ^[extracted]

## The signature template

Recovered "discs" share a recurring hardware signature: ^[extracted]

- **Approximate disc form** — circular, 16-30 inches diameter, painted with **aluminum / silver airplane-dope paint**;
- **Visible "tech"** — wires, condensers, radio tubes, dry-cell batteries, flashlight bulbs, buzzers, photocells, motors with propellers;
- **Salvaged base material** — circular saw blades, hub-caps, garbage-can lids, oil-company signs, kitchen objects;
- **Recent assembly** — sticky tape, fresh paint, bolts visibly hand-tightened;
- **Often an explosive or pyrotechnic charge** — to produce the "swishing/whirring + bang" arrival sound;
- **Sometimes a spurious official-looking stamping** — *"Approved, Dunlap"* (Brasky disc); *"Inspected TS"* (Hasterbrook disc); *"Made in USA"* (Hairston disc).

## What the pattern means institutionally

The 9-event empirical confirmation **vindicates** the AAF's framing in [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]]: "individuals might report seeing flying discs… for personal publicity, causing hysteria, or playing a prank." ^[extracted] But it also **structurally biases** subsequent FBI saucer-investigation: ^[inferred]

- field offices arrive at every site with the **prank hypothesis pre-loaded**;
- the **standard FBI disposition** becomes "turn over to G-2 / AAF" rather than to FBI Lab;
- the **non-prank sightings** (genuine pilot witnesses like Kenneth Arnold, Fred Johnson, the Williams Field P-80 student) get folded into the same "investigation" stream and inherit the prank-hypothesis prior;
- **even Roswell** (serial 18) is approached through the same disposition: the FBI Dallas teletype routes the case to Wright Field for examination, not to FBI for cursory examination — despite Hoover's marginal note insisting the FBI must have access. ^[inferred]

## Where the pattern breaks

Within section 1 itself, **at least 5 events do not fit the prank template** and require alternate framings: ^[inferred]

- **Kenneth Arnold / Mt Rainier** — pilot-witness, no recovered object — see [[references/sighting-arnold-mt-rainier-1947-06-24]]
- **William Rhodes / Phoenix** — civilian-witness with photographs, no recovered object — see [[references/sighting-rhodes-phoenix-1947-07-07]]
- **Lt William G. McGinty USN P-80 student / Grand Canyon** — pilot-witness at 25,000 ft, two round objects "going at inconceivable speeds straight down" (serial 38)
- **MIT / West Rindge NH metallic fragments** — physical fragments analyzed by MIT (Profs Rentges + Kahn), described as *"similar to lining of V-2 bombs"*, treated as classified (serial 28)
- **Bill Turrentine / Norfolk VA** — 15-year-old + photograph + Photo Craftsman enlargement (serial 45-area)

The absence of these from the prank-default disposition exposes the operating presumption's **selection effect**: prank cases are **investigated to disposal**, while pilot-witness or fragment cases are filed-and-forgotten. ^[inferred]

## Related pattern in subsequent sources

Subsequent FBI artifacts in the wiki corpus continue to apply the hoax-default framing, but with diminishing conviction: ^[inferred]

- 1949-1956 — saucer-discourse increasingly accepted as a real reportable phenomenon, hoax-default narrows;
- 1957 [[references/fbi-detroit-100-26505-krasuski-1957|Krasuski Detroit case]] — FBI agent Rathbun's witness-character note ("intelligent, articulate, no apparent motive for fabrication") is the **inverse** of the hoax-default frame;
- 1964 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-438|Byrnes Albuquerque/Socorro report]] — substantive evidentiary investigation, no hoax framing;
- 1966 [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449|LA SAC AFSCA-magazine memo]] — the political framing eclipses the hoax framing.

The hoax-default thus appears to be a **time-bounded operating presumption** active in 1947-48 and gradually dismantled by the 1949 [[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|AIRMEM #4 protocol]] which institutionalizes physical-trace investigation procedures (soil samples, Geiger counters, signed witness statements). ^[inferred]
