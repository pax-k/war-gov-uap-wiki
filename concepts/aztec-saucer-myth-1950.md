# Aztec/Scully Crashed-Saucer Myth-Seed (1950)

A persistent UFOlogical claim — circulated through a small Denver-based promoter network in late 1949 and early 1950, then canonized in [[entities/frank-scully|Frank Scully]]'s September 1950 book *Behind the Flying Saucers* — that **flying disc(s) crashed in the southwestern US (variously NM / Mojave Desert / NM-AZ border) and were recovered with humanoid bodies on board**. ^[inferred] The Aztec story is the **first complete crashed-saucer-with-bodies narrative** in the wiki corpus (note: the [[references/sighting-roswell-1947-07-08|Roswell July 1947]] event in 62-83894-section-1 serial 18 contains a 8th Air Force / Wright Field hexagonal-disc-and-balloon recovery but **no body claim**) ^[inferred] — and it predates the canonical post-1980 Roswell-with-bodies narrative by ~30 years. ^[inferred]

## Why this concept page

The wiki's primary-source documentation of the Aztec myth-seed is **uniquely captured in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|FBI HQ file 62-HQ-83894 section 5]]**, where the same myth-seed lands at FBI HQ via **four independent intake channels within ~2.5 months** (Jan-Mar 1950). ^[inferred] This convergence — multiple non-coordinated FBI/OSI inputs receiving the same content with similar wording — is structurally distinctive: it argues for **single-source laundering**, not independent witness reports. ^[inferred] No other crashed-saucer claim in the corpus arrives with comparable channel-multiplicity; the convergence is what makes this a paradigm-defining concept page rather than a sighting page.

The four channels' **content alignment**: ^[extracted]

- **Geographic anchor**: NM (3 of 4) or Mojave (1 of 4) — both southwestern-US;
- **Quantity**: 2-3 saucers (channels α, β, γ); single intact craft + 18 occupants (channel δ);
- **Occupant size**: **3 feet tall** (all four);
- **Occupant attire**: **metallic / blond-haired / hairless / no underwear / body-taped** (channels α + γ);
- **Causal mechanism**: **radar interference** (channels α + γ);
- **Information-suppression claim**: "Government wanted info spread from unofficial sources" (α); "telephone calls from FBI requested keep info to himself" (δ).

The convergence is **too tight to be coincidental** — it argues that all four channels trace to the **same myth-seed package** ^[inferred] entering circulation no later than late 1949 and being amplified through (a) Denver oilman / radio / advertising networks, (b) Wyandotte-Echo press, (c) DC-PD informal liaison, (d) the Keyhoe/Scully magazine cycle. ^[inferred]

## The Denver promoter network

The four channels point overwhelmingly at a **Denver-anchored promoter network** centered on three figures: ^[inferred]

| Person | Role | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| **[[entities/george-koehler\|George T. Koehler]]** | KMYR radio Denver; 315 Franklin St | β + δ (named); inferred α (the unnamed Wyandotte-Echo "Coulter from Denver Ford agency") ^[inferred] |
| **[[entities/silas-newton\|Silas Newton]]** | Newton Oil Co Denver, Equitable Building; "Mysterious Mr. X" | δ (named) |
| **[[entities/frank-scully\|Frank Scully]]** | *Variety* magazine columnist; Jan 1950 article + Sep 1950 book *Behind the Flying Saucers* | δ (named) |

The post-FBI-corpus history (well outside the wiki's primary-source ingest range): Newton + Koehler are revealed in 1952-53 to have been running an **oil-and-mineral fraud scheme** using the Aztec saucer story as a marketing hook for "magnetic-field" oil-detection devices ^[inferred]. ^[ambiguous] The most-economical reading of the FBI-corpus channels: **Koehler-Newton are seeding the press-and-investigative landscape with the Aztec story to support a fraud scheme**, with [[entities/frank-scully|Scully]] as press-amplifier and [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Keyhoe]] as parallel-but-separate beneficiary in the magazine cycle. ^[inferred]

## Channel ε (this batch) — press-shadow via Drew Pearson syndicated column

Sub-file A of the same FBI HQ file ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-sub-a|62-HQ-83894-A]]) page 67 retains a **fifth Aztec-myth-seed channel** in the form of [[references/drew-pearson-saucers-nil-1950-11-25|Drew Pearson's syndicated column "Reports Plentiful, Saucers Nil"]] (Washington Post, 25 Nov 1950). ^[extracted] Pearson cites *"confidential Air Force files"* and reproduces the **same Coulter-Koehler identity-revelation** that section 5 captured eight months earlier: the "mysterious Mr. Coulter" was [[entities/george-koehler|George Koehler of Denver]] (radio station KMVR / KMYR advertising salesman), the "flying saucer gear" was stamped with **Roman numeral VI**, and Koehler later denied any saucer/occupant claim. ^[extracted]

Channel ε is structurally distinct from the four section-5 channels because it is **press-syndicated and post-resolution**: ^[inferred]

- Channels α-δ (Wyandotte Echo / Denver SAC / Hottel WFO / New Orleans SAC) all received the myth-seed *as live content* in Jan-Mar 1950, before AF investigators had concluded the Coulter=Koehler identity.
- Channel ε (Pearson Nov 1950) is the **public AF resolution** of channels α-δ — Pearson syndicates the AF investigators' conclusion that Coulter=Koehler and that Koehler had retracted.

The presence of channel ε in sub-file A confirms that the FBI's interest in the Aztec myth-seed extended beyond passive accumulation through Mar 1950: HQ Central Research Section continued tracking the **press-shadow of the AF's resolution** of the myth-seed through at least Nov 1950. ^[inferred]

## Why the FBI files this and does not investigate it

The Hottel memo (channel γ) is filed with **"No further evaluation was attempted"**. ^[extracted] The Denver SAC URGENT (channel β) gets Hoover's reply **"NO FURTHER ACTION DESIRED"**. ^[extracted] The New Orleans SAC report (channel δ) is *"furnished the Bureau and the designated offices for informational purposes."* ^[extracted] The Wyandotte-Echo TWX (channel α) is filed **at the OSI HQ end** rather than triggering FBI investigation. ^[extracted]

This pattern is **structurally consistent** with the Bureau's [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|post-October-1947 non-investigation posture]] (Bureau Bulletin No. 57, 1 Oct 1947, referenced in [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-5|section 5]] serial 206). ^[inferred] The file's intake function is **passive accumulation** of UAP-topic material, not investigation. ^[inferred] Filing the Aztec myth-seed via 4 channels does **not** indicate Bureau credence in the claim — it indicates the claim's **broad circulation through public-private intake paths**, all 4 of which the file passively absorbs. ^[inferred]

## Why this matters for the corpus

1. **Establishes the press-and-promoter substrate of the post-1949 saucer wave.** The [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|Boggs-Hearn]] AF Intelligence framing of *"recent magazine articles"* as the cause of post-1949 saucer reporting is **substantively correct** for the Aztec myth-seed: [[entities/donald-keyhoe|Keyhoe]] *True* Jan 1950 + [[entities/frank-scully|Scully]] *Variety* Jan 1950 are both inside the channel-α-and-δ origin-window. ^[inferred]

2. **Predates Roswell-with-bodies by ~30 years.** The popular 1980+ Roswell narrative (Berlitz/Moore 1980, Friedman/Berliner 1992) folds humanoid-body-recovery into the 1947 Roswell event. The Aztec myth is the **first complete saucer-with-3-ft-humanoid-bodies narrative** in primary-source FBI files — and its 1950 channel-distribution arrives **before** any documented body claim about Roswell. ^[inferred] The Aztec story is the proximate ancestor of all "small humanoid bodies in metallic suits" tropes in subsequent UFOlogy. ^[inferred]

3. **Demonstrates the channel-multiplicity test for myth-seed analysis.** Multiple uncoordinated FBI/OSI inputs receiving the same content within weeks is a **structural signature of laundering**, not a structural signature of corroborated reality. ^[inferred] The Hottel memo's naive UFOlogical reading inverts this signature.

4. **Exemplifies the institutional non-investigation posture.** All four channels arrive at FBI HQ post-October-1947-Bureau-termination + post-December-1949-AF-termination; all four are **filed without investigation**. The Aztec myth-seed is the **clearest test case** for the [[references/fbi-boggs-hearn-discontinuation-memo-1950-03-28|Boggs-Hearn]] *"file but don't investigate"* protocol. ^[inferred]
