# Project Blue Book

**Project BLUE BOOK** — the **third and longest of the formal USAF UFO investigation projects** (1952 – 17 Dec 1969), succeeding [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]] (Jan 1948 – Feb 1949) and [[concepts/project-grudge|Project GRUDGE]] (Feb 1949 – Dec 1949). Headquartered at **Wright-Patterson AFB** under [[entities/air-materiel-command|Air Materiel Command]] / later **ATIC** (Air Technical Intelligence Center), where its predecessors also ran.

This page is a **stub hub**. The wiki's primary-source coverage of Blue Book is concentrated in narrower pages — see [[concepts/project-blue-book-termination-1969]] for the 1969 closure, [[concepts/condon-committee]] for the proximate warrant, and [[entities/j-allen-hynek]] for the project's scientific consultant. This page exists primarily so the wikilink anchor resolves and for project-level context.

## Role in the wiki's UAP corpus

- **Continuity within the SIGN → GRUDGE → BLUE BOOK lineage** — the third and longest of the AF's formal UFO projects, all run from Wright-Patterson under AMC/ATIC and successor commands. ^[inferred]
- **Length and scale (Mar 1952 – Dec 1969)** — 17+ years, with **12,618 total sightings** and **701 unidentified** preserved in the wiki via the [[references/fbi-blue-book-termination-fact-sheet-1977|1977 UFO Fact Sheet]]'s 1947–1969 sighting summary. ^[extracted]
- **Scientific-consultant function** — [[entities/j-allen-hynek|J. Allen Hynek]] served as the project's scientific consultant for **21 years** (1948–1969), spanning all three Wright-Patterson projects. ^[extracted from j-allen-hynek page]
- **1968 — Robertson Panel re-evaluation context** — the [[concepts/condon-committee|Condon Committee]] (Nov 1966 – late 1968) is the proximate institutional warrant for closure of Blue Book; the AF commissioned the Colorado study explicitly to provide an external, peer-reviewable basis for closing or continuing its UFO program. ^[inferred]
- **The 17 Dec 1969 termination** — Secretary-of-the-Air-Force termination; **AFR 80-17 rescinded**; documentation transferred to NARA. ^[extracted] The AF's three negative conclusions (no national-security / no scientific-anomaly / no extraterrestrial indication) become the canonical institutional posture. See [[concepts/project-blue-book-termination-1969]].
- **The 1977 FBI-side filing** — the closure is filed back into [[entities/fbi-hq-62-83894-file|62-HQ-83894]] 7+ years later via the **McClanahan-Moore 1977 exchange** (serials 483-484), the canonical FBI-side primary-source artifact of the closure. ^[extracted] See [[references/fbi-blue-book-termination-fact-sheet-1977]].

## Significance for the corpus

- Blue Book is the **last formal Executive-Branch UFO project** before the post-1969 22-year institutional gap (1969 – early 1990s) and is the period the **post-Blue-Book-termination coordination question** [[concepts/post-bb-uap-coordination-question-1977|(1977)]] structurally responds to. ^[inferred]
- The project's name is the **canonical referent** for USAF UAP investigation in the cultural and historiographic literature ^[inferred] — almost every secondary source on UAP history is organized around Blue Book even when the substantive collection ran through SIGN and GRUDGE.
