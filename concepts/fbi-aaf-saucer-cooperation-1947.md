# FBI-AAF Saucer Cooperation Arrangement (1947)

The institutional bargain negotiated between **FBI HQ and Army Air Forces Intelligence** in July-August 1947 under which the FBI undertook to **investigate first-witness sincerity** on flying-disc reports, conditional on **complete AAF cooperation** including full FBI access to any recovered discs. ^[extracted] The arrangement was operationally codified as [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42, Series 1947]] (30 Jul 1947). ^[extracted]

## What each side got

| Side | Got |
|---|---|
| **FBI** | (1) AAF guarantee of complete cooperation including disc-recovery access; (2) AAF promise to share scientific findings; (3) institutional anchor for the FBI's domestic-intelligence interest in disc-reporting subversive-individuals |
| **AAF** | (1) FBI investigative resources to vet first-witness sincerity nationwide; (2) FBI counterintelligence framing of disc-reporters as potential subversives (channel later expanded to AFOSI loyalty checks); (3) leverage to establish the **wire-released-disc cover-story** in field-office baseline understanding ([[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947\|BB42]]'s embedded prosaic explanation) |

## The wire-released-disc cover story

Schulgen tells Reynolds, and the FBI propagates to all field offices in [[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|BB42]] verbatim: ^[extracted]

> The Air Forces have confidentially advised that it is possible to release three or more discs in odd numbers, attached together by a wire, from an airplane in high altitudes and that these discs would obtain tremendous speed in their descent and would depend to the earth in an arc.

This **wire-released disc** explanation appears in **no other source in the wiki corpus** ^[inferred] — it is the AAF's most aggressive prosaic framing offered the Bureau in 1947. The mechanism described (multiple discs released by wire from high altitude, descending in an arc at high speed) is structurally implausible: ^[inferred] no aerodynamic mechanism plausibly produces "tremendous speed" from passive disc-and-wire release; the cover-story functions as a **conceptual anchor for the prankster-explanation-space**, not as a serious aerodynamic claim. ^[inferred]

## The Communist-subversion overlay

[[references/fbi-bureau-bulletin-42-1947|Bureau Bulletin No. 42]] propagates Schulgen's framing that *"reports might have been made by subversive individuals for the purpose of [creating] mass hysteria"* directly to all field offices. ^[extracted] This **counterintelligence overlay** is structurally upstream of: ^[inferred]

- the **dual-classification** of [[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-serial-449|serial 449]] (Oct 1966) under both LA Class-100 organizational counterintelligence file and HQ Class-62;
- the broader pattern of saucer-publication-as-CP-line-evidence that recurs throughout the FBI-corpus into the 1960s;
- the institutional posture that **disc-witness political loyalty is investigable**, not just disc-witness physical credibility.

## The regime's actual operational profile

In the **2 weeks** between 30 Jul and 14 Aug 1947, section 1 shows ~10 cases processed under the regime, **mostly resolving as pranks**: ^[extracted]

- 9 of the 9 prank-disc events tabulated in [[concepts/saucer-as-hoax-default]] all flow through the BB42 channel;
- The Maury Island case (serial 32 + 49) goes through it to its catastrophic conclusion;
- The Williams Field P-80 student / pilot-witness MOICs come up the AAF side of the channel (serial 52);
- The Bailey orbital-germ-bomb hypothesis arrives via Stamford Resident Agent (serial 27) — see [[concepts/civilian-saucer-hypothesis-1947]];
- The MIT West Rindge fragment case (serial 28) flows up via Boston SAC.

**The regime functions exactly as designed for prank-default cases**. ^[inferred] **It is structurally underequipped for non-prank cases** — the pilot-witness MOICs and physical-fragment cases get filed but neither resolved nor escalated. ^[inferred]

## Operational erosion (Aug-Sep 1947) — section 2 evidence

Section 2 of the file ([[references/fbi-hq-62-83894-section-2|serials 53-100]]) captures **three operational erosion events** within ~6 weeks of BB42's distribution: ^[inferred]

1. **The Fugate negative-handover incident** (29-30 Aug 1947, serial 68 + 80) — A-2 4th AAF representative George Fugate Jr. arrives in Phoenix on Springer's orders to interview William Rhodes jointly with the FBI; insists on being introduced as merely a "representative of the United States government" (not AAF); takes Rhodes' photographic negatives despite Rhodes asking for them back. SA Brower flags this as "peculiar procedure". ^[extracted] Hoover's 30 Aug 1947 teletype directs SF + Phoenix SACs that **"JOINT INVESTIGATION NOT DEEMED NECESSARY"** and that Fugate may not sit in on the interview — but **the teletype arrives after the joint interview has already happened**. ^[extracted] The cooperation regime's "**full FBI access**" precondition (Hoover's serial 33 marginal note) is **operationally inverted**: the AAF representative is on-scene before the FBI's prohibition arrives, and **takes the negatives** with the FBI's agent participating. ^[inferred]
2. **The Garrett admission** (~22 Sep 1947, serial 86) — Lt Col [[entities/g-d-garrett|Garrett]] of AAF Intelligence privately tells Reynolds that flying discs **are likely a classified US Government experiment** (and scientist "Mr. Carroll" agrees), citing the Sweden ghost-rocket precedent: *"when flying objects were reported seen over Sweden, the 'high brass' of the War Department exerted tremendous pressure on the Air Forces Intelligence... in contrast... we have reported sightings of unknown objects over the United States, and the 'high brass' appeared to be totally unconcerned. He indicated this led him to believe that they knew enough about these objects to express no concern."* ^[extracted] Reynolds explicitly tells Garrett: *"if it is a fact experimentations are being conducted by the United States Government, then it does not appear reasonable to request the FBI to spend money and precious time conducting inquiries with respect to this matter."* ^[extracted]
3. **The Schulgen 5 Sep 1947 formal denial** (serial 84) — closes Garrett's hypothesis at AAF level: *"a complete survey of research activities discloses that the Army Air Forces has no project with the characteristics similar to those which have been associated with the Flying Discs."* ^[extracted] The institutional consequence: the AAF **publicly denies a USG-project explanation while privately having no working alternative** — structurally upstream of [[concepts/project-sign|Project SIGN]]'s creation in late 1947 / early 1948. ^[inferred]

Together these three events show the cooperation regime running **doctrinally** while **eroding operationally** — not termination, but **competitive-channel friction** combined with **internal AAF credibility collapse on the prosaic-explanation hypothesis**. ^[inferred] The two paradigm-defining doctrinal pivots that emerge from the Sep 1947 reckoning — **Twining's 23 Sep 1947 AMC memo** ("real and not visionary or fictitious") and **Project SIGN's establishment** — both happen ~outside the FBI corpus per se but are **directly downstream of the Garrett-Schulgen-Reynolds chain captured in section 2**. ^[inferred]

## Termination

The arrangement is **silently superseded** rather than formally rescinded. ^[inferred] The Cabell directives of 27 Feb 1948 (10-element template) and 15 Feb 1949 ([[concepts/flying-disc-reporting-protocol|AIRMEM #4]]) shift the inter-agency framework to a Cabell-axis-routed structure, with the FBI's role narrowing to *"FBI records"* as a witness-credibility source explicitly named in AIRMEM #4 Part II. ^[extracted] The Schulgen-Reynolds-Ladd channel of 1947 thus mutates into the witness-credibility-pull channel of 1949 — without intermediate doctrinal closure. ^[inferred] Section 2's evidence (above) shows the **operational decay had already begun within 6 weeks of BB42's issuance** — by mid-Sep 1947, both sides recognized the regime's structural problem (FBI investigating something the AAF privately suspects is its own project) without formally addressing it. ^[inferred]
