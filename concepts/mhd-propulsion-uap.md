# MHD Propulsion as a UAP Explanation Model

A specific physical model proposed in the [[references/cometa-report-1999|1999 COMETA Report]] (§8.1.1.1) for the **silent, propeller-less, low-altitude hover-and-supersonic-cruise behavior** repeatedly described in well-documented UAP cases. The model is **magnetohydrodynamic propulsion (MHD)** — using the air around the craft as the conducting medium for an electrical current that interacts with an onboard-generated magnetic field to produce thrust, by Laplace's law (force on a current-carrying medium in a magnetic field).

The COMETA Report does **not** claim MHD is *what* UAPs use. It claims MHD is *what could explain* the observed flight characteristics if a sufficiently advanced civilization built an MHD-propelled aircraft — i.e., it is an existence-of-physical-model argument. ^[inferred]

## The mechanism

For atmospheric flight without propellers or jets:

1. **Onboard magnetic field** generated by current-carrying coils in or under the craft's walls (same principle as electric motors).
2. **Conducting medium** — air rendered conductive by strong electric fields generated by hull-mounted electrodes. The air becomes more or less luminous when ionized.
3. **Force coupling** — Laplace's law gives the magnetized current a force on the surrounding (now-conducting) air; reaction propels the craft.

The ionization of air is a candidate explanation for **the luminosity** "frequently observed around unknown objects" in UAP reports. ^[extracted]

| **No visible air flow** at low-altitude hover | currents in air rather than mechanical air displacement | [[references/sighting-helicopter-orb-encounter-2025]], [[references/sighting-fiery-orb-western-us]] ^[inferred-as-target] |

| **Rapid acceleration** | scaling of Laplace force with field strength; superconducting wirings expand the regime | [[references/sighting-portland-or-1949-07]], various |

## Status of the technology in 1999

COMETA cites **active MHD research at**:

- **Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (Troy, NY)** — US.
- **Great Britain and Russia** — per *New Scientist* (February 1996).
- US/Japanese **naval MHD experiments** had succeeded for surface and submarine ship models.
- **Superconductors operating at near-ambient temperatures** (discovered 1991) made the very strong fields required for MHD aircraft "a credible prospect" since 1991. ^[extracted]

COMETA's conclusion: *"based on the current state of our knowledge, an MHD aircraft model is conceivable in the short term, while the creation of a craft having the same movement capabilities as the aerial vehicles described by the witnesses seems quite likely to us within a few dozen years."* ^[extracted]

## Limits acknowledged in the report

- **MHD does not work in vacuum** — no medium for current flow. So MHD cannot explain space-segment travel; that requires different propulsion (chemical rocket, particle-beam, antimatter, or planetary slingshot).
- The "**quasi absence of perceptible air flow and noise while hovering close to the ground**" is what COMETA flags as the residual hard problem even for an MHD model — i.e., the report does not claim MHD perfectly explains all UAP observations. ^[extracted]
- Cooling not visible/audible is rationalized only for short-duration flights; longer missions would require visible cooling systems.

## Why this matters for the wiki

- **First detailed physical-mechanism explanation framework** in the corpus that is engaged with at the level of equation-class physics, not just at the report-the-sighting level. ^[inferred]
- **Aligns with the [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame|prepared-skepticism posture]]**: COMETA does not assert "UAPs use MHD." It asserts "MHD is a known-physics framework that explains a substantial fraction of the otherwise-paradoxical aerodynamic features."
- **Pairs with the [[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon|engine-stall phenomenon]]**: COMETA's §8.1.2 proposes that **microwave emissions from UAPs** account for vehicle engine shut-off (ionization and electrical breakdown of air around the high-voltage ignition circuit; direct electronic-circuit paralysis for post-1970s electronic ignitions). This is the same physical regime as MHD operation — both involve strong EM fields directly exerted on or around vehicles. ^[inferred]
- **Pairs with [[concepts/orb-phenomenon|orb morphology]]**: ionization-luminosity is consistent with orb-class observations where the visible "object" is a glowing volume rather than a metallic body. ^[inferred]

## Position relative to corpus

- The 1948 [[concepts/horten-flying-wing|Horten flying-wing]] thesis was a *conventional-aviation* explanation — discredited by morphology and behavior across the corpus.
- MHD is a *non-conventional but known-physics* explanation — it does not require ETI to make sense, but it does not preclude ETI either.
- [[concepts/three-class-alien-taxonomy|Hunter's three-class taxonomy]] organizes by **demonstrated physics command** — chemical rocket, nuclear, Einstein-violating. MHD-propelled craft would fall in **Hunter's nuclear class** (Einstein-respecting but post-current-state-of-the-art) — this places the COMETA argument inside Hunter's chart rather than orthogonal to it. ^[inferred]
