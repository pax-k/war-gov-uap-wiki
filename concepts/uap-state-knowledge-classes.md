# UAP State-Knowledge Classes (COMETA, 1999)

A four-class taxonomy proposed in the [[references/cometa-report-1999|1999 COMETA Report]] (§10.3, "Repercussions of UFO Manifestations on the Official and Unofficial Conduct of States") for organizing nation-states by their relationship to the UAP phenomenon. It classifies states, not aliens — making it **orthogonal** to [[concepts/three-class-alien-taxonomy|Hunter's 1963 physics-command alien taxonomy]].

## The four classes

| Class | Definition | Implied posture |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | States that have **no knowledge** of extraterrestrial phenomena, or believe they are not concerned. | Wholly unprepared; first to be surprised. |
| **(b)** | States that **know** of extraterrestrial phenomena but **lack the means** to investigate. | Aware-and-passive; consume reports from class (c) and (d). |
| **(c)** | States that **know** and **have the means** to investigate. | Active investigators; produce policy and research artifacts. France argues itself into this class. ^[inferred] |
| **(d)** | States that have **entered into contact** with one or more extraterrestrial civilizations, established relations, or entered political / scientific / technical collaboration. | The fully-engaged case; COMETA implies the United States may be in this class. ^[inferred] |

## Source-text wording

Per COMETA §10.3 (verbatim):

> *Based on what can be learned of the reactions of States, it is permissible under our hypothesis to classify them as:*
>
> *a) States that have no knowledge of extraterrestrial phenomena, or believe they are not concerned.*
> *b) States that know of extraterrestrial phenomena but have no means to investigate them,*
> *c) States that know of extraterrestrial phenomena and have the means to investigate them,*
> *d) States that have entered into contact with one or more extraterrestrial civilizations and that have established relations and/or entered into political, scientific, and technical collaboration.*

## What this taxonomy does

- **Provides a vocabulary for the asymmetry between France and the US**. COMETA uses the framework to argue France should sustain class (c) capacity while pressing the US — implicitly placed in class (d) — to disclose. Recommendation 5 of the report ("diplomatic démarches to the United States, with the support of other States and even the European Union, to urge the superpower to collaborate") follows directly from this taxonomy. ^[inferred]
- **Pre-figures modern interagency coordination questions**. The taxonomy implies that any sustained UAP cooperation requires bilateral or multilateral acknowledgment of class membership — which by 1999 had not happened. ^[inferred]
- **Permits a critique of the disclosure asymmetry**. A class-(c) state has the right to expect class-(d) cooperation if class (d) exists; the absence of cooperation is itself evidence relevant to public-policy debate. ^[inferred]

## §10.4 — COMETA's strongest hint that the US is in class (d)

Chapter 10.4's verbatim sentence — newly anchored from the back-half ingest — is the report's most direct statement on US class status:

> *"It is true that the position of that country has been among the strangest since the wave [of sightings] in June 1947, followed by the Roswell affair in July 1947… If the Americans were able on that occasion or on other occasions to collect at least debris or entire wreckage of extraterrestrial vessels in fairly good condition, and even cadavers of humanoids, a certain type of contact would then have been established."* ^[extracted, COMETA §10.4]
>
> *"The simplest response would be that the United States wants to maintain at any cost military technological superiority over rival countries and, perhaps, a preferential contact."* ^[extracted]

The phrase *"a certain type of contact would then have been established"* is the closest COMETA comes to placing the US in class (d) — conditional ("if the Americans were able") but with the §9.1 Corso review and §A.5 Roswell appendix both arguing the conditional is satisfied. ^[inferred]

The §10.4 sentence is also the report's only explicit identification of the **AFR 200-2** and **JANAP 146** regulatory complex as the institutional apparatus underwriting US non-disclosure. Both regulations are independently confirmed in [[entities/leslie-kean|Leslie Kean]]'s [[references/boston-globe-ufo-2000-05-21|May 2000 Boston Sunday Globe piece]] — i.e., a US-press journalist arriving at the same identification of the relevant regulatory apparatus. ^[inferred — convergent attestation]

## Position relative to other corpus concepts

- **Orthogonal to [[concepts/three-class-alien-taxonomy|Hunter's three-class alien taxonomy]]**. Hunter classifies *aliens* by physics command. COMETA classifies *states* by knowledge and capacity. A class-(c) state could face a class-Einstein-violating alien, etc.
- **Couples to the [[concepts/four-phase-contact-strategy|four-phase contact strategy]]**. Class (d) corresponds to states experiencing Phase 3 or 4. Class (c) is plausibly Phase 2-aware.
- **Couples to the [[concepts/scientific-vs-saucer-advocate-frame|prepared-skepticism posture]]**. The taxonomy is built so a class-(c) state can maintain prepared-skepticism without claiming class-(d) status — which COMETA does throughout.
- **Resonates with [[concepts/eti-attribution-statements]]**. The 1948 Swedish AIS "outside the earth" attribution is what a class-(c) → class-(c) liaison looks like at the agency-attribution level. ^[inferred]

## Open questions

- Whether COMETA implicitly places **specific states** in class (d) (US is the strongest candidate). The report's hints (Roswell appendix, MacArthur and Reagan quotes, "preferential contacts… can be attributed to the United States") suggest yes, but the report does not name a class-(d) state. ^[ambiguous]
- Whether any subsequent UAP-policy literature has used the taxonomy as an analytical tool. ^[open]
- How the taxonomy would be updated for the **2020s era of public AARO investigations and congressional UAP hearings** — does the US move from "implicit (d)" to "explicit (c) with disclosure-debate"? ^[inferred-as-question]
- Whether the taxonomy should be extended to **non-state actors** (private aerospace, civilian research, AI-augmented analysts) in 21st-century reformulations. ^[inferred-as-question]
