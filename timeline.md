# Timeline

*Proposed reading order. Every wiki page is indexed here by the **event date its content is about** — not by the date the page was created. Pages with day-level dates appear first within a year, then month-level, then year-only. Read top-down to follow the corpus as a single narrative.*

**Companion files:** [[index]] (alphabetical by category) · [[log]] (ingest activity log) · [[hot]] (recent-session cache).

**Coverage:** day-resolved pages get the most precision; month-only and year-only pages are grouped below day-resolved entries within each year. Pages whose content has no datable anchor (most `concepts/`, most `entities/`) are intentionally not listed here — find them via [[index]].

## How this index is maintained

`timeline.md` is auto-maintained by every ingest/write skill. When a skill creates or updates a wiki page whose content has a **datable event** (a sighting, a memo, a directive, a mission, a cable, an interview), it appends or updates the corresponding entry here in chronological order. See `AGENTS.md` → "Timeline maintenance" for the convention.

**Date precedence used by maintainers:**

1. **Filename date suffix** — `*-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, `*-YYYY-MM.md`, `*-YYYY.md` — is authoritative.
2. **`event_date` in frontmatter** (if present) — falls back here when filename has no date.
3. **Year tag in `tags:`** (e.g. `1947`, `1969`) — gives year-level placement when no explicit date.
4. **None of the above** — the page is **not** listed here. List it in [[index]] only.

**Excluded by design:**

- Most `concepts/` pages (abstract patterns spanning many dates, not single events).
- Most `entities/` pages (people, agencies, organizations — listed in [[index]] under Entities).
- `synthesis/` cross-cutting analyses unless tied to a specific event.
- `_meta/`, `_raw/`, `_archives/`, `projects/` overview pages.

**Entry format:**

```
- **<date label>** — [[category/slug]] — <summary (≤200 chars, no trailing period)>
```

Date label:
- Day-resolved: `**8 Jul 1947**`
- Month-only:   `**Jul 1947**`
- Year-only:    `**1947**`

Within a year, sort day-resolved chronologically first, then month-only chronologically, then year-only alphabetically by slug.

**Regeneration**: when bulk-rebuilding (e.g. after `wiki-rebuild` or `wiki-lint` deltas), prefer regenerating the whole file from frontmatter rather than patching individual entries — the dated-filename convention makes a full rescan cheap.
