# UAP

A research project distilling declassified primary-source archives on Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP, formerly UFO / "Flying Discs") into structured, cross-linked wiki pages.

## Scope

- **Primary sources**: declassified US government bundles dropped into `_raw/` and processed by `wiki-ingest`, plus more recent agency-style briefings dropped into `sources/`.
- **Output**: concept / entity / reference / synthesis pages, plus this project overview.
- **Cross-cutting questions**: how the institutional UAP investigation regime evolved; which sightings have signatures that resist conventional explanation; which entities (people, programs, bases) recur across decades.

## Source archives

Plus Aldrin's lunar-orbit "laser" → "lake" reattribution (astronaut-self-correction with preserved residual anomaly) and Aldrin's Mars optical-illusion (astronaut-self-attributed). **Corpus firsts**: first **sub-class E (strong-positive)** orbital UAP-adjacent observation in the corpus's [[concepts/orbital-uap-sighting]] typology — multi-witness, instrument-mediated, alternative-eliminated, morphology-resolved; first **three-witness simultaneous on-orbit observation** (vs. GT-7 single-witness Borman, Apollo 12 single-witness Bean); first **Mission-Control-range-data-eliminated conventional candidate** in the corpus; first **MSC Mission Operations Branch / Flight Crew Support Division** sub-office instance (distinct from the d6 1973 MSC Training Office); chronologically earliest Apollo-program artifact (predates Apollo 12 d1 by 4 months). The **A vs E polarity refinement** is the corpus's most important orbital-class typological move — sub-class A admits the booster-misidentification reading (GT-7 Borman); sub-class E does not (Apollo 11 d4 §6). Sighting-anchor at [[references/sighting-apollo-11-cylinder-1969-07]].

### 1973–74 NASA Skylab — d7 Technical Crew Debriefings (NEW)

- [[references/nasa-uap-d7-skylab-technical-crew-debriefing-1973]] — 11-page Mistral-OCR'd composite excerpt from **all three Skylab Technical Crew Debriefings** (JSC-08053 SL-2 30 Jun 1973; JSC-08478 SL-3 4 Oct 1973; JSC-08809 SL-4 22 Feb 1974), prepared by the **Training Office, Crew Training and Simulation Division** (renamed *Procedures Division* between SL-3 and SL-4) at **[[entities/nasa|NASA]] Lyndon B. Cover sheets carry **only the FOIA notice** (5 U.S.C. 552 / NASA Policy Directive 1382.2) — no CONFIDENTIAL or SECRET classification stamp; this is a **structural distinction from d4 (CONFIDENTIAL Group 4) and d6 (CONFIDENTIAL with auto-declass)** and reflects the post-Apollo NASA institutional shift to FOIA-controlled-but-unclassified standard technical debriefings. ^[inferred] **The d7 artifact is a multi-mechanism payload comparable in structural breadth to d4 Apollo 11**: it introduces **one new sub-class (F)** in the [[concepts/orbital-uap-sighting|orbital-UAP-sighting]] typology, **extends sub-class D from one to two corpus instances**, and **substantively answers the [[concepts/cosmic-ray-light-flashes|cosmic-ray retinal flash concept page's]] LEO-inside-magnetosphere baseline open question**. ^[inferred]

Kerwin offers the **explicit South Atlantic Anomaly correlation hypothesis** (*"Someone asked me if that was in conjunction with the South Atlantic anomaly. ## UAP morphology (cross-era)

- [[concepts/orb-phenomenon]] — luminous spherical bodies; spans 1–2 s "flash-orbs," sustained-glow hovering orbs, "mother / daughter" launches, and **sequential flare formations**.
- [[concepts/orb-flare-formation]] — distinct sub-class: orbs flare up sequentially in a line/stack, hold stationary, flare down in reverse order. New from the 2025 statement.
- [[concepts/transparent-uap]] — partial-optical-transparency and beam-blocking signatures.

## UAP behavior

- [[concepts/uap-aircraft-engagement]] — orb-class objects approaching/pursuing crewed aircraft, including apparent target switching across platforms. New from the 2025 statement.
- [[concepts/engine-stall-phenomenon]] — recurring physical-effect signature: vehicle / equipment failure coincident with UAP proximity, recovery on departure. **(NEW)** Connects 1944 Krasuski (tractor stall) → 1957 Levelland TX (multi-vehicle cluster, the trigger for Krasuski's report) → 2023 FBI gate anomaly.
- [[concepts/rotating-disc-morphology]] — rotating-disc + stationary-inner-lights joint signature; anchored by the 1955 Trans-Caucasus instance. **(NEW)**
- [[concepts/observer-suppression-behavior]] — third-party operator restricting witnesses' continued observation contemporaneous with the sighting; anchored by the 1955 Soviet-trainmen reaction. **(NEW)**

## Cross-era patterns (NEW)

- [[concepts/eti-attribution-statements]] — explicit ETI-adjacent attributions in primary records, with two anchors: **1948 institutional / foreign-liaison** ([[entities/swedish-air-intelligence-service|Swedish AIS]] via [[references/usafe-tt1524-1948-11|USAFE TT 1524]]) and **1994 witness-side** (Capt Ed Rhodes via [[references/dos-cable-kazakhstan-1994-01|DUSHANBE 00259]]). The two are 46 years apart and structurally distinct evidence classes.
